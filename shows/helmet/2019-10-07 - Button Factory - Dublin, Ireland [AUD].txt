It had been a long time since I'd seen Helmet live before this gig and when
they announced the 30 shows/30 songs format I bought tickets for this
one as soon as they went on sale.

The show did not disappoint - 30 songs over a nearly 2 hour set. 

Page chats to the crowd a few times during the show but I didn't bother
splitting those chats out. 

I taped this one from my usual spot in the Button Factory and I'm pretty happy
with how it turned out. Things got a little hairy during Just Another Victim &
In The Meantime and I was dodging crowd surfers - I got a kick in the head
for my troubles but you can't hear that on the recording. 

For the old fashioned amongst us who still burn to disc I'd recommend
splitting after overrated.

Remember to support Helmet - go to their shows, buy their records & their merch.

Enjoy the show!!
