Helmet
Marquee
Sydney Australia
25th January 1993

Sony Walkman -> TDK D-60 -> TDK-D-60 -> Harmon Kardon tape deck -> SB Live -> Audacity -> FLAC

Taped during Helmet's first tour to Australia when there was the first beginnings of a big stir around them. Taped by a friend on his walkman I was lucky enough to get a copy of his tape around the time.

Sound is typical audience, not bad, but the crowd is quite loud. Quite a vibrant and exciting show. Helps if you crank it up.
