This one needed a little more EQ than I normally like to do, but I think the incredibly thin source coupled with typical radio compression warranted a bit of heavy make-up.

Tech notes: BBE SM Lo Contour around 33% - Process at 5% and output at -2, Waves Q3 Paragraphic + 3.3 @ 49 with a 7Q -  +4.5 159 @7Q and +2.5 @1943 7Q, Waves L3 MM thresh -4.9, ceiling 0.0, lo mids +0.7, Mids +1.1, high mids -0.3, highs -0.3 with Xovers at 80-400-1278-5113.
 

I edited the intros and the 9 second "version" of Yesterday, which was really nothing, certainly not worth it's own track. It sounds nice, much better than I expected when I started it. Enjoy!
