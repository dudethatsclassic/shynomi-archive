Editing: BLG -- EQ, clap removal, balanced channels, raised overall volume in Adobe Audition

Many thanks to Nocna Hubda for capturing this show (and Nick Cave show, the headliner from the July 13th night) and for responding to our request by promptly sharing the original flacs with us! I really did not expect any tapes from this festival to surface, so this was an awesome surprise!! Also, some people (certainly not many!) manage to get decent tapes with internal mics :-) Some wind and volume fluctuation here and there and a bit of audience on the background, but it's an outdoor festival, dammit - really not bad at all all things considered (especially with a little editing by BLG) !!
