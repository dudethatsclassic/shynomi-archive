Notes:

Special "Amok" - record release event, announced as "DJ-set" by Thom & Nigel 
but ended up being a great live performance, just with no gaps between the songs / 
a continous flow of tracks, like in a DJ-set.

Therefore it wasn't really easy to split the tracks, some parts were improvised,
some new material was played, so I've done my best to guess the right spots
and hope it's OK.

Needless to say that it was just absolutely awesome - both were in an excited mood,
the sound was brilliant (didn't expect anything else from the Funktion One PA),
only a few annoying people around me who looked like fancy fashion models on acid and
obtrusively shaked their limbs and drinks like being at a David Guetta show
certainly would've needed a few punches to behave - but oh well...

This recording is dedicated to all RH/AFP fans who didn't have the chance to grab 
a ticket for one of these few promotional events.
