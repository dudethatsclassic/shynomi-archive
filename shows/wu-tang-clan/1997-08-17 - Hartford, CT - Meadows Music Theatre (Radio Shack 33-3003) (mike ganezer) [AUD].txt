Throughout the four days I spent with Ryan of the NIN archive and Mark from LP Live I was hounded to create a Twitter account so they could tag me. I finally relented and lo and behold Mike reached out with the Rage Against the Machine Hartford setlist and tape! We exchanged a few emails and he sent me this tape along with a few others to transfer. A huge thanks to Mike for sending these over. I had been looking for a copy of this show a long, long time and had given up hope tracking it down. Really appreciate him sending this over!

I tried my best to track this one but failed miserably. I can't really tell where some songs begin and end. Sounds like they'd play like 90 seconds of a song, start chanting "WU TANG WU TANG" over and over and then go into another song. If someone wants to provide timestamps or breaks I'm happy to re-track!
