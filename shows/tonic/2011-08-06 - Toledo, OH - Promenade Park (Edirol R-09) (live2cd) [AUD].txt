Notes:

It rained between 7 and 815 with Tonic scheduled to take the stage at 845. 
They were pushed back to 9 and from then on there was not a cloud in the sky!

The crowd was pretty light as most had fled the park (or just did not come) due
to the weather - but it was their loss, not ours! Band sounded phenomenal and 
being this was my first Tonic show (crazy to think that) they more than impressed 
me! I hope to catch them again very soon - next time w/ my proper microphones.
