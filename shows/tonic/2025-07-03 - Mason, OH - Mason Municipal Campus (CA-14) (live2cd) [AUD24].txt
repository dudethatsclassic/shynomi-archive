Tonic and Better Than Ezra were announced as the live entertainment for 
2025's Red, Rhythm and Boom Festival in Mason, Ohio. Tonic took the stage
sharply at 6pm and ended just shy of 730pm. BTE were scheduled for 830pm
thru 10pm w/ fireworks following. This was one of the hottest shows I have
been to. It was in the mid 90s, but felt like it was well over 100 in the
sun. We had no shade and it was sweltering! Thankfully, I drank quite a
bit of water before their set and didnt deal with any heat stroke standing
still on the lawn taping this. BTE was a bit easier as the sun started to
go down when they came on. By the end of the night, I was exhausted!
But, it was worth it - this sounds good to my ears! Thanks for coming to
Ohio twice in the past 2 weeks, Tonic! You have been GREATLY missed.

Graham Whitford is featured on lead guitar since original member Jeff Russo
no longer tours with the band.
