Tonic were tapped to headline the 2025 Powell Festival in Powell, Ohio.
Believe it or not, this was the band's first headline show in Ohio in over
12 years. The past tours where they have stopped in Ohio (be it Huber Heights,
Columbus or Cleveland) have all been opening sets for bands like Collective
Soul, Sugar Ray & The Gin Blossoms. While those were fun, the band really get
going 30 minutes in, so when the set would end, it felt like there should have
been more. Well, finally, we got one! The weather was perfect and thanks to my
friend Rena, taping permission was secured. It was very exciting getting to
hear tracks like "Come Rest Your Head" and "Queen", which I havent heard since
that last Ohio headliner at the Columbus Hollywood Casino in 2013. The stand
was roughly 8ft up, but was positioned on a slight hill above the main pit
area in front of the stage, so it was more like 10ft up from stage level.
My neighbors were quiet (no loud whoo'ing or yelping) and the sound was above
average, so this is a great capture. If you're a fan, I highly recommend
checking this one out.

Mike Meadows is featured on lead guitar since original member Jeff Russo
no longer tours with the band.
