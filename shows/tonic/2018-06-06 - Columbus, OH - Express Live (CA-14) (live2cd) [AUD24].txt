Notes:

Opening for the Gin Blossoms. Vertical Horizon preceded Tonic's set. The PA 
was very heavy on the bass (it got progressively worse during the 2nd song, 
so I did EQ this a bit before normalizing in post. The kickdrum still 
distorts here and there but its now at least a bit listenable. During "You 
Wanted More", I could actually feel the bass thumping against my chest. 
Whomever was in charge of their sound should have been pulled from the board! 
Jesus christ! On a better note, "Liar", "Future Says Run", "Thick" and "On 
Your Feet Again" were played and completely made my night. I had never seen 
these 4 songs played live!
