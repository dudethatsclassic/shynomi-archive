Easily the strongest of all 3 bands this night, Tonic delivered in all
aspects. Emerson's voice was quite strong and unlike their 2012 appearance
opening up this same venue, Emerson was talkative and very appreciative
of the fans. He seemed to be really enjoying himself. "Wicked Solder" was
on the setlist but swapped out last minute for "Go Your Own Way".

"Let's go back to 1996.... shall we?" - EH

Lit and headliner Sugar Ray were also on the bill. [all recorded].
