Notes:

Tonic were the 2nd band on stage at 3pm sharp. I decided to start the day
recording from the right side of the field but after experiencing the screamer
behind me (you'll hear her between songs), I chose the left for the remainder
of the day. We also had less people on that side (further from the VIP lounge).
All recordings that day were extremely bass heavy. I ran each through 3 seperate
EQs to get them to be listenable. Ive literally spent 3 weeks tweaking each set
back and forth and this is the best I could get them to sound. Adjust your home
stereo accordingly. Thanks to Tonic for the great set, as always.

Also on the Hops & Hogs bill: Sister Hazel [not recorded], Fuel, Puddle Of Mudd,
Lifehouse & Collective Soul.
