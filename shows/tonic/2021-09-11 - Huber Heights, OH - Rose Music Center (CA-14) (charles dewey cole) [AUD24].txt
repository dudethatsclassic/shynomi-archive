Features a segue into "Dancing Days", Originally Recorded By Led Zeppelin.

Opening for Collective Soul. Rescheduled from 2020 due to COVID. Tonic were
the first of 3 bands, preceding Better Than Ezra and the aforementioned
Collective Soul. My entire section stayed seated for the Tonic until the
final 2 songs. The sound is pretty much the same, but I wanted it noted.
We had picture perfect weather conditions and all 3 bands sounded amazing!
I was hoping Tonic would play their new song released earlier this year,
"To Be Loved", but it wasnt meant to be. Maybe next time. Its a fantastic track.
