Notes:

This was held in the o.h. Lounge in the middle of the brand new Hollywood Casino 
Columbus. Show was free. Got there early and set up in the direct center of the 
room with my mic stand planted under a drink table. This seemed to work as 
people started using the space in front of my stand as the main aisle to get 
from one side of the room to the other. Thankfully, no bumping or knocking of 
the stand occurred. Recording turned out decent. The stand kept the chatter down. 
Some rare songs performed for the first time in years here. Listening back to 
this recording on headphones, its neat to hear Em mixed on the left w/ Jeff on 
the right. Recording definitely sounds better than the room did, believe it or not.

Soldier's Daughter was on the setlist but not played.
