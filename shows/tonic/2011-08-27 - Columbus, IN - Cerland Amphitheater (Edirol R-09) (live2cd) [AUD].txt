Notes:

* = Originally Recorded By AC/DC - this is only partial, no vocals.

This show was pretty special as the attendance was extremely low (my guess 
is there was little to NO promotion done) and that it took place literally
in the middle of the woods! Seriously, listen for the cicadas in between songs! 
It definitely had a "summer camp" vibe throughout the show, including storytelling
from Emerson, which added to the show. The guy is genuinely really funny.

After seeing their Toledo performance on 8/6/11, my interest in this great band
has since been renewed (I havent been as into them since high school), and before
this month never saw them live. I can safely say in the future I will make every
attempt I have to see them... they really put on one amazing concert. That goes for
Emerson solo as well. I also had hoped to have my CA-14's or DPA's ready in time
for this concert, but as luck would have it, they will be here 2 days late. 
Just the way it goes, sometimes.
