Comments:
Just came from TSS show in Barcelona. What a great show Glen Hansard  & Markéta Irglová and the boys performed...I loved since the very first minute.
The guest appearence of Javier Mas (guitar player from last 2007-2009 Leonard Cohen World Tour) added an incredible atmosphere with his guitar. He played
in Feeling The Pull, Backbroke and Astral Weeks, you will hear clearly his playing.

What happens when the heart just stops I think it is not played very often, and it was a fantastic solo version.

I have included Astral Weeks and High Horses from an alt. source because these 2 songs where played loud and my main gear suffered a little bit...

The recording came pretty well, the hall was full but I found a good spot for taping and enjoying the show...
