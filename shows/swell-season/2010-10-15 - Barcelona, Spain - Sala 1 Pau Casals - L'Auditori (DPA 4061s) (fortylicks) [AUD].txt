Comments: 
Incredible night of music by The Swell Season in l'Auditori, Barcelona. It was one of the best concerts I have ever been, great music, 
excellent sound, perfect view (centered, row 3), and The Swell Season was on fire and in great mood. 
Two examples: Song 21 was an imporv for Rob birthday, and last song was the classic "ranchera", sang by Marketa but with a lot of passion.

A special mention for Javier Mas, what a musician...his guitar puts the songs in a higher leve...
