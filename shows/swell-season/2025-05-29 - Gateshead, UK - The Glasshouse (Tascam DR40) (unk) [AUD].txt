The Swell Season, featuring Academy Award winners Glen Hansard and Markéta Irglová, is back on tour.
Known for their breakout roles in the film Once and the Grammy-nominated hit “Falling Slowly,” 
the duo went on to release the acclaimed album Strict Joy and inspire the Tony-winning Broadway musical Once. 
After successful solo careers and international tours, they’ve reunited to perform their heartfelt, award-winning songs live. 
Don’t miss this chance to experience their timeless music and the magic that made them a cultural phenomenon.

The Swell Season is a folk rock duo formed by Irish musician Glen Hansard and Czech singer and pianist Markéta Irglová. 
"The Swell Season" name is derived from Hansard's favourite novel by Josef Škvorecký from 1975 bearing the same title. 
Their debut album, released in 2006, carried the same name.From The Glasshouse website.
