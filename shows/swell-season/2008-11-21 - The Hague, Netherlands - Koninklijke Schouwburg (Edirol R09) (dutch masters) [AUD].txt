The Swell Season
Koninklijke Schouwburg
Den Haag / The Hague
The Netherlands
November 21st, 2008

Recorded by: Dutch Masters' Apprentice
Editing and other hard work by: Reel Clasio
Photos and artwork by Dutch Masters' Apprentice

Recorded with the Edirol R09 (WAV 44.1kHz/16 bit) and a Vivanco EM35 stereo microphone with battery pack. 
Recorded from 3 metres from the right speaker stack.

The song Say It To Me Now was sung without a microphone and using the acoustics of the theatre.
Unfortunately some photographer was sitting nearby and the clicking of his camera can be heard from time to time, but is less disturbing on the edited recording than it was on the day itself.

Big thanks to Reel Clasio!!!
Special thanks to Mr. & Mrs. Ridemyllama for introducing me to the music of the Swell Season.
