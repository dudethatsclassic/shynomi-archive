This set had it all. Mosh pits everywhere, fights breaking out, a crowd surfer kicking me in the head, two dudes behind me ripping coke talking about the sketchy muscle enhancers they buy on the dark web, talkers galore, the works. Korn had a great set for the last of the year. JD mentioned that next year was 30 years of Korn and that they would be doing something so stay tuned!

Recording notes: I started 40 meters from stage right in hopes of getting one of the angled stacks pointed at me. I couldn't get any closer, I think Korn might've had the biggest crowd of the weekend. But the crowd got so wild that I ended up moving diagonally toward the soundboard cutting through mosh pits. At one point I had to get real low to the ground and cover the mics to avoid a crowd surfer, I think that was toward the beginning. As such the sound varies quite a bit but gets progressively better as I get more center. I was really hoping for a better pull for this set but it is what it is.
