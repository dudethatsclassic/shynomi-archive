Notes:

+ = Features "One" tag, Originally Recorded By Metallica.
* = Features Amy Lee from Evanescence on backing vocals.

Just a week prior to the show, a great seat popped up in section 14 on the aisle
4 rows back from the pit! When I got there I felt like I was too close, but the
recording turned out just fine. I did point the mics upwards moreso than normal
on my hat's brim. The sound was bass heavy at times (it's Korn, afterall), so I
did EQ a bit to improve the overall sound. These guys are always great live, this
was no exception. "Freak On A Leash" with Amy guesting was my favorite moment.
 
Co-headlining with Evanescence [recorded]. 
Also on the bill were Helmet & Jeris Johnson [not recorded].
