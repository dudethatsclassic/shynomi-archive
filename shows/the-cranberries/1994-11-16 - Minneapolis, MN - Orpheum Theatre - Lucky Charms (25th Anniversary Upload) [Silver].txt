Additional details: No fan should be without this show. 
The recording is quite clear for an audience source and the performance is one of the best I've heard. The music is tight and Dolores is in great spirits. 
She does a good deal of chatting with the audience too. It's also worth mentioning that "When You're Gone" makes the set - a new song at the time.
