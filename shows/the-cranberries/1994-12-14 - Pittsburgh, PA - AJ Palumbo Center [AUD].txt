Source: First generation cassette remaster courtesy of Cranberries Library > www.cranberrieslibrary.com

Notes: Of course, a resounding thank you to the original taper!  This one is a favorite for sure.  The sound is full and balanced and no audio is lost.
The band is in top form delivering the very best from albums one and two.

If you love "Yeats' Grave" like I do, you're in luck. As most know, it didn't make the set list often.  It's a definite highlight here.
Additionally, the band surprises this crowd with rare performance of the quietly reflective, "False".  A track that, to date, still remains unreleased as a proper studio cut.

A suggested track split is between 12 (Ode to My Family) and 13 (Empty) for CD-R burn and playback.
