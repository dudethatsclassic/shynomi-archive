Live X shows were intimate, acoustic, stripped-down performances in front of small audiences of 99X listeners who 
received free tickets through calling in to the station.  The shows were recorded and later broadcast by the radio 
station.  This appearance by Chris Cornell was in support of the Euphoria Morning album.  At the time I had not 
fully embraced Euphoria Morning, but I attended this show with the hope of hearing the song Seasons, which is my 
favorite Chris Cornell song.  I was not disappointed as he performed a beautiful rendition, and the lively crowd 
appreciated the entire set.
