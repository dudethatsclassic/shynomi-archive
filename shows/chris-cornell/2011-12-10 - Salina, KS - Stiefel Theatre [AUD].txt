Hope you enjoy! I DID! --- This a major sound improvement compared to my Dallas recording. Lucky
night as I don't think Chris was wanting to go on until it appeared that everyone was in their
seats.....and although officially "SOLD OUT"...there were a large amount of seats up front and in
the middle....the theatre ushers were asking people in the back rows if they wanted to fill in the
closer seats! So I ended up with great seats about 13th row center on the floor in this extremely
beautiful looking & sounding theatre. This was a completely different atmosphere and experiece than
the Dallas show. This was exactly what I wanted to see. I think the seats helped this recording
a great deal.
