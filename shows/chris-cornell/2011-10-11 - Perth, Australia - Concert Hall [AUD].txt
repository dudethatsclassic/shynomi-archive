notes
2nd sold out show at the Concert Hall. The recording came out great, it's one of the nicest recordings I've ever captured. Highlight of the show for me was Man of Golden Words > Comfotably Numb, just a great version. It was nice to have an appreciative crowd that let the guy on stage entertain them with very little crowd interferance during the songs, and actually let the song finish before whistling and clapping. I wish all shows were like this. Any loud claps have been removed and after amplifying the recording I went through it again enveloping and then reducing the crowd levels. The only real bad song tracking is between Like A Stone and Doesn't Remind Me where Chris goes straight from one song to another. Chris is in a very talkative mood, and very entertaining, maybe if the singing thing doesn't work out for him a career in comedy could be the go.

compiled 13th Feb 2014
