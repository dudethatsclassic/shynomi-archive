NOTES:
What is there really to say? An incredible and emotional night of music. Sadly I had to 
catch a 1am flight at LAX so I had to duck out as Black Hole Sun was ending. 

I have done my absolute best to make the massive cast of rotating musicians obvious, apologies
if it isn't clear in some places. I also separated out all speeches and videos in case 
people want to remove them and leave the music on its own. I have also done a minimal amount
of leveling for different artists. I mostly pulled down the extra loud ones (Foos and Audioslave)
but prefer to leave the dynamics more or less unchanged for the rest of the show.
