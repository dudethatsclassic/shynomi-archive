Chris Cornell - 2011 12 15 @ Palace of Fine Arts, San Francisco, CA

Show note:
The Songbook tour. This was recorded very low, and the performance was quite quiet.  I had to raise the gain, and in some cases lower the applause to get a better volume balance. There were some places with mic noise that I removed, or tried to lower to make it less obtrusive.

See a review here: http://www.theowlmag.com/live-reviews/chris-cornell-the-songbook-tour-the-palace-of-fine-arts-12-15-11/ and flickr photoset here: https://www.flickr.com/photos/theowlmag/sets/72157628460902645/
