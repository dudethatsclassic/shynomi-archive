notes:  Slight gap between Doesn't Remind Me and Sunshower.  I manually started recording a new file and missed Chris introducing Sunshower as "the song I wrote for a Gwyneth Paltrow movie" but didn't miss any of the actual song.  There was another song or two on the setlist, at least Spoonman that I saw, that didn't get played due to curfew.  

This show took place during the middle of a week long vacation for me out west with my wife.  We drove from Zion National Park thru a snow storm to get to this show only to have trouble finding parking due to a Utah Jazz game across the street from the venue.  Despite getting there just before Chris took the stage we got some great seats in the balcony off to the side of the stage maybe 10' back from the hanging stacks...the band put on a great show and the recording turned out above average in my opinion.  And the new songs were well received by a great audience, maybe I'm just used to the drunks at shows back home?  After the show we headed to Arches National Park for a couple days.  Best week I've had in a long time!
