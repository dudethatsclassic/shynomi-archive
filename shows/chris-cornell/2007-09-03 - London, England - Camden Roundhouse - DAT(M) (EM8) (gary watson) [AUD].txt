NOTES
-----------------
You Know My Name is with David Arnold.
Billie Jean is a Michael Jackson cover.
Slaves & Bulldozers starts with Chris singing lyrics from Like A Stone.
Tracks 12 -> 15 are Chris performing solo with an acoustic guitar.
Noisy crowd as I wasn't close enough to the stage, probably standing in the middle of the venue.
Silence The Voices has some level fluctuations at the beginning that I've fixed, as best as I can.
Part of the crowd noise during the encore break is missing - sounds like my mic came unplugged?
A fade and edit was made to smooth out the missing encore break crowd noise.
Various sections were at different levels, so the volume of each section has been increased to give the overall sound some balance: Wave Goodbye has a minor but noticeable glitch where this happened, and there are very minor fluctuations still audible at the beginning of Slaves & Bulldozers.
