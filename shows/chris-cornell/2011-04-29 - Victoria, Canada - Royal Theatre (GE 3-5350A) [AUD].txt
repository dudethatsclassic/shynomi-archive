As always, I'll start with a warning about the recording quality. I taped this from the balcony on an old 80s walkman.
It doesn't sound awful - some of the dialog is hard to decipher, but it sounded the same at the show. The crowd was
very loud and very appreciative, and Chris played a pretty impressive set spanning his entire career and a number of covers.

If you want to tweak this recording, go ahead. Just please don't sell it, the buyer would probably be pretty disappointed.
