NOTES:
Another difficult taping night; very invasive security at every possible stage of the experience.
A few cameras made it in and anybody with a cell phone was ganged up on and treated like the scum of
the earth by the guards, who were given a thorough lecture before the gig to be hardline all the way.
Your humble narrator had no direct attention from the goons, but being as I was in the 2nd row next
to the floor, I had a flashlight lit up in my direction on average of once every 2-3 minutes due to
some dufus nearby having the raw, unbelievable nerve of...wanting to take a smartphone picture.

Nonetheless, some brave soul was able to film about 90% of the night from the floor; it's on youtube.

That's a lot of whining above, I know, but I did really enjoy the show once I learned to tune out all
of the lights and screaming (oh yeah, the screamers--wanted to bag and gag many of the people around me).
Many people don't like the eMotive-heavy covers setlist, but most of the songs are re-arranged so much
as to almost barely be a cover song, and I felt they came off very powerfully live overall both nights.

Maynardism of the night could take the form of some unfortunate last-minute lyrics revision to Gravity.
