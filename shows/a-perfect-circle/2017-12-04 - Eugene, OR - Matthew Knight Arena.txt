NOTES:
A brief word about security. Much has been spoken about the thugs-in-uniforms at these shows, and it was
actually enough to scare me away from attempting to record Portland--which infuriated me all that night
in hindsight. An experienced taper can get their shit through any door, and this was really no serious
challenge. Not being noticed by the attack dogs while drunken fools wave their cell phones around like
a homing beacon is another matter... but I digress. It was quite refreshing to see a show without half
the crowd holding up those goddamn cell phones all night, and it's disturbing that it takes shock tactics
to make such a thing happen. Security was actually a lot worse the last time I taped APC in 2011...

Positioned on the back wall of the second level of seating, almost dead-center (slightly stage-right).
The distance worried me--I foresaw a boomy, treble-free capture--but it turned out ok. Sound was good in
the arena. My seat was fantastic, perfect view of the distant stage and at the top of the stairs. Nobody
in front of me because it was at the top of the stairs. Security kept a distant perimeter, only a few
times entered the area to hassle a cellular offender.

A loud drunk girl is next to me. Quite a few moments I came close to re-enacting a scene from Bill Hicks'
"I'm Sorry Folks" performance, but cooler head prevailed. She was actually very friendly and shared her
popcorn, but never once stopped talking. Wherever you are drunkzilla, stay thirsty my friend. Mercifully
this is mostly absent on the recording as I kept some real estate between this cacophony and the mics.

A word about the actual performance now... Fan and collector of the guys since 2001, and I dare say that
APC are at the height of their (disjointed) career right now. Quite a performance from everybody in every
aspect of the show--from the stage lights, instrument balance, to setlist. McJunkins & Friedl are really
a great rhythm section, Iha & Howerdel shred the guitars, and Maynard is actually acting like he gives
a shit from his shadowy podium in the back. Only downside: it was the last show of the tour, and I wanted
to hear Gravity AND Passive. Meh.

If Feathers, Hourglass, Doomed, etc and their general attitude right now is any indication, actually quite
excited to see what kind of album and tour they're coming up with for next year, and I never thought I'd
say that about them again after 2011. Thanks, Donald.

The Beta Machine were very good as well, featuring McJunkins & Friedl to open, but were not recorded.

MJK was sick for the last few shows of the tour, but was sounding pretty good actually. Fucking those
ducks on his day off after Portland apparently did him some good.
