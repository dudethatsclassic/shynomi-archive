Note:  First time seeing A Perfect Circle since touring for Mer de Noms, and I was amazed at just how huge these guys were, especially after not having released an album in 12 years.  Then again, I think this was their first tour in 5 years, so it probably drew in a lot from the fact that no one had probably seen them live for the last 10 years.\
\
Anyway, bought a last minute floor ticket off eBay, and off I went.  Sound is a B+ for the first 2 songs as the crowd gets their shouting done and the mix is cleared up.  Afterwards an A-/A subjectively.  Not counting the 5-10 annoying shouts from the girl two seats down, there wasn't that much crowd noise around me. Pretty happy to have checked another band off my bucket list of bands I still want to record.\
