notes: No EQ, just raised the volume some.  Flew out and caught all 3 shows in the middle of a week long vacation in Los Angeles and San Diego.  Long story short, I wasn't able to use my mics on night one but did tape nights 2 and 3.  Though this is my least favorite album, it was the most enjoyable show of the 3 nights.  Like night 2, crowd noise is minimal as we got front row balcony spots, on the right side this time so we could actually see Maynard.  I particularly enjoyed the stretch of What's Going On > Passive > Gimme Gimme Gimme.  The crowd was very respectful during Fiddle and Drum, so much so that you can hear subtle noises from the bar below us at times.  Though Troy played during 3 Libras on the previous night, he was not at this show.  The Noose was a nice change from Imagine as the closer.  Special thanks to Distortion and Jason for putting up with us and an all around great vacation!
