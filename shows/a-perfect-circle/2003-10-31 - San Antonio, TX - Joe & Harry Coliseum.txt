A Perfect Circle is:

Billy Howerdel : Drunk Gay Sailor
Maynard James Keenan : Shakes The Clown
Josh Freese : Flower-Pot
Jeordie White : Twiggy Ramirez
James Iha : Heidi

Notes: Great Halloween tour opener! The band dressed up for the show.  Maynard introduced Jeordie White as "Twiggy Ramirez" 
(he was wearing a gold/silver prom dress and wig), James Iha as "Heidi" (wearing pigtails and a blue and white dress), 
himself as "Shakes The Clown" (wearing an orange afro wig, clown pants and clown shoes), Billy as "Drunk Gay Sailor" 
(wearing a black short bob wig and a sailor uniform), and Josh as "Flower-Pot" (he had pink hair and appeared to be wearing 
an old yellow Devo jumpsuit). Teases in between songs include "Fred's Got Slacks" by Jeordie White & "I Want It That Way" 
performed by James Iha.  At the end of the show, Jeordie (guitar), James (Vocals), & Billy (drums) wouldn't leave the stage 
and started playing Motley Crue's "Looks That Kill" & then Jeordie played "Fred's Got Slacks: again.  Soon Billy (Bass), 
joined then finally Josh (drums). They played "Master Of Puppets" by Metallica.  Throughout the show, Maynard kept 
saying "Merry Christmas".
