A Perfect Circle
February 17, 2001
UNO Lakefront Arena
New Orleans, LA


Source: Sony MDR-Q33LP > line-in > Sony MZ-R55 minidisc

Location: seats, middle/back of the arena, approx. 5 rows off the floor, 20' behind the soundboard

Transfer: MZ-R55 > line-out > WAV (soundcard via Totalrecorder)

Mastering: Wavlab 3.04a (EQ, dynamic compression, track splits)

Recorded and Mastered by Adam Lancaster (adam_lancaster462@yahoo.com)


Note:  This show was supposed to take place at the State Palace Theater, but was moved a day or two prior.  I had floor seats for the original venue, but only the "pit" tickets from State Palace were allowed on the floor at UNO, forcing the rest of us up into the seats further from the stage.  Side note... I wish I had invested in a pair of true microphones by this time in my taping "career", so this tape would sound better than it does.  This is probably a better listen on a decent stereo, rather than on headphones.
 

Also not that these files were ripped from my original master audio CD-R. Unfortunately I was not in the habit of keeping the original WAV files back then.
