A Perfect Circle - 2024 04 27 @ Las Vegas Fairgrounds, Las Vegas NV

Note:
Sick New World - Red Stage. This was a tough one. A friend taped this and Primus, but his mic cables were defective, making it all unusable. I was able to find a couple of people who recorded most of the full show, but their phones overheated and stopped recording at points, etc. etc. I was able to assemble the whole show, but needed to adjust levels to have a somewhat even show. Camera phones, also, have auto gain, which makes the quiet parts loud, so I needed to adjust them down, usually the bits between songs. Anyhow, A Perfect Circle and Primus had been in Colorado the night before, and they got to the show in time but not their gear, so all of the gear was rented / borrowed, making this a somewhat unique show for them.
