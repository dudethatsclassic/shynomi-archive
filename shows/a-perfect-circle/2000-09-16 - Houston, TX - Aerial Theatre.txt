A Perfect Circle
September 16, 2000
Aerial Theater
Houston, TX


Source: Sony MDR-Q33LP > line-in > Sony MZ-R55 minidisc

Location: Rear wall of the theater (I had no idea what I was doing)

Transfer: MZ-R55 > line-out > WAV (soundcard via Totalrecorder)

Mastering: Wavlab 3.04a (EQ, dynamic compression, track splits)

Recorded and Mastered by Adam Lancaster (adam_lancaster462@yahoo.com)


Note:  This was only my second attempt at taping a show.  Since it was general admission, I stood where I was comfortable... on the back wall, and the sound of this tape shows it.  Probably a better listen on speakers than headphones. 

Notably, my girlfriend and I drove 4 hours to this sold out show in Houston without tickets in hand.  We stood outside looking for affordable tickets all afternoon, only to be given 2 free ones just before the doors opened, courtesy of a local radio station.
 
Worth noting that Maynard dedicated "Ozzy's Cure" to Danny Lohner, and slipped his name into the lyrics just about everywhere he could. (i.e. "Whenever I'm alone with Danny, he makes me feel like a man again!")

Lastly, note that these files were ripped from my original master audio CD-R. Unfortunately I was not in the habit of keeping the original WAV files back then.
