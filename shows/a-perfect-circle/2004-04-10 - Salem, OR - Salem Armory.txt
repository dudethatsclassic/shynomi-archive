Notes:
This is an old recording that I was listening to, and was surprised I had not distributed it. When I listened, I understood why. Not only is the Salem Armory a horrible sounding venue, but my mic connection was a bit jacked during parts of the show. In one channel, there were slight periodic dropouts. I did my best to fix these in Cool Edit, but you can still notice a few pops here and there. Nonetheless, it's quite listenable, and a good show, to boot. Enjoy!
