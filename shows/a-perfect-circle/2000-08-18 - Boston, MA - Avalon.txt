Contrast clause: this torrent is different from http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=691964
because I have applied the following using Audacity:

Lowered the volumes on the worst pops, there were about 15 in total.
These mostly occur in the first half of the show (there were no pops from Track 8 onwards).

Applied the following settings with the 'Bass & Treble' effect:
-4 Bass 
+2 Treble

This also allowed room to raise the overall volume slightly.

It seems a smoother listening experience with these edits.
I hesitate to call this a 'remaster', and this is not intended to be the 'definitive' version of this show.
Thanks to the original taper.
Enjoy!
