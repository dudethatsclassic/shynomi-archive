First song is only last 1:20 as the concert was starting as we were trying to get in, this brought up the question, who starts a concert at 8:45 pm ? None the less security was tight in our section do to the girl next to me who had to get her phone out. Thankfully my mic's were mounted on my jacket so security was oblivious to me. They took the girl out, and assuming had her delete whatever was recorded and let her back in but upon every trip down the isle would stare over. When I record I usually never bring in my phone and while I remain neutral on the whole 'got to take pics, video etc', I have to admit it was refreshing to be at a show with no phones in the air. 

Great show, recording turned out much better than expected. I thought the sound was a bit muddy during the show, listening back to it, its nice, clear and powerful. Always a pleasure to see Mr Keenan in Michigan.
