Source: Church Audio AT831 > 9v batt box > Edirol R-09 line-in (WAV @ 24 bit, 48 kHz)

Location: Section A, Row 1, Seat 10 (front row, in front of Billy Howerdel)

Transfer: SD card > card reader (USB) > WAV

Mastering: Wavlab 3.04a (EQ, dynamic compression, dithering and resampling to 16 bit, 44.1 kHz) > CD Wave Editor (track splits) > FLACfrontend


Recorded and Mastered by Adam Lancaster (adam_lancaster462@yahoo.com)


Notes: Not my best recording by a long shot... not even my best APC recording, but it's more than listenable, I'd say.  Frustratingly, I sent my DPA 4061's off to Core Sound for repair, but they were not able to get them back to me in time for this show, as promised.

When the house lights went down, I was unfortunately out in the lobby, and had to rush to get back to my seat in the front row.  Note that twice during this show, security came through to clear out people that had made their way to the front from other seats.  Very annoying, considering they could have been watching and checking tickets all along.

Enjoy!
