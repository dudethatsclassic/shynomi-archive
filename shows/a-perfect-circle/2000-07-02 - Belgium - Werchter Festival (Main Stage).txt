Studio Brussel cut/spoke-over Magdalena at the beginning, and Breña at beginning and end.  I faded out Judith to the point at which 3FM's playback equipment malfunctioned and the presenters just moved on with the rest of their show.  The brief drop-out during Ozzy's Cure sounds to me more like a tape-deck pause-button problem on behalf of the taper.  My CDR also includes Thinking Of You from Studio Brussel's broadcast (which may also have included more tracks) but I didn't see any point including the song twice.

These broadcasts should doubtless exist in a better format somewhere, but after years of looking these are the only sources I've found.  If anyone's able to track down a better tape then I'm sure it'd be greatly appreciated by many.
