notes:  Missed the intro, hit record right before Annihilation started.  Odd that this was the loudest of the 3 shows so far given that it was the farthest away from a speaker stack I had been.  Mics were taped to the balcony railing instead of the typical under the shirt location.  I left the camera in the car at the last minute but turned out our angle really sucked for filming.  It felt like we were 90 degrees off the stage corner, you couldn't see any of the backdrop.  Overall, a somewhat disappointing show but taping the mics to the railing and be able to sit back and talk at a show was nice for a change given that Distortion sat next to me after debating whether or not to even come out to the show (He did not tape).  The sound was great, but it was hard to get into the show given the angle we were sitting and the lackluster setlist we were hearing for the 3rd time.  Of the songs they switch out, I would rather have heard What's Goin On and Magdalena but we got Gravity and Orestes instead.

Good bit of distortion at loud parts of the show.  I forgot to bring my 1/8" > 1/4" adapter so I used the 1/8" input on my MT.  Unfortunately, this meant that with my levels turned all the way down, I still got some clipping during the loudest parts of this run of shows I taped.  I didn't realize this until after the LA show, which is probably the worst of the 3 dpa>MT recordings I did.  The last night I ran dpa>m1 but it has issues all of it's own.  I debate releasing all of these, but until other sources from these shows pop up, I'm sure there are a few people out there that will enjoy listening to them.
