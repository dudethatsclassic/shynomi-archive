Notes:
This recording noticably suffers from the taper having been stuck in the pit amidst drunken "fans" (if you pay $75 for a ticket, wouldn't you want to hear the band play the songs?). At one point I lost my patience and yelled at someone behind me starting a moshpit during The Outsider (which they did again during 3 Libras and some other songs). Long story short: there's an inverse relationship between the energy of the song and the quality of the recording. In most cases, however, there's merely shouting at the beginning and end of the songs, while the important part is captured relatively noise-free.
I wanted to have a faster turn-around time than this, but real life got in the way. This recording is dedicated to EBC, who braved the long trip and annoying fanboys with me, as well as BC, who finally got to see MJK live.
