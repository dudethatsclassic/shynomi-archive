Notes:
First show of the tour, it was incredible. Great setlist that had a bit of everything. Tape came out good. There is some humming in ‘You Won’t Know’ and ‘137’ from what I believe to be a problematic pre-amp. This has happened to me numerous times when using it. But everything else is good. Hopefully I can patch this up with a source that should be coming soon from a taper that was a few rows in front of me. 

Shout out to Brand New Archive who is archiving all of Brand New’s performances. Check them out on twitter, @brandnewarchive. Really doing a good job and I appreciate anyone who is doing such a task, because I know from experience! Enjoy.
