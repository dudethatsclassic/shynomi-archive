Notes: This mix was originally done back in 2003 and I don't think it ever circulated much.  If I remember
correctly, the KM140 source missed the first part of National Anthem and was in mono until half-way through
Morning Bell due to a loose cable.  The IEM source was also missing most of National Anthem and had a few 
flaws in various places.  Because of this, I mixed in the Nak source for the first 7 minutes or so, and also
patched as seamlessly as possible the flaws in the IEM.


I do not know the origin of the supposed soundboard source, but due to its mix, I am assuming it is actually
from IEM.  I picked up the Nak source via a trade back in 2002, if I remember right, it was taped from the
front of the lawn.
