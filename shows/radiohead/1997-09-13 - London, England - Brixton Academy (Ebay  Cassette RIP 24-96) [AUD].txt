notes: i bought this cassette off of ebay as something I have never seen circulating digitally. there was no info on it so I crossed my fingers and hoped for the best. I don't even have a tape deck so I gave it to a buddy and the results were less than stellar. lots of noise, wobbly, dropouts, etc. I wasn't sure what to do with it. it wasn't worth releasing in that shape. the tape sat on my desk for some months until I stopped being lazy and sent it to pzy from the radiohub, who used his magical Nakamichi Dragon tape deck to get a proper rip. there are still dropouts (noted in setlist) but all in all it's a solid tape that is almost old enough to drink in the US. there's an enthusiastic 'woo' lady near the taper but she is mostly quiet during the music. and an enthusiastic sing-along crowd at times...but who can blame them? it was an exciting time to be a radiohead fan. if anyone has a better pull of this show, please share it!
