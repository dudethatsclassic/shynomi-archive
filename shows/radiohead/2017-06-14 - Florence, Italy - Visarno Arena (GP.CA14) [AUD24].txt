Tape story:
The venue (eventually called Visarno Arena) is at the Florence horse track (Ippodromo del Visarno) 
I arrived at the very last time so I could locate myself only at about 70 m from the stage and 25 m in front of the 2nd row of speakers (left-hand speaker), 10 m behind the soundboard line
I was sure to have locked the recorder but the recording accidentally stopped during Daydreaming when I was putting the R-09 into my bumbag
The result was that about 15 secs of the track were lost but I have managed to merge both sections of the recording in a seamless way
Most of the people around me were completely into the show, others were lovely chatters
The recording is enjoyable considering the not ideal location with occasional clapping and screaming and background chatting on some quieter songs

What about the show? 
It was my third Radiohead show (the others were in Milan 2008 and Bologna 2012, both taped)
Very good show with a greatest-hit setlist based on all the albums (at least 2 tracks for each) but Pablo Honey
OK Computer (6 tracks) and A Moon Shaped Pool (5 tracks) were mainly performed
