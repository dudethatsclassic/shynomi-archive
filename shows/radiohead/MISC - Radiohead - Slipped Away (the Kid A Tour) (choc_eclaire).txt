************************************
*            RADIOHEAD             *
*           Slipped Away           *
*         (the Kid A tour)         *
************************************

In the summer of 2000, Radiohead went on a brief tour of the Meditteranean (mostly) to roadtest
material for their upcoming fourth studio album, exposing the audiences to a host of strange,
adventurous, and beautiful songs. By September, they had a title for the upcoming album - Kid A -
and a new tour to travel on, performing across northern Europe in a specially designed circus
tent, free of advertising.

Across the 41 shows they played in 2000, Radiohead performed 44 songs - eighteen of which were
unreleased at the start of the year, nine of which still were after it. Varying audience members,
to whom I am forever grateful, taped all of those songs (except for You - a single performance in
Greece, which hasn't yet surfaced), sharing them throughout the Internet.

Twenty years later, Kid A is celebrated as one of the greatest albums of all time, a masterwork
of experimentation and artistic growth. To celebrate the anniversary, I listened to all of the
circulating bootlegs from the tour to find the best or most interesting performance of each song,
assembling them into one compilation. The audio quality varies, and some of the transitions are a
little clumsy, but hopefully it's still a fairly enjoyable listening experience.

Many thanks to the great people at the Radiohub for letting me download all of the shows off them,
a NIN fan called NotinCnStopMeNow for inspiration, and of course the band themselves.
Keep everything in its right place,
-choc e-Claire

A note on the [intro] and [outro] tracks:
I've split this into three CDs in case anyone wanted to burn them onto discs for personal usage
(will genuinely be impressed if you do - please send pictures). I've also designed it so that you
can just listen to it as one continuous three-hour set - if you want to do that, just remove the
tracks from the play queue of whatever app you're listening to this in.

All tracks sourced from FLACs except:
Permanent Daylight, Paranoid Android, I Might Be wrong, and Follow Me Around
(.shn > .wav through Trader's Little Helper > .flac)
Fog (.mp3 -> .flac through Audacity)
