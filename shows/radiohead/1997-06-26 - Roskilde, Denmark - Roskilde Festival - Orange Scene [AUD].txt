notes: the master tape recorded with the mic sensitivity setting on the D7 set to "high" which 
unfortunately makes for a recording that is rather saturated, particlularly during the loud parts
of the show. I have EQ'd the wav files with WavLab 3.0 in the following way: 

high: +7.5db
mid:  +5.8db
low:  -4.5db

The bass remains quite prominent in the mix even after the EQ, and boosting the mids and highs 
helps to compensate for the low end saturation, making a much more listenable recording than the 
raw, unedited master. Even so, it is not perfect, nor close to it. But since no other source has 
surfaced of this particular performance (that I am aware of), I figure it's worth putting out.
