You know, I like that idea that Tolkien had about birthdays. That the Hobbits have a tradition where the one who has his birthday gives presents to people instead of opening his arms ready to receive.

Anyway, to promote this fine concept (it really is when you think about it a little), I decided to give you nice people something I had promised to set free for a while now. 

I don't like hoarding... almost 2 years ago I received a DVD with Thom's solo performance at the Trade Justice ralley at the Methodist Central Hall in London on april 16th 2005. I got it as a "thank you" for doing my website (www.citizeninsane.eu). Now I think I've kept it to myself long enough, it's ready to go out. It's a really fine DVD, there's also footage of Thom and Stanley painting before the little gig.

Audio from this has circulated for years, and since last year you could also see some of the videos from this in my website (I just HAD to make use of it there).

So, enjoy!
