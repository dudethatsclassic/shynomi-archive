Source: Audience Sony handycam VHS - Converted to DVD
Recorded by Mick
Produced and edited by Ceb

Video: 720x576, 25.000 fps, 4/3, 7000kbps
Audio: AC-3 192 kbps, 48 khz, 2-channel stereo

Cover design by Ceb

notes:
ALRIGHT! Here's a surprise for all of you who, like me, think that all video material from the glorious Radiohead past has been dug up and seeded. And especially for those still missing the OKC days! Just when you least expect it, here's an incredible gem: a full video of the Dublin show just a week before the famous Glastonbury when our heros shook the world :)

This was shot by a fan on a handy cam; but despite natural shakes here and there - the video is mostly steady and presents an unobstructed clear shot  - unlike most audience footage from back in the days!! The view is center and stage left (Jonny's side); you do catch less Ed, only when he sort of walks over to the middle. A bit blurry, but what an incredible document! Just check out this setlist…. MANY MANY THANKS to Ceb for bringing this treasure to the Hub and dropping it on us!!!!
ENJOY!!!
