Cynthia Woods Mitchell Pavilion
The Woodlands (Houston), TX
October 1, 2003

Source:  Unknown DAT (?) Transfer (No confirmed lineage).
Remastered by TheTooleMan May 2004

The remaster of this recording is dedicated to the memory of my son, Danny Toole. He passed away October 21, 2007, after an accidental fall from his dorm room balcony in Austin, TX. He will always be missed, but will live on in the hearts of the multitude of people who knew him and were touched by him.

Danny went to this concert to celebrate his 16th birthday, which was the next day, October 2, 2003. When I found this recording on Sharing The Groove a few months later, I grabbed it for him. The original is a nice, clear recording, but the levels were low and the overall sound needed some EQ help. One of the two encore breaks, consisting entirely of applause and audience noise, seemed unnecessary, so I removed it. After I remastered the recording then gave it to him. He was thrilled, said it sounded great, and made copies for his friends.

You can read more about Danny at http://www.legacy.com/houstonchronicle/DeathNotices.asp?Page=Lifestory&PersonId=96745369. You can also contribute in his memory to The Danny Toole Memorial Turing Scholarship Fund of the University of Texas. For more information, visit http://www.cs.utexas.edu/oea/services/giving/danny_toole/
