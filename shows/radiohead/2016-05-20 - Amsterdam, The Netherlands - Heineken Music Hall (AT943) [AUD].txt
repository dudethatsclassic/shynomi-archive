SO HAPPY!!!
Here’s the first show of the Radiohead 2016 tour!! We wanted to get it out to everyone as quickly as we could ;) Which means bnz did this fantastic job taping (and deserves most of the credit!), and then the files have travelled to California and BLG did his usual magic mastering, back to the US east coast to me for tracking - and here we are :)
I won’t comment on specific songs, I certainly have my favorites - but I’ll leave it to you to judge! Personally I think the setlist is amazing.

ENJOY!  :)
