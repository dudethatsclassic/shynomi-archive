Notes from Inez:
Happy anniversary!!!
3 years - seems like yesterday - well, we can sort of cross out a year and a half of pandemic...
Anyways - the immense thanks go to the filmer, lordazuretwentyfive, who was on duty every night capturing these incredible shows... You probably have seen the raw footage from the camera on Youtube. I should add: this particular show was transferred and edited AT MY VERY KITCHEN TABLE between gig 2 and gig 3 (we had 1 night off), on a 27" iMac that temporarily moved into my apartment for that purpose (!!), along with 5 Radiohead fans lololol If this sounds in any way weird to you - you have not met enough Radiohead fans yet :)
I am also thanking Edgar profusely again (for being on the job in the back by the SBD) whose glorious tape has now been synced to the video. The tape was released back in 2018 and is still seeding here - http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=626829

The video has been edited slightly right that night and is missing a bit of in-between songs time and encore breaks (about 6 min total), so syncing Edgar's audio was also a 'process' - and of course I am thanking flying horseman for being the man for the job!! 
The original audio from the camera is kept as track 2 - but there is a REASON we are going through putting nice audio on - the little camera is choking at the rail. So were my ears to be honest! Lol

Please enjoy! Stay well and safe, and get vaccinated if you haven't yet because the shows are starting up!
