Radiohead - After The Head Rush EP

This is a little compilation of Neil Young songs covered and performed by Radiohead between 2001 and 2003.  I think they make a good compilation.  They are based around the best available sources.  Some, in the case of Down By The River are pretty rare outside of the 'regulars' collection.

Track listing:

1. Cinnamon Girl - 2001/06/30 Santa Barbara Bowl, Santa Barbara, USA 
2. Down By The River - 2003/07/07 Bergamo, Lazzaretto soundcheck
3. After The Gold Rush - 2002/10/26 Thom Yorke, Bridge School Benefit, Shoreline Amphitheater Mountain View, CA 
4. On The Beach - 2003/12/22 BBC London Studios
