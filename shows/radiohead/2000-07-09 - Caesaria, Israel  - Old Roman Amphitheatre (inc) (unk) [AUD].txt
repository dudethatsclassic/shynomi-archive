Uploader notes: I had this recording as an mp3 for a long time, and have never seen lossless until this magic drive arrived. As with the rest of those filesets - no source/lineage information is provided, except it matches exactly the mp3s I had. While overall, the quality was sort of OK, something crazy happened to D2, maybe it was a loose cable - who knows... but the volume dropped, there was skipping and phasing and what not. So a friend of mine kindly 'repaired' this D2 files to fix phasing and EQ/volume it to bring the levels to D1.
Perhaps the fact that the last 2 tracks from the main set are missing is related to that hypothetical loose cable issue. One more thing: Setlist FM claims Optimistic was played as track 2 - it was not in this files and I question whether it was actually played - because - who knows? Lol

ALL IN ALL - this is a new uncirculated show, the recording is not great, but not terrible either, and it was specifically requested by several people. ALSO, this is the first public premiere of Fog :)
