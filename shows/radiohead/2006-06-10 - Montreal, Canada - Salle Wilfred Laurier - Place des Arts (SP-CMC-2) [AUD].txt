Very little security on this night, had intended to hide most of my rig, but in the end I just walked in with it all in my jacket pockets. Security seemed to be more interested in people bringing their own water bottles in. God forbid that some of us wish to quench our thirst without paying $5! 

This recording is dedicated to the usher who stopped and told me that I could not take photos on night II. When I asked her of the rest of the cameras walking by us she called in her boss who promptly looked at my SD550 and warned me to put it in my bag and not take it out, for the security at the front had been instructed to confiscate all cameras. Well as soon as Thom began playing YAWA, cameras were flashing away in the entire front row, and I was standing there like an idiot. I was perfectly positioned front row with Ed directly ahead of me! Oh well.

Also dedicated to the usher who tried to chase me when I too-casually dis-assembled my rig at the end of the show. Nice try.

Sorry for the clapping at some points, (15 Step, KID A) I just got excited. Come on it's Radiohead!!! Don't think I can tape them in future too into the concert!
