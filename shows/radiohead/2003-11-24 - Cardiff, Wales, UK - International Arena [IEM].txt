Mastered in Adobe Audition 3.0, Har-Bal 2.0 and Izotope Ozone 3.0
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Additional info: My friend was recording this with my equipment whilst I tried to do my first IEM recording. I was not successful. I picked up one of the radio mics used on the drums during There There and recorded that for most of the gig. My friend had battery problems during Just so I patched it with my "IEM" recording. During the encore I realised my mistake and retuned to Ed's IEM. I have added them as bonus tracks. The quality is bad because I had the radio set to narrow band FM. I subsequently learned to switch it to wideband FM for a fuller sound.
