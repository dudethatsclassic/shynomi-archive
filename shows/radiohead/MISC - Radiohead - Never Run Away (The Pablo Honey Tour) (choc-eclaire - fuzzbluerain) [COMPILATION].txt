**************************************
*             RADIOHEAD              *
*           Never Run Away           *
*       (the Pablo Honey tour)       *
**************************************

So long ago now...with Pablo Honey reaching its 30th birthday in February 2023, it's an opportune
time to revisit the time around an often-maligned debut album. Yes, this *is* the one with 'Creep'
on it, an angst-ridden bite of grunge-influenced rock that still serves as the band's biggest hit.
But the other eleven songs on the album offer tantalising hints of the band that Radiohead would
become - Thom Yorke's awe-inspiring voice, squealing and noisy guitar solos from the trio with the
instrument, a willingness to shift into different moods. The band may often dismiss and try to
shrug off the record, but there are *worse* albums out there, and who doesn't cringe at the awkward
teenage photos that Pablo Honey effectively is?

And, for a band who had one album with a standout single, the period is surprisingly well
documented. At the start of 1993 the band had barely left England to perform; by year's end they'd
toured both North America and Europe multiple times and even made it to the Middle East. Sometimes
headlining their own shows, sometimes playing early on festival lineups, and sometimes opening for
fondly remembered bands such as James and Belly, it was a frenetic year for a band that had to find
*any* way to stop boring themselves silly with the same setlist. With the exception of 'I Can't',
which had fallen out of the set by the time the band reached a major label, every song off Pablo
Honey was played time and time again, with another album worth of B-sides and new songs being
rotated in and out - and, believe me, these songs are blessed by the live setting, Radiohead being
almost a punk band at times.

In celebration of Pablo Honey, which is still worth the listen, this is a 2xCD compilation of every
song Radiohead performed in 1992/3, arranged into two semi-coherent sets - difficult considering
the small range! Excellent performances, usually with some astonishing vocal or guitar work, were
prioritised over sound quality, leaving to a few rather dreary examples here, but the hope is that
this is still a fun listen for where the band started out.

Keep everything in its right place,
-choc e-Claire / fuzzbluerain, February 2023
