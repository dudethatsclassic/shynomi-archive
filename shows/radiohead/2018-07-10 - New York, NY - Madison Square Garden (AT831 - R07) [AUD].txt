Immeasurable thanks go to Inez for the ticket help and Josh for the accommodations and hospitality. Without the assistance these two provided, this recording would not have materialized. 

For 2018, this was a fairly interesting show, albeit rather slow in pace. Half of Kid A was played (*without* ether TNA or EIIRP which are easily 2 of the 3 most played from that album). The intensity picked up gradually towards the end but really just left me wanting more. Good thing it was just night number one…
