UPDATED NOTES FOR TEN YEAR ANNIVERSARY RELEASE (8/25/18)

Taper's stories are always fun and seem to be enjoyed by most. Here's one for posterity. In 2008, a buddy and I wanted to see the Radiohead gigs at the Hollywood Bowl. We had no tickets. Even asking on the boards (as a taper) for an extended period prior proved to be fruitless. So I asked my friend who was working at The Bowl at the time if she may have had a solution. After a couple of days, she got back to me and told me she was 90% sure she could walk us into the venue both nights. Down side is that we wouldn't have tickets/seats/space to call our own. If you're familiar with the Hollywood Bowl, you know that this presents a problem. The entire lower half of the venue is 4-6 seat "boxes" and each area is well guarded by over zealous ushers, each of which encountered seems more protective of their section than the one previously passed. MEANING: Moving DOWN from the shitty seats up above is very difficult. Thankfully, I knew this from previous visits to the bowl and acted accordingly. My friend and I knew we would likely not be able to hang together for the show unless we went all the way to the VERY top of the bowl/lawn section. We figured we would try our luck down low for as long as possible since we were walked in behind the stage and "dropped off" on the lower / garden box level. I had some great results taping Radiohead there in 2003 from a centered Terrace Box so that was where I set my sights. Miraculously, I made it through night 1 without having to move at all and pulled a stunning recording from literally my choice of boxes/seats. At the end of the night, I had the presence of mind to think towards the next gig and found a ticket stub from a kind neighbor. This proved to be quite the smooth move as we arrived a bit later night two, yet the stub enabled me to move freely past all ushers - directly to the same area I was in the night prior. After finding a similarly positioned box, I made friends with a Father and Daughter and gave them CD's of the previous night's show. They agreed to share their space with me unless it became a problem. When the other two people arrived, they came guided by an usher, 3 songs into the set. I was clearly the odd man that didn't belong in the box. Again, the stub from the night before proved golden (now that I think about that, these stubs were actually printed on golden/yellow ticket stock!) as the usher didn't boot me from the section (nor did she notice the date on the ticket was wrong) but she wanted me to go to the correct box. So she chased me out and after a brief lap of the boxes in my section, I was back where I started...and I was able to remain there for the duration of the gig. I was and am still grateful to Daniel and his Daughter for sharing their space with me and giving me their second stub that night. Rustic smartphone pics from taper's position are attached! 


Special thanks to M for "assistance with entry" and David for not caring whether we watched the shows together or not.
