This is the best show I have attended in the last decade. Actually every radiohead gig I have seen since 1997 has been a
kind of special, but this one was perfect in every sense: outdoor venue, nice and warm weather, perfect acoustics, great
audience, perfect band, incredible setlist, excellent recording. What more would you expect from a show? For this one I was
borrowed a portable DAT recorder so I did not have to care about changing disk. During the first number I realised that
the recording level was far too high and this resulted in about a minute of saturation on one channel. I could not repair
it properly, so I pasted the good one which results in a kind of mono sound, but it is not disturbing. The rest of the show 
was crystal clear with the correct level. The band was on fire! Here it is my DIME friends, I hope you have appreciated this 
new series of 20 masters. I will see(d) you in 2009. Happy New Year!
