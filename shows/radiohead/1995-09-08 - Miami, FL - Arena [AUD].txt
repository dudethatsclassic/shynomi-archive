Radiohead 1995-09-08 Arena Miami FL (Floridian) (T-fix)

The raw source recording for this release is from the quy who recorded it, Chuck Dagen (Floridian).  He asked me to work on it and fix any problems that I found.  He provided it to me as 2 flac files.

flac > 16 bit, 44.1 kHz WAV >
Audacity > CD Wave Editor 16 bit 44.1 kHz > FLAC

I converted to wav, equalized, balanced, and optimized the volume of the recording.  I removed a few gaps.  Then I split the recording into song tracks.   I did the editing, mastering (tweaking) for this version of this release.
Screen caps of Audacity before (raw) and after (final) are included.
