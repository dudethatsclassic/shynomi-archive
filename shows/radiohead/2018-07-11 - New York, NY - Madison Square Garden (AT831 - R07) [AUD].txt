** editor’s comments: Here comes another gem for you folks! Wonderful show and a beautiful tape to go with it. The gig was great, with about a 3rd of a setlist changing completely from n1. The sound is fantastic - I barely touched it - a couple of screams and some overly enthusiastic clapper had to go ;)
At my end, this was another 24-hour queue cycle with 2 hrs of sleep stuck somewhere in there, another camping with homeless people by Penn Station getting to know Radiohead fans close and personal, another fine, truly outstanding care and attention from MSG staff headed by a wonderful little lady named Jeannie - complete with donuts and water served to the queue at 9AM sharp… - and despite the level of exhaustion from sleep deprivation and stress, I was a happy camper (quite literally!) as it was THE ONLY night of the MSG run when I did NOT have to procure 3-level ticket retrieval and exchanges ‘the day of’ lol So what’s there not to love? ENJOY another fine record :) A pic of the setlist and ticket included **
