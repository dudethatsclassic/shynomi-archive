RADIOHEAD
Movistar Arena
Madrid, Spain
November 5th, 2025 (2025-11-05)

Taped by elly
Source: zoom am7 mic > S22 16 bit 48 kHz 
Location: 2nd row seats, centre side. 
Mastering: Flying Horseman (Izotope/Audition/Waves)
Editing: inez - de-clap, de-whistle and de-wo-hooo (IzotopeRx4) - tracking (Audacity 3.7.7) > 16 bit 44.1 kHz


Returning after 7 years of solo and side projects, this was the 2nd night of Radiohead's Europe 2025 tour. Some lovely folk in GA started up a round of Happy Birthday for Jonny in the encore before Arpeggi, which Ed and then the rest of the band joined in on. 

Editor notes: massive applause and thanks to Elly - the new generation (quite literally) of tapers who started during this tour and wouldn't stop! :-) We will hear a lot more from her I'm sure! 
The show is a historic presentation of 'setlist B' (if we go with this nomenclature for this particular Radiohead tour) - so half of the songs are different from the night prior. Enjoy :-)
