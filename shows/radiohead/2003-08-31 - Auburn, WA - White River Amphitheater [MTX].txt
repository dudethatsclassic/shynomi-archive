Editing and mastering notes*
A lot of editing was done on this recording. The majority of editing was just lowering crowd noise before and after songs. Even with a team of people blocking the rig there were some very loud clappers close to the mics. Some claps were lowered in volume up to 7db. A few quiet songs were  also normalized. There was no EQing, filters, or effects used. Crowd noise was lowered on a song by song basis so you can hear a slight volume shift between tracks, however this does not effect any of the music just the crowd noise.
