Tape story:
The venue is an open-air concert area in Parco Nord (a park in Bologna suburbs where also the Estragon, an indoor concert venue, is located) 
I put myself 20 m in front of the left speaker and 25 m from the stage center
The capture of the sound is very good but the recording is affected by diffuse singing, screaming, clapping and chatting (luckily not all tracks)
Light wind during There There @ 0:11-0:13 and heavy bass saturation during Feral @ 2:37-2:46

What about the show? 
It was my second Radiohead show (the other was in Milan 2008, also taped)
Extremely good show with a setlist mainly based on The King Of Limbs (7 tracks + The Daily Mail) plus 3 tracks off any other album (except 1 track from The Bends and no one from Pablo Honey) and True Love Waits as EIIRP intro
Enjoyable break in the middle of Exit Music (For A Film)
