Radio interview with Ed O'Brien of Radiohead.

Ed talks to George Stroumboulopoulos about their new album " In Rainbows".

The title comes from a comment made by Geroge during the broadcast that he'll be talking to " Ed From The Head".

Apparently the members of Radiohead shy away from the media, and doing interviews, so this is a rare opportunity to hear the thoughts of one of the members of the group.

Unfortunately, I'm missing a bit of the interview.

I don't know how I was able to miss a chunk of the interview, I didn't turn off my stereo or audio recording program, but after I finished recording the show, I found a chunk of the interview was missing.

My deepest apologies, I do hope people will enjoy this despite it being incomplete, maybe someone has a complete interview and is willing to share it with everyone. 

What I dedcided to do with this interview is offer people here two choices. 

One folder consists of consists of a "Condensed" version of the interview, and the other folder is the interview that has the remaining tracks that are apart of the recording. 

I personally don't find them to be totally essential to the listening experience, but by adding them on you'll have a somewhat complete version of the interview, and all that I have to offer the community.

This " Condensed Interview" is missing just two tracks of the interview, I've labeled which tracks were missing from the " condensed interview" for everyone, along with the differences in times between the two versions.

The removal of these tracks was nescessary to create a consistant flow in the recording.
The tracks were also removed due to the fact that the fourth interview is incomplete, and the tenth interview is a small fragment before going into a commercial break.

But, as a collector, I do understand the value in having complete recordings, so I wanted to be able to appease the collectors who value completeness, and the ones that value the smooth flow of a recording.

Some light hiss removal was applied, some light pop/click was added to " Interview Four" ( I've done as much as I could to buffer them out), and fade outs have also been applied to the recording.

During the interview, George played some officially released Radiohead material for his listeners, and honoured a request from Ed to play Feist's " My Moon, My Man", ( very cool that he's developed a taste for Feist)

The officially released material played during the broadcast " Bodysnatchers", " Paranoid Android", and " My Moon, My Man" has been removed to comply with Dime's uploading regulations and policies.

What's been uploaded here is strictly the radio interview. 

Out of all the radio/television personalities on the air today, George is stellar at what he does. 
I've been listening to him since his days at 102.1 FM " The Edge", so listen to " The Strombo Show", and watch him on " The Hour".

Cheers
