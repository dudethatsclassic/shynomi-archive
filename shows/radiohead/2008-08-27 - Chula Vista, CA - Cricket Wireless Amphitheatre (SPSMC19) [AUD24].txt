Radiohead
Cricket Wireless [formerly Coors Amp.] San Diego, CA, USA
August 27, 2008

Audience recording.
Stage left, second row, terrace

SP-CMC-19 > SP-SPSB-1 [no roll-off], Edirol R-09 (48/24), Adobe Audition 1.5 (normal and fades ONLY)

Nice show which with good levels. The talkers were there, but mustly the crowd was quiet.
I was lucky to be standing behind a kid who wasn't interested in the concert at all and just kept quiet while playing with his i-phone.  We all have our priorities.

I left track 01. [intro] long, is I think this buffer music set the tone of the concert nicely.  

Also it was kinda humourous watching Thom crack himself up during "You and Whose Army."

For those of you who are burning this to cdr, put 14 tracks per disc, and you won't need any fades.
