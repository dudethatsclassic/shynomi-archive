Keep In Contact With Old Friends is presented in honor of the 10th anniversary of OK Computer's
release.  whether he remembers or not, the concept for this was originally suggested by keir.
The title comes from "Fitter Happier" which was also suggested by keir.

"Live To The Nation" was edited, authored and shared a few years ago.  video is ripped from 
the original dvd files.  apologies to the purists who prefer not to see re-encoding.  doing
this without re-ripping the VHS sources and re-editing the entirety was not schedulable and
a tribute at this time is appropriate.  :)

Live To The Nation is the tenspot promo with a tripod shot fan cam edited in because the
promo has many titles, and drops out video completely along with smaller portions of audio, 
when MTV cut to commercials during the broadcast of this show.  the promo was blended with
the fan-cam to  make an uninterrupted video without titles and synched to the similarly
"corrected" audio source listed above.  the fan-cam has it's own merits and is used 
liberally throughout, not simply to lose logos or add video when none existed on the promo.

every track from OK Computer was played at this show.  with some track rearrangement, this
makes a complete, in order track for track, uninterrupted performance of OK Computer from
a single, excellent show.
