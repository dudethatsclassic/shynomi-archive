Notes: 
One of Jonny's keyboards malfunctions during Talk Show Host, which causes the band to abort Planet Telex, and to have to use the Rhodes keyboard for Exit Music, making the version of that song a unique rendition.  During Everything In Its Right Place, Thom jumps from the stage and runs down the center aisle, high-fiving the audience on his way out of the venue.  He returns for the encore. The band had the next day off in Barcelona, which Ed O'Brien described on his tour blog as having been spent "eating and drinking vast amounts, broken up by a two hour siesta...ideal....."

Recording sourced from Japanese bootleg CD on the No Surprises label.  Bootleg is on pressed CDs, but origin of recording unknown, considering there are noticeable edits between some tracks.
