Recorded from back of venue centre standing on the bar above level of crowd (hence photographer asking me to move
so he can take some pictures and various crowd members asking me to help them up). Thom Yorke used to study at Exeter University 3yr before. Hence the comments about all the things he has being doing "since I left" and songs dedicated to "all my friends who still live in Exeter". A very powerful set in a place that had an extreme
emotional attachment to Thom. Creep was written on the campus of this university. The Lemongrove is a small bar in the Student Union on campus.
