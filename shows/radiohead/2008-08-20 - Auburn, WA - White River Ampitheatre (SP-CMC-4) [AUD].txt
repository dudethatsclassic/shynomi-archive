Thank you to everybody who was on the floor that evening. 
This was by far the most kind and respectful group of fans that I have been a part
of at a show in a large venue. 
This was a very nice change of pace from most seattle-area concerts.
Everybody who was there wanted to be there and could not have been more pleasant.
And a special thanks to the boys in Radiohead for breaking out "In Limbo".
Amazing...no words, nor recordings of any quality can do this band's live act justice.
