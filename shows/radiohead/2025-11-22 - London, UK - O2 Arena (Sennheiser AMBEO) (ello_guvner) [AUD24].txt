Notes:

What an experience this was. I gave up trying for London, no code, and never fast enough to bag a resale. But then the production/WASTE tickets went on sale 2 days before this gig. I JUST about got these 2 for Saturday, after 2 error messages and no confirmation screen. I knew I was in for an absolute treat that weekend, and I wasn't wrong. I hope you enjoy this, especially to those who unfortunately were not able to attend.

I was looking forward to seeing a different setlist without knowing how different it will get exactly. Personally, I prefer the previous night, but there were still some excellent songs sprinkled in here. Was a bit surprised they ended with Everything in its Right Place though... Glad they dropped that after this one. 

Thanks to Inez for the lovely clean up job, getting rid of some particular claps, woos, whistles, etc. Also thanks to Vie for this lovely cover, based on the O2 photos from Alex Lake!

I also recorded this show on a Zoom H1n. A future release I hope, in case anyone prefers that, or even better: wants to take on a matrix ;)

More Radiohead shows to come...
