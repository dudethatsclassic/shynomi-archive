Notes : I had never seen Radiohead live before, I can die now! That was one of
        the greatest show ever! My recording quality is between very good and ok. The
        thing is that I forgot my mics' windscreens so there is some wind noises
        and some phasing problems. That doesn't bother the listening too much and
        this is a very good recording despite what I said before. I hope you
        will like it as much as I do. Enjoy!

P.S. I chose to call this recording "Fireworks" because during the show, the
Montreal International Fireworks Competition was having its finale at the same time. You
can hear this on the recording. I personnaly think that it makes the show really unique.
