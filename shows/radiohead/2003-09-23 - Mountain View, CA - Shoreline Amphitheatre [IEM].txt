Show Notes:
Fond memories of this show. My second Radiohead show. Wasn't able to hit soundcheck, but had decent seats and able to get all the taping stuff working right. I can't seem to remember what Thom almost played by request instead of Wold at the Door though.

Recording Notes:
I played with using either Wilson's KM140 or the Schoeps source. For mixing purposes, I liked the KM140 better. Sometimes a bit of phasing on the KM140 when turns head or something, but it's a great stand-alone recording. Wireless sources are a pair of mono feeds to accentuate clarity on the audience source.
