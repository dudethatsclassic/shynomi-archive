All thanks to the taper...

A Thom & Jonny acoustic show, this might well be a Pre-FM - it contains no DJ banter at all, but it does contain between song banter with the

audience.  Absolutely stunning quality, & a great performance to boot, this was recorded for KCRW 'Morning Becomes Eclectic'.  The main tracks 

are from the 1st set done that day, the two bonus tracks are from the 2nd set, recorded the same day.  There is an edited online version of 

this at KCRW's website, http://www.kcrw.com/music/shows/morning-becomes-eclectic/radiohead-1 (missing the false starts, several tracks, & 

it's not the same sequence as above), but this cd does appear to be a pre-fm, & the spectral analysis shows no lossiness when run through EAC or

Audacity.


EAC Log, ffp, md5 included

Don't turn this to mp3, decode them to wav in tlh & burn them instead...
