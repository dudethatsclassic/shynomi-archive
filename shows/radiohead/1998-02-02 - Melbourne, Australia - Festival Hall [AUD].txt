Notes: This recording is far from perfect.  It suffers from small pops throughout and frameloss between
a few tracks towards the end.  I was told about 3 years ago when I received this show that the taper was 
not interested in doing a new transfer, so what we have here is as good as it gets.

This is being shared because it's a 1998 recording that almost no one has or even knew existed. The 
choices were to either let it sit in my garage or share it with those who, despite the flaws, would want
to hear it.

If someone has the patience, the pops can be fixed in CEP or Adobe Audition via click/pop eliminator.


P.S. I won't hold my breath, but if anyone was a better copy of this show, or wishes to share a previously
uncirculated Radiohead recording, please do, especially if you download this one.
