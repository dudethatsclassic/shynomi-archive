MISC NOTES:
Absolutely great show on the worst day to be at the worst venue in the DC area.  there was about 2 inches of rainfall during the performance (luckily I was safe under the pavilion) and the temperature was down in the 40's with gusts of wind (which unfortunatly make it on to the recording at times).  Me and the friend I went with ended up completely soaking wet just by walking to the pavilion from the parking lot through the pouring rain.  We wore trashbags with head holes torn out of the bottom to help cut the wind.  All but one road in to the venue was closed due to flooding and it got so bad that police were turning away the late-arriving concert go'ers!  This is among the first times I'd taped at Nissan Pavilion, it's a great sounding place, but aside from it's sound, it adds absolutely nothing to the concert experiance (and in fact takes away from it in many ways).  Great show though, the band was incredable.  Aside from a couple of errors at the begining of "Faust Arp", they sounded absolutely perfect. Thom's voice is amazing and the lights they had on stage were marvelous.  All in all it was worth it, I hope you enjoy the show in the dry warmth of where ever you are listening to it!
