Notes: 

Another huge shout out and thanks to Froyd, the PhD concert taper who did a damn fine job of keeping big-mouths and loud drunks away from the taper.  Fantastic show with a "local" touch from the band......during the initial mass hysteria intro of "The National Anthem," you can hear a radio host say, "The Padres had the day off last night, gaining a half game.......", which they did.  (Monday night's intro was different).  Different setlist from Monday night, with some new songs added in, as well as a great version of "I Might Be Wrong."  Taped 50 ft. away from the right stacks, right of center stage.  About 7,000 strong in attendance, general admission on a large lawn with several thousand pushed up against the stage, everyone basically on top of each other.  A lot of crowd activity over the intro portion of the show, it was difficult to hear so I edited it out.  Included some San Diego crowd noise for the mavens out there.  Can't say enough about how great two nights in a row with Radiohead can be.  Hope you enjoy the show. 

Zepdog5
