RADIOHUB EXCLUSIVE 2018/09/02 - Please do not seed to DiME 

Immeasurable thanks go to Inez for the ticket help and Josh for the accommodations and hospitality. Without the assistance these two provided, this series of 2018 MSG recordings would not have materialized. 

ALSO - BIG THANKS to D for buying a stubhub ticket + agreeing to stick out another show with the taper, once again enduring all that comes with such an evening. 

I fumbled my battery box during the "smuggle my shit inside MSG" moment of entering the building and did not notice the that switches on one channel had been altered. Thankfully, I got the recording, and a clear one at that - in both channels. The switch fumble caused levels on the right to register considerably lower than the left but that was fixed via normalization in Audacity. 

I'm proud of this effort. Procuring GA floor for one night at MSG is not easy...four consecutive is worthy of a rousing and extended standing ovation. Again, Inez is largely, no, enormously responsible and to thank here. The level of consistency recording-wise (for the four night run) is something I had hoped for but again, knew would not be easy. I stood in the *exact* same space for each of the gigs and again, am very proud of the effort put forth by all involved to capture this as well with the results. 

Side note/fun fact: With this gig in the bag, I have successfully captured every NYC performance that Radiohead has given since and including the October 2003 Hail TO The Thief MSG shows.
