Prom 39/Greenwood, Stravinsky
2009-08-14

Broadcast Live from the Royal Albert Hall, London (BBC Radio 3)

A Prom featuring a work composed by Jonny Greenwood, a member of the rock bank Radiohead and the 
BBC composer-in-residence. Popcorn Superhet Receiver is a gritty work scored for strings, which 
was inspired by the medium of radio.

It is followed by the classically-poised Stravinsky ballet Apollo.
