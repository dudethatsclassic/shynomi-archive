NOTES:
I'll start by saying that the band performances utterly saved this festival; I saw some great music which is why I was there.  Other than that, the All Points West festival (the second day, at least) completely sucked.  Don't have nearly enough room here to dwell on the issues.  Unrelated to the failings of APW (but one of my gripes), I did just barely get my recording gear in, literally, by a pube.  The security guy patting me down on the way in got a little, ahem, how do I say this...  'overzealous' while fondling the items in my front pockets, narrowly missed my gear and grabbed my balls.  No joke.  A shout in his face brought him back to reality and he sent me on my way.  Whew!  Usually they buy me dinner and drinks first...

As for Radiohead's show, the weather was perfect, the night was beautiful and the band brought their A-game and killed.  Add to that a VERY cool stage setup with lights and video screens and it made for a great performance.  Was very happy to hear "The Bends" and especially "Planet Telex".  Personally, I hadn't heard them play either song live in a long time and I'm a huge fan of the older stuff.  Great crowd: enthusiastic and there for a good time.  For the most part, people weren't too chatty until the encores and then the sing-alongs began, so the recording came out fairly clean.  Thank goodness for cardioids and location!  My levels were a touch hot, so between the bass-bombs, handclaps and occasional gusts of wind the recording clips a few times (not too badly, IMO).  Awesome show, hope you enjoy what you hear as much as I did.

Big props to T.O. for getting the tickets and driving.  And to the security guy who frisked me: next time, squeeze your OWN nuts.
