Notes:

Sound is slightly distant/reverby due to my location up in the balcony.  Bloom cuts in because I
was one of the last people to be let inside.  It was a treat to attend this taping at the smallest
and most intimate venue the band is visiting during their 2012 US tour.  Songs both old and new
sounded fresh with new arrangements featuring second drummer Clive Deamer.

In the fall of 2012, video of this show will be broadcast on PBS stations around the country.
Respect is given to ACL TV and KLRU, which should receive your donations if you like this show.
This recording is not intended to compete with the professional recordings.
