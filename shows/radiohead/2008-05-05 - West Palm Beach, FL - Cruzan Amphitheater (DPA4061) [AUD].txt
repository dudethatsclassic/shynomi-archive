Source:    DPA 4061s > MMA-6000 > Edirol R-09 @ 24/48
           (around 20th row, sec 2, inside left stack)

Patch Src: JB's DPA 4060 > Edirol R-09 (sec 3)

Transfer:  R-09 > USB > Adobe Audition > dED > (dither
           and resample at highest setting) > CDWave > FLAC

Notes:     Beginning of Optimistic, most of Exit Music,
           and a sliver of Street Spirit are cut due
           to non-technical issues.
           These spots were patched using JB's source, 
           with permission from the taper.  Thanks JB!
           In addition to the patching, quite a bit of 
           digital editing was performed to soften loud
           clapping and screaming.  Also a hi-pass FFT 
           filter was used to cut echo bass below 35Hz.
