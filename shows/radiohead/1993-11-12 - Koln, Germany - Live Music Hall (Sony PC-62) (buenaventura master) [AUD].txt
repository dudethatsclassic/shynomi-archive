Notes from https://the-radiohead-almanac.fandom.com/wiki/1993-11-12,_Live_Music_Hall,_Cologne,_Germany:
"Sound quality here is quite decent. Quite a bit of crowd noise, but the instruments are mostly well defined. The Bends cuts in slightly, but it's very pleasant otherwise.

This is a very strong show from the James tour and is one that would be a good choice to get to see where Radiohead were at this point in 1993. The setlist is identical to the previous night, but the songs are played with passion and energy.

Furthering the 'great crowds make the band play better' hypothesis, the crowd is pretty ace here - despite them not even being here for Radiohead. They seem to recognise most of the songs (including Vegetable, which for some reason gets a big reaction), which marks a striking difference to the show the night before. This tape has a great 'live' atmosphere which adds to its appeal.

One highlight here is Prove Yourself, which is rarely a standout song on '93 shows. Thom's vocals are really gorgeous on this one - it's one of very few performances of this that can actually make me feel something.

Other assorted highlights include You, Lurgee and Pop Is Dead. Really, it's all a joy to hear."
