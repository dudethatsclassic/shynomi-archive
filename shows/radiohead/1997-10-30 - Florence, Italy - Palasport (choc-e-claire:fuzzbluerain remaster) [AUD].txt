Radiohead
Palasport
Florence, Italy
October 30, 1997

Remastered by choc e-Claire/fuzzbluerain in April 2022

One version of this show already circulates, an unknown cassette recording that's evidently been
going around for a while (it's a .shn fileset, and when was the last time anybody used one of
those?). Problem: it's *way* too fast, to the extent that it sounded ridiculous even to a naïve
ear like my own.
Fortunately, the taper - while they didn't leave a name or recording specs - included almost the
entirety of the Fitter Happier intro tape at the start of their recording. Because of this, it was
very easy to work out the ratio to decrease the speed of the show so that it was close to real time
- the answer is 0.913, so DarkAudacity was used to slow the speed by that much.
While I was there, I thought it would be worthwhile seeing if I could clean the sound up a bit, so
I gave it a bit of a light EQ - increasing the highs so that the drums were a bit more crisp, and
decreasing the mids to give Thom's vocals more room to breathe. It's still not a *great* tape, and
others could probably do a better job of remastering it (I've kept the original files safe, so if
you want to try send me a bell and I'll pass them on), but it's a much more enjoyable way to listen
to a good show.
