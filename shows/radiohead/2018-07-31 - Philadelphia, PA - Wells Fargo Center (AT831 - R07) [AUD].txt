** editor’s comments **
Hi all - so here’s one more Radiohead show! I was lucky enough (is this really luck?!) to see them 8 times in this leg and Oh MY GOD THIS SHOW. I cannot rave enough about it, about the setlist and the performance alike (DESPITE Jonny being sick…) It’s truly a gem. May have been the best Wolf I ever heard. IMBW and TSH were first unleashed in Toronto but this back-to-back assault was just something else entirely! TNA Hunting Bears outro bleeding into Idioteque… Decks Dark fully back on and Feral. Ahhh!
Edgar’s recording is stunning in my opinion… I truly wish things sounded like this at the rail :-) But one can’t have it all! Editing involved basically just axing an obsessive whistler, a few screams and …some guy truly needs to take clapping lessons lol Just buy a drum kit dammit :) So he had to go too lol
Much love here - enjoy!!

Taper says:

This was the best show of Radiohead's (out of six) that I attended/recorded in 2018. The band was a solid level tighter than two weeks prior in NYC. Additionally, the appearances of four songs (*and* a hunting bears outro) that were not performed over the stretch of four gigs at MSG, made this show particularly enjoyable for me. I was positioned approximately 25-35 feet closer to the stage (approximately 35-45 feet from the stage) for these shows than the MSG run. This was due to altered proximity of the FOH cage in Philly. To my ears, the results sound comparable to the MSG recordings yet more detailed. I hope you enjoy.
