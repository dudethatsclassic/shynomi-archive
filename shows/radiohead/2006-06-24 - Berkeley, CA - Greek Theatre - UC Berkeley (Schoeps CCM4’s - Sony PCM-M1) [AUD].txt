Editor’s comments:
Hi everyone!
So here’s a wonderful UNCIRCULATED source for you from a killer tour - pre-In Rainbows run of In Rainbows songs!
There was no need to mess with the sound here at all - I just cleaned-up audience outbursts and electronic few-second glitches of what seemed to be a loose connection in one of the mics (mostly at the beginning of the show).
The total time is ~2 min less than some other sources you may have because TheMan paused the recorder during the first encore break - I did not bother filling in 2 min of audience noise :) And I left the original separation into 2 CDs as it was 11 years ago.

Enjoy this great capture! A million thanks to the taper who never lets us down :) - we can celebrate the South American tour 2018 announcement - right?
