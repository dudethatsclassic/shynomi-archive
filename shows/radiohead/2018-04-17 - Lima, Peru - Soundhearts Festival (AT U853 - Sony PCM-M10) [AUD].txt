RadioheadApril 17, 2018 (2018-04-17)Soundhearts Festival, Estadio NacionalLima, PeruSource: SP-CMC-4U (AT U853) > SP-SPSB-11 (Bass rolloff at 69hz) > Sony PCM-M10 (24 bits/48 khz)Transfer: SDHC-Card > PC HDDLocation: 20 meters from stage center, dead center, hat-mounted micsTaper: Gustavo (Paranoidgcm/KidA22)
Editing audience noise/tracking/downsample (inez): Izotope Rx and Audacity 
Mastering (ZH): Waves plugin with Adobe Audition

editors comments:
I am so so thrilled to have been able to contribute to this effort! Huge thanks go to the taper - Paranoidgcm - who I believe was getting his rig together for the first time and seeking some advice on Radiohub to make sure this show is captured and captured well. These were amazing shows down in South America - and the first time ever that Radiohead played Peru — and I have to say - the crowd sounds amazing too! The most unexpectedly emotional moment to me while editing this was when after Street Spirit, the audience picked up the tune, and carried it for a while, even though it was clear the band was ready to jump into Arpeggi - and then someone (Jonny? not sure…) gave them this one more long Street Spirit’y guitar note and it blended into Arpeggi (it’s so very obvious on the screen!!! lol). Somehow this moment totally melted me down to tears :)
The tape worked out great! - I dealt with some overly enthusiastic and uninvited screams here and there… the usual clean-up. And thanks to ZH for additional touch-ups! I hope you all enjoy it!! - and thanks again to Paranoidgcm.
