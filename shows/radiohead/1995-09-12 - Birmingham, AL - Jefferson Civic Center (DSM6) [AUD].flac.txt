Although this show was seeded as 9/20/95, the set list is inconsistent with that date's known set lists. It appears instead to be 12 September 1995, and is apparently missing "You", supposedly performed after "Creep". 

Confirmed at: 

http://www.nepasavaler.net/gigs/index.php
http://www.btinternet.com/~r.j.arthur/rh/rhgigind.htm
http://old.58hours.com/58_displayshow.php?showID=322
http://www.greenplastic.com/gigography/showDetails.php?showID=377#setlist.

However --if ALL these sites used a common source for THEIR set lists, I theorize someone mis-labeled "Just" as "You" [because of the chorus: "You do it to yourself, you do." etc.] and someone else added the correct title in at a later date! Careful listening appears to conclude that this is a complete show with no edits, so . . .
