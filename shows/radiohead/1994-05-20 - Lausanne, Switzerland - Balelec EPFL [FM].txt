Switzerland


Classic21 FM, Belgium (March 5th, 2020) > yamaha rx667-flat lineout > Edirol R09HR 24.48 > normalized in Adobe Audition
Captured and processed by Flying Horseman
Tracked by inez (audacity and Isotope Rx)

What an incredible present amid the global clusterfuck we are living in! This show has never circulated in ANY shape or form - I would have not dreamed of pristine FM! And yet this is what Belgium Classic21 FM dug up! Many thanks to Flying Horseman for capturing this for us :) The FM announcer bits are very non-intrusive - maybe 3-4 in the entire tape and are all in between songs: I left them at the end of a song in each case, rather than the beginning of the next one, and I tracked intro and outdo separately even though there are only a few seconds long.

Enjoy some vintage Radiohead! :-)
