Recorded with internal mics handheld through show resulting in some handling noise (typically between songs) when people needed by or, in a few cases, when some cover from the rain was put over the mics. Great show with an overall great crowd. A few noises here and there but some great performances. I did compress and EQ to lower the applause and add some clairity to mids and highs but tried to avoid destroying the dynamics of the performance. Great show I hope you enjoy!
