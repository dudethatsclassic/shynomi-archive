Here’s our Paris night 2!
And surprises (no pun intended) continue! When I heard Climbing Up The Walls while watching a live periscope in my office, I screamed out loud and terrified my unsuspecting colleagues. Also, just as everyone started commenting how mellow the first encore was, they hit us with 2+2 - and we promptly shut up! :D There was an amazing moment when Thom stopped just as he was hitting that high note in Nude, got security because someone in the audience was fainting, and picked right up that amazing note!! Anyone remembers the moment when he stopped Myxomatosis in Toronto in 2003? yep. 

The show was kindly recorded by bnz who also put his best into selecting a good spot at the venue, BLG did some great and seriously time-consuming mastering to get overly active audience members under control (my goodness, french audiences can be loud!), and I’m just finishing up with tracking/tagging and organizing… Hope you like it!
