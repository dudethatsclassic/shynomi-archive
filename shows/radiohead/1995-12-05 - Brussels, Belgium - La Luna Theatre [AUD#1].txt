Notes:

Courtesy of the Stonecutter Archives is this Bends-era Radiohead show. Most of you probably have a video version of this, but this a straight-up first gen from a cassette master. Sound is a bitty tinny but clear. 

As most of you probably know, this show is best known for the nuggets that all coalesce around the first encore: the first ever performance of "True Love Waits" (the studio version of which wouldn't see the light of day until last year's "A Moon Shaped Pool), a rare performance of "Banana Co.," and a Carly Simon/007 theme song cover.

"Anyone Can Play Guitar" cuts in after tape flip.

It was reported Thom was not in a great mood because of the low turnout, which led him to say that the band prefers playing in the U.S.
