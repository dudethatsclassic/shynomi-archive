Radiohead
Dec 10, 1998
Amnesty International Concert
Palais Omnisports Paris Bercy, France

source: Amnesty International Bootleg CD > EAC > SHN

setlist:
01 lucky
02 karma police
03 exit music
04 talk show host
05 my iron lung
06  no surprises
07  fake plastic trees
08 bones
09 paranoid android
10 street spirit

notes: this concert was held to celebrate the 50th anniversary of the human rights declaration. radiohead was the headlining band at this festival. because of various delays radiohead started more than an hour later than planned. the band seemed to enjoy playing for this big crowd concisting of many different nationalities, although it seemed that thom was a bit annoyed with the people who started to clap along during talk show host and my iron lung. he answered the clapping of the audience by clapping his own hands provocatively during both songs. this had the opposite effect; now more people started clapping with enthousiasm! street spirit was announced by thom as follows: "i know it'll probably gonna sound really corny, but this one is for everyone being mistreated by their government. that's for human rights. if anybody knows what it is. let's hope you don't get tired of hearing about it."
