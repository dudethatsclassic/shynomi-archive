Apologies for the delay! There is travel… and… life… and… stuff. Besides, there was already one tape from this show circulating for us, the the hungry ones! Nonetheless, here’s our Paris night 1 which sounds quite different from either the original Antoine’s recording or its processed version seeded last night. So you can pick and choose or play them all!! :D
Since there is never enough! And never enough CREEP :) That was definitely a bomb! First time ever, to my knowledge, they closed with Pyramid Song (also tour debut) and they gave a 2nd go to Tinker Tailor after a relatively low-key trial run in the opening show in Amsterdam. Tour debut of timeless True Love Waits in its new rendition. And this is Phil Selway ‘happy birthday’ show which makes it even more ‘fucking special’.
As with our previous 3 tapes: our hero is bnz at the center of the floor, BLG mastered the recording, and I’m tracking it as we speak… Hope you like it!
