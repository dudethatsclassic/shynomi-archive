Source 1: SP-CMC-16 > SP-SPSB-10 > Sharp MT877 MD in LP2 Mode

Source 2: Popcorn Superheat Receiver > Sharp MT877 MD in SP Mono Mode

Had a bit of a nightmare with this one. Source 2 had a lot of interference and was largely  
unusable until The Gloaming. I probably spent too much time trying to make something  
listenable. Interference corrected using Auto Heal in Adobe Audition 3.0. For the most part  
it's ok but you may notice some artefacts in the mix. The gig is entirely listenable but put  
some of the more questionable stuff I have put as a bonus. For those songs with some drop outs  
in the matrix on the 'official' gig, I have added those as a single source bonus too. Basically, it's all here and it's all good. Fantastic version of Everything In Its Right Place. See photo for taping position.
