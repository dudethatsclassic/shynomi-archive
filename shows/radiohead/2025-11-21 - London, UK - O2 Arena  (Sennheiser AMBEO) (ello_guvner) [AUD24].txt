Notes: What an experience this was. This gig is up there with Black Sabbath for me this year. I gave up trying for London, no code, and never fast enough to bag a resale. But then the production/WASTE tickets went on sale 2 days before this gig. My brother scored these tickets, and I JUST about got 2 for Saturday (22nd) as well, after 2 error messages and no confirmation screen. I knew I was in for an absolute treat that weekend, and I wasn't wrong. I hope you enjoy this, especially to those who unfortunately were not able to attend.

If I had to give this show a title, it would be "Just Freezing Cold", based on what Thom says before the song, alongside the typical English weather that evening!

I also recorded this show on a Zoom H1n. A future release I hope, in case anyone prefers that, or even better: wants to take on a matrix ;)

More Radiohead shows to come...

Big thanks to Inez for cleaning up this recording!
