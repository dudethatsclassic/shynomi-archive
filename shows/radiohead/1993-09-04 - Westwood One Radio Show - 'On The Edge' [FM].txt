This is one of the first projects I ever seeded, and I'm bringing it back at Easytree.

Radiohead has several "Radio Sessions" bootlegs dedicated to them, with titles like "Radiotick Tracks" and "Unplugged." While most of these bootlegs do compile some incredibly hard-to-find versions of songs (some of the Radiotick track series has stuff I've never seen elsewhere), they're usually compiled from off-air sources, i.e. someone taping a song off of the radio.

A few years back, I managed to stumble upon a few WW1 CDs of the "On the Edge" radio show featuring Radiohead. The entire shows are not suitable for torrenting, as they include many commercially-released tracks, and are probably 50% interview. My first action upon receiving these discs was to pluck out the five otherwise-unavailable performances--the five tracks listed above--and compose something of a "fake" radio show, featuring bits and pieces of our obnoxious host interviewing the still-plucky members of the band. I compiled this for my own listening pleasure, but I think it actually works fairly well regardless.

(If there's demand, I'd be happy to torrent the complete, unedited-except-for-expunged-commercial-tracks shows)

The five tracks above occasionally show up on bootlegs--"Banana Co." shows up on Unplugged, I know--but they appear in studio-outtake quality here.

Enjoy
