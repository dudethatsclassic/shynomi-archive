Tape story:
I have located myself about 15 m in front of the right stack with the mic mounted on hat
I thought the location was optimal because the mic could take the sound quite directly from the monitor but I have unfortunately neglected the noisy (and likely drunk) crowd around
During the concert I have tried to move myself around maintaining the same distance from the right stack but I have never found a place with quiet audience
The result is a very good recording (imho) with regards to the quality of the sound, but severely affected by clapping, screaming and chatting people (sorry my fault)

What about the show?
A rainy day, but fortunately rain stopped just at the beginning of the show
It was my first Radiohead concert and I was impressed by the power, passion and "beauty" of the performance
Lights and backing videos fit appropriately with the show and the music 
Personal highlights: Lucky, Pyramid Song, WF/A, Optimistic, Reckoner, JFIP, CUTW (with Euro2008 live sample on Italian radio), Street Spirit and the whole E2

Technical notes:
