own enjoyment.  But it turned out so well that I thought it would be a waste to keep it to myself.

I guess the only way to find out if the tapers object is to throw it up and see if they say anything.
So if I used one of your tapes to make this and you don't want it out there, let me know and I'll take it down
with sincerest apologies.  I mean no offense at all.
