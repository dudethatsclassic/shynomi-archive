Editor/uploader comments:
I was going to release this over the weekend but then … maybe NOW is a good time?!

First of all - my enormous thanks go to the taper - Klingklang - who (hopefully temporarily!) left the taping (under)world… - The title as appears above should stay, please.
I also give big thanks to ZH for his mastering efforts - Shrine is NOT a great venue acoustically, and the audience was out of control pretty much everywhere in the venue. I also give big thanks to senecastj for introducing me to the basics of … Erm… ‘dealing’ with the audience: there were some out-of-control characters near the taper, and you don’t hear them anymore, Lol (no Radiohead fans were hurt in the process!). 
I am grateful to Radiohead for making it possible for me to catch this show which wrapped up a laughably short US summer leg of the 2016 tour with a 4 (!!) non-festival gigs total. 
It was an AMAZING gig, very special to a lot of people and the band was on fire - so I am really happy to get this recording out there. 

Enjoy!!
