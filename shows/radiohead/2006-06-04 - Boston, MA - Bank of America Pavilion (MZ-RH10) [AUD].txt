Radiohead
Sunday, June 4th, 2006
Bank of America Pavillion
Boston, Massachusetts

Taper’s Notes:
AUD > atrac3plus 256 kbps > WAV > FLAC (level 5)
floor, right, 3/4 of the way back from the stage
Equipment: MZ-RH10 (PCM), RS 33-3013 Mics, attenuator cable

It was my intention to record the entire show contiguously.  That is why atrac 256kbps was used instead of PCM.  No CD extraction or digital editing, other than starting by amplifying the whole show to -0.2 dB.
