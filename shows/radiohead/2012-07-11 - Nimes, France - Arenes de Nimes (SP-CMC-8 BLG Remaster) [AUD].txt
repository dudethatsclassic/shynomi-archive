Uploader’s notes:

This essentially unmodified (just tracked) recording was first seeded by the taper on TTD just a couple of weeks ago (April 2014). The taper encouraged someone to master the recording and indeed it needed a bit of work. I am very grateful to the taper for then forwarding to us the original raw untracked wav file, and I certainly thank BLG, as always, for working the tape up for us all :)

With respect to the show itself: this was the 2nd of the two Nimes shows that opened the delayed European leg of the TKOL tour after several June-July shows were cancelled following the tragic Toronto stage collapse and the untimely death of the Radiohead drum tech Scott Johnson on June 16 2012.
http://news.nationalpost.com/2012/06/17/radiohead-shattered-after-crew-member-dies-in-toronto-stage-collapse/
These Nimes shows were intensely emotional, perhaps not perfect technically, but unique in many ways. 
http://pitchfork.com/news/47131-radiohead-pay-tribute-to-drum-tech-scott-johnson/
I had many close friends attending these shows. There was lots of tears.
So I am thankful for another record of these powerful shows and hope you will appreciate it too. RIP Scott.

inez
