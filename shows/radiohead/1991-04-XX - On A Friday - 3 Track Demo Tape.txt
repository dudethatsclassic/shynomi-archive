RADIOHEAD
On A Friday demo 

3-track On A Friday demo tape, unknown generation

01. WHAT IS THAT YOU SAY

  Original release: N/A [only on On a Friday demo tape (April '91)]
  Other versions: N/A

"Feedback frenzy" from the On a Friday demo.
Jonny says recording it made him realize how much he wanted to work with Thom.


02. STOP WHISPERING

  Original release: Pablo Honey (Feb. 1993)
  Other versions: Demo version - On A Friday demo tape
    US Version - Stop Whispering single, Itch EP, video

Written as a tribute to the Pixies. Chris Sheldon remixed the song for a US release, featuring strings and a slower tempo - featured in the video (which is notable only for Thom's ridiculous hairstyle).

03. GIVE IT UP

  Original release: N/A [only on On a Friday demo tape (April '91)]
  Other versions: N/A

A very soulful early track from the On a Friday days. From the first On a Friday demo along with What is that You Say? and Stop Whispering which helped the band convince Chris Hufford and Bryce Edge to produce the Manic Hedgehog demo.
