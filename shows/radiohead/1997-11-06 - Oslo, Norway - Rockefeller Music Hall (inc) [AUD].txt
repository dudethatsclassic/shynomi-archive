Audience recording
Source/Taper unknown

Recieved the show in a tape trade many years ago.
Transferred from tape and tracked with Audacity.

This is another show that I didn't find at radiodb.freeddns.org.
Unfortunately this is only 2/3 of the show. It might be a
complete recording out there somewhere, but this is all that
circulated when I was trading tapes back in the day.
It's a good recording, but side B of my tape (starting
with 'No Surprises') has lower volume and more tape hiss.
I amplified the transfer of side B to about the same level as
side A with Audacity, but I didn't remove any of the hiss. I
tried, but it didn't turn out very good so I kept it the way it
was. Check out the samples.
