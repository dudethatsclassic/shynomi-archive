Taper’s notes (1/23):

While this was 6+ years ago, listening again recently brought a couple very distinct memories back. 1: our experience was 100 times better on this night than previously when whatever opening act that was came down to the west GA area for social hour after their set. 2: While I unfortunately did not capture this, I will NEVER forget walking out the main entrance of MSG with thousands of others while the slowly moving crowd repeatedly sang the refrain from Karma Police in unison. If you were there, you remember. Thanks again to Inez for the ticket help and being such an immense asset to our community. I feel incredibly fortunate to call her my friend.


Editor's notes (1/23):

I DO REMEMBER THIS SHOW SO WELL!! But as to the release, it's a funny story actually. Edgar asked me to send him the 'final released' copies of some of his tapes he was missing. And going through my records... basically, 'it came to our attention' that there WAS NO 'final released' copy of this show as we never released it! So I diligently went through some minor clean-up and here we are - to wrap dime-free-leach season with a bang!! The amazing Radiohead show, which was only the second AMSP gig in the US! ENJOY!!!
