Editor’s comments:
OK… just to say that the whole MSG experience for the two days that we literally lived there was one of the most intense and precious music-related experiences I have ever had. With all my friends, with the queues, lack of sleep, 90+F heat, endless lists and numbering (I must have held hands of 400 people in 2 days?!?!), the sweetest ultra-pro venue people with the little, rather senior, lady who has been running the action for MSG GA FOR YEARS (!!) who deserves the biggest fucking round of applause - for having us in perfect line, in the exact order in which we queued march to the rail for 2 nights in a row :) I mean, I am just so fucking happy that my home venue didn’t let us down…  

THE SHOW was astounding. It blew us all off our feet. I think my head exploded way before we got to Let Down first time in a decade. The band was ON FIRE. Thom looked so happy, and it was so very mutual…. Was just overwhelming. And so much love.

Now, I know that Edgar had a hell of a time dealing with a bunch of jackasses back in his taper spot. And I truly hope that Radiohead will NEVER have ‘what’s their name’ as an opener and if they do - there will be NO FUCKING GUESTS associated with that fine act. So you can thank them for the delay with this release :) But hey - noone died (even though it got close a couple of times Lol) and, in the end of the day, Edgar got an unbelievable pull, you will love it! - I just needed to eradicate those select few who shouldn’t have been there in the first place. So all thanks go to Edgar for capturing this and not going homicidal.
