Notes:

There are a few excellent recordings from this tour, but sadly this isn't one of them.
To be blunt, the sound was pretty weak in my section, far away from the stage in a
giant venue. I'm torrenting this because there doesn't seem to be an alternate source,
and it's intended for fans who attended the show. Security was heavy, and I set off a
walk-through metal detector twice and a wand three times, with gear that doesn't
usually get noticed.

Minor remastering was done to bring down some audience noise, and there has been
slight EQing, but I decided to preserve the original dynamic range of the recording
and leave any further adjustments to the listener's personal taste. A multiband
compressor could definitely help the recording sound fuller. Note that this show
sounds especially bad on computer speakers and headphones; real speakers are
recommended.


Performance notes:

This was the first performance of Glass Eyes in North America.
