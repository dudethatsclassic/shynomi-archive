This was night 2 of radiohead's two night set at the South Embarcadero 'Viejas Concerts at Bayside'.

The first song of the night (Airbag) fades in about 10 seconds after it started because the taper got there late. It's hard to run with 30 pounds of gold around your neck foo!

http://www.radiohead.com/

Basically the venue is on a grass field park next to a marina on the San Diego Bay. If you see a band you like playing this venue, you can actually see / hear the stage very well from just outside the park fence for free. Or paddle up a kayak or anchor your boat right off off the stage for a free waterfront show.
