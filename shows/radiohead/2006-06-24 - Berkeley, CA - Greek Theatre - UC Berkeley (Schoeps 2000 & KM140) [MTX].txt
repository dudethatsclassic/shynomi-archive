If you're going to destroy the sound of some ultra-spiffy high-end rigs by encoding and spreading them via poorly encoded mp3's, please keep them on your own computer and not dilute the tradepool. The man thanks you.

Ran some of the wave effects to balance things out, selectively adding in other sources when needed a little extra oomph. Only source currently available for the public on this mix is the (fantastic) KM140 source.

Should be a fun mix, good to listen to this a year after the show. Definately a top show of the tour, and blew away night one. One of the microphone sources had some occasional digital noise, I removed most of what I heard but might have missed a few.

If you have any comments/issues on the sound or project as a whole, feel free to email me.
