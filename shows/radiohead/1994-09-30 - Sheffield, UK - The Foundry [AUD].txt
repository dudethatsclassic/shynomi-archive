Notes: Tape flip after Pop Is Dead.

This one came in a fairly large trade I did in early 2003 with a taper from the UK named Gary.  He was a nice guy and had probably the largest Radiohead analog list I've seen before or since, including many shows I've never seen anywhere else.  He sent me this show on DAT, but since at the time I had no way of transferring it, I sent the tape to a friend who then sent it to someone else that was nice enough to do it.  This same person transferred a few others for me including 03/29/96, 08/16/97, and 06/21/00, all of which were shared at around the same time.
