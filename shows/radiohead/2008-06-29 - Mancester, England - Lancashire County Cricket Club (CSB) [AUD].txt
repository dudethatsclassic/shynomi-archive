
Radiohead 'Old Trafford' live in Manchester (29 June 08)

Jun 30th, 2008 by TonyT

This is my recording of Radiohead at old Trafford, stood 20 meters from speaker stack to right of stage.

This show in Manchester did have a surprise though, Lucky was played as a third encore, since the band had time before the 22:30 hr curfew. Thom: “We still have time left before we turn into pumpkins. Or mice or whatever the fuck it is.”
