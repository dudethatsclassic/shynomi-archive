Details:  Radiohead recorded @ The Hummingbird Centre, Toronto June 7th, 2006.  This show was 
	recorded from row CC (3rd row) in the Mezanine, dead centre.  Most people around me were 
	sitting down so it helps keep the audience noise to a minimum.  During the quieter songs
	you can hear people in the crowd yelling stuff, usually followed by a "shut up" from 
	someone else in the crowd.  I missed them the last time they were in town so I havn't seen 
	them since they played in Barrie a few years ago.  They were fantastic then and they still
	are.  The new material is pretty good and I look forward to hearing the studio versions
	whenever that may be.  The best part about seeing them in this great venue is that you get
	to hear all kinds of little things that are usually lost in the outdoor/larger venues.
	This is one of my 1st recordings using the Gmini 402 and so far I'm quite happy with it, 
	especially the transfer times to my computer!
