That was the first of the two shows I attended in the same venue, after 3 years I hadn't seen the band live. There was a kind
of magic on the air, at the marvellour outdoor stage in front of Villa Reale, just outside my hometown and a few meters from
the Formula 1 Racecource. No new album (yet) released, nobody had a clue of what to expect. And infact the band played half 
of the show with unreleased material, totally unknown to most of us. But I knew it was going to be something special. This
one is not on the tracker since a few years, so I took the masters and improved them a lot through equalization (some treble
were added) and balancing the overall volume. Now it sounds nearly perfect to my ears. 2000 was a special year for Radiohead
and here you can really feel the magic we felt that evening of June.
