ORIGINAL NOTES
==============
Main Stage show traded from a friend in Iceland who received this recording from someone at the Icelandic Radio Station. I doubt it was ever broadcast in full (or at all)

Incidentally, I was living in Denmark at the time and saw this show. Only a few songs, but the memories are still strong. Det var meget godt!

REMASTER
========
This remastered version does not include the backstage set that was originally distributed as part of the original seed.

Seeded FLAC > WAV > Adobe Audition > FLAC

Manual removal of any audible clicks and pops. Automatic phase correction. Pitch and timing correction. Custom EQ profile to fix mid-heavy sound
