Hello, this is a collection of masters I would like to seed here. I've been taping shows for more than 20 years
and have an awful lot of masters. Most of them are in the old cassette format, some others are on MiniDisc and
others are on CD. I've taped many shows of many artists over the years, so don't be surprised if you will find
many different artists taped! Some shows are already circulating, some others have not circulated through collectors
yet. But most of all, enjoy! They all come from my mastertapes!

This is the first of the two nights that Radiohead played in Monza, in the beautiful background of the Villa Reale, five
minutes walking from the Formula 1 ring. The band was i top form and changed slightly the set for the two nights.
Many new tracks were premiered during this tour, with the audience who did not really understand what the band was playing.
This is by far one of the best shows I've attended in the last five years and I hope you will like the energy of the
performance!

I taped this one with a Sony DAT recorder that a friend used to borrow me sometimes, then transferred directly to cd, 
transferred to my hard disk, FLAC-ed for your benefit. maybe one day I'll seed the night of the 19th, which i had taped as
well! Be patient! This version has circulated very seldom through collectors, I must have traded it with a couple of 
guys back then.

Sound rate: 9,0 / 10 - it sounds good with no hiss, sometimes a bit distant but in overall the sound is quite good and
clear (I'm picky in my judgement!)
