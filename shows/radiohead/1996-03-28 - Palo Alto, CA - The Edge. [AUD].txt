Transfered from cassette to audacity. A lot of processing was then applied to make this show presentable. 

Quality note: tracks 1 to 10 were presented with huge amounts of tape hiss and rumble, and the higher end frequencies were unbearably loud (many screaming girls within close proximity of the mic). I have done my best to reduce these issues; perhaps someone else can take another stab at it if they want. 
Tracks 11 to 21 had the same problem and audio was in the left channel only. It was also noticeably quieter. I have done my best to ensure that the two halves sound as consistent as possible, but you will notice a difference.
