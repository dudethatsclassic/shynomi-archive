Notes:

*  = Originally Recorded By The Rolling Stones. 
Joined on stage by Emerson Hart (Tonic) & Matthew Scannell (Verical Horizon).

Verizon Horizon and Tonic opened the show. The crowd around me was pretty
tame for the most part and this turned out pretty good. The weather was as
perfect as one could ask for. When the show began it was sunny in the high
70s and by the time it ended we had dipped into the mid 60s. Not a cloud
in the sky! I wish all outdoor concerts were as perfect as this! The encore
was super special with all 3 lead singers from all 3 bands joining in on
the Stones cover. Definitely a highlight! I did cut 2 minutes from the
encore to remove various screams and claps. Jesse Valenzuela had a prior
commitment and could not make the show, so Donovan White (Vertical Horizon)
filled in on guitar. Im not sure how many dates Jesse is out for, but I
hope he returns soon! Donovan did a good job considering what little time
he had to learn a full 75 minute set!!
