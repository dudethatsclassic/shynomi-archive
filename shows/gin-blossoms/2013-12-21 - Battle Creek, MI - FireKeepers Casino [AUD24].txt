Notes:

*  = Originally Recorded By Charlie Robison.

This was held in the FireKeepers Casino Events Center. I originally had seat 9
but when no one took seat 3 (that was in line with the stack) I moved down a
minute or so into "Don't Change" and stayed there for the entire set. The sound 
turned out pretty good, however, you will hear some crackling from the drum kit. 
A good example of this would be the beginning of "I'm Ready". This was the way it
sounded, not much I could do about that. Once you can get past that, it's a good
listen. Robin mentioned this was their first show after having 3 weeks off. They
were very happy to be back on stage and there is some great banter between songs.
