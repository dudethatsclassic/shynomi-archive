As 2017 was coming to a close, The Gin Blossoms added a month of shows to
celebrate the 25th anniversary of their seminal debut record, "New Miserable
Experience" (1992). These dates were mainly east & west coast with a few
scattered in between. Sadly, I could not make any of these living in the
midwest. Finally, they added a final 2 weeks in February and one was in
Detroit. This was well worth the 3 and a half hour haul from Ohio. Their
setlists have grown stale over the past 5 years, so it was extremely
refreshing to hear so many songs that have been ignored for far too long.
I will continue to see and support the Gin Blossoms, but I do not see them
ever surpassing this particular show in my eyes. This is a must download!
