Taper's Notes:
This was a pretty relaxed show. It seems they played most of the songs a
little slower than on the albums. I think it's because it's only the seventh
stop on the Major Lodge Victory tour and they were still getting warmed up.
They had minimal equipment on stage and there was a little bit of trouble
with Robin's vocal setup, note the 2 bursts of feedback during Allison Road
and the quiet parts during the bridge of Come On Hard. Opening acts were
Josh Kelley and Shawn Mullins. I tried to make this all fit on one disc but
even if I were to cut out the encore it still would clock in at 81:24. So I left it
just as it was recorded, with the disc swap done after Hey Jealousy. They
left the stage at this point for a short break and only about 15 seconds of
crowd noise was lost. Note the killer extended version of Hands Are Tied.
Bad ass! Robin introduces himself as Biff DelMonte:D Cover art is included.
Enjoy.
