Gin Blossoms
Central Park Summerstage
Rumsey Playfield
New York, NY
8/15/2026

Neumann TLM170 (hypercardoids, center, just in front of FOH mix position) -> Sound Devices 722 (@24/192)

Mastering:  Adobe Audition 2026 for volume normalization, light 30 band EQ (to boost the bass frequencies a touch), track markers and manual cleanup of several dozen instances nearby "loud over the head clapper" guy right in front my mics using tightly targeted "auto heal".

Note that it takes the FOH engineer until the end of the first song to really get things dialed in.  (And that I had to EQ the first song differently than the rest of the show due to this.  My EQ for the rest of the show gives some extra oomph to the low end, which he mostly EQ'd out of the mix.)

Part of a triple bill with Gin Blossoms and Blues Traveler.  Spin Doctors played first, then Gin Blossoms, then Blues Traveler.  Robin joined Blues Traveler for their encore.
