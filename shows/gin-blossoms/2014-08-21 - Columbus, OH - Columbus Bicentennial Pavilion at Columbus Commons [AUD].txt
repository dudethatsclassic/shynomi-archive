Notes:

*  = Originally Recorded By The Plimsouls.

This was a free show and was very well attended given the wet weather
Columbus had been having all day. We got very lucky and had a dry window 
between 7 and midnight. The show was great, my only complaint being half 
the audience chose to socialize with eachother instead of paying attention 
to the band. I also had a clapper the first half of the set behind me
and to my left. Trust me, I wish I could go back and smack him -- but it 
could have been a lot worse. Enjoy!
