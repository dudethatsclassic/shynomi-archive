Taper's Notes:
This was a free show generously presented by Orlando's light rock
station 98.9 WMMO. Fleetwood Mac's Lindsey Buckingham was the
headline and the Gin Blossoms were the support. They put on a
full-energy show with the usual cell phone serenades, tambourine
tossing and Robin even jumped into the crowd during "Super Girl"
to walk on top of a long length of barrier going all the way to
the sound booth, stopping right in front of me to sing the break
"Are you ever lonely..." line. Sorry I didn't stay for Buckingham's set
but I give you this to enjoy. Cover art is included and some pics can
be found with google and maybe some videos on youtube.
As always, keep it free, don't sell, enjoy the show.
