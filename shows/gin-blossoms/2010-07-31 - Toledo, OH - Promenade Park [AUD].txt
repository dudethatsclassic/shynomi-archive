Notes:

*  = Originally Recorded By The Plimsouls.

Im only uploading this recording for the people who may want to re-live this show,
but to my ears the mix that night was extremely poor. It rained very hard between 
6 & 8pm (Gin Blossoms went on @845), so Im not sure if the condensation had 
anythingto do with the PA (or the boards, etc), but this show sounds bad. The 
guitars and vocals are running way too high in the mix and you cannot even hear 
Jesse's guitar for the most part. I made sure my levels on the Edirol were set 
properly, so this recording is not the result of my microphones or recording device. 

Enjoy, if you dare ...
