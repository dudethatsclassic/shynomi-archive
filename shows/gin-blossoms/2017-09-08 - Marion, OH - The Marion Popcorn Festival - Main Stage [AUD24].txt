This was a free show held in downtown Marion. I had some annoying "whoo'ing"
around me, but outside of that, its a nice listen. Gin Blossoms have a new
album already completed and ready for release in 2018. They performed 3 songs
from it here, "Angels Fly", "Heaven" & "Don't Let It Break My Heart (Be All
Right)". I really enjoyed the 2 covers at the encore, esp. "Tupelo Honey"!
If you listen to only one song from this, I would pick that one. Kind of a
shame to see them ignoring "Major Lodge Victory" as of late. C'mon guys! :/
Please share with other fans and crank it!
