Gin Blossoms were brought in to headline a free concert for Channel 12's "Downtown Goes Live" all-day event celebrating their move to a new location after 50 years at the old one. However after the Tucson tragedy it changed some and the Blossoms named their concert "Arizona Stands Together." Considering it was a free show with Ch 12 picking up the tab, I really didn't know what to expect, but was glad to see them perform a full set. 

my favorite part was the fact they not only played "Pieces of the Night," but gave a shout out to Doug Hopkins which I don't ever remember them doing on-stage since his death. 

The recording is a pretty solid, thick recording. The sound mix from the board was a little bass heavy at times, but was decent overall. I know at least part of this concert was broadcast live, but I recorded since I didn't know if they would cut songs or cut to commercial during them (they did) and plus I always find open air mic recordings more enjoyable than straight SB mixes with DVD and TV concerts and such.
