Third Eye Blind - 2016 04 05 @ UCCU Center, Orem, UT

Show  note:
Very energetic crowd sometimes bleeds into the recording, but it sounds like it was a good time.  Lots of tacks were segued into each other, so I tried to find the best place to split them.
