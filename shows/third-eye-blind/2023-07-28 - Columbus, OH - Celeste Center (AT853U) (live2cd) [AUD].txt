Notes:

Third Eye Blind announced a very limited string of dates for 2023 and The
Ohio State Fair was one! They were last here just one year ago, so it was
a bit surprising, but I usually go see these guys any time they are around
the area. They played DC the night prior at the brand new Atlantis and the
setlist was literally a dream for many fans. 28 songs, over 2 hours, playing
only a handful of radio singles. Feel free to look it up. Looking up the set
online, I had high hopes for Columbus. If this show was a fraction of what
they played in DC, it would be memorable. Sadly, this show was anything but.
This was the perfect example of a "phone it in" show you will ever see. It
felt like the band did not even want to be there, honestly. We did get some
banter from SJ, but it was very limited and the most time he spent talking
to us was during his acoustic strumming of "Shipboard Cook". He tried to get
everyone singing and no one would. The setlist was bland, stale - just not
very memorable for me at all. No rarities, nothing unique. The show felt
like the band going through the motions to get off the stage as quickly as
they could. Before I knew it, after 55 minutes, they were already off the
stage prepping the encore. The encore consisted of an 8 minute version of
"Shipboard Cook" (that should have been half that) and 2 of their biggest
songs to close out the night. Total set time clocked in at 75 minutes. The
absolute shortest headlining set they could probably get away with in the
contract with the State Fair. Thats just my guess, but Ive never seen an
artist play anything shorter having attended many there before. So, I sat
on this recording for some time as I really had no desire to go back and
listen to it. But for those who want to relive it - here you go! Hey,
at least the crowd participation during "Jumper" was spot on. Go Columbus!
You (unlike the band) showed up and gave it your all! By the way, where the
hell was "Motorcycle Drive By"? I want my $45 dollars back.

The Main Squeeze opened. [not recorded].
