Hadn't paid much attention to these guys over the years, at least not since their first two albums. At that time, it was hard not to pay attention. They were everywhere. There has been a lot of chatter about their sixth, and latest effort, "Screamer" that came out October 18, 2019ish. Didn't get a chance to check it out prior to this show, but look forward to doing so in the near future. It's impressive that Stephan Jenkins and whoever may be in the band these days (its changes numerous times over the years) still strives to remain relevant by putting out new music, rather than hitting the nostalgia circuit like many of their contemporaries. I enjoyed this show. Lots of energy, enthusiasm and good ol' Rock-n-Roll. As the saying goes, if you love what you do, it's not work (or something like that). It's pretty apparent Stephan Jenkins hasn't worked a day in his life. Enjoy this one!
