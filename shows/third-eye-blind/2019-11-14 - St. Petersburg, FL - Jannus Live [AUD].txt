Notes: 
Twas a very rainy evening in St. Pete. Outdoor venue. 
Finally stopped, for the rest of the night, right after the
Smallpools set. I had a great spot on the right side and
set my levels at the start of Thanks a Lot. I think the main
mix was slowly crept up song after song because by the
time I glanced at the R-09 toward the end of Anything, I
was steady peaking. So I dialed that portion down and did
a slight high end boost to make up for the mic being under
my shirt. I think it turned out well. Cover art is included in
the torrent and I encourage all to go see the show. Excellent!
