*  = Originally Recorded By Erasure.

Wheatus returned to Columbus and to the same venue they were last at 9 months ago.
The show last year was such a huge success, why not! This show was far and beyond
better. Performance and setlist wise! The entire set was by request minus 4 songs
the band threw in. I yelled for "Dissed Me" somewhere towards the end of the main
set and they encored with it. It was awesome getting to see 8 of 10 from the debut
played here. This was also the first time I had ever seen them perform "Punk Ass
Bitch" live, altho it has been changed to "Jackie Chan" since it was repurposed
for "The Jackie Chan Adventures" a few years after the initial release. I still
call it "PAB". Brendan's acoustic performance of "The Story Of William McGovern"
was another highlight after he explained why it was important to play. The perfect
request given the circumstances. The sound turned out better than last year, which
Im crediting to their new sound guy, Bill. The Basement usually sounds rough, but
this one is pretty great! I had a screamer to the right of the rig, but removed him
the best I could with RX7 in post. He's still there, but its not as intrusive.
