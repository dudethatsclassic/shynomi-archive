Wheatus' first return to Ohio in over 5 years. Thanks to TikTok, their 2000
song "Teenage Dirtbag" became a 2022 viral sensation boosting its streaming
numbers well into the millions. Thanks to this, they were able to plot a US
headline tour (something they havent done in many, many years) and bring
fellow hiphop artist FatTony with them. (He guested on the aforementioned
song, freestyling a verse). It was great getting to hear such a broad 
setlist, covering all eras of the band from pre-debut album ("Satan's 
Orders") to the current era and lineup. My only complaint? Only 3 songs 
from the debut in the main set. I consider that a bondafide classic, so 
hopefully next time, we'll get closer to 5 or more. "Leroy" was an audience 
request Brendan offered to play solo on the lip of the stage afterwards. 
Wheatus have procured a brand new drummer (as of August), so my thoughts
were he is still learning the material. Huge thanks to Brendan and the TM,
Shannon for the taping passes and free ticket. Please be aware during the
performance of "Rock 'n' Roll Damnation", an attendee passed out on the
mainfloor and security had to tend to them. This lasted for roughly 1:40.
