Notes:

* = Originally Recorded By Ed Bruce (partial).

Wheatus returned to Toledo just under a month after last appearing at
this very same venue opening for SR-71 (alongside Harvey Danger). This
time, they were the headliners and played a slightly longer set. Their
set in October was about 10minutes shorter. This ended up being quite
a memorable show in the band's history as they performed 4 brand new never
before recorded songs in "I Am What I Is", "Whole Amoeba", "William McGovern"
& "The Song That I Wrote When You Dissed Me". I had uploaded the recording
on napster and the band's official message board in 2001 and fans from all
over the world collected and traded the mp3s. As far as I know, this was 
their last US gig before heading to Australia and the UK as "Teenage Dirtbag"
eventually blew up the following year. Sadly, they never really returned
to US touring until 2004 or 2005 and by then the interest in them had waned.
I really dug their 2nd record, "Hands Over Your Loved Ones" (2003), but no
proper touring had ever taken place in the US.

In the time since, I have gotten to see Wheatus again (starting in 2017),
and Brendan actually remembered me and my recordings from 20 years ago.
Crazy, indeed! I thought I would upload this for the twentieth anniversary
of the album and tour. This was the third of 3 shows I caught that autumn. 

Technical notes: I did run this through RX 7 Audio Editor's de-click tool
removing some pops and clicks from the 2000 CD-R I had stored in a binder.
I extracted the tracks roughly 5 years ago and ran them through the tool
in 2020. I kept the EQ the same from 2000. It sounds fine the way it is.
