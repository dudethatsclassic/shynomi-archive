Such a treat getting Wheatus back in Columbus for the third year out of the
last 4! This was a band that didn't even tour the States 10 years ago! So,
I'll take it! I was 8 months getting this out, so for that, I apologize,
I said I would get to it and then just kind of forgot. BUT, better late
than never! Massive thanks to Brendan and the rest of the band for leaving
free passes with audio permission and always taking care of me. It really
is crazy to me I first saw this band 25 years ago this year and have known
them ever since! The last 3 Brendan solo songs don't sound the greatest as
they were off mic in front of a large crowd of people who gathered. I almost
didn't include them, but the solo version of "Real Girl" was one of my 
favorite moments of the night. Cheers to the guy who requested that one!
This was the final night of the spring tour and I believe it was the 8th
show in a row. They sounded amazing, considering.
