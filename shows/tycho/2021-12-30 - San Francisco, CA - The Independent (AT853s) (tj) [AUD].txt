Tycho's second night at The Independent as part of a four night residency with three full sets and one DJ set. I was supposed to see the Melvins perform but that show was cancelled so I decided to go back again seeing how much fun I had the first night. Tycho played a slightly longer set, throwing in songs like "Cascade", "PBS", and "Sunrise Projector" as well as sharing a few stories throughout the night. All in all, a solid concert. 

This is my last recording and show of the year and it's been quite the ride. 2022 is set to have some bangers provided the country can pull it's shit together. Get vaccinated, get boosted, and support the artists!
