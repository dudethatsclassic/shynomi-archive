My first time back at The Independent this year (not that there's many opportunities left) to see Tycho, a Bay Area staple. The last time I saw him it was after two days of not sleeping and running an ultramarathon at Burning Man so it was really nice seeing him up close in such a nice venue. Tycho played a great set spanning a few of his albums and while I'm not super familiar with his work it seemed like he had a good mix of songs. This is night 1 of a four night residency at the Independent where he's playing three full sets and a DJ set on NYE. I grabbed the setlist afterwards and included it and while I tried my best to split the tracks correctly I think some of the songs that bleed into each other might've been able to have been cut better. Enjoy!
