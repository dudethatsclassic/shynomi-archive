Show Notes: 
This show is a real treat, and might have been torrented by Geordy on DIME years ago, but never on TTD. He stated that this recording, once dialed in, blew the ALD from this show away.

I decided to spring for the 'base VIP' package for this, and was rewarded with some nice 3rd row ducats on Cantrell's side. There was a 6-8 foot deep pit in front of the front row, so the included pictures look more like I was 5-6 rows back.

The real treat for this one, other than the sound quality, is that Shooter Jennings showed up (he opened) onstage to jam on "Down In A Hole" with the band. I got a couple cool pics of that as well.

I taped 8 or 9 AIC shows on this tour, and this one far and away has the best sound quality. It's meant to be cranked.
