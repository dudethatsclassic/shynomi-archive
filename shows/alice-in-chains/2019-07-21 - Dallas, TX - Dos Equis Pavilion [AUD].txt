Alice In Chains was the 3rd band to play. They were the opening band for KORN. Opening for AIC was Ho99o9 and Underoath. I recorded this from the lawn section and was about halfway up the hill in the center of the stage. So as usual for the lawn section, there were lots of people talking and having a good time. The vocals start out quiet and then improve some as the set goes on. I would call this an average audience recording with a pretty standard setlist for AIC with no surprises. Jerry's Dad was brought on stage and introduced after they played Would? and before Rooster which is about him.
