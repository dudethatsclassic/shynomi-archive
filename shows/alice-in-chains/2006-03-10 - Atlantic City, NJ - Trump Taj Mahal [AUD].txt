Taper's Notes: 

Overall, this is an Excellent Audience recording, but there are several minor 
glitches that do not take away from the overall quality of the recording. 
First, I was still setting up when AIC came out to open the show and I missed the first
few seconds of Angry Chair. Second, my recorder was accidently stopped/paused for 
a few seconds in tracks 1, 2, and 8. In track #2 there are 2 loud sound spikes
in the recording that were part of the show and not the recorder's fault. In the 
intro by Nancy on track 7, the mics must have been slipped out of the recorder slightly and 
only the left track came out, so I doubled it to fill the right track. Halfway 
through track 8, the audio level was accidently lowered and was raised +3db on both
the left and right channels in audacity to even out the track. It was difficult to 
estimate the recording levels, and at times the audio is slightly clipped.
