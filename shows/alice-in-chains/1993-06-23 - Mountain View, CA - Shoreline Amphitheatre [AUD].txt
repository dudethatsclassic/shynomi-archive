Tapers Notes:

My 2nd Lollapalooza in 4 days, and the only 'mission' I remember was to not miss Tool...and I didn't. also had more understanding friends so Primus was taped as well (actually, all but the openers Rage were taped, including Front 242 and Dinosaur Jr...)  the main memory I have from this show was a 4000 person pit erupting on the lawn, to the tune of lighting large piles of waxy paper cups on fire!  multiple piles, like 20+. a great time was had by all, though the first 20 minutes is a bit rough, due to being in the aforementioned pit. big thanks to bubba420 for the upload, and you never know what you may hear next.
