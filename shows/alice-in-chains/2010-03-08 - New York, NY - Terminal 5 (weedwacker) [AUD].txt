Notes:
Excellent sounding recording. I'd rate it up there with the best pulls so far for this tour that are circulating. This show captures the recently reformed AIC touring in support of their Black Gives Way To Blue release; their first formal studio album in 14 years. This was a shit hot show and the crowd was racous, rowdy and rebellious like a good rock crowd should be. It clearly energized the band and they put forth and brutal and inspired performance this night. William DuVall was also a revelation, he may sound eerily like Layne Staley but he sure as hell can hold his own on the classics without sounding like some lame copy of the original.
