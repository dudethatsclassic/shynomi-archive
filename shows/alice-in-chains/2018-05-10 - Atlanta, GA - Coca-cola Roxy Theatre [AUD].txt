Show Notes:
Where to begin, what to say...???

Firstly, THANK YOU DENNIS. (y'all *could* follow my lead on that one HINT HINT)

You took this EPIC (15 shows taped in 15 days, nearly 30 total bands if not more, if you count the openers) trip, put it on your back, and got it to the masses faster than I can usually get my "morning constitutional" out of the way (even with the aid of coffee).

Even if this camper had the 'know how' to build torrents (though that's coming, promise, as I'm sitting on WAY too much tripod-shot audio to let it rot),  there is no WAY I'd have had the time do to the rapid turnarounds that you offer up.

So, again, for that, I truly do thank you.

Secondly, THANK YOU MASSES.

If not for you folks, and your colorful comments, I'd be wondering why the fuck I do what I do...

And thirdly (and most important, lastly), thank you those who use a mic stand.

Why, you ask?

Because it motivates me to show how wrong of a choice you make (unless you're lazy or crippled) to obtain your captures.

Besides the simple fact that 90% of those who use stands tape noodle-nuts rock (think Phish/DMB), it also limits the taper to less that 1% of the available real estate for taping.

And 9 times out of 10, where the stands are placed is faaaaar from any sort of sweet spot...and positioning is really where the knowledge of a taper lies...

So, to get to the meat of the matter, this was the 'cherry on top' of an over 4000 mile, 10 states driven in (GA, AL, MS, LA, TN, NC, SC, VA, and soon IL and IN) trip.

And what a show it was.

Starbenders and Walking Papers (Duff's band, but he was a no-show) opened (both taped, yes, they're coming...), but the crowd was there for one band, and one band only, the mighty Alice In Chains.

And what a SOUND they had (check the samples)...not sure if the first encore song was 'new' or not, but it sure didn't sound familiar.

The intensity just kept building and building, and by the end of "Rooster", you couldn't cut the excited tension with a butter knife.

Also, Atlanta is William's hometown (the "A-T-L"), and you could tell from the way he was whipping out the licks that he was grateful to be home (mentioned by him more than once).

Also, don't miss Jerry's story about the guitar he played "Rooster" on at the show (given to him by Eddie Van Halen in '91, then stolen in '01).

Anyhow, it is time to rest thy weary head before flying to Chicago tomorrow afternoon, so in that spirit, give ME your best "fuck Hagar", so that tomorrow there will be food and gruel for the likes of moi.

There are a LOT of cool things in the works before years end...but this trip is basically done (though if you are a die-hard AIC fan, stay tuned for another stellar capture in about a week...).


Going to bed with a shit-eating grin....

ballsdeep is OVER, and ballsdeep is OUT!!!!!
