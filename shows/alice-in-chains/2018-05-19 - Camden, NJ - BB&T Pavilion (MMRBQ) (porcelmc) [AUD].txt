Notes:

This was a radio festival show and the band was really tight during this gig. Quite surprisingly a mosh pit broke out and I had to move during Dam that River. This gave me the chance to lower the levels and get things aces. While this location might be classed as an "outdoor" venue, its like being in a barn with the back door open. Not really a fan of this venue, and that's a rarity for me. 16oz beer at $13.75 as well. Major rip off folks, thanks for the price gouge.
