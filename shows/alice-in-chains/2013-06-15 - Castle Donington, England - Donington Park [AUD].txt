Recorded by me on the second day of the Download Festival.

I positioned underneath one of the speaker stacks that was alongside the sound and lighting tower in the venue.

This was my first time seeing Alice in Chains live and its a pity that it was ruined somewhat by the weather which
throughout the day was crap, windy and raining. This resulted in some phasing at times and also some noise from the wind and
rain. Thats the main reason why I haven't got round to doing anything with this recording til now as its not one of my best.
Arguably I would say this is for completists only.

But despite all that heres the recording, theres a sample in the first post for you to make your mind up if you feel its worth downloading
or not, so be sure to check it out before downloading. 

I hope you enjoy the recording, but please respect my wishes by not converting or distributing in a lossy format.
