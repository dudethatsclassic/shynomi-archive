*Doors were at 7:00. Sponge went on at 8:00.  Alice in Chains went on at 9:00 and were done by 10:45, 
on a Friday night!  When we drove past the venue at 7:25, the line was all the way around the corner 
and down at least a block.  Went to Mexicantown for dinner instead of standing in the street for an 
hour.  When we got done, it was WRIF that told us Alice in Chains were playing an EARLY show tonight
and were taking the stage at 9:00.  It was 8:30 but we got there with just a few minutes to spare.
Way to go Fillmore.  Both the Fillmore Theater and Alice in Chains websites need better info.  Neither
mentioned Sponge was opening or that this was an early show. 

Great job by Alice in Chains.  What a solid band.  The crowd really got into it and sang along to 
a lot of the songs.  Man in the Box has the best audience parts.  I liked the new songs better live.
I think seeing them incorporated into the show with older material solidified it for me. 

This is the 3rd of the 3 recent Alice in Chains concerts in Detroit I recorded.  I also got audio and
video of the Comerica Park/Kid Rock show and partial video of the 9/17/09 St. Andrews Hall show.
