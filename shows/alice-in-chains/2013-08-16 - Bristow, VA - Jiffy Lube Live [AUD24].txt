MISC NOTES:
This venue has a capacity of 25,000 and the show was tragically undersold.  Several weeks after tickets went on sale I scooped a single lawn ticket for $15 after fees.  The day of the show, having sold less than 20% of their capacity, the venue decided to close the lawn and instead give all of the lawn patrons tickets to sit in the pavilion (the pavilion has a 10,000 person capacity).  It was a nice gesture which everyone seemed to really enjoy, but I ended up moving from the less than ideal far-house-left seat they gave me to a dead-center spot in the only row directly behind FOH.  Luckily no one ever came to challenge my seat assignment, so I rolled tape from that spot for the entire night.

While moving everyone into the pavilion certainly helped the sound quite a bit, the fact remains that a pavilion with a solid concrete floor and a solid metal roof will echo when it is less than half full.  The sound got a little better as the pavilion slowly filled in for Jane's Addiction and Alice in Chains, but it is still a little rougher than I would like.  Further, each of the four bands played very short sets (the longest being a whopping 63min).  All the same, for a $15 ticket, I must say I'm pretty satisfied with the experience overall.  I captured each of the four headlining bands, they were (in order of apperance): Circa Survive, Coheed and Cambria, Jane's Addiction, and Alice in Chains.  I hope you enjoy the recording!
