notes from taper: Recorded from upper level in the back, but still really nice sound.  Recording this show was a surprise, as from the location, you'd have assumed it'd be a mere document vs. how it actually sounds. the location was 3/4 of the way back, up high, but Thomas Wolfe either has super acoustics, or the soundman is a miracle worker, as it has a nice, rich sound. unfortunately, I was unable to escape the "one screamer" who seemed to follow me to many of the shows (SeattleI, the Atlanta gigs and Chattanooga excluded), he's in the left channel, but overall AIC was louder than he was. "Rotten Apple" was a great treat too.
