From the collection of koondog a/k/a Rush-Fan

Transferred & mastered by }{eywood
Lineage: TDK SA90 cassette (2) > JVC TD-W354 playback deck > Adobe Audition 1.5 (recording to .wav @ 24/96, EQ, peak limiting, splits & fades) > Xrecode II (conversion to 16/44.1) > TLH (sector alignment, flac 8)


When I got this tape it was a 180 degrees out of phase mono recording, so anyone listening to it was getting robbed.  After inverting a channel a very nice quality show revealed itself, close and punchy and powerful.
