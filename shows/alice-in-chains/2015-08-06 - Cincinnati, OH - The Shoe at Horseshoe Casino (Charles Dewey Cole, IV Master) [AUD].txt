*  = tease, Originally recorded by Van Halen.

Alice In Chains returned to The Shoe at Horseshoe Casino in Cincinnati for 
the 2nd straight year. Last year was a very chilly May evening and tonight was
pretty much the same -- in August! Temps were in the low to mid 60s with light 
rain falling throughout the first half of the set. "Rain When I Die" was 
indeed a must. Crowd around me was decent, a few screamers between songs, but
nothing too out of the ordinary. The band's performance was excellent as they
are now approaching the end of their brief summer tour. Listen for the brief
Sabbath tease before "We Die Young"...!
