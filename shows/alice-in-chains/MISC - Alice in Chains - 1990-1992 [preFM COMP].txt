Remaster notes:
This is a complilation of pFM radio tracks of AIC in their prime.  
After comparison of several sources, including "Happier than Snot" and "Seattle Connection Pt1"
and the Westwood One cue sheets, I think these are the most correct dates.  
Several songs are played at both gigs, I picked my favorite ones from each.

Audio quality-wise, the folks at Westwood One did a nice job.  
I beefed up the low end on the 1990 set.  Normalized, light eq.
Edited out lots of crowd noise loops and dj talking.
Also a nice bonus, on these discs they did not edit out profanity on 
the 1991 set, mostly on Real Thing.
The FM version clearly edits out Layne's F-bomb, but here we have the.. real thing.
Profanity edits are still present on the 1990 set.
