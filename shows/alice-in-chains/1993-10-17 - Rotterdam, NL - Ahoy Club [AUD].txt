Transferred & mastered by }{eywood
Lineage: Maxell SXII 90 (1) > Optimus SCT-57 playback deck > Adobe Audition 1.5 (recording to .wav @ 24/96, speed correction, NR, EQ, peak limiting, splits & fades) > Xrecode II (conversion to 16/44.1) > TLH (sector alignment, flac 8)

The only flaw in this tape was it was recorded quiet, and therefore had a lot of hiss.  This was fixed as much as possible, but it still has more than the normal amount of hiss.  Any more attenuation left serious noise reduction artifacts.
