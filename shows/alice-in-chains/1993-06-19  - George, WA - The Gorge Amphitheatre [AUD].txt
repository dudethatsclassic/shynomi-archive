Tapers Notes:

this was a pretty amazing show....Alice In Chains "hometown" show, at the Gorge. 170 miles from Seattle. my *only* time going to the Gorge...and they held the show in the fucking parking lot. so I never even got to go 'into' the amphitheater. that said, I'll just let the music do the talking. I taped parts of other bands, but was here for AIC only (Primus was not taped). had a LOT of requests for this one. lots of moshing, but I kept the disruptions to taping to a minimum. big thanks to bubba420 for the upload, and you never know what might be uploaded next!
