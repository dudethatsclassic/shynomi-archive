Tapers Notes:

this was another 'odd' pairing show, which had Circus Of Power (got about 10m taped) and Masters Of Reality (complete) opening for the infamous Alice In Chains. on the UCLA campus. a buddy had seen Lenny Kravitz with Blind Melon there a couple months back, and said when Lenny came out it was like "1964 Beatles in America" with the screaming girls; this show, however, didn't have that kind of an audience ;) . my only time catching them headlining with Layne (though I've taped the newer version 10+ times, as I really like Williams guitar playing in addition to his voice), and they did not disappoint. still pretty amazed that the little D-3 with supplied mic could do such a solid recording.  the first song fades in somewhat as I was in the line for the bathroom when they hit the stage....anyhow, hopefully you folks dig it, I think I have one more Layne-era show in the vault".....  big thanks to bubba420 for the up, and, yes, there's more to come. much, much more....  ---ballsdeep OUT!
