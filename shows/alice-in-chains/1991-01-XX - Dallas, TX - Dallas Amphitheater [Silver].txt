It has been brought to my attention that Queen of The Rodeo is on the official Alice In Chains "Live" cd. The offending track has been removed 

At the moment, I believe this to be the November 5, 1990 at the Marquee in Dallas.

I have seemingly found this with another couple of Alice in Chains concerts, where they were attributed to the wrong date and venue.

This appears to be the same concert as a different download that I have for that other date, though the songs splits are at different points. For example, listen to the end of the "The Real Thing." Layne mentions Dallas and being on the radio with Z-Rock, etc. After hearing it, I immediately recognized it. The same is true with a couple of other similarities.

There are a couple of un-sourced Google hits that attribute the November 5 show being the Z-Rock show.

I think the song "Sea of Sorrow" is extraneous, and from another performance?
