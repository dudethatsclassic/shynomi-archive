Show Notes: 

This was Alice in Chains' FIRST official headlining show of their 1992-93 DIRT world tour. What made this somewhat unique is that "Iron Gland" was NOT used in the intro. Layne is fairly talkative during the show, and includes a nice introduction to Would?

Recording Notes: 

The recording came to me untracked as 2 long wave files (sides A and B). I strongly believe this was a digital master (DAT), because there is no "tape flip" that interrupts a song, not to mention the clarity of the audio. Not sure what mics could have been used, but they were definitely a decent set of external mics or a good single-point stereo mic. 

I am seeding this version because it is the lowest generation recording to have surfaced in nearly 15 years. No DAT's have ever been known to circulate.
