|---------------------------------------SHOW-NOTES----------------------------------------|

First of all: this concert was sold out! I don't know how much in advance tickets sold out, but when I hit up the venue's website before leaving I was kind of surprised to learn there were no tickets left. I've seen other sold out shows at the Gasometer and from what I can tell there was enough room for at least 300-400 people. There's a huge balcony which was closed and even the standing area was not that much crowded. Anyway, no support band, which I would actually prefer to have more often to be honest. There's nothing more horrible than to endure one or even more shitty support bands. The last time I saw a show without support acts was at the first show ever in the newly build o2 Arena in Berlin- it was Metallica playing an exclusive show for all the metclub members.

The last time I saw Alice in Chains was almost three and a half years ago at NovaRock festival in June of 2006. So now about the Alice in Chains show that took place yesterday in Vienna. They had some cool production going on- for example that screen they've had instead of a backdrop and those 4 uber-expensive light machines on top of their amps and rigs. They had an animation of the Austrian flag shown on the big screen before the show started, which looked pretty cool I must say.

They came on approximately half an hour too late and then started of with the first 3 songs without any breaks in-between. The new stuff mixes very well with all their old stuff. Check my brain - WOW- I love that wall of guitars- very cool. But not only the sound was great on that one- the visuals on the screen and the lights turned that song into a beast.

A looking in view was also so much better live than on the actual album. I have never seen Alice in Chains with Layne, but I think William was en par with him, vocally and energetically. You could tell that the man was in a very good mood. Actually the entire band gave me that impression. Vocally there was nothing more I could ask for- Jerry was great and I was very impressed with Mr DuVall's singing. It was also cool to see who sings which parts in those new songs, because on the album I could not tell everytime if it was 100% William or 100% Jerry singing.

After a short introduction the show within the show began- as they like to call it. They played some of the more stripped down/acoustic stuff and the crowd really liked it. My personal highlight was probably the title song of the new album Black gives way to blue- what a killer live song. Goosebumps? Oh hell yeah!

Acoustic stuff ended with some technical difficulties on Jerry's end. No biggy, because William managed to get the crowd going, even when there was no show happening on stage at that moment. Acid bubble, the fifth and last song of the new album and what a great one. All the new songs came over much better live than on the actual album. I don't know why, but I dig those new songs live much more than on CD.

After a pretty long and very loud encore break they came back with two classics which left the crowd mesmerized. They could have easily played a second encore to make the show last two full hours, but man I can't complain and have to say that those 108 minutes were epic enough.

Enjoy!
