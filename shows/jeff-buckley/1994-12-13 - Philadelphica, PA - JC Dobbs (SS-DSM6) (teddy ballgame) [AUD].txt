Nearly 50 dates in, the Fall 1994 Tour is winding down as the band heads north, back to NYC, stopping first at the late, lamented JC Dobbs, a significant stop for many bands from its 1975 opening through 1996 when it was renamed the Pontiac Bar & Grill. It has opened and closed several times since then, most recently as Dobbs on South Street. 

Local taping legend teddy ballgame was on hand to provide us with this excellent recording. The set list doesn't depart from the usual 1994 sets - Chocolate is moved from its usual place as the intro to "Mojo Pin", and instead leads into Kanga-Roo, and there is no "Hallelujah".
