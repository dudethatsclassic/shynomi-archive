The European leg of The Mystery White Tour is winding down as the boys move on from Rotterdam to  Brussels, where they played the historic Botanique on Rue Royale, which is happily still welcoming punters to shows: https://botanique.be/en. In fact this show is still in their advertising archive: https://botanique.be/en/concert/jeff-buckley-1995

This is a fairly good audience recording of a solid show. The set list doesn't deviate too much from the 'usual suspects' but as usual each performance is unique. In tonight's show, "Lilac Wine" takes a strange turn ending with some vocal gymnastics, "Kick Out The Jams" is back in the set, where it will generally precede "Eternal Life" in its full 'Road Version' thunder, and the regular set ends with a brilliant "Lover . . ."

Jeff returns for his usual solo encore, but before "Hallelujah", we get an unexpected treat, the Judy Garland torch song showpiece, "The Man That Got Away" - one of only five known performances, then it's back to Leonard Cohen, followed by traditional show closer of "Vancouver/Kanga-Roo"

This is the final recording from the continental portion of the tour - we return to the UK next for the final two recordings from the Tour.

I received this on a trade CDr many years ago. Apart from some housekeeping, I present it to you as-is. Despite being tracked for two CDs, it will play seamlessly throughout. I hope you enjoy.
