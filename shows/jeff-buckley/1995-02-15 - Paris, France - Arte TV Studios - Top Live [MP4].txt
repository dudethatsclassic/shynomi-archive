AS noted on the previous upload, from Strasbourg, the band returned to Paris for a radio/TV session at Arte TV. The band recorded two sessions at Europe 1. First, a radio session for the Top Live Radio Show, then a filmed session for Arte TV's "Top Live" programme, which included interview segments intercut between songs. The TV session was partially re-broadcast later on Paris Premiere's "Velvet Jungle" programme, hence the continued confusion over the name.

This was extracted from a compilation trade DVDr, which also included material that cannot be shared here in compliance with Dime policy. I converted to mp4 and joined the files in LosslessCut. No other changes or re-encoding have taken place.

This is a terrific show, and it really gives you an idea of how far Jeff had progressed in the little over two and a half years since Gods & Monsters. He is confident, poised and other-worldly in equal measure. The interview - also for French TV, but conducted in English, with German subtitles(!) indicates this version may be sourced from a later rebroadcast on the continent. In case you ever wondered who Jeff's role models were? Well: you'll have to watch and see won't you!
