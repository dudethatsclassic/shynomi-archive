I've had this show in a number of forms over the years. According to Flowers in Time, there are two circulating sources. This one is labeled as SBD, but I tend to think this is another Music Faucet broadcast, as Jeff's performances were frequently broadcast on the station that adopted him early, WFMU-FM at Upsala College in E Orange.

Between April and August 1992, Jeff played 18 documented shows (and probably more impromptu ones) all over NYC, as well as traveling to Dublin, where he played a memorable set at Trinity College, for which no recording exists, as far as I know.

This is the first recording we have of a "Jeff Buckley" concert proper. He is alone, performing both originals and covers. He is in great form, interacting with the audience in his own trademark style, and throwing in all sorts of left-field 'bits'. Just listen to "If You See Her. . .", after which Jeff manages to quote Jimi Hendrix & Sonic Youth in the same breath. This is the Jeff that visitors to Sin-é, the Cornelia Street Café, Tilt, or Skep would have seen, and what created his legend as a performer.

Lots of other firsts. Fans of "Eternal Life" will hear the earliest recording, a pre-"Road version" take that hews much closer to the Grace version. Likewise, the earliest "Young Lovers", the first known recording of "Hallelujah", "Calling You" - only performed five times to my knowledge, and the first "Corpus Christi Carol", which sadly ends in mid-sentence.
