Here is the video portion of Jeff's show at Lupo's. It's hand held and a bit jumpy, but it will give you a good sense of being in the crowd at one of Jeff's shows. This is one of the best-circulated video recordings of Jeff & band. I have six different versions, including compilations. The fan-created "CrimeCrow Productions" release is the source of many of these. However, this is the best available version, direct from the VHS master, and without the annoying out of azimuth ribbon at the bottom of the screen.

My version came from Buckley uber-fan eclect, and his notes follow. I present it to you as downloaded, apart from the conversion to mkv, for easier viewing.
