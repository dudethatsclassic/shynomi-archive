After playing the Reading Festival, the band headed to London, by way of Edinburgh and Birmingham. For US readers, this would be like going from Philadelphia to New York City, by way of Ottawa and Syracuse! And we don't have massive superhighways over here!

Grace had been out for two weeks when Jeff and co hit the stage at London's iconic Garage club, still entertaining the punters in Highbury today. The set is fairly typical for the tour: much of Grace is played, along with an extra Nina tune, culminating in a 26-minute Kanga-Roo. It's been speculated that the recording is missing an opening Mojo Pin, but I don't think so, as Jeff works a good bit of Chocolate (the improvised vocal intro with which he started preceding Mojo Pin on this tour) into the middle of Kanga-Roo. So I think What Will You Say probably begins the show.
