Once again, the label/estate have cherry-picked one track from show, rather than releasing the entire thing. To complete the set, add "Grace" [Track 3 of the bonus EP] from "Grace [OZ Tour edition]" (https://www.discogs.com/release/2262039-Jeff-Buckley-Grace) which has been removed in compliance with Dime policy.

From Wolverhampton, the band headed over to London, first making an appearance on MTV's "Most Wanted", a session which can be seen on the "Grace Around The World" CD/DVD (https://www.discogs.com/master/378293-Jeff-Buckley-Grace-Around-The-World). Then it was over to the Shepherds Bush Empire for the final show of the Mystery White Boy Euro-Japan Tour, a set which was taped for broadcast on syndicated radio, and for which we also have a more complete audience recording - this one. "Lover, You Should've Come Over" played on the FM broadcast, was not taped, apparently, and there are some minor artefacts & drop-outs, but none that spoil the show. The stage patter is very quiet, however, and this recording would benefit from a good clean-up.

At the start of "What Would You Say", Chris Dowd tells the audience, "This is my best friend in the whole world, and I love him so much. And I hope you will understand his gift". What more can you say than that.

This show was re-seeded to Dime by Buckley super-fan Buda (you still out there, buddy?) in 2008. I'll let him take it from here.

Buda's re-seed notes
--------------------
This source has not been altered or changed in any way since I acquired it in 2008. There is some considerable hiss throughout, most probably due to the fact that it comes from a high generation copy. [ED NOTE: It's really not that bad at all] Otherwise it's a good audience recording. Transition between disc I and II is seamless without any fades. There is a cut due to tape flip between "Kick Out The Jams" and "Lilac Wine" but apparently no music is lost. Also, there's another cut before the first encore and the taper must have moved closer as the rest of the recording starting with "Vancouver" sounds slightly better, more upfront.
 
After "Last Goodbye", Jeff invites his [quite drunk! - ED] friend Chris Dowd from Fishbone to perform "What Will You Say" together on vocals, pulling off a "unique" version of the song. "Eternal Life" is dedicated to the 'kinky cam girl' and introduced as a love song. "Kick Out The Jams" is seemingly played faster than usually. "Lilac Wine" is greatly appreciated by the crowd, receiving a thunderous applause after its 8 minute long performance. The audience gradually becomes highly attentive and when "Mojo Pin" continues, one could hear a pin drop in the venue.

"Hallelujah" is dedicated "for Richard and all the men in the audience." Returning to the stage for the first encore in the midst of heavy applause, Jeff says "Thanks a lot! This so means a lot to us! A lot, a whole-lot. Such a fucking big place! (laughs)""
