From Denmark, it was over to Belgium. The Torhout Festival was, for many years, twinned with its sister Festival in Werchter, but ceased operating in 1999, the Werchter site continuing to this day as Rock Werchter. This is a very abbreviated set - only about 35 minutes of music, due to Jeff's commitment to play Elvis Costello's Meltdown Festival that night in London, before returning to Belgium for Werchter the next day! I've included an interview from Belgian radio from the following afternoon, which circulates with the Torhaut set, for some reason, so I have left it here.

This is an average audience recording from a windy day, so it's "swooshy". It was not burned DAO, so there were 2-second gaps between the tracks when I received it, which I edited out with Audacity. Since I was already doing that, I re-tracked it as well.  Apart from that, nothing was changed: the sound is decent, if unspectacular. I received this as a trade CDr, and originally uploaded this to Dime in 2006, but it has long since fallen off the tracker.
