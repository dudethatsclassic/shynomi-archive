Once again, the label/radio station have cherry-picked one track from show, rather than releasing the entire thing. To complete the set, add "Last Goodbye" [Track 10] from "Just Passin' Thru (https://www.discogs.com/release/1050756-Various-Just-Passin-Thru) which has been removed in compliance with Dime policy.


The Spring leg of the MWB tour having concluded at the beginning of the month, the band tied up a couple of loose end recording obligations, first at the Columbia Records Radio Hour, now officially released here: https://www.discogs.com/release/14072009-Jeff-Buckley-Columbia-Records-Radio-Hour then ignoring the sage advice of Michael Stipe, he went back to Rockville, to the studios of WHFS, for a taping of their flagship "Just Passin' Thru" programme.

It's a short but sweet set, made even shorter by the removal of "Last Goodbye". For once, the interviewer actually asks interesting questions, and we get three lovely acoustic performances.

I originally posted a raw version of this back in 2009. Our pal goody was kind enough to apply his magic wand and make this a superior listening experience. My thanks, as always, to him for all he does. Apart from some housekeeping, I present this to you as-is.

I checked the very end of 01 and there's no remnant of the beginning of the word at the beginning of 02.  Nothin' I could do about sloppy editing at the radio station...  ;-)  But if anybody wanted to leave that first track 01 off their burned audio disc, that's how they cut it at the station at some point.  The rest of it flows like those pesky bumpers were never there.  They may have sentimental value to somebody or other, but others of a majority may want them left out.  Can't blame 'em.
