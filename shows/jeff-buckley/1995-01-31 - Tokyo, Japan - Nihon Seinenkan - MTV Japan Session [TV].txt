On Jeff's third day in Japan, the band visited the Nihon Seinenkan in Tokyo for a concert that was filmed, at least in part, by MTV Japan. The partial recording, as broadcast, along with that of the 30th, was bootlegged on the 2-CD "Live In Japan" release, and is likely the source of this file set. It's got a lot of 'swoosh' indicating possibly an original TV or radio source.

I received this as part of a compilation trade CDr many years ago. To complete the set, add "Hallelujah" [Track 5] from the CD "Grace Around The World" (https://www.discogs.com/release/2562392-Jeff-Buckley-Grace-Around-The-World) which has been removed in compliance with Dime policy.

We can safely assume that Sony has all the concerts excerpted on that release in its vaults, and frankly it is beyond my comprehension that they have not released them all in their entirety, either individually or as a boxed set. I assume we have the estate to thank for that. In the meantime, I'll continue to present as much of these concerts as permissible.
