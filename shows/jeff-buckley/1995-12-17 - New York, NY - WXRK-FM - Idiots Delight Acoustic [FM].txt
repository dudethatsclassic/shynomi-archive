Once again, the label/estate have cherry-picked tracks from a show, rather than releasing the entire thing. To complete the set, add "Lover, You Should've Come Over" and "I Woke Up In A Strange Place" [Tracks 1 & 2 of the bonus EP] from "A Voice To Hold In The Dark" (https://www.discogs.com/release/4239093-Jeff-Buckley-A-Voice-To-Hold-In-The-Dark) which have been removed in compliance with Dime policy. 

Good luck with that, as it is only available an Italian book which has been out of print for more than 20 years. The other tracks are all officially released, either on "Grace" or the artists' original releases (Jordan, MC5 & Hawkins)

1995 is rapidly drawing to a close. The tour is over, and Jeff's commitments for the rest of the year are just a couple of New Year's Eve shows - a tradition for Jeff every year until he died. But before that, a long-overdue visit to WXRK-FM studios for an hour with another of my heroes, Vin Scelsa. Scelsa was my hero as a teenager, and who I wanted to be. He started on the station which kick-started Jeff's career, Upsala College's WFMU-FM, which he actually talks about in these interviews, and at various points in his career, worked at every significant NYC radio station: WBAI (who brought Dylan and Arlo Guthrie to the world), WLIR-FM, WFUV (Fordham's college station), WPLJ (formerly WBC-FM, home to Zacherle), and of course WNEW-FM, where he was a fixture until format changes sent him to WXRT.

Vinnie was a champion of Jeff's music and this is a terrific interview session. Jeff rooted through Vinnie's CD collection to select a dozen things to spin. They are all mentioned, so you can create your own "Jeff Likes" Spotify if you choose. Despite no music actually being performed (Jeff strums his guitar whilst Vinnie promos upcoming shows at the late, lamented Bottom Line); he also performs "Lover" and a nascent "I Woke Up In A Strange Place", which sadly had to be removed.

I hope you enjoy this window into Jeff's mind, and a trip through the mid-90s music scene in MYC.
