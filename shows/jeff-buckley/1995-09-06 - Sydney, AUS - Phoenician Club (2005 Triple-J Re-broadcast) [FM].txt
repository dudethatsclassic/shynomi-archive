Once again, the label/estate have cherry-picked one track from show, rather than releasing the entire thing. To complete the set, add "Last Goodbye" [Track 5] from the OZ CDS "Everybody Here Wants You" (https://www.discogs.com/release/521871-Jeff-Buckley-Everybody-Here-Wants-You) which has been removed in compliance with Dime policy.

After the hideous recording offered from Melbourne, I'm happy to offer this palate cleanser, as we arrive at the end of the 1995 Mystery White Boy Tour, with an incomplete FM broadcast from Triple-J Radio of most of the final show, in broadcast order. It's a good capture, which I received in a trade many years ago. Once again, this file set was created by Buckley hero eclect, to whom my grateful thanks and admiration for Keeping The Flame Alive for so long.
Apart from the usual housekeeping, I offer it to you as I received it.
