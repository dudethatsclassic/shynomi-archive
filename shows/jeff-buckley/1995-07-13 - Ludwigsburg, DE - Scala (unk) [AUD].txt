Festivals behind them, the band headed for Ludwigsburg, Germany and the historic Scala on Stuttgarter Straße, a landmark cultural institution for more than 100 years. Our unknown taper provides us with a very good recording of a terrific show. I think this run of summer, 1995 shows is the band's peak. Jeff's voice isa thing of awesome beauty, and his guitar playing, especially during the 'rock out' trio of Eternal Life/Jams/That's All I Ask (in its new Two Ninjas arrangement). I suggest turning this part way up!

No surprises or easter eggs in this one until we get to the end. The taper had apparently turned off the machine, but Jeff returned to the stage for a rare second encore. Calling up the band, he launches into a mammoth "Four Sticks" tease, before moving into the Van Morrison cover which closes the show - which is a scorcher of a scat fest. This is a very rare and unique performance: to my knowledge, the only (nearly-)complete Led Zeppelin cover we have on tape.

I received this in a trade many years ago. There some faults which I didn't repair. "Last Goodbye" is not tracked well; The taper stopped a few times, and in the first case, fades were applied, but not on the last few tracks, where apparently they were confused as to whether the show was over or not! there is a hard cut at the end of "Hallelujah", and the beginning of the second encore is a bit ragged. That said, it's a unique listen. Apart from the usual housekeeping, I present it to you as-is.
