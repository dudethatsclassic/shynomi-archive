Jeff and the band appeared at the Lowlands Festival of 1994 - their first of many Festival shows as a band. In the 90s, the European Festival circuit was chockablock all summer from the UK across France, Sweden, the Netherlands, Denmark, Germany, Belgium, even Hungary had the Sziget Festival! Sadly, many of these are no longer being held, and prices for them make them unaffordable for all but the most affluent music fans. BUT: in the 90s, you could follow a band across the continent fairly reasonably and, as a result, we are blessed with recordings from most of the major festivals held in 1994 & 1995 when Jeff and the band were touring.

The Lowlands appearance followed a couple of nights in Ireland, both the Republic and NI. The day after Lowlands, they flew back to the UK for the Reading Fest, which will follow. I got this from a Dime upload in 2007 or so. It may have been one of Lambchops, but I have no name attached to it. I'm grateful to the original uploader and even more grateful to the fam who kept the DAT.

Original Uploader's comments
----------------------------

This is what the guy who gave me the tape told me, "I was there at that show on a Saturday morning at the Lowlands Festival. Jeff was the first act to appear in the Charlie-tent that day, as the Festival crowd was waking up. He was not a common name yet, but when he hit the first chords it was immediately clear that this was a very sincere and heart-felt performance. It sent shivers down my spine. 

I managed to get a hold [of a DAT SBD feed recording] that was made from the first three songs. I kept it as something special.  Now is probably a good tome to start sharing this, as listening to the tape again made me aware how good he was back then." 

So there you have it: a direct copy of the Master Soundboard DAT that is COMPLETELY uncirculated, that nobody except this guy has ever heard, completely digitally transferred. It should make a nice set with the video I posted earlier. [ED NOTE: I'll be uploading a couple of videos from the show]. Theere is a little hum under the sound, but that was in the PA system and can't be helped. Also at the end of the last track there is some noise that I could not get rid of.
