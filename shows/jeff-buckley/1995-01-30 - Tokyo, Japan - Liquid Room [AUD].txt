From Paris, the Mystery White Boy Tour moved to Japan, for six dates starting with a taping at On Air Azabu Studios, the results of which can be found on the Australian Tour Edition of Grace https://www.discogs.com/release/2262039-Jeff-Buckley-Grace and on a US promo of the So Real CD single:  https://www.discogs.com/release/1710949-Jeff-Buckley-So-Real.

On the 29th, the band played the first of two dates at Tokyo's Shinjyuku Liquid Room. Video of Jeff performing "Hallelujah" purporting to be from the 29th can be seen here: https://www.dailymotion.com/video/x2lycs. Unlike the performance from the 30th he does not segue into "I Know It's Over". 

This show from the second night, bootlegged and edited to fit a single CD, circulates widely as "Japanese Whisper" (Godfather Records GR14 - art included), with 'Vancouver' & 'Kanga-roo' removed. Here you can enjoy it in its complete form. The stunning solo encore of Hallelujah paired with "I Know It's Over" alone make it worth the listen. I hope you enjoy.
