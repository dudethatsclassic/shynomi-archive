1993 was, comparatively-speaking, an easy year for Jeff, following his Music Faucet show featured in my last upload. Much of his time was spent in the recording studio. 

In February, after dates at The Knitting Factory, The Fez and 1st Reformed Church, he recorded 16 tracks at Shelter Island Sound with engineer Steve Addabbo. These sessions remain unreleased until the 2016 release of "You And I" (https://www.discogs.com/release/8235792-Jeff-Buckley-You-And-I), later supplemented by the RSD release, "In Transition" (https://www.discogs.com/release/13469596-Jeff-Buckley-In-Transition).

The Spring of 1993 saw additional dates at the usual places, a recording session at the Knitting Factory, from which a reworked version of Strawberry Street emerged. This can be found on Songs to No One, and - critically - a gig at Columbia University's Postcrypt Coffee House. It was after this gig that Jeff met Mick Grondahl. They jammed all night and the seeds of a band were born.

On 6 June, Jeff returned to Sin-é for an afternoon Music Faucet remote: a short and sweet set of just a couple of songs, but it's VERY Jeff, plus it contains the first known live recording of "Lover, You Should've Come Over". 

I hope you enjoy.
