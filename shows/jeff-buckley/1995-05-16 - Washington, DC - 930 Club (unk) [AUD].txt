After another travel day, it was back to the 9:30 Club for a return appearance, this time opening for Soundgarden. It's a pretty much by-the-numbers show, with no surprises or easter eggs this time. And it's not the greatest listen, either, which is too bad, as the bands performance is strong.

There are two circulating 'versions' of this show, according to FIT, although they are the same recording: they both begin with the same chatty people close to the taper discussing their fitness regimen(!). This the slightly longer version 1A. I got both as trades many years ago, and kept this one. Neither recording is optimal. A combination of the taper's location, drop-outs, poorly cut track markers and the very high generation of this copy makes it one for the collectors, mostly.

I didn't feel it was worth my time re-tracking, etc. What you're getting is what I got 20-odd years ago, apart from some basic housekeeping.
