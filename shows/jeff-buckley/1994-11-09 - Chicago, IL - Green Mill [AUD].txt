From Toronto, Jeff and band headed south to Chicago, via scheduled dates in Michigan, Ohio, Missouri, Kansas and Minnesota, which - sadly - remain unrecorded/unreleased. This is the second of two nights at Chicago's famed Green Mill, former watering hole of Al Capone, opened in 1907 and still doing great business at 4802 Broadway. This show is as famous for its unrecorded previous night as on its own. Having downed a bottle of Cuervo before hitting the stage, the performance - in front of critics and industry personnel - was a shitshow, by Jeff's own admission. A month later he was still apologising to his fan club via postcard (ah: remember those days, before the internet made us everywhere all at once). A review of the notorious first night at the Green mill can be found here: https://notwithyoubutofyou.blogspot.com/2017/11/pathetic-galvanizing_28.html

eclect's notes:
--------------------------
There is definitely tape somewhere in the heritage of this show, and you can also hear the taper riding the gain occasionally. Even so, this is a marvellous show - Jeff is as broodingly intense as ever, and the energy on stage is fantastic, for what also sounds like a small venue and audience. It sounds like Jeff's friend Chris Dowd may have been in the audience as well - Jeff exclaims 'Chris! I didn't think you were coming!' after "So Real".

Quoting from a message to his fans on Dec 16, 1994, commenting on his 2 shows in Chicago: "NO MORE CUERVO NIGHTS!!! DON'T WORRY, CHICAGO!!!! Of course, the stinking journalists didn't show up for night number two at the Green Mill gig, WE MASHED THE NIGHT BEFORE INTO GOATSHIT!!! That's o.k., there'll be plenty of articles for them to write in hell..." and I must say I tend to agree with him - regardless of how the first Chicago night was, this was an excellent show.
