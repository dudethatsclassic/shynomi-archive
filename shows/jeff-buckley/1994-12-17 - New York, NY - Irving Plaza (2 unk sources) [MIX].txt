The Fall 1994 tour ran for two months and a little over 50 dates starting at CBGBs in New York City, and ending 30-odd blocks north at the slightly swankier Irving Plaza in Union Square, which I'm glad to see is still very much in business. I saw a lot of shows there in the 90s, but not this one. This would've been my third JB show, but I opted to see Jules Shear & Paula Cole at The Bottom Line that night instead, having seen Jeff already at that point. I know I'm not the only fan who figured Jeff would be around forever.

Irving Plaza is a great venue. This is NOT a great recording. If you're looking for sonic perfection, then I'd suggest you move on to the next upload! But in the interests of completion, I'm sharing this frankenstein's monster of a show. I know eclect got this from Buckley mega-fan Mr A in Norway. I'm not sure which did the assembly, but they did the best they could with what they had, and this is the result.
 

From eclect's Notes
-------------------
Complete show created from two inferior sources, both edited and with Noise Reduction before their use here. Applause is occasionally cut short, and some tracks miss the first few notes.
[There's] weird garbling at the beginning [that] sounds like NR, further confirmed by the different levels of tape noise found when comparing tracks. Rolloff in both sources at 15k strongly suggests a lossy heritage in source. (probably MD/MP3) 
--------------------
