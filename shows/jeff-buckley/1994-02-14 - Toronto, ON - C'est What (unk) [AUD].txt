After LA, Jeff worked his way back east, playing dates in Texas, Illinois, and Michigan, before turning north for this date at Toronto's C'est What, still operating (since 1988) on Front Street in the St Lawrence Market. 

This is a fairly good audience recording, for which the master has yet to appear. It contains a few cuts, noted above, as well as rare takes of Nina Simone's "That's All I Ask" and "Be Your Husband" (one of only three known recordings), the only known recordings of Motšrhead's "Ace of Spades" (usually just teased) and Gershwin's "I Loves You Porgy", plus "I Know It's Over" and a mind-bending 13-minute take of Van Morrison's "The Way Young Lovers Do" to close the set.

But that's just the songs. The whole point of a Jeff Buckley show was Jeff on stage, interacting with a (usually) appreciative audience, and this is a cracker. As Jeff says, "There is no fourth wall in my shows"!

I received this in a trade many years ago, and traded frequently with my own artwork, which is now lost, apart from the cover, which is tagged onto the files.
