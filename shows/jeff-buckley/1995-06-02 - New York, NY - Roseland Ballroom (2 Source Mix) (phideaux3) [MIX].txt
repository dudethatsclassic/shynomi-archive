The 1995 Mystery White Boy Tour ran through at least 108 confirmed dates across three continents and in four major legs. The second leg is finally coming to a close as we return to NYC, and a show that is very special to me, as it was my fourth (and final) Jeff show. It took place at New York's famed Roseland, once known as THE place to go for big band and swing on Broadway, but had moved to its W 52nd St location by the time of this show. One of the things I remember best about the shows I saw at Roseland in the 90s was the floor, which bounced up and down if the crowd was really getting into it.

The tour 'offically' concluded a couple of days later with live taping at Sony Studios for broadcast on WNEW-FM. That recording has been officially released as Columbia Records Radio Hour: https://www.discogs.com/release/14072009-Jeff-Buckley-Columbia-Records-Radio-Hour

But back to Roseland. I was a pressed up against the stage for this performance. You are getting a better sounding show than I heard LOL. This is the show which was attended by Paul and Linda McCartney, and some pictures (as proof) are included in the upload. Once again, Jeff was the opener for Juliana Hatfield. Jeff mentions early on that it's the end of the mini-tour, and the band are determined to go out with a bang for his adopted hometown crowd. It's mostly the typical shortened set, but we do get the final solo "Hallelujah" spot, which tonight includes the Smiths tease. The crowd had been shouting for it all night, and he definitely delivered. It's a terrific show, spoiled somewhat for me by the rude audience members who continue to talk on all sides of the taper, throughout the quieter portions of the programme. I think summary execution would quickly bring an end to such rude behaviour, but that's just me.
