Once again, the label/estate have cherry-picked one track from a show, rather than releasing the entire thing. To complete the set, add "The Man That Got Away" [Track 10] from "Mystery White Boy" (https://www.discogs.com/master/81022-Jeff-Buckley-Mystery-White-Boy-Live-95-96) which has been removed in compliance with Dime policy.

Following on from the KFOG interview, Jeff and the boys decamped to the spacious confines of the historic Great American Music Hall, for the first of two (apparently) sold-out shows at the Tenderloin venue. This is a solid recording from an unknown taper, of a show with a few surprises, including a Shonen Knife cover, two runs at That's All I Ask: first the heavy Two Ninjas arrangement, and then a lovely a cappella take closer to the Nina Simone version. The evening concludes with "Hallelujah" and the usual encores.

I got this from eclect's Dime upload in the early aughts, and apart from some general housekeeping, present it to you as-is.
