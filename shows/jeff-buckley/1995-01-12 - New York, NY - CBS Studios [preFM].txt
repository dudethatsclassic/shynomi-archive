The Fall 1994 Tour finished mid-December, and the band took a well-deserved 2-week break. Jeff played solo shows at Sin-é on New Year's Eve and at St Mark's Church on New Year's Day. A portion of the Sin-é show was filmed and can be seen on the Live at Sin-é bonus DVD: https://www.discogs.com/master/81033-Jeff-Buckley-Live-At-Sin-é

On the tenth, the band visited MTV Studios for an appearance on MTV's late-night 120 Minutes programme. This performances can also be found on "Grace Around The World" https://www.discogs.com/release/21075679-Jeff-Buckley-Grace-Around-The-World

Contemporaneous interview here: https://notwithyoubutofyou.blogspot.com/2017/12/orgasm-addict.html

On the twelfth, they headed to CBS Studios for the first of two appearances on the Columbia Records Radio Hour syndicated programme. On this occasion, they were the 'opening act' for up and coming singer Natalie Merchant, newly solo after leaving 10,000 Maniacs. The boys played the usual 2-song set and here it is for you.
