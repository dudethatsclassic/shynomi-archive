Gods & Monsters (late '91-early '92)
------------------------------------
Gary Lucas - Guitars
Jeff Buckley - Guitar & Vocals
Tony Maimone - Bass
Anton Feir - Drums

Gods & Monsters, and particularly Jeff Buckley, became regulars on Nick Hill's Music Faucet radio programme on college station WFMU-FM, and appeared on a number of broadcasts, including at least one phoned-in performance, which will appear later in an Odds & Ends collection. This is one of several performances from the Knitting Factory which were broadcast on the station, and is one that suffers from the arbitrary nature of Buckley's catalogue releases.

To complete the set, add "Cruel" [Track 7], "Malign Fiesta" [Track 10], and "Satisfied Mind" [Track 6] from "Songs To No One 1991-1992" (2002) by Jeff Buckley & Gary Lucas (https://www.discogs.com/master/67549-Jeff-Buckley-Gary-Lucas-Songs-To-No-One-1991-1992)
