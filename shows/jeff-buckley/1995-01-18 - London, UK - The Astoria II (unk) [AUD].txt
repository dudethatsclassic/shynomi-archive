Their press shows behind them, it was time for the band to soldier on. If 1994 had been a busy year, 1995 was a gruelling slog: four separate tours put the band on the road for more than a hundred confirmed dates, pick-up media sessions and press appearances. We have a slightly better representation of live performances from 1995, most likely due to Jeff's burgeoning fame growing his audience incrementally. At the beginning of 1995, Grace had been out for four months, making scarcely a ripple one the charts and - in those pre-internet days - depending largely upon the fan club and word of mouth to bring out the crowds. The venues remained on the smaller side, but the performances show a band operating on all cylinders, if perhaps growing a bit tired of the material at times. Still: there are many solid shows in 1995.

Prior to this show, the band had played in Dublin and Bristol. We have set lists from both. A review of Dublin can be found here: https://notwithyoubutofyou.blogspot.com/2017/12/feral.html and Youtube clips from Bristol can be found here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=poR4LLOg31A and elsewhere.

In London, they appeared on the BBC's Late Show. "Grace" from that performance can be found on the "Grace Around The World" DVD https://www.discogs.com/master/378293-Jeff-Buckley-Grace-Around-The-World

The next evening provide this upload, which I received in a trade many years ago. It's a solid audience recording from an unnamed taper and is a good listen. In the course of an otherwise fairly typical set, complete with equipment problems that sabotage "Eternal Life", we are offered the final known recording of Jeff covering The Creatures' "Killing Time" which no doubt pleased the English fans in the audience. As usual, the show is full of Jeff-isms as he entertains the crowd between songs. "Mojo Pin" has a spoken introduction in places of the usual "Chocolate", and "Hallelujah" is by now well-known enough that the punters are calling out for it. "Vancouver" is back in the set and a sort but sweet Kanga-Roo completes the show.
