The Mystery White Boy Spring 1995 Tour continues apace with Jeff's fourth radio appearance in a week, this time at San Francisco radio's late, lamented KFOG-FM, a mainstay of AAA radio in the 90s - responsible for launching many careers, and advancing those of many others. Sadly KFOG is no more - another sign of the rapid dissolution of the greater music landscape post-2000.

This one is short and sweet, just a quick interview and couple of songs, but it's tasty.
