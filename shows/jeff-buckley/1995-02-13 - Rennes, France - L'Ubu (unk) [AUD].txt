After the Bataclan, the band took the day off on the 12th to travel to Brittany, specifically to its scenic capital, the city of Rennes, where an unknown taper captured this performance. The performance is outstanding. Sadly, the recording is not the greatest, but it's the only record we have of this show at l'Ubu, a 450-seater opened in 1987 and still entertaining the public with concerts and other events to this day: https://www.lestrans.com/saison-ubu/agenda_ubu/

If you can put your 'bootleg ears' on for this show, it is well worth the effort. The band performs for a boisterous and hugely appreciative crowd, who urge him at one point to run for President(!) The usual set is supplemented by two rarely played songs: first an impromptu solo take of The Smiths' "Boy With The Thorn In His Side" and, having concluded the show with the Vancouver/Kanga-Roo showstopper, Jeff encores with a rare 13-minute solo outing of Van Morrison's "The Way Young Lovers Do, before concluding with a breathtaking nine-minute "Hallelujah". But the crowd is not willing to let him go yet, and they continue shouting until he returns briefly to sing the crowd off with Brahms' Lullaby. And really: can you think of a better way to be sent home?

The notes I got with this trade CDr indicated that there may have been an interval after the Smiths song, and that Eternal Life was played but not recorded. There are some cuts & fades between songs, so I suppose it's possible, but I don't know of any other Buckley shows with multiple sets. 

I received this in a snail mail trade many years ago. Apart from some basic housekeeping, I present it to you as-is.
