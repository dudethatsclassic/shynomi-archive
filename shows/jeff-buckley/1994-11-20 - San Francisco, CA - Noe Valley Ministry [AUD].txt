From Chicago, it was off to the west coast, with scheduled dates in Colorado, Utah, Washington, Oregon and British Columbia (none of them recorded, sadly, to my knowledge), before landing at the Noe Valley Ministry, a unique non-denominational church on San Francisco's Sanchez Street. Churches have always figured highly in Jeff's performing history, starting with his debut at St Anne's, and continuing through appearances in houses of worship in Dublin, Toronto, and elsewhere.

Sadly, the existing recording of this show suffers from levels which drop off dramatically after "What Will You Say", and don't entirely recover until "Grace". I tried to do a quick fix, but boosting the levels only exposes the listener to an unpleasant level of hiss and, as I'm not working from a master tape, I decided to leave it as-is, in the interests of historical value. Fans who are not completists may want to pass on this one; otherwise, you WILL need to ride the levels during playback to enjoy the entire programme. 

No real surprises on this show, but we get a lovely Piaf cover. It's possible that more songs were played after "Lover," - usually the shows ended with Kanga-Roo. But that may not have been played on this occasion, and Jeff says goodnight after introducing the band at the end, as the tape ends.
