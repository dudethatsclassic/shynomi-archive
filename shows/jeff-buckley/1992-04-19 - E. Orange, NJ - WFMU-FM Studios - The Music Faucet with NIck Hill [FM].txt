After the Roulette gig, Jeff & Gary played a couple more shows around NYC. Once the Gods & Monsters commitment was over, Jeff returned to solo performances. Here is a lovely short set of covers from Nick Hill's Music Faucet programme on the Upsala College radio statio, featuring songs by The Creatures, The Smiths, Velvet Underground, and the oft-covered Satisfied Mind. I'm of the opinion that Jeff was never better than when he was interpreting the work of others whom he admired.
