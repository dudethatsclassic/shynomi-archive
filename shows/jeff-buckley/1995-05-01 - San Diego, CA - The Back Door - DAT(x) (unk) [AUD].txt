After Texas, the band took a travel day before heading west to LA, where we are blessed with an amazing five-day run of eight recordings, beginning with this one from the Back Door, in the SDSU's Aztec Centre. The former bowling alley was repurposed to a coffee house cum concert venue, and like so many similar venues around the country, hosted many superstar bands in their infancy and, on May Day 1995, that included Jeff & the boys.

This is an audience recording - probably from a DAT source originally, but well-removed from its source, per the lineage. Nonetheless, it sounds great, and is apparently the entire show, but "Hallelujah" cuts during the final applause, so it's likely the usual "Vancouver/Kanga-Roo" was not recorded. Even without those, it's a nice long show.It's a fairly standard show from this time. The central rock-out section has now been expanded from Eternal Life & Kick Out The Jams to include a much harder, heavier, almost unrecognisable version of Horace Ott's "That's All I Ask", made famous by Nina Simone, and apparently now called "Two Ninjas (That's All I Ask)" if Columbia's notes on the officially-released King Theatre show are to be believed.

Apparently Jeff's family was at this show: https://notwithyoubutofyou.blogspot.com/2017/11/the-back-door.html He mentions that his grandmother is at the show before "So Real".
