January of 1994 began a grueling two-year period of virtually non-stop touring, beginning with the so-called "Live at Sin-Ž" solo tour of 30-odd dates across the US & Canada, before leaving for his first European tour in mid-march(!) Of these, we have recordings of just five, including this charming visit to KCRW-FM studios in LA, for a session of "The Man In The Moon" with host Liz Richardson. This is an outstanding solo performance, made shortly after the "Live at Sin-Ž" EP was released, but before sessions for "Grace" were completed. 

Jeff shows his typical range, jumping from a frantic Bad Brains cover to a mesmerising version of Billie Holiday's Strange Fruit.

I received this concert as an audio CD many years ago in a trade, and grabbed the art off the net. My thanks to whomever made it.
