From Brussels, it was back to the UK, where the band played shows at Glasgow's Garage and the University of Manchester, before this sold-out gig at Wulfrun Hall at the University of Wolverhampton where taper BelFrank was on hand to record this terrific show. Jeff's popularity had grown significantly by this time: we can hear some of the punters singing along at times, and they are clearly very appreciative - shouting out their live and approval.

It's a solid show with few surprises - we get a Nina double feature - after a pretty hilarious Neil Young impression, "Vancouver" is introduced as "This is a song from our new album" in Jeff's rock star voice. A furious Kanga-Roo ends the show, sending the crowd home well pleased.

Technical notes:
----------------
This show circulates in two versions - apparently two different people recorded the show. Belfrank told the uploader he saw another person taping and that may be the source of v1 of this show, which has inferior sound. This has known lineage and is apparently v2, per FIT. 

I got this from a 2007 Dime upload. There are some minor problems with this recording. It starts out a little rough, but smooths out before "Dream Brother" ends. "What Will You Say" is improperly tracked and contains the opening seconds of "Lilac Wine" which ends abruptly, but plays again in full at the track marker. There are some minor mic noises: apparently the taper moved around a bit. Not sure what happened here, but I'm presenting it as-is. I'm sure some enterprising person can fix it.  All that said, this is a good listen, overall.
