From Queensland, after a day off the band played the Barton in Adelaide, before turning its sights on Perth, and the beautiful outdoor Belvoir Amphitheatre (seriously: look at the enclosed pics in the upload!), a rare non-festival outdoor performance for the boys. We are lucky that an unknown taper was there to capture it - perhaps not with the best of equipment - to provide us with this recording of the evening. As eclect noted in his original upload notes, it ain't audiophile. That said, with your 'bootleg ears' on, it's far more listenable than the Worker's Club show. At least we can make out what Jeff is saying, despite the occasional distortion.

The set is pretty fixed at this point, so it's the performances that make this well worth a listen. Check out Jeff channeling Wes Montgomery at the start of "Lover", and his continued vocal explorations throughout, which remind of nothing so much as Coltrane's 'sheets of sound', particularly in his later recordings. Jeff was clearly searching for the same sort of unreachable spiritual attainment as the late sax player, and his last recordings - which is where we are, sad to say - are at times equally stunning and bewildering.

Apart from the usual housekeeping, I present this to you as uploaded in 2007.


eclect's upload notes:
----------------------
Pretty appalling recording, probably done with a dictaphone, but still the only extant version of this show. [It's] quite an early generation tho, so this is probably as close as you'll get to the original source. Jeff is in a typically playful mood, and there is some amusing commentary, esp in Hallelujah. Tape transfer was done in a studio using high end equipment, so this is as good as it gets for this source. Tape flip between tracks 11 and 12.
