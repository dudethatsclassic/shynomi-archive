The never-ending Mystery White Boy Tour continued through the spring months of 1995, at a pace that seems pretty breath-taking to one who is not a performing musician. The night of the "On The Edge" Session, the band played the famed Agora. After a day off, they picked up with dates in Cincinnati and Detroit, before heading north to Canada, starting with a date at Toronto's Music Hall, followed by two dates in Montreal at Le Spectrum, the famed Concert Hall on Saint-Catherine, which sadly closed in 2007. Jeff was scheduled to play Montreal on 27 May, but a second date was added on the 28th - it's not on his itinerary, so that's most likely due to demand for tickets. This recording is from the second night.

Le Spectrum was a dinner theatre, which explains Jeff's comments about 'enjoying your steak'. Happily, the taper was well-situated, so we get a minimum of knives, forks & plates. As they note below, Jeff is in great voice, and the vocals are the highlight of this well-balanced recording, which suffers from a little distortion at the loudest parts. Once again, Jeff was the opener for Hatfield, so it's a shortened set, with no easter eggs. But what we get is a great listen.


Taper's Notes:
--------------
Magnificent set.  Buckley's vocals are unearthly, impossible, unbelievable, and the band adds appropriate sounds.  The audience is well behaved and their appropriate hoots and whoops add to the ambiance.  But the dynamic range is huge so please be careful;  talk and first vocals are quiet, then boom!  The first jump is at t01-04:23.
