Sadly, this show is also a victim of the label/estate's ridiculous cherry-picking of tracks when creating posthumous releases. Three of the performances from this show have been released on video, with a further track also available on the CD accompanying the DVD release. To complete the set, add the missing tracks from "Grace Around The World" (https://www.discogs.com/release/2562392-Jeff-Buckley-Grace-Around-The-World) which come from this show and have been removed to comply with Dime policy.

As noted in my previous upload, there are three recordings of this most excellent show available. We are lucky that Mike W (dimer ekimrf) was there to record the show from the balcony, as it is the most complete version available. 

This show was uploaded to Dime in 2007 by Dime superstar hanwaker, source of a lot of amazing shows in circulation. Super thanks to him for sharing so many shows here. Apart from editing the show for Dime compliance, and some minor housekeeping, I present it to you as uploaded in 2007.
