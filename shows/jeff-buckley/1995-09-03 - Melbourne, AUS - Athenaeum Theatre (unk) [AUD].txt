Apologies in advance for uploading something so dire.

The night of the Rooftop set, the band played The Lounge in Melbourne. The next afternoon, they did another in-store, this time at Gaslight Music, and that set - from crankingamps' master audience recording - can be found here: http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=669578

On the second, the band played the Prince Patrick Hotel, and then on their, it moved across town to the historic Athenaeum Theatre on Collins, the oldest cultural institution in Melbourne. The neo-classical 880-seater, established in 1839, was completed in 1886. 

Luckily(?) for us, an unnamed taper was present and left us this souvenir of the evening. It's a really rough listen, over-modulated and super saturated, and I offer it up only for the completists in the crowd, as there is nothing otherwise special about this performance. It's a pity, as the crowd is super-appreciative and vocal. But they can't all be pristine soundboards, eh?

I got this in a trade many years ago. It was sourced from Norway's super-Buckley fan Mr A and archived by eclect, without whose loving care, Buckley's legacy would be considerably less. Apart from the normal housekeeping, I pass it to you as-is, for better or worse.
