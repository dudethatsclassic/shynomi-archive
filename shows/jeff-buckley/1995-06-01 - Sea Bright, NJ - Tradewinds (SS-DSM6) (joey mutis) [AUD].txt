The night of the Mama Kin taping, Jeff played the first of two nights at Boston's famed Avalon. The next day, after a Tower Records In-Store, he returned to the Avalon, and then it was off to the Jersey shore where Jeff and the boys played the much-missed Tradewinds on Ocean Ave - a venue more used, perhaps, to the Boss, who played there eight times before it closed some 20 years ago.

Happily for us, taper Joey Mutis was there to capture this opening set, which was uploaded to Dime in 2018. Apart from some housekeeping, I present it to you as-is. It's a good listen, if a bit over-modulated during the loud bits. It's a pretty typical opening show, with the solo spot and the encores dropped for time. Enjoy!
