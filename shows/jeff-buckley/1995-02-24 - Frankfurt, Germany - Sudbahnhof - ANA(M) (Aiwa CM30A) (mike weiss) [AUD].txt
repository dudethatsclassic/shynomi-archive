Sadly, this show is also a victim of the label/estate's ridiculous cherry-picking of tracks when creating posthumous releases. Three of the performances from this show have been released on video, with a further track also available on the CD accompanying the DVD release. To complete the set, add the missing tracks from "Grace Around The World" (https://www.discogs.com/release/2562392-Jeff-Buckley-Grace-Around-The-World) which come from this show and have been removed to comply with Dime policy.


After I posted my version of the Südbahnhof show, I got in touch with the taper who kindly offered FLACS directly from the master. He also added some background to his recording:

The "pre-show" Grace was actually not pre-show: as Hessische Rundfunk
(HR) was the host for this, they recorded this version of Grace for a
tv-programme called 'Musik & Diskussion' which was broadcast on HR3 at a
later stage in March that year: there's a videoMaster off cable tv
somewhere in my vault ... whether it would still play, who knows??

The 'Musik & Diskussion' talk show was subsequently also broadcast on
Bayrische Rundfunk (BR3) and 3Sat (which is a conglomerate, like the
US-PBS, consisting of Germany's ARD, Austria's ORF and Switzerland's
SRF) and only included this one track as part of the whole production.

Following this recording session, there was a short reshuffling on stage
... IIRC nobody left. Then, the tv host brought them on again for the
showcase set.

My thanks to Mike for his memories and his recording. I pass it along to you as I received it.
