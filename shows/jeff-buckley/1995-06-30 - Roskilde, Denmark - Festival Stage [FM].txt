After a day off following the EXO 7 gig, it was over to Denmark for the next Festival appearance, this time at Roskilde, still going strong after 53 years! IT's a festival set - a bit longer than an opening set, but not a full show. Vancouver & Kanga-Roo are moved to the end of the set, following which the band leave the stage for Jeff's solo "Hallelujah" to close the show.

This show was broadcast on multiple European radio stations - each version is slightly different in terms of station IDs, etc. At one point I had five of them. I got this from Karma1's upload in the early aughts. It's the best-sounding version and, apart from the usual housekeeping, I present it as-is.
