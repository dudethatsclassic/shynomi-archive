I got this from dimer phideaux3's upload some years ago. I'm not sure if he made the recording, but either way, my thanks to him for sharing it. It appears to be from a US re-broadcast on the syndicated "Live From The Underground Lounge" programme, according to the DJ IDs. The DJ - Gary C, or Gary Cee - mentions that the broadcast is in lieu of a 'secret gig' that was to have been broadcast from an 'unknown location", so we know this broadcast came while Jeff was still living. I'm super-curious to know where that un-broadcast gig would have been. The band was in NYC during April of 1995, but no gigs - apart from two radio appearances - are known to have taken place that month.

There's a little radio 'drift' and the usual FM compression, but it's a pleasing listen and, apart from some minor housekeeping, I present it unchanged. "Lover, You Should've Come Over" is exclusive to this upload: it was apparently not recorded on the separate audience recording.
