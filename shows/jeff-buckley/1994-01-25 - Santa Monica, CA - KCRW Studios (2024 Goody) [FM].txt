January of 1994 began a gruelling two-year period of virtually non-stop touring, beginning with the so-called "Live at Sin-é" solo tour of 30-odd dates across the US & Canada, before leaving for his first European tour in mid-march(!) Of these, we have recordings of just five, including this charming visit to KCRW-FM studios in LA, for a session of "The Man In The Moon" with host Liz Richardson. This is an outstanding solo performance, made shortly after the "Live at Sin-é" EP was released, but before sessions for "Grace" were completed. 

Jeff shows his typical range, jumping from a frantic Bad Brains cover to a mesmerising version of Billie Holiday's Strange Fruit.

I received this concert as an audio CD many years ago in a trade, and grabbed the art off the net. My thanks to whomever made it.

In 2009, following my original upload, the file set was much improved by goody. After hearing it again, he offered to tweak it further, making this the ultimate version of this fine recording.
