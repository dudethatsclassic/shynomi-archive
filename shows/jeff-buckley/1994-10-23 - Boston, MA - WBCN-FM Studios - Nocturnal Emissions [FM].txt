The Peyote Radio Theatre Tour having wrapped up in Paris, a night after the Antwerp show, the band returned to the States, playing a few dates around town including the CMJ Music Marathon - where I saw Jeff for the first time. The Fall 1994 Tour kicked off at CBGBs on the 19 October and ran for 50-odd dates through December. I saw Jeff a second time at his return to St Anne's the night before this Boston show. Sadly, recordings for either of those shows have yet to surface.

This is the first recording we have from the Fall '94 tour, a short acoustic solo set for WBCN's Nocturnal Emissions programme. Mike and Matt are in attendance but not featured. As Mick Grondahl was moving home that day, he was not there. The interviews conducted by the legendary Boston DJ Oedipus make this worth a listen.

I got this version from Milesian's 2005 upload. It comes from the 1997 Memorial re-broadcast shortly after Jeff's death, which also featured Jeff's set from Boston's 'Mama Kin', which will follow in due course. This is a superb FM.  Milesian's edited version of the set lacked the  complete intro and outro to the show, so I added them back in from a different version of the show I had at the time.
