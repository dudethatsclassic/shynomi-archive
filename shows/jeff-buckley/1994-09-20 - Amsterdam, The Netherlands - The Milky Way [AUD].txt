From Night Life to The Milky Way, Jeff and band moved on from Frankfurt to Amsterdam and the fabled Melkweg, the former milk factory (hence the name) turned cultural venue, where so many bands have played over the years. This is a fairly typical set with no major surprises, apart from the manic improv intro to Eternal Life, which is now very much the Road Version. The crowd is super appreciative and the band is smoking. I took the title of the show from Jeff's remarks to the crowd mid-show.

The actual set appears to end after Lilac Wine: Jeff thanks the crowd, says good night, and then plays at least two more songs - three if Kanga-Roo is actually from this show. When Kanga-Roo was featured, it was generally the final song in the set. 

I had two versions of this show - both of them incomplete. One had Kanga-Roo inserted in place of Grace, which was missing. A careful listen to the show indicates the above is most likely the correct running order. Apart from an apparent edit at the end of Lover, and a hard cut at the end of Eternal Life, the concert flows continuously. Kanga-Roo may well be from another date, though, as the recording has more hiss. I'm leaving it here, in the interests of sharing as much Jeff as possible. And if you're a Matt Johnson fan, you'll want to turn this Kanga-Roo up to eleven!
