From Berlin, it was a relatively short hop to Frankfurt, and an appearance at the famed Nachtleben (Nightlife), which had been entertaining punters since 1976 on the Kurt Schumacher Straße, and remains a go-to spot for indie music fans to this day. 

This show is typical of the tour: most of Grace is performed (Corpus Christi Carol having been largely jettisoned from live shows by this time). Hallelujah had yet to reach the anthemic status it was to attain later, particularly after its use on the widely-viewed Season Four episode "Forever Young" of Grey's Anatomy, (and then seemingly on every TV show and in every movie for a while!). It didn't feature on this night, and the final Kanga-Roo is a relatively short ten minutes. However, the standout of this show is Jeff's ode to First World Problems, "White Man Trouble", largely improvised and during which, according to unconfirmed fan accounts, the band switched instruments with Jeff taking drumming duties - not entirely out of character as he was a drummer before he was a guitarist!
