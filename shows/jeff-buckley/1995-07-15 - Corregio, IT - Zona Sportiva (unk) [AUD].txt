As Jeff tells us mid-way through this show, the end of the tour is in sight. This is from the "Arena" Festival, with two nights left. After this show, they played festival sets in Bern and Saint-Florent-Le-Viel and then the tour was over - well almost, but we'll get there. The French Festival featured an appearance by Jeff with Azerbaijani sacred singer Alim Qasimov, which can be heard on the Live from Olympia (sic) release here: https://www.discogs.com/master/81032-Jeff-Buckley-Live-A-LOlympia. I have a terrible quality video of that performance which cannot be shared, per Dime policy.

Meanwhile, back in Correggio, we are blessed to have not only complete audio and, rarely, video of the performance. The audio not the greatest - it's high gen and recorded well back, but we get the entire show.  I got this from eclect's Dime upload many years back, and apart from the usual housekeeping, present it to you as-is.
