Tokyo was followed by Fukuoka, where Jeff and band played one of the best shows for which we have a recording. Jeff is in great form - there are the usual quotes from other bands and silly jokes, and we get a rare outing of the Dylan classic, "Mama, You've Been On My Mind" - the last known recording of that one.

This is a pretty solid recording - not too far removed from the master from the sound of it, and I've had it a long time. This came from one-time Buckley trader "NewHomeBrew", about whom little is known, but who flamed briefly through Buckley fandom in the nineties, offering 'rare' recordings in exchange for others. Unfortunately, he disappeared, along with the master recordings of an unknown number of Buckley shows, never to be heard from again. I'm loathe to give him any publicity, but he's the source of this show - he got it from a Japanese trader, apparently, and apart from some housekeeping, I pass it along to you as-is.

From NewHomeBrew's notes
------------------------
Originally taped on a 90 minute analog cassette (you can tell by the cassette noises and tape flip at 45 minutes) the sound quality rates 7/10 with some issues: left channel level is a touch lower in gain than the right; some analog overloading in places; taper adjusts levels during the first track; overall the levels are somewhat low

Amusing comments between songs about the first visit to Japan. The brief bit of Star-Spangled Banner spoofs the Jimi Hendrix version the same way Jeff was fond of lampooning classic rock heroes like Led Zeppelin.  You can hear him give out roses to the audience when they come back on for the encore.
