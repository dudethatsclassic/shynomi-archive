After playing McCabe's, the band headed to Atlanta, with stops in Phoenix, Austin and New Orleans on the way. As was fairly typical of this and subsequent tours, Jeff did a great deal of press, including record store stops and college radio appearances. This is a great semi-acoustic performance from Atlanta's WNNX-FM (99x), presumably recorded the afternoon of Jeff's appearance at The Point.

I received this as a trade many years ago.
