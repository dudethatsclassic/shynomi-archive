To complete the set, add the missing tracks from "Grace Around The World" (https://www.discogs.com/release/2562392-Jeff-Buckley-Grace-Around-The-World) which come from this show and have been removed to comply with Dime policy.

After Hamburg, the band had a TV taping in Cologne on the 23rd - sadly, unrecorded, apparently - before continuing on to Frankfurt for one of the best known circulating shows. Sadly, this show is also a victim of the label/estate's ridiculous cherry-picking of tracks when creating posthumous releases. Three of the performances from this show have been released on video, with a further track also available on the CD accompanying the DVD release. 

The show at Frankfurt's South Station (Südbahnhof) was taped for broadcast on the "Aus Den Südbahnhof" TV programme, and three versions of this circulate: a professional audio recording of the complete TV taping; an audience recording of the entire show, and video of the programme as broadcast. I will be posting Dime-Friendly versions of all three. Sadly, no "complete" video recording (including the songs not broadcast) appears to exist.

This show was uploaded to Dime in 2007 by Buckley uber-fan eclect, source of so many Buckley shows in circulation. My unreserved thanks to him for Keeping The Flame alive. Apart from editing the show for Dime compliance, and some minor housekeeping, I present it to you as-is.

Eclect's Upload Notes
---------------------
Not entirely sure of the origin of the source (VHS/Reel?) but it is of a very low generation possibly off the master? - as the sound is excellent. The only tech glitch of note is a brief dropout right at the end of 'Lilac Wine'.

This is the complete show including 'Eternal Life' & The Smiths/Leonard Cohen combo, 'I Know It's Over'/'Hallelujah' [which were not broadcast]. This is NOT a rip of the widely circulated German TV broadcast which fades out during 'Grace' [indicating] it is taken from a pre-broadcast copy of the show. [Further evidence] would be that the audience is definitely more chatty, especially during the last two songs [indicating] the "On Air" lights had been switched off.
