After his appearance in DC, Jeff played a few dates in Philadelphia, including his appearance on WXPN's "World Cafe", which can be heard here: http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=724095

Then he headed to Charleston, WV, by way of New Jersey, for a taping of West Virginia Public Radio's "Mountain Stage" at the Culture Center. This was broadcast on 2 April, and the show is frequently mis-identified as being from that date.

This is satellite FM broadcast, a little compressed, but highly listenable. Jeff plays three songs from the forthcoming "Grace" and finishes off with an impromptu jam on "Good Morning, Little Schoolgirl", featuring fellow performers from the programme.

This is the final recording from the first leg of the "Live at Sin-é Tour". Jeff decamped to the UK on 10 March for a 10-day tour. Sadly, we have no recordings from that trip, but a few years ago, Radio 6 re-broadcasted a portion of Jeff's appearance on Greater London Radio from 18 March 1994. That cane heard here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mcUK4HTy55U&t=1s

We'll pick up with Jeff's return to The Fez for a Music Faucet broadcast from June 1994 next.
