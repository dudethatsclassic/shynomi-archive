After nine shows in four days, the band got a well-deserved day off on the sixth. Then it was up to Seattle's King Theatre on the seventh, for a show which has been officially released here: https://www.discogs.com/release/14072021-Jeff-Buckley-Live-From-Seattle-WA. On the eighth, they played Portland's Aladdin Theatre (sadly not recorded to my knowledge). Another day off followed, and on the tenth, the band arrived at the historic Bluebird Theatre on East Colfax in Denver, CO - still entertaining crowds well over a hundred years after its opening. This is a solid, if well-removed from the master, audience recording, and includes a couple of real gems and an amazing Easter Egg.

The rock out section of the show extends the Eternal Life/KOTJ medley into another "Two Ninjas" arrangement of Harry Ott's "That's All I Ask" - usually performed in the Nina arrangement. "We don't know what that was", Jeff comments at the end. There's some humorous banter about shamans and Jim Morrison. Jeff tells the crowd he wants to tell them a story and plays a desultory "Mojo Pin" - at 2:45 probably the shortest on record. And then the fun begins. 


	"Wait a minute. Wait a minute. I have a request. "Once I Was". I dunno...I'm feeling kind weird right now. Please wait. Please wait. OK? That cool? Just for you. Just this one time. Sometimes I hit it when I'm just not concentrating [unintelligible]. Check this out - you know what? I'll do it. Thank you. I'm going to sacrifice myself right now. You all love that kind of stuff I know. Check this out. I'm gonna show all the hippies how good I can do this song. So they can all go away and masturbate naked to their copy of Hello Goodbye, and leave the rest of us normal people alone! I want you to know one thing. I don't hate my father. Sometimes yes. But check this out: my father is sorta like that t-shirt: 'I went through the 60s and all I got was this stinkin' t-shirt'. I was somebody's son, and I was somebody's stinkin' t-shirt. But let me show you how cool this song is, OK? This is the only time you'll see it and I swear to fuckin' God. All right? I don't even know all the words" 


After which Jeff performs one of his dad's songs for the second and last time. Jeff hated being requested to do Tim's songs, and generally flat-out refused to do so, so this is an incredibly special and rare performance; to my knowledge the final time he covered any of his dad's songs. Once finished, he says, "Are you satisfied now? Are you really? Then shut the fuck up for the rest of my life... Love and kisses from the living one... Jeff Buckley"

I'm pretty certain this is the complete show. There are several edits throughout the show, including one after Jeff says goodnight following "Last Goodbye" which may cover the encore break. Jeff usually concluded the main set with a solo performance of "Hallelujah" and someone does call out for it, to which he replies, "Yeah - whatever you want baby you got it", but doesn't play it. He complains that his voice - and his mood - is shot, but he'll play a couple of songs he doesn't usually play, launching into a solo rendition of Dylan's "If You See Her", which seems to be it for the evening, as Jeff once again says "Goodnight".

I got this from eclect's Dime upload many years ago. My notes above are considerably expanded from his which accompanied the original upload. Apart from that and some general housekeeping, I present it to you as-is.
