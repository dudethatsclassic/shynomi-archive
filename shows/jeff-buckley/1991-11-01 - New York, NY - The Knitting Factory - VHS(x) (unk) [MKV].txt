I received this on a DVDr in a trade many years ago, as VIDEO_TS and its associated files. As there were no chapters, I converted it to mkv for easier watching. 

To the best of my knowledge, this is the earliest known video of Jeff Buckley. The video is the complete show, including Gary Lucas' set. Personally, I'm not a fan of Lucas: I find his playing too busy, and full of 'look at what I can do' technical stuff without a lot of substance. However - he did write the music for Grace and Mojo Pie, arguably the two greatest Buckley originals, so there's that. And without G&M, Buckley would not have come to the attention of Nick Hill at WFMU-FM's Music Faucet. But let's leave that for now.

The quality is not the greatest, due to the VHS generations, and there are a few glitches: at 01:40 someone apparently recorded over the tape for 4 seconds(!), but despite that, we have the earliest band performances of Mojo Pin and Grace which, apart from the superfluous Lucas guitar freak-outs, remained remarkably unchanged two years later when they were finally committed to tape.
