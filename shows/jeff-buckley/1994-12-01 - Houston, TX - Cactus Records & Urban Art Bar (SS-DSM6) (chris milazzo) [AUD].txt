The night after the Austin double-header, the band played the 21st Amendment in Dallas, which apparently did not go well, per this review: https://notwithyoubutofyou.blogspot.com/2020/12/in-concert-buckley-falls-from-grace.html The club in Deep Ellum took its name rather cleverly from the document which ended Prohibition in the United States, but is apparently no longer open.

Moving on to Houston, the band started with the now-familiar afternoon In-Store, this time at Cactus Records, Houston's oldest indie record store - still doing business on Portsmouth Street. That evening they played the Urban Art Bar, a popular night spot in the nineties, now sadly closed. Happily for us, Texas taper Chris Milazzo was again present, and provides us with these terrific audio recordings of the events.

Cactus Cafe review Here: https://notwithyoubutofyou.blogspot.com/2020/12/austin-american-statesman.html

Taper's Notes:
--------------
The Urban Art Bar crowd was pretty thin.  I think most of the people there were friends of the drummer, Matt Johnson.  I ended up sitting on the floor in front of the couch.  I had a coffee table in front of me - very convenient for hiding my deck!

The Cactus in-store was around 7 pm I think.  I think the stage was set up near the front of the store - I can't really remember.  I was in Cactus this past year and could not for the life of me remember where I was standing for the show.  Jeff signed autographs after the show and a scan of the sticker he signed for me is included.  Right after he signed it he peeled part of the backing off and stuck it to his forehead - I don't know why. No remastering was done do this recording.
