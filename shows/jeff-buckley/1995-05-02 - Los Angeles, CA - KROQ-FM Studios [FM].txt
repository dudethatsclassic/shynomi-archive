Our west coast mini-marathon continues with Jeff's appearance on KROQ-FM radio on the 2nd May. He played the American Legion Hall in Hollywood that night, but it's the only show of nine in the area that wasn't recorded. We'll get to his double-header from the next day in the next two uploads. This is a lot of fun: once again an obviously-enamoured young interviewer does her best, but fails to ask original questions, so we could probably provide most of Jeff's answers ourselves by this time. He has a good time, anyway, and we get solid acoustic performances of "Last Goodbye" and "So Real" for a change.

Based upon the interviews & conversation, this is definitely 2 May 1995. First - Tammy [KROQ DJ] mentions having seen Jeff at Luna Park [which was 11/22/94]. Next she references "tonight's gig at the American Legion Hall" - which was 5/2/95. Finally, "tomorrow's gig at Tower Records", which was 5/3/95.
