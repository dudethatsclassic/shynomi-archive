From the outset, I have to make clear that this is not the best video ever offered up on Dime. It has clearly been through multiple VHS generations and whoever made the DVD transfer played the tape back on a machine with bad azimuth and a dodgy set of pinch rollers. There is a persistent ribbon at the bottom of the screen and the video simply 'flakes out' from time to time.

That said, this is the longest uninterrupted video we have of Jeff & band performing since the Gods & Monsters days, so as a historical record, it is priceless. I'm posting it as the best I have. Please accept it in the spirit in which it was offered.
