Immediately after their appearance at Lowlands, the boys jetted back to the UK to play Reading the following day, before heading to London, with stops at Edinburgh & Birmingham on the way(!). A quick look at a map of the UK will show you how crazed that schedule is. We'll feature the London show in the next upload. 

Reading is, I believe, the oldest continuously running music festival in the UK, predating Glastonbury by 13 years. I'm happy to say the festival celebrated its 69th anniversary this summer, having missed only the pandemic year since it was born (as was I!) in 1955.

I've had a number of versions of this over the years, all from trade CDr until a few years ago, when the master was posted here on Dime and elsewhere. My thanks to the original unnamed taper, as well as the original uploader, who noted that Tindersticks were the headliners on this stage, which also featured performances by Echobelly and Archers of Loaf. A copy of the full festival schedule, as well as a ticket scan are contained in the upload.
