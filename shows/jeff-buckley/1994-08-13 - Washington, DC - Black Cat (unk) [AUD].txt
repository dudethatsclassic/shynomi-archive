The Peyote Radio Theatre tour continued apace through the summer of 1994. From Atlanta, they returned to DC, with stops along the way at Memphis and Nashville, for a return engagement at The Black Cat. This is not the greatest recording. It has some minor cuts which dot not affect the songs, and was taped well back in the venue apparently, as the crowd is loud and continues chatting throughout an epic 15-minute Chocolate -> Mojo Pin, here split into two pieces. It isn't until the song is well under way that they pipe down somewhat. Between songs, the behaviour worsens. It's a shame, because the fans in the audience are clearly appreciative. 

That said, if you can deal with the crowd, the show is a good one. Eternal Life is not yet the Road Version, but the aggression is beginning to take over the song, surely reflecting Jeff's frustration at the sound and the crowd. Most of Grace is performed, two days before the official release, along with an a cappella Nina cover leading directly into a 20-minute Kanga-Roo. They finish with what I assume is an unusual encore of "Lover,You Should've Come Over" 

The is the final US recording from the tour which I'll be sharing. After DC, the band returned to New York City for a thunderous performance at Wetlands, which has now been released inits entirety by Columbia: https://www.discogs.com/release/14072031-Jeff-Buckley-Live-At-Wetlands

Next, it was off to the UK for the European leg of the tour, which we'll visit on my next upload.
