After Frankfurt, it was a quick hop across the Dutch border to Rotterdam's NightTown Hall, where the band played a spirited set that was captured for Dutch radio. This selection of tracks comes from the early 1995 bootleg, "Dream Brother", which also included snippets from Frankfurt the night before, and the Roskilde Festival from 30 June, which is forthcoming, chronologically. While we have the complete set list, I do not have a complete recording of the entire 13-song show, and I'm uncertain if one circulates. It was not in the FIT archive of shows. 

Sadly, this show is also a victim of the label/estate's ridiculous cherry-picking of tracks when creating releases. Two of the performances from the commonly-bootlegged portion of this show were released on an early int'l EP, with a further track also available on the EP. To complete the set, add "So Real" and "Grace" from "Live From Nighttown" (https://www.discogs.com/master/1610004-Jeff-Buckley-Live-From-Nighttown) which have been removed in compliance with Dime policy.
