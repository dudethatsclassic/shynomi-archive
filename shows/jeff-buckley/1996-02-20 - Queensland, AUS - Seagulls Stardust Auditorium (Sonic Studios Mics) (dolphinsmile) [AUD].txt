The 1996 Hard Luck Tour of Australia & New Zealand continued through February and March of 1996. at a much less breakneck pace than the previous two years of worldwide touring. After two more nights at the Enmore in Sydney, the band played Festival Hall in Brisbane - we don't have recordings of those, sadly. However, and happily for us, an unknown taper was present to capture a listenable, complete show - the first of the tour. The set list is more or less back to the typical one, with two new songs being featured, neither fully formed, and neither terribly impressive, but we gat the first band recording of "Moodswing Whiskey", and another go at "I Woke Up In A Strange Place". Jeff's vocal experiments are the highlight here: never satisfied with performing the same song the same way twice, he vocalises extemporaneously all over the place, and the audience eats it up.

After the first two less-than-satisfying recordings on the tour, it's a pleasure to bring you a substantial improvement. The recording itself is a good one. It was poorly tracked, so I re-tracked it, but apart from that, and the usual housekeeping, I present it to you as uploaded to Dime a dozen years ago by superfan Dolphinsmile.
