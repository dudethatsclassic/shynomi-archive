From Rennes, the band traveled west across France to Strasbourg, close to the German border, where an enthusiastic audience was on hand at La Laiterie, a 900-seater on the Rue du Hohwald, still packing them in today: https://www.artefact.org/la-laiterie/programmation

It was St Valentine's Day, a fact revisited often throughout the evening, production some inspired "Jeff-ness", and a couple of unusual teases: we get a bizarre Valentine's rendition of Zappa's Dangerous Kitchen - or a bit of it anyway, and a much longer Jimi pastiche, complete with what sounds like Jeff playing guitar with his teeth! Jeff again wows the French crowd with one of his Piaf tributes, and the show is capped with a gorgeous and rather unique "Hallelujah"

I received this in a CD trade many years ago, and apart from some housekeeping, present it to you as-is.
