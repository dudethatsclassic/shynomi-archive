After Glastonbury, it was a short hop across the channel to France, where the band played the Theatre Municipal in Tourcoing, before heading to Petit Quevilly in the suburbs of Rouen, to play in the intimate setting of the 200-seat EXO 7, sadly shuttered and demolished in 2007, after 30 years of concerts and other events.

The unknown taper who provides us with this excellent, well-balanced recording, got a great capture. The crowd is respectful - the French clearly LOVED Jeff - it's clear from the response he gets at every show played there. You can get an idea of the small size of the venue, both from the pictures in the upload and the sound: Matt's drums are thunderous. 

No real surprises in this full-length set, but everything we get is first rate. Hallelujah gets a dreamy guitar intro, and instead of The Smiths, we get Edith Piaf for the French crowd to close the main set. The band return for a raucous Vancouver/Kanga-Roo. The former is still wordless at this point. 

I got this in an trade many years ago, and present it, with the usual housekeeping, as-is.
