Jeff's catalogue reissues, being such a mess of poorly thought-out, magpie-gathered compilations, tracks from these sessions have appeared all over the place, most notably of course on Grace itself, along with the deluxe reissue edition, the Peyote Radio Theatre promo EP, and elsewhere, making them a proper dogs' dinner to try to gather together. A truly respected artist would have seen a proper "Complete Grace Sessions" release in a nice boxed set. Ah well: maybe the 50th anniversary release that most of us won't live to see.

That said, there are many, many bootlegs in circulation purporting to be 'complete' sessions from Grace, outtakes from those sessions, etc etc etc. I myself had eight different collections, including the professional bootleg, "Born Again From The Rhythm" which is heavily brickwalled on CD, so I chose not to use those versions. Instead, most of these came from a fan-created 2-CD compilation I received about 25 years ago as a snail-mail trade (remember B&Ps?) From that, I have extracted these 14 tracks. I have removed everything that's been released commercially. Sadly, this means losing the studio chatter and jokes which were excised

Jeff, Mick and Matt, accompanied by Andy Wallace and engineer Cliff Norrell, decamped to upstate New York and the calm of Bearsville Studios in Woodstock to undertake recordings for what would become Grace. The band stayed there through much of September and October, producing around three dozen tracks altogether. 

Here are all the ones I have that have not been released by Sony in any format. I hope you enjoy.
