After Lupo's, the band played the Trocadero in Philly before heading west to Pittsburgh's Metropol, a longtime fixture of the Strip, which sadly closed in the early aughts. Jeff was opening on and off for Juliana Hatfield, formerly of the Blake Babies and, briefly, Evan Dando's Lemonheads, so the longer sets are Jeff's headlining nights and the shorter ones, like this one, are from his opening dates.

This is a pretty standard set: the band only had an hour, and they fill it well, skipping Jeff's solo spot and the encores, but we get the rest of Grace, apart from the Britten, which was dropped from the set in 1994. The highlight is probably a lovely take on "Lover". 

This is an ok recording, sadly well-removed from the master, and bears the usual high gen hiss, but it's quite listenable, despite that. I got this from Buckley super-fan AintNoBody's 2006 Dime upload, and apart from some basic housekeeping, present it to you as-is.
