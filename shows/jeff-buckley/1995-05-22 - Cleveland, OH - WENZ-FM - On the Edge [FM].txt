From Pittsburgh, it was a short hop over the border to Cleveland for an afternoon session at WENZ-FM, for their "On The Edge" programme. This is a short and sweet 20-minute set, but a nice listen. I received this in a trade many many years ago as one continuous track, which started, mid-sentence, in the unnamed DJ's intro, and cut out just as suddenly at the end of "Lover". I split the tracks and added fades to the intro & the end using Audacity.
