After their Miami Beach appearance, the band played dates in Georgia, Tennessee, and North Carolina before his appearance at the 9:30 Club, still going strong for nearly 35 years, on V Street. When Jeff played, it was still at the old, FAR smaller location on F Street, where less than 200 lucky punters were there to see. As we listen to these cherished recordings, we sometimes forget just how small some of these venues were, and just how few people were lucky enough to catch a live Buckley performance over the less than 500-odd dates he played.

This is a good recording of a spirited show, sadly not that close to the master, from the sound, but listenable enough. The set is standard for the 94 tour, with few surprises: we get a gorgeous Hallelujah (not often played at this point), and you are right in the middle of the crowd to hear it. I hope you enjoy. Contemporaneous interview here: https://notwithyoubutofyou.blogspot.com/2018/02/the-life-ahead.html
