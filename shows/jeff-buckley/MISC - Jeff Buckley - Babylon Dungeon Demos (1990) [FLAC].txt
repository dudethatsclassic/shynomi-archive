Tracks 01-06:
Jeff Buckley - Guitar & Vocals
W/unknown accompaniment


* 'Unforgiven' is the initial working title for 'Last Goodbye'. The instruments, lyrics and vocal melody of the song are a bit different.

+ 'Strawberry Street' previously unreleased, originally written in 1988 - is one of the first songs penned by Jeff. There are three versions in circulation. The earliest a capella version dates back to these sessions. The second was recorded during the Shelter Island sessions which also produced the material found on the You And I and In Transition releases, and can be found there. The final version (track 7) was recorded in a rehearsal at The Knitting Factory, NY, in May of 1993, with an early try-out combo of bassist Tom Goodkind (of The Washington Squares) and drummer John McNally. That version can be found on the Grace Legacy edition as well as on a blue vinyl 7" offered with later pressings of Grace on vinyl.
