Notes: 
* This was night 2 of an adventure of epic proportions: not only did I get to see one of my all-time favorite bands live twice 
  in one weekend, I also got to meet some awesome people who I'd known for ages but never met in the flesh. Call me crazy for 
  flying into NYC for just 2 days for this, but I haven't regretted it for one second before, during, or after the event. In 
  fact: my ears are still ringing! I haven't recorded the first NYC show on 2012-08-24 @ the Bowery Ballroom: I was too busy 
  going nuts in the pit. I chose to watch from the side for the second night and record for all of you!
* Being a total fanboy for these guys, mastering this recording I couldn't help but notice the band isn't the tight unit they 
  were back in the day, but don't let that get in the way of enjoying the music: it's still awesome! And it's not as if they're 
  not trying...Walter screaming his lungs out, TC3 annoyed whenever his pedal board was almost destroyed by a stagediver (and 
  there were A LOT of those!), Sergio jumping around, Alan's tight drumming...these guys are just awesome... Do yourself a HUGE 
  favor and catch one of their shows of you can...
