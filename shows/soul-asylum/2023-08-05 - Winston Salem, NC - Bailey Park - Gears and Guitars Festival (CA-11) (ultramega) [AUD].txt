Soul Asylum headlined Saturday night at the Gears and Guitars Festival and put on a damn fine show. For the most part the crowd stays in the background of the capture and doesn't really intrude. I was kinda concerned because just before Soul Asylum came on these effin' geezers placed themselves in the gap between where I was sitting and the row of spectators in front of me and talked the entire time. Sometimes yelling to be heard. Hell, one even kept his fingers in his ears for most of the show. WTF! Anyway, for the first couple of tracks the sound guys were still making adjustments and its a little hot at first but evens out after that. Between songs Dave tells some jokes and mentions the cycle race going.
