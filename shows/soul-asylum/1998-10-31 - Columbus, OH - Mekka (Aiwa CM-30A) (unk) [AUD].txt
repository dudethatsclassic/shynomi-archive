This was a very strange evening. The Mekka wasn't around very long, but it was an old warehouse or factory or something that was transformed into a techno/goth dance club. Soul Asylum showed up on Halloween for quite a party. They were all decked out in costumes (I don't remember who was dressed up as what), but Catwoman would bring out a new guitar every once in a while. I don't remember too many people being at the show, and those that were there weren't really into the band. I think they were there more for the costume party. Me, I was definitely there for the show, had a blast and a bunch to drink, and really enjoyed the show.
