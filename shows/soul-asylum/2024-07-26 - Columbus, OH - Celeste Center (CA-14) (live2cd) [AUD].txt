Notes:

Opening for Stone Temple Pilots. [recorded].

My first time seeing Soul Asylum since February 2020 right before COVID hit.
I honestly was not that impressed with them then, but this night was different.
They were firing on all cylinders and I was left wanting them to play one or
two more songs. Sadly, they only had 45 minutes. The majority of the audience
stayed seated for this show, so I walked 2 seats over to the end of my row
and stood part way in the aisle to not block any views directly behind me. 
The right stack was slightly to my left, so the sound leans that way. I did
adjust the balance of the channels to try to correct this on playback. It is
barely noticable, but wanted to mention it. The sound is usually great in The
Celeste Center, however, on this night it was only really good. I have made
far better recordings from this exact location before.
