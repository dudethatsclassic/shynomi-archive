This appears to be uncirculated and also misdated.  The cassette was marked "Feb 8 Athens, GA" but the band was in Philadelphia on the 8th.  It was in Athens on February 13 (at the University's Tate Center) and 14 (Club 688).  There's nothing on the recording to indicate venue or date.  So, the 13th or 14th could be correct.  

The recording is up front and loud, as you might except from a small theater or club.  The levels take a few moments to settle in.  There was a strange channel imbalance during Can't Go Back, which has been fixed.
