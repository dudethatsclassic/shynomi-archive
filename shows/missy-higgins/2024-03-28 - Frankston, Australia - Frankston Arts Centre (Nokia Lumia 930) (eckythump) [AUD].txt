Show notes: Missy's first show of her sold out Australian tour, with a first set performing solo new songs and rarities, and the second set performing The Sound Of White in its entirety, albeit in a slightly more friendly concert order compared to the album which puts the big hits early in the album. A fantastic show with a very enthusiastic crowd. With all the chatting between songs and a 30 minute interval between sets the show was 3 hours long, definite value for money. This is the only show of the tour I know of that featured an encore after The Sound Of White, this show was so long already presumably Missy decided she didn't need to do one for the rest of the tour, but being show 1 we got a little bonus.

Recording notes: Sounds excellent, the Frankston Arts Centre is a small theatre with nice acoustics and I was 6 rows back in the center. Enjoy!
