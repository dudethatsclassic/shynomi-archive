Show notes: After seeing opening night of the tour in Frankston, I caught up with the tour again 5 weeks down the track in the much larger Palais Theatre in St Kilda. Perhaps to be expected, compared to opening night this performance seemed a little slicker, with 15 minutes trimmed off the running time to bring it down to 2 hours and 15 not including the interval between acts (no encore, 2 fewer songs in Act 1 and slightly less Missy talking, but only slightly). Missy brought out some different special guests across the shows at the Palais, including Angie McMahon and Kate Miller-Heidke (more shows were added in June to keep up with the demand), with tonight's crowd being treated to Missy's brother David coming out to play piano on 2 songs which was quite special. Somewhere along the line in the last 20 years Missy became elevated to Australian icon status, she is truly adored as you can hear by how loud the audience was reacting to every song as well as the very loud singalong to Scar, and The Sound Of White holds a special place in many people's lives. A truly special night of music.

Recording notes: An excellent sounding recording, nicely mastered by Matt, whose father sadly passed away earlier in the day of this show, with The Sound Of White taking on extra special meaning hearing it performed on this night. This one goes out to him. Some photos Matt took are also included. Enjoy!
