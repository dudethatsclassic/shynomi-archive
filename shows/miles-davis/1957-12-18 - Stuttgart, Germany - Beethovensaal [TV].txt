Notes:

- A good TV broadcast recording with minor audible hiss. Tracks are 
discontinuous and d1t03 is incomplete. There is a small splice in d1t01
at 0:051.575 that can be heard as a minor “blip” in the Miles solo. No 
other notable flaws. Tracks d1t02 and d1t03 feature Miles playing some 
inspired solos interspersed with the big band sound of the Erwin Lehn 
Orchestra. 

- According to Peter Losin, this show is known to circulate in various 
versions with 4 different fragments of the incomplete d1t03 "Walkin'".  
Apparently what is in this seed is the most complete version
with no voiceovers, listed as 4, below (all timings approximate):

1. Walkin' (R. Carpenter) (rehearsal)	0:55
With voiceover: Unknown announcer (0:06-0:21), René Urtreger's wife 
talking about Davis's 1957 visit to Europe (0:21-0:55).

2. Walkin' (R. Carpenter) (fragment)	1:56
With voiceover: Mrs. Urtreger continues (1:35-1:56).

3. Walkin' (R. Carpenter) (fragment)	0:58
With voiceover: Unknown announcer (0:53-0:58).

4. Walkin' (R. Carpenter) (incomplete)	2:17
