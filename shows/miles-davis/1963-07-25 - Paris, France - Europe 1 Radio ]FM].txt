Notes:

- d1t01: There is a two second gap at he end of this track leading into the next.

- It looks as though this date also has joshua on record as being played. This track is 
missing from this source. 

- The sound is muffled and distant on this recording. The playing is very intmate and 
subtle. A great specimen if you can forgive the mediocre sound of the recording. 

- On their way to the Antibes Festival, the Davis played one night at the Théâtre du 
Champs-Élysées. The fragment of "Joshua" is from a different source (and the tape is 
slightly faster) than the other two titles. The Quintet must have played a 
50-60 minute set, but this is all that has surfaced so far. - Credit to Peter Losin, 
http://www.plosin.com/
