Notes:
This matrix version is incomplete; missing are the first 2 minutes and the final two tracks, Burn, and the encore, Maze. The first few bars of Burn are at the end of track 10.

This is the second last Miles Davis concert with Robben Ford on guitar; Ford played his final gig with Miles the following night in Detroit.
