- d1t01: This track is not complete according to the timings on Peter Losins site. 

- d1t02: This track is not complete according to the timings on Peter Losins site.

- d1t04: Drop out at :09 through :14. 

- d1t05: It sounds like the last few notes are cut on this track

- d2t01: The first few bars may be cut from this track at the beginning and end.

- d2t02: It sounds like the first few bars are possibly cut from the beginning of 
this track and the end of the track.

- d2t04: The sound changes drastically from d2t03 to d2t04. This may be a different 
source or at least heavily worked on. The end of the track is cut also.

- d2t05: The beginning of this track is cut. 

- This source is most likely derived from Tempo di Jazz CDTJ 703. 

- There is a steady Hum thats pretty noticeable in the quiet sections of the recording
,a slight hiss, and some low end distortion. The recording is still very listenable.
The other source for this date is LL170 which has a nicer sound, but looks to be 
from an inferior, possibly even lossy lineage. It is also less complete than this version. 
If your picking and choosing, this would be my pick of the two.  

- According to Peter Losin, there is an intro that is not included on this source.
