Notes:

- d1t01: There are instances of light static throughout this track

- d1t02: There are some light static bursts at :07, :09, :14, 8:41, 8:43. There 
is a signifigant drop in levels at 4:44 - 4:46, and 4:58. The track fades as the music 
is still playing

- d1t03: There is a tapping sound starting at about 4:40 and ending when the tempo drops 
back at 6:44. It sounds as though it's under the music, so I'm not sure exactly what the 
source is. It doesn't sound like a natural occurance in the music but I have my doubts it 
digi noise, or static as it is usually right on the beat. 

- d1t04: This track also has the tapping sound from the start to about the one minute mark. 
It alsmost seems as though it's may be the high hat cymbal causing the noise somehow. Slight
drop out at 2:33, 5:21, 7:10- 7:12, 8:43 . 

- Not a bad source. A little hollow sounding and heavy on the high end, but very 
listenable. All tracks have a 2 second gap between play. 

- According to Peter Losin's site, this date should also have "All of you" and "The 
Theme" but they are missing from this source. 

-  "So What" was apparently only broadcast in this incomplete form. Tapes in general 
circulation include incomplete versions of "All of You" (10:52) and "Joshua" (9:18). 
The "Miles Impro" included on Moon MCD 021 is an excerpt of "Joshua" from Stockholm, 
October 3 -- not from this concert."- Credit to Peter Losin, http://www.plosin.com/
