- Thanks to Peter Losin for supplying the source. 

- "These tunes are usually dated (as on Philology) sometime in 1950, but according to 
Norman Saks the correct date is January 19, 1951. From the two tunes with trumpet solos
it seems likely that the trumpet player is Davis, but it may be Red Rodney. Davis and
Parker did get together for a studio session on January 17 (the "Au Privave" session for
Mercury), so maybe they shared the stage at the Diplomat."- Credit to Peter Losin, 
http://www.plosin.com/
