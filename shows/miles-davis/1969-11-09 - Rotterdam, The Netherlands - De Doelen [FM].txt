- According to Peter Losin, there are a few other versions of this out there. 
There is the original VARA 1969 broadcast, a BBC Radio rebroadcast, and a
digital XM broadcast. Both the BBC and the XM are supposed to be better in 
quality than this one which I believe is the longer version described below.
The track timing are all I have to compare and none match any of the timings
provided by Peter at Miles Ahead.   

- There are at least two older VARA broadcasts of this material. The longer one (A) 
begins with a short studio introduction (titles are announced as "Directions," "
Listen to This," "About That Time," and "Masqualero"), and contains an incomplete 
version of "Bitches Brew" (a splice at 8:27 excises 8:29-11:19 of complete version). 
The shorter broadcast (B) is sonically better and includes a Michiel de Ruyter studio 
introduction (0:51 -- titles announced as "Listen to This" and "About That Time"); 
Bitches Brew (12:36); I Fall in Love Too Easily (2:33); Sanctuary (3:55); It's About 
That Time (3:59); closing announcement (0:16).- Credit to Peter Losin, 
http://www.plosin.com/

A So What CDR is mastered from the first broadcast (A), but the tracks are wrongly 
indexed: 1. Introduction (0:12); 2. Directions (9:51); 3. Bitches Brew (9:44); 4. 
I Fall in Love Too Easily (2:36); 5. Sanctuary (4:02); 6. It's About That Time (16:58); 
7. Masqualero (14:49)."- Credit to Peter Losin, http://www.plosin.com/
