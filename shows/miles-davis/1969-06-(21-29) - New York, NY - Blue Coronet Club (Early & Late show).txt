Notes:

- d1t01: The track starts cut and has some level issues and small dropouts throughout. 

- The levels are much hotter in this source from LL145, Almost to the point of oversaturation at times causing 
small dropouts and distorion. While the added clarity compared to LL145 is nice, I'm more partial to adding a 
little volume to LL144 and not hearing the boomy bass and overpowering cymbals. I don't hear much of a speed 
correction between this source and LL145 either so I'm guessing there is another source out there yet that 
runs slow according to Peter's notes.  

- "I'm not sure of the date of this music. It circulates with the date "April 1969," but 
this date is pretty clearly wrong. There are two alternatives. The Quintet was booked 
at the Coronet June 21-29, and again in October (probably October 6-12)." 
- Credit to Peter Losin, http://www.plosin.com/

- "The later engagement ended early when Davis was shot after the October 8 performance. 
According to one report, "Miles Davis had appeared at the Blue Coronet Club October 8, 
finishing his last set on the morning of October 9. He drove back to New York with the 
lovely Marguerite Eskridge, 24, as a passenger. He pulled up in front of her house on 
141 E. 13th St. at 4:30, when a cab drove up alongside the car and someone inside 
fired. One of the bullets grazed Davis' side. The young woman was not injured." 
After being treated at the hospital, Davis was booked for possession of marijuana. 
As a result, remaining shows at the Blue Coronet were canceled. The Quintet was also 
booked at Boston's Jazz Workshop (October 13-16), but these shows may have been canceled 
as well." - Credit to Peter Losin, http://www.plosin.com/

"My guess, based on the titles performed and the playing, is that these tunes are 
from the early summer 1969, a very busy time for the Davis Quintet: Village Gate, 
New York (April 25-26, May 23-24, and July 29-August 10); Plugged Nickel Club, Chicago 
(June 4-14); Blue Coronet Club, Brooklyn (June 21-29); Morgan State Jazz Festival, Baltimore 
(June 22); Newport Festival (July 4); Central Park, New York (July 7); Juan-les-Pins Festival, 
Antibes (July 25-26); Rutgers University Stadium, New Brunswick (July 27); Sheraton Park Hotel, 
French Lick (French Lick Jazz Festival) (July 31); The Spectrum, Philadelphia (August 15); 
Grant Park Theater, Chicago (August 22); Crosley Field, Cincinnati (Ohio Jazz Festival) 
(August 23)."- Credit to Peter Losin, http://www.plosin.com/
