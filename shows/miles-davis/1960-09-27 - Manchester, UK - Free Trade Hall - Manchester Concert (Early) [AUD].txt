Notes:

- There are small pockets of static and level fluctuations present throughout this 
recording. 

- Peter Losin and miles-trees both list this as an audience recording. The sound and a 
spectral analysis both lean me toward a soundboard recording. 

- This was released on a bootleg record as a soundboard. 

- d1t03: The track fades at the end with stitt still wailing away. I would have to say 
there was more played than what was recorded.

- This is a fantastic recording of a very tight performance. A must have for any collection
