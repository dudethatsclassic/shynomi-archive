Notes:

- d1t03 has a few seconds cut at the end.

- some static/hiss present, minimal. May be vinyl artifacts.

- d1t02. Adderley out.

- Broadcast on Mutual's Bandstand USA, which aired live on Saturday nights beginning in 1956.

- In the notes to JMY (Jazz Music Yesterday) 6402, Enrico Merlin places this session at an unknown venue on June 30, 1958 (a Monday). The Sextet was booked at Washington's Spotlite Lounge on June 10-15 (Tuesday-Sunday) and August 5-10 (Tuesday-Sunday); a broadcast is known to have been made on Saturday, August 9, and this is probably it.
