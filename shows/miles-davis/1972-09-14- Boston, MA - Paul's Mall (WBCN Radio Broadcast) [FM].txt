This does not appear to be on any of the usual trackers at this time.

From track 3 onwards, the balance was noticeably off - with a heavy bias to the right channel.
It could be argued the bias was in place on track 2 - however the instrumentation 
across the stereo picture on listening meant that for that track, Black Satin, I left well alone.

For track 3 to track 6 I have raised the gain in the left channel to balance things out.

No EQing was done, it sounds fine as it is I felt. Personally I didn't find this the most
exciting Miles Davis concert - I have just been listening to a lot of 1970-71 shows - but the energy
was there, and I thought of a jazzy Velvet Underground at times (who I love).

Anyway there a lot of Miles fans out there, so I thought why not share.
