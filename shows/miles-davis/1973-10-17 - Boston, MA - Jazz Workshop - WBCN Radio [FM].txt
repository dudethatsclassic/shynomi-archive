October 17, 1973 (3 items, 58:40)
Jazz Workshop, Boston
WBCN radio broadcast
Miles Davis Septet

Miles Davis (tpt, org); Dave Liebman (ss, ts, fl); Pete Cosey (g, perc); Reggie Lucas (g); Michael Henderson (el-b); Al Foster (d); James Mtume Foreman (cga, perc)
 


Ife (M. Davis) 34:05 
Agharta Prelude (M. Davis) 13:16 
Foster and Mtume play the "Tune in 5" percussion vamp from 2:42-7:10, while Henderson, Lucas, and the soloists continue with "Agharta Prelude." Incomplete (5:30) and issued as "What About Rhythm" on LFYE 002. 
Zimbabwe (M. Davis) 11:19 


 
The Davis Septet was booked for several nights at the Jazz Workshop in mid-October 1973, and there is an audience recording of two sets from another show during this engagement. Given the tunes performed here, this is probably from the second of two sets on this date.

A week earlier, Liebman took a couple days off to record Lookout Farm at Generation Sound Studios in New York (October 10-11).
