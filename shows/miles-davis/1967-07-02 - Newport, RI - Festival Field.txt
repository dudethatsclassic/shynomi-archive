Notes:

- d1t01: This track has multiple instances of small jitters, distortion, and level fluctuation
 too many to list. All are pretty minor. 

- d1t02: This track is also subject to the same types of flaws as listed above. at 7:57, there
is a sever drop in levels.

- d1t03: The level drop from d1t02 carry's over to this track until 1:12. Then the levels return
to normal. At 3:42, a warble becomes very noticable and carry's through to the end of the track.
at about 4:10 the sound recording gets nearly inaudible through the flaws until the song is cut. 

- Not a bad sounding source. There are some level fluctuations, slight distortion and very small jitters, 
but overall, a listenable source. This source is at the original speed. LL121 has the "speed correction".
