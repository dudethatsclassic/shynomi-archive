- this is a degraded soundboard recording, not an audience recording

- Plosin's site also says that an audience tape of this concert was broadcast on KPFA in the spring of 1971

- there is some clipping

- the instruments are well separated into left or right for the most part

- there is no sense of a common mic location - each instrument appears balanced in the recording

- unlike the prior night, the hiss is more prominant and the audio signal rolls off around 8 khz

- d1t01: fades in. Plosin's site references a Bill Graham intro of ~ 0:04 which is not on this recording

- d1t02: 8:36 thru 9:09 many little pops and crackle

- d1t03 is a nice version of "What I Say" which highlights the ensemble support of the various lead instruments 

- d1t05: abruptly cuts off

- Opened for Leon Russell. 

- there is another version of this source and at some point both had a common ancestor. That source had additional padding at the start of track 1 and small gaps betwen tracks indicating a poor extraction, most likely.
