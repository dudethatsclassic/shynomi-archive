- d1t01: The first 5 seconds are pretty rough. Drop outs at 2:34, 2:40, and 3:13.

- d1t02: Digi pop at 8:04

- d1t06: Digi pop at :27, 2:59. Drop out at  2:38 - 2:44 and 5:45 - 5:52

- d1t07: This track starts with a Ghost into of sorts. Between  2:40 - 2:43
the sound gets a bit rough.

- d1t08: There is a rough spot at 1:58 - 2:03. Digi pop at 2:24 and 3:46. 

- d1t09: is cut at the end of the track. 

- Overall, a very nice sounding source. No hiss and minimal flaws. The playing is 
very tight. In some of the quite parts of the recording and the longer drop outs, 
can hear an echo of sorts that is actually the proceeding measures of the show. The
only differences between LL141 and LL142 are that I retracked and flaced. A few small 
occurances of dead air and the 10 or so minutes of nothing at then end of LL141 are 
deleted on this version. Since  there really is no difference aside from tracking the 
original .ape files, we won't be seeding LL142.
