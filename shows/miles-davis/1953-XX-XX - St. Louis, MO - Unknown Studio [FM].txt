Notes:

- The playbacks of officially released material have been removed. 

- The normal slight hiss and crackles are present with a small amount of low end 
distortion, but overall, a nice sounding specimen of Miles talking about his life and 
how he came to be where he was. 

- "The nightly Fresh Air program was broadcast live on KXLW at 7:30. In the course 
of the interview, Frost plays several records: "Miles Away" by Rolf Ericsson's Swinging 
Swedes, "Milestones" with Charlie Parker (August 14, 1947), "Godchild" (January 21, 1949),
"Hallucinations" (Royal Roost, September 4, 1948), and "Woody 'n' You" (May 2, 1952)."
- Credit to Peter Losin, http://www.plosin.com/

- "Fixing the date of this interview is very hard. According to the radio listings 
in the St. Louis Post-Dispatch, the first Fresh Air broadcast was on Tuesday, June 23, 
1953, and the show aired nightly (except Sunday) until Saturday, August 29, 1953. Frost 
mentions that John Cotter's group is playing at the Juke Club "tomorrow, Wednesday through 
Sunday" -- the interview took place on a Tuesday, probably in July or August 1953. 
I'm grateful to Chris DeVito for help with this interview." - Credit to Peter Losin, 
http://www.plosin.com/
