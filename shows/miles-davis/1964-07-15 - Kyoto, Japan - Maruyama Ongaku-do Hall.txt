speed correction:
side 1 (tracks 1.-3.) needed to be sped up gradually 130 to 160 cts,
side 2 between 70 and 100 cts, with lots of local fluctuations to be worked away, I may have overlooked 
some ...

that much discussed diginoise glitch in track 2 at around 5.47 is removed, you'll be hearing a mere plop ...

overall volume adjustment, to make it all sound more even in both channels.

- A very nice sounding aud with a very minor hiss! Herbie Hancock is very low in the mix in the beginning of the recording 
but this gets better as the recording moves on. This source has a little brighter level that either LL113, 
or LL114. The diginoise in d1t02 has been smoothed, the pitch has been adjusted, and some level adjustments 
made. The sound quality on all three versions of this source are very close. The sound, for a Miles aud 
for this period is very nice.
