continue so I'm guessing autumn leaves is incomplete.

on the first two tracks.digi pop/static at :21,:46, 1:56,9:20, 11:29. Sounds as though 
the last bars are missing from this track.

on the first three tracks. Digi pop/static at 4:30, 5:22, 6:36, 6:45

- d1t05: This track also has a lot of the same type of distortion and static heard 
on the first four tracks.

- There is a decent hiss on these. Lots of static/digipops and distortion. But it's 
still a a bearable source. One point of interest, most of the appluase on this source
really sound unatural. My guess is that most is editied in.
