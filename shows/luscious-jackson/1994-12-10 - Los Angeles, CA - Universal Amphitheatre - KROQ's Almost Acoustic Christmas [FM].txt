Notes: Acoustic Christmas is an annual concert run by the Los Angeles radio station KROQ. Since its inception in 1990, the concert has attracted increasingly popular alternative rock bands and singers. It takes place over a weekend in mid-December, and has in recent years been broadcast for free on KROQ's web site.

Every year it has taken place at Gibson Amphitheatre (what was formally referred to as the Universal Amphitheatre), in Universal City, except for 1998 where it was held at Shrine Auditorium, in Los Angeles and in 1999 where it was held at the Arrowhead Pond, in Anaheim. If you listen close you can hear Jill jokingly say that "City Song" is about Universal City…usually this song is dedicated to New York where the band is from. 

The disc that these two tracks were extracted from was a Westwood One In Concert: New Rock Semi-Acoustic Special Part 2 Show #95-20 which was originally broadcast the week of May 8 1995.
