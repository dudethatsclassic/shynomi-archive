For those that don't know the CMA Festival is the longest running music show of its kind bringing top name country acts and up and coming new artists to about 85,000 people that descend on Nashville.  The main concerts run Thursday to Sunday but over the years they've added quite a bit and you could spend the whole week there.  Each night maybe 7 or 8 of the top artists perform and do maybe 5 or 6 songs each. Many of the artists also do meet-and-greets during the day or just show up at a bar on Broadway.

This was my second CMA Fest having taken my daughter back in 2008.  This time we added the wife.  It was in the upper 90's and hot as hell.  But it didn't seem to interfere with the fun everyone had.

I'm not a huge country fan but do enjoy the country rock, southern rock and old school guys.  Chris Stapleton fits in the last category for me.  Our seats were club level to the right of the stage.  Not soundboard quality sound but not too bad for just the internal mics.  I edited out about a 90 second hesitation after the announcer introduction until the first song started.
