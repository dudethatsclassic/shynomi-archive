Comments:  The Glastonbury show appears on this cd in it's full glittering entirety.  The show was either sourced from an FM broadcast or the soundboard because the sound is really excellent.  Courtney is in a good mood - you can just imagine her bounding around onstage in her wings and layers of pink tulle.  She picks people out of the crowd as usual, citing one girl that needed to be up on the stage because she "looked disturbed".  The band throws in a nice classic "Beautiful Son" and the new version of "Good Sister, Bad Sister"  is really cool with a bunch of rambling improvised lyrics.  Courtney goes on and on about how she's so proud of England for it's anarchy... On "Celebrity Skin" one lucky girl gets to sing a bit with Clove.  Tracks 15-17 are from a beauuutiful acoustic session in Paris.  They are simply awesome.  Track 18 is Hole's great debut performance on the David Letterman Show.
