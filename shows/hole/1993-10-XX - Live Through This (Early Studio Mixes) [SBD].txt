HOLE - 10/93 Triclops Sound Studios, Atlanta, Georgia
------------------------------------------------------

This is an early mix of Hole's Album "Live Through This", including the outtakes "Old Age"
and "Rockstar". You'll surely notice several differences compared to the final mix.
There are also some between song studio noises and count-in's on here not found anywhere else.
This tape was owned by Hole's bassist Kristen Pfaff and is thought to be of pretty low generation.
Mixed by Sean Slade.

Generation: CASS(x) > Flac 
Transferred on a NAK Deck and other high quality equipment. Tracked with EAC.
