Notes;
My first memories on this show are the terrible drive back home after the concert. It was extremely foggy weather and practically impossible to see. I remember vividly that I had to drive with the windows of the car down, hoping to hear upcoming traffic. Luckily I made it home safe. About the concert: It wasn’t busy in the venue, guess only about 200 people. Dutch girl punk band Bambix opened the night. At some stage, you hear Courtney Cobain asking the audience for a ride to Amsterdam to see her boyfriend.  It happened that Nirvana was playing in Amsterdam that night… On the tape, you can hear a lady offering Courtney the ride. This girl was a member from Bambix (Sorry Bambix girl, cannot remember who you were).
Somewhere in a box, I should still have the handwritten set list by Courtney.
This tape has seen a limited release in the cassette tape circuit back then. At some stage I owned a silver disk bootleg cd of the show, taken from my master. However, that cd version had a considerable amount of hiss. Which makes me think it was an x-generated copy down the track. I may, or may not have traded this show in cdr format but again, it would only have been a handful of times, if all
