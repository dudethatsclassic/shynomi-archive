Hole - 1991 12 04 Rose Club, Koln

Wednesday 4th Dec 1991
Rose Club, Köln, Germany

Big thanks to Buenaventura for this recording. Nearly 30 years later, I'd say listening to this is still harrowing.  The band is very raw (purposefully so), and lots of things that the listener can pick-up in hindsight. They tease Teen Spirit after Violet and right before Where Did You Sleep Last Night ("Hello, Hello ...") This was the time Kurt and Courtney started their romance.

Hole was:
Courtney Love – lead vocals, rhythm guitar 
Eric Erlandson – lead guitar 
Caroline Rue – drums 
Jill Emery – bass

Taper note:
Listening back to this, 29 years later and not having listened to it in the years in between, I'm trying to remember whether the rawness and the abrasiveness shocked or fascinated me. Like all the venues hole played on this part of their tour, the Rose Club was pretty small. Ii don't think it was particularly packed. 

Transfer note:
Left channel was a bit weaker than the right, so that was brought up. Tape flip after Burn Black. Removed about 6 digi-clicks on track 13.
