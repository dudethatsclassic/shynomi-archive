Comments:    Courtney dedicates the set to Kristen Pfaff and then they play a slow sad "Sugar Coma".  Frances is at the show and Courtney gets the crowd to all say hi to her, it's really cute.  She tells everyone a story about "Softer Softest" and the specific girl it was written about.  They do  messy versions of  "Best Sunday Dress", "Whose Porno You Burn (Black)", "I Think That I Would Die"... but the rawness feels soul-baring.  You can tell it's still the first couple months of tour for the band.  Other highlights include a brief veer into "Gold Dust Woman" which was never performed live elsewhere, a gorgeous solo performance by Clove of "Pennyroyal Tea" with the crowd singing along,  and the always lovely "Drunk In Rio"       with improvised lyrics from "Best Sunday Dress" and "Only Rape I Know" all thrown in.   I've done some noise reduction and hiss removal on this show to improve sound quality.
