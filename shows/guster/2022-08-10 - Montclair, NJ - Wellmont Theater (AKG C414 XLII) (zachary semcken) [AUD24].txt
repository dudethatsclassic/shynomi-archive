Dave Butler was substituted by Jeremy Yaddaw for this performance.

A power outage occurred in the Montclair area for a little over 10 minutes during the performance. The venue's emergency lighting was able to keep the stage lit, allowing the band to assemble at the stage lip for an impromptu three song acoustic performance. During this section, occasional noise from the crew can be heard.

During "Doin' It By Myself", Ryan saw my mic stand towards the back of the orchestra, climbed on the chairs, and sang a portion of the song directly into my microphones. I attempted to correct the audio to make it sound more balanced, but it's still glaringly obvious.

My undying gratitude to the entire Guster crew, especially Brian & Jim, for going to bat for me with venue security and made this recording possible.

This recording is dedicated to all the tapers that have taught me to love archiving live music.
