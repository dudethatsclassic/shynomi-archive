NOTES:
This show was originally scheduled for 1/17/2019, but was rescheduled due to the band booking a television appearance on Late Night With Seth Myers. It was then announced that the band would be playing 2 sets that night which would include a full performance of the band's 1999 album 'Lost And Gone Forever' for it;s 20th Anniversary.

'Window' was performed in front of a giant screen while the original music video was being played.
-- Here's a video of the performance(thank you Curt):   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tkVq1ptM50U
--The original music video is here:           https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CEz0TgTyv8s

In-between sets, the original 1999 LAGF Electronic Press Kit was shown on the big screen (8-10 minutes long).

'Jesus On The Radio' was on the setlist but was not performed.
