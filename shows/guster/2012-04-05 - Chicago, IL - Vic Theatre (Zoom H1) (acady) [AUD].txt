Guster

Vic Theatre
Chicago, IL 
April 5, 2012

Taper: acady

Location: Center Stage, about 5th row (Zoom mounted to a railing about waist height)

Lineage: Zoom H1 (44khz/16bit)(low cut off, auto level off) > Micro SDHC > PC > CD WAV (track split) > WAV > Trader's Little Helper > FLAC (level 8 encoding)

Guster performed a stripped down acoustic set with a small string section at the Vic Theatre. The setlist comprised deep cuts, b-sides, unique arrangements, and general unpredictability. Jeff Garlin of “Curb Your Enthusiasm” and Mat Edgar performed stand-up comedy before Guster. It was a special night of music and comedy. 

No editing has been done to this recording. This is not a spectacular recording, but certainly very listenable and captures the atmosphere of the show. Talking, screaming, and singing can be heard around me. Occasionally you can hear some bumps against the unit from where people were banging on the railing the unit was mounted to. Instruments and vocals are pretty clear, and thankfully, the recording isn't super bass-heavy.

Quality: 7.0/10 

ENJOY!
