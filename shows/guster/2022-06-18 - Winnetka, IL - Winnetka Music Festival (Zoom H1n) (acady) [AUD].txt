Notes:

- The Record Company opened.

- Ryan mentioned his father grew up in the neighboring village of Skokie, and when he learned the band was playing in Winnetka, he commented that Winnetka were “very fancy people”. 

- The afternoon of the show the band was invited to a guy's house for jambalaya and to swim in their pool. He thanked the people of Winnetka for making the band feel especially welcomed.

- The festival was a green event, asking for people to recycle and compost to help make it a zero waste event. Reverb would have been proud.
