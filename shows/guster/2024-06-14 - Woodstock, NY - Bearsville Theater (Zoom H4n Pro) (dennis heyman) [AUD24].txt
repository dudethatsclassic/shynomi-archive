Guster makes their triumphant return to Woodstock to perform at the Bearsville Theater. Ryan says they have spent a lot of time in Woodstock over the years. Four of the nine of their albums were recorded within 10 miles of the venue. 90% of Ooh La La was recorded in Woodstock. This is night one of two performances. Ryan announces they will be performing four sets over two nights without repeat
ing any songs. They will be digging deep into the catalog.
