SOLD OUT 'FREE' SHOW

This was a 'Free At Noon' press tour radio performance at WXPN in Philadelphia for the album release of Ooh La La. The band had some issues with the equipment truck transporting their gear overnight. Ryan said he got numerous texts last night about the issue and if he ignored them it would just fix itself. He woke up in the morning and all was good! They had to use a slimmed down back end and some rented gear. They used an XPN drum set and Brian killed it with a single conga, bongos and tambourine. They didn't have guitar mute pedals as could be heard by the loud pop as Ryan switched guitars.

Enjoy Guster fans!
