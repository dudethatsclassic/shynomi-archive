* with Nick Blaemire, Gerard Canonico, Sara Chase, Jenn Damiano, Damon
  Daunno, Van Hughes, Pomme Koch, and Rohan Kymal on vocals

The guest vocalists on Empire State are all Broadway performers who are
currently collaborating on a musical with Ryan. It is believed to be an
adaptation of 2012's "Safety Not Guaranteed", for which Ryan wrote the
original score.

Track 8 was an improv song about the importance of communication in a
band that has lasted for over 30 years.

Tracks 16-18 were performed "fireside" at the front of the stage, where
the band sat in chairs around a spraypainted cardboard fire and played
acoustic versions.

I had forgotten that the Beacon no longer allows mic stands, so I was
forced to handhold my microphones on their mount at chest height for the
entire show. I tried to minimize handling noise by only switching hands
during loud applause, but there is definitely more crowd noise than
usual at 5 feet than at the typical 8 or 9.
