Boston Public Radio hosts Jim Braude and Margery Eagan broadcast this
show live from the Newsfeed Cafe, and Guster joined them for a 40-minute
session of songs, interview segments, and a quiz on current events. 
In the band's honor, the cafe offered a "Cheasy Wonderful" sandwich.

As a mic stand would have been very obtrusive, I chose to clamp my mics
to the metal casing of the V3, which was in my taping bag on the ground,
about 30 feet away from the broadcast location. There were some small
speakers set up around the cafe but they weren't particularly strong, so
the interview segments can be somewhat hard to hear at times.
