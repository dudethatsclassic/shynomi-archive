Billed as "An Evening Of Acoustic Music & Improv," Guster performed an intimate concert with strings and Connor Ratliff from the Upright Citizen's Brigade Theatre MC'd and provided comedic anecdotes. This was such an interesting show to attend. 

No editing has been done to this recording. Talking, screaming, and singing can be heard around me. Thankfully, the vocals are clear and the recording is not overly bass-heavy.

ENJOY!
