Entire show performed with the National Symphony Orchestra, conducted by
Enrico López-Yáñez.

Orchestral debuts of Kid Dreams, When We Were Stars, and This Heart Is
Occupied.

After Black Balloon, Ryan read a statement from the band in support of
the cast and crew of the musical Finn, co-created by his friend Michael
Kooman along with Chris Nee and Christopher Dimond. Finn had debuted to
acclaim at the Kennedy Center, which was set to sponsor a nationwide
tour of the show in 2025. This tour was cancelled, with the decision
ascribed to financial reasons, but coincidentally, the creators were
informed of this only hours after the new board took over.

After Ryan's statement, the band welcomed Michael Kooman to the stage,
along with several of Finn's original cast members: Kalen Robinson,
Kara-Tameika Watkins, Caelyn Williams, Jamie Goodson, and Ben Ribler.
They joined the band to perform Hard Times, which was the only song that
did not feature the orchestra.

Recording was made very close to the stage, so it sounds more distant
than it actually was due to speaker placement. Crowd chatter is very
audible, and applause is louder than the music as a result of using 
body-mounted recording equipment.
