Notes:
1) I set up behind the soundboard because I didn't want to intrude on anyone's space. Unfortunately Gordon (sound) 
intentionally adjusted the mix so that it would sound best for those people sitting in front of the board (the majority of the audience).
The result is a loss of some high frequencies and some odd phasing at times. It isn't a fanstatic recording, but as far as I know, 
I was the only person recording, so this is the only copy available.

2) The band left the stage after the first encore and the houselights came on, so I stopped the recording. When I realized that they weren't done,
I started recording again. The result is that some of the crowd chanting/animal noise is missing, but none of the music is lost.

3) PA crackle on track 8 @ 4:57 normalized using Sound Forge 8
