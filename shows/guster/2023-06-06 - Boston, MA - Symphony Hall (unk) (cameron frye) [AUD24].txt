First performances at Symphony Hall since their original symphony shows
in June of 2005.

Entire set performed with the Boston Symphony Orchestra, conducted by
Keith Lockhart.

"Two At A Time" performed with the five winners of the Fidelity
Investments Young Artists Competition: William Ge (piano), Jack
Haroutunian (voice), Simona Karadoneva (voice), Brian Lee (cello), and
Aika Oki (violin).

"Jesus On The Radio" performed acoustic, stage right, with

Lockhart on shaker.

Second ever performances of "Black Balloon" and "Witness Tree".
