* with the Guster kids on percussion and general mayhem

Tracks 1-15 are Keep It Together in its entirety, in honor of its 20th
anniversary. The band created a giant RealPlayer window (look it up,
kids) and had their children carry it back and forth across the stage
between songs. They changed out the track display for each song,
complete with an ellipsis at the end of titles that were too long to fit
in the window. There was even a 30-second track of Silence before the
"hidden track" of Two At A Time.

Track 7 is a callout to a girl whose parents Ryan met after their
performance of the Violent Femmes' Blister In The Sun on a platform near
the port-a-potty cluster. She ended up being on someone's shoulders a
few dozen feet from the stage.

Track 18 is Ryan's reaction to Brian pointing out that 2024 would be the
30th anniversary of Parachute, followed by an open acknowledgment of
Dave Butler, the unofficial fifth member of Guster, and all that he
contributes to the band.

Track 22 is a tribute to the "amazing, amazing women in our lives that
are just really some of the baddest women that you'll ever meet in your
life, and we don't take it for granted that we get to run around the
world and play rock star and our shit gets taken care of at home, and I
just wanna say that very publicly."

Track 24 is a recognition of "our road family who's been running around
with us. There's so much stuff that goes on, and we just take it for
granted that everything's just gonna work when we stand up here. It's
invaluable to have these go-go-gadget arms, so we wanna thank them too."
