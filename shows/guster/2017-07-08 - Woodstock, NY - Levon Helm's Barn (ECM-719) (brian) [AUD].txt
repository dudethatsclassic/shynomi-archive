NOTES:
========
After playing primarily 50-minute sets opening for Dispatch in the Summer of 2017, 
Guster played a lengthy 27-song set at this intimate 200 person venue.

They also had the use of the venue's grand piano & Hammond B3 organ for the night.

Drummer Brian Rosenworcel's chilren were in attendance for this performance.
