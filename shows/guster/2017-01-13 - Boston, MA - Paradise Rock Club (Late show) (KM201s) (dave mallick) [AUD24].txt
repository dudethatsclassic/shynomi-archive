Notes:
- The second show of Guster's four-night run at the Paradise in honor of
  their 25th anniversary as a band and the 40th anniversary of the club.
  The band also played a short acoustic set on WGBH-FM at the Boston 
  Public Library earlier in the day.
- The last two songs of the main set were performed acoustic around a
  single microphone as a throwback to the band's Parachute days.
- "Tim!" was an improv song dedicated to fan Tim Gammel, who Ryan had
  joked around with earlier in the show when he identified a song based
  on the first chord.
- Many thanks to Guster sound engineer Dan for not only allowing me the
  ceiling clamp space above his console, but for complimenting me on it.
  I often get self-conscious about being obtrusive, so thanks!
