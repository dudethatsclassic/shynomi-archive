Guster played four nights at the Paradise Rock Club to celebrate their 25th Anniversary. They created four distinct shows that ranged from the early days to now. This is night three. There were only 7 songs on the setlist, the rest of the show was comprised by spinning the Wheel Of Songs and the band playing whatever the spinner landed on. Selections included rarely played songs and fan karaoke. Ryan said, "This will be the weirdest Guster show ever." It was a great improvised setlist. 

No editing has been done to this recording. This is not a spectacular recording but certainly listenable and captures the atmosphere of the show.  

Quality: 7.0/10
