The Black Rose was the site of the band's first off-campus show, but it is not known if this is that show or not. Ryan recalled in a MusicTimes article:

'[T]here's this one band I knew from Dallas called Jackopierce who I knew when I was in high school and Cary Piece booked the open mic. I called him I think drunk one night when I was a sophomore, and he was like, well if you like our band why don't you organize a show and your band can open for? So we did that we played this little bar in Cambridge called The Black Rose and we brought like 200 kids and they rocked. And, I was like holy shit this is their job, you know? I think I remember them driving away and having this feeling like, "F*ck now I have to go back to school and they just get to do that again today." It became, I think at that moment, if that band can do it we can do it.'
