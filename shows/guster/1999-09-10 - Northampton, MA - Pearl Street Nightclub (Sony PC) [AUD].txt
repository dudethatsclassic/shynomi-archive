Notes:
- Disc 1 was in extremely bad shape; track 11 contains "X-Ray Eyes", "Center Of 
  Attention" and "Window", but these three songs were completely unlistenable 
  despite attempts at low-level extraction on a number of computers; they are 
  left here for historical purposes only. There are also random, light bursts of 
  noise throughout most of the tracks as well due to extraction errors.
- Disc 2 is thankfully clean, but brief - the filler was a bad rip of Guster's 
  set from Woodstock '99, likely sourced from the satellite TV feed.
- I extracted each disc as one big WAV, removed the two-second track break gaps,   
  retracked properly and added fades. I couldn't do much to clean up the 
  recording quality - it's still a low-profile recording, done from the middle
  of the crowd with a clip-on mic, but it's the only known copy out there.
