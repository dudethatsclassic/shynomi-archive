Billed as "An Evening of Acoustic Music & Improv"; the band brought
improv artist Connor Ratliff of Upright Citizens Brigade, violinist
Bridgid Bibbens and cellist Rebecca Chung Filice on tour with them.
Connor acted as master of ceremonies, bringing the band on stage one by
one, decorating the front of the stage area with giant candles from the
prop room, adding to the between-songs banter, and pulling sticky notes
out of a fishbowl for the band to riff on between sets.

The musical performance was entirely seated, though not entirely
acoustic; songs with an asterisk featured Bridgid and Rebecca on their
respective instruments except for Window, which Bridgid performed alone,
and the finale, on which they played shaker and percussion.

Due to the seated nature of the show, I ran at head height so as not to
obstruct anyone, so the recording is a bit lacking in treble at times.
Some of the louder applause early on overloaded the preamp briefly, but
a slight level adjustment later fixed that. Also, Ryan sang directly
into my microphones during "Overexcited" while roaming through the house
on his wireless mic adventure.

Many thanks to all who came up to say hi to me on this first night of my
solo adventure, with special thanks to Jill and Rob Whitham for being
my local companions and hosts!
