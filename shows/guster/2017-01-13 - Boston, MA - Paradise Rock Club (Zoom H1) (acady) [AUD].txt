Guster played four nights at the Paradise Rock Club to celebrate their 25th Anniversary. They created four distinct shows that ranged from the early days to now. This is night two. 

No editing has been done to this recording. This recording has great sound. Vocals and instruments are very strong. As I was at the back of the venue, you can hear a bit of talking around me but it isn't too distracting.
