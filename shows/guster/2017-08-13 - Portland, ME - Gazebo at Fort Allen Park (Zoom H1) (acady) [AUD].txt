Guster did a 2:30 pm free show in Fort Allen Park that wrapped up Guster's "On The Ocean" weekend of events in Portland, ME. The show was announced via the band's social media at 9 am that morning. About 200 people showed up. My husband was one of about 12 lucky people that got to sit behind the band in the gazebo as they played. Neat little show.

No editing has been done to this recording. Talking and singing can be heard around me. I had a very chatty woman sitting next to me. Occasionally there's some wind noise. Instruments and vocals are very clear.
