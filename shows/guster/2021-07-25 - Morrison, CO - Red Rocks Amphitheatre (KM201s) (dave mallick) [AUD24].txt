Tracks 6 through 21 were accompanied by the Colorado Symphony Orchestra,
with concertmaster Yumi Hwang-Williams and conductor Christopher Dragon.

There is a good deal of rumble due to the wind whipping through the
on-stage microphones, and the wind also makes for some phasing effects
that result in the loss of higher frequencies from time to time,
especially earlier in the night. It does calm down later on, but this is
very common for outdoor recordings in general, and Red Rocks recordings
in particular. 

That being said, it was an honor to be able to record this - many thanks
as always to the band and crew for being so permissive and even helpful
in making this happen, so that anyone can relive the experience.
