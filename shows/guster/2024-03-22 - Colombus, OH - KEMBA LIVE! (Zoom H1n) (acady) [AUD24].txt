This show was billed "An Extra Long Evening With Guster", and it did not disappoint. It was full of skits, costume changes, and music from their 30-year catalogue. 

No editing has been done to this recording. Instruments and vocals are clear. Recording is fairly balanced. Talking and singing can be heard around me. I found it ironic that Taylor Swift's "Karma" started playing when the house lights went on at the end of the show.

Quality: 7/10

ENJOY!
