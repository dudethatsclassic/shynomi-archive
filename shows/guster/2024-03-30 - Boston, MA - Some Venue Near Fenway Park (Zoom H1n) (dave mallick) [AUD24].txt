Rather than doing a straightforward album tour, Guster wrote and
performed a two-act, two-plus-hour career retrospective, featuring
sketches, props, costume changes, and original music ("Thunder Song").
Narrated by drum tech, drummer, and keyboardist Dave Butler, the show
spans 33 years, from their initial meeting at Tufts University through
the release of "Ooh La La", their upcoming ninth studio album.

The sketch names are taken directly from the stage setlists, and are
largely self-explanatory. I have chosen to separate them from the
musical tracks as they are performances in their own right. The encore
was performed "campfire" style, with the band playing acoustic
instruments and sitting next to each other on the "acting bench".

Thank you to the band for 30+ years of allowing fans to record and
distribute these recordings for free among the fan base, and to the crew
for offering space, assistance, and advice while still providing
enthusiastic and expert support for these complex live performances.
