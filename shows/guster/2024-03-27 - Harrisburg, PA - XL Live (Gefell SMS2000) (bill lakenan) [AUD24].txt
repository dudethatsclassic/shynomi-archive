Notes:
1) About 90 seconds into '(Nothing But) Flowers' there is a few seconds of shifting soundstage as the mics were moved to a few feet to a safer location.

2) This show was very unique in that it was an Extra Long "Evening With Guster" Set and called "We Also Have Eras". It was a theatrical and chronological tour spanning the 33 years and 9 albums that Guster has been making music. Many skits were interspersed within the show describing the many aspects of the band from their forming at Tufts University through their upcoming 9th studio album Ooh La La. This tour will come to an end on March 30, 2024 in Boston for the CD release party concert.
