First performance of Say That To My Face since 2002.

Second performance of Timothy Leary since 2018. 

Live debut of It's Wild.

Second known live performance of Well; first since 2020.

First performance of Every Moment since 2019.

As he did during the short-lived 2020 tour, Connor Ratliff served as the
master of ceremonies for this show. He introduced the show, joined
fellow improv legends Greg Hess, Holly Laurent, and Kayleigh Llewellyn
on several segments throughout the performance, and came up with the
unofficial "We're on the ocean" theme later in the set.

Track 2 features Josh Kantor, best known as the organist for the Boston
Red Sox. He often takes requests from fans for songs to play during home
games, and has played several Guster tunes in the past. This segment
featured requests for ballpark classic Roll Out The Barrel, plus Your
Love (The Outfield), Mr. Blue Sky (ELO), Satellite (Guster), Whip It
(Devo), and Take Me Home, Country Roads (John Denver). Josh came back for
several songs later in the set; he was rather low in the mix and hard 
to hear, and I didn't write down which songs he played on.

Tracks 5 & 6 are Maggie Rose performing solo, while Track 8 is a
stand-up set from comedian Josh Gondelman.

Track 9 was an audience vote between Melanie and Two At A Time, but the
band rigged a tie so they could play a medley of both songs.

Tracks 12 & 14 are improv "explanations" of the stories behind the songs
the band had just finished playing.

Track 16 is a Moth-style storytelling of Brian's adventures in karaoke
while raising money to fund the production of Evermotion.

Tracks 17 & 18 are Joe & Marc Pisapia; Luke plays bass with them on
track 18. Joe then stays on stage for the rest of the show.
