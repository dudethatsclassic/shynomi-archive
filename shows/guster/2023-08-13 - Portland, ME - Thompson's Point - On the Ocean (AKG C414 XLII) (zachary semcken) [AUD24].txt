Earlier in the day, Guster had an impromptu performance of "Blister in the Sun" on a scissor lift next to the festival ground restrooms.

During the "Keep It Together" play-through, a prop made to look like RealPlayer window showing the song titles was paraded across the stage between songs by the band's family children.

Huge thanks to Guster's management for organizing this festival and for allowing us the opportunity to archive their performance.

This recording is dedicated to all the tapers that have taught me to love archiving live music.
