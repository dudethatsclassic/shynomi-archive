Originally billed as just Ryan & Adam, but the event page later listed
"Ryan, Adam & Bri". Opener Brooke Annibale spilled the beans during her
set, and then Brian came up after Amsterdam and played the rest of the
show, leaving the stage before Parachute.

My mic stand wasn't allowed in, so I clamped my mics to the metal casing
of the V3, which was in my taping bag on the ground, about five feet
away from the left stack. Very occasional foot traffic in front of it
but it shouldn't really be noticeable.
