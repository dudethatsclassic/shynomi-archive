Notes:	This isn't the best sounding Guster show by a long shot...not even
	the best that I've recorded, but after 14+ months another copy
	hasn't surfaced and I finally got off my lazy ass and encoded it 
	so here it is...better than nothing and enjoyable show taped under
	less than ideal conditions.

	The Chance is a tiny little GA venue that wouldn't allow me in with
	my taping stuff so I had to stealth it.  I stood dead center on the
	balcony and there was a lot of traffic so there's some talking.  
	Also, halfway through the show they put a garbage can by me so you
	can here beer bottles being thrown out now and then.

	Quite an unusual conversion set up, but I fried my digital soundcard
	and the CNJB3 tranfer worked fine.  It was all digital so there was
	no compression or quality loss from the round-about transfer.
