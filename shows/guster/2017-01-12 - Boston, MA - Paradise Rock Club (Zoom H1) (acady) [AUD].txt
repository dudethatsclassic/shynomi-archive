Guster played four nights at the Paradise Rock Club to celebrate their 25th Anniversary. They created four distinct shows that ranged from the early days to now. This is night one. The River 92.5 FM broadcasted the show live on the radio that night and hopefully someone captured that and will post it here as well. This source is the audience version.

No editing has been done to this recording. This is a decent recording, certainly listenable, but is a bit bass-heavy. The instruments tend to overpower the vocals. Talking, screaming, and singing can be heard around me. Occasionally you can hear some bumps against the unit and the air conditioning seemed to be blowing right in my direction so you will also hear some wind noise at times as well.
