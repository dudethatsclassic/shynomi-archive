Part of the band's release-week tour for "Look Alive", the band appeared
at The Strand bookstore in New York City. After an interview with author
and TV host Ted Allen, they opened it up to questions from the audience
and played a brief acoustic set. The interview portion was recorded from
the front row, after which the band moved to the back of the room for
their performance.
