The Peace Soldiers are old touring partners who bear a striking
resemblance to the members of Guster. They met in a parking lot in
Nashville in 1998 and played various shows together over the next four
years, but their paths had not crossed again until now. They covered 
ZZ Top, Neil Young, the Byrds (originally the Louvin Brothers), and 
Bob Dylan.

Guster ran a "join our band" contest where fans sent in audition videos
in hopes of playing with them on stage at this show. A total of eight
songs were chosen:
 
Overexcited featured Jackson Crawford on saxophone
Two Points For Honesty featured Hannah Wetovsky-Devitt on vocals
Amsterdam featured Gregg Charest on steel drums
So Long featured Suzanne Monen Miller on cello
The New Underground featured Darci Willis on drums
Hang On featured Nick Fede on vocals
Homecoming King featured Tyler Wood on bagpipes
Jesus On The Radio featured Amanda and Gavin Cox on vocals
