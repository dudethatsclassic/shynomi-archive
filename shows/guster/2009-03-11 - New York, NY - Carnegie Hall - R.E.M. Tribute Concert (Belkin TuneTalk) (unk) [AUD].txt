It was amazing evening of beautiful R.E.M. music, as performed by 20+
outstanding artists!

I had excellent, first-level center balcony seats, but acoustically,
this was a very difficult show to record, mainly because each artist had
their own sound volume and style.  Certain songs, like Sitting Still or
Carnival of Sorts, are overpowering while others, like Wendell Gee or At
My Most Beautiful, recorded much better.  Overall, I would give this
recording a B-, but it is definitely worth the download! :-)

Thanks and enjoy!  And, thanks R.E.M., for doing what you do.

Show edited down to intro and Guster track only for upload to archive.org.
Tracks were originally in mono (and therefore not CD quality) so I split
the one channel into two just for format's sake. It'll still sound exactly
the same, of course.
