Track 1 fades in because I accidentally left my recorder on pause at the
beginning of the show. The band took the stage as the recorded intro to
the song played (the keyboard drone and bells) and then kicked in their
instruments at the appropriate time.

Track 7 is Ryan talking about the listening party they had at Bissell
Brothers earlier that afternoon, where they played ten potential songs
from their untitled album, to be released in 2024. He explains that they
were worried about putting themselves out there, but then says "Vivien
sent us a message and she said 'new album is a vibe, y'all'." That sent
him off on an improv song about how various things are "a vibe".

Track 15 is Ryan's attempt to make up for the fact that his 50th
birthday had a big celebration during the fall tour, while Adam's and
Brian's came while they were off the road. The lighting engineer put up
a picture of them on the backdrop with a "100" caption underneath while
someone brought a sheet cake onstage.

Track 20 is Ryan raving about the quality of the opening acts (Brooke
Annibale, Ballroom Thieves, Madison Cunningham, and Bahamas) and then
trying to get his vocal effect working properly for the electric version
of Don't Go.

"That's No Way To Get To Heaven" was on the setlist as the first song of
the encore, but was skipped due to time.
