SOLD OUT SHOW

This show was very unique in that it was an Extra Long "Evening With Guster" Set and called "We Also Have Eras". It was a theatrical and chronological tour spanning the 33 years and 9 albums that Guster has been making music. Many skits were interspersed within the show describing the many aspects of the band from their forming at Tufts University through their upcoming 9th studio album Ooh La La. Today was the CD release party. The band signed the new CD for fans prior to the show. The show stopper of the night was Brians magical flight for Thunder Song.

Enjoy!
