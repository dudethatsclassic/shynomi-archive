The seeds of Secret Stash were planted by Yukon Kornelius, a group
featuring Stefan, Adam, Eric, and Ed Robertson of the Barenaked Ladies.
The group played several shows in the 2008-2011 timeframe, but in early
2011 Stefan booked two shows as Secret Stash, retaining Eric but adding
Brett and Warren DeMartini of RATT. The project has evolved over the
years, but has always been for benefit shows. This one in particular was
to benefit the Spruce Peak Arts Center Foundation.
