In preparation for the band's singalong show later that afternoon, Ryan
and Adam held an open rehearsal for anyone who wanted to practice
harmonies beforehand. About 40 people showed up; we arrived late so I
didn't have time to set up my taping gear, but I wanted to document it
anyway, so I just recorded it on my phone, which was sticking out of my
pocket. Three members of the UVM Cat's Meow a cappella group (Kerry
Brosnan, Brigitte Durieux, and Rachel Rubenstein) assisted with
arrangements of the harmonies.

Obviously this is not a high-quality recording given the circumstances -
there is chatter nearby, it's difficult to hear Adam and Ryan talking,
but the music is at least audible and fairly clear. And it's cool to be
able to hear the harmonies here, which were not nearly as obvious at the
singalong show itself.
