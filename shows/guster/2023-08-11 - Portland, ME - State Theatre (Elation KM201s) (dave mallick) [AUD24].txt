The band billed this evening as an "Enchantment Under the Ocean" dance
with a prom theme. Everyone who was so inclined dressed up fancy, and
three lucky couples/families were introduced on stage during track 10.

Live debuts of "Careless Whisper" (Wham!), "Rock Lobster" (The B-52s)
and "Good Riddance (Time Of Your Life)" (Green Day), all sung by Brian.

Mics were lowered to head height to get out of camera shot, so there may
be more crowd noise than usual.
