20 tracks of anecdotes, song intros and chitchat by famed producer
Steve Lillywhite and Guster lead singer Ryan Miller. The first
ten tracks are Steve by himself introducing various songs of his
choosing; since the music itself is copyrighted, it is not part of
this fileset. Ryan joins Steve on track 11, and Steve leaves after
track 16, leaving Ryan on his own for the balance of the show.
