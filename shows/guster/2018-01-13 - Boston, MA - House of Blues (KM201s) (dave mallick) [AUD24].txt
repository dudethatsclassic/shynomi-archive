Before G Major, Ryan acknowledged the "elephant in the room" - the fact
that the Patriots had a playoff game at the same time. So rather than
everyone checking their phones all the time, the band arranged for a
whiteboard at the side of the stage, and called up Mark, a fan from the
audience, to keep the score updated. Ryan later went over to him during
"Doing It By Myself" and they sang part of the chorus together, and
Mark's wife Trisha came on stage to join him during the encore.
