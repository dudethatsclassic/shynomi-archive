The second show of Guster's Third Annual On The Ocean Fest was held at Thompson's Point. It featured two full sets, including a 20th anniversary playing of Lost & Gone Forever in full. 

It was a full day of events including two stages of entertainment, activities for kids, and a lobster bake. Former member Joe Pisapia hosted an intimate “Joe’s Place” stage which included music from Joe and friends, a Q&A with Guster, and Spicy V’s Hot Sauce Eating Contest.

No editing has been done to this recording. Being that I was in the pit in front of the stage, there is non-stop talking, screaming, and singing around me. Better sources exist for this show, but I present my recording here for completists. Instruments and vocals are clear. The recording is a bit bass-heavy.

For videos from the show, visit my youtube page: https://www.youtube.com/user/acady6
