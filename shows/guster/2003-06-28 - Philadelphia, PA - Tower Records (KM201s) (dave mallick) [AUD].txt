Recording notes:
- d1t01 has several rough spots of distortion in the left channel as
  recorded on the master; I may have had a short or perhaps something
  was bumping the mic stand (it was set up behind a display by the
  stairwell on the right-hand side). I noticed the left channel spiking 
  all throughout Amsterdam but could not diagnose it before it 
  mysteriously disappeared after one final distortion 27 seconds into
  Happier. If anyone is feeling adventurous and feels like fixing or 
  minimizing these problems, please do!
- Several blips and dropouts in Happier from the original transfer were
  repaired by re-recording a couple of segments on the mastering deck, 
  hence the D8 also appearing in the transfer lineage above. Overlays are
  flawless and result in no dropped samples but it's worth mentioning.
