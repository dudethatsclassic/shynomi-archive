Guster
July 2, 2000
Viv's House
Andover, MA

This show was a "semi-private" event for Guster's Reps.  It was held in a
rep's backyard. The sound system consisted only of a small PA and
4-channel mixer.  As a result the low-end is relatively weak in the
recording.

Source: Microtech Gefell SMS2000/200 (card, XY90) -> Oade M148 -> Sony
SBM-1 -> D8
Location: 30 feet from stage, DFC
Taper: Bill Lakenan <blakenan@us.net>
Conversion: Panasonic 3700 > Lucid PCI24 > .wav > .shn (No DAE)
Converted by: Bill Lakenan <blakenan@us.net>
