Night One of Guster's 2nd Annual On The Ocean weekend kicked off with a concert at the State Theatre. Attendees were asked to write song requests on ping pong balls as they entered the show, which were later thrown on stage at the end of "Airport Song" for the band to choose from to create a unique setlist. 

No editing has been done to this recording. Talking, screaming, and singing can be heard around me.  The recording is quite bass heavy. Luke's bass must not have been dialed in fully at the start of the show as it dramatically gets louder at 1:33 into "G Major" and from there on out the bass drowns out the vocals. Other sources exist for this show and will probably be better sounding than mine, but I present it here for diehard fans.
