Billed as "An Evening of Acoustic Music & Improv"; the band brought
improv artist Connor Ratliff of Upright Citizens Brigade, violinist
Bridgid Bibbens and cellist Rebecca Chung Filice on tour with them.
Connor acted as master of ceremonies, bringing the band on stage one by
one, decorating the front of the stage area with giant candles from the
prop room, adding to the between-songs banter, and pulling sticky notes
out of a fishbowl for the band to riff on between sets.

The musical performance was entirely seated, though not entirely
acoustic; songs with an asterisk featured Bridgid and Rebecca on their
respective instruments except for the finale, on which they played
shaker and percussion.

Many thanks to team Reverb for adopting me and letting me get in the
venue early, to the venue staff for being accommodating of my gear, to
everyone who came up and said hi (too many to name!), with special
thanks to Jill and Rob Whitham for being my local companions and hosts!
