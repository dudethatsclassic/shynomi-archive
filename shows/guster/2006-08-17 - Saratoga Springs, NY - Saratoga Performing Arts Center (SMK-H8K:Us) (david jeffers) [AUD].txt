Show Notes:
Guster's first every appearence in Saratoga.  A Gusterino
(younger roadie) played the drum kits, tamborine, etc on
a bunch of songs, but I can't remember which to properly
note.  Mr Clean, bald roadie, played keyboards on a few
tracks as well.  GREAT crowd really into the show and
singing along, but no real screamers.  Also a pretty
decent number of ping pong balls on Airport Song.
