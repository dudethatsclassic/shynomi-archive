Guster played four nights at the Paradise Rock Club to celebrate their 25th Anniversary. They created four distinct shows that ranged from the early days to now. This is night four. Ryan's voice was shot by this point, stating that "25 years of singing like this has caught up with me" so fans filled in where his voice was failing. 

No editing has been done to this recording. There is a lot of talking, screaming, and singing around me. Instruments and vocals are quite clear as I was standing in front of the speaker stack.

Quality: 7.5/10 

ENJOY!
