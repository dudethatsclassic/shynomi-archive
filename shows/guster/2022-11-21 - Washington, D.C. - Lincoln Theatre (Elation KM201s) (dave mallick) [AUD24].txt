Ryan Miller's 50th birthday celebration. He thought he was introducing
the band playing in sync with their own video of "The New Underground",
but Brian interrupted him and instead introduced a video of various
friends, celebrities, and family members wishing Ryan a happy birthday.
Sort of. You'll just have to listen and hopefully a video will surface.

The start of the encore consisted of cover songs that were meaningful to
Ryan. Full-band debut of "Moon River" (Henry Mancini and "This Must Be
The Place" (Talking Heads), the latter of which had been performed by
Adam and Ryan at the Secret Stash show in January of 2020. "Dreams"
(Fleetwood Mac) and "Blister In The Sun" (Violent Femmes) had fewer than
ten known performances previously.

Thanks to Bill Lakenan for coming out of taping retirement for this
show, getting there early to a spot, and for the clamp space! He had
to lower the mic stand in order to pass Ryan's mic cable over it during
"Doin It By Myself" but it doesn't seem to be audible.
