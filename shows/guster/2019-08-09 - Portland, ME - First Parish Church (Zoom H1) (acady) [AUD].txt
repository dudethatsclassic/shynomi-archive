Guster's Third Annual On The Ocean Fest kicked off with a concert on Friday night at First Parish Church. Those of us that were lucky enough to get tickets were treated to a spectacular show. Due to the ungodly heat at last year's show at the State Theater, the venue change was welcomed by fans until we discovered very quickly that the church also did not have air conditioning! Despite the sweltering heat, we sang, we clapped, we danced, and we went to church!

No editing has been done to this recording. Talking, screaming, and singing can be heard around me. Instruments and vocals are clear, and thankfully, the recording isn't too bass-heavy.
