Notes:
- The Melody Tent is a theater in the round, which doesn't make for the
  best taping situation; there's virtually no open floor space to set up 
  a mic stand, either at your seat or behind them. Even the soundboard
  area was packed full with nowhere to set up, so I stowed the stand away
  and hand-held the mount and mics on my leg for the whole show. We were
  sitting in the sixth row and I angled the mics up to the PA (which
  hangs above the rotating stage).
- Yes, that's Will Forte guesting on the encore. He and Ryan became
  friendly when they met at "Dylan Fest" at the Bowery Ballroom in NYC
  back in May. Will was staying in Hyannis while shooting for the Adam
  Sandler film "Donny's Boy" (formerly "I Hate You, Dad") and texted
  the band when he heard they were coming to the area.
