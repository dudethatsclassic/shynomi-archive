Philadelphia starts off the 2025 leg of the "We Also Have Eras" Tour 2.0. It was a theatrical and chronological tour spanning the 33 years and 9 albums that Guster has been making music. Many skits were interspersed within the show describing the many aspects of the band from their forming at Tufts University through their upcoming 9th studio album Ooh La La. This tour continues on and will end on the west coast.
