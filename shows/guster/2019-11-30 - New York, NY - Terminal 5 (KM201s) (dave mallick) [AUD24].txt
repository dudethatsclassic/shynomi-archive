A make-up show for the band's August performance that was cancelled
after five songs due to a severe thunderstorm. They played two sets, the
second of which was entirely fan requests taken through online voting.

The 'set break' had Hot Butter's cover of "Popcorn" playing over the PA
while Adam led a conga line through the crowd, Brian played cowbell, and
Ryan did a quick off-stage costume change.

First Medicine since 2017, second since 2012, and third since 2007.
