- Set I intro was the band taking the stage to an untitled Neutral Milk
  Hotel track they used to use years ago. Set II's intro was a reference

  wearing tuxedoes, to the theme from 2001: A Space Odyssey. This time,
  they were normally-attired, and the same song was butchered by an
  unidentified (and possibly self-produced) horn section.
