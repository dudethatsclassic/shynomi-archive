Notes:
- A secret show, played after the final show of Guster's four-night run at the 
  Paradise in honor of their 25th anniversary as a band and the 40th anniversary 
  of the club. The band booked the back room of a Somerville bar under an
  assumed name they had used to play secret shows in their early days.
- The band members rotated among various instruments during the show - at one 
  point, Adam was playing drums and Brian was playing guitar.
- Tony and Samantha's history with Guster goes almost back to the beginning - 
  Tony co-founded the band Papas Fritas at Tufts with drummer Shivika Asthana,
  and much later co-wrote "Amsterdam" with the band. His wife, Samantha, was
  Shivika's college roommate, and the two now play in Jenny Dee & the 
  Deelinquents together.
