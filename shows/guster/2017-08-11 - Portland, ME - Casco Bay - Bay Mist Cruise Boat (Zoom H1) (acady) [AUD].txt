Guster played on a Casco Bay cruise boat for 200 fans as part of their "On The Ocean" 25th anniversary weekend. The first nine songs were a regular set, while the final five were a string of "yacht rock" covers that they worked up for the occasion.

No editing has been done to this recording. I was standing just to the left of the speaker stack so the recording is bass heavy and loud. This is not a spectacular recording, but captures the atmosphere of the show. Talking, screaming, and singing can be heard around me. 

Other sources for this show can be found here: https://archive.org/details/Guster
