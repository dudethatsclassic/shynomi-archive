Guster did a 9:30 am free acoustic show outside Hadlock Field prior to the start of the Portland Sea Dog's baseball game. This pop-up show was part of the 2nd Annual On The Ocean weekend of events in Portland, ME. The show was announced via the band's social media at 7:30 am that morning. About 75 fans showed up. Immediately following this the band played catch with fans on the field and then threw out the first pitch.

No editing has been done to this recording. Talking and singing can be heard around me. Instruments and vocals are very clear.
