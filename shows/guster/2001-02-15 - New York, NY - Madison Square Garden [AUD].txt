If you listen through headphones you'll get the 'real concert experience'(tm)!

I hadn't been real familiar with Guster before this concert.  I was only looking to check
levels with the opening act, go to the bathroom and see how it came out.  However, when they
said they were Guster I figured what the hell, & recorded the whole thing.  At least they're
not an Alanis Morissette wanna-be like BNL's first opener that night, Chantel Krezeviak. Ugh.
