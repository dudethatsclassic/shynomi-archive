The first show of Guster's "On the Ocean 2019" weekend, played in the
sanctuary of a Unitarian Universalist church in the heart of Portland.
The third-hottest show of Brian's life, he said. 

Old friend Joe Pisapia joined the band for the weekend and was going to
open the festivities, but his plane arrived late, so he played a few
tunes between sets (tracks 15-16) instead. 

All five played the live rarity "Every Moment" to kick off the second
set. He went on to rejoin for several songs in the second set - hopping
on the sidestage piano when the mood struck. They finished with a
five-man Jesus on the Radio played up in the rear balcony; Ryan played
the pipe organ pedals with his hands.
