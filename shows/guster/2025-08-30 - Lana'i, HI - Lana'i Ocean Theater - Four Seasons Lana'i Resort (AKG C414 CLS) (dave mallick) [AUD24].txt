Entire show with Jonny Lam on guitar, standing in for Adam

Guster's first show in Hawaii, their 49th state. Show was complimentary
for guests at the Four Seasons, and also open to all residents of
Lana'i. Venue capacity is 600, and it was well-attended.

Adam had to miss the show due to a family emergency, so the band worked
their connections and called upon the prodigious talents of Jonny Lam,
formerly of Brooklyn, now of Hawaii. Check him out at @jonnypartys.

Venue aside, this recording was an adventure. I forgot my mic stand at
home, so I set my gear at the bottom of the center aisle, under the
railing. At the time, everyone was sitting well clear of the bag, but as
the evening progressed, people walked by it constantly, little kids
swung on the railing in front of it, and one unsupervised toddler
couldn't resist squeezing the foam windscreen at one point. Two kids
even went over the rules of hide-and-seek about three feet from the
mics during the introduction to CDASH. All part of the experience.

I set my levels too hot early on, so the kick drum spikes constantly for
the first 26 minutes. I adjusted it downward at that point, then again
at 40 minutes. The second half definitely sounds better than the first.

I would be remiss if I did not acknowledge the constant and incredible
grace and generosity of the band and their management, as well as the
staff at the Four Seasons Lana'i. This was without a doubt the most
expensive "complimentary" concert ever, but a uniquely marvelous and
intimate experience that we will never forget.
