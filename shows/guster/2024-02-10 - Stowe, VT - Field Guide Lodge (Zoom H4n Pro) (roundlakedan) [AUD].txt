Notes:
Guster played to a sold-out crowd at a small ski lodge in Vermont. The show took place just before they are set to begin final rehearsals before their "We Also Have Eras" tour in March. The sound quality is not terrible, all things considered, as the space was quite loud and there were a lot of folks who weren't exactly "hardcore" Guster fans talking in the back where we were set up to record. The show included two sets, with some rarely played deep cuts and fun covers.
