Notes:
- The weather was threatening all night long, so rather than run from
  our seats, we ran directly in front of the board, under a two-foot
  overhang thanks to the general awesomeness of FOH engineer Gordon.
  Fortunately the venue sounds fantastic even from back there.
- Ryan was in rare form - "Manifest Destiny" features some improv
  lines about milkshakes from Dairy Queen, and the last song before
  the encore featured him braving a 2-foot deep moat of black water
  between the stage and the crowd. Video here:
  http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pVtvLQEbHJE
- "Jesus On The Radio" was played acoustic in the middle of the crowd to
  the right of the soundboard; the band stood on chairs and was surrounded
  by fans. It's almost impossible to hear, unfortunately.
