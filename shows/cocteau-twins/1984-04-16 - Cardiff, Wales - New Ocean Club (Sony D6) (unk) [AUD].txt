Cocteau Twins, New Ocean Club, Cardiff, 16 April 1984, inc soundcheck *M1* 

I've recently heard a snippet of a high gen. copy of this recording via the Cocteau Twins forum. This low gen. version is for Dynamite and Leesa. 

The gig and soundcheck are from two different sources. The soundcheck was recorded by MP who then promptly dropped and broke his machine and taped none of the other gigs that week. I met MP during the soundcheck and he gave me a copy of his tape. The gig was recorded by the mate I went along with. I put the two together, so if you have a copy of this with the soundcheck, it's probably a descendent of my original pairing - like Dynamite's. 

Quality is very good, which unfortunately only serves to emphasise the problems with my mates Sony D6 during the first two songs of the gig. I'd borrowed this very same D6 to record Dead Can Dance at their first appearance at the Brixton Loughborough Hotel just two days before this Cardiff gig and it played up then as well. Bastard D6. No artwork, but I've included a scan of the backstage pass.
