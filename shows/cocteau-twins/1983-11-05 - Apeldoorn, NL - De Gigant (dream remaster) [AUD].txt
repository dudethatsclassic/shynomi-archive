Remaster Notes:-

An average audience recording for it's age.
It is 4 or 5 out of 10.
So don't expect to much & you may enjoy it.
Audiochecker results were mixed, so I left it in flac (which was how it arrived to me).
Took a fair amount of restoration, I did what I could to make it more listenable.
To much reverb from the soundboard on "Sugar Hiccup" it is not from my work.
Lots of talkers around the taper adding there own cheers/whoops/clapping.
Probably one for hardcore fans only.
If you take the plunge hope you enjoy it.

Dream Remastered @ Reflecting Sound Studios, Southampton, England, 2018.
