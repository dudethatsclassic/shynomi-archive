Notes:
This is the second master recording I'm sharing with the DIME community.  This was the last concert I taped with my Aiwa HS-J2, and also my fifth and last time seeing the Cocteau Twins live.  My friend Stan flew to the Columbus, OH gig on 1985-09-19, and met the band backstage.  Stan recorded that show with my Aiwa, and most likely it is the version that has circulated on DIME previously.  Thanks to Stan, he got us on the guest list with backstage access at the Paradise Theatre. Unlike some of my other Cocteau Twins recordings (Danceteria 1984-01-04, 1984-01-06, and possibly ICA London 1983-10-08), my Paradise Theatre recording has never been circulated or traded to anyone.

About me:
I've been going to concerts since 1975, starting with Lou Reed at the Felt Forum, 1975-04-26.  I started taping on 1978-03-26 with Renaissance at City Center. From 1978 to 1982 I used a portable Panasonic RQ-2309 mono cassette recorder with built-in condenser microphone, as it's all I could afford. I mostly recorded my favorite band at the time, The Shirts, but there were some other new wave and punk bands from that era also.  In mid 1982 I upgraded to the Aiwa HS-J2 walkman style recorder with the CM-2 stereo mic. Some of those recordings have appeared on DIME as I used to trade tapes. Most of my master recordings are indexed on etreedb.org ( https://etreedb.org/10thFloorClown ), and I welcome requests on what I should upload to DIME next. I'd love to connect with some of the other NYC tapers from back then; please feel free to send me a PM.
