Notes: 
1983 saw me travel to London to see Siouxsie and the Banshees for their two shows at the Royal Albert Hall.  A completely unexpected bonus was a series of nights at the Institute of Contemporary Arts billed as "Pop! Goes the Easel". One of the highlights was seeing The Cocteau Twins live for the first time.  I altered a backstage pass that I found from 06 October to "08" which gave me backstage access to chat with Liz and Robin. I've included the best photo I took that night; unfortunately they all turned out poor due to the dim lighting. Checking the DIME database, there is a different recording of this show, first uploaded by josefkarol on 2007-04-28 and later uploaded by bertiebear23 on 2019-12-08 (from the richardbajor collection).  That recording sounds better than mine.
 

About me:
I've been going to concerts since 1975, starting with Lou Reed at the Felt Forum, 1975-04-26.  I started taping on 1978-03-26 with Renaissance at City Center. From 1978 to 1982 I used a portable Panasonic RQ-2309 mono cassette recorder with built-in condenser microphone, as it's all I could afford. I mostly recorded my favorite band at the time, The Shirts, but there were some other new wave and punk bands from that era also.  In mid-1983 I upgraded to the Aiwa HS-J2 walkman style recorder with the CM-2 stereo mic. Some of those recordings have already appeared on DIME as I used to trade tapes. Most of my master recordings are indexed on etreedb.org ( https://etreedb.org/10thFloorClown ), and I welcome requests on what I should upload to DIME next. I'd love to connect with some of the other NYC tapers from back then; please feel free to send me a PM. If anyone has any live recordings by The Shirts or Deaf School please contact me!
