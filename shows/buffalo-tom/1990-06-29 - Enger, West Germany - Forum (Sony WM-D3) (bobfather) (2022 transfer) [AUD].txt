"Birdbrain"-Tour


SOURCE: SONY WM D3 / unknown mic > 1st gen TDK SA 90.
TRANSFER: nakamichi cr 7e (azimuth adjusted) > marantz dr 6000 > mac rip to disk > soundforge > flac by dear Bobfather.
REMASTER: Merged, retracked, added more depp/mid range in favour of the high end. 
The recording starts quite rough but settles nicely after a few songs for Side A of the tape. Side B has more hiss. 
Taper seems to have moved a few times, so audience noise had sometimes to be smoothed. Still a raw but very listenable recording of early BT music.
