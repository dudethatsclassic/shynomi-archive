Hi everyone!
Here’s an example of truly a group effort to make this captured… Austin never taped a show before but was willing to give it a shot, and we got a ticket hoping we’ll figure out the rig ‘locally’. As time drew closer, and I wasn’t getting any encouraging response from the UK tapers, we figured we should ship the rig from wherever ASAP. I decided the mics + battery box will make it a light, easier to ship and less taxable (!!) transatlantic package whereas shipping my new’ish shiny recorder is probably pointless as they are not all that different. Actually, 25 pounds worth of taxes were collected in the UK even just for the mics with the bbox (!!!). At the same time, we arranged for my friend blackijack, who was traveling from Berlin to see this show, to borrow ZoomH2 from another Berlin-based Radiohead fan Michael (a.k.a. Wonsy on Atease) and hand-deliver it. And Austin put it all together on-site - he’s the man! :-) 
There were some wrinkles: during the last piece (which has nothing to do with Jonny Greenwood - it's Gorecki’s Symphony No3), the recorder went on strike - so we are not including that part of the show with this upload. Another issue is that, not knowing what to expect from the classical music concert or from the recorder, Austin recorded at very levels - so the tape came out way too quiet. This, however, was significantly improved with dedicated mastering by BLG (thankfully, the 24bit recording lets us boost those levels quite a bit). SO after this London-Berlin-NYC-LA - rama - we are releasing this little show here :) Hope you like it!
Many many thanks to everyone involved!!
