NOTES
-----
Recorded from the upper level, stage left near one of the PA stacks.  Last song performed without use of PA, in the middle of the church amongst the pews.  Therefore, it was INCREDIBLY quiet, so I had to boost the levels, thereby raising the hiss levels.  This has been filtered, and I believe it sounds a lot better than being left un-amplified.  Once the song actually starts, and the audience noise drops, the "noise" caused by the hiss reduction is almost un-noticeable.  There are a few slight scuffles and mic knocks, but nothing too extreme, luckily the crowd was very respectful and quiet (apart from some sniffing and talking!)
