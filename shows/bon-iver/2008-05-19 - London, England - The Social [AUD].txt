Bon Iver's first UK headlining show, in a tiny little basement in central London. Fantastic show, kept the crowd as quiet as mice, until the big singalong to The Wolves of course. Tape turned out nicely - only needed some volume adjustments. Tough to tape Bon Iver with the wide dynamic range, but I lucked out on this one. Highlight was the un-mic'd version of Skinny Love, performed as luck would have it about 3 yards in front of me. Thrilling stuff. Got to meet Mr Vernon briefly afterwards, and rarely have I met such a polite, humble guy.\
\
Enjoy.\
