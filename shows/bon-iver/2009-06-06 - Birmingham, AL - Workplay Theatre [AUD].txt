Notes: 

First time seeing Bon Iver live, a very nice set with a couple hundred people in attendance.  Thanks go out to DJ Froyd, who not only assists with the taping but also helps to introduce me to great new bands.....like Bon Iver.  Sound quality here is damn good, with the only issue (always one or two of those, right?) being that the theatre sold glass bottles of beer, and we were set up nearby the 30 gallon plastic garbage can......so every now and then you'll hear some uncouth slobs dropping glass on glass.  It gets to the point (just before the start of Re: Stacks) that the singer has to say "Hold your bottles on this one," you have to wonder if some in the crowd were born in a barn......then you remember that this is Alabama.....'nuff said.  If you listen close, you might here a well-timed request for Triumph.....anyone remember Triumph?  Comments are welcome.  Enjoy.


Zepdog5
