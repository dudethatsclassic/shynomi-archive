I`ve been to a zillion shows in the past 25 years some were good and some were bad, but this show will stay in memory forever. I was driven to tears when Justin played "Re:Stacks". 
The recording is very, very good. No audience chatter. During "Flume" I had to adjust my mic and you can hear it, if you have good ears. That`s why I don`t give it an "excellent".

The record is my favorite record of the year, if you don´t have it: Go and buy immediately !
It`s so great how the band transform the studio work on stage. 4 guys singing heavenly harmonies, from superquiet to freaking-out-feedback-party, two drum sets...if you have the chance to see them - go (and tape).
