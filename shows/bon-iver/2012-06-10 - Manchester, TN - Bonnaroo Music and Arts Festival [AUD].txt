MISC NOTES:	
This is yet another camp-out tape from Bonnaroo 2012.  I brought two rigs to the festival and on Sunday I left this rig with other tapers and my girlfriend at the What Stage all day to record all the shows on the stage that day (Gary Clark Jr, The Beach Boys, Bon Iver, and Phish) and meanwhile I took my smaller rig and ran off to tape Kenny Rogers and Fun. elsewhere in Centeroo.  Huge thanks to all of the people without whom this tape couldn't have been made, Brian, Will, Nate, and of course my lovely girlfriend, Daphne.  I am not very familiar with Bon Iver's music so this setlist may be a little funky, but I tried my best to get it as close as I could by Googling snippets of lyrics and comparing my setlist again other ones I found.  Enjoy!
