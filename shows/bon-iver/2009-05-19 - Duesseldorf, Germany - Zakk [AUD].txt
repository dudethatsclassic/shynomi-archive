Gosh...how great they are ! Some new songs and some "classics". Unfortunately they didn`t play "Flume", but you can`t have it all. Found a good position to tape. 5 meters away from the speakers surrounded by very respectful people, who were extremly quiet during the songs and enthusiastic after the song. Think that the recording captured the dynamic of their show. They go from superquiet ("Re:Stacks") to wild & loud ("Wolves Act 1 & 2").
They really got me with their record & their live shows. I saw a loooot of bands over the years, but this band is definitely among my top 5 live bands ever. 
If you have the chance to see them...don`t wait....and don`t forget to take your recorder with you !
If you don`t have their records....don`t hesitate...go and buy them !
