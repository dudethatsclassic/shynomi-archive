

R.E.M.
Monster 1995 Tour
PalaEur, Roma - Italy
23rd February 1995




Here's R.E.M. second date in Rome 1995. Audio quality is not so bad as I thought (just the echo effects are strong
due to the worst kind of venue you can imagine to host a concert) and the setlist is different enough.
The recording is complete, there are no partial or missing songs.
Happy listenig and all the best everybody!
