20 July 1999 - Stirling Castle, Stirling, Scotland 
support: Stereolab 
set: Lotus / Wake-Up Bomb / So Fast, So Numb / Fall On Me / Suspicion / Daysleeper / How The West Was Won And Where It Got Us / Driver 8 / Sweetness Follows / The One I Love / E-bow The Letter / The Great Beyond / Losing My Religion / At My Most Beautiful / Pretty Persuasion / Star 69 / Walk Unafraid / Man On The Moon 
encore: Falls To Climb / Why Not Smile / What's The Frequency, Kenneth? / Tongue / Cuyahoga / It's The End Of The World As We Know It (And I Feel Fine)  

The tracks are arranged in Disc One/Disc Two folders with the break coming just before Losing My Religion. Files 1-12 are in Disc One. Files 13-24 are in Disc Two.

As always the setlist is taken from Darryl White's incomporable and essential REM Timeline ( www.remtimeline.com - your one stop shop for all matters related to REM shows and setlists).

This is the fifth of the UK shows that I recorded during the Up Tour and the second of the three shows that they played at Stirling Castle.  The weather wasn't quite as bad as the previous night but it was still cold.  Michael again complains of how cold he is.

I was standing Mike side of the stage about 20 metres back from the PA. with the mic duck taped to the top of of my umbrella. There was lot of wind drift that you can hear on the recording but it still sounds pretty good  

Recording Lineage: Sony ECM - MS 907 electret condensor mic>Sony MZ-R35 MD recorder N.B. in MONO mode> Cool Edit 2000 (with no tweaking at all!) >CDWav>flac level 6
