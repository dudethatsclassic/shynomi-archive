R.E.M.  1984-09-28
University Of South Florida Soccer Field, Tampa, FL 
I got my cass copy of this show within a week of the show.
Got mine from Steve and he got his from Nadia.
Hers could have been a 1st gen sbd. 
I played back my copy on my Nak CR-5A on mormal bias and EQ setting and adjusted the azumith.
The audience u hear is thru Stipes mic, most of the crowd was jammed close to the stage.
Back by the sbd it was wide open allowing me to dance 20 yards in any direction,
which i did. What a show. Maybe someday the Boys will give us a prestine copy.
