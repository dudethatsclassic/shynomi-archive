Peter Holsapple guests on Sloop John B and Louie Louie. 

Keith Streng of The Fleshtones guests on Windout. 

After track 17, a huge birthday cake was rolled out and everyone sang "Happy Birthday" to REM's manager, Jefferson.

That portion is missing from this 1st gen copy.  But you won't notice a cut, because I blended it seamlessly.

If you don't have this R.E.M. in your collection, than this will make a nice addition.

I believe the master tape has never been circulated, though Rich believes it still exists.

This 1st gen is the next best copy.  A big thanks goes out to Rich for sending me his tape to work on and share.

For this being a hand held walkman recorder, it's very listenable.

I added in a photo of the cassette j-card.
