Notes from www.remchronicle.com:
One of the first shows on tape that features some of the new Fables songs on record, including Feeling Gravitys Pull, Maps and Legends, Life and How to Live It and Cant Get There From Here, which offer slight differentiations in the ways they were performed. These probably make this show a more important guide to see just where the band was on recording this album than any of the recordings that have surfaced so far inclucing what are referred to as the "Rough Mixes". These are much more "Rough" and still in development. While the sound quality of this particular show is not the best, the fan of Fables might enjoy it.
