Here's another rarity from Rich's collection.  This tape first came from Dave Thomas's REM collection.

This date was not in the etree database.  

I'm sure it was traded out many years ago, but I'm going to bet this will be new to a lot of REM fans.

The following nights show had them traveling all the way to St. Paul, MN. which also exists on tape.

It seems that the length of shows for this leg of the tour, were somewhat short at just over an hour.

This recording is complete, except for some minor tape cuts.

Track 12 cuts in after the tape flip,  A little music missing.

There is a tape stop / start between tracks 15 and 16.  No music missing.
