From www.remtimeline.com: "About the venue itself, from an interview by David Fricke with Peter Buck on 15 November 1990 taken from The Rolling Stone Files book (pg. 114): "There was a place in Greensboro, North Carolina, called Friday's. It was a pizza parlor, and the guy had bands play. It was an L-shaped room; you could see through the bar to the ovens, with the guy with the long stick with pizzas on it, and see us, too. He'd charge a dollar, we'd get 150 people in there, and we'd get the door. People would let us sleep on the floor. There were clubs like that in every city." 

Taken from the CD bootleg "T.G.I.F," which lists the date as March 31, 1981, but is in fact November 6, 1981. 

"Ha (We Get Paid For It)" is the arbitrary title of this song.  It's unknown what the true intended title was, as it was quickly dropped from the set and wasn't recorded in the studio.
