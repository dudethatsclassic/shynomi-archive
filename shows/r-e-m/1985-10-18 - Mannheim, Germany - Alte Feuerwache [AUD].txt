R.E.M.
Alte Feuerwache, Mannheim, Germany 
October - 18 - 1985

from MILAN collection # 084

Rec. Info:
Aiwa Walkman with plug-in mic -> Mastercassette -> transfer via Onkyo tapedeck TA-2330 -> Magix Music Cleaning Lab (edit/remaster) -> FLAC -> DIME 
This is the master tape of the show. The sound is excellent (Ex-) with the exception of some mike problems mainly at the first few songs.
Some sound level fluctuations were balanced and the audience noise between songs calmed down.

This show was on dime before uploaded by hanwaker in 2010 but definitely from another source as this recording here was done by myself.
Soundwise it is one of my best recordings if not the many mike problems would have occured.
What was not mentioned in the hanwaker upload is the fact that they start the song Maps And Legends and stop it after approx. 1 min (I can't say why).
A copy of the ticket stub is attached (the show was initially scheduled for 7th of October).
