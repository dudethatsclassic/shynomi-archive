As promised here the 2nd of the concerts I recorded of R.E.M on the Green tour. This time
from the Aud in Buffalo.I recorded this show from the side of the stage about 10 rows up and 25ft from the stage 
using a high end Sony recording walkman and Aiwa Mic. The recording turned out pretty well and I recently digitized
it without much audio adjustment. The Aud show had a much better song selection than the previous Toronto show that
I recorded and they even played "Belong" from their follow up album (not released until 2 years later) and some early
stuff. I hope you enjoy it.
