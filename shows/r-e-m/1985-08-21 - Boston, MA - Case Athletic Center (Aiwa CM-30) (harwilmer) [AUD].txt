   * the Harwilmer collection *
welcome to yet another new series here on dime (and TD for as long as possible)
featuring the recordings and acquisitions of dime member "harwilmer"
these will include a few other bands too, but mostly R.E.M.
shows from the mid 80's, since he recorded several of them, 
including a bunch from the 1985 tour. this show is one of those.
R.E.M. has been rather popular around here, even 40 years after their "heyday", 
and several of these shows are likely upgrades of earlier circulated shows, 
some may be new to digital circulation entirely, or new "alternate" sources.
this week, we have one of his several REM recordings from 1985, from Boston, Mass.
this one is a stereo recording with good channel separation, from a decent seat location, 
but the acoustics in this place are not good at all, it's an echo chamber.
everyone can still be heard clearly. 
seems to be almost the full show, except most of pretty persuasion is missing.
a fine show too, as many were in 1984 and 85. a bunch to come from this era.
as a few others here have been known to do, harwilmer copied several of his masters (including this one)
onto 1st gen. copies so to optimize tape space, thus even the ones he recorded 
are 1st gen tape copies of masters that no longer exist.
some of his acquired tapes are also 1st gen. analog cassette copies, so most of these will be 1st gen. recordings.
they all sound quite clean and apparently made without dolby.
thanks to him for sharing these with me to share with the very global R.E.M. community.
samples will be included in these posts as long as tech allows them to be,
since there will be some variance in quality.
it is quite likely most or all of these will be in the best quality 
made digitally available before from these recordings. 
(some of the trade acqusitions are good quality and a few others not as good.)
