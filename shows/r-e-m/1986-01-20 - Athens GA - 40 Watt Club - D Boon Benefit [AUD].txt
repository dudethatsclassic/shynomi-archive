This was a benefit performance for D. Boon of the Minutemen who had died in a car crash only a couple weeks before this. The Minutemen had opened several nights on the Fables tour. The show is soundboard btw, not great but soundboard nonetheless. 

There are three main things wrong with this performance.
1. Diginoise - This is part of those Athens shows that have been released recently. This one has the same issues that the others do.
2. This is not a full performance. The following songs are cut out from this disc. 
	a. Radio Free Europe
	b. Dream (All I have to do is)
	c. Just a Touch
	d. Pretty Persuasion
	e. Wild Thing
3. There is an "Accoustic" performance that takes place during this set, however it is very hard to hear the vocals of those particular songs. 

I revisited my original 1/20/86 show and I realized that with even these errors that this show is still a major upgrade for peoples collections.
