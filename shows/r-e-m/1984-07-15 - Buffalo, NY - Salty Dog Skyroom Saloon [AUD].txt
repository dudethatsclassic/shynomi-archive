Show Notes: I rate this show just below the 17 August 1985 Barrymore's show in terms of unique R.E.M. setlists. R.E.M. had always enjoyed playing cover songs in their sets, but they were usually limited to one or two, particularly by 1984, by which time they were enjoying increasing popularity after 2 critically acclaimed albums and 1 EP, and venue sizes were progressively increasing, particularly in the major cities. One week from when this show was recorded R.E.M. would be playing 2 shows at the Beacon Theatre in New York City (recordings of which also have circulated). This night at the Salty Dog Skyroom Saloon in Buffalo however saw the band play a much smaller room, packed with people yelling out requests for their own songs, none of which the band felt like playing. The catalyst for the cover songs seemed to be Peter picking up on a request for ZZ Top, which sparked a 7 song run of cover songs, some which the band had played before, others they just seemed to be winging as they went along. (Peter after playing Sharp Dressed Man - 'I told you we take requests!'). A very unique show and one every R.E.M. fan should have in their collection.
