I recorded this show from the floor about 18? rows back using a high end Sony recording walkman and Aiwa Mic. 
The recording turned out pretty well and I recently digitized it without much audio adjustment. This show was 
early on in the Green tour and the band was just getting used to their evolution into megastardom. In fact I 
sensed that Michael Stipe was still a bit uncomfortable with the mainstream success and chose to play a decidedly
"anti greatest hits" show. I guess I was expecting them to roll out the big singles, but not to be on this night. So no
"one I love", "Can't get there from here" or "fall on Me". However a nice trip back in time for me. Hope you like it 
and let me know if you want the Buffalo show from this tour (I think September of the same year) which I also recorded!
