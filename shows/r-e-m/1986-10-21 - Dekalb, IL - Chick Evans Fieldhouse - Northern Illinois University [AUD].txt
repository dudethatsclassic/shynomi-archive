Notes: this is a below average AUD recording from the Pageantry tour. 
There are a few clips of this show on YouTube,  but I have never seen a full audio version torrented.
For Superman, the band is joined on stage by Gary Zekley, who wrote the song.
You can hear Mike saying "This is Gary. He wrote Superman" and Michael adding "and we're gonna make him rich!"
This recording contains some rareties, like the fake commercial tune "Cushy Tush", and early versions of some Document songs.
All in all, this sounds like a pretty rowdy performance, with Michael in full joking mode and the whole band sounding more than a little drunk.
