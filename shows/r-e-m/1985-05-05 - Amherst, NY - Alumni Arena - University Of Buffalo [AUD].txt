Notes: this is a (mostly) really excellent sounding AUD recording of the pre-construction tour.
At first I thought it might be a SBD, but no, but it's as close as you can get. 
Except for the end of track 19 and all of track 20. They are completely corrupted and unlistenable.
That is a shame, because this is the entire show. Still, the rest is so good, it's worth a download, IMO.
