Alas it is not that, but I did find it to be a staggeringly clean copy of the “Working” LP. I cleaned it up some more using the click & pop tools in Adobe Audition 1.5, which seems to have eliminated more or less all evidence that this is from vinyl. “The One I Love” is missing from this recording, there’s a note at the bottom about that.

A note on the missing second encore:

I have been wondering about whether or not the remtimeline allocation of “The One I Love” to the third encore was true - finishing with the big hit single doesn’t seem like something the 1987 version of R.E.M. would have done. So I’ve been looking for clues as to where else in the set “The One I Love” could have featured. During the break between “Pop Song 89” and “Ghostrider”, Stipe berates those who yelled over the acapella song, other than the very brief “Simple Gifts” there is no acapella song in this recording. So I figured, that Stipe is referring to “Harpers”. As with the other California shows, it is probable that “The One I Love” was played after “Harpers”, whether any other songs were played is a moot point (in the original sense), but “Strange” is the only other obviously missing song. The missing songs could come before or after See No Evil, most likely after because there’s a noticeable cut in the recording here. They could also conceivably have been played before 1,000,000, but having them in the middle of the encores feels right when compared to the two previous nights.

I would welcome input from any one who can help solve the mystery (if indeed it is a mystery).
