

R.E.M.
PalaEur, Roma - Italy
22nd February 1995



I received this show long time ago in a tape trade.
The recording is basically complete, only the song no.25 is missing by a few opening seconds, and the audio
quality is more than acceptable also considering the "catastrophic" acustic of the place. 
The show was affected from two power outages who stopped instantly the sound, leaving the venue
in a complete obscurity for minutes.
I have a little worked on the sound to made it clearest/ crispest doing nothing of distructive anyway,
to avoid to ruin it.
Ok, I hope you like it. All the best to everybody!
