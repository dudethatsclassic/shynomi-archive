COMMENTS
"Fab Fab Fab" i wrote in my diary that night, it was a hot summers night and the Go-Betweens were support band starting at 8pm. 
There are now two known sources of this show. This mondoprune recording is a significant upgrade to the other source (unknown taper).
As well, the other source missed 9 songs that are all on this recording. The circulating versions of the other recording are very problematic with major speed issues and numerous gaps throughout songs that were likely caused by a dodgy transfer process or faulty CD burn. If anyone knows anything about who taped that other source or has a version closer to the master without the gap issues please send a PM. It would be great to find a 'clean' version of that other tape and properly patch it together with this mondoprune source to create a complete version of this show.
