This was a great concert, despite the terribly bad thunderstorms that hammered down throughout the entire night and forced everyone to evacuate before R.E.M. took the stage.  In the end, it turned out to be another incredible R.E.M. show, with many nice surprises tossed in, not the least of which was ITEOTWAWKI.

Because of how bad the weather was, there was no real encore.

Despite the noisy rain and thunder, the audio on this recording sounds very good.  We were in about the 10th row, right in the center.  In between songs, you may hear what sounds like old-time LP record hiss and crackle.  That was the RAIN hitting the umbrella!

I hope you like this, my very first Dime torrent.  I will also be recording Philadelphia and NYC this week.  Stay tuned!
