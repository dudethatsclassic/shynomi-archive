R.E.M.Nippon Budokan, Tokyo Japan1st February 1995Gear and taper: unknown Cassette transfer via Technics RSBX404 through a MOTU 828MKII into Logic pro at 24bit/44.1kHz. Noise reduction (extensive tape hiss only) by waves Z-noise. EQ by Kush electra and Kush clariphonic. Subtle level enhancement and dithering to 16 bit by slate digital FG-X. Freac convert to FLAC and md5 files created by MD5.* I received this tape in a trade back in 1996. Again, it’s a show that I haven’t seen online anywhere else. Sadly, 6 songs are missing in the middle of the set which is a real shame because the setlist was fantastic. If anyone has a complete version of this show it would be amazing to have. Again, this probably doesn’t appeal to the purists but I thought i’d share this very rare show so that people can help complete their collections. *01 What’s the frequency Kenneth?
