So I found out quite by chance that this show isn't well circulated, even amongst the hardcore collectors of old, so much so that there were serious questions raised over 
whether this recording even existed. The quality is OK, not amazing. It will definitely fill a gap in your collection with a fine and entertaining show 
from a great period in the history of R.E.M. no doubt!

From R.E.M. Timeline:
Steve Wynn guests on vocals & guitar on 'There She Goes Again'. 
'Ghost Riders In The Sky' was performed 1st as an encore, then Stipe and Wynn came out on stage with their arms and legs linked, 3-legged race style.
