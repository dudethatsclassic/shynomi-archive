REM Westwood One In Concert Pre-FM

1995 at the Milton Keyes Arena in London

This was broadcast live worldwide as it happened, and Westwood One rebroadcast it a year later on their In Concert series. I bought the WWOne discs at a swap meet, so this is direct off the silvers. I bought it because it has about the best version of Country Feedback I've heard the band do.

Two problems here:

1. They always tend to edit these down when they rebroadcast, so if anyone has any missing songs, please seed them.

2. I've done the full unedited discs here, with all commercials, etc, intact because there seems to be demand for that around here. Because of that, both discs of the double-disc set are here. The music alone, however, would fit on one disc. If there is any call for me to do an edited version of this -- without commercials, announcers, and the songs properly tracked out -- let me know and I'll do another edited seed.
