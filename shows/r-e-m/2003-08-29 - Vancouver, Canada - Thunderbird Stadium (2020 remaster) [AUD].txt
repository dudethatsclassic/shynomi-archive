Show notes: After a month break recuperating from the European tour (except Peter, Scott and Bill who played a Minus 5 gig in Seattle on 15 August), the opening night of the North American greatest hits tour saw the band starting out in one of their favourite cities, Vancouver. A terrific setlist full of classics, but perhaps this show is best remembered for Thom Yorke's guest appearance, singing the Patti Smith part on E-Bow The Letter, and joining in on the finale End Of The World (not that you can really tell listening to the audio). Radiohead were scheduled to play the same venue the following night (what a great weekend for those in the area!), and Michael returned the favour the following night and sang on Karma Police, which I included here as a bonus track. This was the first performance of 'World Leader Pretend' since 15 June 1991, as well as the tour debut for 'Nightswimming' and 'E-Bow The Letter'. A terrific start to the North American tour.

Recording notes: This is a fine recording by Greg, not the best of the 2003 tour, but pretty good nonetheless. I gave it a spit and polish in iZotope and I think it sounds better for it. If you have this show already I consider this an upgrade.

Torrent notes: I was positive this had been on Dime before, but I could not find it in the Dime archives. I can't even remember where I got it from, so I'm not sure how widely circulated this one is. It's a great show and deserves to be everyone's collections.
