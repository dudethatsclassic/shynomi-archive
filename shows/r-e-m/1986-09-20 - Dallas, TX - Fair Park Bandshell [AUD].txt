Today's closet archive offering is from perhaps my favorite REM tour, recorded in the middlle of a packed general admission crowd 15 feet from the stage (note Stipe's appeal from off stage about 15 minutes before they came out).  I'm really not sure how I pulled this one off as I was in the middle of the mess he is talking to and it was indeed packed shoulder to shoulder, once I got the mics up high enough to record there was no moving them up down or sideways.

My master tape untouched with the exception of a bit of volume smoothing.
