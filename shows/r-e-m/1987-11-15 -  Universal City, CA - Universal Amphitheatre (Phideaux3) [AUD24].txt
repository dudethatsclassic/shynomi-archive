Notes, rambles, etc.:

There’s a vinyl boot of this show (that’s been torrented here, probably more than once) called “Working” that’s missing ‘Driver 8,’ Dark Globe,’ and ‘Harpers.’    The info file said “According to The Timeline the last song played was The One I Love. This is missing from my recording.”  The last thing you hear on my recording just after the crowd goes ‘Ugh’ from the lights being turned on is my friend saying “Surprise, surprise.  No 'One I Love'.”

There were a number of mic crunches (I’m thinking loose or bad cable) mostly occurred in between songs and were easily excised, though there are a couple spots in songs where the volume drops because of it.  Tape flip eats the end of ‘Riders in the Sky.’  Tape swap was done during 2nd encore break, and caused the loss of the very beginning of ‘Harpers.’
