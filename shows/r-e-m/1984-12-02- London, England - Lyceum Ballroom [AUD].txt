R.E.M. - The Lyceum, London – December 2, 1984

Audience master > ? > Cassette > CDR > dBpowerAMP > WAV > FLAC Frontend > Flac files

Some nice ’84 shows have been posted over the past few weeks, and this was always one of my favorite tapes, so I finally got around to converting it.  This is a nice, crisp recording with lots of high end.  I do not know the lineage, but I got my tape from a person in London about one year after the show took place, so I am assuming it is very close to the master.
 
Great show, nice setlist.  There are a few Fables songs, including “Wendell Gee”, which they did not play very often.  Not much else to say.  Maybe they met Joe Boyd and chose him as Fables producer when they were there for this tour?
