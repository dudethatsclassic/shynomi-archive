Notes:-

This show is somewhat rare as it has only been recently circulating among trading circles. 
This is a soundboard with a bit of a diginoise aftertaste to the recording. 
I still think it is quite listenable.

Remaster Notes:-

As the above notes state this has some diginoise with it.
Removed the noise best I could & remastered it.
It ran way too fast to my ears so it has been slowed down a fair amount.
A great recording of a early show by the band, a must for fans!
Sounds great with "the treatment" applied if a little bright at times, 
not the end of the world but you should know about it.
For me this is the version to listen to.
Hope you enjoy....

C.
