Notes:
I received this show a while back on two cd-rs in a trade. I left the original track splits but made separate tracks when Michael's
talking got a little too long so you can skip it more easily. I also combined the ending of the first cd-r and the beginning of the
second.
I think this is a real fun show with more banter and improv bits than usual.
I love this version of Country Feedback - beautiful and intense - it never fails to amaze me.
This concert was broadcast as 'R.E.M. Grand Live' on SVT2 (Swedish TV). Michael Stipe mentions that they are also live on the radio.
I couldn't find further info about that.
I have taken the info about the 'main set' and the 'encores' from remtimeline.com.

Checksums and a cover are included in the torrent.

Please support the artists in any way you can.
