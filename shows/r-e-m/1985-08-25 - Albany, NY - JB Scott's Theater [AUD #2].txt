August 25, 1985 - JB Scott's Theater, Albany, NY  

Sony TCD-6M/Nacamichi CM-300 > Audience cassette master > Cassette > Cassette > CDR > dBpowerAMP > WAV > FLAC Frontend > Flac files 

Here's another David O. Thomas recording, and it is very similar to all of the others.  I think this does not sound as good as Toronto, but it is better than the Barrymore's show.  Probably somewhere between A-/EX- and B+/VG+.    

This is another great show from this run.  The reason to get this one is the version of "Time is on MY Side", which is mostly faithful to the Stones version and I think the only time it was played by R.E.M.  The "Satisfaction" medley is much sloppier.  Overall, a great show and recording.
