Show notes: The 25th show of the North American leg of the greatest hits tour takes the band up into the New England area. Although a fairly standard setlist for this leg of the tour with no real rarities, of note the woman Mike wrote Rockville about, Ingrid Schorr, is in attendance and he dedicates the song to her. Michael also tells the audience he wrote Fall On Me in Boston, which if true must have meant he wrote the lyrics in early May 1985 when the band played at the Massachusetts Institute Of Technology in Cambridge, 3 months before the song made its live debut, which is entirely plausible. It's a fine show with another terrific setlist.
