R.E.M.

27 October 1985 - The Powerhouse - Birmingham

This is the first of 3 shows (28th and 29th Oct ’85 being the other two) which were all taped by the same individual. 
This is an audience recording, of that I'm pretty sure, and it's another decent-sounding tape. 

The tapes for the 28th and 29th by the way are taken directly from original masters for the bootlegs Can't Get There From Here LP, 
and These Days CD. The source is the person who put those boots together, so I'm told.

Audience tape came from Tim again, and the conversion was by me.
