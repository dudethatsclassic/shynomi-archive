Notes by viper:

This is another I picked up from a trade back in the late 80's and had been boxed up in my garage until recently (when I had a friend transfer several shows from cassette tapes to WAV files).  Not a huge R.E.M. fan but I attended quite a few shows at Maxwell's in Hoboken (late 80's up until their "closing week" a few years back), so I picked this one from another guy's list.  According to the setlist.fm page, this is the first known performance of "7 Chinese Brothers".  Enjoy!
