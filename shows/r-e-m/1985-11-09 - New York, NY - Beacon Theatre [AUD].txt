notes from Stonecutter: A very rare acoustic set from R.E.M. It might be worth noting, that as far as I can remember, this is their one and only public acoustic set of the entire 1980s. Something they wouldn't do again til 1991 and then very rarely after that as well (except, of course, their 2 MTV Unplugged shows).

these are the notes from R.E.M. Timeline: "R.E.M. performed at the end of the awards show, which was broadcast on MTV, & hosted by Cheech & Chong. R.E.M. won Best Album Award for 'Fables Of The Reconstruction'. '(Don't Go Back To) Rockville' features Peter Holsapple on guitar and Susanna Hoffs & Michael Steele of The Bangles on backing vocals. This show was made infamous due to Peter Buck throwing his guitar at one of the television crew members and storming off the stage after 'Can't Get There From Here'."

Please Note: To our knowledge, no video of this R.E.M. performance has ever surfaced. If you know of any video of any portion of this performance, PLEASE let us know!! Thanks [doesn't it seem odd that the incident with peter includes throwing a guitar at a crew member, if they weren't taping?)
