Notes: I received this in a trade not long after this show was recorded (sorry I can't remember who) and the CD's have been long gathering dust. I came across the CD's while doing some house cleaning and thought it was worthwile sharing, as to my knowledge it's never been widely circulated before, and I'm pretty sure it's never been uploaded anywhere before. A pretty good audience recording (don't know what equipment was used) and a great show - this ended up being R.E.M.'s final Australian tour and while they didn't have the popularity they did when they previously toured in 1995 (this being the tour promoting Around the Sun certainly didn't help ticket sales), they were in top form, and the Around the Sun songs sound so much better live than they did on the album. A very worthy show to add to your R.E.M. collection!
