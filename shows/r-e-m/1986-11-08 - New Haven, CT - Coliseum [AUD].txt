8 November 1986 - 
Coliseum , New Haven, CT 

Source: 1st generation analog cassette
Taper: David O Thomas
Equipment: Sony TCD-6M/Nacamichi CM-300 
Lineage: cassette master>cassette>Dak's Wave Editor>wav>flac

Dave and I worked very close to each other.  He would let me borrow his equipment to tape shows.
I would return his equipment with a copy of the show I taped and he would give me shows he taped.
He gave my roommate and I alot of REM shows.  Some of those are not widely circulated.
This seems to be one of them.  Another great show and another very good recording.  He had a
problem during Harborcoat (Track 2) and there is 3:49 of dead air.  The dead air is track 3.  Track 4 is
Lightning Hopkins as it cuts in. You can decide if you want to include the dead air.  

Enjoy!!
