***Additional Info***

When I acquired these files the md5 verified correctly, but upon inspection tracks 11 & 12 had either been joined together at

some point, or they hadn't been split to begin with.  Audacity was used to split the tracks, & also to speed-correct the files

(an increase of 9%) so that the band no longer sounded like they'd been feasting on valium liberally sprinkled with mogadon so

the lineage for these files is now :

Unknown Gen Cassette > Technics RS-BX501 Stereo Cassette Deck 
> Line Out > Roland R-05 Wave/MP3 Recorder > WAV > SD Card > 
PC > CD Wave Editor (Track Split) > FLAC (Level 8) > Audacity (Track split & speed corr.) > Wav > TLH (Flac8) > Dime...


The gig itself was an interesting one in front of around 40,000 highly partisan U2 fans, who spent most of the day waiting

impatiently for their gods, amusing themselves by hurling abuse & missiles (bottles, cans, food, anything really) at anything

that was non-U2 related & dared to step onstage...add to that bad weather throughout the day, however the taper managed to get 

a fairly decent pull of REM - it's not crystal clear, but it's certainly listenable...

The info file which came with these files has been included, along with a new md5 to allow for the track split. No ffp was 

included, so one has been created as the tracks required renaming as well...


Don't turn these to mp3, decode them to wav & burn 'em...
