Timeline notes: During the soundcheck, Michael was sitting in the audience section listening to the 3 instrumentals and writing down lyric ideas, before joining the band onstage to sing the remaining songs. Amanda Brown plays violin on 'Losing My Religion', 'Binky The Doormat', 'Strange Currencies', 'Find The River' & 'Everybody Hurts'. 'Find The River' is played for the 1st time. 'So Fast, So Numb' performed at soundcheck was released on 1996's 'New Adventures In Hi-Fi'.
