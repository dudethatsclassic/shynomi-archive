R.E.M.Southpark Meadows, Austin Texas. 17th September 1995Gear and taper: unknown Cassette transfer via Technics RSBX404 through a MOTU 828MKII into Logic pro at 24bit/44.1kHz. Noise reduction (extensive tape hiss only) by waves Z-noise. EQ by Kush electra and Kush clariphonic. Subtle level enhancement and dithering to 16 bit by slate digital FG-X. Freac convert to FLAC and md5 files created by MD5.* I purchased this concert on cassette tape in a shop in Dublin in 1996. There are short cuts between so many of the songs but I’m not sure if this happened during the recording of the show or whether the person who compiled the concert did it in order to fit the entire concert onto a 90 minute cassette tape. A number of the songs (as listed on the track listing) have instances where the song either cuts in or cuts out before the end of the song. This probably isn’t a concert that the purists will love but this is such a rare recording that I thought it was still worth sharing *01 I took your name (cuts in)02 What’s the frequency Kenneth?03 Crush with eyeliner04 the wake-up bomb05 Bang and blame06 Binky the doormat07 The one I love08 Undertow09 Strange currencies10 Revolution11 Tongue12 Man on the moon13 Country feedback (cut)14 Losing my religion15 Departure16 Pop song 8917 Get up (cuts in)18 Star 6919 Let me in (Cuts in)20 Everybody hurts21 Band intros22 So. central rain23 Begin the begin (cuts in)24 It’s the end of the world as we know itSupport R.E.M. by purchasing official releases and merchandise.Do not profit in any material way from this recording.
