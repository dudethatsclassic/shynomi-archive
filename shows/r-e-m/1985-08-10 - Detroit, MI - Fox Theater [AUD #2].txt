Comments: First time seeing REM and I was really excited for this show.
We were in the 1st Balcony section not too far up, and a little stage 
right of center. (Closer to center than to the wall). The sound was
not great up there, and the shear loudness of REM blew out our ear
drums soon into the show. The recording came out about average for
the location and equipment used. One good thing was that REM plays
pretty loud continuously, not allowing the mics to "relax", and making
for a decent recording. REM's vocals were not audible to us during the show,
however, they are clearer in this recording. So here is a rare REM from
the "Reconstruction I Tour", enjoy!

Recorder Note: 

Before REM took the stage 3 other bands played, a local band, then The Three
O'Clock, and then The Replacements. we did not record any of them.
This recording has never been traded, and is uncirculated.
Back then I probably re-used the metal tape master, that is why I only have
the 1st gen copy off the master.
