OK this recording was kindly sent to me by Amy a while ago. Its not the best recording in world but its the only known recording of this show.

There are terrible EQ and bass distortion problems, some pitch issues in some places and 'Disturbance At The Heron House' has oddly merged with bits of 'Exhuming McCarthy'. 

This recording is for die hards only who want 'every' show and people who were there and are after the memories. But im glad to see there is a record of this show.
