R.E.M.
1st of April, 2005 (2nd Show)
Sydney Entertainment Centre
Sydney, Australia

Original Source: Audience, recorded with a Canon Powershot S60.

Lineage: Straight from the camera into the computer.

Quality: Depending on what video quality, excellent to poor. Most excellent, tried to keep the quality high without running out of space on the camera's memory card. Missed the last couple at each concert i.e. unfortunately missed 'DJ' both times and 'country feedback' in Melbourne.

Summary and Review: Pics + Video from the 2nd Sydney show. They were having heaps of fun - very close to their 25th anniversary. Peter kept pulling out the oldies (i.e. see all the Document songs). My favorite show of the two I attended, perhaps since it was the first (the other was the Melbourne show).
