REM at Brown University, Providence RI, 27 April 1985.

Yet another tape bought at Camden Markets 1985. This was pretty exciting to find as Fables had not yet been released, so there was 12 new songs to me including the 9 from Fables.

Pretty clear audience recording, check the sample. Cuts for tape flip between tracks 12 and 13 for tape flip and between 20 and 21 to cut out audience between encores.
