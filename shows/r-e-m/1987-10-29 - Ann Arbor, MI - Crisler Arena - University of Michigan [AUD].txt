REM
1987-10-29
Crisler Arena-University of Michigan
Ann Arbor MI

Recorded By: Robert Mullen

Source: Audience Master Cassettes

Equipment/Lineage: 2 DAK-5245 Mics>Sony D6c Cassette Recorder
(Dolby C on)>Master Cassette's (TDK-SA-X90's)>Yamaha Double Cassette 
Deck K-902 (Dolby C off)>CD Wave Editor>THL flac level 8.

Comments: Here is a very nice REM concert I recorded from right behind
the soundboard. I had just plugged in my mics...and they started...then 
I pressed record...missed a second or so. During Auctioneer had to flip
the tape...missed just a bit there. Otherwise, this recording is 
complete. This was a period of REM I really enjoyed. 

I noticed there is an incomplete version of this concert around from a 
different recorder. This recording has never been traded, and is 
uncirculated.
