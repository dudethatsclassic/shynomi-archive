Show notes: 2005 saw the band on a very large world tour, beginning the year in Europe before heading to South Africa for the first time, then back to Japan for the first time in 10 years. This was the second show of the Japanese leg after Tokyo the previous night in the city of Nagoya. A very enthusiastic Japanese audience was on hand, and they were treated to some lesser played songs on the tour such as Maps And Legends, Sweetness Follows and The Wake-Up Bomb, but this was a tour to promote 'Around The Sun', which got 6 tracks here. One of the recurring tropes from the 2005 and 2008 tours was Michael's introduction of 'Imitation Of Life' as their first ever number 1 single... in Japan. Here he definitely got a thrill out of introducing it that way while in Japan. A fun show in front of a very appreciative crowd.

Recording notes: This is a pretty decent recording, not as good as Osaka the following night, but an enjoyable listen nonetheless. I gave it a touch up in iZotope to make it sound a little nicer, and also removed some small gaps between the songs, presumably a remnant from its CDR lineage, as well as seamlessly rejoin the 2 CD's. If you enjoy listening to later era R.E.M. you'll like this one.
