Pine Knob Music Theater
Clarkston, Michigan

Recorded and transfered by: Robert Mullen (lvrwm)

Source: Audience Master Cassettes

Transfer Date: 2010-11-20

Equipment/Lineage: 2 DAK-5245 Mics>Sony D6c Portable Stereo Cassette Recorder
(Dolby C on)>Master Cassette's (TDK-SA-X90's)>Yamaha Natural Sound Stereo Cassette 
Deck K-902 (Dolby C off)>CD Wave Editor(tracking)>TLH flac level 8.

Comments: Here is a bit better than average recording, and a good performance (IMHO),
at the "Knob". We were located a few rows in front of the soundboard, and a little stage
right of center. The sound was decent from that location. The crowd was very excited 
during this show, and not too badly behaved near us. Enjoy this "Green Tour" recording.
