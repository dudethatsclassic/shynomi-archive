7 September 1995 - 1995 MTV Video Music Awards, Radio City Music Hall, New York, NY
other acts performing: Michael Jackson, Live, TLC, Red Hot Chili Peppers, Bon Jovi, Alanis Morissette, Hootie & The Blowfish, Hole, Green Day, White Zombie
R.E.M. soundcheck: The Wake-Up Bomb
set:
Michael Jackson: Don't Stop Till You Get Enough - The Way You Make Me Feel - Scream - Beat It - Thriller - Black Or White - Billie Jean / Dangerous - Smooth Criminal / You Are Not Alone
Live: I Alone
TLC: ('CrazySexy Medley') Ain't 2 Proud 2 Beg - Kick Your Game - Creep - Waterfalls
R.E.M. (Introduced by Natalie Merchant): The Wake-Up Bomb
Red Hot Chili Peppers: Warped
Bon Jovi: Helter Skelter - Something For The Pain (Times Square, New York, NY, 7 September 1995)
Alanis Morissette: You Oughtta Know
Hootie & The Blowfish: Only Wanna Be With You
Hole: Violet
Green Day: Stuck With Me (Johanneshovs Isstadion, Stockholm, Sweden, 2 September 1995)
White Zombie: More Human Than Human
notes: 1995 MTV Video Music Awards. R.E.M. performed 'The Wake-Up Bomb', after introduction by Natalie Merchant. R.E.M. were nominated for 'Viewers Choice' video for 'What's The Frequency, Kenneth?', but lost out to TLC's 'Waterfalls'. After introduction by Drew Barrymore, R.E.M. were presented with the Michael Jackson Video Vanguard Award, which was accepted by all 4 members. Bon Jovi played live from Times Square, whereas the footage of Green Day was filmed 5 days earlier in Stockholm.
