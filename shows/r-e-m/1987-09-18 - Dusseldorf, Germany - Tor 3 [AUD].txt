This is the second in series of posts celebrating R.E.M. on their lesser known Work Tour, which happened to coincide with R.E.M.’s transition into mass market radio in the US. It will be interesting to see how the character of the shows changes as The One I Love becomes increasingly popular. At this stage of the tour R.E.M. are playing 4 quick promo shows in Europe to relatively small audiences, before heading back to the USA to rehearse properly for the 45 date US tour.

I’m posting this particular show not because of the quality, which is only so so, but very listenable, but because of the energy and vitality of the performance. For all its flaws this is one of my favourite R.E.M. shows, un-rehearsed and going full tilt – check out the botched opening to Begin The Begin, the crowd lapping it up.

There’s a bunch of great moments in this show:

The introduction to Auctioneer is messed up, but the band sort of cover. The Caroline and Lake story familiar from the Pagentry shows is recited over the outro to Auctioneer rather than the intro.

Between the 2nd and 3rd encores Stipe delievers a solo rendition of Angel. The rest of the band quickly reassemble, Stipe says “Good luck guys” and the song begins in earnest.

Prior to 1,000,000 the band are trying agree what to play next, first Mills indicates his preference by bashing out the bass line to Toys in the Attic, then Berry does the intro to We Walk. Mills has another go at Toys, Stipe offers a fragment of Whole Lotta Love and then they launch into 1,000,000. 

Like I said it’s not the finest quality, but it’s a really fun one to listen to.
