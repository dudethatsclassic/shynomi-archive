24 July 1984 - Warner Theater, Washington DC 

CDRs from snail mail trade>EAC>Flac Level 6>you

I have no taper information for this. However the sound is very clean and there is little hiss, so this seems to be a very low-gen source.

This was originally turned-up by Brett as I recall and shared among the hardcore collectors. I got this from Eric von Pariscarters within a few days of the original circulation.

Quality is fairly decent, bit distant, but clean.
