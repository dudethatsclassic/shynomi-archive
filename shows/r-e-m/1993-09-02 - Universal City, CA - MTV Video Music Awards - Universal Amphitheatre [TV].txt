2 September 1993 - MTV Video Music Awards, Universal Amphitheatre, Universal City, CA
other acts performing: Madonna, Lenny Kravitz, Sting, Soul Asylum, Aerosmith, Naughty By Nature, Spin Doctors, Pearl Jam, The Edge, Janet Jackson
set:
Madonna: Bye Bye Baby
Lenny Kravitz: Are You Gonna Go My Way?
Sting: If I Ever Lose My Faith In You
Soul Asylum: Runaway Train (w/ Victoria Williams & Peter Buck)
Aerosmith: Livin' On The Edge
Naughty By Nature: Hip Hop Hooray
R.E.M.: Everybody Hurts - Drive
Spin Doctors: Two Princes
Pearl Jam: Animal / Rockin' In The Free World (w/ Neil Young)
The Edge: Numb
Janet Jackson: That's The Way Love Goes / If
notes: MTV Video Music Awards. Peter Buck joins Soul Asylum on guitar for 'Runaway Train', Brian Harris joins on bass on 'Everybody Hurts' & 'Drive' & Duane Saetveit on french horn on 'Everybody Hurts'. Christian Slater introduced R.E.M. prior to their performance.
