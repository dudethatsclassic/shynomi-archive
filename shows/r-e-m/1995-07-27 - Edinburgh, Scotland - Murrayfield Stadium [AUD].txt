There has been some discussion about the this REM show  and many theories have been put forward about its source.  No one has arrived at a definitive answer  but I’ve heard various theories about it.  It’s a liberated soundboard tape, it was an FM broadcast but only aired in Eastern Europe, it’s the best  audience tape you’ve ever heard…   You takes your pick :) 

What is clear is that this is a completely different show from the widely circulated Milton Keynes Bowl BBC broadcast from the same year which was, as far as I know, the only other FM broadcast from the 1995 Monster tour.

The CDs came to me with the TV broadcasts on the end of the second disc so I’ve left them on.  It might fill in a few gaps for the completists who, no doubt will be thrilled at the prospect of so many virtually identical versions of Daysleeper!.
