It was amazing evening of beautiful R.E.M. music, as performed by 20+ outstanding artists!

I had excellent, first-level center balcony seats, but acoustically, this was a very difficult show to record, mainly because each artist had their own sound volume and style.  Certain songs, like Sitting Still or Carnival of Sorts, are overpowering while others, like Wendell Gee or At My Most Beautiful, recorded much better.  Overall, I would give this recording a B-, but it is definitely worth the download! :-)

Thanks and enjoy!  And, thanks R.E.M., for doing what you do.
