This is a fresh transfer at 24/48(Re-sampled in soundforge) of my master tape that was used for the boot CD of the same name.  The name came from Bill Berry having to be hospitalized due to a brain anneurysm, effectively canceling the remaining Europe dates and the first few dates of the US tour, making this the opening night.  Great show, rained for most of the night, thankfully Shoreline has a tent for most of the seated area and stage.
***The Artwork for the CD has Get Up and Star69 in reverse order.**
Sonic Youth opened.
