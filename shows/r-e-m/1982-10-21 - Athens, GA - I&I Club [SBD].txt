The first of two soundboard recordings from consecutive nights at the I&I Club.  

No-one knows if these were the complete sets but the quality is really good.

These recordings originally came from "a respected author and journalist" who  made a cassette copy  
for another a respected author and journalist" around ten years ago.  The recordings were cleaned  
up in Cool Edit Pro sorting out a couple of tracks which were split, had gaps etc.  They were I think 
orignally seeded on the now defunct dedicated REM tracker.

The CD-r on which I received them a couple of years ago was extracted using EAC then everything else was done on TLH
