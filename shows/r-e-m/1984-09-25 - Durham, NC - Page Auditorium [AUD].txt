Notes: this is the last of Chris S's shows from 1984. It is a fair AUD recording.
Attention: this is NOT the show that is widely known from the bootlegs "Unreleased Live LP" and "We Are Having A Heavenly Time".
That is the show that was played the next day, in the same venue. 
Confusingly, that often bootlegged show is sometimes listed as having been played on this date (1984-09-25 instead of 1984-09-26).
The show that is being offered here is not from any known bootleg, and I have not seen it on any tracker before, OR on anyone's trade list except Chris's.
