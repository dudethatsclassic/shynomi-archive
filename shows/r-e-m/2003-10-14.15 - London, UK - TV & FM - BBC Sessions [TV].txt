Recorded from my masters, these are two sessions done for BBC radio & tv on a brief promotional tour of Europe.  The Radcliffe session is

intact, & features an entertaining interview & a cracking version of 'Orange Crush', whilst the Holland session is truncated - during the

interview, the band are shown a brief clip of them performing 'Pretty Persuasion' on the OGWT from 1984 - as this has been released

officially on a BBC dvd of the OGWT, the clip has been removed.  Similarly, 'Nightswimming' was released officially on a BBC dvd of various

'Later' perfomances, & has also been removed.  Nothing else from these sessions has been released officially...
