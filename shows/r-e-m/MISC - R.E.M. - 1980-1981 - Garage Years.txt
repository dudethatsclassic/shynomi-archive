Before REM released their debut EP 'Chronic Town' in 1982 and first album 'Murmurs' in 1983 they already been a band for a couple of years. They formed in 1980 en played many shows in the Athens area (most notably Tyrone's) for a year or two, somehow soundboard recordings of some of those shows exist. So, that 31 years later we can still listen to one the greatest Rock & Roll bands ever in their embryonic stages.

I've compiled four historic early shows. Disc one will get you their earliest know performance and a performance at Wuxtry Records. Three legendary soundboard recordings from Tyrone's are on the other discs.

-1980-05-30 (40 Watt Club)
- 1980-06-06 (Wuxtry Records)
- 1980-10-04 (Tyrone's)
- 1981-04-10 (Tyrone's)
- 1981-05-12 (Tyrone's)

You'll hear some early versions of songs from Chronic Town and Murmur aswell as some covers, but you will also hear many many unreleased tracks. Unreleased tracks like: Baby I, Route 66, Lisa Says, Wait, A Girl Like You, Narrator, Bodycount, Action, Nervous Breakdown, Rave On, Hey Hey Nadine and many others....
