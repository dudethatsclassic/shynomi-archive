This is a straight transfer of the "Half A World Away Part One" R.E.M.  (bootleg) CD. 

Half of this is R.E.M.'s KCRW 3rd April 1991 radio session, the rest is made up of the Christmas singles they gave to fans from 1988  to around 1991 (ie up to just before the CD as released). As it states in the article linked to below - "From 1988 until 2011, the Athens rockers sent limited-edition Christmas packages to members of their official fanclub. "
http://www.theguardian.com/music/2013/aug/13/rem-release-rare-singles-charity

I have been advised that if REM gave away the singles this should be ok to upload.

I shoud add that on reading these might be released one day, particularly as a possible charity release, I did not feel comfortable about trying 'perfecting' this recording - so I did not use Click Repair for example. I believe that these 'un de-clicked' singles are a good 'taster' for any future, more complete collection. 

Also with regard to the KRCW radio session, I already put a 'processed version up as a torrent here, so 
I thought a 'raw' version might not be a bad idea. This CD is described as the best CD of this session in one of the REM bootleg guides - well, there is slight difference in quality between this version and the Live in USA 1991 CD, but it is just slight - so it is also a different version than the current dime torrent of the latter CD.
