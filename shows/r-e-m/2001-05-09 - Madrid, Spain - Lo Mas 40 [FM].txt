Here we have a rare FM broadcast from REM's visit to Madrid's Lo Mas 40 studios.

It appears that all of the songs played, weren't broadcast over the radio.

There is a DVD of this that does include additional songs.

Songs not broadcast are "I've Been High / She Just Wants To Be / Find The River / Cuyahoga".

The benefit of this FM tape, is that it includes 22 minutes of interviews, done with a translator.

Along with 4 live tracks.

This was spread over a 3 hour DAT tape, which I had to scan through and pull all the REM material together.

Removing all the commercials and other non related music.

This is definitely the entire broadcast. I believe I did a good job at editing this together.

Thanks to Rich for collecting this gem, now to be enjoyed here for the first time.
