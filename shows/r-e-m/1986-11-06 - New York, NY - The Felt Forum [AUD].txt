Rapid Eye Movement presents REM-25: FELT FORUM, NYC 11/6/86
Recorded: The Felt Forum, New York, NY, Nov. 6th 1986

As long time REM traders know good quality Pageantry Tour shows are 
real rarities so it's my great pleasure to announce the release of 
this particular beauty, taken from the first night of the band's two 
night run at the Felt Forum - the 2nd night was released as REM-14 
and this show matches it for sound quality and performance. On this 
particular night a very active audience caused major problems 
crushing against the stage and R.E.M. can be heard imploring them to 
move back and spare the unfortunates at the front. Unfortunately the 
crowd were way too out of control resulting in the band cutting short 
their set and quitting the stage early. However, this doesn't detract 
from the performance and with inspired takes on old "chesnuts" 
like 'Pilgrimage' and 'Moral Kiosk' alongside the new songs 
from 'Pageant' this weed also showcases some fine takes of Fables 
classics like 'Auctioneer', the brooding meltdown of 'Feeling 
Gravitys Pull' and a majestic 'Old Man Kensey'. This one's a keeper 
folks.
Enjoy!
