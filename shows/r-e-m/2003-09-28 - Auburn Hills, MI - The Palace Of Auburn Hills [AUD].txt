Show notes: After starting September in the Pacific time zone, the greatest hits tour rolls into the Eastern time zone much to Michael's delight (even though the previous days show was also in the Eastern time zone), and the band continue rolling out the chestnuts, including a very rarely performed 'Shaking Through' in the first half of the main set for the second time in two days, and 'Radio Free Europe' in the encore. Michael is even in such a good mood he tells the audience a story about seeing Aerosmith when he was 15 in 1975, then tries to sing a few bars of 'Sweet Emotion' which the band joins in on, but doesn't get far. Perhaps Toys In The Attic would have been better? He does reveal a little known fact however, that 'Me In Honey' is a re-write of Sweet Emotion. That little snippet has never been well publicised. A terrific show.

Recording notes: This is not the greatest recording from this tour, but it's not bad. While it's quite clear and crisp, it unfortunately suffered from distortion during the louder portions of the music. I ran the 'deconstruct' module in iZotope RX8 to try and eliminate it, and it helped, but it's not a miracle worker. Still, it's very listenable and one you'll enjoy listening to, though if you want just the best 2003 recordings in your collection, this is not the one for you. Samples provided so you can decide for yourself.
