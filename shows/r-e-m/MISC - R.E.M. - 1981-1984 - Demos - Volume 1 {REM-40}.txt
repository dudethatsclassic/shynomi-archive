DISC 1 TRACKS 7-14: RCA Studios, New York, NY February '82
DISC 2 TRACKS 1-6: Reflection Studios, Charlotte, NC February '83
DISC 2 TRACKS 7-10: Rhythmic Studios, San Francisco November '83
DISC 2 TRACKS 11-15: Reflection Sound Studios, Charlotte, NC
Dec '83/Jan '84

Ok so why would I be interested in a collection of R.E.M.'s studio
demos you may ask yourself, well apart from some rare outtakes which
are "a must" it's the performances of these old familiar songs that
stand out as being essential - this is raw R.E.M. before the songs
were polished by Mitch Easter and Don Dixon and so quite a different
proposition from the layered textures of 'Murmur' and chiming sounds
of 'Reckoning'. But perhaps more importantly the first disc
illustrates just how extraordinarily accomplished as songwriters the
band had become in little over a year of treading the boards. The
rough hewn 60's flavoured garage rock of their early days had given
way to a maturity of composition and arrangement that was to ensure
R.E.M.'s future as the leading lights of the new American
underground. What is captured here is the genesis of that sound
delivered with all the energy of live performance and the result is
simply enthralling - this one is a must! Enjoy!
