This show = vintage R.E.M.  

The bandy wae at the top of their game in the 1980s, and I loved their first 5 full-length albums.  By 1988, when "Green" was came out, my interest in the group started to wane significantly, and by 1991's "Out Of Time", I officially jumped off the bandwagon.

That said, this excellent-sounding SBD show does an excellent job of capturing the band at one of its finest hours--fresh off its critically-acclaimed "Murmur" and "Reckoning" albums.

And the sound quality is excellent!


If you like/appreciate this show, won't you please consider taking a moment to post a comment on the board?  Thanks, and enjoy the show.
