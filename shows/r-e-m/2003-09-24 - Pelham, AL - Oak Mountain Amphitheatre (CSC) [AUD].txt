Show notes: The 17th show of the North American leg of the greatest hits tour saw the band roll into Pelham, AL (just outside Birmingham), the same venue they played back in 1986 for the first show of the Pageantry tour (the venue had only opened that year so R.E.M. were one of the first to play there). Like that show, this one also started with Begin The Begin and These Days. Coincidence? Probably not, Peter used to do his setlist homework. That 1986 show was also the debut performance of 'The One I Love', played early in the set then but saved for the encore here. Unlike that show, they even pulled out the rarely played post 1984 'Radio Free Europe' here, one of its 10 performances in the 2000's (and only 29 times after 1985 if you're into stats). Another great show from one of my favourite R.E.M. tours.

Recording notes: I'd rate this recording as 'not bad'. It's nowhere near the best recording from the tour, but it's also nowhere near the worse. It's an easy listen, but it lacks some of the crispness of some of the other recordings from the tour. I ran it through the iZotope machine to get it sounding as good as possible which has improved it somewhat. In my view it's worth listening to. Samples provided.
