Show notes: The greatest hits tour of 2003 makes its way to New York City with the band in fine form. A terrific setlist with plenty of IRS era songs sure to please the old school fans, particularly the rare inclusion of Gardening At Night in the encore, only played 4 times on that tour. 

Recording notes: While already a very good audience recording, I put it through the iZotope machine and it's come out with a sheen and a sparkle it was previously lacking. A very enjoyable listen, the only flaw being a small skip in 'Drive' at the 2 minute 12 mark. Unless anyone has access to the master recording or received a copy directly from the taper it's unlikely this will be rectified. It's not a huge deal but be aware of it.

Torrent notes: This one seems to have never been circulated widely, circulating amongst a small group of collectors and never onto the wider torrent circuit. Time to fix that. Thanks to Nakeddave for supplying the wavs we can all enjoy.
