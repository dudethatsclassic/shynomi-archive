R.E.M 17 July 1999 - Mancester Evening News Arena, Manchester, England 
set: Lotus / What's The Frequency, Kenneth ? / So Fast, So Numb / Suspicion / Daysleeper / Low Desert / E-Bow The Letter / Everybody Hurts / The Apologist / Sweetness Follows / The Great Beyond / The One I Love / At My Most Beautiful / Find The River / Losing My Religion / Pop Song 89 / Star 69 / Walk Unafraid / Long Legged Girl / Man On The Moon 
encore: Falls To Climb / Hope / Why Not Smile / Crush With Eyeliner / Tongue / Fall On Me / It's The End Of The World As We Know It (And I Feel Fine) (part 1) / Suspicious Minds / It's The End Of The World As We Know It (And I Feel Fine) (continued) 
notes: Falls To Climb and Hope were again played by Stipe solo. Mike does backing vocals on E-bow rather than Patti Smith. Long Legged Girl is an Elvis song which Stipe sings a few verses of. 

The tracks are arranged in Disc One/Disc Two folders with the break coming just before Losing My Religion. Files 1-14 are in Disc One. Files 15-27 are in Disc Two.

As always the setlist is taken from Darryl White's incomporable and essential REM Timeline ( www.remtimeline.com - your one stop shop for all matters related to REM shows and setlists).

This is the third of the UK shows that I recorded during the Up Tour. I was sitting in Row C, about 10 metres away from the Mike side P.A.stack.  I think it's the best sounding of these shows but you can be the judge of that.
