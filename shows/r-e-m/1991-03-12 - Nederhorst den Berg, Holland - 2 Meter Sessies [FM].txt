REM
2 Meter Sessie, Bullet Sound Studio's
Nederhorst den Berg, Holland
12 March 1991

FM Radio Broadcast - Excellent Stereo

Files sent to me of the '13' version of this (original text file contained in fileset). 
I de-clicked some clicks in the recording using Click Repair.

Lineage was FM > CASS > CDR > EAC (secure) > HDD > CEP > Flac8


01 Fretless 
02 World Leader Pretend 
03 Half a World Away 
04 You Are The Everything
05 Radio Song 
06 Losing My Religion
07 Belong 
08 Love is All Around

Lovely session here, the last one I've heard, in that I'd not heard it before.

As well as having the rarely performed Fretless, there are lovely keyboard sounds here.

World Leader Pretend may be the best I've heard yet from these 1991 sessions?  Wonderful "maritime" accordian like sound here, though I've been told they were actually using a harmonium. Similar sound on You Are The Everything too.

On Half a World Away it's more like a church organ.

There were several from small clicks scattered across most of the tracks, and a *big* click at the start of Love Is All Around that made I found annoying so I dealt with these using Click Repair, carefully checking the results before and after using Sennheiser headphones.

Great congas on Losing My Religion! and again nice joshing around by Michael before Love is All Around.

Enjoy this.
