Notes from the (in-)famous Timeline: 
This was a great show. They played in the War Memorial Auditorium (where the Greensboro Symphony Orchestra normally plays) so the acoustics were great. The band wasn't big enough yet to play the main Coliseum I guess. The encore numbers of Ghost Riders In The Sky and Carnival Of Sorts still stick out in my mind as highlights of the show - Tom Ivey

I got this in a trade a couple of years ago, so have no idea of the lineage. Enjoy!
