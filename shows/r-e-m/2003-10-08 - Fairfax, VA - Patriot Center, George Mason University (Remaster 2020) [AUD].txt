Show notes: Only 3 shows left on the North American leg of the 2003 tour and the band starts moving south towards home territory, with a stopover near the nation's capital. With George Bush Jr. still in the White House, it's not surprising songs like Exhuming McCarthy and World Leader Pretend made the setlist. Also with Rockville, MD not far away it's no surprise Mike got up to sing that one here. I love any show with I Believe in the setlist, so this one is another winner for me.
