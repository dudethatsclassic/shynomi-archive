R.E.M.
Alte Feuerwache, Mannheim, Germany
October - 18 - 1985



Matrix of 2 different recordings of the same show.

Source 1: Stuart Percy recording (1st gen) uploaded to dime by hanwaker in 2010
          recording and transfer info: 
          analog aud-Master taped by stuart percy
          aiwa-CM30--> sony D6 (onto TDK SA 90)
          first digital transfer hanwaker 2010-11
          1.gen cassette played back on nakamichi CR-7e ( azimuth aligned)-> edirol 04
          wav file smoothed in audacity ( fades , volume/channel correction), tracked in cdwave--> flac(8)

Source 2: MILAVALENTI master recording uploaded to dime by stacheleber in 2013 (that was falsely given as my own master)
          recording and transfer info:
          MILAVALENTI master recording 
          SONY WM-D 6 with AIWA CM-30 Microphone
          digital transfer by MILAN: cass transfer via Onkyo tapedeck TA-2330 -> Magix Audio & Music Lab -> flac


Matrix arrangement:

flac files both sources -> Magix Audio & Music Lab (edit/remaster) -> FLAC -> DIME

Source 1 is missing the fragment of Maps and Legends where the band started to play it but stopped after 40 seconds to a reason unknown to me.
Source 2 has the complete show with some seconds missing of audience noise compared to source 1. Unfortunately the recorder had some problems with the mike mainly during the first half of the concert.
Both sources have an exceptional good sound (with the exception of the mike problems of source 2) and the matrix is close to excellent (I tried to eliminate the passages with the mike problems as good as possible). Source 1 was running a little bit slower than source 2 so I adjusted the speed of source 1 accordingly as this was a 1st gen recording.
