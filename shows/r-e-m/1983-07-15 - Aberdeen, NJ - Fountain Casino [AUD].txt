This is, at best, an  OK-ish audience recording that I obtained in a trade a long time ago in a galaxy far far away. Sound is far from perfect and varies wildly throughout (it really messes up halfway throughout 9-9 and doesn't sort itself out until Moon River) but it's a relatively uncommon show so I thought I'd up it.

I can't think of another show where Stipe segues into Moon River from Radio Free Europe or into The Lion sleeps tonight from We Walk.

You can safely split the two discs at the beginning of Track 21 We Walk so that 1-20 go on Disc One and 21 - 23 go on Disc Two.


Unknown audience recording > Maxell XLII C90 > SONY TC-WE435 > Cool Edit 2000  > CD Wav > Flac 6 > you.
