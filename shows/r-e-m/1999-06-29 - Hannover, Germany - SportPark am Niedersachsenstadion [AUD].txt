Show notes: This is a great show by the band - Germany loved R.E.M., and the energy the crowd gave them definitely inspired the band to greater heights on this night. Some great banter with the audience, which began with Mike showing off his German language skills to Michael's (feigned) annoyance, and an inspired setlist with some great chestnuts, as well as a very rare performance of 'Low Desert'. This one comes highly recommended.

Recording notes: This is a terrific sounding recording, the taper doing a great job. While CD 1 sounded great from the outset, there were some issues with CD 2 due to what I presume is some slight degradation of the CDR due to its age. There were some minor pops and clicks every so often, and similar to a CD I received from someone for the 6 July 1999 show, there were small gaps between the tracks (CD2 only, CD1 was fine). I fixed the gaps in Audacity, and then let iZotope do its thing to fix the pops and clicks, and gave it some EQ adjustments to fine tune it. The end result sounds great to my ears. This is the complete show, with no cut songs. A fine recording now sounds even finer.
