Alrighty folks, hot out of the oven another new DAT release from Stonecutter collection.

We have a brand new R.E.M. recording that's been sitting in the dark for too long.

The opening act was Spacehog, followed by over 2 hours from R.E.M.

Deni Bonet guests on violin on "Losing My Religion" & "Nightswimming".

Patti Smith sings lead vocals on "E-bow the Letter".

The recording levels were a bit low and the sound was somewhat distant.

I amplified the volume 12 decibels, which seemed to pull in the sound a little closer.

Thanks goes out to Rich for taping and sending his master tape over my way to be shared here on TTD.
