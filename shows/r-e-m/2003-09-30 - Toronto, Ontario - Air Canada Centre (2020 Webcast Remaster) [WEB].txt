Show notes: The greatest hits tour crosses back over the border into Canada for 1 night only with a terrific setlist, including some songs not often played on the tour, such as Maps And Legends, Feeling Gravitys Pull, I Believe, Get Up and Sweetness Follows, which was the tour debut for that one. A bittersweet happy birthday song to Bill Rieflin in the encore, and Roy Lamb, presumably one of the crew members. RIP Bill.

Recording notes: Taken from the REM HQ webcast capture. It's easy to forget live web streams were a thing back in 2003, and this was the second one of the tour, after the first on 31 August in Missoula, MT. Pioneering stuff. First part of track 1 is patched with the Steve Stroud audience recording as well as a 1 second dropout in the middle of the same song. All of his recording was used for track 25 which was missing. Given the lossy nature of a webcast recording I wanted to test out the new spectral recovery feature in iZotope RX8. Whether it's helped much I'm not sure, but regardless this is a nice sounding recording.
