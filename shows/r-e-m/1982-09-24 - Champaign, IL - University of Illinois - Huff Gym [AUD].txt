History:
Cassette received originally via trade about 25 years ago - and beloved!  Same recording source recently made available, digitized and cleaned-up (via Audacity), version on CD-R.  A/B comparison with above contrast indicates better sound, though limited by original recording.  Most significantly, no tape-flip gaps remain and peak levels (overall, and cross-channel) are more consistent.  Comparisons subjective, but you be the judge!  As always, this recording still is beloved.  Ort!
