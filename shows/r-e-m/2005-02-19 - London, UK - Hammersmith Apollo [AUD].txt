Notes:
This is a different and previously uncirculated source of this show. I hope you like it - it's a pretty good show with a more interesting encore than most on this tour, featuring sublime versions of Sweetness Follows and Swan Swan H. Seven Chinese Brothers is obviously a very welcome addition to the main set. Given that Mills was visibly unwell during this show, they pull the whole thing off with great gusto. Finally, though it's split for 2 CDs it's designed to be played from end to end with no breaks so d01t16 and d02t01 will seamlessly rejoin if you feel so inclined. Thank you to Rob for loaning me the DAT recorder, and to Dave for the SP mics.
