This is an interesting show, although the quality is not as good as the others I have posted.  It is still very listenable, and is probably an upgrade for most of you since it is complete and has no lossy lineage.  I got this only a few months after the show, so I am confident that it is very low generation.  I give this a B, but give the sample a listen because it is still pretty good.

Artwork included.
