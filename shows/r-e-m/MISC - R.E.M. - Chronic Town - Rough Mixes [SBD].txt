-Chronic Murmurings-R.E.M.- Toasted Records-1985? 1988?

Out of every R.E.M. bootleg out there, this one is considered by many to be the defining one. Chronic Murmurings was released sometime in the mid-to-late 80's (label says 1985, Discogs says 1988) in Australia by a bootleg record label called Toasted Records. They've released a couple other well known ones like Mada Mada and Documentary, but this is easily their finest.

The cover is very well done for a bootleg, with a hand-colored promo photo of R.E.M. from their Chronic Town days. My personal copy was signed by Peter Buck and Mike Mills on the Australian leg of the Green Tour. (Note: I bought this off eBay. I'm the third owner of this LP and live ~45 minutes away from Athens, GA.) This bootleg contains a ton of unreleased content ranging from 1980 to 1983. I'll give a brief explaination on everything on the bootleg:

-Wuxtry Rehersal Tape - June 1980 Wuxtry Records, Decatur, GA- I personally am a frequent customer at their Athens location, but I've never been to this one (hopefully that will change.) These aren't exactly 'demos' per say, but rather a pre-concert rehersal shot on a video camera for promotional purposes. It wasn't persued and ended up being bootlegged on cassette around the Atlanta/Athens area. Often referred to as 'Garage Rehersals', the this was originally thought to be the earliest recording of the band for quite a while.

-Chronic Town Outtakes and Rough Mixes - October 1981 Drive-In Studio, Winston-Salem, NC- These were recorded on a 4 day weekend up in Mitch Easter's Drive-In Studio, where the famous Cassette Set was recorded. Two of these tracks are rough mixes, while the rest of them are completely different from the final tracks. Here are the differences from the final tracks: 1,000,000- this one is mostly the same, with a slightly different mix in the bridge of the song. However, the track is a little funky for a couple seconds; it can't decide if it wants to be mono or stereo. Ages of You - one of my personal favorites, this one's an amazing outtake that has never seen the light of day. It's completely different that the one on Dead Letter Office, and is much more raw and abundant with energy. It also has a fun snapping intro. Gardening At Night - same as Eponymous, with someone talking in the studio before the track. Carnival of Sorts - has a different vocal track and has no fadeout like the final EP mix. It's very similar to the first performence of the song on 9-22-81 at Tyrone's O.C. Stumble - while it's basically the same as it appears on the final EP, it has a slightly differnent mix in the 'hipster town' section, as well as not having the peculiar paper tearing intro. There's also a tape dropout, so that sucks, but oh well.

-RCA Demos - February 1982 RCA Studio C, NY- This might be my favorite thing the band has ever done. Ever. These demos were recorded for RCA Records, as the band was attempting to sign to their label. However, the deal was abandoned as soon as I.R.S. Records got a hold of their Cassette Set and well, the rest is history. The majority of the tracks were recorded in a three day stint, while a couple more small additions were made later that month. My personal favorite of these tracks is Laughing. As opposed to Murmur, it uses the original lyrics for the song and features Rickenbacker only with no acoustic guitar. (Fun fact: these lyrics were used consistently for 2 years until they were altered for Murmur.) This version of Romance, in my opinion, blows away the version on Eponymous. Shaking Through also has different lyrics, which, like Laughing, had these lyrics consistently for 2 years, and is the first version to have the piano track. Honestly, I'm saddened the band hasn't released these and they probably never will. Last I heard, a copy of the tape was given to the owner of a radio station in Atlanta by Mike Mills.

-More Chronic Town Outtakes - January 1982 Drive-In Studio, Winston-Salem, NC- These were just some last minute things the band did for I.R.S. Records. The fast version of Wolves, Lower is here and so is another mix of 1,000,000. There's not much else to say...

-Reckoning Outtakes - December 1983 Reflection Studios, Charlotte, NC- These two tracks were recorded live in-studio and actually have had an official release pretty recently. Anyway, Windout (With Friends) has Jefferson Holt on vocals. It's kind of amusing, but also really weird. Just A Touch is kind of like the final version on LRP, but without the crazy breakdown at the end.
