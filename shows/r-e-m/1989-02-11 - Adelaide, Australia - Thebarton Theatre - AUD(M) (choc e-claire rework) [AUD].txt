Here's my humble contribution to the bootleg world, the first show that I've ever done any audio
editing on. Fairly minor stuff, and the recording's not *great*, but I think it's better and more
listenable than it used to be (I've included the original file in the folder if you want to compare
and have a go yourself).
Strong show from the early days of the Green tour, R.E.M.'s first time in Australia, and a setlist
that includes a ton of tour rarities. Kick back and enjoy.
-choc e-Claire, April 2021
