Notes: this is an incomplete show. It started with 3 songs not on my recording, then what I have was the main body of the set, of which 4 more songs were missing. 
The band finished with an 8 song encore. Total performance was 25 tracks.
This show kicked off the European leg of the 1985 tour: Reconstruction II.
The reasons I am posting it here anyway are: 
1) the sound quality is fantastic, it may very well be a SBD, and it is bass heavy, which I LOVE
2) it's in Paradiso, which is my favorite venue here in NL, and I have been there many times (it's still going strong).
3) I am hoping this will rustle up the full version, which I would give my left arm to hear!
