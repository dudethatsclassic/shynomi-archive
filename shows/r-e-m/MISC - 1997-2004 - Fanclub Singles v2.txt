I would recommend putting the 1997 tracks on the 1988-1996 CD, because they
will not fit on this CD without overburning.  That's why they are named as they are.

Lineage: 

Tracks 1,2: Fanclub VHS -> Canon Optura30 -> 48Khz 16-Bit Audio on DV -> SoundForge 8 (for re-sampling to 44.1, and normalization, all done at 32-bit and dithered down to 16) -> FLAC Level 8

Tracks 5-7,0.0,0.1: optical digital out from turntable -> Alesis Masterlink -> CD -> Exact Audio Copy -> SoundForge 8 (for normalization, done in 32-Bit, and dithered down to 16.) -> FLAC Level 8

Tracks 8,9,11,14,15: Fanclub CD -> Exact Audio Copy -> Soundforge 8 (normalized to a lower dB level to match the rest of this compilation, done in 32-Bit, and dithered down to 16) -> FLAC Level 8

Tracks 3,4,10,12,13: Fanclub CD -> Exact Audio Copy -> FLAC Level 8
