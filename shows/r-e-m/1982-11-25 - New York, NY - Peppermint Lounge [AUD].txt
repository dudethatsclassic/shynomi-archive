This should be something new for R.E.M. fans.

On a technical note. This is a probable 1st generation copy.

Upon closer inspection of the wave file, this appears to be a 2 channel mono recording.

The left and right channels have an identical wave pattern.

There are 3 cuts during this recording.

There is an unexpected tape cut during track 14 @ 1:45.  A little music missing.

There is an unexpected tape cut between tracks 14 and 15. No music missing.
