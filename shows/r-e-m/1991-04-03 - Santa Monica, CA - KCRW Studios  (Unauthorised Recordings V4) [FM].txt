'The Unauthorised Recordings: R.E.M. Losing My Religion (Vol. 4) - Live in USA 1991'*

Tracks 1-15:
KCRW Studios, Santa Monica, California, April 3, 1991.

Tracks 16-17: 
Saturday Night Live (I think) New York, April 13, 1991.

*For a small time in the mid-90s in Australia, a legal loophole allowed these 'Unauthorised Recordings' to be widely available (usually on the 'Banana' label). They were usually re-releases of previously hard-to-find boots. So no doubt some of you have this title under a different name and possibly a less edited version.

This is a very cute, loose REM radio session with the boys in a playful mood. Quite a few oddities and snippets such as Spooky, Tusk and Jackson make this a compelling listen. The last 2 tracks are tacked on at the end and are not particularly noteworthy I guess.
