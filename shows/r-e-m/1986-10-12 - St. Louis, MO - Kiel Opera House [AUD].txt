12 October 1986 - Kiel Opera House, St. Louis, MO 

This list the last in the three night series taped by Doug Morris which I began torrenting before Christmas.
From what I have heard from people who emailed me, it seems that Doug, who was pretty well known around 
St. Louis, passed away a few years ago now. This show is is the best of the three REM shows he taped that autumn, 
and I dedicate it to Doug and his taping efforts. Coming up next time, 31-10-1986 or 10-31-1986 in English(US).

Taped by Doug Morris on a Sony D-6 deck, mics unknown. Converted from a 2nd gen tape.

As the lights come up after So. Central Rain you can hear some guy who wanted a fourth encore go "bummer" 
just before the recording ends. Well I found it amusing.
