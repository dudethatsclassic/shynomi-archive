Show notes: Originally scheduled for 3 April 1995, this was one of the few European indoor shows from March and April that was rescheduled. A fairly standard Monster tour setlist, but of note this was the first performance of 'The Wake-Up Bomb'.

Recording notes: A decent sounding audience recording. If you like this era R.E.M. you'll enjoy listening to this.
