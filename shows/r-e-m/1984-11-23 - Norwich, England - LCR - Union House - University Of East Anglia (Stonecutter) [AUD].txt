Notes:

Courtesy of the Stonecutter Archives is this audience recording from R.E.M. Yes, a nice soundboard of this show circulates, but it runs just a touch under 80 minutes, so clearly it probably had been pared down for the CDR trading age. 

The Lucy Show opened. £3.50 admission, £2.75 advance.

Fair warning to all ye who grab: beginning of the tape plays extremely muffled, but that clears up about 27 minutes in, revealing a rather decent audience capture. 

Other flaws:
The end of "Rockville" is cut. 
There is a cut at the start of "Auctioneer."
"Moon River" and "Second Guessing" cut in.
"Pretty Persuasion" has a bit of static breakup early into the song, but that could possibly even be from the venue's PA.

Gotta better copy of this? Let's hear it!
