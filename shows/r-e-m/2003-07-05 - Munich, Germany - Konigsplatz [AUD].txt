Show notes: A great show with a typical 2003 setlist, this show features the rarely played after 1989 Feeling Gravitys Pull, a fantastic Country Feedback which featured a snippet of Chorus and the Ring from Reveal, and an impromptu performance of Iggy Pop's The Passenger, preceded by a few chords of Patti Smith's People Have The Power. A few notable things that you have to visualise:
- Peter has to start off She Just Wants To Be 3 times because Michael is giggling the first 2 times
- After Peter's Country Feedback solo (quite different from his usual one), Mike leaves the keyboard, kneels down in front of Peter and bows about 5 or 6 time before him
- Michael sits on the piano posing like a diva with Mike kneeling before him during Losing My Religion
- After the last encore, Peter, Mike and Michael chase each other around, finally Mike and Peter grab Michael and carry him off stage

Recording notes: A great sounding recording, Matthijs is well known in R.E.M. fan circles and recorded a lot of R.E.M. shows from 2003-2008. We owe him a lot! Nice and clear and crisp, this is a great show to listen to. Thanks Matthijs.

Torrent notes: This was seeded by Matthijs to Dime on 10 April 2005, and surprisingly the Dime tracker archive says it was only on for 52 days and was snatched 38 times. I can't find any instance of this being reseeded, so I suspect many people out there don't have this one. Matthijs also included his recording of the soundcheck from Locarno 2 days later as bonus tracks. I never saw a recording of the Locarno show, so possibly something went wrong and he didn't record the actual show. He also included some artwork by Guru Mosh which I've left in - his website can be found at http://www.torsten-freitag.de/rem/, but it appears it hasn't been updated since 2003 and is now a time capsule.
