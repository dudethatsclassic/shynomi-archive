This is a composite of 2 sources, both of which were incomplete, but combined form the complete show. The majority of the show is from a 1st gen cassette I received in a tape trade back in the 1990's. The other source was a mint unplayed copy of the bootleg titled 'Documentary', which filled the gaps the cassette version was missing. R.E.M. fan extraordinaire Mark B took my tape and the vinyl bootleg and seamlessly combined them to create the complete show for your listening pleasure. Originally shared online back in 2005, I'm unaware of it being shared since.

From http://remtimeline.com/1987.html:
'Simple Gifts' is an adapted version from the 1848 Shaker movement song/hymn; "Tis a joy to be simple, tis a joy to be free etc...". 'Rollers Show' is not the theme from the Bay City Rollers tv show, it's simply the song 'Rollers Show' by Nick Lowe which poked fun at BCR fans, this then segues into a snatch of 'Saturday Night' which is a Bay City Rollers song.
