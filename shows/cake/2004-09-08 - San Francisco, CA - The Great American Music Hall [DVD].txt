This San Francisco concert was broadcast as a free-for-view on DirecTV in November of 2004.

It must be said that this concert absolutely rocks.  The band was in fine form and the crowd was very into the show as well(other than the few new songs where they were a little puzzled).  According to reports, John claims that this is the longest show they will ever do, as they are taping a special and want to get everything just right.  They play several songs twice including No Phone, their new single at the time and also beak into some old favorites like Ruby Sees All (which didn't make the cut).  The band is on fire and play with great energy.  Xan really is an impressive guitar player really brings it and Vince and Gabe are brilliant as usual.  The whole band is incredibly tight and powerful. Stand out tracks include Carbon Monoxide, Stickshifts & Safetybelts, and of course I Will Survive, the closer to the show.  Unfortunately, Directv only needed an hour concert so they cut several of the songs so this is not a complete show.  I have to say, if you like CAKE at all, you will love this show. Haters, and I know there are some out there, need not apply.
