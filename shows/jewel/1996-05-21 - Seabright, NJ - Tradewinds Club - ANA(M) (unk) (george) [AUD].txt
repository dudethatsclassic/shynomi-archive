======================================================


I used a patch from an alternate source 2 for track 14, of similar quality from a 2nd gen tape.

With the 80 second patch added, that makes it exactly 80 minutes.

Both tape sources I have each end with "Who Will Save Your Soul",  even though 'Chime Bells' is usually her closer.

The rare track in this show is the eerie and haunting "Nicotine Love'.

Which is about a serial killer,  it's one of her best.

Also rare is 'See Sassy Wake Up'.  Along with the version of 'I'm Sensitive', which is sung with different lyrics.

The next night Jewel would be performing in Pittsburgh. Then on the following day, Jewel would be celebrating her 22nd birthday.

Not many have heard this particular recording.
