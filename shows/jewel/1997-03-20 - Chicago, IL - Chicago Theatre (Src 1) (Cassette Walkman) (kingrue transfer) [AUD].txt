This show I attended and recorded from the 17th row.
This recording has never been traded or shared before.
This is when Jewel was on her Tiny Lights tour and was nearing her peak of popularity.
In 1997 Jewel dominated the airwaves. MTV and VH1 were playing her videos nonstop.
She was on the cover of numerous magazines and won numerous awards this year. 
Her fanbase we’re completely hooked and addicted on this girls performances.
At the time she was something brand new and unheard of in a world of alternative and grunge music.
She stuck out of the musical crowd and got the attention of a lot of fans.
With a voice like an angel there wasn’t anyone else like her. 
This was the best time to see her in concert. Long before her concerts became subdued and predictable 

The opening act Rugburns were also her backing band. 
Which included Steve Poltz who cowrote her big hit You Were Meant For Me, during the time they were dating each other.
