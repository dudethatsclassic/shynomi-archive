It appears that 60 min tapes were used for the master recording, which makes for an extra tape flip/cut.

Later copies were squeezed onto a 90 min tape, omitting some bits and pieces. 

Side B of the master was flipped too soon, which cut off the last few words of track 16.

At the tail end of track 25 you can hear the opening notes of "I've Got The Blues More Than You Do Blues".... but it's cut off and missing.

Also missing is the beginning to track 26 and the last song is cut off on my copy.  The taper should of used 90's to record with..... just sayin'....

The show starts off with Jewel explaining the meaning of the song "Fragile Flame" as the opener to her show.

For the fans who know about the meaning of that song, that was unexpected, Aside from that the rare songs for this show are: 

"If This Is What Love Is" & "Totally Tupperware" & "Partial to Mary" & "Like An Electric Fan". 

Also rare is track 24, which totally stumps me for a title. 

We also get the mental imagnery of "Painters" and the rarely played "Nikos".  

Before Niko (Kilcher) and family ended up on a reality show about the Alaskian Wilderness with the Kilcher Family.

The quality is decent. samples provided.
