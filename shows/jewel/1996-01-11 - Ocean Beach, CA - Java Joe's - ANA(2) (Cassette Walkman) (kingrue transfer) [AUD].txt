Here’s an early recording of jewel at her old stomping grounds Java Joe’s. 
Jewel does two sets this day and the Setlists are filled with many rarely played songs. 
During the first set, the song called Emily was played but it is missing from this copy. 
I’m not sure if it was missed during the tape flip or if it was purposely omitted. 
When Jewel takes a break there is another performer that does a couple songs and then Jewel comes back for her second set.
Unfortunately all we get from that is the first song Little Sister and then the tape stops. 
Clearly there’s more blank tape available but why the rest of it is missing is a mystery. 
I have written out the rest of the second set so you know what’s missing, 
I have a feeling that we will never hear the rest of it so this is all we’re getting.
