Jewel and her band 'The 3D's' were opening for Neil Young on 13 dates, this is gig #5.

I'm speculating on the tape source as being a Sony D7, because that's what circulates from the Neil Young's show release.

I got this so many years ago and this being a 3rd gen, I needed to do a little pitch correction on this.

It's now near pitch perfect, though still has a very slight tape warble. I've love to find the master tape.

I've had some down time between projects, so I figured I'd share some more of my own tapes.

I have another box of tapes coming any day now from BR.  

Once I dig into those tapes then you'll start seeing a bunch of different artists.  

So for now, enjoy these rarely heard Jewel shows.
