For this repost, the notes and lineage have been updated and the tracks titled.
Ken no longer has the master tape. I used my 1st generation tape for this torrent.
There is another source in circulation (which I also have).
Track 11 cuts in due to the tape flip. Otherwise this is complete.
I carefully selected individual loud hand claps between songs and reduced them by 6 to 12 decibels.
This allowed me to boost the volume of the entire recording.
This is a really good set list.  Jewel fans will recognize all the songs.
My favorite here is the rarely played "Nicotine Love". (About a serial killer)
By this point in Jewel's career, she had finally established herself, besides being another pretty face with a guitar.
This chapter would soon come to a close as she takes 1998 off from touring to try her acting ability in a movie role.
Only to return in 1999 with a full blown arena world tour.
The qualitly is average, a bit distant and distorted. Not as clear as i would like.
Check out the samples and decide if you want it.
