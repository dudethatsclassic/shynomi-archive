Lineage: CA-11 > Zoom H2 (44.1 khz, 16 bit) > PC > Adobe Audition* > CD Wave > TLH (.flac 6, align on sector boundaries checked but not needed, .md5)

*Remove clapping, increase volume 10dB, Normalize 97%

Notes: Ashley Monroe opened - her first big performance.  

This is a decent recording (never before released) and of course a great performance.  I was surprised that a couple brought their baby to the show (near the front row).  The baby was pretty good but some drunk women had to make a fuss over it and of course they all later talked, laughed, cried and clapped at odd times.  One girl a few rows back kept making a "twilling" (???) sound that was much more annoying in person than is conveyed on the recording (thankfully).  Enjoy!
