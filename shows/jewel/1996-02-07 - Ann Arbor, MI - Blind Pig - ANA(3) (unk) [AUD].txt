Review from Dan Stark 

I just got back from The Blind Pig in Ann Arbor, where Jewel played on the 
Edwin McCain tour tonight in their "Detroit" stop.I thought I'd post the 
setlist and a brief review for whoever is interested. 

This is the first time I've seen Jewel in a few months, and I thought she 
looked a bit different since I last saw her.  In fact, she looks like she's 
really taking good care of herself - I noticed a darker, dirty blonde hair 
color, and she wore a very striking, low-cut black top tonight.  

She played for about an hour and twenty minutes, and tended to play one 
song straight into the next more than I've seen before, and kept the 
between-song stories fairly brief.  I expect that's because she had limited 
time onstage before Edwin McCain's set, and wanted to get as much in as 
possible.  It wasn't the quietest crowd I've been in, but they weren't too 
bad compared to the Innocence Mission show I saw there last month...plus I 
was camped out 3 feet in front of the monitor tower so the noise wasn't too 
distracting.  The Blind Pig is a fairly small bar room, and the show was 
sold out for some time, so it was jammed. Jewel was in good voice, missed a 
few notes here and there on the guitar, but it was a decent bar room 
performance.  She played 3 songs that were new to me, and 2 others that I'd 
never seen her do live, so I walked away happy.  The highlight for me 
tonight was her performance of Tiny Love Spaces, a song that had never come 
across on tape for me like it did live tonight.  She also pulled off a 
wonderfully long vocal sustain during Who Will Save Your 
Soooooouuuuuuuuuul, which drew a great response from the crowd.  She played 
Painters at someone's request, something she doesn't usually do in the 
bars.  And she played Foolish Games, which I think is new to her live 
repertoire.  In the past she's always claimed that she's "forgotten" how to 
play it when people called for it, which I think was a cover-up for the 
fact that she never played it on guitar to begin with.  She did tonight 
though, but with the disclaimer that "It doesn't translate well to 
guitar"...I thought it sounded just fine.  Notable in its absence was You 
Were Meant For Me, the current single!  Despite that song getting some 
decent airplay here, she got far more requests for I'm Sensitive.
