Here we have source 2 from the Cotton Club show.  
Even though this is from a 2nd gen cassette, I think this sounds just a little bit better than source one.
This source also has the song Pieces of You complete, whereas source one was cut due to a tape flip

I’ve added in scans of the newspaper advertisement and a ticket stub from the show.
Not your typical ticket, More like a raffle ticket. 
It’s not often that we get 2 sources for any Jewel show.
So I’m sure the collectors will enjoy this one.
