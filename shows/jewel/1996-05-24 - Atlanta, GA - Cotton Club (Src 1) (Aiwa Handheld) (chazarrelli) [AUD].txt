Fan Review:

The doors were supposed to open at 9:00pm, so my friend and I showed up
about 6:00.  There was no one there yet, so we walked next door to an
Italian cafe and relaxed for awhile... turned out we were sitting next to
the guitar player and drummer for Duncan Sheik...also met Jewel's
babysitter from about 10 years ago while I was waiting to play our check...

the doors opened at 9:00, we walked quickly over to the front of the stage
and planted ourselves right in front.  Unfortunately, security wouldn't
let us bring in the whole vase of flowers we had brought as a belated
birthday present, but we had one flower each in our hands...

Duncan Shiek was really good I thought, I especially liked the song
"Serena", it's on the promo tape they gave out, but the live version was a
hell of alot better...

I don't remember the exact set that Jewel played, but someone else from
the list recorded the whole thing.  Unfortunately, in my excitement upon
seeing Jewel, I completely forgot your name... :( 
send me email if you see this and I'll trade you copies of my photos for a
copy of your tape! :)

Jewel didn't sing Chime Bells...some guy kept yelling for her to yodel, but
she told him to shut up, and that she was tired of the song or something to
that effect..maybe her voice was tired...i don't remember...

After the show, my friend and I ran outside to see if we could catch her
as she left...we just missed her, nad it seemed like the security people
were hustling her out of there real quick... she drove away in a little
black Honda civic, seemed kinda strange since there were two tour buses nearby...

The only downside to the concert was the security people..they were being
extremely anal, but oh well..

Chris Hoeh
