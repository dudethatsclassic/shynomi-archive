Jewel had just finished her participation in the Lilith Fair tour, followed by eight days off.
Now she was starting her next headlining tour here in Boston. 
This tour would continue through September 13, 1997.
Mostly consisting of East Coast and some Midwestern states.

This tour would find Jewel with her best backing band yet. 
Incorporating a cello into many of her songs, which really improved and advanced the songs to a new level.
This is a new second source originally from a DAT, this has no cuts.

opening act was David Baerwald.

Band line-up
Jewel - acoustic, electric guitar, vocals
Matt Brubeck - cello
John Ginty - Hammond B-3 organ
Craig Young - bass
David Reveli - drums
