here's something out of my collection that didn't get widely circulated.

Jewel opened for Edwin McCain.

I've added some concert reviews from 2 people that attended.  One concert goer even claimed to have recorded the show,  

but I never traded directly with this person, so I don't know if I have a different source.

The quality of this recording isn't very good.  It suffers from really bad tape warble and there is nothing I can do to fix that. (as far as i know)

One thing i learned early on is that Jewel allowed taping but her fans for the most part were NOT tapers.

This meant that many of them were just grabbing whatever recording device they had available and was basically taping for the first time on low quality recorders.

Sometimes we'd end up with something like this.  This will be for the Jewel collector that is looking to fill in a gap in their collection.

These early 1996 shows are filled with rare and non-LP tracks. Fans were hearing some of these songs for the first time.

I was actually able to track down the girl from the review below and give her a copy of this show, she was thrilled.

From: Joanne Matson <joanne_matson@xxxxx>
Date: Mon, 26 Feb 1996 11:38:25 -0800 (PST)
Subject: Jewel in Vancouver

Hi!
Well after sweating about it for weeks, I was actually let into the show by a very nice door guy who didn't even check my i.d. ( darn, I didn't 
get a chance to try out that oh-so-convincing excuse why I left it at home). By the time Jewel took the stage it was quite packed, and I was 
standing near the front near the mic. I couldn't believe it when she actually came on, I was only about 5 feet away from her! She wore a tight
black t-shirt and faux-leather pants, if you like to know these things.  Ever since I joined this mailing list I have been hearing about how Jewel 
is even better in person, and I can't believe that it's actually true! Her voice was absolutely perfect, and she is getting really good at 
playing guitar (which she tuned with her back to us, so as not to show her nostril-flaring habit). Because I was directly in her line of vision, 
every now and then she would look at me (I swear!) and do that smile where she wrinkles her nose and raises her eyebrows :) I guess it was 
obvious how glad I was to be there. It was kind of amusing to see the crowd's reaction because the people standing around me were completely 
still while she was singing - I was worried that someone might keel over from lack of circulation! 99% of the crowd was very quiet, and she told 
the other 1% (who are now undoubtedly headed for eternal damnation) to talk at the back in that sweet-sarcastic David Spade-ish way.  When she 
took requests, a lot of people yelled out for "Race Car Driver", which surprised me, since it's not on PoY. I wanted to hear "Nicotine Love", 
but I was too nervous to ask (plus I didn't want her to worry about my mental state). After her fantastic performance, Jewel left the stage 
quickly, and I didn't want to run after her in search of an autograph because she looked pretty tired. Only one other thing would have made the 
night even better - t-shirts!! Unfortunately they didn't have any ( *really* sorry Kam, I know you wanted one badly :(  ). I wrote down the 
set list immediately after from memory, so it's not perfect:   Thanks for letting me share my joy :)   - Joanne

From: brjohnso@xxxxx (Brian Johnson)    This is the taper I want to find !!!
Date: Mon, 26 Feb 1996 23:28:44 -0800 (PST)
Subject: Jewel in Vancouver

I saw Jewel last Friday at the Town Pump here in Vancouver. It was incredible! I used to live in Toronto and heard WWSYS a lot on the radio, 
but that's the only song I knew. I had the best time! I got a wicked spot in the crowd too - right in front of her for the entire show, front row! Even 
better, my friend lent me his tape recorder, so i got the whole show on tape. It's pretty good quality too. The crowd wasn't too bad, but she did 
tell some people to shut up a couple of times. The only bad part of the evening was that I didn't get a chance to meet her. I stuck around for the 
Ed McCain show, even though i think they suck, hoping that she would come back after to sign autographs. She didn't show up, so I went outside and 
waited by the tour bus for 45 minutes. Finally Edwin came out, and I pretended to be a big fan of his, hoping he might be able get me Jewel's 
autograph in the bus. Unfortunately, he told me that she doesn't travel with them, and that she had left right after her set. Arghhhhhh! 

continuing >

Jewel explained (last night in Santa Cruz) that she had to leave immediately after her Vancouver show to fly to Aspen for a VH-1 taping of 'Crossroads'... 
Which she missed because of a (1) bomb-threat on the plane, and (2) engine problems after the bomb-threat.
