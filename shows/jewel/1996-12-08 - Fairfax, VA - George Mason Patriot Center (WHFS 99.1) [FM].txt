Here's an FM broadcast of Jewel's short set.  

I've had a few different versions of this and I just recently obtained a DAT from my friend Sean.

This is the best source I've heard so far.

Before Jewel starts track 4, she tells the story about her trip down to Mexico with Steve Poltz and going on a drug bust with Federales on a speed boat. 

I added a picture taken from their July 1995 adventure.  Jewel and Steve posing with AK47's and a ton of pot.  Good Times. 

Jewel has a backing band for most of this show.  On a side note, there is Amateur video from this concert as well.

I have that, but don't plan of posting it yet.  so for now, this FM will be just fine.

Jewel will be starting her 'Tiny Lights' tour in Feb 97, which really puts her on the path for bigger venues.
