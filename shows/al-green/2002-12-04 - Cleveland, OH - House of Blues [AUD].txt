Taper's Notes:  Excellent show.  Sound quality is excellent.  A short, but high energy set. I was down on the floor and there was a fair bit of clapping, singing and shouting, as you would expect.  I had to use the low cut filter becuase I was getting blown away by the bass.  As a result the low end is not as robust as it could be with my setup.  Either way, I am fairly happy with the results.
