Rec. Info:
Aiwa Walkman with plugged on mics -> MC -> This cassette (TDK SA90) (1stgen.) -> playback on Harman Kardon CD 491 -> Zoom H2 -> WAV (16/44) -> Audacity (edit/remaster) -> 
WAV (16/44) -> TLH8 -> FLAC -> DIME -> YOUR EARS AND MIND... Recorded by Christian Z. Thanks gratefully for your efforts! 
CAUTION!!! Unfortunately this isn't a particularly good recording. Not the fault of the taper, but the inhouse sound is very muddy and the vocals are almost completely inaudible. 



---
The kings of shoegaze psychedelia captured in a small club in Bavaria's capitol. Tons of screeching feedback and low down drones connect almost each song, which made a secure tracking
almost impossible. But as I'd seen quite a lot of interest on the previous posted master tape from Hamburg, I'm not gonna hold this back. Hope you like it. SB.
---
