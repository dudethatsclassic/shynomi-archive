**Remastered** By thir13en Productions

My Bloody Valentine
1992-07-xx
Vancouver, BC, Canada

From "Loom" silver bootleg disc.


A very nice recording from them containing the best of their catalogue. The spaced-out fuzzy guitars cut through with grit, and the vocals are sufficiently buried just as they are on the albums. 

Some weirdness with this source, as a -4db right channel drop existed until about 1 minute or so into "Soon", then is ramped up to create an even dual channel output from there on. This is now fixed throughout. Plus the very unfortunate and at times extended muted dropouts in "You Made Me Realise" have been fixed as well. The last such dropout had some volume changes after the music kicked back in, I fixed that as best I could, it's still a little funky for a few seconds but isn't anywhere near as distracting as the original was.

Enjoy!
