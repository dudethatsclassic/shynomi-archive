#  Originally Recorded By George Michael.

First time seeing Limp Bizkit since 2003 on Summer Sanitarium with Metallica. I did
not record that show or the prior time seeing them in 1998 on Ozzfest. Since 2003, I
really have not listened to them much. Decided to give the new record a spin this past
Halloween and was pretty impressed. It sounds like they picked right up in the early
2000's and released a follow up to Chocolate Starfish. So, since Halloween, I have
gone back and rediscovered their catalog again. When dates got announced for their
STLL SUCKS tour, if Ohio was on the list, I was going. Youngstown got a Wednesday date
(did not see that coming!), ignoring the major cities. I had to make plans with work
to go, but made it happen. Whats 6 hours of RT driving on a work night? Pshhh!

The recording could not have gone smoother. I had no one in the row behind me,
a quiet couple in the row in front of me (seated, mind you) and no one to my right.
The aisle was to my left. The sound in the Covelli Centre was excellent, as well.
I just had to hit record and enjoy. Im glad I did not record from the floor, it was
an all out assault in the moshpit during their heavier songs. That would have been 
brutal! One highlight of the set was during "Full Nelson". Fred brought up a random
fan from the front to sing with him and he killed it! His name was Tristian and from
West Virginia. I got to meet him after the show while walking out and he was still
filled with excitement. I dont think he'll soon forget this show. The entire arena
was cheering for him.

"Is marijuana legal here?" - FD
