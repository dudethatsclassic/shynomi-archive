Included with the recently (2025) unearthed soundboard of the Essen show that has already been posted here, is this Munich soundboard. It's been posted here before, but now inactive and deserves to be up! 
Short but good. Apparently this is a mix of both afternoon and evening shows that occurred on this date. It's incomplete, evidenced by the start of 'She's A Woman' that cuts off into 'Baby's In Black', also missing 'If I Needed Someone', 'Day Tripper', 'I Wanna Be Your Man', 'Paperback Writer' —  but good to have this much soundboard at all! You might have seen footage of 'Nowhere Man' in the 1995 Anthology documentary, and/or the existing videos of the concert online. Some show fragments of missing songs, wisely not included here being too unlistenably cut apart. 
Enjoy this fab recording!
