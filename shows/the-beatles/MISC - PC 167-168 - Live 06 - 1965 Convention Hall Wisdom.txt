The Beatles
Live 06 - Convention Hall Wisdom
Purple Chick


The sixth volume in our overview of The Beatles' live career finishing
off their first U.S. tour and bringing us to the end of 1964, plus an
orphan show from 1965 (there won't be room for it next volume!)

As always, some shows already sounded great (like Indianapolis), our
proprietary audio equalization and clean-up system made some
significant improvements to others (like Philadelphia) while some
shows were really beyond redemption (like Montreal).
