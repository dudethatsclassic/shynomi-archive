The Beatles
MissHimMissHimMissHim
Darthdisc

This 8 CD set reviews the "Paul is Dead" mania of 
October and November 1969. It assembles vintage material 
that shows the madness as it unfolded, as well some 
explanatory material from 1970's rebroadcasts of the 
orginal shows - and even new looks at material from 
the 1990's.
