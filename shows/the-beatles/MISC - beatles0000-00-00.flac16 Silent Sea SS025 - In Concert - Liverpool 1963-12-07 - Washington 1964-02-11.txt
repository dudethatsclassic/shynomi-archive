Comments
Two shows are presented here. First an afternoon concert to 2,500 members of the Northern Area Fan Club at the Empire Theatre in Liverpool on Saturday 7 December, 1963. 30 minutes of this was telecast the same evening by BBC Television as It’s the Beatles! Second up is The Beatles’ first American concert on Tuesday, 11 February, 1964. This show was recorded by CBS Television before 8,100 fans at the Washington Coliseum in Washington, D.C. It was shown in closed-circuit broadcast in American cinemas by National General Corporation on Saturday 14 and Sunday 15 March, 1964.
