The Beatles
Live Before America
Purple Chick

You probably don't need another live Beatles collection, but here's one
anyway: every surviving non-radio performance between the Star Club and Ed
Sullivan.

Carefully compiled and restored from the best sources we could find. As
always, we didn't just copy the audio from the listed souces - instead, we 
tried to work some of our patented sonic magic when necessary. Of course,
the quality still varies from dire to delightful, but you knew that already.

Anyway, enjoy this peek at the Beatles at their peak:  before they landed
in the U.S.A.
