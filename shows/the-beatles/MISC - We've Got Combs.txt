This is another one from JFC Records. A Nice 2 disc set called "We've Got Combs" We have Disc one, with the Philadelphia, Pennsylvania; September 2 1964 Convention Hall with interviews and Concert. Disc two has Indianapolis, Indiana; Indiana State Fair Coliseum with interviews and concert.
