THE BEATLES
SECRET SONGS IN PEPPERLAND
Remastered Edition
RMW 629

Here's another disc that is so scarce that it took me a lot of years to find. In fact, more people will be familiar with this material from its release on Vigotone's "The Lost Pepperland Reel," but this was their source disc, made in Japan by Masterdisc.

It features mono mixes prepared for the "Yellow Submarine" film which never received commercial release, along with the raw effects tapes used on the "Sgt. Pepper" album. Pretty much everything on it is a highlight, but the standouts are the unedited mono mix of "It's All Too Much," "Lucy In The Sky With Diamonds" with the first verse sung by Dick Emory, voice of Jeremy Boob, and "All You Need Is Love," which does not fade out at the end. The remainder of the tracks are unused true mono mixes which still have never been officially released. The sound quality is superb on all tracks.

Pitch, phase and levels corrected at Remasters Workshop January 2011.

Artwork and verification files are included.

Enjoy!
