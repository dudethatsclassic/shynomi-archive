From MasterJedi: Here, for your listening pleasure, is every available non-common version acetate by The Beatles. All available sources were studied to determine the copy least damaged by bootleggers. Suspected fakes and versions that were also available on (or could be created from) other higher-quality sources were removed. The speed has been carefully corrected when it was possible to determine the original speed. The EQ has been adjusted to match various other sources that were studied. No noise reduction was performed. No declicking has occurred that wasn't already present on the best available source, other than choosing the best channel for those sources that were originally mono. In many cases, no work was done, other than to determine the best available source. Basically, this is the least affected these tracks can get with the sources that are currently available. As always, the guidance provided by Mr. Winn's books was invaluable, and those that supplied sources for some of the rarer tracks are thanked on the back cover. Enjoy!
