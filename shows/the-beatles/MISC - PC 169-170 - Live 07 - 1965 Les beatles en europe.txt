The seventh volume in our continuing overview of The Beatles' live
career, highlighting their brief 1965 European tour and then moving
onwards to America.

As always, we think these are (objectively) the most complete and
(subjectively) the best sounding versions of these shows to date.
Performance-wise, they're not so bad either. Of course, your mileage
may vary, and some shows will always sound pretty dire.

Erratum: So it turns out that the last volume's Toronto Maple Leafs
Garden segment was not what it seemed. "You Can't Do That," "All My
Loving" and the intro to "She Loves You" are duplicated from the
Seattle show a couple of weeks (and discs) earlier. On the other hand
"She Loves You" itself is indeed from the Toronto show. Who knew?
