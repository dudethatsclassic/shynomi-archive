The Esher Demos
Cdr-EAC-Flac

This recording was done at George Harrison's house in late may of
68.  
  It's John, George and Paul, recording some the tracks they had writen in India.
  Fom the sound of this recording they must have been haveing a very good time..
Perhaps some very good Hashish???  Whatever, it never inpaired their playing at all.
  This is my favoret of all Beatles recordings.  
  It can't be called an outtake recording, just John, Paul, and George sitting around and
jamming together.  
  As the insert say's, this is about as close to an Beatles "unplugged" as you'll ever
find.
  On a personnel note, Thank God, George Martin didn't stop by.... just pure pleasure
of the Beatles playing guitars & singing....
