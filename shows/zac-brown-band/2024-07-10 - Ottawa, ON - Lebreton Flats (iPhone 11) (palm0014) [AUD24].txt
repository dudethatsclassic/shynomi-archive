Recording notes: With some rain in the forecast I was trying to figure out how to record with potential torrential downpours.  In the end I opted for a microphone windscreen stuffed into my shirt pocket and then placed the iPhone upside down.  The rain didn't come in massive quantities so I had my rain jacket open the entire night.  I would say that from a technical perspective this worked out fine.  It was a rowdy crowd so it's a sup-par festival recording as always with a lot of chatter.

Show notes:
Zac Brown Band has been a white whale for me for quite some time so I'm glad to have finally seen them.  The band is full of amazing musicians and they did not disappoint.  It had been raining hard all day so we were worried about the night but we managed to get through it with a bit of drizzle.

Ottawa Bluesfest has a very strict 11PM curfew and the band was caught off guard with this so they ripped through an abridged version of Chicken Fried.  I see the next night in Quebec they managed to get a few more songs in so maybe they scaled back the all out jam session on Isn't It Lovely/Neon?  

Either way, great show!
