Notes:
Opening for Good Charlotte after Eve 6 performed. Both of which were recorded.
As far as I'm aware, this is the initial release of this tape. The taper was liquidating his
massive collection of master recordings and I was fortunate enough to grab this and a
handful of other bands before they were scattered to the winds and possibly never released. 
I did some subtle high end boost then applied compression in Audition and placed fades
at the start and end. The taper hit a magic button at 0:54 on track 2 and it stayed on for the
remainder of the recording. I hope this reaches all the diehard Goldfinger fans out there and
perhaps inspires the release of more unheard Goldfinger tapes. Cover art is included in
the torrent. Please continue to support the artists by attending their shows and buying
their official merchandise and releases.
-kevdogg
