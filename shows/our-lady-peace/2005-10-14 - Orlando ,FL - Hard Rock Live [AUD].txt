Taper's Notes:
I don't really remember much about this show. I had a real bad allergy problem
on the night of the show and was on a heavy dose of Benedryl. All I really
remember is how much I wanted the show to end so I could get home and go
to sleep. They played the opening chords of U2's "All I Want Is You" right
before "Superman". The disc split is just as the minidisc swap was made.
Though I should have waited until "Superman" was over to do so cause they
left the stage for a couple minutes at that time. The recording sounds pretty
good considering the venue's reputation of very bad acoustics. It's very bad.
