Recorded from center field straight out from the stage, last couple rows of seats.  Traffic was ugly first getting up from the south side of Atlanta and then the place is deep in a residential area so I just made it to our seats as the band came out so I was scambling to get everything set up.  I turned on the recorder ASAP but still had to get the mics set up so the first bit of music is a little "phasey" as the mics get moved around.  The vocals are up front but overall it makes a better headphone recording as it's kind of thin on the instruments, just a function of the location.  And then there's some talkers, not terrible but they are there.  The 6-7 people in front of us talked non-stop for the next 3 hours.  I've never listend to this band before but kind of enjoyed it.  Same set list they've apparently played all tour long.

Enjoy,
MrFender
