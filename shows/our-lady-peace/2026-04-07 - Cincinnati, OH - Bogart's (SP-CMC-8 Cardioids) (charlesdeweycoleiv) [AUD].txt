Our Lady Peace out for their 30th anniversary (in year 32, no less) and this time
they're hitting the US! Finally! Our Lady Peace have not extensively toured the US
as a headliner in many years (2017, maybe?). I was able to catch this one, Detroit
at the Fillmore and Vegas at the HOB. The setlist was career spanning performing 
(most) of their singles and some great deep tracks for the hardcore fans. In Cincy
we got the "Right Behind You (Mafia)" opener (a total shock!!), "Temporary Healing",
"Paper Moon" & "I Wanna Be Your Drug" as the alts that have been swapped in and out
of recent setlists. Missed "Naveed", but thats not played every night. Recording
turned out nice, had very respectful neighbors. Thanks to the band for taping
permission via discord, which was new in 2026. Great to know they support tapers!

(This was rescheduled from March 21st to April 7th due to Raine losing his voice).

The Verve Pipe opened. [recorded].
