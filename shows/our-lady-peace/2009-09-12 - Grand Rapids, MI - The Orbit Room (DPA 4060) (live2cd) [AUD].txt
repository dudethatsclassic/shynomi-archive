Believe it or not, I have not seen Our Lady Peace since September, 2002 at the
State in Detroit - so I was very excited about seeing them in Grand Rapids.
The Orbit Room reminded me a lot of The Rave in Milwaukee, WI. Everything from
the sound to the extreme darkness of the club to the stage size. The Rave does
have a bigger floor, tho. The setlist looks to be the same they have been doing
for the past month, sans swapping out "Denied" for the new track, "The End Is
Where We Begin" off the new release, "Burn Burn". I was very happy about that
as the track happens to be a personal favorite from the new album.
