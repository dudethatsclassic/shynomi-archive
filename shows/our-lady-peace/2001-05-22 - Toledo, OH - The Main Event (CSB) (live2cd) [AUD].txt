I have no idea why OLP were billed to play here in 2001. At the time, they
were playing much larger venues (even in the States), so this sold out
relatively fast since it was a club. My only real memory of this show was
waiting for the band after the show for autographs, seeing Raine pop out
from backstage to walk to the bus and yelling "Hey Raine! Where was the
Birdman tonight!?" he turned around, laughed and kept walking to the bus
not stopping to talk. Oh well. That is still a favorite song of mine, they
just did not play it this night. 

American Hi-Fi opened. [recorded].
