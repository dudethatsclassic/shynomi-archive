Show notes: I've seen Our Lady Peace quite a few times over their thirty year career and let me tell you, they nailed the 30th anniversary setlist.  The hits, rarities and new songs...everything I want from a setlist celebrating everything I love about a band.

It was a Friday night so the crowd was ready for a party and the band delivered.  It's been awhile since I've seen OLP in a a headlining spot in an arena.  It was good to see them celebrate 30 years.
