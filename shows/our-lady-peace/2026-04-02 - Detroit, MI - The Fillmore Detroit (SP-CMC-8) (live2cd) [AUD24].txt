Our Lady Peace out for their 30th anniversary (in year 31, no less) and this time
they're hitting the US! Finally! Our Lady Peace have not extensively toured the US
as a headliner in many years (2017, maybe?). I was able to catch this one and
Cincinnati (which was rescheduled due to illness from March 21st to April 7th).
The Detroit date remained unchanged. Raine Maida unfortunately lost his voice in
Indianapolis. He saw a voice doctor on the east coast and was able to get back out
and finish the tour (with the 4 rescheduled dates tacked on at the end). He does
not sound 100%, but to the untrained ear - you wouldn't even be able to tell he
was so sick a week prior. So glad he was able to pull it together and sing for us!
The setlist was career spanning performing (most) of their singles and some great
deep tracks for the hardcore fans. I was floored when it ran nearly 2 hours.
I used to see OLP in Detroit frequently back in the day, so this was a special
show for me to see them back where they played so many times in the late 90s
and early 2000's. Not to mention with The Verve Pipe opening, who are from the
state of Michigan! Special thanks to the band for taping permission.

The Verve Pipe opened. [recorded].
