The Wallflowers - 2022 10 03 The Guild Theatre, Menlo Park, CA

Show note:
My wife said, The Wallflowers are playing, want to go see them. The answer to that type of question is almost always "yes" ... expecially when the show is 10 minutes away.  I hoped that since it was a Monday night, the audience who showed up would be there to listen to the show. Unfortunately, not really the case.  I ended up having to tell people to stop talking, somebody thanked me after the show because they were really bothered too. Anyhow, the sound at the Guild is always top notch, the staff there is always great.
