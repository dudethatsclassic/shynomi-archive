I'm guessing the location is to keep the sound from traveling to the surrounding neighborhoods as was the problem with the now deceased State Fair Ampitheater. There was a sign language person on the right of the stage, Sharon I think he said her name was. At the end of We're Already There he asked her if he was going to fast, Then Rami started doing his impersonation of sign language. There were also these Sheriff's in training there, Jakob had a field day with one of them towards the end of the show. This was a very loud show, the mics seemed to hold up well and the volume drowned out the female yappers right behind me. I think they were talking about diet secrets and it looked like the secrets didn't work at all on any of them :>)
