Not the best recording I've made, but possibly the only one made at this show.  As expected at a cheap 
outdoor festival, the sound isn't the best and some attendees are only there to talk to each other, 
even when the music is playing.   But, considering that there wasn't a previous setlist on Setlist.FM and
I don't recall ever seeing this show up on torrent sites, this may be the only capture of it.  And, hey,
it don't cost nothing.
