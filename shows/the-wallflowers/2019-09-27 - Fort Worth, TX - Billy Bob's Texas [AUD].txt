Sporadic talkers, clappers, and bar noises

During "One Headlight," Jakob makes reference to Billy F Gibbons.  I'm guessing there was a guy with a long beard up front.  Also during "One Headlight," it appears that security was concerned about a fan looking to get an album autographed. Both of these can be heard in the provided sample.

The Who were scheduled to play AAC in Dallas this night, but the show was postponed due to Roger losing his voice two nights prior in Houston.  JD makes reference to this when they play "The Kid's Are Alright" (partial).
