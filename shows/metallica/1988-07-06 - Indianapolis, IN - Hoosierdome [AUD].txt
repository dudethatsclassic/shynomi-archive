Sadly I must say this is not one of my best efforts.....it was taped from the upper bowl of The Hoosierdome and you can tell by the taped intro that there wasn't enough power given for Metallica to fill the stadium with "metal" and I have to believe that it was intentional as it was Van Halen's "Monsters Of Rock" tour and Metallica was 3rd on the bill. This is off of my master and if there is a better version of this out there feel free to offer it up....I won't be offended! There is some distracting audio mic bumps during the songs between tape flips. Only for the hard core collector
