Notes:

The queue to get into the venue was an absolute nightmare. Because of this, I did not get inside in time to capture Kevelertak's set, as was my initial intention. I missed the first few songs and decided not to tape the rest. In retrospect I wish I would've. I could've used their set to reconfigure my rig. My recording location was eye level with one of the corner stacks and the sound was absolutely punishing! So I went hot for a few songs but got sorted during Seek and Destroy, the 3rd song. Aces from there. 

Also mic and initial gear adjustments forced me to leave the intro tapes and the first half of Hardwired on the cutting room floor. They did get taped, but quality is poor. Lastly, I had developed a severe cough in Barcelona that was forcing me ill during this capture. I was however a good boy and didn't drink any beers before or during the gig, only water and cough drops to soothe. So you might hear an a cough or 2 during the show. Obviously I couldn't control that and tried to only cough in between songs. 

Show is great overall and setlist kicks ass so enjoy!!!
 

It's cool if listen to Orion first in honor of Cliff. Cheers!
