“Master Of Puppets” Demo’s – Recorded in San Francisco, CA?? on 14/07/85.

Metallica Line Up Number 5
Vocals & Rhythm guitar – James Hetfield
Drums – Lars Ulrich
Bass – Cliff Burton
Lead Guitar – Kirk Hammett

Tracks (All instrumentals unless noted):
1 – Disposable Heroes – Take 1 (6:02)
2 – Disposable Heroes – Take 2 (3:08)
3 – Welcome Home (Sanitarium) – Take 1 with vocals, (3:54) (Incomplete)
4 – Welcome Home (Sanitarium) – Take 2 (9:34)
5 – Battery (5:24)
6 – Master Of Puppets (8:42)

Notes – “Disposable Heroes” has different lyrics. Take 2 of “Welcome Home (Sanitarium)” has an extra four minutes of music towards the end of the track, this music was replaced in the final version. The extra four minutes was expanded and reworked until it became the track “Orion”, many bootlegs list this track as “Orion” when it is actually half “Welcome Home Sanitarium” and half “Orion” due to this error on many bootlegs I am only listing track detail for the cd`s that I have had confirmed. The track “Disposable Heroes” was played live six months before the release of the album.
