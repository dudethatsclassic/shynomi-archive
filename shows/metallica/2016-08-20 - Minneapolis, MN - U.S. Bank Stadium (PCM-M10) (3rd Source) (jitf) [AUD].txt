Recording Comments:
An amazing concert to say the least. Although the setlist was nothing special (with the exception of the 'Hardwired' premiere), the crowd energy and volume was intense. The newly built stadium has a capacity of 73,000 and was occupied by a sold-out 55,000 people. During the last song, "Enter Sandman", Metallica released hundreds of bouncy balls from the roof and at the end of the concert had a full-on fireworks display inside the stadium (the retractable roof was not open, making it that much more awesome). I was roughly 50 feet back and to the mid-left on the Floor/GA. I have labeled this as the 3rd source, being that at this time I'm only aware of the obvious LiveMetallica.com SBD recording and the Pandora Live Stream. As an added bonus that will help separate this source from the others, I have included the 93x radio station intro and sound check, as well as isolated and made a separate track for when James Hetfield realizes he accidentally forgot a part of Leper Messiah. I also recorded both opening bands, Volbeat and Avenged Sevenfold. The pictures included with this were taken with my Samsung Galaxy S7 Edge at 12 megapixels and the two video clips were also taken with it at 1920x1080 resolution.
