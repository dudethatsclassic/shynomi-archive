This was the 21st performance in London since they first played here on March 27, 1984. 

This was a special small theatre show for members of The Metallica Club (www.metclub.com), radio contest winners, and industry folks. 

This was the second time that Until It Sleeps had been performed in the last eight years. 

This was the second performance of That Was Just Your Life, The End of the Line, and Broken Beat & Scarred. 

This was the third time that Frantic was performed in London.
