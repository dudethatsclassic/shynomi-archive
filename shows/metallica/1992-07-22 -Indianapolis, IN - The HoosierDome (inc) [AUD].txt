Today Paul would have been 40 years old.... way too young for any soul to leave this earth. So in his memory I want to share this show with all of his fellow Metallica fans.

In loving memory of Paul K Manzo II 1/23/1971 - 7/23/2007 R.I.P.

 I am posting this show which has not been shared to many outside a close circle of friends. This was recorded @ The HoosierDome when Metallica & Guns 'N Roses did a co-headling tour. Unfortunately I had some kind of malfunction and the encores was not recorded.... and most of the Guns 'N Roses set was not recorded either as me & my friends had passes to hang out with Metallica and chose to do that instead. After it ended we listened to a little bit of G 'N R but left early due to Axl's pompous attitude towards the Indianapolis crowd.

This is all there is..... enjoy it for what it is!

Metallica 1992-07-22 The HoosierDome Indianapolis IN
