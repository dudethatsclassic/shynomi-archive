Hi Metallifucks!
 Jan 23rd would have been the 38th birthday of my dear friend Paul K Manzo II (aka "Motorcity Sanitarium") and I know if he had beaten the cancer he had been battling for so long he would be hounding the shit out of me & Dave to make it up to see Jason Newsted play again with Metallica when they are inducted into the Rock And Roll Hall Of Fame at Public Hall in Cleveland Ohio on April 4th!
 So in the memory of Paul and to also show appreciation for Metallica offering an "olive branch" out to Jason I will try my best to upload a bulk of my Metallica Master recordings between now and the anniversary of his passing 6 months later. Please no requests as I want to do it on my own terms.

I had this show in my hard drive forever and have tried to find the pre-show backstage comments for this and 2 other shows from 1993 with little luck. If I should find them I will include them in a future upload but for now I will up the shows without them.

This is the last show from 1993 that I have have to share.

If burning to cdr start disc 2 with Track 10 James Speaks & disc 3 Track21A Nothing Else Matters

Enjoy!
