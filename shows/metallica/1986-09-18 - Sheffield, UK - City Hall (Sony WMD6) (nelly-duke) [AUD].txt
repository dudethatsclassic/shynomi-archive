Metallica: City Hall, Sheffield 18.9.86

My master. Sony WMD6C + Aiwa CM30.

Not many there that night. Ground floor was 75% full max. I was in the Grand Circle which was 1-2 rows full. Upper Balcony was empty. This is probably the best I've seen Metallica. They were young, hungry and just went for it for the entire gig.
