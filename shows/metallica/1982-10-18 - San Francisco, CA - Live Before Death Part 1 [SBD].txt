Comments: Another rare recording to come by.
Anyway, tracks 1-7 recorded at The Lyceum Ballroom, London(UK) 12/12/84 (Sbd) and 8-11 are Ride The Lightning demos(supposedly). There's crowd cheers before and after these songs, they possibly put the fake crowd on these just like they did for the "Creeping Death/Jump In The Fire" bonus songs. None the less they are still great to listen to. There is some differences between these versions and the ones on the albumn "Ride The Lightning".
I personally love this CD, it ranks up there as one of my all time top five recordings. Given the time/technology/cost to do this at the time, with all those factored in, make's this A MUST OWN recording. I, myself, have never found a recording this old from Metallica that sounded soooo good! 

I decided to offer both of these together for the fact, that nobody wants just part 1 or part 2. If you're like me, you want both! So here they are, for all to enjoy. I hope these old shows of Metallica keep popping up, it's what the world's missing........GREAT F&%KIN' MUSIC!
Enjoy.
