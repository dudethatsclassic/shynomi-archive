show notes: alright,...have shied away from notes from most of these Woodstock recordings, as without going thru the pics, I really don't remember much. however, I do remember Nine Inch Nails getting all muddy and rocking the fuck out. 


and the mud, from the 'urban legend', was to enhance their 'pedestrian light show' (still pretty bad ass ,there was an easy quarter-mil watching the mainstage at that point), as the "deal" was, that only "Woodstock lighting" would be used: #endofstory

well, Metallica crushed that theory all to shit. before they're 30 second into "Breadfan"......BOOOOM, it was Pyro City. apparently, Metallica didn't believe that the rules applied to them, and blew shit up all night long. this angered Aerosmith, as they were the 'finale band', and I can't remember what exactly happened, if they got extra lights or what...it was all kind of a blur by that point. (lotta rain over those 3 days, but the deck never got fried)


for some reason the Aerosmith set sounds a *lot* better than this one. might have been shifted around by the crowd a bit for this one. even from 300 yards away. but this show does clean up some a little less than halfway in. it's nothing special, but it is an audience document of the broadcast.




you can hear me try to light my joint repeatedly during the intro music (wet lighter?). followed by the "cough cough" afterwards.



"Breadfan" has a couple minor level fluctuations. LOTS of bass (the bass really was amazing at this show)





this is the 'no noise reduction' version. yes, there's hiss. but it really is from the master. started using Dolby soon after this. also found Maxell XL-IIS's to be much better tapes soon after this (by May of '95, anyways) 


thanks be to bubba. more to come.

---ballsdeep OUT!!!
