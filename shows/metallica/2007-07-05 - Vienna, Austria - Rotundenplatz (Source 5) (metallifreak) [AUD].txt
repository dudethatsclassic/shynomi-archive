This was the fourth time that Metallica has performed in Vienna. The last time was 11 years ago on September 6, 1996 at Stadhalle. It was the opening date of the European Poor Touring Me tour. 

This was the first time that Stone Cold Crazy has been performed since Salt Lake City on November 22, 2004.

This was the first time that The Memory Remains has been performed in Austria. 

This was the first time that Ride the Lightning has been performed in Austria.

This was the first time that …And Justice for All has been performed in Austria.
