QUALITY: Pretty rough quality. This was really rough to start with, but I've managed to
do some EQing to it, which I think improved the recording. There were a lot of cuts in random
spots, I cleaned those up as best I could. You may notice some of the bigger cuts, but it
is so much better now. Best left for the collectors.

SOURCE: unknown mics > unknown recorder
TRANSFER: unknown gen cassette > Adobe Audigy Platnium > Goldwave > CDWAVE > WAV (transferred by Jeff S.)

CORRECT SHOW: Yes, Lisbon is said several times.

FLAWS: Numerous cuts that were fixed, but still doesn't flow in some spots.

BAND PERFORMANCE: Solid performance by the band. Another one of those recordings where
it's pretty hard to tell specifics because of quality. Overall it's the standard set
for summer '93. There are certainly better '93 shows to get.
