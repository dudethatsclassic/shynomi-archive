I believe I got this originally from the ZOMB in the spring
of 2006. It came with both the live show and demo recordings so 
I choose to leave them together.

Unfortunately no lineage information came with this show, but
info I found on the internet confirms it is a soundboard. It sounds 
very good and is the complete set according to what I discovered.

The demos seem like rehearsal recordings, and sound like 
multigenerational cassette recordings and have a lot of hiss, but
are still an interesting listen and once the songs get going you
don't notice the hiss as much. 

One bit of info that did come with the show is that it has been 
released as a bootleg CD under 4 different names:

Into The Gates Of Hell
Puppets In El Paso
Dallas Goes To The Dogs
Metal, Inc.
