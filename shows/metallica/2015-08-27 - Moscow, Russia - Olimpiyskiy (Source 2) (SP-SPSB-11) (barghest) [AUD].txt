Notes provided via LiveMetallica.com
------------------------------------
This was the fifth time that we played a show in Moscow.

We first performed in Moscow on September 28, 1991.

This was the first time that "Ride the Lightning" was played in Moscow. It was also the first time the song had been performed since November 8, 2014.

This was the first time in eight years that "Battery" was played in Moscow.

"King Nothing," "The Unforgiven," "Lords of Summer," "The Frayed Ends of Sanity" and "Whiskey in the Jar" were all performed in Moscow for the first time.

The boys played 17 songs off of nine albums and one non-album track: Kill 'Em All (1), Ride the Lightning (3), Master of Puppets (3), ...And Justice for All (2), Metallica (4), Load (1), Reload (1), Garage Inc. (1), Death Magnetic (1) and Non-Album Tracks (1)
