Notes provided via LiveMetallica.com
------------------------------------

This was the 19th time Metallica played in Paris and the 37th time they’ve played in France.  They first played Paris on February 8, 1984 at the Espace Ballard.

The last time they played Paris was on June 23, 2004 at Parc des Princes.

This was the first time in 16 years that Disposable Heroes was performed in Paris.  It was last played on June 13, 1993 at the Hippodrome de Paris Vincennes during the Nowhere Else to Roam Tour.

This was the third time that Metallica performed The Unforgiven in Paris.  It was last played on June 13, 1993 at the Hippodrome de Paris Vincennes during the Nowhere Else to Roam Tour.

This was only the second time that Metallica has EVER performed Stone Dead Forever.  The only other time it was played was on December 14, 1995 at the Whisky a Go-Go during Metallica’s secret gig as “The Lemmy’s” as they celebrated Lemmy’s 50th birthday.
