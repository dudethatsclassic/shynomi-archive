The Whiplash-No Remorse demos are the only recordings featuring Dave and Cliff together.

14. Hit The Lights 
This is the very first Metallica recording & was recorded in 1981.
This version of this song has LLoyd Grant on lead guitar, James Hetfield on rhythm guitar and bass & Lars Ulrich on Drums.
