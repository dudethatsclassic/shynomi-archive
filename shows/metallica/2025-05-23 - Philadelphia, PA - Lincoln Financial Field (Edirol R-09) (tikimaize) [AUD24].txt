Celebrated my birthday with Family, Friends, and Metallica, It doesn't get any better that that!
Yet another awesome show as always. Was funny hearing James get booed when he said he is a Raiders
fan, only in Philly! Really nice setlist with Cyanide replacing the usual 3rd 72 seasons slot. 

Being my birthday I had 3 goals, get a show poster, get a show specific shirt, and a pic with 
the date on it, went 3 for 3! My sister got the pic for me since I had seats. My best friend
also grabbed me a giant beach ball.

As an extra I've included pictures from Philly and Virginia Tech. The close-up pictures from
Philly were taken by my best friend, I had seats this time. I was going to record the audio from
VT as well but my SD card was corrupted, and I didn't find that out until I was in the parking lot.
At least I can share some nice photos.

Enjoy my friends!
