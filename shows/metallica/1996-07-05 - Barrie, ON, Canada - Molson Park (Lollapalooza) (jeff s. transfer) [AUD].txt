Notes and comments:
Transferred by Jeff S.
Taper is far back on this one, so it's distant. Even better he's standing next to someone who likes to talk and sing. During Creeping Death the guy sings "So Let it Be Written, So Let it Be Done" over and over because that's the only lyrics he knows. He also likes to say "Yeah" and then likes to say the song titles after he figures out what song they are playing. Lots of charming things come from this guy. That being said, this one doesn't sound half bad, but due to the fan-boy it's probably best left to collectors. Towards the later half of the show you don't hear the guy as much, either he or the taper must move away.
The city name is not said during the show, but I got this shortly after the show happened so I'm sure it's correct. For reference right before Until it Sleeps James says "You guys aren't throwing shit are ya?" Something unique to compare other shows to incase there is a mislabeled show.
