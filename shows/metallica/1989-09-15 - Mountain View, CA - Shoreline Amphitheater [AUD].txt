Taper: Karl Lundmark
Gear: Aiwa CM 30a mic > Sony WMD6
Transferred & mastered by }{eywood
Lineage: Maxell XLII 90 and TDK SA60 cassettes (1) > Optimus SCT-57 playback deck > Adobe Audition 1.5 (recording to .wav @ 24/96, Speed correction, EQ, multitrack patch repair of flawed spots, peak limiting, splits & fades) > Xrecode II (conversion to 16/44.1) > TLH (sector alignment, flac 8)


One of a series of California shows Karl bought a new deck to record.  This one suffers from crackly distortion in the right channel and a large amount of mic bumps from the rowdy crowd.  The mic seems to have been hidden below the crowd for most of the show, and the soundstage moves around a lot.  In one way or another every song was flawed in this recording.

I've painstakingly removed all the mic bumps either by EQing out the low end on them or by deleting them altogether.  All deleted data was patched in from             .  The changes in the soundfield have been corrected as much as I could, but the constant changing was impossible to correct completely.
