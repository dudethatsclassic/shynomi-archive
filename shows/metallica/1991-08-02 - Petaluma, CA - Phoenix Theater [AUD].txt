Metallica
Phoenix Theater
Petaluma, CA
August 2, 1991


From the collection of koondog a/k/a Rush-Fan

Taper: Karl Lundmark
Gear: Aiwa CM30a > Sony WMD6
Transferred & mastered by }{eywood
Lineage: Maxell XLII 90 cassette (1) > JVC TD-W354 playback deck > Adobe Audition 1.5 (recording to .wav @ 24/96, EQ, peak limiting, splits & fades) > Xrecode II (conversion to 16/44.1) > TLH (sector alignment, flac 8)


Hiding beneath the surface of a very muddy sounding tape with virtually no high end was this gem, one of the rehearsal shows for the Black Album tour.  After some radical EQing the show revealed itself to be quite good sounding.  A tapeflip just after seek & Destroy was patched with the show played here the previous night, also taped by Karl with the same gear.

A couple of files got corrupted in the 24 bit version as I was finishing prepping the filesets.  I'm not going to do it over, so only a 16 bit is being shared for this one.
