TT: 119:09 This IS the COMPLETE show. Yellow Ledbetter was NOT
performed at the end of this show. I don't know who got that idea... 

I accompanied the taper to this show and it was a real experience. Every
heavy-metal stoner in Pittsburgh and surrounding areas was there and 
everyone was very loaded to say the least. I had never seen the band before
and while I came away from the concert not a real big Metallica fan, it was
an enjoyable and interesting experience. Queensryche opened and they played 
a great 45 minute set to an enthusiastic crowd. It was Jason's birthday so
you can call this gig "Jason's Birthday Party" if you're into naming your
live shows. The band played for about a minute under 2 hours and it was a
ferocious performance. If you want to know my initial impressions of the band
members after my first Metallica concert, here goes. (If you don't care, skip
ahead ; these may not be to your liking). 

James: loud-mouthed obnoxious punk. This guy and his stage 
banter really got on my nerves quickly.
Jason: pretty good bassist but not incendiary.
Kirk: excellent lead guitarist. This guy shredded all night.  
Lars: excellent drummer, his later Congressional (or was it the Senate ?)
whining and crying notwithstanding... 

So there you have it. My two cents worth...Didn't mean to piss off any
die-hard fans and I'm not arrogant ; I just thought some people might
be curious about a 1st timer's 1st impressions.

As far as the recording quality goes, it's excellent minus IMHO venturing
into excellent. No hiss, speed problems, distortion, etc. At the end of
MOP (I think) there is a brief thump-thump sound at the end of the guitar
solo. This is familiar to us older guys who've done a lot of recording on
cassette tape. It's a malfunction of the tape that usually occurred at the
end or beginning of a cassette side. It's short and pretty quiet so no big
deal. It's on the master. The entire concert is presented here except for
what I deemed to be boring applause and screaming after certain songs. NO
MUSICAL CONTENT WAS CUT. This is also the raw (undoctored) tape. No EQ or other
sonic screwing around was attempted. The sound quality in the good old Civic
Arena was excellent that night and you're hearing what I heard that evening.
The SQ came through impeccably. Pretty good for a 21-year-old tape made on a
$ 60 USD machine ! Wellllll, that's about all that I have to say so let's get 
this show on the road...
