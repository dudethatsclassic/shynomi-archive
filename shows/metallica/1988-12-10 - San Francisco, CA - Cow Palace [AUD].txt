From the collection of koondog a/k/a Rush-Fan

Taper: Karl Lundmark
Gear: Sony D3
Transferred & mastered by }{eywood
Lineage: TDK SA60 cassettes (1) > Optimus SCT-57 playback deck > adobe Audition 1.5 (recording to .wav @ 24/96, speed correction, EQ. peak limiting, splits & fades) > Xrecode II (conversion to 16/44.1) > TLH (sector alignment, flac 8)


Although the recording starts out sounding decent during the intro tape, Karl spent most of the first few songs wandering around the venue looking for a good spot to tape.  The sound stabilizes around Sanitarium and the rest of the show sounds good, if a little distant.  TYapeflip patches in Master of Puppets and Seek & Destroy taken from Sacramento Dec. 12, 1988.
