Great fucking time. Went with a friend and met up with Nick for 
some beverages before the show. Show came out very nice. You will
notice a change in quality midway through. During All Nightmare Long
I moved to a different location that was less crowded, therefore less
"noisy" so the sound improves a little. I appologise for the comment
during Ecstasy but my friend had a bit to drink and decided to speak
in the mic, but at least it was only the intro. :) I also included 
the soundcheck as track 00. If you dont want it feel free to get rid 
of it. It's just a little bonus for those who want it. Enjoy my friends!
