Hello friends! I finally decided to record a show and give something back to the Metallica community.
I feel it came out pretty good considering this was my first live recording and the use of the internal
mic on the recorder. The Edirol cost me enough and I didnt have the cash for mics (this time). I have a
new respect for tapers. I was a little nervous going through security with all the nightmares reported
about the previous night. Also for the first time in my life I was not in the crowd trying to get up front
or screaming every song at the top of my lungs! And to all you video tapers out there it must be 10x harder.
This show goes out to all the people who take the time to record shows for us. Thank you!!

Enjoy!

Metallica February 1st, 2009 Prudential Center - Newark, NJ USA
Source : Edirol R-09HR > Internal Mic > 24 bit Wav @ 48.0kHz
Transfer : Nero Wave Editor > Nero > CD Image > Windows Media Player > 16 bit Wav > Traders Little Helper > FLAC > You
Location : Center pit, ~25 feet from the stage

Taped by tikimaize

Note : I had to convert to a cd image because FLAC would not encode the 24bit files. I was able to convert
from the lower bit cd image.


DO NOT CONVERT TO LOSSY FORMAT!!!!!!
