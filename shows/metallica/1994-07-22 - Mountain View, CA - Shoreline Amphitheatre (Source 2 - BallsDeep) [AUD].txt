show notes: well here you have the almighty Metallica on the Summer S*** tour. I had high expectations of varied setlists and Alice In Chains when buying tix to 4 of the shows. instead, I got "the same songs played in the same order with the same between-song banter said between the same songs played in the same order" each night....and CANDLECROTCH (nee: Candlebox). needless to say, I was pretty disappointed in this tour, both with the setlists and with the playing, as even "Disposable Heroes" sounded more like a Sabbath sludgefest ("The Writ"? "Children Of The Sea"?) than fiery Metallica. but I *do* realize you collectors 'want it all', so here you go, directly from the master. enormous thanks to the buzzsaw known as bubba420 for the upload, and who knows what I'll be fired up to dig out next???
