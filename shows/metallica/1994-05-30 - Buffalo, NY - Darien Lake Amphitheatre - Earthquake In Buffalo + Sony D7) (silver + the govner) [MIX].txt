 This is NOT a third source, but instead it is a matrix of the two sources available for this show that I created, This is my first ever MTX! I think overall it turned out great. It sounds a bit muddy
because the second source was a bit distorted, I cleaned up a little bit and lowered the volume slight to cover up the muddy sound. The 1st source however is really great and makes up for the messy 2nd source.
 Put them together and we have this wonderful MTX. Once again I can't help if it sounds muddy at times because its how the 2nd source was recorded, maybe its just me and usually no one else notices but I think I
 did good for my first attempt. This show is just one of my many MTX to come in the future, and better sounding sources = better sounding MTX. 

Any cover art will be appreciated.

This is the first Metallica show from their 1944 Summer In The Shed Tour. This is also the first time that The God That Failed has been played live. It is also the first time Kill/Ride was played live.
