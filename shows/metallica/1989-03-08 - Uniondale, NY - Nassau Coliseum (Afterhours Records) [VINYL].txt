Here's a Metallica source I didn't see on the TTD tracker.

I own the vinyl for this and transferred this many years ago.

I can't remember what brand of turntable i used in 2006,  (i don't have it anymore).

I had to pitch this up +1% and manually scrub down some of the needle pops.

The album was very clean with almost No surface noise.  I would consider this an average audience recording.

I haven't heard the other sources, but considering where the tape breaks are, this vinyl source seems to be different.

Does anybody have the video sources they can share here ?

I've included scans of the album artwork in the folder.

This version is missing the into music The Ecstasy of Gold and the final song Breadfan.
