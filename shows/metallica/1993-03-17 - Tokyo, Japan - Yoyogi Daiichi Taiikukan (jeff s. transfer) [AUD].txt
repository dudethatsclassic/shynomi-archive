QUALITY: Below average quality. The music is pretty much smashed together to make one sound. The vocals come across fairly well. Probably best left for collectors, it actually sounds a little sped up
as well. 

SOURCE: unknown mics > unknown recorder
TRANSFER: unknown gen cassette > Adobe Audigy Platinum > Goldwave > CDWAVE > WAV (transferred by Jeff S.)
TIME: 148 minutes

CORRECT SHOW: Yes, several references to it being the second night in Tokyo.

FLAWS: Slight cut in 1st disk for tape flip, can't remember which song it is. Second disk I
screwed up a bit when removing the tape break. A small snippet of One repeats itself, I should've
done it better, but in the end my care factor is low on getting that error fixed.

BAND PERFORMANCE: Second night set so we get to hear The Four Horsemen, and they swap out Whiplash
for Battery. They can't play Tokyo without doing a Godzilla teaser..hah. The crowd
starts off pretty quiet, but towards the end of the show the crowd gets a lot more into
the show. Not sure if it is due to those polite Japan audiences or what.  James makes
a few comments early on about finally making noise.
