Notes:
As far as regular live shows go this is a rough one, but as far as old Germs shows this was always one of my favorites because the band seems to be pretty tight at this point and Darby at least starts out singing in the mic. I have never been able to pinpoint a date. I figure it has to be from the other Masque and the date should be late 1978 or even early 1979, based on the songs they are playing. Brendan talks about a show at the end that should make it easy to figure out the date, but I haven't found anything about the show.
