I got this quite awhile back in a tape trade. The date on this is suspect and may not actually be Sep 22 79. They played several shows at the Hong Kong Cafe when few other places would have them. Several of these tracks appeared on the "Life is...." series. The band are in good form and appear to be playing to a small crowd. This show contains some classic Darby dialogue. I am not sure if they played Shutdown as there is a bit of an intro at the end of the last song. The recording on my tape ends at that point. I've often wondered if they pulled the plug on the show? Somehow it does not seem complete. If anyone has something more complete please post it. Enjoy.
