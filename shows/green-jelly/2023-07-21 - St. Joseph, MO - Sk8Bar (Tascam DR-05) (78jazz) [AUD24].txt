When I saw this was coming to my hometown, I knew I had to go. I am not sure I would go again though. All of the members of the band had little "side projects" that played 3 songs each and the band doesn't hit the stage until 12:10 A.M. Maybe this should be July 22, 2023 then? Bill drops a lot of F-bombs here, so this likely NSFW and one may want to use parental discretion. I would have saved more of the soundcheck but there a sample of Van Halen's Jump so I cut that out. I may have one of the songs wrong as there is a bit about a cow god (if you know this error please let me know so I can correct the track naming). As could be expected, Three Little Pigs is what garnered the most response. Overall, this was a wild show with the berating Bill Manspeaker engaged in. As I left, he went to blow up industrial fireworks at an intersection in downtown just 2 blocks south of the police station. I saw these as I was leaving (I was parked southwest of the venue) and expected to hear that law enforcement paid him a visit. To my surprise, when I asked the venue proprietors about this they said nothing happened.
