Notes: 
Palacio Euskalduna is a huge auditorium. You'll notice that in the sound of this recording. It's reverberant. But, the biggest sound problem to solve has been the difference of levels between music and audience noises (mainly applauses). Moreover, the PA wasn't loud enough (naughty soundmen...), so slight distortions may be heard in some moments due to applauses or sudden voice peaks ("Too Far Apart"). Sorry for that.
Otherwise, the sound is clear enough and the gig was awesome. At the end "Spiders" a couple left their seats and went to first row to dance. The bouncers (they were also two) started freaking out. It was a tense moment, though, and you can feel it during the long "clap-show" just before the end of the song. 
Enjoy it!
