Audience Master Recording

patched to Sony D-7 DAT

DAT Master > Panasonic SV-3800 > digital coaxial > Audigy 2 ZS Platinum > 
Soundforge* > 16 bit 44.1 wav > CD Wave (tracking) > Flac Frontend  (level 7, 
align sector boundaries) > flac

*Soundforge was used to capture, boost levels (150%), two patches, and set fades.  
This 9 year old DAT was extracted 5 times before I could get an acceptable extraction.  
Two skips in the best extraction were patched with identical pieces from a prior 
extraction, but one small blip in the loud portion of Kingpin could not be completely 
fixed (you might not even hear it).

The Show:
Three of us tapers brought our rigs to this show, but the venue would only allow one 
rig--so we chose the Schoeps guy.  Thanks a ton to the guy who ran the rig, and gave 
out two patches.
This is the infamous "deli tray" Wilco show.  If you're a Wilco collector, you probably 
have a dry soundboard recording of this show, and the DVD of the HBO partial broadcast 
authored by the excellent JHamm.
Of all the Wilco shows I attended, this is the best one.  The band's performance is energetic, 
fun, tight and inspired.  This recording captures that energy in a way that the soundboard 
recording does not.  I received a DAT of that soundboard years ago from my friend Mark 
Linton, and he agreed that my audience master was a better listen.  Enjoy!
