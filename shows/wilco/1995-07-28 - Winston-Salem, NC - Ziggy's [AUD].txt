This post is all about freely sharing some great music, spreading the vibe of these bands, increasing sales of their goods and attendance at their shows and having a little fun.

______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

Ever since ademotte planted a healthy cache of dynamite at the base of Tweedy Dam a few weeks ago, blowing it to bits and unleashing this fine flood of early Wilco shows we've been enjoying, I've been scouring the basement for a few Wilco masters that hadn't seen the light of day since ye olde analogue tape trading days - if ever. Finally came up with the first one - this great set performed under the musical big top that was Ziggy's. I'm pretty positive this one has never left the building til now.

Some recollections of this show - First and foremost, Wilco absolutely killed. This was an opening slot for The Jayhawks and I didn't envy them having to follow Wilco on this sweltering July  evening. Also this is one of very few opening acts I've witnessed where the band not only played a longer set but was called back for an encore and obliged. Talked with Jeff after the show and he came across as a very reserved, humble and shy guy. This was a bit of a contrast as he's downright chatty during the show. Another thing that was apparent - they were having an absolute blast. I love all the stylistic phases of Wilco but the '95-'97 AM/Being There shows were arguable the most fun of the bands career. Jay Bennett seemed to embody that fun and I think some of it vanished when he did.

As for the recording, I was having a tape deck issue when this show came 'round and I had to revert back to my old MD to record this one. It got one over on me as I did not notice the ALC was engaged. For that reason you get some slight "breathing" in certain areas. I've got examples in the comments to illustrate so you can make an informed decision. I also ran out of MD as the band hit the last note of the last song so it is minutely cut, but cut nonetheless. Check the samples and use the democratic process. Y'all enjoy.
