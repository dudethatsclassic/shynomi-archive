Notes on the patch:

To patch this I used cool edit pro 2.0 in a multitrack session.  At 7:06:180 on the sbd, I layered the 
2nd half of spiders from 10-31-04 onto the track which enabled me to slowly fade it in over a 7.423 
second period so that it makes a good slow transition into the the audio recording.  On the aud 
recording I used a slow fade over that 7.423 second period which gradually brought it from 20% 
amplification back up to its original 100% setting so that it sounds seamless.  I then raised the 
remaining minute and a half of audience yelling on the soundboard 2.5 db (except for the part where 
Tweedy says "thanks a lot")  to match the aud and then slow faded down the audience recording 
during the last 40 seconds to bring it back to equilibrium with the soundboard by the end of the track.
