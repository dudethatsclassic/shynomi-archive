WILCO
August 25th, 2025
Seattle, Washington, USA
Woodland Park Zoo

Recorded on an iPhone, so lower your expectations.

iPhone 7 > cooledit > CD Wave editor > Trader's Little Helper

This is the second of two nights that Wilco played in Seattle on their "Evening With Wilco" tour, 
which consisted of two long sets and an encore. This night also happened to be Jeff's birthday!
So there is a birthday theme running through the night, which features slightly different songs from 
the previous night, and fixes the Star Wars and Ode To Joy neglect, leaving Wilco (The Album) as
the sole record left unrepresented. Sad camel!

The show took place in a big field at a really cool zoo, and I stood in basically the same spot 
I was the previous night - front row on Pat's side, in front of a speaker. My iPhone recording
turned out decently, and since no other recording has surfaced (to my knowledge), I felt it was
time to share. I hope somebody digs it!
