Thoughts
------------------------------------

Another fabulous Wilco show at the even more fabulous Fillmore. Like my recording of the Wednesday 2016-09-07 show, I was able to set up in the far left corner of the auditorium with mics flying on a stand. The song selection was great; probably 9-10 repeats from Wednesday and a slew of other classics. Bit of a noisier crowd, but hey, it was Saturday night. All in all, I'm glad to have seen and recorded 2/5 of the Fillmore run.
