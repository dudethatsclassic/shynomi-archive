NOTES

Here is my recording from this show, an alternate source to Jeff S's recording. I believe that Jeff's recording is the better overall document of the show, but I like this one as well. I'm pretty sure that I only traded Jeff's recording so this version is previously uncirculated.

PROS OF THIS RECORDING:

- Jeff S was set up by the soundboard, which at Lounge Ax was against the left wall maybe 50 feet from the stage (just a guess), whereas I was closer and centered in the sweet spot about 15 feet from the stage. As a result this recording has a bit more "life" from the the wider sound field and increased stereo and instrumental separation.

CONS OF THIS RECORDING:

- Jeff's mic was at least 12 feet up on a stand and back behind much of the crowd, whereas I was right in the middle of the crowd that was rowdy as hell...pretty funny at times but loud. The "Pecan Pie" guy was right next to me (you can also hear him on my recording of Mississippi Nights from Dec 23, 1994), then he missed the song (maybe in the bathroom) and tried to get the band to play it again! Most of the crowd noise is between songs. Listen to this recording with headphones and you will hear exactly what it was like to be at Wilco's very first Chicago show.

- The low end is a bit mushy and boomy compared to Jeff's recording, his mic was much higher quality.

- There is occasional phasing from me moving to let people pass (it was packed tight). 


I stayed at Jeff S's place that night and staying up very late comparing our recordings and talking shop was great, geeky fun.

Enjoy,

mrpember, January, 2021
