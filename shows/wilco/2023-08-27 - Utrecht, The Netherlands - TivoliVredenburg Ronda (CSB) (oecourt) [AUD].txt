This was the fourth show of my 3 countries/5 shows/7 days trip with Wilco through continental Europe.
After the TOdays show in Torino, I left the city early in the morning at 7 a.m. to head back north and home.
Soon after crossing the Alps, the temperature dropped for the first time during the whole week below 30° Celsius
and by the time I reached home at 5 p.m., I had seen plenty of rain and felt fresh enough for the second
part of my trip. I picked up my dear old friend Bou later in the evening, shared some beers and laughs
and by 11 a.m. the next morning we were on the road to Utrecht. All went fine, with the hotel a mere 100 meters from the venue.
I've seen the Tweedy Band before at the Grote Zaal, but the smaller Ronda was new to me and as both 
Utrecht shows were sold out, I quickly decided to find a spot up on the balcony instead in the packed crowd.
On the first night, I did not manage to get a DFC spot but nonetheless, the recording turned out very well.

Everything else there is to say about the show has been posted by bböp on his (as always !) great
re-cap on the trusty viachicago.org forum, go ahead and read it, even if you'd have to register.

In terms of the setlist, Evicted, the lead-off track of their forthcoming (09/29/2023) album 'Cousin',
was back in the set, along with most welcome returns of the too seldom played Mystery Binds and
that old chestnut, Box Full Of Letters. I think/hope Bird Without A Tail will become a setlist regular 
in years to come, and on this night, Courtney Marie Andrews finally got to sing on California Stars.

Hello and thanks to Bou, Wee, Katja & Thomas, Samuel and bböp. 
TS, sorry to have missed you ... didn't know you were there.
