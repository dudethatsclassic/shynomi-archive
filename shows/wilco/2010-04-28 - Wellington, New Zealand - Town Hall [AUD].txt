Wilco - Town Hall, Wellington, NZ - 28 April 2010

Got this shortly after the show from the taper. To the best of my knowledge they haven't circulated and I've only provided copies to a handful of people, but thought others may be interested. 
The audio quality is good, and the show is solid, but not outstanding. Having said that, it does include a few songs that are now rarely played, a couple of songs that show up irregularly, and a nice Big Star cover.
