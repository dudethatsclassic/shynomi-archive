Tapers Comments:
This was Wilco's first ever appearence in Belfast and they played quite an amazing set.A long and very loud show!.The gig itself was in a Marquee with a wooden floor and the ceiling was covered in hundreds of twinkling lights to resemble stars.Jeff even passed comment on it.It was a fantastic show from start to finish.In my 13 years of going to see Wilco,this gig was up there with the best of them!.I was standing about 10 ft from the front between Jeff and John just right of centre.The recording turned out mighty fine!(to my ears anyway!) Enjoy, Bigjim!
