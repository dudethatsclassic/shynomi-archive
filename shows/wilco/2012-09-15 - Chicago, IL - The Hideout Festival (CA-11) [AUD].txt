Comments: 

Really solid performance kicking off the most recent leg of their "Whole Love" tour; I believe this may be the first time in Chicago rock history 
that a band performed at Orchestra Hall and in a parking lot next door to Chicago's fleet of garbage trucks within a 12-month period.  The band 
was really energized and in good spirits, although Jeff was nursing a head cold (not terribly evident during the music, but he sounded really 
congested while chatting between songs).  The setlist was a good mix of familiar "classics" and "deep tracks," and the band was in good form.  Highlights
included an under-rehearsed "Nothing'severgonna ...", soulful versions of "Poor Places" and "Radio Cure," and a rocking encore.  Fun show.

In terms of sound quality, I'm very happy with the fidelity of this recording -- sound was excellent for an outdoor show and there was very little wind.
I had low expectations given that it was a sold-out GA show on a Saturday night, and there is quite a bit of chatter evident during the quiet parts (and a 
few noisy outbursts that I tried to mitigate with Audio Cleaning Lab) but it settled down for the last half of the show.  If you can live with a little 
audience interference here and there during the first hour, this is a really good recording.

Download, and don't forget to buy the band's studio and live releases (there are now 8 of them from the current tour) at www.wilcoworld.net.
