Wilco - Live at Rolling Stone Weekender - Weissenhäuser Strand - 11-10-2011

Audience recording, recorded on ZOOM H2.

A decent Wilco show I recorded at the Rolling Stone Weekender on November 10 2011. Wilco headlined the first night of the festival. Good setlist and performance. Due to time restrictions they "only" played a 90 minutes set with no encores.  This is the complete show . Just the first four bars of the opener are missing since I was too slow setting up the recorder. Don't be fooled by the overall level of the first song. It starts really quiet but thats the way the band played it.

Lineage: Zoom H2 -> Realtek HD Audio -> Goldwave V 5.55 EQ, Peak Reduction, Track Splitting  -> Traders Little Helper Flac Encoding, Checksums and Torrent.
