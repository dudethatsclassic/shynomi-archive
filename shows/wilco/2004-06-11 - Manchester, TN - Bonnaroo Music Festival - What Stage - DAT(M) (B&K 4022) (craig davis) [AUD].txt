I've found out that there are many Wilco shows up to ~2005 that have probably never been torrented (at least not since ~2005)
but have only been traded, presumably in the last years of traditional trading. db.etree currently lists ~130 such Wilco/Tweedy shows !!

Somehow, these shows were forgotten in the first waves of torrenting - or deemed not worthy of digital distribution 
... or simply those traders in possesion never made the step to torrenting and then stopped trading altogether, like most everybody did.
I've searched high and low, checked countless old lists and sent a lot of asking mails to ancient email addresses. 
About half of them bounced, another quarter didn't reply - and the ones that did hadn't traded (or stopped collecting) in a long time. 
But then I found some nice persons who were willing to share shows with me. 

Arrangements were made and in 2018/19 I was able to share close to 30 Wilco and Jeff Tweedy shows with you.
As a result, a couple more Wilco collectors got in touch and provided me with some more shows that I'll be able to share with you,
some have never circulated, others have not been torrented for a long, long while.
I still have not listened to them all, most need some work, it'll take some time, please be patient. 
Now, by August 2023, all in all almost 50 shows have been unearthed - and there are still about 15 shows you may look forward to !

If anybody knows of or has a connection to 'old-time' Wilco traders or even tapers, please ask them to get in touch,
there are still a couple dozen shows out there that are known to have been recorded and traded in the pre-torrent era.
