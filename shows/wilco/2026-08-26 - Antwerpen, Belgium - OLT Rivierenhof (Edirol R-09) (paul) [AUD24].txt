As so often, most everything you want to know about the night can be found on Paul's substack:
https://paulsuwan.substack.com/p/wilco-26-august-2026-antwerp-belgium

Night 2 of the return to the beautiful and fabulous OLT after last year's two outstanding "An Evening with Wilco" shows.
After the near fiasco in Amsterdam the previous week, I didn't want to risk anything and decided to record the first show with the trusty Edirol internals 
from the same great spot I taped last year's second night. And that turned out to be a really fine recording.
So no Experiments for Night 2, moved just one row up and maybe a seat to the right or left. But as you'll hear, the sound is totally different !
As this was the post-Jeff Tweedy Birthday Show, there's a lot of banter about that night and other things, "... I mean in general"
Some nice setlist shake-ups.round up another great two-nighter in that friendly city. Tweedy muttered something about coming back next year...
As always, some photos included.
