Notes: This is a significant upgrade over the previous version I shared back in 2012.  I removed almost all of the screams and woos and was also able to improve the sound.  From what I remember, it was a bit windy, so there is some phasing here and there.  The Hollywood Bowl is one of the worst venues in LA to tape from and this night was no different.  I did not see any open taping rigs and there is no Roadcase recording from this show because the venue wanted to charge the band $10,000. 

Listening back now, this was an excellent show.  It was also the last North American stop on this long tour.
