First Wilco show for almost 4 years, probably more as I wasn't well during the Glasgow show from 2019. A lot has changed in that time, it looks like a whole new road crew and there's a new sound engineer following Stan Doty's retirement and some slight tweaks to various arrangements. One constant among all this is the line-up remains the same as it has been for the last 19 years, never seen a poor Wilco show, ever. Jeff was engaging despite his current hip woes which will see a couple of hip replacements early next year.
