Notes: Was walking in right as the band started so therefore missed the first 2 songs (Normal American Kids, If I Ever Was a Child) and the recording fades in for Cry All Day. As is, sound takes a couple songs to settle. When it does it's a pretty good approximation of what being there was like. I had seats pretty much at the very top of the venue, but the sound is pretty good throughout the place so not too bad. I would say this is an exceptable recording while we wait for the soundboards to go on sale on their website. Not as much variation in the setlists as one would normally expect from Wilco for this short residency. However, they pretty much always bring it so the show is excellent anyways.
