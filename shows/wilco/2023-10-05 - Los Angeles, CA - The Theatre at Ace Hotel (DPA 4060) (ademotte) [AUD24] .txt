Comments:  Second of three nights at the Theater at Ace Hotel in Los Angeles. Beautiful, intimate venue with excellent acoustics. I was basically at the soundboard and the sound was perfect. On a positive note, the crowd was by and large very respectful, so there is very little interference even in the quiet moments -- and there were many, particularly in the first half of the show when the band focused on the more intricate material on their new album, "Cousin." Unfortunately, the audience clapping between songs is very loud in relation to the music -- if there is anyone with the tools and skills to level this out, that would be great. Other than the applause issue, the recording itself is excellent.

In terms of performance, a solid show and what one would expect at the beginning of a tour. The new album features great songwriting but is very low-key; the show doesn't really take off until the second half. "One Wing" and "Either Way" are lesser-played songs and were welcome. The "Many Worlds" coda after Via Chicago featured a great guitar duel between Pat and Nels and was worth the price of admission on its own. 

Hopefully this run of shows will be released as part of the "Roadcase" series, but for now this is a pretty good document of an early "Cousin" show.
