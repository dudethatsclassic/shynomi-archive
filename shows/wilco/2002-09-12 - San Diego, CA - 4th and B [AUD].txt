
Wilco 9-12-02 4th & B, San Diego,CA + 9-10-02 KCRW FM


Source: Sony ECM-MS907 > Sony MiniDisc MZ-R70 > CDR > FreeRip > You

This show was recorded from the balcony at 4th & B in San Diego. There is some chatty crowd noise here and there at the beginning but they settle in after a while and just listen. The Minus 5 opened up the show but I didn't tape them for some reason, although they put on a really good show. Peter Buck and some of the other Minus 5 came out during "California Stars" and helped out. Peter really gets twangin' on the main riff.

I have also included the "Morning becomes ecclectic show" from two days prior on KCRW FM in Santa Monica as filler on the end of disc two.

Recorded and upped by Dylan75
