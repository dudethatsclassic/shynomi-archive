One from the Basshead archives! Great show and great recording. I really loved the band during this period. THey were still a bit edgier, still making those great mistakes of a growing band. A year later they would all rise so much higher as musicians...This album played so well in the Paramount, it was incredible. The Encore cover was a nice treat...overall a remarkable night....Enjoy! -Basshead
