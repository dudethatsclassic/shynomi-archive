WilcoZurich Volkhaus2012-03-07This was the penultimate show of my second European Wilco tour of 2011/12. This 10 day trip included stops in some of europe’s most expensive cities – Brussels, Paris, Zurich & Milan. Thankfully hotel prices were fairly reasonable as it was still out of season. Two days in Zurich didn’t help the budget but the extra time spent there enabled me to explore more than I would normally and meet up with old friends. From what I can remember, it was a good show, approximately 2 hours long despite the advertised early curfew, which would have resulted in a show of under 90 mins. A varied setlist with the rarest song played being Candyfloss followed by Side With The Seeds. The acoustic Spiders and Laminated Cat were now becoming staples having been re-introduced to the rotation a couple of months earlier during the US west coast leg of the tour. Setlist as played1.
