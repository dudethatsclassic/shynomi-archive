NOTES 

Wilco's first ever show in Chicago, the last stop of a week of shows that was their first mini-tour. I'm pretty sure there were no advanced tickets, just a $10 cover charge at the door.

Ah, to be young again...back when driving solo from Chapel Hill to Chicago to see a show seemed normal. AM wouldn't come out for four months but there were demos of 6-7 songs circulating, and I had a cassette of their show from Cicero's in St. Louis the previous week that kept me company on the drive, so I was familiar with most of the material. To be honest I thought the songs were a bit hit and miss, an opinion I still hold about that first album. But after seeing a bunch of shows on the last lame-duck Uncle Tupelo tour earlier in the year it was great to see how relaxed they were and how much fun they were having onstage. And very cool to see Jeff play a few songs solo, and hear multiple Uncle Tupelo songs that I don't think they ever played live. I was especially floored by the ANODYNE outtake "Wherever" that they hadn't played at Cicero's and that I never even considered that they might play, and the solo "Gun" that we've all now heard 100 times but was a revelation at the time. This was a very good night.

There are multiple high quality recordings of this show, I think this one by Jeff S is the best overall as his high-end mic was way up on a stand above the very rowdy crowd.

Enjoy,

mrpember, January 2021
