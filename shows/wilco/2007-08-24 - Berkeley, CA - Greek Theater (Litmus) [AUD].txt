- 00:00 to 00:40 from 08-24-2007

*** 42 "nothin'"s in coda.

- great night in a great place. the greek is a classic venue and wilco clearly enjoys playing there. great energy and an interesting setlist dipping even back to "A.M." i wish nels had been turned up a bit more and more active, but the overall band dynamic was superb.
