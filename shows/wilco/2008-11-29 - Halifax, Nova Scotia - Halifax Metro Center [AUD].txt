Wilco - Blue Halifax Sky
Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada
Halifax Metro center
2008 Nov 29

Opening with Everst for Mr. Neil Young

Lineage - Custom homemade mics -->Battery Box with 160hz roll off filter-->24bit 44k1   Edirol R-09-->Soundforge-->adjust volume-->downsample to 16 bit--->FLAC

Recorded from the floor, infront of the mixing board.

No alterations to the sound was done on this show. I only increased the volume..no EQ..nothing.

In hindsight, I think that the lower levels can be a little higher, but I will leave that up to you.

I was unsure of the track titles, so I did not include them.

As always, feel free to trade and post on other sites, but I ask that the title and info notes
remain with the files.
Do not sell or reconvert to lossy format unless it is for personal use only.

As Neil Young says.."keep on rockin' in the fre world!"

Please feel free to let me know what you think of the recording. I love the feedback.
