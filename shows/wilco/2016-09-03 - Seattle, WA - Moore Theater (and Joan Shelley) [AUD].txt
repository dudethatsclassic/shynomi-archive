Notes:

 ~ Bill Frissell sat in for "True Love Will Find You" and "California Stars".

 ~ There was a medical emergency of some sort in the audience during "Company In My Back".  After the song, the band left the stage for about ten minutes while the patient was being taken from the theatre out to an ambulance and off toward the hospital.  So there's a discontinuity in the recording; but none of the music is missing.

 ~ Be warned: The crowd this night was completely lit.  It meant that the atmosphere was wonderfully electric -- but it also meant that audience-members were talking and carrying on and having a gay old time right during the goddam songs.  I tried getting people seated near me to quiet their dimpled asses down, but, alas, it was a pretty hopeless cause.  Not to say that it will ruin the listening experience...but it will serve to annoy.

 ~ Additionally, I was more impressed with the sound inside the theatre for Friday night's show.  Don't know whether it had to do with my location Saturday (ironically, much closer to the stage, and with an absolutely perfect sightline), or perhaps something to do with the mix, but I thought it sounded less warm and more echoey for this show -- and that difference, to my ears, is reflected in the recording as well.  Again, it's not as though it's an outright travesty or anything; but: FYI.
