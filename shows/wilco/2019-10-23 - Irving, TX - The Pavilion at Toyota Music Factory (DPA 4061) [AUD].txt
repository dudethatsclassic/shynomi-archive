Interesting night.  Never seen a crowd passively/aggressively take over from security and turn a seated show into a semi-standing room show.  The band fed off this and turned in a great show.  29 big ones.  Unfortunately the DPA's left channel decided to go to the great beyond a few songs in, so I made a mono recording of the right channel.  So in the old school world of the SPARS code this is a DAD recording.  However, I think it sounds pretty, pretty good.  If I didn't mention it most wouldn't notice it.  I EQ'd it a bit for my ears.  I know it's an old school transfer, but check out the samples.  Tracks done on the fly so not even close to perfection.  Enjoy
