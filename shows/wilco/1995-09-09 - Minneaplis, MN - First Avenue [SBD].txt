Wilco  09.09.1995  1st Avenue, Minneapolis, MN... "1st gen SBD." from cassette-AWESOME early Wilco...

I got this from a friend who recorded the cassette for me, he had it marked "1st gen". I believe him, 
as he was well known to be a frequent recorder of good shows, and knew many cool people...He also 
kindly gave me the next night as well, the "all-ages" show at the same venue...Thanks, man!!

Personally, I like this era of Wilco a great deal, maybe better than "current" Wilco. This era of the band
was more like a continuation of Uncle Tupelo.   Really Terrific Stuff! ... R.I.P. Jay Bennett...
