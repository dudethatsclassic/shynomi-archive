Show notes: The Spring 2010 US 'An Evening With Wilco' tour was remarkable for its very lengthy sets, and this one is one of the longest of the lot, clocking in at a Springsteen like 39 songs and 3 hours and 2 minutes! This is a tremendous show in front of a very appreciative crowd. Jeff also revealed to the crowd the details about Solid Sound Festival to the crowd, which was the first time it was made public. A simply fantastic show.

Recording notes: This was already a very good recording, but I gave it an azimuth adjustment and an EQ adjustment and I now think it sounds a little crisper and sharper than the original. It's an excellent recording any Wilco fan will enjoy listening to.
