*You will need to write disc in a slight “overburn” to get all 18 tracks onto disc 1.  However, if you leave off track 18, it will be under 80 minutes and nothing will be missed.  For those who burn to longer formats, remove Track 18 disc1, and track 1 disc 2 for a seamless show.

When I purchased my ticket to this show, I chose line of sight over sound.  I knew I was taking a chance after sitting in a similar spot once for a Ryan Adams show at McFarlin, and literally turning my recorder off during the show.  Luckily this show wasn't as bad, but........  It ain't great either.  I did some serious twisting and tweeking of the knobs to make it sound better for my ears.  It does sound better than a raw transfer.  If you don't like amateur mastering jobs, please do not download.  To answer two obvious questions:  Yes there was that much bass rattling our seats, actually more, and yes there was that little Nels in the mix from my seat.  A nice souvenir if you were there, at least until another version shows up.  Beware of a close whistler to taper, and someone asking seat directions in track 1 disc 1. It scared the crap out of me at the show, so I can't imagine what it would do to a listener with headphones.  Tracks done on the fly, so they are not perfect.  Enjoy.
