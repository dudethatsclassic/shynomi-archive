NOTES

[1] with the New Orleans Horns

To seamlessly join the two discs, simply remove D1T15 and D2T01.

Anticipating a longer 2nd encore, I stopped and re-started my recording during the applause between the last song of encore 1 (D2T07) and encore 2 (D2T08) to ensure I didn't run out of file space mid-song of the 2nd encore.  In hindsight, given the short 2nd encore, I didn't need to do so.  Better safe than sorry!  The band clearly wanted to play more, but it seems the Aud Th shut 'em down.  At any rate, guaranteed:  NO music missing, only a few seconds of applause.  I cross-faded the applause for a seamless transition between the two tracks.

Started the recorder a bit late due to sorting out some technical problems.  Patched the beginning of the first song with an alternate source.  Thanks to Neutrino for the patch source:
