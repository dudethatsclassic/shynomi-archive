FIX NOTES : 
This is a fixed version. The original recording had the channels out of phase.
Most likely the way in which the signal was split between the soundman's MD and my DAT
caused one channel to have inverted polarity.

Basically when one speaker was supposed to move in it actually moved out. This caused
the recording to sound not quite right and lack some bass. It sounded pretty good anyway
and it didn't occur to me that there were problems. This fix brings out the low end and
the whole thing sounds much smoother. Please don't distribute the original files anymore.

ORIGINAL NOTES:
The recording levels are quite high, but there's no audible distortion or other flaws.
