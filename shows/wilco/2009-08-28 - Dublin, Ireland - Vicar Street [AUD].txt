Thanks to Wilco (The Band) for allowing audience taping and trading and making this happen.
all photos included in this torrent courtesy of hb007, many thanks for those and everything else !

For the second night, I had tickets for the ground floor after the first night at the balcony and so I decided 
to go up front  for the optics and doing recording just as a by-product. 
I ended up at the front row rail right in front of Nels Cline with no problem (again, what a great and friendly venue ...)
As you may imagine, Nels is featured very prominently in the mix of the recording and sometimes vocals are hidden 
by some spectacular guitar parts. I did nothing to alter the sound, just raised the volume level, and what an impressive performance that was !
