*During the encore several people decided to get up and leave/return to their seats. Due to my need to move from 
so they could pass at the end of the aisle, I had significant trouble with my microphones. There are several dropouts 
and static moments during these last songs. I did not edit out the silence when the signal was lost but did try to 
do some click restoration. My hope is by posting this show warts and all, someone else who attended and taped will 
post a much better copy! If not, I felt it was important to share the tape.
