Show Notes: Opening night of Wilco's Sky Blue Sky festival at the Hard Rock Hotel Rivera Maya in Mexico, and boy what a show it was! The band is on fire, the best I've ever heard them. Many rarely performed songs and some fantastic audience singalongs, it was a real treat to be here to witness this. Too many hightlights to mention, just listen and make sure you come down for the next time they do this!

Recording Notes: Pros- This show was recorded from the balcony of a concert courtyard room so it's completely devoid of audience talking which is great, and it sounds nice and clear. Cons - As it was left on the balcony it was subject to the whims of the wind, and you'll hear it on the recording every now and then as it was quite a breezy night, with the wind coming in straight off the ocean. Still I don't think that's enough of a distraction, you'll enjoy listening to this one! 

At time of writing Wilco had not released this recording as a Roadcase - if they do please purchase it!
