This was the second show of my 3 countries/5 shows/7 days trip with Wilco through continental Europe.
40° Celsius outside during the day, and not much less during showtime, another memorably hot show.
The show took place in the yard of an old castello, build in 1870. I arrived early in the afternoon, having stayed
in the mountins above Genova on my way from the show in Ramonville-Saint-Agne two days before.
Check the photos to see the beautiful venue which almost sold out I guess. Unfortunately I couldn't take any
pictures of the actual show, but you know how the guys look like anyway.

The sound at the show was pretty good, actually better than it may appear from the recording, where there's some 
kind of echo audible, coming from the brick buildings surrounding the yard that I didn't notice during the performance.
The entire audience was really enthusiastic, as you can hear by the chant after Impossible Germany, 
and I'm sure it gave the performance of the band a boost on this hot summer night !
I tried my best to avoid the more 'hydrated' members of the crowd, with mixed success as you´ll hear.

In terms of the setlist, the most interesting part of course was the second ever full-band performance of
Evicted, the lead-off track of their forthcoming (09/29/2023) album 'Cousin'.
I think/hope Bird Without A Tail will become a setlist regular in years to come, and I especially enjoyed
Side With The Seeds and Spiders (Kidsmoke), including full audience chorus, as well.
