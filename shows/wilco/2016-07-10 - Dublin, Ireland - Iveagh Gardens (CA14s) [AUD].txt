An excellent show from Wilco on a wet & Windy night in Dublin's Iveagh Gardens.

As I knew it would be quite windy in the venue I went with my omni mics directly
in front of the left stack.

This one turned out quite well despite the bad conditions - some wind noise during the 
acoustic set and lots of talking around me too.

For the old fashioned amongst us who still burn to disc I'd suggest splitting after Jesus Etc.

Remember to support Wilco - go to their shows, buy their merch & their albums.

Enjoy the show.
