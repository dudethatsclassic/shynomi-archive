Great set, superb singing by Jeff Tweedy.
Nels Cline is a genius :-) He played an array of electric guitars, 
including a double neck and two lap steel guitars.
A stamping, cheering crowd didn't want it to end; the band was on stage
for almost 2 hours.

Sound: the clarity improves during the first 2 songs, otherwise 
good throughout, clear, well balanced.
There's a sound glitch at the end of "One By One" (not mine) and
a couple of slight recording glitches during the set (loose connection).
Not enough to spoil it in any way IMO.
