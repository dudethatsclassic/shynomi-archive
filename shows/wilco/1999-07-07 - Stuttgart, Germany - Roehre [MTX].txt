Wilco - Stuttgart (GER), Roehre, July 7, 1999 aud/sbd matrix

lineage:
aud: OKM II R > Sony TCD D8 > DAT > WAV
sbd: sbd > DAT > CD-R > EAC > WAV

matrix created using Samplitude back in July 1999

I received the sbd shortly after the show in a trade from HP (thank you!) but it was a crappy one (missing parts of some songs, missing instruments in the mix etc.), so I decided to fill in the missing parts using my aud master of the show. I think it turned out quite good although it was my first and only attempt to do a matrix... But decide for yourself if you like it or not.

According to Wilcobase this is the only time they played "Come Together" (Beatles cover).
