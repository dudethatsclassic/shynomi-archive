I went to this show without being very familiar with Wilco.  Another taper, Seth, couldn't make it and offered me his ticket if I'd go and record.  So thanks to him for making this recording possible.

Jenny Lewis and her band opened the show, and played on the last two encore songs with Wilco.  It was a sold out crowd, and a fairly well behaved audience.  Wilco played one song after another with very little time in between tracks.  They were recording themselves too.  The first 10 songs are what makes up their latest studio album.
