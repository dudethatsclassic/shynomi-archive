This was the first of 2 nights Wilco played in Sydney on the weekend. I was on the right side of the theatre about 40'back from the stage. 
As always wilco were amazing but the bass did seem a bit thin on the night. This is reflected in the recording so I have not applied a 
high pass filter as I normally would. There is a great copy of the second show on here already, my version i will post on etree as soon as
I get the chance.
