An incredible show by Wilco in the excellent venue that is the Iveagh Gardens in Dublin. It was raining at times and was a bit gusty as well, but thankfully this is only slightly evident on two of the six acoustic songs.
Overall I am very happy with how the recording turned out. 

And we were treated to wonderful support from Lucinda Williams and her excellent band who played for about an hour.
I also taped Lucinda and I will upload her set over the coming days.

Support Wilco, go to their shows and buy their albums.

Enjoy!

BigJim.
