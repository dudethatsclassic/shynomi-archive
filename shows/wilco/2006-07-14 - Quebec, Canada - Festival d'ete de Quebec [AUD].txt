This might have been Wilco's first visit to this beautiful city and the setting couldn't have been better.  A beautiful summer night in a nice outdoor setting to see Wilco at this amazing annual summer festival!  This was my second show in as many nights as I saw them at Metropolis in Montreal 2 nights before.  It's always interesting to see a band a couple of nights in a row to see what they'll do, play different songs, their interaction with the crowd...etc.  They played a slightly shorter set than MTL but great nonetheless and a few new songs as well from the yet to be released Sky Blue Sky.  The sound outdoor was really good, I was standing about 100 Ft. from the stage dead center.  This is a rare uncirculated show I saw no tapers at that gig (except for me of course)!  This is a patook recording/production enjoy and distribute freely!
