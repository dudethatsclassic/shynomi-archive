I caught Wilco live for the first time, and I've gotta say, I was pleasantly surprised. The night kicked off with a nice and easy country song, but as the gig went on, the rock vibes amped up. Jeff Tweedy, the lead singer, rocked the stage and seemed like a pretty cool guy too. He had some really nice moments interacting with the crowd, which was awesome to see. My highlight of the night was their beautiful performance of "I Am Trying to Break Your Heart."

The recording turned out pretty darn great. I'm stoked about the gear I'm using now—those Soundman mics and the compact Zoom H1n recorder do the job well. I'm thinking about maybe saving up for an Audio Technica microphone next. It'd be sweet to have it ready for when Keane hits the road again next year. But for now, I'm totally satisfied.

Give the recording a listen, and I hope it puts a smile on the faces of fellow Wilco fans around here.

P.S. I'm also recording tonight's show at TivoliVredenburg, so keep an eye on the site!
