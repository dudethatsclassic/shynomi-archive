Wilco making is first visit to Alaska in an attempt to complete the few remaining states they had not yet played (call me when you guys do Hawaii!!). Fairbanks outdoors in the summer is usually the land of the midnight sun, this summer was the coldest wettest summer in 30 years. Luckily we only had some wind and clouds, with only one little shower during Spiders. There is some very low wind noise throughout the show, mostly only audible during the quiet parts between songs. DUring SPiders the rain came and the winds kicked up, so there is a bunch more noise during that song. It clears up during the encore break and doenst bother us again really. NIce long set by the boys, can wait for a new album! THe Blue Loon was a fun place for a show (despite the novice level of managing a show and security, but hey, they dont get shows up there often!) ENjoy!- Basshead
