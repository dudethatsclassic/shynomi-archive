notes to the 2018 edition:
I've found out that there are many Wilco shows up to ~2005 that have probably never been torrented (at least not since ~2005) 
but have only been traded, presumably in the last years of traditional trading. Somehow, these shows were forgotten in the first 
waves of torrenting - or deemed not worthy of digital distribution ... or simply those traders in possesion never made the step to
torrenting and then stopped trading altogether, like most everybody did.
I've searched high and low, checked countless old lists and sent a lot of asking mails to ancient email addresses. About half of them
bounced, another quarter didn't reply - and the ones that did hadn't traded (or stopped collecting) in a long time. But then I
found two friendly persons, one in Seattle, the other on the East Coast who were willing to share shows with me. Arrangements 
were made and now I am able to (re-)introduce about two dozen Wilco and Jeff Tweedy shows to you.
I have not yet listened to all, most need some work. I guess many of the original CDs are flawed by now, but apart of some
occasional odd digi scratches, everything can be rescued or restored, it'll take some time, please be patient.


Finally, I've found the last five tracks of this show !
WilcoBase lists t26 as Just Wanted To Thank You, but of course it's I Can't Keep From Talkin'.
This segment surely come from the same recording, but (for whatever reason) the five tracks are in Mono !
As the first 11 songs are missing from the new version, I have just added the new tracks to the 2009 edition instead of 
preparing a complete new mono version. The new segment was pitched and slowed down by ~0.3 semitones with Soundforge.
I also found an ugly flaw in t12 that I was able to repair. Thanks Robert.
