Comments: 

Pretty good show from Wilco's brief summer tour.  The vibe felt more like a street festival or county fair than an actual concert performance; it took place in a minor-league baseball stadium which, due to the low ($45) ticket price, was populated with a lot of young families and casual concert-goers who were coming for the "event" rather than the concert itself.  I haven't seen such a broad range of ages and demographics in a long time -- many young kids running around, and there was even an area to the side with inflatable play equipment for the kindergarten set.  It was a nice summer evening for all, but challenging for the fans who actually came to hear the band.

Performance-wise, I thought it was a good show, but the complex, delicate arrangements of the newer songs in the first half of the show got lost in a stadium setting with an inattentive, chatty crowd.  The second half caught fire with a great mix of new and old, with the odd Neil Young and Big Star covers thrown in.  Not the best show I've seen from them, but it was worth the trip from Chicago.

I was in as good a location as a stealth taper could be -- on the field in line with the speaker stack, and I slowly edged closer to the stage (from 30 feet down to about 20) as the crowd pushed in.  Sound is very good, but the quieter songs and banter fall victim to the crowd chatter that becomes distracting.  This recording is certainly listenable, but can't compare with the pro rigs that are usually present at the board at Wilco gigs (and are therefore generally somewhat separated from the audience).

I waited a month before posting because I'd been hoping a different source would pop up; given that I did not see any tapers on the field that night and no other sources have surfaced, I figured I'd share mine.  I'd be interested in feedback from those who choose to download it -- I may be too critical of this because I get frustrated by audience interference on my masters.  The vast majority of the recording doesn't have much (or any) audience noise, and the sound is surprisingly good considering the rig I was using.
