Comments: 

Third of four nights on Wilco's "Winterlude" residency at Chicago Theater. Robyn Hitchcock opened the first two nights and Sharon Van Etten opened the last two nights; she came out for several songs to contribute backing vocals.  I skipped the second night, but compared to the opening night the performance was much more spirited.  Seems like the band rotates in about 1/3 of different material every night, but even using the repeat songs as a baseline, they were more "into it" than the first night.  Overall a great show, and the song substitutions helped to build to the encore crescendo.

In terms of sound quality, I think this is comparable to my post from night #1.  I was much closer (9th row, vs. 30th row) but didn't have the benefit of the repeater speakers which gave clarity to the high end.  I was fortunate to have a courteous crowd around me, and there is very little interference.  I used an EQ to inject a little more life to the recording (I was close enough that although I got the benefit of Glenn's unmiked drums, I also missed most of the sound from the overhead PA); the sound was phenomenal and the stereo effects do find their way into the recording.

Highly recommended.  If they do release this commercially, I will pull this from the tracker, but for now, this is a good document.
