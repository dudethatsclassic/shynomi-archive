Notes:
This is a new completely digital transfer (November 2007). The original transfer had an analog (MD D>A > soundcard A>D) generation.
-I used a second MD recorder (Sony MZ-R37) to record for a few seconds while I switched discs in the MT180 after 80 minutes. I edited the pieces together in Sonar which is how the recording of the show is seamless from beginning to end.
-No fades in between discs so that the show can be joined seamlessly on longer media.
