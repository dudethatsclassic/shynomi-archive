notes to the 01-2017 edition:

trade CDs > EAC > Wav > Sound Forge Pro 10.0 > CD Wave 1.98 > TLH > FLAC

I have ripped this show from CDs that I have received a little more than ten years ago in a trade, 
There were two loud clicks (most likely from a bad CD copy further up in the lineage) which were easy to eliminate. 
Additionally, I re-tracked the show and spliced the two CDs seamlessly. 
Please note that the lengths of the CDs given above were incorrect, the whole show runs 134:14

I'm A Wheel is cut at the end, not much missing. The following Kingpin (just that one track) was MUCH louder, mostly clipping,
than the rest of the recording, which was on a fairly low level, at least on the circulated copy. 
I lowered the volume for Kingpin, then normalized the whole show. Looks a bit funny in a wave editor but it works ...

All due thanks to the taper. Support the artists, go to their shows. wilcoworld.net
