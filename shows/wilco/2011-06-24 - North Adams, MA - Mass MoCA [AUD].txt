Wilco
Mass MoCA
North Adams, MA
June 24, 2011

Editing was done with Audacity just a touch of the low end was removed to make the recording less boomy.  It was pouring rain for this show, my gear was on my stand with my umbrella attached, in the quite parts you will hear the rain drops, they sound like static from an analog recording, it reminds me of the good old days.  Wilco was using a projector at the sound board for this show, I was working with the crew to make sure the umbrella wasn't interfering with the projections, for the beginning of the show the mics were still a little low and close to the audience, so there is a little chatter.  This was my three sons first Wilco show, they were a little enthusiastic at the very beginning of the show, they had a shout out for their mother!

Original taper - John Bosco
Editing and packaging - John Bosco
