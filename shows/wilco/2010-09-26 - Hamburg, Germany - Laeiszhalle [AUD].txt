The Laeiszhalle is a great venue, seating 2000, over 100 years old, a fine example of neo-baroque with a lush interior, cream and gold plated stucco, a big organ integrated in the stage’s back.
But if it’s the best venue for a rock show is at least doubtful. The plush seats are great, no problem to sit for 2 or 3 hours (if that is what you’ve come for). Most people who had come for Wilco definitely wanted to sit through it, I’ve rarely seen such a gentrified audience, you hardly saw a couple of heads bopping, everybody stayed seated until the last song.
Is it the venue, that draws such a crowd to a Wilco show or is it the band itself and the music?
 
I hadn’t seen Wilco for 5 years, but the music is still great – not a great many new songs on the setlist (only 4 of the recent one, 5 of YHF / AGIB and SBS each) - which  I didn’t mind as I like the old ones better and got plenty of my favorites. 

Hardly any banter, but a short comment regarding the St. Pauli match they had seen the evening before.
 
Hardly anybody talked during the music, good recording conditions, but unfortunately I messed up something fiddling in the dark with the tiny knobs: I set the recording mode to Hi-LP instead of PCM, so the recording is lossy – shame on me. Apart from that it sounds fine (and I'm too old to hear much difference anyway).
