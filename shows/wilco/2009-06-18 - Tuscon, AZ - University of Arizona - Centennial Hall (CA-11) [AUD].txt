Wilco: "It's all your fault Tucson"

Centennial Hall - UofA Tucson, Arizona
June 18, 2009

Recorded by u22utoo. IMHO, this is a nice recording.  I pulled it from just stage right of the Soundboard. There are a few, very short, sound issues caused by me moving the mic connection at various times during the show.  I had to do a lot of up and down (from seated to standing).  These are all minor issues.  By some miracle, I had no loud clappers, coughers, sneezers, talkers or anything of that nature close to me.  Just good, clean Wilco music!  If you are a Wilco fan, you won't be disappointed with this one.  I promise.
