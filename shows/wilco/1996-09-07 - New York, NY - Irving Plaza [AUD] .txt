This is the infamous show in which Wilco "opens" for Johnny Cash. Johnny decided to go on first,so Wilco had
the pressure of having to follow a legend. This was also the first time many of the songs from 
"Being There" were preformed live and some of them, like Casino Queen and Passenger Side sound really 
different from the album versions...this show gets a big write-up when the Greg Kot Wilco book
"Learning How To Die." I don't know the taper source of this show but it certainly sounds like an 
audience recording. I will try to find and post the artwork later..enjoy!
