Thanks:
To Wilco (The Band) for allowing audience taping and trading and making this happen.
To Ecki for the gear, to Christian for the tickets and to Samuel for the photos used for the covers.

We were late !!! Although fellow Dimers c and fupduck gave their all on the highway, Brussels once
again was a nightmare. We had our way mapped out nicely but we got lost nonetheless ! When I 
could finally enter the venue after some (polite) debate at the gates, I thought the show had just begun as 
(The song) was just being played and I pushed the record button while looking for a way into the hall.
Only when the setlist was posted later that night I realized my error. Frustration.
As the show was sold out, I could only set up at the back of the hall, behind the soundboard at the rear wall
under an overhanging ceiling. Took me two songs to get everything set up, all the while 'tape' ran with the 
Edirol laying on the floor. Despite all this hassle, what actually is there sounds pretty good, sound musta
been great that night, I only wish I could have been 3 meters more to the front ... and have less talkers around.
I was trying to apply some EQ but in the end stuck with the original raw files, no harm was done.
