Show Notes: My first trip outside of Australia since the pandemic and what else would I do but go and see a Wilco show at the iconic Greek Theatre in Berkeley? I last visited this wonderful venue back in 2008 seeing R.E.M. (with The National and Modest Mouse), and it's still as impressive today as it was back then. A sold out crowd for the biggest show of this leg of the tour, promoting their latest release Cousin, which I think sounds great live, particularly the title track with really rocks. Rather than me recapping the whole show, it's already been brilliantly done by uber fan Paul at https://viachicago.org/topic/55630-wilco-%E2%80%94-14-october-2023-berkeley-ca-greek-theatre/ so go read his terrific review. Totally worth the long flight. Can't wait for Wilco to head back to Australia for the first time since 2013 in March 2024!

Recording Notes: I was in the first row of the concrete tiered seating which meant no one to block my view, or talk in front of me, and this recording came out really great as a result. Nicely mastered by Chris, this is an excellent recording. Enjoy!
