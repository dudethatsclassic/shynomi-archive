Sound : The GRCH is a large, echoey venue, so this is less immediate sounding
than my recording of their Barrowlands set last year.
It was a lovely set, and the recording has decent sound quality, although I wish Jeff's vocals had been a bit higher in the mix.
Note : at the start of Rising Red Lung, a fight broke out in the balcony.
Jeff dealt with it very well, and re-started the song.
