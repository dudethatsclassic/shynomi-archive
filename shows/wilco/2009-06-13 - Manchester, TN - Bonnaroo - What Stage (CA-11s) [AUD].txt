Thoughts
------------------------------------

another amazing wilco performance. years ago when i was watching uncle tupelo at slim's (1993), i never would have predicted that jeff tweedy would have been the more interesting and adventurous songwriter/bandleader coming out of the ashes of UT. but with this incarnation of wilco, he seems to have found the perfect mix of collaborators who don't try to compete with his vision (though jay bennett brought a lot to the table, RIP, he always seemed to be jostling with tweedy for the position of first banana) but rather add their own unique contributions that make the whole thing come together. i'm a huge nels cline fan, and his work during this set was typically stellar.

favorite tweedy quote of the day: "I hate the sun." classic.
