This was an amazing show put on by Wilco to the fans who had brought premium tickets for the State theatre show. The Factory Theatre is a small club only fitting 500 patrons. 
That atmosphere was electric from the start and Wilco fully lived up to expectations! The setlist was varied and dipped into places seldom touched in their back catalog,
all in all an amazing night for the fans put on by arguably the best live band in the world right now.
