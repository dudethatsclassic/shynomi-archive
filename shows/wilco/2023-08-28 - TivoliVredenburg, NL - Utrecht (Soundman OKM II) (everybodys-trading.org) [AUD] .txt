Taper: Everybodys-Trading.org
Location: upper level of the stairs, first balcony on the side
Lineage: Soundman OKM II > A3 Adaptor > Sony MZ-R70 (mono) > Zoom H1n (16 bit 44.1 kHz)
Editing: normalize and labeling (Audacity)
Cover art: YES

Wilco's second night at TivoliVredenburg was a blast with an excited crowd. They mixed up the setlist from the previous night, which already made the trip to Utrecht totally worth it. Plus, I snagged a better spot this time!

Now, about the recording—I decided to try something different. I got my hands on this old Sony minidisc recorder and thought, why not? The tricky part was I couldn't adjust the audio levels while recording, so I just went with my gut. The result? The recording sounds a bit boomy, to be honest. It's still pretty good to listen to, but I can't say I'm totally satisfied. My recording from the 27th is way better, actually. But hey, maybe I'm just being hard on myself. What do you think?

P.S. As a backup, I also turned on my Edirol during the concert. Anyone interested in this can email or message me. The recording is fine.
