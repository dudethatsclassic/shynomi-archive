Wilco were only band I wanted to see at Forbidden Fruit and they didn't disappoint.

They headlined the Monday night of the festival and were only alloted a set of
1 hour & 15 minutes and it was a fine set they delivered.

No messing around - just straight up energy from the start.

This recording turn out pretty good - usual amount of chatter you'd expect
from a Dublin crowd but nothing too intrusive.

Enjoy the show.
