------------------------------------
Thoughts
------------------------------------

How many times have I seen Wilco over the years? A lot. They never cease to delight, amaze, and deliver. This stripped-down, more acoustic version of the Wilco stage show was just as intriguing and captivating as the more traditional 'electric' show. The "Schmilco" songs sounded great alongside the rest of their stupendous catalogue, which is saying a lot considering I'd only been able to spin the whole record once before this show. All in all, a great show and a nice waltz through the history of Wilco, song-wise.

Taping at the Fillmore is a rarity, so it was nice to be able to set up with a stand (thanks to the band), even it was in the very back corner. Sounds pretty good considering I wasn't able to get right next to the board like I prefer to do.
