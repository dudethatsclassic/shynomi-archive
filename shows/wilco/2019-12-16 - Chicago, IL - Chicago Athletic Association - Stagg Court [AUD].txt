Comments:  

In the midst of their four-night Chicago "Winterlude" residency, Wilco performed a free lunchtime concert for roughly 150 "Songkick Live" concert winners at Chicago's Chicago Athletic Association.  The band focused on material from their current album "Ode to Joy" after opening with their "Summerteeth" Chicago love letter "Via Chicago."  After roughly a half hour performing, Jeff took questions from the audience, moderated by local comedian T.J. Jagodowski.

A review of the concert can be found here:  https://www.forbes.com/sites/jimryan1/2019/12/17/wilco-hit-pause-during-chicago-winterlude-for-special-songkick-live-pop-up-concert-in-front-of-150-lucky-fans/?fbclid=IwAR2Vlko-dbgOE-SIe3FSmzLLh_9M-gbt1jOgaYJwLvF__rZR_BjXMnpyjjo#2bc7e30a7d59

Your enjoyment of this recording will depend on how much you like their new album.  I initially didn't care for it, but after seeing the material live a couple times, I do like the more (relatively) upbeat songs like "Everyone Hides" and "Hold Me Anyway."

Nice recording with minimal audience interference.  I retracked the material from the CDR that was mailed to me and boosted the high end a little, but otherwise left the recording alone.
