notes~
I had never recorded from so close to the stage.  I was 
in the 3rd wave of people directly in front of Tweedy.  
Obviously not a great spot to record, but I wanted to be down in the action 
that night and saw a pair of mics behind me covering the action from the balcony.
This recording is what you might expect from a pair of Neumanns in the 
pit of the 9:30 club.  I was in front of the PA so the vocals are a little weak. 
I got a lot of guitar from the stage and, of course, a very excited crowd around me.
The only problem spot was during 'Kingpin' - I was running my levels 'hot' when JT 
had the crowd scream several times... cooked.  Enjoy.
