Notes:
* - full band acoustic

Royal Thunder opened (not recorded). This is NOT a flawless recording, but it is the only known source and I believe is still listenable. The venue did not allow recording, so I had to go 007 to pull a tape. There was an issue with my right mic capsule that went completely unnoticed until after the show and resulted in an audible buzz in the right channel for 3/4 of the show. As a result, this is a mono recording consisting of only the left channel doubled to cover both channels. Also, despite using windscreens there is some intermittent wind noise, a couple of trains pass by on the tracks that were about 100 yards behind the lawn and the crowd gets a little loud with their clapping/cheering a few times. Second encore was performed unamplified with the entire band on acoustic instruments played into mics arranged at the front of the stage. I did not boost the recording levels for the second encore, so the levels are lower during that portion. Many thanks to Wilco for allowing the recording of this show.
