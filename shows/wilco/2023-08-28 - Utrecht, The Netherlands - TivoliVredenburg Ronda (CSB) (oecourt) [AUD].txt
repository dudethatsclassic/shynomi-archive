This was the fifth and last show of my 3 countries/5 shows/7 days trip with Wilco through continental Europe.
After the great Sunday show, Monday was spent hanging around town, checking out the Plato record store,
as usually when in Utrecht (all other record stores seem to be closed on Mondays), and having a couple of Utrecht's own
'Oma Lodewijk' beers ("Een vol en rijk blond bier die door zijn verfrissende droge afdronk heerlijk doordrinkbaar is!").

I've seen the Tweedy Band before at the Grote Zaal, but the smaller Ronda was new to me and as both 
Utrecht shows were sold out, I again decided to find a spot up on the balcony instead in the packed crowd.
On the second night, I managed to get a DFC spot and sure enough, the recording turned out excellent again

Everything else there is to say about the show has been posted by bböp on his (as always !) great
re-cap on the trusty viachicago.org forum, go ahead and read it, even if you'd have to register.

In terms of the setlist, Evicted, the lead-off track of their forthcoming (09/29/2023) album 'Cousin',
was again back in the set, along with the most welcome return of Hell Is Chrome, rarely played these days.
Side With The Seeds was outstanding, as usual. I think/hope Bird Without A Tail will become a setlist regular 
in years to come, and on this second night, Courtney Marie Andrews got to sing on California Stars and You and I.
The two Utrecht shows were advertised as 'no repeats' shows on the Wilco and Vredenburg homepages, 
alas that did not happen. But 12 new songs compared to the previous night's setlist ain't that bad either...

Hello and thanks to Bou, Wee, Katja & Thomas, Samuel and bböp.
TS, sorry to have missed you ... didn't know you were there.
