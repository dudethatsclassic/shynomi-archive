A hot August night and the continuing threat of Covid Delta wouldn't keep the Dallas fans away from one of their favorite bands! The Band was in good form and sounded great. I had a decent crowd around me and very few distractions on this night. Of course they played Shake Your Money Maker in it's entirety to start things off and ripped into some blistering jams and had everybody on their feet dancing, myself included. Dos Equis Pavilion is notorious for boomy recordings. So I ran this through Ozone 7's house warmth plug-in and it remedied the boominess nicely. Crank it up!!
