Notes:

This was the 2nd of two nites at The Forum. Great venue with good PA sound. Easter Good Friday so it was great to hear the band play their Good Friday song. Recorded on floor in GA, 5m from the stage, to the right (aimed at the right speaker stack). The band is old-skool in that they use wedges for foldback, not in-ear. Daym i love this band. Still sounding as amazing as the 90s. They rokked. As it was the 2nd nite at the same venue, they played some new songs for the first time making this a special performance. Well mixed by the sound guy who admitted to me afterwards that it was only his 4th time engineering for the band! Vocals are nice and forward.

Some good banter between songs and stories which is nice. As far as audience recordings go this one is clean and clear, no distortion, with hypercardioid mics keeping room reverb to a minimum. 

Enjoy!
