It's been 25 years since I last saw the Crowes, and I really enjoyed that Sven Pipien was still (or should I say, again) in the band. I love his sublime bass playing. Both the second guitar and the drums were very good as well. Like everyone, I always liked Steve Gorman, but to say that the Crowes are no good without him is just wrong to me. Cully Symington is a great drummer and adds a really straight forward drumming style to the band's sound while still getting a really earthy sound. Awesome snare technique, too.
