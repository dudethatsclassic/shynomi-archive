The Black Crowes 2021-09-17 Wantagh, NY AUD master


Lineage: Zoom iq5 -> iPhone 6s -> Goldwave (track splitting, level boost 6%) -> TLH (FLAC level 8) -> Dime

I kind of lost interest in TBC for the last 20 years but this was really great.
They kept it rocking even for the rest of the set after they played the first album entirely and they looked like they are enjoying themselves.
Isaiah Mitchell's guitar playing was outstanding.
There are 4 5-6 seconds cuts throught the show where the mic got loose and briefly disconected.
I was pretty far out and on the side but the sound was very good and the recording came out pretty decent for an outdoor event considering where I was.
Sample will be provided in the comemnts.
