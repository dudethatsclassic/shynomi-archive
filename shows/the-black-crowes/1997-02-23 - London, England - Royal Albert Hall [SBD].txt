* I landed at Gatwick on 13 February and met up with Mike & Becky, Mike Rathbun and the rest of our crew in Amsterdam two days later. The six of us amoricanized our way through the Netherlands, Germany, Belgium, France and The United Kingdom in what started out as seeing one acoustic performance flowered into eight shows as we finished our ten day romp through Europe at the RAH. What a beautiful venue! I had a seat at the end of the row and danced the night away. Whats up with the Brits and dancing in that place? Very reserved... Maybe it was just me and quite a few pints, but no sitting for a Rock N Roll show! A Great Ale selection and not nary a bad seat in the house makes boa one smiley amorican. Cheers!
