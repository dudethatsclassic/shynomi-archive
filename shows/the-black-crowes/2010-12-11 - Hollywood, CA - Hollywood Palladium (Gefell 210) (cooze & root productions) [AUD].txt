Thanks to: The Black Crowes for their generosity towards tapers. The SE for 
his accommodations. A quick shout and acknowledgement to our friends: 
Fillmore_Crowe & his lady friend, datpat and tedyun (who helped hold down
the section).

NOTE**First time ever at this venue and this was a lot of work. No one knew about
taping and once that was resolved we were hassled inside and told we would have to
sign some kind of waiver/contract that never materialized. Man, this place is a
drag. Once the show kicked off everything was back to normal. Fantastic vibe from
the band. I don't think I've ever seen Chris so animated and just having a good time.
On another note, and being from Colorado I don't see this ever...When we were exiting
the venue they had street vendors selling bacon wrapped hot dogs! I'd seen them on
the Food Channel but never thought I'd get to sample this tasty little treat.
Excellent way to end the night. Enjoy!
