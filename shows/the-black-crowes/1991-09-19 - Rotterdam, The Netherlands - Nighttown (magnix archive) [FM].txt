Another 'tape' from the Magnix Live Archive.

Previously, on November 5, 2010 a transfer of a 2nd generation tape of this broadcast was shared on Dime (it was on the tracker for 868 days and was snatched 274 times during that time).

Also, the exact same tracks have been rebroadcast on October 19, 2007 (they were/are allowed to broadcast 'old' material only) on the Dutch KRO "Legendarisch Live" FM radioshow. I have recorded that broadcast as well, straight from FM cable to WAV. However, the first track of that recording, "Twice As Hard" had a short (16-17 seconds) cut halfway the song, because I accidently switched of the radio while taping to my harddisk (how stupid one can be sometimes). I thought I could use the 1991 tape to add the missing piece, but the difference in sound between the two sources was quite noticable (both are excellent, but there's some sort of difference in the 'colour' of the recording, if you know what I mean). Since most of these "Legendarisch Live" broadcasts are available on demand as well, I have used the 96kbps WMA-file to add the missing piece. Also note that the 2007 broadcast has quite some extra inbetween song banter and crowd noise ...

All that said, presented here is a tramsfer of a 1st generation tape from the original FM broadcast from 1991 in excellent quality!

Disclaimer.
Please note that this is a real FM capture, no cable, but straight from the airwaves. Also, this comes from a tape that was 16 years old at the time of transferring (October 2007). This means that some FM and/or tape anomalies or degeneration may be present. Most has been fixed to the best of my abilities and most of this stuff is of very good quality.
