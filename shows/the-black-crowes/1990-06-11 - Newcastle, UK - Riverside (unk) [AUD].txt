The Black Crowes
1990.06.11
Newcastle Riverside, UK

Tape bought from sketchy South Shields record shop left unplayed for 35 years > Audacity > xAct 

This is one of a bunch of tapes that I bought from a sketchy record shop in South Shields, so I have no knowledge of its lineage prior to me buying it. The shop was run by a couple of guys that looked like they wanted to be in the Quireboys, and they seemed to stock more bootlegs than genuine releases. It was down near Tyne Dock. That's all I have on it, I'm afraid.

But ... whoever recorded it did a great job. For an audience recording in the sweaty heaven that was Newcastle Riverside, it's a bit of a gem. It's not currently on the tracker, so I don't know if this has been shared here before, maybe someone can enlighten me. Anyway, a headline show, in support of their first album, in a club that felt very busy if there were 500 people in it. Whoever sold it to me made a couple of cuts to fit it neatly on the tape, so I've patched it up a little, without losing anything important. The gap before then encore was clearly much longer before they mucked around with it, but that's about it ...
