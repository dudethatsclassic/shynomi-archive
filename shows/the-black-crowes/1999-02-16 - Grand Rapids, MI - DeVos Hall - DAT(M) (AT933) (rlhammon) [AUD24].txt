Notes:

Like many tapers during COVID I started backing up my master DATs into digital format. I ran out of time tracking and properly sharing shows, but I'm slowly correcting that.

I saw that the anniversary of this show was coming up and after checking out crowesbase.com I saw that there was only one recording showing. I figured it's way past time that I got mine out the fans as well.

This is a raw dump of my master DAT and tracked out. No fades, no EQ, no normalization. This recording will benefit from a little work mainly due to the levels falling off after a few songs. There is a single "pop" early on in Remedy, it's in the master DAT it is not a transfer error.
