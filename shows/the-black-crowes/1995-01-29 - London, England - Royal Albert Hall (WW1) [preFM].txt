From 2 seperate Master Pre FM Westwood One 
radio station shows I was able to compile
an almost complete concert !! Only missing My Morning Song
- Could I've Been so Blind & Mellow Down Easy.

I have the Master WW1 radio station disc from the first 
airing on May 08 1995 as well as the re-broadcast on May 05 1997.
 
I used Cooledit to remove the commercials and to seam 
the songs together in the correct order.

Running time is approx. 85 minutes so it must go on 2 CD's.
Not sure where the best break would be. Maybe D2 starting with
Jealous Again ??
