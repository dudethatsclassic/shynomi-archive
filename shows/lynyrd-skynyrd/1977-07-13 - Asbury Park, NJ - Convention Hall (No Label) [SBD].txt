This is the last known recording of the original Lynyrd Skynyrd band.
The band would play only eight more shows before going down in the 
plane crash on October 20, 1977. This show has long been though to 
have been a soundboard recording, though the possibility exists that
it is an audience recording. Nevertheless, the sound is quite good.
There are two short gaps of silence in That Smell, and the first few
seconds of Sweet Home Alabama are missing. The performance is amazing,
enjoy.
