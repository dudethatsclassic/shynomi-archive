The date of this is uncertain; the artwork and several sources say it's November 6, while other
sources say November 4.  The site below has a detailed tour schedule and says it's the 4th, so
that's the date I went with.

http://www.chromeoxide.com/lynyrd.htm

Update: The correct date is indeed November 4, 1975:

from Jeff, who was at this show:

"Just to confirm the date of this show is Tuesday 4th November 1975 at the Capitol Theatre, 
Cardiff support act Sutherland Brothers & Quiver ticket prices were £1.65, £1.35 & £1.00 ah those
were the days :-) I was there and have the poster hanging on my wall.

Jeff (Wales/UK)"
