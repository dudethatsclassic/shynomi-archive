03. T For Texas (Rhythm)
04. Simple Man
05. Sweet Home Alabama (Solos)
06. Workin' For MCA (Solo)
07. Searching

These tracks were recorded in Leon Wilkeson's living room in June of
1976.Steve Gaines had just joined the band.Here we have Allen Collins
teaching Steve Gaines some of his guitar parts.  Gary Rossington joins
in on Searching, and Cassie Gaines can be heard singing faintly during
Simple Man. This only lasts about 13 minutes.

Bonus Track
08 Cottonmouth Country

Cottonmouth Country is an unfinished outtake from the Nuthin' Fancy
sessions and was never released.
