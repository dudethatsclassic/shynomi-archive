Dan Lampinski recorded over 100 concerts in the Providence/Boston area, mostly between 1974 and 1978.

He recorded many of the major 70's bands: Yes, Genesis, Pink Floyd, Queen, Blue Oyster Cult, Frank Zappa, Jethro Tull, ELP, Kiss, Black Sabbath, The Who, Al Stewart, Alice Cooper, Jeff Beck, Bruce Springsteen, Supertramp, Jean-Luc Ponty, Moody Blues, Neil Young, The Faces, Rush, Rick Wakeman, Kansas, as well as several "under the radar" acts.

Dan was very meticulous about taking good care of his tapes and was very pleased that these recordings circulated among the trading community. Please honor his kindness and generosity by sharing these recordings freely.

Always remember - the more generous you are with your music, the more it comes back to you.
