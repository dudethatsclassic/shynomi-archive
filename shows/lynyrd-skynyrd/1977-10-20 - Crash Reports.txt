October 20th, 1977 reports regarding the Lynyrd Skynyrd plane crash.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynyrd_Skynyrd_plane_crash

01 Terri Hemmert - 93.1 WXRT Chicago
02 Bob Brown - ABC News

Terri Hemmert started at WXRT in 1973.
She still hosts “Breakfast With The Beatles” on Sunday mornings.

Bob Brown started at ABC in 1977, and worked a long time on their 20/20 program.

This content was added as bonus to a cassette I got in a late 90s trade.
The main show on the tape is Skynyrd 07/13/77 Asbury Park, NJ (unknown gen).
I got rid of my cassettes, except for this one.
I would lose it for years at a time, as it got stashed in drawers or boxes.
