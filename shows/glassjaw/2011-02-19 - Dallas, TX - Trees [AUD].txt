Notes:
The opening bands were 'Tidal Arms' and 'These People', both played solid sets and are worth checking out.  I picked up both opener bands cds and the full lenth 12" Tidal Arms vinyl after the show.  The Glassjaw EP 'Coloring Book' was also given out to every attendee on the way out of the venue.  Overall the show was very exciting.  Although I would've preferred a bit more crowd interaction, Glassjaw put on an awesome and energetic show.  The audience was right up to the stage which was low enough for the occasional stage diver to get up and jump into the crowd.   

The in-crowd audio sounded like it had too much treble and was turned up about 30% louder than it needed to be.  Everyone I spoke to after the show was having trouble hearing and as of this writing my ears are still ringing a bit.  The only audio I removed from this torrent was roughly 6 minutes of silence and crowd noise durring the break before the encore as nothing of interest happened durring that time.  Enjoy the show.
