Notes: 
Great show. First GJ show since 12/22/2006 in Detroit (which I also recorded). This show is available in mp3 and FLAC formats for people who want quality and for people who just want the recording and don't care. Great recording. Better than my 06 recording. Taped this right in front of the right stack. Definitely helps out washing out the crowd on the recording. I get bumped a few times during the first few songs and my mic gets bumped pretty bad at the beginning of "Mu Empire." The whole encore was pretty amazing hearing the new EP from start to finish. That great picture that is used for the album art is by good friend Mike White. Please visit his site deadlydesigns.com he has some great shots from other bands too! Enjoy!

Ryan
