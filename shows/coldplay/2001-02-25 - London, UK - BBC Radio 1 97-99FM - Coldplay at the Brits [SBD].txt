Comments (BBC Radio 1):
The hot topic of gossip backstage was the rumour that Craig David wouldn't be receiving any 
awards this year, despite being nominated in six categories. Coldplay are nominated in five, and 
they're certainly hoping they'll pick up a few. They followed Westlife in the rehearsals, with an 
intimate rendition of 'Trouble', the song they'll be performing on the show. It certainly cuts a 
stark contrast to Westlife's performance! Singer Chris' voice was sounding a bit croaky in 
rehearsal - fingers crossed it'll hold out on the night. Rajesh spoke to Chris and Will from the 
band about how many awards they think they're going to win (five!), who they'll be hanging out 
with at the show (At The Drive-In), and what they think about those Craig David rumours.
