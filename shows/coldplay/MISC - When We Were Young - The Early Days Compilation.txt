I discovered Coldplay in the early stages of their career, through the Brothers & Sisters EP. One of the first songs I heard
was Bigger Stronger, which is still my favourite. I think they were great at the beginning, then after Parachutes they lost
their true spirit to become a successful pop band. The intimate feeling of Spies or Bigger Stronger was something which is
missing today. So I pay tribute to this great band when they were a great band.
