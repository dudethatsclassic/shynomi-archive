Recording notes: Typical recording issues in a large crowd.  Lots of crowd noise, beer cans being crushed, etc etc.  I also changed positions when the band went to...the c stage?  Whatever the stage near the back of stadium is called.  Also, near the end of the show I positioned myself near the exit due to the horror stories of folks spending hours trying to get back home.

Show notes: I had spent the past few years avoiding spoilers of the latest Coldplay tour in hopes that I would one day catch a show.  It paid off and the show I saw in Toronto was one of the greatest shows I have seen in my life.  Enjoy.
