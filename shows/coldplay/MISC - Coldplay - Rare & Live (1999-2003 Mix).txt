These were just some personal favorite acoustic or live performances of already released song and some others that were not released at the time. 
I searched through quite a few shows to come across some of these versions. I will warn you guys though that there are a couple tracks that are as 
good as they are going to get, quality wise because those were the only sources I could come across, but they are still good and worth a listen. 
Enjoy guys.
