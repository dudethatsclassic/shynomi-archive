Another Coldplay from the early days. This was actually towards the end of their tour supporting Parachutes album where they
visited Australia for the first time. Quite an achievement for a band that had only one album released 1 and half year earlier
and completely unknown to large audiences. The band was already into new material and during that tour they premiered In My Place
which was the single of the second album. Top notch quality here from radio broadcast (Triple J maybe?). Enjoy!
