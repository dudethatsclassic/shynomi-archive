=Coldplay=

Venue
=====
Big Day Out Festival, Bassendean Oval, Bassendean, Perth, Western Australia, Australia

Source - Audience Recording
===========================
Sony ECM-717 Minidisc(Mic=Sharp MS-721) > Philips CDR 770 (Via Sony JE520 Standalone MD) >
EAC > CDWave > FLAC

Tapers Notes
============
Although I usually try and clean up recordings, I decided not to EQ or master this show at all. 
I didn't really want to mess with it. Feel free to do that at home in your own time...  ;)

The slight drop outs early on in the piece are an unfortunate feature of my old workhorse -  
the Sharp MS-721. When changing from Recording volume 10 - 11 (Low-High) you get a dropout. 
Nice feature Sharp. Good work.  

This BDO show came as Coldplay were just starting to make an impact in Australia. 
The afternoon crowd were buzzing and curious. Fortunately though, they weren't going completely nuts
so I was able to get relatively close to the front with a nice spot for taping. 
To get this close for bands later in the day would usually involve getting a boot in the head 
while some 15 year old crowd surfed on a bodyboard above you... The crowd were mainly waiting for 
"Yellow", but besides some minor chatter, seemed to enjoy the set. The references by Chris Martin 
to coming back to Australia and having "fireworks and big explosions" was a cheeky jibe towards 
Rammstein, who were headlining that years BDO. 
I saw them later that night, and "wow, do they love fire!" It was incredible.

Enjoy

- Junior Franger
