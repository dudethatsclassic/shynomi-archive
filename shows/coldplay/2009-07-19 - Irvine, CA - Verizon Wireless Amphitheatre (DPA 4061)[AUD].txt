Last of Coldplay's three LA-area shows.  Chris shows lots of love for Los Angeles, at one point calling it the band's "home away from home."  I had a wonderful taping location--dead center, several rows behind the soundboard, and almost directly in line with the speakers due to the steep pitch of the seats.  Probably the best recording of the eleven I've done so far this tour.


Chris Martin: Lead vocals, piano, guitar 
Jonny Buckland: Guitar, backing vocals 
Guy Berryman: Bass, backing vocals 
Will Champion: Drums, backing vocals
