01. Interview                13:23



Long wait for Coldplay CD 

Coldplay played the first night of their UK tour in Aberdeen last night (Monday) and tried a few new tracks out on the audience, including one called 'Murder'. 
Chris couldn't speak to us because he's still experiencing problems with his vocal chords and has been told he can only sing not speak - so the rest of the lads did the talking and had a bit of bad news for fans - there'll be no album for at least a year: 

"I think we're probably going to start in September, I think realistically the album won't be out until next summer, maybe a single some time before, but when?"
