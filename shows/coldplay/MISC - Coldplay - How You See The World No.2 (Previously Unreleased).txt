Song Review

Coldplay joined the recording of the "Help: A Day In The Life" charity album at the eleventh hour. The band donate the song 'How You See The World' in an effort to record and release an album in the fastest possible time for the War Child organization. War Child executive Mark Waddington said: "It went really close to the wire but it's great to have them on board."
How You See The World had been previously available only in Japan, but the band remixed it to create a new version with different lyrics for the LP. A spokesperson for the charity added: "Chris Martin re-wrote the lyrics for the project and recorded the vocals at a studio in New York on Thursday 8th September 2005. The vocals were then sent digitally to 'Help' producer Mark Stent who remixed the track on the last night."
