Notes: Time to break into my "archives". I'll start with this one. A friend who works in radio 
had an extra ticket to this show, and dragged me along as a birthday gift. He didn't know where  
the tix were until we picked them up at will call, and lo and behold, we were 2nd row, dead center. 
Obviously, a GREAT seat. Unfortunately, I wasn't bringing my camera to shows then, or I would've 
gotten some GREAT shots. Anyway, being 2nd row, I might've been just a little too close for recording
purposes, but it turned out ok. It also turned out that the show happened on Chris Martin's 
birthday, and there were signs everywhere wishing him a happy one. You can also hear people
yelling "Happy Birthday" at him during the show.
