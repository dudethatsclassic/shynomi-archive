Notes:

I believe I got this off the Coldplay hub when it was still around.  It was a great place to get Coldplay shows, and where I got most of my collection.  This show did not come with a text file, md5 or ffp, so I made all of those.  Trader's Little Helper says this is lossless, so good news there.  Bad news is it is very distorted, lots of bass, some crowd and once in awhile you can't really make out what they are singing, and only know because you remember the lyrics.

This recording though unremarkable, was remarkable I think because they played Everything's Not Lost, Low and did a duet with Richard Ashcroft.

This show is definitely only for completists, but would be interesting to see it remastered to perhaps bring the vocals out a bit.

- Corum
