Notes:
  
* = Performed on stage B, located on the main floor. (Shorter, techo versions)
+ = Performed on stage C, located between sections 227 & C127.

You will hear a few level adjustments during the 2nd stage performances.
They were a tad bit lower, so I boosted the volume by 6% to even out the feel of the tape.

Tracks d2t6 & d2t12 are not the band playing live. These are CD cuts playing over
the PA. One being the band moving back to the main stage, the second being the closing
music as the house lights came back up & people began to leave. I decided to stay & record.

All in all, I lucked out w/ my seating location, as I had a speaker stack pointed directly
at me. I'm also extremely glad I decided to bring my gear. I almost did not. I am usually 
against making arena recordings with my omnis, as they sound very distant & muddy, but I am pretty 
happy w/ this one. I hope you enjoy the recording as much as I did taping it! :)

This is a truly wonderful band.
