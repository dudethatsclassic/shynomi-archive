There was a few sound drops and burst on 01 - Life In Technicolor,
that i have tried my best to adjust. I guess these were where Jon (taper)
was adjusting his recording levels or finding a good recording position. 
From Violet Hill onwards there is no interference on this recording 
But i did have to remove a few seconds from the Scientist's Intro
as at that point all volume was completely lost  for a short time. 
Overall this is a very good Aud recording.

Sony Soundforge 8.0 was used for all fade-in & fade-outs 
and for the adjustments to soundlevels on Life In Technicolor.
It was also used to break down this recording into single tracks.
