Interview with Guy Berryman of Coldplay
25 June 2003
al Foro Italico

01. Guy Berryman Interview Part 1
02. Guy Berryman Interview Part 2



The Guy Berryman interview was conducted by Paolo Gallori two days after Coldplay's gig at Centrale del Tennis. Berryman discusses the 2003 tour, the Iraqi war, Radiohead's Hail to the Thief, and above all, an unofficial release date for the 3rd Coldplay album, due in 2005.
