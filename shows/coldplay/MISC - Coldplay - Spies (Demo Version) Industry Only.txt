Spies (Demo Version) 1999 Industury Only CD
18 track industry only promo CD for Parlophone Records. 

Features 18 tracks by different artists, 
one of which is a rare and early unreleased version of Spies 
with a more electronic feel. 

This version is frequently known as the "Nikolai Levey" version. 

However, to my knowledge it is not officially known as this. 
This CD was issued prior to the Blue Room EP
