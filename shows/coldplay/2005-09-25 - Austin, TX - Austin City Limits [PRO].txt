Uploader Notes
==============
Coldplay played Austin City Limits for the first time in September 2005. The show was heavily edited, with the setlist order and broadcast order differing in several places, and a few songs left out of the broadcast.

They played a Christmas song as a second encore with the intention of it being left in (as the show was broadcast just 8 days before Christmas, but recorded in September) but in the end it was left out of the broadcast.
