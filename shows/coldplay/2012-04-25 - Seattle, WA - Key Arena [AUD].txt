Neither the taper (nor I) are particular fans of Coldplay, but his son really enjoys them, 
so as part of an Xmas present package, he took him to see them.   From what I saw of the
photos and heard of this, it sounded like a really good show, and his son had fun.

He was pretty happy with the quality of this recording, although he noted that at the
start of Yellow, you could hear the girls behind him start to scream.  As for me, this
*almost* turned me into a fan...but I would have loved to hear SR's Schoeps turned onto
Diiv, who were playing the same night.

But for those of you who like Chris Martin and mates, I hope you enjoy this.  It's not
always about pleasing yourself...but by serving others, you also serve yourself from
