Coldplay
Rarities 2013
5-Disc Set


Description
-----------

After an exhaustive re-collection of bootlegs I came to the conclusion that
a series of Rarities must be done. All the tracks were extracted from shows between 1999 and 2012, so I hope you will enjoy these sets made specially for you. 

On the other hand, I included a proper artwork (is not that fancy, but at least I tried my best).
