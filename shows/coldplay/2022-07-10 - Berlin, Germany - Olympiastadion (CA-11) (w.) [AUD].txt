This is the complete recording of the 1st date Coldplay had in Berlin this year. Many unexpected things happend during the concert.
"A Sky Full Of Stars" was interrupted by an ambulance operation and "Something Just Like This" was played twice. The 2nd time it was with an ukrainian children choir.
In the beginning the sound was not really at it's place but during the first couple of songs the engineer(s) managed to fix it.
Our seats were far away, nearly in opposition from stage, so this recording should be rated as average mostly. Just check out the sound sample in the comments, please.
