Coldplay

Smirnoff Amphitheater- Dallas, Texas
 9/23/05 


taper seat: 10 feet right of SBD




Crisp clear recording with a average amount of audience chatter and clapping. I also must 
note a Woo Hoo-er and off time clapper behind the taper rears his ugly head periodically.


This was the show during hurricane Rita weekend which canceled the bands Houston gig.
There 
was a "cool" vibe at the venue that night in anticipation of what track the storm might 
tale. The news all day was about the cluster-fuck traffic with evacuees getting out of the 
Houston area. The band made mention the the storm and Houston and said they would be playing
a longer set than normal because of the cancelation. If that was true I'm not sure, this is the only show from the tour i have heard.

My hope is that anyone that may have missed the 
Houston gig or maybe even missed the Dallas gig because of being caught in the traffic, will 
find a copy of the recording at some point in their lives.  

enjoy!

"time was just a joke"
