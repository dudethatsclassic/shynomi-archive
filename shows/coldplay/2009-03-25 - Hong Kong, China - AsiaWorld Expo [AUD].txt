Coldplay recorded live at AsiaWorld Expo in Hong Kong on 25th March 2009.

Attending the show in the company of only women, our seat selection was geared towards optimizing the view of Chris Martin rather than sound quality, so this is far from my best work (for those familiar with my recordings, it’s not as good as Coldplay from Earl’s Court in 2005, but better than Foo Fighters from the same venue on following week).

I gambled that they would not play for more than 94 minutes – the capacity of my MD in PCM mode – but they did, and so I missed the encore.

Recorded from stage right, level with the stage, using Sony MZ-NH1 (PCM mode) + Sony ECM-719 --> WAV -->, EQ --> Individual tracks (Goldwave) --> FLAC (TLH - Level 8).
