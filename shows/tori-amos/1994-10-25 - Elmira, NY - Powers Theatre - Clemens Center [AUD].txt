notes: I have never been a fan of Tori Amos, but in 1994, my brother was. Figuring there may be interest in her shows, I asked him to take my recorder to the show he was going to and he agreed. He had never taped a show before or since. In all those years since then, I do not believe I ever once traded this out. This is the first processing of the show. One of the odd things, to me, is the number of different songs she performed when compared to others in the same week. After tracking this show I can say with confidence, I still have no interest in Tori, but hope that others may enjoy the efforts of my brother all those years ago.  

If you have masters that you would like to share, but lack the tools, time, or know-how, please contact SMORES on DIME and I will be happy to discuss them with you. thanks.
