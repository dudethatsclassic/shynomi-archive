Tori played a beautiful show!! She was in a fantastic mood, happy and shining and told us that almost everything on a setlist was a request that night. Brilliant performance! 
Taping wise: I can’t praise enough sound quality at the Beacon. If I could make every artist I want to hear play that specific venue - I would!!! Tori of course has many at times very enthusiastic ;) fans… So they do lose their shit now and then. Overall - the crowd was loud mostly in-between songs - except at the beginning of the Lizard Lounge covers :) and during Cornflake Girl/encore when everyone rushed forward, so you get some whooos and screams… NO TALKING anywhere though. As always - counting on BLG to get these guys’ emotions under control! ;)
