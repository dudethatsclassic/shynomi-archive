Source: cassette, from the collection of Wally Breese

Transfer by Lisa071573: 100-min Maxell Type II cassette (gen 1?) > 
Marantz tape deck/graphic stereo equalizer > line in > 
CoolEdit Pro (amplification, vocal presence boost) > CD Wave Editor (wav splitter) > wav > FLAC Frontend > FLAC (level 8)

Comments: Tori's vocal starts out very low, but somewhat levels off during Happy Phantom. There are a few other parts that are somewhat 
quiet and I tried my best to boost Tori's vocal and/or amplify these but there's quite a range of highs and lows.
I think this is at least a first-generation cassette, copied from two 90-min tapes (based on the notes on the 100-min tape).
