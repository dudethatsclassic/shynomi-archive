Notes: The range on this - like most of the 1992 Tori boots - is all over the place. Some of the stuff is very quiet and other songs are louder, almost clipping. I amplified Me and a Gun because it was very quiet, but didn't amplify any of the rest of it so as not to clip the recording during the loud parts.
