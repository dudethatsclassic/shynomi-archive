Tori Amos
August 28, 1998
Kansas City, MO
at the Starlight Theater

Source: Cassette from the collection of Richard Handal, cassette from Danica
Transfer: Transfer: JVC KD-W5 > Realistic 31-2020 10-band stereo equalizer (tape hiss lowered by lowering 8KHz) > Marantz Stereo Receiver SR940 
> Line Out/In > Tascam DR-07 > SD Card > Card Slot > Copy wav to HD > CoolEdit Pro (append tracks, amplification, hard limiting)
> CDWave (wav splitter) > Wavs > Trader's Little Helper > FLACs

Notes: There were a few sound dropouts that sounded like the taper hitting the pause button or starting/stopping taping. Two of these are in the middle of 
encore applause and so nothing important is lost, but one is at the beginning of Pandora. 

Comments: This isn't the best bootleg but seems to be the only known recording of this show. The second half sounds a bit clearer than the first half.
