Tori Amos
April 10, 1994
Manchester, England
at Free Trade Hall

Cassette from the collection of Wally Breese, generation unknown

Transfer: Marantz Stereo Cassette Deck SD156, Dolby NR on > Marantz Stereo Equalizer EQ140 (tape hiss lowered slightly by lowering 8KHz) 
> Marantz Stereo Receiver SR940 > Line Out/In > CoolEdit (amplification, append tracks, vocal presence boost, light hiss removal) > 
CDWave (wav splitter) > Wavs > FLAC Frontend > FLACs

Notes: I did a little extra amplification on the open talking part (to try to decipher what she's saying) so there's a jump in volume after the initial applause.I left the remainder of the show alone so some of the other talking parts are harder to hear and she had a cold for this show so she's a bit muffled anyway. (I'm never sure if it's better to tinker with the quiet parts or leave it as is...)
