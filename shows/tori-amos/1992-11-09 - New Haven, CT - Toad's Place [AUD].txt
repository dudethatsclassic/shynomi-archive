Taped and Seeded by woj(@smoe.org)

Toad's had the chairs out for this show, one of the few times I've
been there when they did. This made taping a lot easier.

As usual, the Toad's crowd was rowdy so there is a lot of audience
interaction. Fortunately, it's more entertaining than irritating.

The background hum is Toad's most excellent ventilation system. I
tried removing it but doing so made Tori's vocals distractingly 
burbly, so I opted to just only remove the tape hiss instead. The
hum is not bad -- you'll only hear it at high volumes and through
headphones.

The tape I used to record the show was not tightly wound so there 
are a couple spots where the sound "burps" when it unwound quickly. 
These whirrings are noted below.

Finally, you get to hear me yell for "Sweet Dreams" at the beginning
of the first encore. Hey, even a taper has to have a little fun too!

Despite all the above caveats, this is still a good recording!
