
Jimi Hendrix - Drone Blues
Catalog #: Great Dane Records GDR CD SAT 2

Digital clone of silver > EAC > FLAC

This CD features members of the band Traffic as well as B.B. King. 
Some good performances, although other than Traffic and B.B. King on one song each, I'm not sure of the band lineup, 
although it's probably Mitch on drums, and Billy on bass.



TRACKLIST w-LINEUP & VENUE (Courtesy Of Catfood):

1. Drone Blues  8:36
Record Plant, New York, NY, April 24, 1969
Guitar: Jimi Hendrix
Bass: Billy Cox
Drums: Rocky Isaac
Percussion: Al Marks

2. Lord I Sing The Blues For Me And You a.k.a Cryin' Blue Rain  4:15
Hit Factory, New York, NY, September 6, 1969

3.Traffic Jam  19:36
Electric Lady Studios, New York, NY, June 15, 1970
Jimi welcomed friends Steve Winwood and Chris Wood from Traffic to the studio.
With Mitch Mitchell unavailable, Eddie Kramer volunteered assistant engineer
[and former Amboy Dukes drummer] Dave Palmer to sit in on drums for an
impromptu jam session.

4. Once I Had A Woman  5:18
Record Plant, New York, NY, January 23, 1970
Guitar: Jimi Hendrix
Bass: Billy Cox
Drums: Buddy Miles

5. B.B. King Jam  19:48
Live at Generation Club, New York, NY, April 9, 1968
Plug Your Ears website thinks this might've been recorded on April 15, however
since Jimi did a set at Generation Club on April 7, April 9 seems more likely.
Jimi with B.B. King on guitar and vocals, Elvin Bishop on guitar and vocals,
Paul Butterfield on harmonica, Al Kooper on organ, Buzzy Feiten on bass,
Don Martin on guitar, Phillip Wilson on drums and Stewart (surname unknown)
on piano

6. Country Blues  6:34
Record Plant, New York, NY, January 23, 1970
Guitar: Jimi Hendrix
Bass: Billy Cox
Drums: Buddy Miles

7. Everything's Gonna Be All Right  7:00
Live at Cafe Au Go Go, Greenwich Village, New York, NY, March 17, 1968
Jimi with Paul Butterfield on harmonica, Elvin Bishop on guitar,
Harvey Brooks on bass, Herbie Rich on organ, Buddy Miles on drums,
Phillip Wilson on drums and James Tatum on sax

8. Further On Up The Road  1:38
Electric Lady Studios, New York, NY, June 24, 1970
