Well, I thought maybe I could archive (back up) some of my Jimi recordings, but like everything it just turns into a larger than expected project.
Jimi was my first love of music, & I started collecting the tapes in the mid-late '70's, so I have many versions, but many versions have been upgraded and improved upon.
This first project I wanted to document the November 28, 1968 Philharmonic Hall recordings.
I know the folks that bring us the great ATM series of bootlegs did a great job of putting together a 3 disc version of sources from the November 28, 1968 show, but there is also a lot of merging of all the sources, and even some fake stereo effect mixes, which I hope is not part of these offers.

Contrast Clause #1: Previously a version of the November 28, 1968 portion/show was offered at: http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=429142
That version is considered to be the "3rd Source" and comes from a Liberated LP (album) Bootleg "Live at the Philharmonic", Sagittarius (label) released in the U.S., 1969.
This version is also from the considered "3rd Source" from the Liberated Bootleg "Philharmonic Plus" CD.

Contrast Clause #2: Previously a version of the November 28, 1968 portion/show was offered at: http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=465676
That version is from the "1st Source", where this version is from the considered "3rd Source" from the Liberated Bootleg "Philharmonic Plus" CD, an Alternate/Different Source (Different Taper, & Different Taping Equipment).

Contrast Clause #3: Previously a version of the November 28, 1968 portion/show was offered at: http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=465677
That version is from an Alternate/Different Source (Different Taper, & Different Taping Equipment), which is a long version but different from the long considered "1st Source". Again, the supposed "2nd & 3rd Sources" are quite a bit shorter.

Contrast Clause #4: Previously a version of the July 10, 1969 portion was offered at: http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=429142
That version comes from a Liberated LP (album) Bootleg "Live at the Philharmonic", Sagittarius (label) released in the U.S., 1969.
This version comes from the Liberated Bootleg "Philharmonic Plus" CD.
