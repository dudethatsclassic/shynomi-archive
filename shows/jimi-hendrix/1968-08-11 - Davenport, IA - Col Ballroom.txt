2 - Show description & comments follow:

In putting this together I noted with sadness that the entire band has left us.
What a loss.... Here's an incomplete show from Iowa, of all places. This was made
into a rare vinyl bootleg, and is likely missing "Purple Haze" and "Wild Thing", which
have never surfaced. While this is no sonic masterpiece, for 1968 in Iowa, it is 
still very listenable. I suspect none of the instruments were mic'd, and as usual
at many Jimi shows, the P.A. was inadequate. Still, most of the Hendrix folks I 
knew thought this was the best quality version they'd heard. Anybody got one 
better and complete? Enjoy!
