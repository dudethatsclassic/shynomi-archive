2 - Show description & comments follow:

In putting this together I noted with sadness that the entire band has left us.
What a loss.... This recording always reminds me of what could have been, if the
taper hadn't under recorded it. Under recorded at a Jimi concert?! That sure sounds 
strange, but you'll understand what I mean once you hear it. I think if somebody
had the time and expertise to work with this, it could be made to sound much 
better. Everybody I knew in the days of my Hendrix trading said this was by far
the best quality version of this show they'd heard, which makes sense since I
lived in the Baltimore suburbs when I got it. If you've got one that sounds better,
then post it! Enjoy!
