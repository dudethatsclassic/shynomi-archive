Digitally restored:

Phase offset corrected, variable pitch correction performed, some varying volume adjustments to the left channel performed, and finally a handful of clicks (due to tape damage) and some momentary single-channel dropouts (also from tape aging/damage) fixed. 

Modifications:

There were a handful of bad places on the original tape. These problems have been sorted out here, and the problem areas have been patched with excerpts from the 2nd Source (aka. Far source).

There's one patch each in Foxy Lady, Lover Man (actually after the song during the applause), and Hear My Train. 

Machine Gun had 3 problem areas, of which one has been patched. The other 2 were cuts where respectively 2:00 and 1:03 of music was missing. The track is included here in 2 versions. One has these cuts smoothed out, but not patched, while the other (composite version) has the cuts patched, and thereby restored to its original length.
