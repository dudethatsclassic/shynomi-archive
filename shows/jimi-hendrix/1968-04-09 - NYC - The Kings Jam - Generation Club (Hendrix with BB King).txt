Jimi Hendrix & B.B. King
The Kings' Jam
April 9, 1968
Generation Club, NYC


from http://www.geocities.com/SunsetStrip/Underground/9099/116.html :


B.B. King and Jimi Hendrix. Can you imagine two greater guitarists playing together? I know I 
sure can't. Unfortunately they do not play together for this entire CD, just the last 3 tracks. 
And Jimi only appears on 4 in total. A very bluesy CD, as if it would be anything else considering 
who plays on it, and they are backed up by the Paul Butterfield Blues band. Apparently the entire
jam session is not presented here on this single CD, as it was over 90 minutes in length. But 
what is here is pretty good stuff. An okay Like A Rolling Stone (I personally haven't heard a 
better version than the Monterey one). Then what follows is some blues jamming, which does not 
include participation by Jimi. But it's good music none the less. Jimi comes back in on track 6 
until the end. If you are a fan of the blues(and if you like Jimi you must be!), you'll like this 
CD. 

Quality is quite good considering it was recorded in a club without the proper equipment. A 
decent CD, my only problem with it is there's not enough B.B. & Jimi playing together. But 
defintely worth picking up and adding to your collection. 


1. Like A Rolling Stone 
2. Blues Jam #1 (Part 1) 
3. Blues Jam #1 (Part 2) 
4. Band Introduction by B.B. King 
5. Blues Jam #2 (Part 1) 
6. Blues Jam #2 (Part 2) 
7. Blues Jam #3 
8. It's My Own Fault 

Note : Jimi only appears on tracks 1, 6, 7, & 8
