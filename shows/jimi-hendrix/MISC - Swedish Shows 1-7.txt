notes:
- Compilation of Swedish Concert recordings and interviews 1967-69.
- All concerts are from the lowest generation masters available; All
recordings are straight transfers.
- This set features all available Stockholm recordings 1967-69 (incl.
recordings made for radio and TV).
- Available Swedish recordings from this period not included in this set
are Stora Salen, Akademiske Foreningen, Lund 10.09.67 [1st Show]
<http://home.online.no/%7Ehpjohnse/hendrix.html#lund_1> and Lorensberg
Circus, Gothenburg 08.01.69 [2nd Show], 1st Source
<http://home.online.no/%7Ehpjohnse/hendrix.html#690108_2nd> and 2nd
Source <http://home.online.no/%7Ehpjohnse/hendrix.html#kb_5>.
- Later Swedish recordings available are Stora Scenen, Gröna Lund,
Stockholm 31.08.70, 1st Source
<http://home.online.no/%7Ehpjohnse/hendrix.html#1st_complete>, 2nd
Source (Loud Guitar Source)
<http://home.online.no/%7Ehpjohnse/hendrix.html#sorry> and 3rd Source
(Short Source) <http://home.online.no/%7Ehpjohnse/hendrix.html#s70_3rd>,
plus Stora Scenen, Liseberg, Gothenburg 01.09.70
<http://home.online.no/%7Ehpjohnse/hendrix.html#162>.
- Some of the interviews featured on this set are upgrades of the
versions available on "Sweeping Up The Pieces Of Yesterday's Life - The
Jimi Hendrix Interviews"
<http://home.online.no/%7Ehpjohnse/hendrix.html#Sweeping>.

* The Swedish Shows Vol.5 (Jon's Attic
<http://www.kadu.demon.co.uk/index.html> / Sharing The Groove
<http://sharingthegroove.org> 008 / 2004 / 1CDR)
(Radiohuset, Stockholm 08.01.68 (Interview by Leif Andersson for
Swedish Radio "Pop 68 Special") / Konserthuset, Stockholm 08.01.68
[1st Show ] / Radiohuset, Stockholm 09.01.69 (Interview by Lennart
Wretlind for Swedish Radio "Pop 69 Special"); 63:03) Konserthuset
08.01.68: Aud; 2nd Gen [Master Cassette > Reel > Reel > DAT > CDR]
/ Interviews: Very Low Gen
[Quote from the Release notes: Pease note that the original
cassette recording for this show was transferred by the taper to
reel to reel. There was then a subsequent transfer so the all
copies originating from it should be considered 2nd generation.
There are no master copies. Note also that the show [Konserthuset
08.01.68] is marred by lots of microphone clicks early on. The
afflicted tracks are "EXP" (4), "Up From The Skies" (5) and
"Spanish Castle Magic" (7). Most of the noise was generated by
recorder equipment / mic malfunctions and also the taper adjusting
the gear. Some of the sharp rapid pops resemble digital faults but
they are not. They probably could be safely removed without
affecting surrounding material.]
