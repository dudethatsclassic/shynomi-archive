Notes:

The first track here is Jimi's last ever interview which took place at The Cumberland Hotel, Great Cumberland Place, Marble Arch, London on 11 September 1970. It's quite interesting to hear Jimi speak about how concerts in the future would look and also hear his thoughts about entertainment in the home.

The rest of the disc is made up of a performance by Eric Burdon and War at Ronnie Scott's Club in London with Jimi sitting in on the last two numbers. This show took place on 16 September 1970 and was apparently recorded by Jerry Goldstein who filmed Jimi's 1969 RAH performance. However the fidelity of this recording is far from professional and is probably an item for completists only.
