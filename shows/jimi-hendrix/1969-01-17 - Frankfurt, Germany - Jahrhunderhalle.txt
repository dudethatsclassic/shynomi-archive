Jimi Hendrix Experience
Sporthalle, Koln, Germany. 13th January 1969 (master)
Jahrhunderhalle, Frankfurt, Germany. 17th January 1969 (master)
Two Experience concerts from the European tour of January 1969. 
Both in their own ways rather good audience recordings considering their age _but_ 
they are audience recordings. I've included a cover to house both shows - 
(does anyone actually use the covers?)

CD1:
Sporthalle, Koln, Germany. 13th January 1969

1. intro
2. come on (part 1)
3. foxy lady
4. red house
5. voodoo chile (slight return)
6. fire
7. spanish castle magic
8. hey joe
9. sunshine of your love
10. purple haze

Note: This is the second source for this show. It turned up within the past
couple of years and up until fairly recently had only limited circulation amongst
a few collectors. It is taken directly from the master. This is exactly how the
recording came to me on CDR.

There has been another source for this show circulating in poorer quality for a
number of years now and you will often find it wrongly listed as being from the
Jan 19th show at the Liederhalle, Stuttgart. That source lasts only 28 minutes
and is missing Voodoo Chile.
