Jimi Hendrix - guitar, vocals
Billy Cox - bass
Mitch Mitchell - drums



The taper, John, came over from the USA with a friend specifically to go to the Isle of Wight Festival. This was the last set that he taped at the festival. I suppose he was always fighting a lossing battle with how much tape he could carry but this is the most he taped of any of the bands.  Mostly he only got 2 or 3 songs by each.

In later years when he passed away he left all of his masters to a friend and fellow taper Karl Ryan.  Tim met Karl in 1990 and Tim's cassettes were dubbed from John's masters.


Notes:

- There isn't anything to patch this with so the following bits are missing

"Machine Gun"  - 24 seconds, I've faded the two parts together
"Lover Man" - First 3 seconds
"Red House" - 10 seconds at the start and 3:24 in the middle, again I faded the two parts togethr
"Midnight Lightning" about 2.5 seconds at the start but this doesn't fade out like the official release.  You get about 7 extra seconds
"Foxy Lady" - last 1:11 missing

- Pitch corrected by reference to the official release.
- Tape was paused between tracks they have been smoothed with crossfades.
- Some background noise has been removed.
- Vocals were low in places so they have been boosted.
- Some eq has been applied to bring up the bottom end.



Thanks to Karl for passing this on to Tim and Tim for letting me borrow his tapes. And big hand to John for taping.
