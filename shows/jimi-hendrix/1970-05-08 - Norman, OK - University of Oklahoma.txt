Notes:
This was one of the first seeds I did over on STG (can't recall if I did it here). Anyway this is a reseed or new one (depending on how you look at it) as this show has been requested by a few people. This has to be one of the best sounding audience recordings from Jimi's 1970 American Tour but sadly it probably will never see the light of day as a Dagger release although in my humble opinion it should be strong candidate. A cracker of a concert from start to finish, it has spellbinding versions of "Red House" and "Hear My Train A Comin'". The version of "Machine Gun" here is an absolute killer. The more familiar version on the Band Of Gypsys album seems like a rehearsal for this one. Artwork included: 8 page booklet with the story of the recording, eyewitness accounts and contemporary reviews as well as widely uncirculated photos.
