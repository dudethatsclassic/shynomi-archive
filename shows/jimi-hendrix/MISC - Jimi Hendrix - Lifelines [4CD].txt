Jimi Hendrix 
Lifelines: The Jimi Hendrix Story (1990)

Unreleased tracks, outtakes, alternative takes and a 1969 L.A. Forum concert highlight Jimi Hendrix’s boxed set, “Lifelines: The Jimi Hendrix Story.” The first three CDs contain a 1988 rockumentary, which features many treats. But the show’s narration quickly becomes annoying.

Fortunately, the final disc’s concert presents the guitar pioneer in fine form. A worthwhile investment for fans.
