Jimi Hendrix - Hell Fire Red
Collectors Disc (2002)

Jimi Hendrix : Live at Winterland 10 october 1968 1st & 2nd Show

The Hendrix afficionados amongst you probably are well aware allready of the issue of the 
Experience Hendrix (EH) /Sony 4 CD Box Set "The Jimi Hendrix Experience - Winterland" 
from december 2011. It has an amazing remix by Eddy Kramer of most of the tracks played on these
6 concerts. But  EH made a kind of mish mash of the original set lists,some tracks were left out
(Like A Rolling Stone from the 1st show) others were spliced together (This Is America,which is a kind 
of embryonic Star Spangled Banner) was a composite of the two versions from the 1st & 2nd second show.
And they also edited the rather funny song introductions by Jimi.

Well here you have a carefull reconstruction of the first two concerts of this 3 days vennue at  the "Winterland"
San Francisco CA on 10/11/12 october 1968.
I used the Eddy Kramer mixes for most of the tracks,and these are all EAC ripped from the original Box Set.
and are left unaltered.

The Exceptions are :

First Concert
1.Intro 1st Concert (which is from an excellent sounding collector's Tape (Bob Terry)
6.Like A Rolling Stone (same collector's tape)
7.This Is America {Star Spangled Banner} (which is a reconstruction of the original track
             using the first part of the Box Set & the second half of the collector's tape)
  
Second Concert
7. This is America {Star Spangled Banner} (also a reconstruction using the first part
           of the collectors Tape & the second part of the collector's tape)

The music from the Tape has been slightly EQued to match the fantastic remix of the Kramer tracks.
furthermore have Jimi's song introductions been carefully reconstructed and pushed up a bit on volume
if neccesary.

These reconstructions have been quite a meticilous job,and will probably only be recognized by the
die hard Hendrix fans,but if you're just a Jimi fan, I would still advise you to check this one out.
Jimi was on fire during these concerts, and they never sounded better !!!!!!!!!!!!!

They are all Flac8 and art work is included.
So Enjoy & turn up your Amp. (Jimi cared for your ears that's why he didn't play so loud LOL)
