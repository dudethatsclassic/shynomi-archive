Are You Experienced Acetates/Demos/Remixes

Lineage:
cassette>ads-tech>WAV/TLH>FLAC(7)>audacity(tracks)>WAV>TLH>FLAC

These recordings come from another old cassette I got from Mikesline. Mikes converted the recording to a single flac file and I used audacity to separate them into tracks and to reduce spaces between tracks. No other tool was used.
The title and the artwork are by Mikesline.
