Here's the followup to my "Are You Experienced?" Deluxe Edition. It includes all 3 mixes of the "Axis: Bold As Love" album, plus all of the available rarities from the sessions which happen to fill up one disc. Everything has been remastered. EQ and volume were adjusted for conistancy. Channels were aligned on the bootleg material. Pops and clicks were removed by hand from the vinyl material, which was properly collapsed into mono to elimiate stereo crosstalk and noise. Noise reduction was not used on any material. For the original stereo mix of the LP, I used the original Non-NR'd Reprise CD. It is the purest representation of the original master tape that we have. For the mono mix, I used the excellent Classic reissue LP. The "alternate" mix comes from the West German Polydor CD. The mono 45 mix of "Midnight Lamp" comes from the Classic Singles Box, and the flipside "STP with LSD" comes from the original French 45, which has much better sonics than any other version. The remaining material was culled from various sources.
