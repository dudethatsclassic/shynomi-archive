This is a tape that used drive me nuts. I received it in the early '90s. In the years before many studio tapes had turned up, which later expanded in complete sessions like the three Mannish Boys tapes of over a half hour of recordings each. Around the time I got this tape unfortunately the well turned dry. The tape seems to be an appetizer for some-one to spend a lot of money for the complete tapes. Most of the tracks are deliberately distorted with echo. However, to my ears there is one highlight: the complete take of Easy Blues. The 2 minutes + version of Beginning is much too short :-(
I don't know much about the linage but it seems to be a real low generation.
