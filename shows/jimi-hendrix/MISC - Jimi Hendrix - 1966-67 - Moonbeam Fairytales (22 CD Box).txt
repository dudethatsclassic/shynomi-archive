The Jimi Hendrix Experience

"Moonbeams & Fairytales"
(Rock of Ages ROABOX102, St. Vincent & the Grenadines - West Indies, 2008, 22CDs)

- Bootleg copy of the of the collector's set:
"Moonbeams & Fairytales:  Jimi Saga (2)" (Rev.1, 2005)

- Lineage:
Original CDs -> EAC -> WAV -> APE

- Artwork included 

Notes:
- I "borrowed" the info file from the original
collector's set. Changed are just track-timings which 
are often considerably different. I couldn't establish 
exactly why is that so, because I don't have the 
original set. However, some timings like for "Little 
Wing's original stereo, mono, and backtrack versions
are listed as being longer than 3 minutes which is an
obvious mistake in the original collector's info text.

- Disc 12 contains only 8 tracks (instead of 9 as listed 
on the original cover). Short interview listed as 
" 06. Burning of the Midnight Lamp (interview)" is in 
fact just a short final segment of the interview that can 
be found complete on disc 16 as track "01.Interview 
with Jimi, Noel & Mitch by Lief Anderson".

- Tracks 09-15 from disc 10 and and complete disc 14
were taken from "The Spartan Studio Upgrades" set. 
Since that set circulates with many errors (read digital 
clicks, pops, SBEs and small gaps of silence) all these 
are present on this set as well. I've repaired majority 
of errors on disc 14 but left the disc 10 as it is.
