OK, so this is not quite finished. Then again, with the new sources available, I could be mixing and merging forever...
Basically the best sources are 1,2 and 4 and when 2 cuts out, 3 is substituted. 
As I have been working on this for months (off and on), I really wanted to "finish" it, that is, provide the best representation of the tapes possible at this time. I decided to keep a few tracks from my previous "old source" merge. Just because the merges take so long to get right...there wasn't much difference, although the new source 4 really did help brighten things up. 
I WILL redo some of the last tracks at some stage, but not for a while. I just wanted to put this to bed for now. 
These files are the product of scores of merges and submixes, I can only hope I have chosen the best ones...Also as they were done over many months, had to try and keep them sounding as uniform as possible... 

Why did I not use all six sources? I try to merge only the best sources and mix as if it was a SBD recording. Merging six tapes just gets really messy, and you get diminishing returns piling noise on top of noise.

Did you use the 24 bit versions of the Copenhagen sources? No. Reason being I started the project before they became available, and was not in the mood to start all over again. There is no audible difference anyway - maybe in future, or maybe someone else can do it.


That said, there is room for improvement - so consider this a Version 1.0, and I will perhaps create new versions in future, but I just had to "finish" it - because if I didn't finish it over the holidays - it would never get done! It is tedious work, and I don't have much time these days.

Happy Holidays
