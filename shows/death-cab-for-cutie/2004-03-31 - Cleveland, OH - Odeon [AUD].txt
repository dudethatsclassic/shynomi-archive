NOTES:

Full house at the Odeon!  The venue initially stuck me in the back corner of the room - still technical FOB, but a crappy sounding location in an already crappy sounding venue.  After a brief discussion with security (who in turn checked with the soundguys and stage manager) they let me clamp DFC to the front of the soundboard structure.  Sound is pretty good, though I think the following night's Club Laga recording sounds better.
