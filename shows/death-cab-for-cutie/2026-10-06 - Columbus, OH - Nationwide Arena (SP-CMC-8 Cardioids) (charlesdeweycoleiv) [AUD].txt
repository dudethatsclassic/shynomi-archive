Weezer released their self titled "Gold" album this past August and are
out on their "Gathering" World Tour supporting. Similiar to the last time
Weezer played Nationwide (September of 2024), the sound in the arena
was fairly below average. I've heard so many great sounding shows inside
Nationwide Arena, Im assuming the blame falls on their FOH and I would be
willing to bet its the same one from the last tour. Thank god I had a close
seat to the corner stack, it was probably the loudest and most clear at
my vantage point. The crowd around me was pretty good, although I did use
RX7 to remove some faint screams and yelps when needed. I couldn't believe
how many kids (mostly teens) were at this show, and had a girl a few rows
behind me who liked to scream "I LOVE YOU RIVERS!" every few songs, so I
did my best to remove her when I could. Speaking of kids, I brought my 13
yr old with me who loved the show. I just had no idea Weezer was suddenly
so popular with Gen Z. When did THAT happen? Pretty cool, but I was a bit
taken back. I dont recall this many at the "Voyage" show just 2 years ago.
The biggest surprise was how big "Go Away" has gotten 14 years since it's
original release thanks to TikTok trends. Rivers even mentions it in the
beginning of the song. This was news to me!

Silversun Pickups and The Shins opened. [not recorded].
