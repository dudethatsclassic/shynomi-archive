This was not a band for me but they weren't terrible.  I do understand why/how some people could like them.  I found the vocals to be a bit low.  I'm not sure if that's a soundman thing or the way the band likes it.

Things to listen for: 
One girl telling another girl that she will be right back and the other saying "you better be".  She never did come back.  Someone shushing those girls for me.  Some guy realizing that it's just too crowded to keep moving forward.  Why do these things always happen during the quiet songs?

This recording will naturally sound much better cranked up loud with quality headphones!

Peace & enjoy,
Seth
