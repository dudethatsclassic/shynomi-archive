Notes:
Very excited for this show when it was announced. Both albums impacted me a great deal in my youth (as to many other fans). This is actually the first time I have seen Death Cab since I saw them a few months before Transatlanticism was released. Back then, I was able to open tape them and filmed the show in Royal Oak. Wild how times change. I remember thinking back then that the 'new' songs from this album sounded amazing. Honestly, it is their best record. It was great to hear many of these songs again live. I saw Postal Service in 2013 at the Fillmore and I think this set was better than 2013. Cool to see Ben jump on the drums for a few songs! I've been living in Metro-Detroit for about 28 years and it's crazy it was my first time at Meadow Brook. Very good venue. Sound was impeccable here.

Very happy with the results on this one. A huge bummer is that the right active cable shorted out. So I only had the left channel. Good thing is that I was in line with the left stack where we were sitting. So I am not sure that much would have changed the sound. Matrixing the two sources definitely negated the issue that arose prior to the show. 

Enjoy the tapes!
