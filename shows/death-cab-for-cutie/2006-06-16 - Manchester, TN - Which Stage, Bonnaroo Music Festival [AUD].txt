NOTES: The area in front of the soundboard was packed, so I opted to set up just behind the soundboard and run the mics above the level of the tent. The resulting recording is quite good. I had hoped to run my PCM-M1 DAT recorder instead of the minidisc, but the DAT was having problems with loading and unloading tapes. The MD SR-60 is my old standby recorder, and did the job nicely. There are one or two dropouts, probably caused by my over six year old MD deck just being old. I also ran the MD to the brink of brickwalling to maximize the saturation of frequencies. The MD tends to sound flat with more conservative recording levels. There may be an instance or two of brickwalling. As far as I know, this is one of only a small handfull of recordings of this show.
