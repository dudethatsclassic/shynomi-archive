I'm sharing these together, because I figured most people would be interested in both sets anyway. I'm
going to apologize up front for my loud "whoo"s in between songs; I'm used to being among the crowd
during shows, where making noise between songs blends in more with everyone else. We were in the 
balcony for this though, and the crowd was a bit more subdued up there, so my yells definitely stand out.
It's only between songs and I did my best to lower the volume there, so hopefully it doesn't ruin
anyone's listening experience of this show!

Anyway...I was really excited for this one, though honestly just because of the Postal Service. I 
remember being very disappointed that I couldn't make any of the shows on their 10th anniversary tour in
2013, so I made sure to get tickets as soon as they went on sale for the 20th anniversary tour. I'm not
super into Death Cab, but I do enjoy some of their songs and it was cool to see Transatlanticism in its
entirety as well. Both bands sounded great, and doing two full albums didn't stop Ben Gibbard's energy
one bit. He even changed between sets to coordinate with the bands - black for Death Cab and white for
Postal Service. Other than the albums, we got an acoustic performance of "Such Great Heights" by Ben
and Jenny Lewis in an attempt to "steal the song back" from Sam Beam of Iron Wine. Then the rest of the
guys from Death Cab came back out for a great cover of Depeche Mode's "Enjoy the Silence." Overall a 
great experience finally getting to see one of my favorite albums played live.
