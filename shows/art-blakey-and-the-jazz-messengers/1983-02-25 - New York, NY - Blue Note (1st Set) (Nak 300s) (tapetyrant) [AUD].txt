Welcome to Volume 24 of our master series featuring the master recordings of dime member "tapetyrant". The series will predominately feature captures in the Northeast and Southern California areas. We will touch on multiple genres with a strong focus on Jazz Fusion, New Wave and Rock. 

Tapetyrant was an active taper in the 1980's and much of the 1990's before, like many of us, hanging up the mics. What he captured during that time is uniformly excellent quality and his master tapes have been safely stored, the first batch are in pristine condition. Some shows may circulate from lower generation copies but most have remained uncirculated until now and have never circulated directly from the masters. 

Prior to DAT, the heavy lifting was entirely done by a Sony D5 with NAK 300's and taping was serious business, acquiring the best seats possible and working to keep neighbors in check. 

Volume 24 is a VERY welcome edition into our master series and the first of many shows we will present outside of the Fusion and New Wave vein.

Art Blakey and The Jazz Messengers played 5 nights at the Blue Note in New York City from February 22nd through February 26th playing at least 3 sets a night.

Presented here is TapeTyrant's brilliant audience capture of the first set on February 25th. This is one of those very exciting releases that comes out of the blue when kicking off a new master series, a brilliant performance of a random set of one of the master jazz musician's and his band from the last 60 years.

TapeTyrant also FM captured the 3rd set from the night after returning home, though we are not presenting it just yet due to appearing incomplete at the moment. The quality of TapeTyrant's audience capture blows away the FM capture which has some intermittent issues and lacks the rich fidelity of his audience capture. 

Thanks as always to goody for the pitch confirmation/adjustments and extra help on some setlist gaps on this one and, of course continued thanks to "tapetyrant" for doing such a great job capturing these important shows, for reaching out and entrusting me to present his excellent work for all to enjoy for decades to come. 

We are indebted to his generosity (tapers don't let those tapes and your hard work get lost to the ages, there are plenty of folks to get in touch with here to help get them out, we're all working to leave no tape behind).
