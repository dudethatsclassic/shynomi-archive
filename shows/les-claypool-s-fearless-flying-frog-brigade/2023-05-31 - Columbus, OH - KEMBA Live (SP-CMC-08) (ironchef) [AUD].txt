Notes:  I kept hoping that a better recording of this show would pop up, but I haven't seen 
one yet.  I didn't see any open tapers, and I can't find a soundboard.  It's certainly not the best 
recording of the tour, but I thought I'd finally put this up in case anyone was there and wants it.

The first 20 or so minutes were recorded from the far right side of the pit.  The place was packed 
throughout, and that seemed like the best option at first since I could get close to the PA.  Once the 
crowd chatter became too much, I decided to move. It's been a long time, but I think it was during 
"Long in the Tooth" and the first part of "Genie" that I was moving around.  I was back by the soundboard 
for a few minutes and then over to a spot on the far left side where I was able to maintain some open space.
It was somewhat distant-sounding, but at least I didn't have talking right around me.  Enjoy!
