If I had known that 2 other tapers were going to capture this as well as least 2 for Louisville but no sources for Denver (at the time of this writing - June 2023) I would have driven out there to catch that and this. I am also grateful I saw this band do a cover of Thela Hun Gunjeet as well since it now appears to be out of the set. I was slightly ahead of the board and about 15-20 feet to the right of it as well. I wanted to aim for a direct line to the PA stack, but got caught up talking to an old colleague/friend (I love him to bits, but he is also why the entire intro as well as about 9 seconds of the intro to Thela is missing). My recording may be the least good sounding of the 3 that exist, but I don't think this is terrible. Because of this, I decided to share the 24/44 version instead of just keeping that for me and sharing a 16/44 version instead.
