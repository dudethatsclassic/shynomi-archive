Notes:

Will this be my final Candlebox show? Well, I personally do not believe it will be,
but this is "The Long Goodbye Tour", and this was the only show I caught all year.
The band has stated repeatedly in interviews and in press this will be it for them.
So, we'll have to see. Im betting we see more shows in one way or another a few years
down the line. Kevin Martin is too talented to bow out of performing forever. A few
notes on the recording: For the first 21 minutes of this show, my position was the
opposite of whats in the notes. I was 25ft back from the right stack. The problem was
I had the most obnoxious and annoying girl near me who would not shut up. I used
RX7 to remove as many screams and yells as I could, but its still pretty obvious.
During "Blossom", I decided to abandon my spot - which I NEVER do. But, there was no
way my new spot would be any worse than where I was. I snuck in on the other side of
the room at the same distance from the left stack and remained there. I had much less
annoying people around me and it made for a more enjoyable show. Usually the band
grant permission (to use my stand), but my messages went unanswered. Therefore, I had
no choice but to use the hat mount and deal with the crowd. I wish it sounded better
in there, especially if this is the "LAST" time I see them, but it is what it is.
Without question, its more than worthy of a listen and the sound improves as the
show goes on - as does the crowd noise! Turn this one up and sorry for the 3+ month
turnaround time on getting this one out. Ooops! Thanks to Theresa for the ticket.
