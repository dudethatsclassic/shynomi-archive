Notes:

This show was recorded at Rib Fest in downtown Indy on a beautiful Sunday evening.  There
are a few things to mention about this recording.  For about a minute & a half during the end
of "Don't You" & the beginning of "Underneath It All", the lead singer, Kevin Martin came out
into the crowd directly behind me & I admit I made the cardinal sin of turning while recording
to get a few pictures.  I hope it doesn't detract from the recording too much for you.

Yes, track 8 of disc 1 is correctly titled: "Marriage Proposal".  A couple who is friends with
the band got engaged on stage!

Lastly, there is a small PA failure in track 10 of disc 1 towards the end.  I've reduced the
volume of that to not ruin your speakers.  But, there is an even bigger failure during track
2 of disc 2 & you will notice that the PA doesn't sound quite as loud after that.  The band 
was professional & kept on rocking though!

Also included is a few pics I took during the show.

ENJOY!!!
