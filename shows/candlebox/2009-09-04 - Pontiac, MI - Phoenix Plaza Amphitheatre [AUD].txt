The sound was not the best. It was held in The Phoenix, which is a very boomy 
outdoor venue. I did the best I could! Had I gotten there much earlier, I may
have been able to secure a better seat towards the center of the floor, but I
had to make due with 8 rows back on the left side. I also had to be polite to
the people seated behind me with a large microphone stand in the air. 

Show was very well packed, I would estimate atleast 3-4k people on hand. Great
performance. Worth the download for "10,000 Horses" alone.
