This silver CD comes from Candlebox's 1994 tour in support their debut album.  I saw them on this tour several times, and they rocked.  The set list itself is a mix of soundboard and audience recordings, and as such the audience tracks (8-12) suffer from some distortion.  That said, the soundboard tracks (1-7) make this disc worth the money I paid for it back then.  I'm in the mood to up some of my long-ago purchased silver CD's... so here you go.
