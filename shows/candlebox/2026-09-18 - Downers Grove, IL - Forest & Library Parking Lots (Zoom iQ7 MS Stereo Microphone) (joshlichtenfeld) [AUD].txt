Notes:

^  live debut.

Candlebox were scheduled to take the stage at 930 and play until 11, however
the promoter came on and told the audience they would be coming on at 9 and
would playing a full 2 hours!!! Candlebox took the stage at 9 and ended at
10:25, not even cracking 90 minutes - pretty funny. HUGE thanks to Josh for 
taping as I could not make it! The recording starts off sounding relatively
good and after 3 or 4 songs, the sound gets a tad more distant and a TON
of talkers show up. So much so, you can listen to conversations during the
lesser known album tracks. So, when listening, please keep that in mind.
I spent a good amount of time in RX7 removing a loud whistler and screamers
who were near Josh's rig to improve playback. Otherwise, you would be running
to the volume knob at the end of every song - it was that bad!

Candlebox took the stage at 9pm on a double bill with Local H [recorded
by another taper], who went on stage promptly at 715pm.
