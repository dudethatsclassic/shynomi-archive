Woodstock '94....on Friday. I don't remember much about this, other than the metal detectors were ***tipped over***,
 so getting my D6 and 20 tapes and 40 batteries in was a breeze. walked in during King's X's set, 
and I waited to set up camp until it was pitch black at 1am. 
Was able to catch the sets of Sheryl Crow, Collective Soul, Candlebox and the Violent Femmes, 
before they threw on "Clerks" for the late night movie. VERY bass-heavy sound for the entire weekend, 
I did my best to clean them up. hopefully, you folks enjoy!!! -balls(deep) OUT!!!
