Notes:

This was held in the o.h. Lounge in the middle of the brand new Hollywood Casino 
Columbus. Show was free and completely packed. Got there early and set up in the 
direct center of the room with my mic stand planted under a drink table. This 
seemed to work as people started using the space in front of my stand as the main 
aisle to get from one side of the room to the other. Thankfully, no bumping or 
knocking of the stand occurred. Candlebox is spending 2013 celebrating the 20th 
anniversary of their eponymous release, "Candlebox". They were advertised to play 
the full debut but omitted "Rain" from this performance. Otherwise, it's all here. 
Show ran almost 2 hours and featured 22 songs. Definitely check this one out. 
Recording turned out good to great. The stand kept the chatter down. Some rare 
songs performed for the first time in 16 years.
