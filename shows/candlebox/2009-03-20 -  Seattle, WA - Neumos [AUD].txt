Before 'miss you' Kevin mentions the song is for his dad, and how he 
was a WW2 vet and stormed the beaches of normandy. Pretty cool.
I was very surprised when the band 
bothered coming back out for an encore.
The crowd was reacting as if they had just seen somebody make a nice put.
Unfortunately no 10,000 horses, but still one of the best candlebox 

shows i've seen. They had it all. A little fightin, a little drinkin, 
and a little jammin.

"You don't fight the candlebox, you fuck the candlebox".
