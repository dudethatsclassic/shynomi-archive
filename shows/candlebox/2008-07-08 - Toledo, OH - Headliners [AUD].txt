Notes:

^  = Segues into "Voodoo Child (Slight Return)", which was originally recorded by
     The Jimi Hendrix Experience.

This is one of my favorite bands from my teenage years & it was great seeing them 
for the first time in 10 years. I missed them in 2006 & 2007. While hearing 7 new 
songs was nice, it was a real bummer not hearing "10,000 Horses", "Simple Lessons" & 
"Happy Pills". Those are concert staples, IMHO. I will just have to see them more!

The new song "Consider Us" was on the setlist before "Far Behind", but was not 
performed. The Hendrix segue was pretty neat to hear during "Far Behind".

Also note, I amplified the right channel by 1.0% due to it being slightly lower than 
the left. It may have been due to the way I was standing in accordance to the right 
stack.
