Notes:

^ = Features "Riders In The Storm" tag by The Doors. 

The new song "Lucy" was on the setlist before "Rain", but was not performed. 

Due to the large size of the crowd, it was very hard to get in a position where 
I did not have constant talkers and drunks in my way, but I think I did an ok job. 
This was my 2nd time taping Candlebox this week & I love how they changed up their
setlist & played 4 songs they did not in Toledo. 8 of the 17 songs played are new. =)
During the encore (immediately after "Consider Us"), the pavilion lights were turned 
on, but the band continued on & played "Rain" anyway. Very cool.
The crowd started to disperse during this point.

Also note, I amplified the right channel by 1.0% due to it being slightly lower than 
the left. It may have been due to the way I was standing in accordance to the right 
stack.
