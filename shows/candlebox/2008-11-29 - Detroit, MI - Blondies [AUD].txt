Notes:

^ = Features "Riders In The Storm" tag by The Doors. 

This was a tough show to tape. The band ended up going on 45 minutes late, which was 
around 12:15am. It had been sold out & people were cramped in there like sardines 
waiting & waiting for Candlebox to take the stage. When they finally did, it was all
worth it. Another great performance from the guys kicking off the first date of 
thirteen shows thru the month of December. This was a very hard show to record due
to the circumstances including the size & attendance, but I'm pretty happy w/ it.

Also note, I amplified the left channel by .5% due to it being slightly lower than 
the left. It may have been due to the way I was standing in accordance to the right 
stack.
