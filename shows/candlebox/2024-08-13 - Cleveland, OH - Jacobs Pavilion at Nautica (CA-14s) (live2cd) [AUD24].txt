Notes:

%  Joined by Nick Brown (of MONA) on vocals.

First time back at this venue in 5 years. I remember the sound being very
good back in 2019, but unfortunately that was not the case this night. The
sound was much lower than I felt it should have been for an outdoor pavilion
and the mix was very muddy and distant. Bush was the loudest of all the bands,
but still did not sound as great as I expected going in. Because the sound
was low, talkers were everywhere and most bothersome during Jerry's set. Oh
well, what can ya do? This is still very listenable and a blistering 45 minute
set from Candlebox.

Tim Montana, Jerry Cantrell and Bush were all on the bill. [all recorded but Tim].
