Notes:

The venue was seated the entire show. Im not used to going to see a rock show
where everyone remains seated after having their dinner. Fortunately, I was
given early entry with a VIP ticket, so I was able to choose my seat before anyone
else. Well, 2nd. The guy in front of me went for front row on the stage. I chose
the front of a 2nd table back from the right speaker stack. There was an aisle
in front of me, so occasionally you will hear a waitress speak to someone, but
its barely audible. The sound was relatively good from my spot, but there was
one minor issue - I was really only hearing vocals & acoustic guitar coming from
the above stack. So, while her vocals are nice and clear, the band is mostly
coming from the stage to the left on the recording. Especially the drums. Its
almost like FOH didnt even bother turning them up in the PA. This was a very
intimate show (and while I felt lucky being there), I wish I picked a different
spot! Thank god I was close enough to the stage to pick up everything! Just wish
I was more center to the stage with this one. I boosted the right channel by roughly
2+ db's to even out the playback a bit. Does it fix it completely? No, but its
a much nicer listen. Michelle rarely tours (shes only booked 14 shows this year),
so seeing her in any capacity is a real treat. This was well worth the trip from
Ohio! Ricky Woolstenhulme Jr. was featured on drums and overall, the band is a
massive upgrade from the 2017 tour. "Tongue Tied" is a brand new song and this
was the first time it was performed in public. 

Valley James opened. [not recorded].
