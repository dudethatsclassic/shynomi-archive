*  Originally Recorded By Judas Priest.

This place did not sound the greatest, so this is what it is. I mounted
the stand to the right of the soundboard area and raised it to roughly
8 ft and hoped for the best. Due to the size of the crowd, I could not
get any closer if I tried. Always a treat getting to catch Fig Dish live,
as it doesn't happen all that often. This was my third time seeing Fig
Dish! (all 3 have been opening for Local H - 2008, 2015 and 2025).
"The Ragged Ones" was on the setlist, but skipped due to time.

Opening for Local H. [also recorded].
