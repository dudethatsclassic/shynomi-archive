This is the first time i transfer and share my master. It has been sitting there for almost 20 years... it was about time to see the light of day :-)
This was the first concert of Billy's The Future Embrace Tour and it was a surprising show due to all the distance between the Billy here and what we knew from the SP. The concept of the show was good as was all the scenario but what killed me was the sound. It was no good and for a room like Aula Magna it was thin and dry. It lacked dinamycs. Still the songs sounded good. Listening to it now i find that there are some good songs in there and Billy is indeed a good songwriter. The recording is not amazing, sound is a bit distant but is is very listenable and better than other version i have of this show.
Enjoy and share
