Smashing Pumpkins frontman Billy Corgan stopped by the dts audio Sound Space at #KROQ to play some Smashing Pumpkins songs live (with a little bit of help from his kids), including the first-ever acoustic performance of Beguiled. Billy also chats with host Nicole Alvarez about the evolution of the band and more!

2. We Only Come Out At Night
3. Cyr
