Here's a rough, but extremely listenable mono recording of the 3rd and final set from the Zuzu's opening. I wasn't expecting to catch Billy as I had heard he had started playing at 1pm, just hoping to check out my new neighborhood tea shop. Walking in there was a buzz, and I spyed two acoustic guitars set up, so I planted there an waited... and shure enough he shows back up and doesn't disappoint. It was a nice, fun, intimate set complete with a sing along and "stump Billy".
