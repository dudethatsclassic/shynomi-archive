boombox's notes february 2024:
This was a short, but sweet "appearance, not a gig" in the back room of a record shop to promote latest album 'Natural Magick'. They guys have done a slew of these and mixed up the setlists a lot (hopefully in preparation of the upcoming shows). No new unrecorded songs like 'Rational Man' or even 'SOS' which Oxford got, but as a huge fan of PPA, 'Shower Your Love' was nice to hear with Jay back on board. This preceded a long signing session afterwards where the guys were very gracious in signing everything and chatting with fans of all ages, including some second generation followers introduced to the band by their parents. All in all, it was a very pleasant evening for less than 15 quid and you got a signed copy of the new CD thrown in too.

The room is little more than a concrete block storeroom, but this came out very well indeed. Minimal talking too from the real fans there and a surprisingly good mix, meant almost nothing to do other than reduce a few very loud claps during applause.

If you liked this, go out and buy the new album, which is very good and get a ticket for the summer.
