My notes:

When Direwolf-pgh uploaded this show at the traders den it was in two FLAC files.
One file was side one of his Maxell UD II 90 cassette and the other was side two.
I decoded the FLAC files to wave using FLAC frontend.
Once decoded I used CD Wave to track the show.
I then used FLAC frontend (level 8) to encode the tracks to FLAC.

I did not alter the recording in any other way!

Thanks go out to Direwolf-pgh for recording this show and sharing it!
