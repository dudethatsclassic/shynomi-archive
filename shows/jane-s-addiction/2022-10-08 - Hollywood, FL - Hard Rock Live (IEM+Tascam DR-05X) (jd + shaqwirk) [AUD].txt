Notes:
Finally got some time to work on this. JD sent over a feed, which I believe it Eric Avery's feed. A little bit more prominent on the bass, but overall a wonderful mix. I supplemented the IEM feed with the audience recording that was seeded here in October by Shaqwirk to help with adding more highs in to the mix. Overall, this is a fantastic tape to listen to. Nothing was done to the audience tape in this mix. Only work that I did on the IEM was add a small amount of reverb to make the IEM feed not so dry.
