show notes:  

this was a fantastic experience all the way around.

I filmed this show (3 cams,a Hi8 and 2 digital) AND audio taped it....from the woods *behind* the fence at the back of the venue.

not only that, but this recording is from the *internal* mics on the Tascam DR-2D....I taped it up in a tree about 6 feet high, and at the very end of "Up The Beach", you do hear me walk up to the deck to check the levels (crinkly leaves), the levels increase a bit, and then the show really starts.

other than that one minor flaw, this is a borderline soundboard experience, with how the deck is 100 yards from the stage, and the crowd is 70 yards away as well....they almost sound like they were mic'd separately.

again, *internal* mics. nothing fancy at all.

the other funny about this show, I gots to hand it to Mr. Farrell, they had a few stripper-chicks onstage with them (see the attached photos), and near the end (I think it was "Three Days"), they were swinging around on each side about 15 feet in the air.

and one of them only had one leg.
