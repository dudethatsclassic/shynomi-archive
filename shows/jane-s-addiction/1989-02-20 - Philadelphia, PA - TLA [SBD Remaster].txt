Jane's addiction
1989-02-20
Theatre of Living Arts 
Philadelphia PA 

Requested remaster of a pretty rough source...

But I took it on anyway. The levels on this ran different across 4 sections of the show. Add to that the volume jumps and balance shifts going wild in the first 3 minutes and it was an interesting and pieced together workover.

In the end, it's not bad really. Certainly a smoother listen than before and a better and cleaner mix. I've heard higher quality JA boots, but this one's still good to go in my collection.
