Notes: Pretty good soundboard recording. This show is very common. It was bootleged on a vinyl called Love Junkies, then the vinyl to transfered to a boot CD called Whole Lotta Love. This recording basically comes from there, but I attatched 2 songs from the show that are missing from that recording.
This was the first Jane's live show I ever got, and it's still a personal favorite.

Show Information: Bulimia Banquet opened. Show frequently misdated as 1/27/87. Circulating copies of this show are from a soundboard recording.
