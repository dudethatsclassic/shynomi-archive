Album Notes:

This is an excellent show.  It captures the band during their cohesive peak, where the band is close and performing at an incredible level, and the disc does a good job capturing it. The back cover of the disc incorrectly notes the date as Feb, 29, 1989 when in fact this show took place on March 29, 1989.
