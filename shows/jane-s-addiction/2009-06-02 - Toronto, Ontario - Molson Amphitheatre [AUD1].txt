NOTES:
Jane's freakin' Addiction - all 4 original members w/Avery now back in the fold. The only Canadian stop on the "NIN|JA Tour", the opening show of the season @ The Molson Amp & 2 weeks or so from the tour's wrap. Nice way to 
start the summer most certainly - with a reunited Jane's concert down by the lake & meeting up w/old friends: good to see Adam/Sue & later Paul. Bummer the pre-sales only allowed for 2 tickets/per order. 1st song into the encore 
("Summertime") looked over & my woman's smiling away & grooving - life's not too bad all things being equal. Great time, but was surprised that the place thinned out a bit after NIN were done. Oh you old goths ;) Very cool that ALL 
bands onboard agreed to a FULL open taping policy for the tour. Much thanks to each group for this. That being said, an odd patdown @ the gates coming in where my gear was fine (didn't walk in advertising it, but they opened my 
storage case), but the small umbrella (wrapped in a plastic bag) was a no-no that had to be checked. Huh?? Dunno.. Missed Jane's back before the split, so this was long overdue (only caught w/Flea @ The ACC in '01 ). Had my 
pre-amp running pretty hot, and unfortunately there was some bass clipping up till 30 mins or so into the show (very minimal @ times, but still). Thanks to krewechief for allowing the patch to complete my source (and sending along 
the raw file for me to work with) - as I missed the pre-taped intro & 1 1/4 mins or so into "Three Days". Love trying to make it back to my seat w/16K of my closest friends... As this special show warranted it IMHO, ended up turning 
into a full matrix of both our sources. 1st time I did one, but am pretty happy w/how things came out sonically (this is mix #5, after a few false starts w/too much/little bass) - especially as we were running similar rigs, save for mics & 
neither source was directly forward facing to the stage, so..  Special thanks to all the photogs who shared their visual captures of this show as well. Included here:
