JANE'S ADDICTION

1989-01-16 New Orleans, LA - Tipitina's (http://tipitinas.com)

WTUL FM radio broadcast or (more likely) soundboard 

Source was two wave files of the entire show which was purported to come from someone connected to WTUL radio station. The raw recordings of radio spots in track 16 seem to support this. I put in track markers.

I did some searching on google to see if this was out circulating and there appears to be a boot named "Idiot's Rule" (http://janesaddiction.org/bootographies/boot_details.phtml?bootID=18) with some of this show but not as much as in this recording.

Enjoy.
