Show Information:
LA-area Lollapalooza 1991 show, the 1st of a 3 date stand at the Irvine Meadows Amphitheatre.

Recording Information:
Very good quality soundboard recording. 78:10. The version circulating is not quite as clear as the 7/23/91 or 7/24/91 soundboard recordings from the same venue, but still a good one. The show's final song, "Summertime Rolls", is cut at the very end.

Notable Moments:
Early in the show, Perry teases the security personel to lighten up on the crowd.
The middle of "Had a Dad" gets messed up a little when Perry comes in too early. The start of "Ocean Size" is a bit off as well, when Perry crosses up Dave and Eric by yelling at someone before his usual "three, four!" count-off.
