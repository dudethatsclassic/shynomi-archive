Jane's Addiction 
Agora Theatre, Cleveland, OH
27 November 1990 

Recorded by my friends and I. Rough recording (C-/D+) 

It was a great night. Janes was just starting to pop in Cleveland. It was like we were in a secret club with 2000 other weirdos. The show was sold out. It was a GA show. My friends and I were able to sneak the recorder in. Some of them, I believe, they sat in the balcony while recording. The rest of us were down in the pit. I dove off the stage a couple of times. Just the best. If you ever get a line on that SB I would be all about it.

- MrDoots
