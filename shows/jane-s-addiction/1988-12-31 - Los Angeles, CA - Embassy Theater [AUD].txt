notes: at this show well...it's best explained by dave--"I'll tell you a story about Tom. Tom Morello was in a band called Lock-up, before Rage Against the Machine, and there was a show on New Year's Eve, where they dressed up as Jane's Addiction, and came out as us, as a joke, and they broke into Pigs in Zen, and so Tom was basically pretending to be me. He wore a longhaired wig. It was amazing. It was a joke on the audience, and of course, moments into it, the audience realized it was a joke and they left the stage and we came out. It was really funny."--so the first pigs is lock up, tom morello's band. tom has also comented on this saying jane's suggested the joke. lock-up opened this show, which is tom morello's band before rage against the machine.
