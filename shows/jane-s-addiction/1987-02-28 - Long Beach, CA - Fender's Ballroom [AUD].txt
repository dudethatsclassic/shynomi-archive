Jane's Addiction
February 28, 1987 
Fender's Ballroom, Long Beach, CA

Lineup: Perry Farrell
Dave Navarro
Stephen Perkins
Eric Avery
 

Set List:

01 Beat It (tease)
02 Three Days
03 Standing In The Shower... Thinking
04 Stop!
05 Summertime Rolls
06 Ocean Size

Show Information:

This show was originally scheduled to take place in December of 1986 but was postponed to this date.

Jane's opened for Peter Murphy. Cathouse and Scratch Acid also performed.

This show is frequently misdated as 2/27/87.
