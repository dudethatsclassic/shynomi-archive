"Simply stated, this is one of the best sounding Jane's Addiction bootlegs around. It contains a clear soundboard recording where the audience doesn't overwhelm the music. At times the sound is a bit muffled, but in comparison to other recordings, this is still a very good sounding disc.

This is the very last performance the original lineup of Jane's Addiction played, and until late 1997, the last time Eric Avery and Perry Farrell saw each other. Contrary to popular belief, this is not a Lollapalooza show."
