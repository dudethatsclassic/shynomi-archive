A friend in high school traded for these from a guy he thought
was Erik from LA. I remember the Erik guy had some list with 
some nice artwork of Janes, like everyone used to do with their
lists back in the day. He got out of the hobby, I ended up with his 
tapes, and I eventually put them to CD. I purged all my analog 
tapes about a year ago, so these are from the CD masters I made
off those tapes
