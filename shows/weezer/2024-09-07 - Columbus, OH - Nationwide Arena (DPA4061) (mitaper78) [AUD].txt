
2024-09-07

Weezer
Columbus, OH, USA @ Nationwide Arena

Source: DPA 4061 > DPA MPS 6030 > Sony A10
Position: Stage-Left, Lower bowl, Approx 50' from speakers
Taper: MITAPER78

Notes: The previous time I taped Weezer was in 2001 with Jimmy Eat World and Tenacious D (went for the D). Just never had another itch to record them until this tour when they performed the Blue Album in it's entirety. MY buddy and I made the four hour drive to Nationwide Arena from MI with plenty of time before the show. Weezer was on point this evening and performed a very good show. It was not just a concert but they had decent visuals for the most part, was not boring. The recording, originally recorded in 48khz I down sample it to 44.1khz in case anyone wants to burn it to CD, had a good amount of low end thus I had to do some work in post in order to get the audio where it should be. Enjoy.
