Cody's; Visalia, California, USA
1994-07-08
Taper: ??? (AUD)
Comment: This is one of the first Weezer audience recordings, and the earliest known performances of Getchoo and You Gave Your Love to Me Softly. Not a great sounding show, but I did my best to remaster it. It's worth noting there was a loud high pitched hum throughout the entire recording, which I've done my best to remove. That said, there's a noticeable hole at around 8.7khz, which is a left over. Cover photo by Justin A. Moulder, taken a month later at the Trocadero (there's no photos circulating from the Cody's show). Enjoy!
Hailey Carol, April 5th, 2025
