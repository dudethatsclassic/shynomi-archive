I think a Weezer fan would love this.  Easily my best recording so far...
Listen to the San Diego crowd jeer on the guy they bring up to play on Undone, everything's cool till they find out he's
from Denver ;]
Overall, a great show, brought many a smile to faces, and many singalongs were present.  The guy next to me mercifully picks his times
to shout.  Only doing so between songs, good on him.  Other than him there is no excessive talking or other noise.  The few
audio distortions you may hear were all from the PA, this recording sounds exactly as it did that night, a little echoe-y maybe.
I know some people don't care, the intro is When You Wish Upon A Star from the Disney Pinnochio movie.  I'm sure most don't care
if it's there or not but in case you are like me, and like the show to have an intro on till the end, you can contact me for a
trade of the full version that I cannot seed here.  None of the other bands were taped by me.


NO EQ or other mastering done, only normalized to 98%
