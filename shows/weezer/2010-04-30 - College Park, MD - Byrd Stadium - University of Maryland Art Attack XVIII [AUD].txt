MISC NOTES:
This was my first time seeing Weezer in over half a decade, and my first time at Byrd Stadium.  The show must have been very nearly sold out (5,000 people).  This performance was opened by Ben Folds solo (which I also recorded).  I went in expecting a lackluster show from my once-favorite band, and though they did still play more new material than I would have liked, I must admit that I was very pleasantly surprised by their showmanship.

Aside from some phasing issues that resulted from the intermittently strong winds, I am pretty happy with the recording.  The biggest flaw is the 10seconds or so of dead silence that occurs about 3 minutes into Surf Wax America (my mics unplugged from my preamp).

The song I called "Pop Medley" incorporates the songs "Kids" (by MGMT), and "Poker Face" (by Lady Gaga).

Track 18 began with Rivers walking back out on stage for the second encore by himself.  He played the drums and looped it, then picked up the tambourine, bass guitar, and acoustic guitar, and looped all those.  He then trained the audience and began the song about 2 minutes and 20 seconds after the opening of the track.  The band came out later and joined in to continue the song at about the 3:40 mark.

Great show.
