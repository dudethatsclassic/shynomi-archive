The first time I saw Weezer was on the 25th of October 1994 in Phoenix, AZ when they opened for Live. 
When I saw them entering the stage I thought that they were a school band covering as an opening act 
because they would not have a proper one. I was so wrong! OMFG they blew us away. 
I could not believe it that those small and thin geeks that look like that they would fall over because 
their guitar is to heavy for them just blew out head off.

In February 1995 they played in my hometown Frankfurt, Germany in a small 300 capacity club in downtown. 
Besides the people behind the bar only a handful of Germans were there - the rest were GIs that are 
stationed in Germany.
When Weezer started playing people in the front started pushing each other and jumping around.
It was so tough that the speaker stack were shaking so much that I thought that it will fall on me. 
I recorded the show 10 feet in front of the right stage side speaker stack. 
This show is under my top ten shows that I have ever seen.
