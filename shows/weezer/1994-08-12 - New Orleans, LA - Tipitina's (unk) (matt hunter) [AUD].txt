Weezer
Tipitina's; New Orleans, Louisiana, USA
1994-08-12
Taper: Matt Hunter
Comment: The third known Weezer audience bootleg, which was later mislabeled as a St. Louis show and released on the bootleg CD American Holiday. This version however is a transfer of the master and includes "My Name Is Jonas". Special thanks to Juan Cena for helping find the original taper of this show. Enjoy!
