Notes:
Alive Quantum is an unofficial 2xCD released by the label Wonder Boy Records in 2002 in Japan. Tracks 1-13 on Disc 1 were recorded at Hultsfred Festival 2001, Sweden on June 16, 2001. Tracks 14-19 on Disc 1 were recorded at the Y100 Sonic Session on June 20, 1997. All tracks on Disc 2 were recorded at The Spectrum Arena, Philadelphia on September 26, 2001. Many titles on Disc 1 are incorrect, often labeled as different songs, making a confusing mess.
