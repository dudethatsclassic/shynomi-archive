Weezer & Pixies co-headlining Spring tour. Each band got roughly 75 minutes and
packed in as much as they could (both playing over 20 songs). I opted against
buying a ticket in the lower bowl or floor as the tickets were all exceeding
$100. Around Christmas this past year Ticketmaster ran a holiday deal on club
level seating for $44 a pop. I knew section 222 was facing a stack, so I bit.
It was well worth the cost. Sound was pretty good (well, as good as an arena
can get) and the crowd near me STFU for both sets! Awesome sauce! Sadly, no
one in my section (or any nearby 200 level sections wanted to stand). Fun set,
but way too many covers. I get they are promoting their recently released
Teal record, but come on guys, play some more original Weezer. I much rather
would have heard Troublemaker, Memories, Only In Dreams, Dope Nose, In The
Garage, Tired Of Sex, etc over any of these cover selections. Especially
when they are only getting 75 minutes up there! That minor complaint aside,
it was a real fun show. They even had some pyro going off! Didn't expect that!

For all the =w= fans, enjoy!!
