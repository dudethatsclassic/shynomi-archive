It's been really interesting to see Weezer's resurgance over the past 10+ years. They're my wife's favorite
band, so we've seen them 5 times in that period, each time at progressively bigger venues, starting at
the House of Blues in Myrtle Beach in 2014 (capcity 2,000) and going to this show at the Spectrum Center in
Charlotte (capacity 20,000). They've got a sound that works great in small clubs, but they also figured out
how to adapt to an arena show as well - adding some fun (albeit a bit cheesy) theatrics that add to the
show but don't go so far as to overshadow the actual songs. The "power outage" bit they're doing on this
tour is fun little way to incorporate an acoustic set into the middle of the show, and having Rivers and
Pat switch instruments for "Pork and Beans" lets them harken back to the days of Josh Freese on drums while
not betraying the classic lineup people love. The set list is also solid, including all the hits of course,
but also some deep cuts like "Longime Sunshine" and "Slob." I was particularly excited that "Keep Fishin'"
 has been brought back into the mix - I will not stand for Maladroit erasure. Bringing out Nikki Monninger 
from opener Silversun Pickups to sing the band's two duets and the female-led "I Just Threw Out of the Love 
of My Dreams" was very welcome as well.

As for the recording, I think it's fairly decent but suffers a bit from the sheer amount of excited 
screaming from the girls around us. We were front row at the barricade, so I guess I should I have
expected as much, and I can't blame them for having a good time! The mix at the venue was pretty good
though, and I did my best to reduce the volume of the screams between (and some times in the middle of) 
songs without it being too noticeable.

I also recorded the Shins' opening set, but not Silversun Pickups.
