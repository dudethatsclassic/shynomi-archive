This was a solid show on Weezer's Indie Rock Road Trip tour. The set has stayed pretty consistent
throughout the tour, and is more or less the greatest hits you'd expect to hear, plus a few deeper
cuts. Rivers has been doing a different solo acoustic song at every show, and we got "Lover in the Snow,"
which was released on one of his Alone demo compilations. I think my only gripe with the set is that a
lot of songs are truncated or even just pieces of songs - the "1 More Hit" intro to "Pork and Beans" and
just the solo from "Run, Raven, Run" as the outro to "Say it Ain't So," for example. I will say I 
really enjoyed the reworked version of "Perfect Situation," though, which was a more piano-based take on 
the song. All in all, they did play for a full hour-and-a-half, and sounded great. I'm seriously 
impressed that Rivers doesn't sound like he's aged a bit in the last 30 years.

I was front-row center, right in front of Rivers and directly in front of a speaker stack. The sound was
great and it shows in the recording. Joyce Manor and Future Islands opened. I had intended on recording
Future Islands, but somehow managed to not hit record properly and didn't realize until their set was over.
They put on a great show, so I was pretty bummed about that. Joyce Manor were fun too, though they didn't
really leave much of an impression on me. Kind of standard pop-punk stuff.
