\cf0 \
Great show.  Band starts off with "Greatest Hits" portion of show, small break, then plays the entire "Blue" Album from start to finish.  Crowd is very into it and you can hear them singing lightly in the background during some songs.  No one is singing near the taper.  \
\
All of "Blue" was played as a four piece with Pat Wilson playing drums.  Greatest hits set was played as a five piece with Josh Freese on drums\
\
Sound turned out better than I expected and I hope it pleases all you Weezer fans.  Nothing done to the audio except put a fade at the beginning of "Memories" and at the end of "Only in Dreams".\
\
There are 2 spots where there is a tape dropout, one is during "Memories" and the other is in between the songs "Memories" & "I Want You To" while Rivers Cuomo is talking.  Recovery of those sections was not possible so it's left as is. Very minor.  \
\
I would like to see some comments on the sound as I'm curious to know what others think.  \
\
If someone could make artwork that would be great.  I've seen some great CD covers made here on Dime so I'm hoping that someone can create some.}
