1995-02-04
Taper: ??? (AUD)
Comment: Rarely circulated bootleg of Weezer in the Netherlands, which I received from Dan Hewitt recently. I've cleaned it up in Izotope RX 11, reducing the bassy distortion present, removing mic squeaks and dropouts. The recording is also notable for a rare performance of Susanne (taking the setlist place of Getchoo), which has seldom bootleg recordings out there. Be aware this is likely not the complete setlist as My Name Is Jonas and No One Else were usually performed as the openers to 1995 shows, but it's possible the two weren't recorded by the taper. Enjoy!
Hailey Carol, January 5th, 2025
