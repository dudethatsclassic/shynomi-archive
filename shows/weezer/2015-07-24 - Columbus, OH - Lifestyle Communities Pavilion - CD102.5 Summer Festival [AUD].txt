Notes:

Huge thanks to Ryan Teske for lending me my old pair of Core Sound Binaural
microphones I gave him in 2005. Im in the process of replacing some gear, so he 
was a life saver! The LC is usually known for great acoustics (indoors and 
outdoors!), but tonight something seemed off. The bass also was, very heavy. I did
use the roll off w/ the CSB's, but I still had to EQ a bit in post. You may want 
to adjust your stereo system to your preference while playing back. 

Im a huge fan of their recent 2014 release, "Everything Will Be Alright In The End", 
however they only performed 2 songs from it. Very disappointing. On the other hand,
it's hard to complain about getting 6 songs from Blue!

The crowd was LOUD and sang a long to many of the songs. Definitely worth checking 
out for that reason alone.
