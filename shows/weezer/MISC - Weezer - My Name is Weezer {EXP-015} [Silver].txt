Weezer
My Name Is Weezer (EXP-015)
Label - Expire

My Name Is Weezer is an unofficial CD release by the label "Expire" in 1997. Tracks 1-6 were recorded on November 6, 1996 at Shorecrest High School in Seattle, WA. Tracks 7-9 were recorded on May 21, 1997 for KNDD 107.7 FM in Seattle, WA. Tracks 10-15 were recorded on August 18, 1996 at La route du Rock Festival 1996 in Saint-Malo, France. The CD's track list has various small typos and incorrect song names, such as three separate instances of the title "Undone The Sweather Song".
