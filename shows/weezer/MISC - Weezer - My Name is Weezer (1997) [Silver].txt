Notes:
My Name Is Weezer is an unofficial CD release by the label "Expire" in 1997. Tracks 1-6 were recorded at Shorecrest High School, Seattle, WA on November 6, 1996. Tracks 7-9 were recorded for KNDD 107.7 FM, Seattle, WA on May 21, 1997. Tracks 10-15 were recorded at La route du Rock Festival 1996, Saint-Malo, France on August 18, 1996. The CD's tracklist has various small typos and incorrect song names, such as three separate instances of the title "Undone The Sweather Song".
