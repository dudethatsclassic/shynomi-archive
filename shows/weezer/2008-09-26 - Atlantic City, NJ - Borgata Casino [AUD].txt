Notes ----------------------------------------------------------------------

The vocals on this recording are very low. I was standing front row directly in front of Rivers, and wasn't about to give up that spot so that my bootleg would sound better. I apologize.

There are also issues with crowd noise. The worst of it is the Angels & Airwaves fan who was behind me. She was tone deaf and annoying, and liked to sing along and scream. Thankfully she only knew a few songs, but when she sings, it's impossible to ignore. Sorry about that. I tried to shield my mics from her, but she was literally pressed against my back, so it was impossible.

Near the end of "El Scorcho", the audio gets a bit muffled, and you can hear a lot of yelling and talking. Security was on top of me yelling at a troublemaker in the crowd behind me, and eventually pulled him out. You will hear the group around me cheer at the beginning of "Morning Glory" when this happens, then the audio gets clear again.

As the band was about to play "The Greatest Man That Ever Lived", Brian disappeared from stage. Scott had the crowd yell for his return. You can hear this, but Scott's banter is very muffled and hard to understand.
