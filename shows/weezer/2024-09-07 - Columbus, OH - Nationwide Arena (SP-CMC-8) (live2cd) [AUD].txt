Weezer are out celebrating 30 years of their massive debut album, "Blue".
The show was space themed with a voyage to the "Blue" planet, which was
a great way to perform 2 sets (with the first leading up to Blue). I have
recorded in Nationwide Arena quite a few times (its my home arena) and I
have never heard a show lack bass like this one did! I thought maybe it
was the earplugs in but when I got home and loaded the raw wave file, it
solidified what I heard. I was directly in front of the left side hanging
stack in 116, so the sound should have been perfect. While its good (for
an arena), I was hoping for better results. I did add some bass in Adobe
Audition and tested it on my home stereo (as well as my PC) to ensure it
sounded fine before dithering and tracking to FLAC. I did have to remove
an intermittent screamer 2 rows in front of me in RX7 (when needed). The
sound is good for an arena, but dont expect greatness. Im still fairly
happy with it considering how important this tour is to a lot of people,
myself included. Only 22 NA dates and thats it. Go see them if you can!

Dinosaur Jr. and The Flaming Lips opened. [not recorded].
