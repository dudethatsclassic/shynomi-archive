Loose Dog (LOOSE-052)

Studio Jam June 2001 is an unlicensed bootleg Weezer CD-R released by the label Loose Dog. Tracks 1–4 were recorded for the 2001 BBC Sessions at Maida Vale Studios on June 13, 2001. Tracks 5–9 were recorded at the Soundgarden Club in Dortmund, Germany on June 21, 2001 (erroneously listed on the bootleg's art as June 6, 2001). The bootleg is one of many Weezer-related releases by Loose Dog.

There exists a re-released version of the bootleg with lower quality artwork and a unique disc design which includes the additional label code "WEEZME 011"
