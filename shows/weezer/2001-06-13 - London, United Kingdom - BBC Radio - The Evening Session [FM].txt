The band was asked to do 4 songs, including "Hash Pipe", "Island In the Sun",
and a cover. However, the guys were so excited about having an opportunity to
record, that they convinced BBC to let them do all new songs! And not only
that, they worked extra fast and recorded 8 songs as opposed to the required 4!
It was quite a battle, but they got their way...Due to time restrictions, only
4 of the songs were polished up, and the other 4 will not see any airplay. The
4 songs that the BBC will be featuring are:

Burndt Jamb
High Up Above
Take Control
Listen Up

and the 4 "unpolished" tracks are:

Keep Fishin'
American Gigolo
December
Saturday Night
