MISC NOTES:
Weezer was touring with The Flaming Lips and Dinosaur Jr on their "Voyage to The Blue Planet" tour, commemorating the 30th anniversary of Weezer's release of their self-titled debut record.  I recorded the Flaming Lips and Weezer, but did not record Dinosaur Jr.  Fantastic show at a fantastic venue, and the first of their two-night engagement at The Anthem!
