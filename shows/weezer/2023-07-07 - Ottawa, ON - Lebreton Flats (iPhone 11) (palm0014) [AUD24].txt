Recording notes:
Bluesfest.  You might have my heart each year when the summer festival season starts up but you did not play well with recording conditions this evening.  In a sea of 25,000 my wife and I found a spot where everyone seemed focused on the party and not the band playing for the party.  You want a horrible recording, you got it!  Crushing of tin beer cans on the ground, people having conversations right near me, really bad singing along.  :-). You get my point.  This is probably the worst of the batch from my recording this year.  It was a bummer for my first night out.  Was this going to be a trend for the entire festival (Nope, it got way better later on.)

Show notes:
The show itself was pretty great.  I do enjoy Weezer and they had a good setlist across their career.  They really know the festival circuit...play your hits and pepper some other songs in between.  

There were fireworks that went off over the Ottawa river at one point which was cool to watch while Weezer was playing.

As mentioned before, the crowd was way over the top into partying that night.  It was Friday night and the first weekend Bluesfest was open and you have Weezer providing the tunes.  Yep, recipe for a messy night.  My wife wasn't appreciated the sweaty crowd surfers zooming by us and drenching her in sweat.  Haha!

Long live Weezer.
