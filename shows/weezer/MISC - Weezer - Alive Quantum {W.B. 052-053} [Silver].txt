Weezer
Alive Quantum
Released 2002
Wonder Boy Records (W.B. O52/053)

Alive Quantum is an unofficial live Weezer bootleg released by the Japanese bootlegging label Wonder Boy Records in 2002. The release features three live Weezer performances across two discs.

Overview:
The track listing of Alive Quantum contains a large amount of errors. Due to Wonder Boy Records being a Japanese label, it is most likely that some titles were lost in translation, or displayed on the incorrect track number.
