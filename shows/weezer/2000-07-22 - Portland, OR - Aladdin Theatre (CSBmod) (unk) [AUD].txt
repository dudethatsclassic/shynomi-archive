Notes: This show was a part of Weezer's first headlining tour following their hiatus from performing and recording starting in 1997. Previously, they had played a few "secret" shows under the pseudonym Goat Punishment prior to playing the Yahoo! Warped Tour. Fresh off these dates, the band continued it's comeback, playing new Summer Songs 2k (SS2k) tunes on a consistent basis, which would all be put aside a year later in favor of what would become The Green Album. Played for the first time this show was Modern Dukes. Modern Dukes was later demoed for Weezer's fifth album, but has been scrapped for new (and hopefully better) material. The following is Weezer roadie/webmaster/farm hand/boot knocka/photographer Karl Koch's brief notes on the show:

Tonight's show at the Aladdin Theater in Portland was very cool. Thats a really swell place to do a show. And once again the crowd went ballistic! Tonight another new song was unfurled: "Modern Dukes", and it sounded great. Also, our good buddy Patrick Finn's band, Organic Mechanic, opened up, sounded great, and got a really warm well-deserved response from the crowd. They will also be opening up for today/tommorow(?)'s show , up in Seattle. Today also saw the debut of a new weezer babydoll t-shirt, with a sort of anime design. Hopefully we can get a picture up on RWA soon.
