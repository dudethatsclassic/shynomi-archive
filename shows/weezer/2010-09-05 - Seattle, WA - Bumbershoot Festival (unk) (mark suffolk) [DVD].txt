Notes -
Just a few notes on this show and some others that I will be sharing from Mark Suffolk's collection. Some of you might know Mark, he was very active in the late 90's and all throughout the 00's, he was a very well respected trader who started taping and filming shows. Mark helped me get started in trading when it all went online in the late 90's/early 00's and I owe the guy a lot, he was an absolute pleasure to deal with. Unfortunately Mark passed away recently and his wife got in touch with me regarding his bootleg collection, TravisBickle1963 very kindly helped collect the collection and go through a lot of discs so a lot of credit goes to him for making these available to the fans, check out his YouTube channel if you ever want your tapes transferring - https://www.youtube.com/@TravisBickle1963

But of course, all credit to Mark for filming and taping so many great shows, a great loss to the trading community. If you decide to share this elsewhere, please give the credit to Mark please

This is his old trading site if this brings back any memories - https://web.archive.org/web/20080101124722/http://www.freewebs.com/bootlegs4trade/index.htm
