Comment: Another uncirculating Weezer audience recording, this was the last show before they shot the Buddy Holly video and before Rivers Cuomo cut his hair. This was remastered by MaestroDavros with additional remastering by Hailey Carol, and transferred by Cnacker. Quality isn't that great but I'm happy with how it came out, assumedly it was taped on a cheap portable tape recorder. Included are also scans of the master tape. Enjoy!
Hailey Carol, March 9th, 2025
