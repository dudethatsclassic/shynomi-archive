Good Life - Germany Aug. 18 is an unofficial CD released in 1996. Tracks 1-10 were recorded at Bizarre Festival 1996, Cologne, Germany on August 17, 1996. Tracks 11 & 12 were both recorded in Japan on an unknown date or location. Even though the title of the bootleg mentions "Aug. 18", the show was actually recorded on August 17. The bootleg features a few typos or incorrect titles, which have been transcribed below.
