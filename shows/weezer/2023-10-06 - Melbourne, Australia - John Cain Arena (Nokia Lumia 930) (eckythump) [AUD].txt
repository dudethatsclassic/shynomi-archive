Show Notes: Weezer's first Australian headline shows since 2013 where I was fortunate enough to attend the shows in Melbourne where they played all the Blue album one night and all of Pinkerton the next (they were also here supporting Foo Fighters in 2018 which I missed). Weezer were originally announced as the support act for KISS' final Australian show in Sydney, with headlining shows for Melbourne and Brisbane subsequently announced. The setlist seemed to follow the pattern of their other 2023 shows, with the exception of a newly learned KISS cover to show their excitement of scoring the support slot, as well as the first performance of Photograph since 2015 (according to the setlist.fm contributors anyway, and given the petty squabbles that go on in the comments there, they'd know!). I thought it was a fantastic show, my only gripe being many of the songs were abbreviated, like they were afraid of losing the audience's attention in this ADHD era (and given that I saw many people heading to the toilets/bars during Strutter, maybe they were right). Extending out the show from a sharp 90 minutes to 100 minutes would have made me more satisfied, but hey it was still a really entertaining show, and the crowd was well into it.

Recording Notes: This is an excellent sounding recording. I was in the seats behind the standing floor facing the speaker stack, and it came out nice and clear. While I had some enthusiastic guys behind me singing loudly, you can't hear them on this recording. Nicely mastered by Matt Jones to enhance the listening experience. Enjoy!
