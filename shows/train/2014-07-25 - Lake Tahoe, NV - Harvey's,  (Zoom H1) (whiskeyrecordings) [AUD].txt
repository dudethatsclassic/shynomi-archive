The opening act was a country band called "Striking Matches". Who's singer Sarah Zimmermann sang the duet "Mermaid" with Train.

Before they played the song "Mermaid", Monahan invited around 25 girls onto the stage to dance and help sing back-up with the band.

My favorite part of the show was of course the Led Zeppelin song. Pat used to front a Zeppelin cover band in the early 90's.

I think Jimmy has found his next lead singer for that Zeppelin tour we will never get. HA

The girls really seem to love this band, more so the singer.

The sound quality is pretty good and enjoyable.
