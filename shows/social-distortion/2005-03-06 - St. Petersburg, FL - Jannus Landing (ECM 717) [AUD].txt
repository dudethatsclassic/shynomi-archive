  A nice mix of old songs with songs from the most
recent release, "Sex, Love & Rock and Roll".  They even played
a brand new song that's not even released yet, "Diamond in The Rough" 
   Mike & the boys were really on fire and seemed to be in a good mood. 
Lots of crowd interaction.  There's a humorous part at the beginning of disc two
where Mike brings a youngster (13years old) onstage to show the next 
generation of punk fans and the kid just steals the show!!

  Jannus Landing is a small outside venue in downtown St. Pete. Usually very 
easy to get a "rig" into.   The show was sold out and the crowd was pretty well -
behaved. ...note to self : take two MORE steps back from the mosh pit while recording.
At one point, I was dodging heads and elbows. 

  The recording came out pretty good considering it was a punk show at an 
outdoor venue with moshing, but of course, your mileage may vary. 

Artwork, ticket stub, newspaper ad included.
