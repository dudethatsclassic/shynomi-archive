This is an EXTREMELY RARE recording of Social Distortion.  The first to surface from this leg of the tour that I am aware of.  This show was recorded just three days after Lead Guitarist/Vocalist Mike Ness suffered a broken wrist while skateboarding.  Ron Emery fills in on Lead Guitar.  They also open the show playing the entire Mommy's Little Monster Album!  Enjoy!!!
