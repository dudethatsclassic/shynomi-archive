Social Distortion
May 14, 1992
Campus Club
Providence, RI

FOR TRADING/SHARING ONLY 

Until February 2009, this was a virtually uncirculated recording.  Only one analog copy was made back in '92 and no digital transfer from the master existed.

The show itself was a pretty good one.  I liked the set selection better than when I caught the band on tour with the Ramones about 6 months later, as this one is longer and more diverse in terms of old and (at the time) new material.  There is some audience chatter.  However, it's not constant and it doesn't carry into the songs.

Mike Ness was a bit more talkative during a few song intros, so those are separate tracks.  At least one of these pauses was related to over-aggressive bouncers ejecting people in the crowd - unfortunately a common occurrence at the Campus Club.

Two other things noted below are worth repeating here.  First, the initial 55 seconds of the recording has saturated levels.  Once corrected, the levels are fine.  Second, a tape glitch occurred on the last two songs, so a bit of each song was not recorded.  This is not a cut in/out type of a thing, but rather a quick one-time tape garble on each of those two songs.  It's as if battery power briefly cut out and then returned.
