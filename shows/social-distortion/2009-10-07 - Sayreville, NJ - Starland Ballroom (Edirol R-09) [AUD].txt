   What can I say about Social D.? This was a very special show for me because I 
was on a mission. See I have been recording shows now for over a quarter of a century.
This was my 6th Social Distortion show. But I was hoping it would be my first Social D. 
recording (at one that one could consider listenable.) I ahve had nothing but nightmares 
trying to record this band. But last night was different. Last night was special. The 
person I was supposed to go with stood me up. Her ticket is probably still in the will 
call window. I went in anyway. Jam packed. Sold Out. Crazy crowd surfing, including a guy
in a wheelchair. Surfing with the wheelchair and all. Twice! There were talkers near me on 
tracks 1 & 15. Unfortunately they are noticable on these tracks. And ther is a slight flutter
sound here and there but most noticably on track 23. Otherwise I think it sounds pretty good,
so without further hesitation I present to you:
