Social Distortion - 2023 01 01 @ The Guild, Menlo Park, CA

Band
Mike Ness - vocals, guitar
Brent Harding - bass
Josh Hobe - guitar
David Kaylich - keyboards
David Hildago Jr - drums

Show note:
Rescheduled from November due to a band member having the flu. Johnny Two Bags didn't play as he is recovering from a neck and shoulder injury. Despite all of that, it was a great show. Mike said that it is time for them to go make a new album, and they played a bunch of track from it.  During the encore, Mike told the audience that it was time for the last song, and went into a story about Johnny Cash.  Someone yelled out "Ring of Fire" to which Mike say that they might play two Johnny Cash songs, and launched into Folsom Prison Blues, which was not on the setlist.  For the final song, "Ring of Fire", Mike spotted two kids in the audience and motioned for them to come up on stage, which was really cool. A good way to start the new year.
