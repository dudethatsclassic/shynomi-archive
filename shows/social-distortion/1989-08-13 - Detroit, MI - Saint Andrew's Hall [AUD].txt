Some mastering, EQ and widening done.  

This was an insane show on a hot and steamy, summer night in Detroit. 
They were carrying people out of the pit to waiting ambulances on 
stretchers. The self-titled record that would break the band had not 
been released yet but they played several songs from it. Mike Ness was 
exceptionally chatty and really seemed to appreciate the role Detroit
has played in the history of the band, going back to their movie 
and the song Another State of Mind. I'd seen them 9 months prior at 
a little dump called Blondie's on the west side of Detroit with about 
30 people in attendance.  .  

I haven't seen many shows from this era, and this hasn't been widely 
circulated, if it has at all. This is a new rip from the master tape 
through much better software. It's noisy, the crowd is rowdy, and 
recording conditions weren't ideal, but it's a special one. I'd give
the sound quality a B/B- but any SxDx fan going back to the early days
will dig this unique look at the band right before they hit it big.  .
