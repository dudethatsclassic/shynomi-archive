Presenting recently surfaced master collection of Marco Moir. 
 
Marco never traded via mail, never had a list. He only recorded for himself. 
Back in the day, if you wanted anything he had, friends, Brian Emery and myself, 
you would have to borrow a handful of tapes to take them home to make copies. Therefore, 
several of Marco's recordings have been circulated over the years by Brian and myself. 
Most were never circulated.

Marco released his collection to be shared. 
I have done all the transfers, these are transfers directly off the master cassettes.
Enjoy ~ Wes
