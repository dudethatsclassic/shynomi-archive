One of the more "muddy" sounding recordings so far with the Tascam DR-07. I attribute that to the venue PA as well as the fact that I had to stand on the floor in front of the soundboard, with the crowd in front of me. Great show though, with Social D tearing it up. The new drummer adds a new sound to the band, and Mike Ness was on fire as usual. There were several times where the other musicians had technical problems, but you really can't even tell on the recording.
