Social Distortion - 27th June 2009 
Pitkä Kuuma Kesä Festival, Suvilahti
Helsinki - Finland


Zoom H2 internal mics (120 angle) -> 48/24 -> Goldwave for EQ & levels & cue points etc -> Sony Soundforge for dithering & resampling to 44/16 & Split to tracks


First time ever Social Distortion in Finland and also gig I really wanted to see.
So the taping wasn't THE thing. I was really close to stage surrounded by drunken and dancing  crowd... So there's clapping & singing, but recording is still very listenable. Outdoor gig is always a challenge by itself, so I guess this turned quite okay anyway. And there's not too many 2009 gig's around, so grab this and enjoy. Just be a judge yourself and leave feedback what you think, since there's no point to make any samples cause sound varies a lot during a gig.
