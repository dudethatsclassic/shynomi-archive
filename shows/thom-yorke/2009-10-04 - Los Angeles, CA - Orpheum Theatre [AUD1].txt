I posted some Mp3's of this source a few months ago. This is the first time the lossless files have been shared. 

The amount of time and effort that Thom and company put into rehearsing was quite evident once the show started, and the band really performed as one incredibly cohesive unit.
Being well aware of the level of professionalism that these musicians represent, I went in with fairly high expectations (I'm not at all easy to please gig-wise)
and even though the performance was less than 90 minutes long, I left completely satisfied - feeling not only privledged to have been able to enjoy it, 
but was elated at the thought that I was going to get to see them do it again the following night! 


Sometimes it's just meant to be.
