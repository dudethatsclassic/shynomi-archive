Notes: Monster thanks to the input of those who aided in this recording's creation.  It was a true 
group effort and counters alot of conventional thought about Radiohead fans. This show is really cool
for old/rare things in the set (both Radiohead & Thom solo)and the debut of Present Tense. This 
recording is pretty darned nice recording but it needed some love which it received as follows:

Levels were super low in this raw recording. I hard limited a few noisy spots in some songs and eq'd 
down the bass in some songs as it overpowered everthing else.  All songs were normalized to -0.01db.
I boosted the breaks between songs to equal levels but much less level than songs. All the 
aforementioned done in CEP2.0. I left the breaks long and let them "run over" the songs and faded as
best I could so transitions are smoothe. Fades and final mix down done in Vegas. All timing is true
to the master recording.

Nothin' but love,

form
