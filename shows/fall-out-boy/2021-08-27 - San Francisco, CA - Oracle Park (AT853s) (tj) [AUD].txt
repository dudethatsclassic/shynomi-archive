Notes: Ok so this is one for the more serious collectors only.
My SO bought us tickets to the Hella Mega tour with pretty high up seats so I brought my equipment and rolled the whole night.
I had a lot working against me: bad seats, chatty / drunk neighbors, strong wind, shit AQI, 16 dollar Hazy Little Things, and pretty bad phasing / volume shifts, but I still ran the recorder anyway.
Haven't seen anything really pop up on Dime from this tour so thought someone would appreciate it.
It reflects what I heard, so at least that's accurate.
I aimed the mics at the right repeater since that was the closest speaker to me.
I also taped Weezer, Green Day, and the Interrupters, although the Interrupters I only captured with onboard mics.

In terms of the actual performance Fall Out Boy was great.
Been hearing their music for years so it was nice to finally see them play!
They light of Fireworks several times throughout the night and I think their guitarist might've had a guitar that could shoot flames out of it.

I run a Rage Against the Machine live archive at http://ratm.live.
Please get in touch if you have any recordings of Rage!
