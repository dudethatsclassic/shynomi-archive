Original Notes:
I originally planned to up some MFSL stuff. However, given the uniqueness of this tracker, I thought I would up stuff that would not be allowed on the main tracker. I've decided to start working on a series of live recordings that I personally recorded and mastered. I've made hundreds of live recordings going back to the early 1980's. I'll only be uploading my favorites in terms of sound quality and performance.

I'll kick things off with a show that blew me away. The stars were lined up properly that night and the crowd was very low key, quiet and respectable so as to not ruin a wonderful, passionate and dynamic performance by Low. 

I hope you all enjoy this recording and I hope to see some comments regarding the performance and the sound quality of my recording.
