Notes:
In December 2016 I travelled to Northern Ireland and Ireland to attend the last three shows of Low's Christmas tour, in Belfast, Dublin and Kilkenny. Erik Koskinen and Gaelynn Lea were the support acts, and Erik also joined the band during their first set.
Never got around to sharing the recordings of these gigs, until now. The support acts and other dates will be shared at a later stage. 
Of all the three dates that I attended, the Kilkenny one stood out the most for me. Wonderful venue, great crowd, great ambiance, the band were in joyous spirits and the recording came out great. Also the town itself is very nice, if you ever are in the neighbourhood, I would very highly recommend to take a tour! 
There's a humming noise to be heard at the start of the set (which was also heard during the supports), coming from one of the speakers, or amps on stage. It got fixed after a couple of songs. The bar was pretty loud, so you can hear in the background the slamming of fridge doors and rumbling of glasses. But once you get used to it, it adds to the ambiance.
