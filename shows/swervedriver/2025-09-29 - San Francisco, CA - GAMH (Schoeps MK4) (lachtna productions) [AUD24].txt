Swervedriver - 2025 09 29 @ Great American Music Hall, San Francisco CA

Note:
Was really excited for this show, I missed the Slide festival in LA, which I wanted to catch, but a full Swervedriver set is always better than a festival set. The show started a little late, seems like "Plan 7 Star Satellite 10" had to loop as they were adjusting amps. Good crowd, everyone was very into the set, no stupid chatter or anything, one of my favorite sets of this year. Oh, and it was incredibly loud, maybe one of the loudest shows I have seen at Great American Music Hall.
