Swervedriver
January 26, 1992
Club Babyhead
Providence, RI

DO NOT SELL - FOR TRADING/SHARING ONLY 

This is an excellent recording thanks not only to a great sound mix, but also that the crowd allowed me the opportunity to tape from a perfect spot close to the stage without worry of a mosh pit erupting or a body flying my way.  No loud talking, either.

I first heard of Swervedriver the year before thanks to a tape-trading friend, Paul, who visited from the Netherlands.  He had picked up the Raise LP during his travels and it impressed me to the point that I knew I had to be on the lookout to see this band live.  As luck would have it, Swervedriver soon came to a tiny club in my area near the start of the band's US tour.  The show was ever better than I had imagined.  The set even included an encore of Jesus.

On a side note, this was the night of Super Bowl XXVI (Washington Redskins over Buffalo Bills 37-24).  It was weird to get to the club a bit early, find it nearly empty and then see the place packed in a matter of minutes after the game ended.  Even shoegazers watch football.
