This was taped with two pzm mics (supposedly on the wall) into a tascam portastudio by a friend. The tape basically sat in a box for a couple of years and then he gave it to me. Another two years went by, and I made a torrent of it. That's basically the lineage. This was the only night I ever got to see them live so it holds a tender spot. It suffers from the typical cassette type defects, but it was a fantastic show, and Im glad it got captured at all. As far as transfer, I took the first gen tape (arrgh) and made 16 bit 44.1 wavs in audition. From there, I did nothing but set track markers. It could use some eq work here and there and might be made a bit better, but I left it as is straight from the tape. Any restorative suggestions would be much appreciated to make this sound a bit better, beyond basic eq that is.
