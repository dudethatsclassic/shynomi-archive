My first time seeing Finger Eleven since April 9th, 2007 (when they were
opening for Chevelle). Crazy, right!? I used to see them (and tape them!)
quite a bit between 2000 and 2007, but I kind of fell out with them around
the "Them vs. You vs. Me" record. Just didn't dig the direction they were
going in. Ironically, its when they scored their highest charting single
with "Paralyzer". Go figure. I will always stand behind their first 2 
records as some of my favorites from that era. My main attraction to this
show was seeing BRKN LOVE, but knowing F11 were headlining was a nice
addition, hoping they would play some older songs. Thankfully, they did!
Their newer stuff sounded pretty good, too! I may have to check out their
new record in November. If you download this show, be sure to check out
their version of "Welcome To The Machine" during the encore, wow!!! The
sound improves after the first song once they have it all dialed in.

BRKN LOVE & Alien Ant Farm opened. [both recorded].
