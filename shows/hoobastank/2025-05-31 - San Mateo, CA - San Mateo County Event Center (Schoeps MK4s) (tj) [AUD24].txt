Notes: what a show. Security wouldn't let anyone actually on the barricade and everyone had to stand a few feet back from it which was patrolled aggressively by some lady who was giving everyone grief. Security actually stood between the crowd and the barricade which seems to defy all logic if the crowd rushed the barricade but hey, what do I know. The band seemed to be in fine spirits and very aware of the type of venue they were playing. Crowd was definitely into them and overall it was a great way to spend the evening.
