Notes:
I had tickets for row W but a severe lightning storm in the area convinced the management
to allow everyone in the uncovered seats and on the lawn to move forward under the roof.
The storm kind of just forked around the place and went on it's way. HFB's overall levels
were noticeably lower than Metric's and Garbage's. That combined with the worst kind of
neighbors a taper could have resulted in some harsh screams and loud clapping at the
very beginning of the set. I had no choice but to manually lower the screams by 20db and
each clap by 10db. Fortunately they settled down once Noel started singing. Nobody
around me seemed to be able to stfu for one single song so there is some nearby chatter
heard thoughout most of the set. I didn't have any of those issues for Garbage though.
I'm so glad I decided to record Tampa as well, those turned out phenominal. Please share
this freely, keep going to live shows and keep taping. Enjoy.
-kevdogg
