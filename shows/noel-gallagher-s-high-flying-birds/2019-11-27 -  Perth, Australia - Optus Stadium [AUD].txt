Noel Gallagher's High Flying Birds

Optus Stadium, Perth, Western Australia (with U2)

Recorded on iPhone using RetroRecorder and Zoom iQ6 microphone. Sound is not great, but it is actually a good capture of the sound at the venue.  I was in the very top row, as far from the stage as it was possible to be.
