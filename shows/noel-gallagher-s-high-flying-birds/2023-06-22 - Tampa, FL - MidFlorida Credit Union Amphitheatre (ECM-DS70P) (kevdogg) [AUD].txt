Notes:
This amphitheatre is huge. Only about half the back section was occupied. I had a quiet family
in the seats behind mine but aside from that, nobody else within 20-30 feet. Made for a great
recording of an awesome show.  Noel was much more talkative than the night before and I
apologize for my laughter during his comments before Masterplan. He says of his '90's music
"Yeah, you fucking love that shit". He also seemed a bit bothered by his image cast upon the
big video screens but it was very nice to have those in the far rows. Stormy Daniels was at the
show and the overall crowd was very much into all 3 bands, it was a great atmosphere. Please
share this freely, keep going to live shows and keep taping. Enjoy.
-kevdogg
