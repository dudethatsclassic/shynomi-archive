Emmylou Harris & the Hot BandEbbets Field, Denver, CO 6-24-75 Late Show (partial)This is my partial tape from Emmylou's second night at Ebbets Field. I was standing on top of a bench in the back right corner of the small club, bracing myself on the ceiling with the same hand that was holding my microphones. I was trying to be inconspicuous but I finally got busted by the house security. I managed to reach into my bag and slip the guy a blank cassette and got away with this. Note that this band includes Rodney Crowell and brilliant guitarist James Burton, whose concert performances are very rare.The filler is from a tape I was given about 30 years ago. Scotty Stoneman's fiddle playing is insanely good.Lineage: Sony TC-152 w/AKG D1000E mics AUD-MC>EQ and compression using T-Racks software>CD Recorded and seeded by Cary Wolfson 1 Cash on the Barrelhead2 You're Still on My Mind3 Tuning4 Coat of Many Colors5 Your Good Girl Is Gonna Go Bad6 If I Could Only Win Your Love7 Sweet Dreams8 Bluegrass rap	9 I Don't Believe You've Met My Baby10 Angels Rejoiced	11 Bluebird Wine12 Boulder to BirminghamEmmylou Harris, guitar, vocal Glenn T.
