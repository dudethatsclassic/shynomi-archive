Eagles of Death Metal - 2024 09 07 @ Potrero del los Park, San Francisco CA

Note:
Free concert put on by Noise Pop, San Francisco Parks Alliance and the Kilowatt Bar. Great weather - sunny and warm, three great bands - what else would you want to do on a Saturday afternoon?  "Heart On" was on the setlist after "Silverlake" but not played. "Ace of Spades" was Lean on Drums and Jennie Vee on bass and vocals only. I took a bunch of picture which are included.
