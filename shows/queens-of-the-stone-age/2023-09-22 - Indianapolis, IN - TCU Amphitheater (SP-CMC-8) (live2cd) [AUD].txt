Notes:

QOTSA returned to touring in '23 after a 5 year hiatus, so seeing them this year
was an absolute must. They did not announce any headliners in Ohio (altho their
first show back was an hour set at Sonic Temple), so I decided on Indianapolis.
"In Times New Roman..." is one of my favorite albums of the year, so I was very
much looking forward to hearing a few of those cuts. Thankfully, they performed
5 of them here in Indianapolis. "Negative Space" is one of my favorites, so I was
happy to get that. The recording went fairly well. I normally do not buy seats
this far back in a pavilion when taping, but I got into the presale late - and
these were all that were left. Soon after, the entire pavilion was sold out, with
only lawn being left. I was pleasantly surprised how good the sound was in 401,
and may consider sitting there for future shows. I got to meet fellow taper,
"ironchef" from Dime, and had a blast talking with him after the show. He taped
from the rear of the pit, so it will be exciting to hear his source, when its
out! I had fairly good neighbors and was able to fix the few screams and yells
in RX7. The weather was near perfect at showtime. "I Think I Lost My Headache"
was on the printed setlist following "Made To Parade", but was skipped.
