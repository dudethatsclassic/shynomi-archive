NOTES:

Much like Pearl Jam the night before, BottleRock 2024 also presented to me my first opportunity, surprisingly enough, to catch Queens of the Stone Age live (and that was a major deciding factor, alongside Pearl Jam, for me to buy tickets and make the trek for the weekend's festivities). I had missed them at previous festivals, though I had seen other Josh Homme projects before such as Them Crooked Vultures and Post-Pop Depression.

I camped out all day at the Verizon Stage (primarily for this set, though Offspring too), getting my spot at 11:45am, and not moving afterwards (right in front of the soundboard at the inside corner). Naturally, fellow taper and friend from DIME, thomascirca, found his way to the same stage/spot and we ended up getting to hang out all day and shoot the shit, so that made the day/time between sets pass much quicker.

I thought the crowd for Queens would be much larger, but I suppose Ed Sheeran on the main stage really did pull the mass of people his way (though I saw a few people with Sheeran shirts on who stayed for the entire Queens set instead - strange). Halfway through the set, I practically had no one in front of me, which made for good views, and more importantly, no talkers! A festival miracle!

It still boggles my mind how little crappy Napa has a giant music festival, and this, of all places, is where I finally got to see Queens of the Stone Age. Pretty amazing I must say. And also, quickly, I had looked at previous setlists of the last few weeks leading up to this show, and wasn't seeing much of anything from their first/self-titled album being played. I told thomascirca that I would love, though had no hopes of hearing, to hear either "If Only", "Regular John" or "Avon", and he told me there was a good chance they would actually play those. I shook my head and smiled, thinking there was no fucking way, and then what happened? They opened with "Regular John". Another festival miracle. Thank you for talking it into existence!
