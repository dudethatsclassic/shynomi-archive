The Warfield has three tiers in the general admission area.  The first tier was pretty wild and had all the moshers.  I stood on the right side of the second tier right at the rail.  Therefore, there was nobody in front of me at head level.  Mercifully, there were no knuckleheads around me so the recording is free of chatting or woots and hollers.

I think that the sound quality of the recording is pretty darn good.
