Show notes:
Listen, I would have to say I'm a fair-weather Queens of the Stone Age fan so I can't rave about the show as much an Uber-fan.  But I can say that the two hour long pit I saw on the floor was pretty impressive and I also enjoyed what the band was offering up in terms of music as well as some funny banter.  So...it was a good show!  With my limited knowledge of their discography I would say it was a good mix of their discography as well as their new album.
