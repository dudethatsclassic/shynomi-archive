Show Notes: A great show, despite Josh admitting that he was throwing up all night the previous night due to food poisoning. If he hadn't told us I'd have never have guessed. This was a fun one to attend and to listen to. Any Queens Of The Stone Age fan will enjoy this one.

Recording Notes: I recorded this from the seats at the back of the sterile Margaret Court Arena, and it came out really well, it's an excellent sounding recording in my humble opinion. The recording was clipped (it was loud!), so I fixed that in iZtope RX7, and gave it some punch in Ozone 9, but it didn't need much, it already sounded really good. A pretty respectful crowd around me, the worst interference comes from me coughing every so often. I'm not sure why I didn't share it on Dime back in 2018 as it's a really good show and recording, but here it is for you now. Enjoy!
