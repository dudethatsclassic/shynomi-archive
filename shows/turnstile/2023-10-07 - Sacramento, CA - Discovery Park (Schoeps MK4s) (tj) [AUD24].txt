Fantastic headlining set by Turnstile. Unfortunately there was hardly anywhere there as folks were camping out at the other stage to see Korn so it was pretty sparse. I had the whole soundboard rail to myself which is a first for a headlining set. Either way, Turnstile plowed through their set and crushed it as per usual. Sounded fantastic and is probably the best festival sound I've heard from them.
