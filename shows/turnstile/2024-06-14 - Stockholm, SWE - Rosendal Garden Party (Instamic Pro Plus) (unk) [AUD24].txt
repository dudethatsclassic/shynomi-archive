Day 1 of this festival had a huge setback when Refused cancelled their appearence due to Dennis Lyxzén's heart attack the day before. Turnstile were supposed to play in the afternoon but now became the main attraction of the first day as they were the only heavy band on the bill. Bassplayer Freaky Franz Lyons played the whole gig wearing a Refused hoodie in tribute to Dennis Lyxzén.
