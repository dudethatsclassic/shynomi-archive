Notes:

Failure out supporting their new LP, "In The Future Your Body Will Be The
Furthest Thing From Your Mind". Setlist touched on all eras of the band,
which was great. I loved how they ended the show with 2 Comfort songs. 
Getting to hear 6 from the new record was also a great surprise. The band
sounded flawless as ever. The crowd around me were pretty tame. I had a
girl behind me to my left who kept "whoo'ing!" between songs, but I went
through and deleted almost all of them. I even removed her shouting request
for "Paralytic Flow". The room could have sounded better, but it is what
it is. Turn it up and please go tape Failure if you have the chance!
