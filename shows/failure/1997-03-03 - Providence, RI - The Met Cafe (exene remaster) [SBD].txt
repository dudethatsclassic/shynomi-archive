***Remastered by exene***

Great show by a criminally underrated band.  Buy their albums.  

They throw in the riff from All The Young Dudes at the end of The Nurse Who Loved Me, nice touch.

It was already a solid sbd, but levels were low, quiet.  Too quiet...
Now boosted and balanced, a few high freqs enhanced, sounds much more punchy.
Also fixed a few annoying skips and pops at the start of several tracks.

******************************************

I'm pretty positive this is soundboard.
I think they were taping it for the possibility of
releasing a live album. At the beginning Ken Andrews
is talking and it sounds like it's studio vocals at
the beginning segue-ing into the first song.
I hope some people around like this band because they're
possibly my favorite and the sound quality on this is
amazing (although the volume is a little low, so you have
to turn it up) Anyone who isn't familiar with Failure might
recognize the last song of the set as covered by A Perfect
Circle. When I saw them open for Tool Maynard said they
were his favorite band and has done work with them in various
side projects. Also Troy Van Leeuwen the touring guitarist is now in
Queens of the Stone Age and was in A Perfect Circle.
Greg plays guitar in Autolux and also Ken played the guitar solos
on the first Tenacious D cd. 

I highly recommend this to everyone.
