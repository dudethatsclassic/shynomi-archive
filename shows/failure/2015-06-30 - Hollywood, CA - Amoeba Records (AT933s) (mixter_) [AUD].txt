Failure is...

Ken Andrews - Vocals, Bass
Greg Edwards - Guitar
Kellii Scott - Drums



The performance:

The L.A. based trio performs in front of 250+ ardent fans and passerbys at Amoeba Music in Hollywood. The 
band returns in support of their long awaited follow up to 1996's "Fantastic Planet" titled "The Heart Is 
A Monster". They play a rather succint half hour set comprised of six songs, including three from the new 
album. The band is presently touring in support of the new album. They seem primed to take advantage of 
the current "Grunge Renaissance" along with Soungarden, L7, Local H and Mudhoney.



The recording...

A very solid pull (IMHO) 50' directly from the center ceiling stack. I was about 2 aisles further back than 
I had hoped to be. Hence, a modest amount of clapping between numbers had to be carefully attenuated. One 
individual directly to my left was blowing out my levels. Thankfully, his excitement was restrained and 
isolated between songs. In fact, this particular audience was surprisingly respectful. Clearly those that 
turned out for a Tuesday afternoon show were there to listen to the music and not to drink and socialize. 
I'll be 50 this year (God willing), so here's my Old Man Rant... "Back in my day, we used to go to concerts 
to listen to music. We didn't have no Twitface. And if we carried a stick, it was to poke someone, not to 
take their picture. Now Get Off My Lawn!!!" In all seriousness, it sounds great!



Of note...

My first experience with Failure was through their side cover project Replicants. They were cool!
