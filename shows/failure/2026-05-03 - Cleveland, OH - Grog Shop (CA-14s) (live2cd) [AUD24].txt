Notes:

Such an absolute treat getting to see Failure return to the road for the
first time since 2022. The real surprises in the setlist were the return
of "Solar Eyes", "Smoking Umbrellas" & "Undone" - which were last played
regularly in 2019. They also played 3 tracks from their brand new record,
"Location Lost", which released a week prior to this concert. This show
sold out shortly after it went onsale (with a capacity of only 400), so
the room was elbow to elbow. I decided to mount the rig high on a pillar
above the board directly in line with the right stack. The recording ended
up being a tad bass heavy, so I did EQ a bit in post. I thought it turned
out relatively well given the circumstances! Failure do not tour very often,
so any chance we have to see them live is a gift. Please go out - support,
buy their new record and watch their great recent documentary, "Every Time
You Lose Your Mind: A Film About Failure" streaming on Hulu and on bluray.
