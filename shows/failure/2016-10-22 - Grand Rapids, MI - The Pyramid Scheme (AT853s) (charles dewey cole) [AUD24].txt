Notes:

To celebrate the 20th anniversary, Failure embarked on a short 19 date tour performing
their seminal 1996 classic, "Fantastic Planet" front to back in its entirety. Failure
are now in their 3rd year back since reforming and have already released a stellar new
record, "The Heart Is A Monster". Since they are focusing on FB for this tour, only one
song from THIAM made the setlist - featured in the encore. The Pyramid Scheme's capacity 
is a mere 425 and this (not surprisingly) sold out months in advance.

Recording the recording, the crowd was very respectful and the screaming and cheering
were kept to a minimum around me. I was very close to the left speaker and held my head
still for the hour and a half trying to get the best possible recording. I am very
pleased with the results and this may be, in fact, my favorite recording I've made all
year. Huge props to Failure's live sound engineer and the head of house at The Pyramid 
Scheme. Without the exceptional sound system, this recording would not sound nearly as 
pristine as it does. Turn this one up!
