Notes:

Show was sold out at max capacity of 700. This was my first time seeing Failure play
Chicago and it was quite special. The crowd loved every minute of this after having 
not seen Failure tour since 2019. The new material sounded amazing live and encoring
with the final 6 from "Fantastic Planet" sent this show into legendary territory for
me. Special thanks to the 2 or 3 fans behind me who offered to block for my stand on
the floor!! Without you guys, taping would not have been as easy in a sold out room.
We had no trips, bumps, knocks, nothing! The Bottom Lounge almost always sounds great
and this was no exception. Very happy with the recording. Enjoy and please support
the band, buy their merch and most importantly, see them live!
