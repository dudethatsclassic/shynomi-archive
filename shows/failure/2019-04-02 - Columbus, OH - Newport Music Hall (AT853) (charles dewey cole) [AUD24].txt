Notes:

Failure out supporting their new LP, "In The Future Your Body Will Be The
Furthest Thing From Your Mind". Setlist touched on all eras of the band,
which was great. This was my second time catching them on this tour and
the sound was much better in the Newport than the previous at Spirit in
Pittsburgh. The only (small) gripe I had was the omission of "Macaque"
from the encore, as we got it in Pittsburgh. But, like I said, that was
very small. It happens to be a favorite song from "Comfort". Turn this up!
Go out & see these guys!
