Notes:

Very sparse crowd in Cincinnati, but everyone who attended were clearly hardcore
into the band. Out of a full capacity nearing 400, I would say the crowd was 
just under 100. Other than Ken's guitar being out of tune, leading to a restart 
of "Stuck On You", everything sounded great. Very strange seeing "Pillowhead" &
"Heliotropic" omitted from a headlining set, but those are small gripes from an 
otherwise excellent show. "Undone" was a nice surprise!
