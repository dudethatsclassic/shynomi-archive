A lot of people aren't familiar with Failure or the excellent albums "Comfort", "Magnified" and "Fantastic Planet" that they released during the 90's. Most folks , however, are familiar with Tool, Queens Of The Stone Age, A Perfect Circle, and Replicants. All of these bands have a connection to Failure, either thru it's members or it's great songs. Look up their Wiki entry and you'll be amazed at the numerous connections Ken Andrews and Troy Van Leeuwen have to a huge amount of mighty fine music. This set is comprised mostly of material from "Fantastic Planet". Enjoy.

2. Leo

4. Smoking Umbrellas
5. Frogs
6. Another Space Song
