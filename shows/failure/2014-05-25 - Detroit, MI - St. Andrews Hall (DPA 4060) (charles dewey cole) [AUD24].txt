Notes:

Like a lot of fans, I never got to see Failure in the 90's, so this was a must-see
concert anywhere within 300 miles. Thankfully, they booked a Michigan show. Maybe
they will hit my home turf in Ohio on the next go-round. I havent been this blown 
away by a band (for the first time) in awhile! The performance was amazing. My 
introduction to Failure was through Year of the Rabbit in 2003 and while I saw Ken 
with YOTR, this was on a completely different level. The show was not sold out so 
the crowd around me was light and spaced out a bit. I did not have too many problems 
while taping the show. 

"Bernie" was not listed on the setlist and was added at the encore.
