Notes: Slide Away Music Festival, night 1 of 2.
Awful sound the first night, the brickwalling is entirely on the mix at the Palladium.
Supposedly much better night 2, but I didn't bother to attend.
The sound calms down a few songs into it but the first 20-30 minutes were extremely loud.

Escape From New York [John Carpenter] was the intro music and I dropped from 15 on the A10 to 12 since I realized the band would be much louder than Nothing who proceeded them.
I recorded Waves (Hum's first song) at level 12 but dropped it to 11 during the gap before The Scientists.

Only uploading for completeness and a friend helped me track it out.
2015-09-17 remains my favorite recording of theirs.
