Hum - 2026 05 30 @ The Palladium, Hollywood CA

Note: 
2nd of two sold out shows at the Hollywood Palladium for Slide Away 26 festival. This was a surprise when it got announced, when Bryan St. Pere died in 2021, I thought that was all. The show was loud, the pit got crazy, and I basically fought to keep my spot. There's some crowd noise, and some annoying dudes talking through Cloud City, but this recording holds up well which may be the last ever Hum concert. Bonus points for hanging with fried_morals for part of the festival.
