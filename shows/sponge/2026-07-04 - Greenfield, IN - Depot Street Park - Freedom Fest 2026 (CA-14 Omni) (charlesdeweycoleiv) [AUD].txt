Notes:

This was a very frustrating day to put it mildy. I lived 3 hours away and
made sure to be at the park by 2pm to get a great spot to set up the gear
and chairs. I knew there would be a ton of people coming. I got the exact
spot I wanted and then we waited in the 95 degree sun all afternoon with
gatorade and umbrellas to try to stay shaded. The first band, Revealed
(an acoustic 2 piece playing 90s covers) took the stage at 4 and they were
fairly enjoyable. Red Not Chili Peppers came on at 530 and they were 
really good, I'm really glad I taped their set. Then, Sponge took the
stage at 7pm. Halfway thru their set the skies started getting gray, so
everyone started checking the radar on their phones. It did not look good
around 815, but it was still possible it would go north. Well, it did
not go north and hit us directly. It wasnt just a rain shower, it was a
full on heavy thunderstorm with everyone running for cover. Sponge ended
their set fairly quickly at 8pm while the stage hands started covering
everything up. I did the same with my gear thinking it would pass and
Candlebox would play. Well, the rain was so bad (with flooding in the
area) that the show was cancelled 30 minutes into the storm. But, we got
a great hour set from Sponge (featuring the song, "Rainin'" 2nd to last),
which actually ended up being a bad omen. Im still trying to laugh about
that one. I ended up passing on The Wallflowers in my home of Columbus
as well as Switchfoot and Fuel down in Cincinnati (both shows went on),
but you can never predict the weather... I just lost out this year.

Opening for Candlebox [rained out]. Revealed and Red Not Chili Peppers
[recorded] were also on the bill.
