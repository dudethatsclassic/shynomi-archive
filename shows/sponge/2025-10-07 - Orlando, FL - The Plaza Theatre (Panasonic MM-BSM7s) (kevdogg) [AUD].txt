Notes: 
Opening for Local H and Everclear on the Sparkle & Fade 30th anniversary tour.
All were recorded. I was quite disappointed in the shortness of their set but I sort
of made up for it by catching a second show on the 12th in Ponte Vedra. This was
an awesome bill and it's been a successful tour for all three bands. Good stuff.
I was getting some mic connection static during the intro music but luckily it was
resolved by the time they started Wax Ec~static~. In Audition I repaired the static
bursts, gave a subtle increase in the 2-6.3khz range with the EQ and a hard limit
to -.1db with a 9db boost. I am quite pleased with the results. Cover art is included
in the torrent. Please share some more Sponge if you have it! Enjoy the show.
