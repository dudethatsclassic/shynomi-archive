Notes:

First time seeing Sponge in 3 years - hard to believe. They were only
given 30 minutes and they used their 30 minutes in a strange way. They
walked out to a 2 min intro (which they've been using for years), they
spent time during the song "Glue" going Facebook live (another thing
they've been doing for years) and extended "Plowed" for 2x the length
of the actual song with a call and response at the end. Had they just
cut the bullshit and played, they could have easily fit in an extra 2
songs. I would have loved to have heard "Drownin'", "Rainin'", or
"Have You Seen Mary". So - odd choices, but they were still awesome -
they always are.

Opening for Everclear. Local H was also on the bill. [both recorded].
