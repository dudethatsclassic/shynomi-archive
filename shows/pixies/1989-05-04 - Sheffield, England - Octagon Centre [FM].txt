This is a show that is already on dime but in a different format. The one previously available is from a bootleg disc and has the tracks in a different order. It also sounds to me as if the recording is not as good (it sounds slow to my ears), but I'll leave that to you to decide.

This copy is from a radio broadcast and is only about 30 minutes. It is from a tape given to me many years ago by Stu1201 (hi Stu) and is presumably 2nd or 3rd generation - it is very good quality) Stu put three songs from Sheffield on the same tour on the end, so I've left them on. I've also left on the DJ outro as I love the accent.
