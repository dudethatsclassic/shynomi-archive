Info: The last show before Bossanova was released. Also apparently the first time Cecilia Ann and Here Comes Your Man were ever played live.
I have 2 different sources of this show. There is a cut in the middle of There Goes My Gun. The other source has the cut in Something Against You. The sound on this one is a little muffled - likely from the recording location. Also has audience chatter.
The Pixies Live Guide (https://sites.google.com/site/pixiesliveguide/) lists this as a one-off show. It also only lists one recording source, however, as I've mentioned above, I have 2 different sources, and neither matches the lineage listed on the site. Is there a third source?

My copy came as part of a very extensive (100+ cd's) collection of Frank Black and Pixies recordings I have recently acquired from a long-time collector. Most of the Frank stuff has not seen much circulation, and I will endeavor to get through some/most of it in time.
