* With Parkington Sisters (2x violins, keyboards, backing vocals)

I don't know what I can say about this show other than it was magical.  Tanya Donelly's bout of laryngitis and technical difficulties would do nothing to take away from the overall magic of the performance.  Gail did an awesome job of acting as the MC and the Parkington Sisters (violins, keyboards, backing vocals) added to making this one extra special.  A wondrous version of "Seal My Fate" was the hightlight here, imho, even with Tanya's voice cracking.  "Low Red Moon" was also fantastic.  Of the new songs, "Remember" was my favorite.

Last, this recording came out really, really good.
