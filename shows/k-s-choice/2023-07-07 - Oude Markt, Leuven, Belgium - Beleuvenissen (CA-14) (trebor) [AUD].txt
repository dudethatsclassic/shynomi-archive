The bass from the sound system was far too present during this concert.
It was not at all pleasant to listen to live! 
Luckily I had hearing protection! 
On the other hand, I am quite satisfied with the rendering of the recording. 
However, I slightly lowered the bass in post-production. 
It's up to you to adapt according to your wishes with the equalizer of your audio player.
Free outdoor festival and therefore enthusiastic people partying, applause, comments,... but nothing too disturbing! Pleasant concert to listen to!



Recording at 24bit/96kHz, R09HR input level at 33.
Post production: paragraphic EQ remove low rumble below 80 hz, volume & balance adjusted, fade in (tracks 01 & 23) / fade out (tracks 22, 23 & 24), 96-24 to 44-16, tracks created and tagged with SoundForgePro.
Tracks in wav --> Flac 8, Align on sector boundaries using fix and .ffp with Trader's Little Helper.
