Comments: 

K's Choice, in their thirtieth year, are musically at a peak, even if the voice of Sam (Sarah) has unfortunately lost its uniqueness due to the sex change; the emotiveness, the passion are stronger than ever, the timbre continues to be heard, but the timbre has changed very clearly. Nevertheless, every minute is a joy and the show is worth it for We Are Glaciers alone. The OLT Rivierenhof is one of the most beautiful open air venues I've had the pleasure of experiencing, and the standing room directly in front of the board at the end of the tiers is wonderful for recording (if the right people are around). Many (quiet) conversations, the one or other scream, but otherwise a very good recording.
