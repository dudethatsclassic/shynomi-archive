Taper's Notes:
A pretty decent recording from the pit. Middle of the field, halfway between
the stage and soundboard. Lots of chatter in the beginning but it improves
throughout the show. This was a concert organized to raise $$$ for the
American Red Cross shortly after the many Florida hurricanes of 2004.
Other bands on the bill were Saliva, Nonpoint, Skindred, Authority Zero,
Shinedown and Chevelle. I got all except Nonpoint and Skindred but I'm
aware another taper has those recorded. Ed Roland climbed the lighting
rig in Bono-circa-'83 fashion at one point in the set. That was the one and
only time I've seen such a thing at a concert; pretty cool and he probably
got in some trouble for doing it. Cover art is included in the torrent. Enjoy.
