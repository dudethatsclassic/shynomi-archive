Notes:
A lot of people try to list this show as occuring in 1998, but that is not the case.
According to the band's tour schedule, this show happened on 7/04/97.
They played no show in 1998. This was allegedly a free show in Grant Park in
Chicago as part of the Petrillo Band Shell Festival. It featured Big Head Todd
& The Monsters, Collective Soul, Jayhawks and Liquid Soul.
