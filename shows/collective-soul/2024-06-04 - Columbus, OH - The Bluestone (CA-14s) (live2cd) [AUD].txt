Notes:

* =  Originally Recorded By AC/DC.

Collective Soul are currently out on tour opening for Hootie & The Blowfish.
They scheduled 6 headliners on days off to fill their schedule. These were as
follows: Columbus (what you are listening to), Erie, Omaha, Evansville, Grand
Rapids and North Myrtle Beach. So instead of their 60 minute opening set, we 
were blessed with (almost) 90. Now, anyone who has followed Collective Soul on
tour for the past 10 years knows that they don't really do anything special.
They have a static setlist they have stuck to (the hits) and will pepper in
a new song or two when they have an album out. No different here. We got 3 new
songs (which was nice) plus their standard GH setlist that hasnt changed in
forever. They also threw in a cover of AC/DC's "Dirty Deeds", which I felt was
pretty weak, but it is what it is. I just didnt feel it fit Ed's voice. It was
nice to see they dropped the R.E.M. cover they had been doing for over 4 years
prior, tho. Im hopeful one day CS will play more of their back catalog and bring
back some album cuts we never hear anymore. I really miss those live. Thats what
made their shows so great in the 90's. I say this every time I record in this 
space, but the sound in The Bluestone sucks. Its an old church with horrible
acoustics. I was 20ft back from the left hanging PA (using cards!) and it sounds
like I was down the hall around the corner in the bathroom. For some reason,
living in Columbus more and more of my favorites get booked here. Lucky me.
