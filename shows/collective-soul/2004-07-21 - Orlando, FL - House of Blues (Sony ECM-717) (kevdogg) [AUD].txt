Notes:
Titled "Orlando Pt. 2" because this was their second show in Orlando that year. They actually returned
again that October for the hurricane relief festival. The last 2 tracks are of Ed in the WJRR studio just
a few hours before the show. He ends the interview with a solo acoustic version of Satellite. At the show
I found a great spot, visually, in front of the left pa stack. Mic was clipped to my belt, I had HOB security
for a neighbor the entire show. I was a rookie taper then, experimenting with different spots in the HOB;
which turned out to be on the steps just to the right of the soundboard if you're curious. I thought being
that close to the p.a. would drown out the crowd noise more and sound better overall :/ I also wanted to
try for once recording in mono to eliminate the need for a risky disc swap. So I recorded this one in mono
at the MZ-R50's default input level and just copy/pasted to make it stereo in ProTools. Check the samples
before proceeding with download. Cover art is included. Enjoy another noisy one!
