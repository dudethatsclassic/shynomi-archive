Notes:
What a killer show this was. Be warned that it is just like being there, in the pit near the front rail with
a couple of girls right behind you that are really into the Roland brothers. This was recorded back
when I didn't understand the importance of being out of the squashed pit and away from the rowdies.
Otherwise it's an excellent setlist and obvious the band is going full throttle the whole way through.
There are fades between tracks 17 & 18 so be mindful if you're going to -burn-. Sorry, I hadta do it....
Cover art is included in the torrent. Enjoy this noisy trip down memory lane!
