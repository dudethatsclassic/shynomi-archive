Notes:
This is the first date of the official See What You Started Tour. No need to mention exactly how much
ass they kicked but you shall hear for yourself. Setlist featured 6 songs from the brand new album,
which wasn't even released yet at the time, a brand new song "Looking Around", as well as some live
rarities such as "Scream" and "Pretty Donna". "Shine" was performed with Ed sitting at the electric
piano for a VERY refreshingly different take on the song. It was a packed house and the crowd was
feeding back all the energy the soul machine was giving. Pics are included in the torrent for the sake
of anyone wanting to make coverart. Please do not seed elsewhere or sell this recording.
