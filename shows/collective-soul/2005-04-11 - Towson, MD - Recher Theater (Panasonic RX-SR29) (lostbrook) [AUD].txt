Collective Soul - The Lostbrook Tape Series Volume 87



Collective Soul has a loyal following in Towson, MD.  This 
was their second sold-out show at the Recher Theater in a 
few short months.  I thought their latest release, "Youth" 
was extraordinary, so I traveled two hours to see them for 
the first time.  In my opinion, all eleven songs on "Youth" 
could have been legitimate hits. The knowledgeable, enthusiastic 
crowd was easily coaxed into some memorable sing-a-longs, and 
a good time was had by all.
