Notes:

Such a wonderful show held at The Toledo Museum of Art Peristyle Theatre. I've 
lived in the area now for close to 13 years and this is the only rock band I 
could remember scheduled to play there. I was 18 when I recorded this and was just
getting the hang of taping shows. This has always been one of my personal favorites. 

IMHO, this is the pinnacle of Collective Soul touring behind their best work to 
date, 1999's Dosage. I also included some television performances to round out the
2CD set. They were captured on MD (as well) straight from a stereo Hi-Fi VCR. 

* = Originally Recorded By Ozzy Osbourne.
^ = Originally Recorded By Elton John.

Tracks 5, 6 & 7 are sourced as follows:
track 5: Taken from NBC's Tonight Show with Jay Leno - February 12th, 1999.
track 6: Taken from NBC's Late Night with Conan O'Brien - April 2nd, 1999.
track 7: Taken from NBC's Tonight Show with Jay Leno - September 17th, 1999.
