This was the first time I had ever seen Collective Soul. I tried seeing them in 1995
and 1996 (both in Toledo), but happened to be out of town that week in 1995 (for Rally
By The River) and then they cancelled the 1996 concert (at the Asylum) a day prior when
given the chance to be a late fill in on Letterman. So, with the release of Dosage in
1999 and knowing my friend Steve was driving up to Detroit, I rode along! In the 90's,
I was using a handheld stereo mic with minidisc and got pretty decent results given
the location. This was my third visit to The State Theatre (the 2 prior visits were in
the balc), so I stood roughly 20 feet back from the right stack and just pointed the 
mic upwards. The crowd was pretty loud at times, but overall, its a great capture
considering how long ago it was. The Dosage era was the best time to be a CS fan and
I still consider it their best album so this is a recording I go back to often!
I also saw them in Toledo (recorded) and Jones Beach (not recorded) later on in 1999.

Marvelous 3 opened. [recorded, but lost the MD's].
