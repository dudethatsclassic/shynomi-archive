I really enjoyed this show. I thought the new songs were great and fit right In with the hits. On Goodnight Good Guy
Shane Evans the original drummer came out and played the drums so that was pretty cool. There is nothing fancy about
these guys they just let the the music be the focal point of the show and rock out.

I had forgottent to replace my batteries from recording the Evanescence show a couple of days prior so I did switch the recorder back
 to stamina mode and only recorded in 16 bit mode. 

Enjoy!
