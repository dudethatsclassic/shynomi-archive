Collective Soul headlined the "Just Looking Around 2021" tour. Opening
were Better Than Ezra and Tonic. The set is pretty standard, but it did
feature a brand new song, "Who Loves" - which opened the set. The sound
was better than the previous couple times the band has played the Rose.
I did reduce the bass a touch in post and removed some claps and screams
when needed in iZotope RX7. Hopefully these guys release some more new
stuff in 2022 and beyond!
