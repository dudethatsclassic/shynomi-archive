I can't believe this band is still kicking *ss. This was a small venue in Beaumont,Texas
that really got crowded by the time CS hit the stage. I had previously seen Collective
Soul in Houston almost a year ago at the verizon wireless theatre (06.06.05). They ran
through their hits and played some of their new songs. They took the stage late, like at
ten thirty at night. If you're a fan you will appreciate how much the band sounds live as
well as their studio albums. If you're not a fan, you're missing out on a great band! The
recording has a lot of bass, I was center stage about 25 feet back from the band and
by the end of the concert, I must have been like 5 foot away from the band. The sound
is almost a little hot, due to the heavy bass. During Home, there was some distortion
due to the band,not the recorder. Don't know what happened, too much bass? or
something made the speakers crackle.
All in all, a decent recording from an awesome band.
