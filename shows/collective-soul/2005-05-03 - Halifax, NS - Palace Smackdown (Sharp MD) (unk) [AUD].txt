2005-May 3

Lineage: Custom made mics-->custom battery box with 160hz bass roll-off filter-->SharpMD-->Soundforge-->PC-->EQ levels-->master CD-->FLAC8-->you

I can't remember if I seeded this master recording before, but if I did, here it is again!

Recorded from about 10 feet center stage in the pit. This was the first CS show in Eastern Canada and the crowd was pumped and very vocal. 
The sound mix was bad and the volume was low. The crowd overpowered the amps many times.

The Palace security are on the watch for anyone having fun and promptly throw them out. Track13 on cd1 is called "Fightus Interruptus" as a scuffle breaks out and security move in and remove an innocent bystander. I know this because it was right in front of me. 

If you are looking for a soundboard sounding show, this aint't it!


Recording ends after the first encore track as I ran out of space on my MD. I recorded the opening act (the waking Eyes) as well and only took 2 MDs with me..

On a scale of 1 to 10, this would rate a 6-6.5.
