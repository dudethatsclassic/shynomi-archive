Notes:

* =  Originally Recorded By R.E.M.

Collective Soul scheduled 3 socially distanced shows at Atlanta's Coca-Cola Roxy
earlier this year. Outside of their NYE performance down in FL, these were their
first shows back (proper) since COVID. Ed spoke quite a bit on the past year 
(you will see I gave him 2 separate speaking tracks) and filled everyone in on all
of the recording he's been doing. He performed a few new songs, as well. The band
have a new record "Vibrating" set to be released this year, but the date has not 
yet been set. The new tunes performed in Atlanta may or may not be on that release
as Ed mentioned having 4 unreleased albums to come. For night 2, I was sat at 
table 19 (which was slightly right of the right stack) and under the balcony overhang.
We were allowed to stand and remove our masks during the show, so I stood as far
left as I could in our 4 person seated pod. We had some clappers and whistlers
in the area, so I removed them the best I could in RX 7 Audio Editor. I kept the
clapping in between songs, of course. "Therapy" was played twice. They did the same
every night. We had some talkers in the pod to our right, they are fairly audible
during "Here Today" & "A Letter From You". All 3 nights were recorded! Stand-up 
comedian Paul Ollinger opened all 3 nights with Collective Soul following on stage
promptly at 9pm.
