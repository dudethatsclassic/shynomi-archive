notes from Earthly:
According to Jack Lefton, there is a soundboard/audience tape from this date. The first and second sets both run 87 minutes, and neither is quite complete.

Avreeayl Amen Ra made a longer tape (up to 225 minutes) on the bandstand during this gig. Avreeayl’s tape includes a complete blues in the first set (this is incomplete on the soundboard/audience tape), then follows with most of a “Queer Notions” that is missing from the other tape, picking up partway through the “Prelude to a Kiss,” which is complete on the soundboard recording. At the end of the second set, Amen’s third tape (unfortunately very poorly recorded) continues to the end of “Sunset on the Nile” and another 20 minutes or so through the last two pieces.

Tunes and personnel identified from the soundboard tape by rlc; ct is responsible for the supplementary information from Amen’s tape.

An announcement at the end of the January 5 concert places the Arkestra’s next gig in Birmingham on January 17.
