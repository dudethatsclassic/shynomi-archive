notes from Earthly:
According to Jack Lefton, there is a soundboard/audience tape from this date The first set runs 84 minutes (leaving out the beginning of the improvisation that opened the set; Lefton’s tape picks up with the passage for Hill’s trombone and Radford’s electric bass), and the second is 72 minutes long (it omits most of “Watusi,” including only a couple of minutes for drums and percussion at the end). Tunes and personnel identified by rlc.

Avreeayl Amen Ra also made a tape on the bandstand which has been checked by ct. Amen’s first tape runs 90 minutes from the beginning of the first set to the end of “Sunset on the Nile.” The second tape continues from there to the end of the second set, so it is essentially complete.
