By request, here is the FNM show of December 19, 1992 in Zurich.
I've heard from a few people that this show was uncirculated as of the
last few years.  The quality is variable during the show as the taper is 
in the crowd and seems to be moving around.  The first couple songs 
are pretty rough.  Also the last song, "The Real Thing", as noted on
fnm.com is missing.  My tape cuts out at the end of the 90 minute cassette
with the band coming on for the last encore and making a couple comments to 
the crowd.  Is it missing on all copies except those sourced from the master on
100 minute or longer cassettes?
