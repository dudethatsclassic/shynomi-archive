Here's my own recording of the Faith No More gig at the Hove festival in
southern Norway. It was a good performance, but I was also at Hurricane festival
few days before, and that show was much better (unfortunately I didn't record
it). I still believe that The Real thing is a better set opener than From Out Of
Nowhere. That said From Out Of Nowhere really rocked the hou... err... the sand
field.

As for the quality of the recording I shouldn't be the one to judge it. It's
listenable, but I really couldn't be bothered to stand perfectly still for the
whole duration of the concert. Not when these guys are playing anyway.

I hope you like it.
