Faith No More
Monash University Melbourne Australia
6th May 1993

FM Broadcast -> TDK SA-90 -> Harmon Kardon tape deck -> SB Live -> Audacity -> FLAC

This was a JJJ FM radio broadcast at the time and taped on good equipment by me so it sounds pretty good despite sitting in a box for the last 15 years. Was inspired to transfer this tape after getting the Sheffield SBD of the Angel Dust tour from the Zomb tracker. This tour was really great.

Saw the show in Sydney on this tour and it was pretty good though this show seems a better performance. I do remember them coming out and doing a 'secret" encore when 80% of the audience had filed out with the house lights on of "Lets Lynch the Landlord". Unfortunately I didn't tape in Sydney. I have previously seeded an incomplete audience tape from this tour recorded in Wollongong as well.

Shame its not the whole show... I wish JJJ would release full shows from its archives as they did a really great job recording and mixing them unlike a lot of other FM stations.
