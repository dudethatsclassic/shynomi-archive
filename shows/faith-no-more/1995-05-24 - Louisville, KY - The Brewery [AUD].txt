24th May 1995 - The Brewery, Louisville, KY, USA
DAT master to 1st gen cassette to CDR
Quality : A- / B+
Audience recordings
First and only time the band played "Paranoid" by Black Sabbath.
 
OK enough with the silvers... time to DIG deep into the FNM vaults. Seed it up!!!
 
The story here is that this was the final night of Steel Pole Bathtub opening for FNM. To fuck with the crowd, Steel Pole Bathtub played Paranoid over and over and over and over each time in different tunings, singing styles etc until the place was on the verge of a riot. As you can imagine Patton and Co. loved every minute of it. One of a kind show. Absolute Must Have.  One of the best ever. They start out numerous songs with the paranoid riff to continue fucking with the audience.
