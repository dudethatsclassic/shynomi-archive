About 4-6 one second intervals were edited out due to drop outs on the tape. Other than the end of We Care A Lot being cut, this show is complete.

During Let’s Lynch The Landlord, several fans made their way onstage and were sitting behind the monitors. By the time the Crab Song began, there were hundreds of kids on stage dancing around and diving off the drum riser. You can here my surprise as I was talking to my friend Debbie about it when it happened. We couldn’t see the band anymore there were so many people onstage. The lights came on as soon as the last song was over. I think the Tower staff were shitting about the possibility of a riot. The following tour in ’94, FNM came back to the Tower and Patton invited people onstage and they all sat down and behaved themselves.
