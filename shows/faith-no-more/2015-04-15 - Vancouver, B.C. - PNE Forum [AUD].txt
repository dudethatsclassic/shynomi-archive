The PNE Forum was pretty much sold out.  One of the worst acts I've ever seen opened the show (Christine).  It was so awful that it was almost funny.  Almost.  I saved my tape for Faith No More, who would end up playing 3 new songs live for the first time.  Mike mentioned the Canucks several times as there was a hockey game that night.  He had some funny banter throughout the night.  Someone threw a Bic lighter on stage and he said, "if you're gonna throw a lighter, you better throw a cigarette," and someone threw a smoke up which he lit and smoked for a bit.

Some of Faith No More turned out really good, but during side 1 my mics got covered by my jacket a few times without me realizing it.  I was pretty wrecked that night.  The show barely fit on one cassette.  After the second encore, which was completely unexpected, the tape ran out just after the last song finshed.
