Notes:

taped in front of the left speakers, from approximately 5th-6th row behind the railing. as we knew the sound was going to be a bit more quiet (due to the 75 db limit at the venue/park), i decided FOS would be the way to go. besides, i tend to like FOS recordings: what they might sometimes miss in the width of the stereo image, they give back in having more detailed clarity. 
anyway. it surely wasn't anywhere near too quiet there in the front. the crowd was great so there's some singing here and there (but luckily most of those singing near me were mostly singing in tune - or close enough ;)). really couldn't be avoided, considering the atmosphere in the show. i don't think it's all so bad as i feared - and at least you get the feeling on being there. 
i think the recording turned out pretty good indeed, considering everything and considering it was outdoors anyway.  

enjoy.
