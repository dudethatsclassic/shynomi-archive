Weldermatts notes:

FAITH NO MORE@
Jakki Brambles Session
Maidevale Studios
Radio 1 FM
England

December 4th 1992

Grade: A

Lineage: FM > cass (TDK D90, Type I)(M) > Denon DR-W24HX (w/Azimuth applied) > .wav > CD(M) > .flac > you!

Here's some classic FNM in response to some of the great stuff posted here recently. Broadcast live by BBC Radio One on the jakki Brambles show, this has the boys playing 4 songs in front of a small audience at the BBC's Maidevale studios, with chat in between. The songs are the usual fair, but the banter is highly entertaining. The band sarcastically work their way through topics like trashing hotels, the infamous G'n'R tour and their lack of popularity in the US.

I taped this myself, I don't recall the equipment, but I think I had a beatbox w/FM lined through my amp to my old Marantz deck. I edited live, 'on-line', pausing at the jingles, so all you get is the important stuff! There is some FM hiss, but this is a rock solid 'A'. Thank God I was unemployed at the time and able to tape this!!! (I didn't feel quite so lucky at the time!)

Setlist:
1. Intro
2. Everything's Ruined
3. chat
4. Epic
5. chat
6. Midlife Crisis
7. chat
8. RV

Thanks to all the great FNM and Bungle posters, your time, effort and sharing is really appreciated.
