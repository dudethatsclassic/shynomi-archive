There was a small second hand cd shop back in the city were I used to Live. I used to go often there to find cheap cds, and once in a while you would find something special. 
I found many a bootleg there, and in a way I kind of miss that feeling of finding something tangeable that kind of makes your day.

On this particular show, Surprise! You're Dead starts with just drums and bass, and I always figured someone in the band probably messed up with his gear, seeing that they 
werent getting along during that period. This, of course is pure speculation on my part.
