Notes by hothcanada: I received this labeled as the show from Irvine, CA 1989-09-23. Having seen the video footage of that performance, I can safely say that this audio isn't from that gig. The setlist found here doesn't match that date either. According to fnm.com, this set is in fact: @Pavilion, Concord, CA, USA 1989-09-14. Excellent sound-quality without a trace of the usual analog cassette generation.

Patton has his voice for this one.....he wouldn't the following night at Mountain View (or 9 nights later in Irvine). FNM opened for Metallica & the resident metalheads were far from receptive on this night!!. The crowd "boos" from Epic thru Woodpecker.  

Tapers' comment: "you guys don't know what good music is".

The jeers change to cheers as Kirk Hammett (or possibly James Hetfield) joins-in on "War Pigs":
