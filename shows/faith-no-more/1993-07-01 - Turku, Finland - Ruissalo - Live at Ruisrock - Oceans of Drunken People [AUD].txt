Quality: B+

Not much tape hiss, so I´m positive this is low gen. During The Real Thing and onwards the show gets a 
bit louder (or taper moves or something) and we get a little distortion.

Good, aggressive show. Patton and Roddy try to encourage drunken Finnish people to take a swim in the sea.
The festival is held on a beach, that´s why.

Show highlights: Midlife Crisis has a nice profanity ending. During Easy Patton basically tells a "bootlegger"
to go fuck himself. I guess he saw our taper! Sometime during this show there was a can of home-made booze thrown
on stage and Patton pissed in it and threw it right back to the audience. My guess is that this also happens during 
Easy since he keeps asking "what is it??".

The crowd number was estimated 15000. FNM was headlining.

I included a couple of photos from this show as seen on a Finnish magazine and on the official Ruisrock book.

Mastered to clean the track transitions. Gave it a bit ballsier and clearer sound.
