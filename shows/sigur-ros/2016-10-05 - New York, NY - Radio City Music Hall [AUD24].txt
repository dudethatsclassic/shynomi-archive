The first of four nights for me seeing 
Sigur Rós this fall. on the heels of Austin City Limits the weekend prior, this was a pretty great week for me. I had some minor concern going in that the lack of full backing band would somehow impact my enjoyment, but I could not have been more wrong. I have to thank inez for the ticket and my boss for hiring me and agreeing to let me start, work for a week, and then take a week and a half off to see some shows! 

if you have not had a chance to see 
Sigur Rós please do yourself a favor and check them out live, it is a very moving experience, and I hope you enjoy this tape.

Happy Holidays! here's to a better 2017 with more love and kindness in the world.
