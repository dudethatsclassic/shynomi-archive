Recording Notes:
The show was recorded about 7 rows back and maybe 3 seats left of center. Mics were clipped the the zip 
of a jumper: facing forwards and slightly outwards. I didn't get a chance to check my levels due to 
stealth taping this so I had to raise them later. Because of this there is a bit of a hiss on the recording.
This is only as noticable on the acoustic part of the set. I had time to adjust my levels for the Q+A so I 
only needed to raise the volume +16db.
