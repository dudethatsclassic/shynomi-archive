Taper: inez
Location: 2nd row orchestra center
Lineage: AT831 (SP­-CMC­-2) > SP­-SPSB-11 > Sony PCM-A10 : 24 bit 48 kHz
Editing: de-clap and de-wooooo (IzotopeRx4)
Mastering: loughney [ SoundForge (bass & treble adjustment, volume normalized) ]


Oh my, what a unique show this was!! I didn't really know what to expect exactly and this was just a wild experience. I still can't quite understand how they fit all these people and their instruments on what I always felt was a pretty compact stage - but I personally had a great view... and THE SOUND. My gosh... that orchestral sound in all its glory and THAT VOICE. I think several of us were crying.
I was so totally psyched, I checked the recording same night and even went through de-clapping (and occasional de-woooing and de-whistling) the same night instead of working (just like I planned to work for 2 hours now - but instead I am tracking!! Lol) but I am thrilled how this turned out. Also thanks a million to loughney who agreed to master the recording since I don't really have the tools - and I approached him with this totally out of the blue request!

If you are anywhere near where this production plays - I think there's only 6 shows left (the whole tour is 8...) - try to make it!
