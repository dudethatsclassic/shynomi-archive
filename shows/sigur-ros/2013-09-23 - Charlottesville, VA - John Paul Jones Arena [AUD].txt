A very small crowd witnessed this performance.  An usher told me before the show that only 1500 tickets had been sold.  I had an extra ticket that was difficult to find a home for when my son backed out to study for an important test.  Ethiessen1 agreed to take the ticket but illness prevented him from attending.  My son was finally able to give the ticket away to another UVA student.  A guy I met before the show also had an extra ticket that he couldn't give away outside the venue.  The upper level of the arena was completely empty and was probably closed off.  The lower level was one quarter full at best, and the GA floor about a third.  In the end, Sigur Ros delivered a masterpiece to an arena that should have been filled to the rafters.
