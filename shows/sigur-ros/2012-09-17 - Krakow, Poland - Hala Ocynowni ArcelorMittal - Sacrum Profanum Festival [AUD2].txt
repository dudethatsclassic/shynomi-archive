Last night of the Sacrum Profanum Festival and second performance of Sigur Rós.
Although it was very similar musically, the show itself felt quite different,
though I may not be a good judge on this. There was significantly less lights,
at least in my opinion, but the audience and the band were having a very good
time, just like the previous night. After the show, the band gathered to talk
to the fans, give them autographs and whatnot.

Special thanks to my boyfriend Robert, thanks to whom I had the chance to
experience pretty much the same music with very different feelings accompanying
me (when compared to the previous show).
