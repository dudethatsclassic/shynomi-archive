Here is another Sigur Ros bootleg for you all. This was recorded from 'P3 Live', a swedish radio show, earlier this year. I haven't seen any other source from the show, so this is probably what you'll get. I think it sounds good and quite poppy. I could actually go as far as saying these 7 songs are my favourite SR-songs. Very high standard. Many of you might have not heard of 'Gong' and 'Smáskífa'. These two songs have been played a lot through the 2003-tour. 'Gong' comes with strings & piano and is quite an up-tempo pop-song. 'Smáskífa' is a b-side from the 'Vaka' release. That song doesn't sound very impressive on this one though, it's cut too early and the noise from the crowd ruins the passion and mood of the song. The radio show did not include the songs 'milano', 'Olsen Olsen' and 'hafssol'. Strings on this concert by amina.

I have done my best trying to do a translation of the swedish radio dudes intro (was a bit insecure about the last part):

'icelandic sigur ros is a quite special band. tonights concert is recorded at the roskilde festival last summer. within sigur ros goes up on the stage they lay their mat on the edge of the scene. the bands frontman jónsi appears, takes off his shoes, walks over the mat and the concert can start. 20 000 fans scream and shout while jónsi and the rest of the band plays on. jónsi with his melancholic voice, sings icelandic songs- sometimes with the mic tight into his forehead and sometimes even right into the guitar. together with a nice lightshow this gives the concert a quite special atmosphere. from last years roskilde festival: sigur ros!'
