Recording Notes:
Show was recorded on the left side of the sound desk. About 5ft left and 1ft forward of where the sound guy was standing.
Microphones were clipped to the brim of a hat about 6ft from the ground and spaced 6 inches apart.
My levels were low so I raised the gain during Glósóli. I added more volume to the first song and a half post-production so 
it sounds even the whole way through. During All Alright some guy directly to my left yells out. I lowered that section 
about -15dB so that it sounds even with the other yells.
