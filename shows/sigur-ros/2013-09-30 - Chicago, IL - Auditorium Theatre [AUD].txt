Notes
- Excellent show all around. Saw them earlier this year at UIC Pavilion. This size of a venue
  is far more appropriate for Sigur Rós. The show was well attended but there were a few
  handfulls of seats unoccupied.

- The crowd around me was excellent and the crowd in general was respectful overall.

- The guys and Okkr Ensemble powered through this ninety-four minute set in one go- no breaks!
  Stormur is a welcome addition to the set.

- Pull turned out all right, certainly better than the one from the UIC Pavilion in April.
  Maybe you'll like it, too!
