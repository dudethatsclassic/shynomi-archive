I used two camera sources for the audio. inez's lumix had some clipping, and she missed part of varúð. I didn't start my lumix early enough to catch the beginning. So I used inez for the very beginning, and as soon as I could, I patched in the other sources. the timing is good, but i think you notice where the sources switch (both cameras are normalized, for example). About 2/3 of the way through varúð the iriver recording picks up, and all is pretty good, with the exception of a bit more crowd noise, mostly between songs.it's not as clean as the first night, but it's a pretty decent tape. - please don't remaster this and distribute it.- please don't sell or do anything other than trade or share it freely. - DO NOT DISTRIBUTE IN ANY LOSSY FORMAT (no pleases here!) if you're going to degrade the quality of this recording, keep it to yourself.
