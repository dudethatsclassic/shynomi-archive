Hi everybody - 
I am still in a 'recovery mode', especially after the Saturday night show. But I'd like to bring you something before my job gets the best of me, pulling me kicking and screaming right back in!
This is Friday night at the Beacon. What can I say? I know some of the perfectionists would be complaining that Jonsi wasn't going 'all the way' (as he sure WAS the following night!!) - but it was a powerful show nonetheless, and it made me cry (ever so silently) a few times. God, I missed them so much!! And sadly, my work trip collided with the gigs in DC, Philly and Brooklyn :( 
Now, this is my first ever run on a brand new A10, I was still getting a hang of it, so Set 1 was recorded a bit hot [there are three spots where they get particularly loud] - thankfully Flying Horseman kindly applied his usual magic to that set only. I wasn't taking any chances after the intermission lol

OK, I included a few pix in the 24 bit set - so you also know where I was sitting. I took quite a few actually...

Enjoy!!
