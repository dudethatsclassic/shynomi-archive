I'd like to share one of the best show I attended and I'm glad I recorded it.
The venue was great as it's an auditorium with great acoustics and some nice reverb. The audience is really nice and quiet,
almost no screams or applause during the songs.

The sound was quite low, sometimes you can hear the buzz of the smoke machine on stage during the quiet parts.
I did some editing on the files : normalize, lower applause, some fade in and fade out at the end & beginning.
Some minor glitches are present at the end of Smaskifa, I had a problem with my MD
