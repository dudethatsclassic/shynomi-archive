Editor’s comments: Hi all - to keep the incredible Sigur Rós flood going, here’s a show I was meant to share months ago - but didn’t get to working on it till this weekend. So thanks to my Icelandic adventures and those of you posting SR shows on dime for the reminder. This Bay Area show was captured by TheMan - so we are lucky. For full disclosure, the audience here was VERY non-icelandic :) So taking out some wild screams and whistles and… I would not even know how to describe some of these ‘wounded animal’ noises they were (past tense!) producing - took most of my weekend; but THE GOOD NEWS is THEY ARE GONE!!
The show is beautiful - and the band sounds amazing, so I’m happy to bring this gem to you so we can keep filling the gaps in our collections…
Enjoy!
