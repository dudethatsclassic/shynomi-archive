Downloaded from victoryroselossless.blogspot.com.
I had recieved several 2002 soundboards from Jon earlier this year - he left this one off the trade, however, I did receive an audience recording of this gig from Jon (any interest I can post that).
Recently he posted this concert to his blog - he has remastered it. I downloaded it from his blog in flac format.  Like the others, it had some tracking and track transition issues (small gaps) so I converted to wav and fixed the transition issues, and retracked it. I haven't done anything else.
It appears Jon might have remastered each track individually as there are some small but noticeable changes in sound between several of the tracks. However it does not distract from an outstanding concert.

Here are his remastering notes:
Superb recording with an exceptional stereo field. I corrected it slightly to bring Jónsi's voice back to the centre. 
Added a small amount of low end as it brought some benefits to tracks especially during Von & Popplagið, tracks that really enjoy emphasis on the drums + it sits really well within the mix. 
Also reduced the cymbals on Ny Batterí as the high ends where quite over powering. 


Enjoy,
