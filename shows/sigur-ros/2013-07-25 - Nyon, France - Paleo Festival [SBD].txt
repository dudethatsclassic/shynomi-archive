Hi everyone - so here's our rip of the webcast with some improvements as specified. Volume was super-low, and uneven between channels - so to work it up a bit, we had to convert it to flac. This however is looking very good - the spectra go all the way to 19 even 20 kHz and it's nice to have a SBD recording of an awesome show!
