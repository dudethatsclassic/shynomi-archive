I had a friend who worked at a local recording studio do the transfer for me. I don't know what equipment he used, but he is a taper and the transfer was done digitally. 


**notes**
There are some volume fluctuations in the beginning of the show and at the very end. Overall, the show is of good quality considering the caliber of the gear used. There is no crowd chatter as I hung my mics over the edge of the balcony. 

There is a bit of hiss which is more noticeable in some parts more than others. This was due to the slightly noisy stock mic pre-amp in the DAT deck combined with some self noise from the mics and the relative "quietness" of some sections of the music. (this was noted specifically for the real sticklers...it shouldn't be a problem for the majority)
