TAPER NOTES:

That was a great 1st festival day with many great band included Kings Of Convenience, dEUS and Sigur Rós, obviously.
I went there with some friends and we made it to the first rows which was very nice. As I went there with some friend
you'll hear some chats here and there but most of them are between songs or at the very beginning or end. You'll hear
some near claps... Sorry but I couldn't make anything... I moved but I solved nothing. This is really my first serious
tape some many mistakes could be found but I wanted to share it as there are no so many tapings available. Hope you like
it!

There's no real eq'. I just amplify the whole thing a little bit (taking care of the saturation, so no worries about that)
and I "hide" some claps a little bit but no further eq' was made.
