WELL.... Just when you think it cannot get any better!! ALSO, ok, NYC night-1 was at the Beacon which is FAMOUS for its exceptional sound quality... - and yet!!! Here comes Kings in Brooklyn - and it's a riot! Now, of course - stage geometry is different in every theatre and for the ORCHESTRA this may make a big difference. And sure, my personal location here gave me a fantastic view of Jónsi and often Georg and Kjartan which wasn't quite the case at the Beacon. But the main factor here though is still: NIGHT 2 for most bands is nearly always better, and here it was just pure magic. Not just my opinion :) every one of my friends who did both shows felt the same way!

As to the recording - it's just like I said: IT TURNED OUT AWESOME! Of course, because this is an orchestral show, the dynamic range is enormous - there are parts that are extremely quiet and then it suddenly rocks the walls... That's normal. I reduced the audience intrusions - all in between songs lol And many many thanks again to loughney for mastering and making it even better! 
ENJOY!

You still have a chance to catch them out west!
