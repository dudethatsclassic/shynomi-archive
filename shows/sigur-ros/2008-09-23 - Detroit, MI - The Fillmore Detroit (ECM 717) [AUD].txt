Notes: I wasn't going to go to this show because my sister couldn't make the show. But I went anyways just so I could get a tape to share with everyone. Pretty decent show. I love Sigur Ros' studio albums and live they are pretty good. I taped from the 2nd level in front of the board on the floor at the Fillmore. I think this sounds really good. As usual the crowd is very quiet and respectful. THe highlights of the show were Ny Batteri, Gobbledigook and Popplagio. This show is dedicated to my sister, I hope everything is well. Enjoy.
