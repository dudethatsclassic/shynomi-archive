Notes:

Dont't quite know where to start here, as I kind of blown away by this gig. I have been a fan on the band for a number of years now, but have never managed to catch them live before now. When I saw the tickets first going on sale, I jumped on it, which was just as well, as the gig was sold out well before the night. The band had been slimmed down to a four piece for this tour, so whilst I would have preferred the "full bhuna", the performance from these four guys was phenomenal. If you haven't seen SR before, get the finger out and go and see them live.

The band are known for a good stage show and great venue sound to go with the music and did not disappoint on this occasion. The sound at the Carling is not generally praised for it's high quality, but I guess the sound engineers for other bands need a lesson from whoever was in charge here. The confetti cannon was amusing, as I had not paid much attention and no idea what this giant ear trumpet was doing by the PA stack - until Gobbledigook, of course. :-)

The whole show was superb, from opening epic Svefn-G-Englar, through the upbeat and at times moody atmospheric set through to the closing finale of Popplagið. Loved Hafsól with Georg using a drumstick on his bass - great sound. Kjartan Holm - from fellow Icelandic support band, For A Minor Reflection - joined on this song to play xylophone, then all of FaMR came on stage with drums to join in on Gobbledigook. Orri got pasted by a speaker falling on him during Hafsól, but didn't miss a beat. What a trooper! At the end, the band certainly deserved the applause they received when they stood exhausted at the front of the stage and took a bow. Hurry back guys!

Sound? Well it was pretty good in there, so I only had to apply a little eq and amplified by 3db. I had to hard limit the "happy clapper" that stood in front of me. You can hear her throughout, but due to the sold-out status of the gig, there was no room for me to move from the front left position I was in. In general, the crowd was pretty reverential when it came to the quiet passages of the music, only a few people talking at different points, plus our happy clapper!

Please support the band by buying their albums & merch and by going to see them live.
