It was kind of a last minute decision, so glad I decided to go see them again. A local band from Santa
Barbara I always love to see them when they play in my back yard. The band put on a great show and the crowd
was really into it. I had a good spot with very little distractions around me. I was able to sit down, which
always helps at this venue. They mentioned a new album, and several newer songs were performed tonight, and
they sounded great! Crank it up!!

I ran the raw 24/96 master file through a complete remastering process using Isotope Ozone7. I cleaned up
some of the clappers, and then boosted the vocals and bottom end. It always sounded great, but now it's
amazing, so crank it up!!
