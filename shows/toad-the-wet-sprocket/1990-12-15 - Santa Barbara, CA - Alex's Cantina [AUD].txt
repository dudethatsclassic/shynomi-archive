NOTES:  This is an nth-generation cassette I was given for transfer from Jake Williams (wiljake@yahoo.com).  He probably got it from Darren Phaneuf (darren_phaneuf@yahoo.com), though that's just an educated guess on my part.  The recording is very nice quality for an old cassette audience job, though it's blown away by modern multi-mic DAT and minidisc productions.  What makes this show special, though, is the early arrangements of the songs.  A few of them have different lyrics.  We also have here the first listenable recording of Toad's biggest hit song, "All I Want"--and the only decent-sounding version with the original lyrics.  Any serious Toad fan should get this recording.  Period.  Enjoy!

ADDITIONAL NOTES:  I did not use hiss reduction, but if anyone wishes to do so themselves I left a brief second-or-two-long noise sample at the end of the show for just that purpose.
