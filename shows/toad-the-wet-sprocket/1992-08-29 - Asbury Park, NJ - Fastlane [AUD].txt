NOTES:  This recording comes from a high-generation cassette, and needless to say its quality reflects that.  You can barely hear any of the songs, and the in-between chatter might as well be pure static.  The setlist is nice, including semi-rarities "Chile," "Little Heaven" and "Cinnamon Girl."  This is the only known Toad performance of "I Will Take My Time," but on the final cassette copy it cuts out after three minutes!  How's that for disappointing?  Even the master cuts out in the middle of that song, though, so this is about as complete as you can get.  I used a Pioneer CT-W103 and Creative Labs Sound Blaster Audigy LS for the transfer.  Adobe Audition and CD Wave were both used to master the audio.  Enjoy (if you can)!
