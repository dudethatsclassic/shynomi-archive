Notes:
First time seeing Toad and I had a good time. By the time I left for the show there were
only two tickets left to buy (all tickets were assigned seats), and based on how things
looked when Toad took the stage it appeared to be sold out. Hats off to the guys for
playing 90 minutes straight through without a single break, not even for an encore!
