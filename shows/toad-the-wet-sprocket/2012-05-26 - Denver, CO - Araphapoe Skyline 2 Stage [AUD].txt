Jonathan Kingham, on tour with Toad the Wet Sprocket, played multiple instruments and sang back-up vocals thoughout the show.

Recording notes: There is wind rumble on this recording. I tried to minimized it as much as possible with EQ. Also, the band Toto was playing at the same time on a stage close by, so there is some bleed from that stage on the quieter parts of the Toad the Wet Sprocket performance.

Recorded, processed and distributed by Chuck Miller (chuck@taperssection.com)

Event notes: The third annual Denver Day of Rock, Concerts for Kids was a free day of concerts held in downtown Denver. Toad the Wet Sprocket played on the Arapahoe Skyline 2 Stage from approximately 8:00pm to 9:30pm. They were the final band to play on that stage for the day.
