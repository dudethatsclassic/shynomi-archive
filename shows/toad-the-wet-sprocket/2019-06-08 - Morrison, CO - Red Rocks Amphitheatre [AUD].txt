Technical detail: Between "Something's Always Wrong" and "Walk On the Ocean" someone bumped into my left microphone. I did what I could to minimize the problem, but decided to leave it in the recording as there was stage banter during that and I wanted to maintain the integrity of the complete performance. There is also some loud wind noise at times throughout this recording.
