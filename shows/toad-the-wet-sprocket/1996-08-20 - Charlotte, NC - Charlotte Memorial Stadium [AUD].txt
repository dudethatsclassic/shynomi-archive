Charlotte, NC

TAPER:  Michael Greene
TRANSFER:  Ben Wallis (hurtstotalktoyou@yahoo.com)
LINEAGE:  CAS(M)>FLAC

SETLIST:
01.  Good Intentions
02.  All I Want
03.  Something's Always Wrong
04.  Desire
05.  Don't Know Me
06.  Don't Fade
07.  Dam Would Break
08.  Whatever I Fear
09.  Fall Down
10.  Hold Her Down
11.  Brother
12.  Walk On The Ocean (with Darius Rucker)

NOTES:  The first three tracks seem to be about a half step lower than they should be, while the last eight sound about right.  "Desire," the intermediate between the two portions, is played in a different key altogether.  I'm not sure exactly was going on to give that effect.  Maybe Toad had tuned their guitars down, or maybe the tape speed lagged for the first few minutes.  In any case, the quality is fairly nice for a cassette recording.  Note the appearance of unreleased "Don't Know Me."  Darius Rucker (from Hootie & The Blowfish) sings on the last song.  Enjoy!
