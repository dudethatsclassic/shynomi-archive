Notes:
Altough it's not really noticable there is clipping during the first part of the recording. I checked the levels during the opening act's set and thought there was plenty of headroom but when I checked during The Hound (Of Blood And Rank) it was peaking. At that point (2:12 into The Hound), I lowered the gain and subsequent volume adjusments were done to even it out. The Billboard is a smallish venue and it was very loud. Thank god for my earplugs.
