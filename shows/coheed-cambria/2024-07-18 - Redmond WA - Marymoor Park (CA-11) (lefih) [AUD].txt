I had seen this band once before in 2015 at the Monster Mash AZ Festival in Tempe, AZ.  They didn't really do it for me then and I forgot about them.  

I went to see Primus, but since I was there I hit record.  Both bands had their own VIP add-on packages which got you in the venue early in addition to other bonuses.  We had regular tickets to this show and since we were plenty early, we joined the general admission queue in anticipation of the gates opening at 16:30.  Part of Coheed's VIP package was a pre-show acoustic set, which didn't begin until 16:30, leaving us stranded in the sun for an extra 20-30 minutes while our ears were tortured by C&C Unplugged from outside the fence.  

I hope you enjoy this more than I did.  My friends and I could not stop laughing at the drummer's face.
