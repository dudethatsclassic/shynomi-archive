NOTES:
* - Unfortunately I was young and dumb when I was creating / transferring this recording, and as a result of the process I used, and my sloppiness, there are some windows noises sprinkled throughout this entire recording.  To make matters worse, I erased the original Minidisk.  Needless to say, it's been a learning experience.  My rather novice experience level also is responsible for the clips and momentary pauses and skips which appear sporadically throughout the recording.

I had my mics mounted on the rails of the Bleachers--  as you can (particularly later in the set) some mofo started drumming on the railing, completely intruding on the recording.  Sorry for it, there was nothing I could do!

It was windy as hell at the show and I hadn't bought any windscreens, so I MacGuyver'ed some by slicing my socks up like crazy and wrapping up my mic's in sock pieces.  I'm glad with the result!
