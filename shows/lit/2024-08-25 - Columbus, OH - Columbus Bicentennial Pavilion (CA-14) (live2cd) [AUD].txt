Notes:

*  Originally Recorded By The Cars.

FREE concert sponsored by The Columbus Downtown Development Corporation & CAPA.
Lit took the stage at 7pm and gave a blistering 50 minute set. The one real
surprise here (for the hardcore) was a brand new song being played, "Sick Of
Me Too", which was JUST written a few weeks prior! According to setlist.fm, it
had been played only 2 or 3 other times. Outside of that, more of the same if
you've followed their sets lately. Always a fun energetic band to see live!

Tonic and headliner Sugar Ray were also on the bill. [all recorded].
