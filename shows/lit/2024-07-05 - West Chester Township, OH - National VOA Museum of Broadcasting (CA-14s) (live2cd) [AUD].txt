Notes:

*  Originally Recorded By The Cars.
+  Originally Recorded By Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers.

What a great surprise seeing Lit was returning to the Cincinnati area after
having just played Mason Ohio last July with the Gin Blossoms. The only slight
difference between the 2 sets, was last year's was slightly longer. Missing
from this year's set was "Down" and the Morgan Wallen cover of "Last Night",
but thats OK. The rest of the setlist was there and they moved their Cars cover
to the first song which was a great way to kick things off. It definitely got
everyone off of their feet. When I arrived to the festival it was cloudy and
windy and there was a small threat of rain, but thankfully outside of a 5 min
light shower 30 minutes prior to showtime, the wind and clouds vanished! Yay!
They took the stage at 815 and played til roughly 925, which was followed by
fireworks at 10pm. Show was completely free and all around just a great night
was had by all. Lit is playing Columbus this August with Tonic, might go see
them again.
