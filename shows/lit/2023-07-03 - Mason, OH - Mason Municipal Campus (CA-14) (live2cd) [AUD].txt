+  Originally Recorded By Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers,
   featuring Robin Wilson from the Gin Blossoms. 

I may have been more excited about catching these guys over the headliner,
the Gin Blossoms, simply because I have only ever seen them once - and it
was on the 2012 Summerland tour where they only played 30 minutes. While
that was a fun set, it was massively abbreviated from what they bring in
a full set. When Mason Ohio announced this FREE show with fireworks, I really
wanted to make it work. I even showed up early with chairs to get a good
spot at 3pm. Lit was scheduled for 6pm. I had no idea how long they would
get, but I was expecting at least an hour. I was very happy to see they were
given 20 minutes more than that! They may have played a few more minutes than
the headliner! There were some technical difficulties during their set which
probably added 5 minutes of unnecessary banter while they corrected what was
wrong, but still - very grateful to get a full set from these guys. Especially
FREE! The issues were as follows: Jeremy's guitar only coming out of the right 
stack for a brief period of time (hardly noticeable on the recording), A. Jay's
mic kept cutting out on him early and then when they replaced that, the new mic
was buzzing for a song or two until that was fixed. Nothing was going right,
but the playback is still great. Very minor issues. The sound was great and
no distractions nearby. I highly recommend downloading if you're a fan.
