Show Notes:
- This show was originally going to be Set B, but it was changed to Set A due to the filming (see Other Notes).
- A few technical problems plagued the band at this show. The bridge of 'New Divide' had some sample issues, and samples at the beginning of 'Rebellion' and end of 'Robot Boy' were also messed up.
- On the transition outro of 'Runaway', Mike rapped verse one of 'Wastelands'. Chester sang the first line of the bridge over the outro transition.
- The intro of 'Castle Of Glass' was extended to let the crowd clap along with it. Joe did not play the samples at the start of the song.
- Mike's solo portion of the show featured a 'hybrid' verse of 'Wretches And Kings', the chorus of 'Remember The Name' (by Fort Minor), and a 'hybrid' verse of 'Remember The Name'. After the 'hybrid' verse of 'Remember The Name', he went into the chorus again.
- Austin Carlile from Of Mice & Men came out to perform 'Faint' with the band. 
- To start the encore, Chester came out and had everyone sing happy birthday for his twin daughters.
- Mike rapped the first verse of 'With You' over the extended intro of 'Until It's Gone'.
- During the bridge of 'Bleed It Out', Rob performed a drum solo and Chester lead the crowd in a sing-a-long of the 'Bleed It Out' chorus, skipping the refrain from 'The Catalyst'.
