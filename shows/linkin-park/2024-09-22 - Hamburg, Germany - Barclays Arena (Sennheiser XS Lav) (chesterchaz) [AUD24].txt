Notes: 3 weeks ago I would never have imagined to see Linkin Park live again, but here we are! I was lucky enough to get a ticket, and thought I could as well try to tape it for old times' sake. Got a fairly cheap mic to plug directly into my phone and unsurprisingly, results came out mediocre. A lot of distortion on the low end on the heavier songs. You're probably better off with one of the videos on YouTube. Sharing this one nevertheless for all the collectors out there! Small file size as it's basically Dual Mono. RIP Chester.
