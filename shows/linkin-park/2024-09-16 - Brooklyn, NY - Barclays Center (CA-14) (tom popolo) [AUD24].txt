Show Notes:

Dope ass concert by Linkin Park, Emily sounded killer, the band was on point, and Brooklyn was ready to welcome them back with open arms. They did not disappoint whatsoever. Before My December, someone shouted “WE LOVE YOU CHESTERRRR” which erupted shouts and screams and Linkin Park chants all around. Looking forward to seeing them at Barclay’s Center in July this year (2025).

Shoutout to the dude to my left in the recording, he was screaming his guts out. Also shoutout to the dude to my right, he showed us a picture of him and Chester at Webster Hall.

The recording isn’t bad, I tried mastering it to compress it a tad, and to control the sub frequencies as they were very hectic. Also at some points you can hear a few seconds of ruffling from the right channel, my microphone cable was being moved around. I also check my recorder periodically to make sure all is well.

Enjoy! Encode to Wav, MP3, Whatever just enjoy it!
