Comments:

To my surprise it was an excellent show - the band had fun on stage, Emely was a great performer (and shouter), you can't expect more. It's impossible to replace Chester that's why they didn't bothered with that and started something completely new. Try to catch them if you're still in doubt.

MK22 did an excellent job (as did Grigio), but recording from the pit always means singalongs, shouting, screaming, clapping and people talking here and there. Still a very good capture of that night well above average.
