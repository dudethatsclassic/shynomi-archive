Show Notes (LPLive.net):
- Chester sang part of Esaul (the demo for A Place For My Head) during the chorus of A Place For My Head.
- Chester played Morning After acoustically again for only the second time ever. This is the only recording that exists of it from 2001. Chester said that it was a song that he wrote about "the anticipation of one day making it in the music business."
- Joe played a short DJ interlude in the encore.
- Step Up featured an extended bridge and ending.

Debuts:
- This was the first time that Step Up was played live.
