This recording came out in mid 2014. Previously there was only a very bad sounding recording of this show so this was a major upgrade.

This is the only recording from the 1988-89 tour where he sings Smooth Criminal, Bad and The Way You Make Me Feel completely live without lip sync. The show is all live vocals except for the first 4 minutes of the last song.
