Notes:
New transfer - April 2025

In the final months leading to the passing of my (un-official) father-in-law, my girlfriend helped him sift through his things, and thereby discovered a long lost tape from when she was a teen.
Back in the day, she and her father had gone to an MJ concert with a friend of theirs, who taped the show and gave her father a copy. This is it.

"They Don't Care About Us" and parts of the song before and after is missing, as someone had accidentally recorded it over with a song from an MJ studio album.

PLEASE NOTE: The is an AUD recording, and not sourced from the TV1000 video broadcast of this show.
