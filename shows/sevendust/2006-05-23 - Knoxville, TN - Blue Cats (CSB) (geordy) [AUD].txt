My 3rd attempt at taping Sevendust, at least I have a recording to show for it this time. Sevendust brought in their own soundboard, lighting, barriers, sound techs, you name it. They apparently asked for extra security as there were more Blue Cats employees playing the security buzzkill than I've ever seen at Blue Cats. They were mostly just busting people with cameras and keeping the mosh pit from getting too violent. Also my first time getting patted down at this club in almost a year, glad I didn't try and sneak a video camera in and have to keep that in my crotch all night long. 

So Sevendust's sound techs and couldn't see the warning lights from the amps because of all the shit they had stacked on the stage. Our local tech, Jason, was able to get up on the stage and make adjustments at various times during the show, but there is some distortion throughtout, some songs sound much better than others. I knew the show would be loud, so I had put my levels all the way down and just let it go since security was worse than the last Tool show I attended. Had to bump up the overall volume by about 10db afterwards, so trust me, it's not my mics/levels overloading, what you hear is what I heard, there wasn't anything I could do about it. Next show I went to I talked to Jason and he said they blew 5-18" speakers that night. I captured it all perfectly from about 5' back.

Thanks to specialk for taking a stab at EQing this show and making it sound much better than it did that night. Really a disappointing show, I guess the saying 'the louder the better' didn't apply here. At least I have a recording to show for it this time.

Gotta love the chick at the end of the show 'I look like sweaty shit, but I got VIP!' She was standing next to me in front of the stacks, turned to me and said that right after one of the roadies reached out and gave her a VIP pass. Hope she had fun. Show was just over 83:00. If you want to burn this to 1 cd, just leave off the first two intro tracks.
