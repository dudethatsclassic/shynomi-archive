Notes:
This was a very good performance considering the conditions. This is a very small venue with a
high energy crowd resulting in poor taping conditions. It's a great recording despite the occasional
screamers around. Clint and Morgan were in a jamming mood this night; notice between some
songs like Suffocate and Prodigal Son. It's good groove and may be a taste of the new material
being written. Lots of video footage was made of this show for some reason so be on the lookout
for any info on that. Cover art will be posted shortly after taper recieves pics from the show. Please
continue to support Sevendust by purchasing their official material and merchandise.
