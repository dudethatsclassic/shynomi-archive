Notes:
Their fourth show after a 6 month break from the road. Some of the guitar effects sounded a little
off throughout the first half of the set and they left the stage after Rumble Fish so the techs could
sort it all out. After about 18 minutes they returned to the stage and finished what they could given
the local ordinance. That means they had to drop Enemy and Bitch from the setlist. A helicopter
can be heard flying over during the long break so I'm thinking this was an outdoor show.  Alpha
album wasn't released yet so Deathstar and Alpha were new songs in the set. Other bands on
the bill were Rev Theory and He Is Legend. If either were recorded, I don't have them.
