Notes:
The first of a four-show run leading to their departure on the Shiprocked cruise, to return
for one more date (Dec 1st) in Ft. Lauderdale. It's a decent recording considering my
location in the venue as well as having the low-cut engaged on the R-09. I was very close
to the rail and the subs were really kicking. This has been shared before on ttd but I felt it
was time to remaster it (as I have become much better at it) and to create some cover art
as well. I started from scratch using the raw wave file and used graphic e.q. to boost the
low frequencies and did a hard limit to -.1db with a 4db boost. I also eliminated the encore
break track mark and left the encore as part of track 13. If you already have this in your
catalog, I suggest replacing it with this version and giving it a listen on the 13th anniversary
of it. It was a fun night. Me and my girl got to have a drink and chat with the band and crew
after the show and this was a couple years before they started marketing meet & greets.
Nonpoint opened but I didn't get to tape them. Cover art is included in the torrent. Enjoy.
-kevdogg
