Notes:
The second of a four-show run leading to their departure on the Shiprocked cruise, to return
for one more date (Dec 1st) in Ft. Lauderdale. Given my proximity to the p.a. and the subs I
decided to engage the low-cut switch on the R-09 which resulted in a loss of low end which I
hadn't noticed until recently. This has been shared before on ttd but I felt it was time to remaster
it (as I have become much better at it) and to create some cover art as well. I started from
scratch using the raw wave file and used graphic e.q. to boost the low frequencies and did a
hard limit to -.1db with a 4db boost. I also eliminated the encore break track mark and left the
encore as part of track 13. If you already have this in your catalog, I suggest replacing it with
this version and giving it a listen on the 13th anniversary of it. It was a great show. LJ's voice
was starting to get rough but it's only noticeable during the banter. This was an outdoor concert
held in the back courtyard of the Cuban Club. Nonpoint opened and I recorded them as well.
Cover art is included in the torrent. Enjoy.
