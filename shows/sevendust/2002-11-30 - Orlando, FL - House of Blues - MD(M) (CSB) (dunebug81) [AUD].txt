Notes:
This is the 2nd of three shows they played in Orlando during the 2002 leg of the Animosity Tour.
Openers were Stereomud, Closure and Cinder. As far as I'm aware, this is the initial release of
this tape. I was at this show but I wasn't a taper at the time. So I am very glad there was a taper
there and after all these years, has decided to dig it up and allow the rest of the world to enjoy it.
There was always an incredible energy in the room when Sevendust played HOB. This tape
captured that well and I'm pleased to be the one to present it to everyone. I did some low and
high end boosts with graphic e.q. in Audition and placed fades at the start and end. I hope this
reaches all the diehard Sevendust fans out there and perhaps inspires the release of more
unheard Sevendust tapes. Cover art is included in the torrent. Please continue to support the
artists by attending their shows and buying their official merchandise and releases.
