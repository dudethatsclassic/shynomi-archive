Notes:
This is the second NYE show they played in Orlando, which later became somewhat of a tradition.
Many of their friends and family in the house including members of Creed/AB and Johnny Damon.
Openers were Ill Nino and Skrape. As far as I'm aware, this is the initial release of this tape and I'm
pleased that after all these years, the taper has decided to dig it up and allow the rest of the world
to enjoy it. He made a good recording but unfortunately this show was plagued by many FOH issues.
Vocal and drum mics cutting in & out, bursts of static and dropouts. There were a few songs that
went without a hitch but altogether it never got fully resolved. Listening back it doesn't seem like it
took away from the energy of the show so that's good. I did some low and high end boosts with
graphic e.q. in Audition and placed fades at the start and end. I hope this reaches all the diehard
Sevendust fans out there and perhaps inspires the release of more unheard Sevendust tapes.
Cover art is included in the torrent. Please continue to support the artists by attending their shows
and buying their official merchandise and releases.
-kevdogg
