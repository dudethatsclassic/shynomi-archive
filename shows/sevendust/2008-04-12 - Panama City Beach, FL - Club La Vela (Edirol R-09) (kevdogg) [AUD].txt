Taper's Notes:
An awesome recording of Sevendust's first show in PCB.
Taper was at the front row, behind the p.a. so what you
get is a lot of Morgan's drums and John's guitar monitors.
An interesting perspective, I know, but what do you say to
your girl when she wants to be "up close"? This is obviously
a shorter set than the previous two shows; this is due to
Morgan straining his back during the Denial/Terminator/Assdrop
medley. I saw him signaling a "cut" to John during Terminator.
This just adds to the uniqeness of the show and I was totally
exhausted at the end anyways. Three 7d shows in one week and over
two thousand miles driven to get to them. A tour we will never forget!
As with every other fan-made recording, do NOT sell this. Please
support the band by purchasing their official merch and music releases.
Enjoy the show!
-kevdogg
