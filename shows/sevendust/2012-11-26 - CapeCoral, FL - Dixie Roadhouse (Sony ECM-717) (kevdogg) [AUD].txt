Notes:
The last of a four-show run leading to their departure on the Shiprocked cruise, to return for
one more date (Dec 1st) in Ft. Lauderdale. I started out dead center but I did have to relocate
a few times throughout the show to avoid the pit madness. Sheena Brook's band opened but
was not recorded. I think they did a good cover of Tool's Sober. This has been shared before
on ttd but I felt it was time to remaster it (as I have become much better at it) and to create
some cover art as well. I started from scratch using the raw wave file and used graphic e.q. to
boost the low frequencies and did a hard limit to -.1db with a 4db boost. I also eliminated the
encore break track mark and left the encore as part of track 13. If you already have this in your
catalog, I suggest replacing it with this version and giving it a listen on the 13th anniversary of it.
Cover art is included in the torrent. Enjoy.
