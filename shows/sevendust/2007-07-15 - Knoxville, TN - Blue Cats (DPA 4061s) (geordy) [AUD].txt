#Someone split a pitcher of beer on Sevendust's soundboard.  A 20 minute pause ensued as things were moved around so that they could finish out the set using Blue Cat's soundboard.  Lajon comes out finally and explains what happened to the crowd before they finally start Waffle again.  I stopped taping during this time to save the battery to get the rest of the set.  Oddly enough, Sevendust's soundboard was fried the last time I went to a Sevendust show (3/10 Atlanta), that time by the opening band throwing water out into the crowd.  

Maybe it's just me, but I think starting with Tr08 (Waffle - take 2) that the show sounded better.  Kind of odd that a $20,000 soundboard would sound that much better than an $80,000 soundboard?!

##After the encore break Lajon came out to talk to the crowd while everyone else in the band was huddled in the corner of the stage with a birthday cake.  Turns out it was Sonny's birthday.  Everyone came up on stage and Lajon then led the crowd in singing 'Happy Birthday' to Sonny.  Then he sang to Big Red, their soundtech, who's birthday was the next day.  At the request of someone in the crowd Lajon then sang the first half of Angel's Son solo, with the crowd's help, before the full band played Driven and Face to Face.
