Notes:
It appears I have lost the original info file on this so I will share all that I can recall.
This show was brutal. Very loud and just one heavy jam after another. It was cold
outside but hotter than hell in the Masquerade. Big Red was still running the
sound back then. I really miss that:/ I found some cover art someone had made
and included it, although it only represents the first 14 tracks. The soundcheck is
pretty cool to hear but if you don't care for it, just scrap the tracks and burn one disc.
Enjoy this 20th anniversary share.
