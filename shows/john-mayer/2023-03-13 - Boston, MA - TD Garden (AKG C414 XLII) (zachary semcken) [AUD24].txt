During "Your Body Is a Wonderland", the video screen did an overlay of unicorns, rainbows, and hearts circling John.

During "Drifting'", the arena spontaneously starting swaying their "lighters" (phone lights) in the air. This caused John to become choked up during the ending portion of the song.

Emoji of a Wave, I'm on Fire, All I Want Is to Be With You, and Dear Marie was on the printed setlist but was not played.

Obnoxious claps and trills in the right channel are courtesy of a drunken fan that was asked several times by security to return to their seat. I made some edits in post to minimize their impact on the recording.

Big thanks to the TD Garden security crew who made this quite possibly one of the best arena taping experiences I've ever had at an arena.

This recording is dedicated to Laura Medeiros, a wonderful security team member at TD Garden that went above and beyond to make sure that people didn't wander near my equipment. Without her help, this recording would be significantly worse.
