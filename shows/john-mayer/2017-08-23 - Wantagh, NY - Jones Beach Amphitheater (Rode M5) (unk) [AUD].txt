NOTES:  After searching for a place to put my stand, I gave up and decided to gaffer tape the mics to my hat and run from my seat.  I think this wound up being a good decision.  FOH is really far back at Jones, plus there was a decent breeze and I forgot my windscreens (doh).  Thanks to the M5s being cardioid mics, they did a good job at filtering out the screamers around me and keeping the music at the foreground.  That said, during quieter parts there is some audience noise, but it’s not to bothersome. Enjoy! It was a great show and solid setlist.


Thanks to Chris for all the venue advice; and most importantly to Debbie who got me hooked on taping shows to begin with!

Support artists that support live recording, they are a dying breed.
