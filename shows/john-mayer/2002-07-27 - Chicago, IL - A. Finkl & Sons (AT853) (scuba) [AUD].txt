Taper's Notes: This version of the A. Finkl show is the MiniDisc master I ran on the same stand as the MC012 DAT copy. The mics used on this source had better wind screening, and turned out a little better than the DAT source as far as wind noise. Some people may prefer the lack of compression in the DAT source, so I released them both, and you may decide for yourself which you like better.

Because of the tape flip just before "Something's Missing", I spliced in the MC012 DAT copy. I adjusted the levels so that they would be "good", but you will probably notice a slight difference in clarity and wind noise. Sorry. To put it lightly, the taper gods were not smiling the evening of July 27th, 2001. This is the best I could come up with. Enjoy.
