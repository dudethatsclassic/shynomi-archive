Lany opened for this John Mayer show. John does not finish the whole song "Comfortable" due to not remembering the words


RECORDING NOTES: 
This was the quietest crowd I have ever experienced and it was a pleasure to have been in that energy. Everyone was completely there for the music and nothing else.
We were forced to move from our seat that was to the left of the sound board ,however, after some reasoning with security we were able to set up on the first level balcony shoulder height.
There was about 30 open seats all around us but one group of nice girls came down and started to scream for John Mayer to take his pants off you will hear it repeatedly in the recording.
