A totally unique hometown show.  I have to admit that after seeing 6 solo shows, they were getting to be a bit repetitive.  While the setlists were changing the show itself followed the same format.  Not in Atlanta!  He cut the piano section and spent the whole time up in front playing as much acoustic music as he could until the curfew hit.  After seeing 6 shows, this one was totally unexpected and broke the mold.

This tour has made me think about our friend Debbie many times, who would have loved to have been a part of this one.  Debbie passed on her love of taping shows to many of us, and I'm proud to say I'm part of her "coaching tree."  Now that the tour is over, it's time for some pineapple upsidedown cake

Thats it for me this tour.  Until the next run....
