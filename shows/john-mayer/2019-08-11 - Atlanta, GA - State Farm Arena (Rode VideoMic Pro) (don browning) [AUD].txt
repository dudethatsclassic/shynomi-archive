Notes: 
Great “hometown” show. Lots of banter about the origin of Room for Squares era songs and an old school acoustic set. DRH preforming “I Will Still Be Loving You” may actually be the highlight of the show. Venue security was aggravating for sure, but once inside things were fine. Many thanks to Chad for allowing me to set up in FOH.

Enjoy it! Support artists that support tapers!
