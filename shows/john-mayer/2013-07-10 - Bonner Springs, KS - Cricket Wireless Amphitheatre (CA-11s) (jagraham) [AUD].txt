This recording is dedicated to my mother, Linda Pagans Graham (6/22/48-7/14/13). Our last conversation was over the phone the day after this show and included me telling her about Mayer's shout out when he saw my mics. She was very proud of any accomplishment of mine, no matter how small, and had spent her last days sharing the story with family and friends.
