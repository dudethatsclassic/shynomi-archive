NOTES:  Thanks a ton to John’s FOH engineer for allowing me to set up/keep my gear in the soundboard area.  He also helped to protect me from the overly hyper venue security. Thanks to Justin and Chris for all the advice; and most importantly to Debbie who got me hooked on taping shows to begin with!
