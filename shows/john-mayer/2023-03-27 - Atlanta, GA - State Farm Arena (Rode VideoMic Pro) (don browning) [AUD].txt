Atlanta shows never disappoint, and this one is no different.  '83 made an appearance for the first time since 2010 (I think), plus a partial Something's Missing that had John reaching deep into his memory (which made it all the more awesome).  I think the high point was A Face to Call Home, which is not only a deep cut but the first time I'd seen it live.
