Taper's notes:  The staff/security at the arena were all amazing...until when about 3 minutes before the opening act, J.P. Saxe, came on stage, when I was approached and told I had to lower my mics.  I explained that if I lower them to the 6 foot max they were asking, they'd be blocking people's view, rather than being 10+ feet in the air, and only a thin pole would be visible.  Then they gave me two options: either lower the mics to 6 feet or move behind the soundboard, where there was one other taper.  Given the time restraint, I opted to lower the mics, and move to the back of the soundboard during the break between acts.  I had many technical issues during the opening set.  After the opening set ended, I moved, re-set up, and corrected all the problems, before making a perfect recording of John Mayer.
