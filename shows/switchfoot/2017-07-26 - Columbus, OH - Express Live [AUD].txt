Notes:

* = Originally Recorded By Cyndi Lauper.

Co-headlining w/ Lifehouse on the "Looking For Summer" tour. Switchfoot took the
stage around 8:00pm. Switchfoot's main set time was (1:10:50). After the show
finished, Jon tweeted he would be coming out to perform an aftershow in 20 minutes.
I waited around and sure enough, there he came from the bus with his acoustic
guitar. I only expected 2 or 3 songs but he played 7 songs totaling nearly half
an hour! He also let a very lucky fan sing backing harmony with him on
"Twenty-Four" to close out the evening. I was literally 5 ft in front of him
with the mics still clipped to my hat. During "Twenty-Four" he actually moved a
tad bit closer to me to let the fan in to sing next to him. Definitely a memorable
experience to say the least.
