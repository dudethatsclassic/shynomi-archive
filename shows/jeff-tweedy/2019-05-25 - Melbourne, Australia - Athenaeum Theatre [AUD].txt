Show Notes: This show can be summed by Jeff in one sentence - "This is a strange audience I'm not gonna lie to you!" Yes it certainly was, with a woman calling Jeff uneducated, which got brought up at the next 2 shows, amongst other weird random things people were yelling out. Melbourne, you were pretty disrespectful. Go sit in the corner and take a good long hard look at yourselves. Still, it was a highly enjoyable show, one I'm sure Jeff won't forget for a long time to come!
