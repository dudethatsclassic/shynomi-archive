Jeff Tweedy
Meyerhoff Symphony Hall
Baltimore, MD
June 9, 2014

Editing was done with Audacity is was used between songs to correct level changes at the beginning of show and bring the audience level down and make the recording more enjoyable.  If you've been to a Tweedy show you know he likes to joke with the audience, instead of creating tracks for each banter with the audience I just left the banter at the end of each track.
