NOTES

This was the first of Jeff Tweedy's legendary acoustic shows at Lounge Ax, and his first ever "solo" show.  He was visibly 
nervous before and during the show, but I thought and still think that he pulled if off really well. I had mixed feelings 
about Wilco at the time based on the shows I'd seen in Chicago and St. Louis, so I was very happy that I enjoyed this show so much. 
Very nice setlist, with songs from every Uncle Tupelo album, most of the better songs that ended up on AM, and some choice covers, 
some of which were only played at this show. This was the first time that I really came to appreciate Jay Bennett, his guitar and 
harmonica work were stellar.

The recording is very good for a stealth audience, though as is often the case with quiet shows in rowdy clubs the audience is a bit 
loud between songs.

Dedicated to my road trip partner, Jeff Downing.
