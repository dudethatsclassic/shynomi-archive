Notes: This was the 3rd of 4 shows at Largo, which is a tiny 280 capacity theatre.  Tickets were next to impossible to get and Largo’s website crashed during the onsale.  Luckily a friend managed to get a pair for this show and took me along.  

This was also the quietest show I have ever taped, basically like an entire show preformed off mic, and once in I was not able to check my levels.  The crowd was completely quiet during the songs, but the applause was roughly 30db louder than the music.  I spent a considerable amount of time balancing this out so one can listen without blowing out their ears or speakers.  Overall, I think it came out pretty good.  

The show itself was great and was full of Jeff’s typical hilarious banter.
