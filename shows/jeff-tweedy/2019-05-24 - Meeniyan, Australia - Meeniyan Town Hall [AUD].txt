Show Notes: 2 hours of solo Jeff in a tiny 260 capacity venue in a small town 2 hours east of Melbourne. An amazing show full of great songs and hilarious banter. It doesn't get much better than this.

Recording Notes: There were annoying drunk talkers to the left, and behind me at this one unfortunately. I've seen a lot of shows at Meeniyan before and this is the first time this has happened, usually the audience is really respectful and quiet. The women behind me aren't audible on the recording thankfully, but the woman to my left can be heard at times, including asking everyone around her for a bottle opener. And then she asked my partner if she wanted a glass of wine, she said no she just wanted to watch the show, then this woman told my partner she must have poor conversational skills!! She was so annoying the poor guy at the table in front of her turned around and pleaded with her to stop talking at the start of Theolgians which even Jeff heard and almost stopped playing thinking he was asking Jeff to stop. Of course she didn't see anything wrong with what she was doing and fought back. I tried to get away from it as much as possible, but it's all there to hear. 

Comment of the night went to the woman behind me when she was asked to please be quiet and respectful - "If you don't want to hear people talking you should stay home and listen to the records".

I also yelled a few out a things during the show in response to Jeff talking, sorry I couldn't help it. It was one of those nights.

Anyway aside from those issues, this recording sounds great. Enjoy!
