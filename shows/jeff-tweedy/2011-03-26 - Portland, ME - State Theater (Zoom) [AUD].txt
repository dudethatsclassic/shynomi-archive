Jeff Tweedy
State Theatre
Portland, ME
March 26, 2011

Editing was done with Audacity is was only used between songs to bring the audience
down and make the recording more enjoyable.  If you've been to a Tweedy show you 
know he likes to joke with the audience, instead of creating tracks for each banter 
with the audience I just left the banter at the end of each track.

Original taper - John Bosco
Editing and packaging - John Bosco
