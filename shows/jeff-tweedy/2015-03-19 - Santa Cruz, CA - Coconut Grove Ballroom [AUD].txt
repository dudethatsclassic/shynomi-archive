Tweedy Band
2015-03-19
Cocoanut Grove Ballroom
Santa Cruz, California USA 

Recorded & Transfered By: Robert Mullen (lvrwm) 

Equipment/Lineage: Audience/SP-CMC-25 mics (Croakies)>SP-SPSB-2(Battery 
Boost Box)>Sony PCM-M10>CD Wave Editor(tracking)>Traders Little Helper flac level 8. 

Recorder Settings: PCM-M10 at 44.1 kHz/16 bit, low mic sensitivity, manual 
record, limiter on, mic power off, low cut filter off, speed control off. 

Comments: Here is our recording of Jeff (Wilco) & Spencer (Son) Tweedy, known as
Tweedy Band. The venue was a mid-sized Ballroom (great for weddings?), with some 
"solid" sound. We were located in front of the soundboard, dead center, 25 feet from 
stage. The general admission standing only crowd was excited and pretty well behaved 
near this recorder. Keep in mind, it is a very "Intimate" venue, with a bit of crowd
interaction with Jeff throughout the set. Jeff and the Band appeared to enjoy themselves
during their performance (though, a person rudely taking pics with a "FLASH", was 
removed from the venue during the show). The Tweedy Band appeared initially, then Jeff 
Acoustic Solo, then the return of the Band for the last third of the over 2 hour set. 
Scott McCaughey-The Minus 5 (The Young Fresh Fellows), joined in on a few songs 
throughout the set and the final and Peter Buck-The Minus 5 (REM) joined for the final 
song (California Stars). Enjoy!
