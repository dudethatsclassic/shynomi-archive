Show Notes: The first all GA standing show of Jeff's tour created a different dynamic, with a very different setlist to the previous night. A really great show full of hilarious stories, great songs, and more weird yellers for some reason, including some terrible shouting (it wasn't singing) during a few songs rather than a respectful singalong. Melbourne, you really need to have a good long hard look at yourselves. Still, a tremendous show.

Recording Notes: This is my best recording of the tour. Crystal clear and a nice quiet crowd around me, this makes for a great recording. Occasionally you can hear a door bang when someone enters or leaves the room but that's about it. Enjoy!
