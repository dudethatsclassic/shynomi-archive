Notes: 
Jeff Tweedy played as part of the Mission Creek Festival taking place at multiple venues around the city over the course of the week (March 28 through April 4). The openers were Snowblink.

This recording and show are very unique. There was an audience member who decided to engage Jeff very early and this created some unique banter. Jeff also dedicated the song So Much Wine by the The Handsome Family to the Mayor's daughter. Then, during Jesus, etc. you will notice clapping of the moment she got up and left the venue. While this created some interesting moments I also wonder if the set was affected as a result. My recording is not without flaws. There were multiple signs posted about no photography or use of cell phones. An attendee next to me wanted to quickly snap a few pictures and was approached by security during I Am Trying To Break Your Heart. As a result, they also asked me what device I had in my lap. You will hear some of this exchange and permission to continue with the audio recording. I am glad for many reasons I was able to continue!
