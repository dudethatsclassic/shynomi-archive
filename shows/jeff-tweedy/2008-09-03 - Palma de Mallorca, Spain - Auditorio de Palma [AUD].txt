Jeff Tweedy
Palma de Mallorca, Spain
Auditorio de Palma
Sept 03, 2008

My own recording
Sony MZ-NH700-> Sony ECM CS10-> Sonic Studio-> WAV-> Traders Little Helper

Great and friendly show, funny and moving, very alert audience, little noise, great recording from my point of view. My regards to the couple from Manchester(btw: City or United?), who in 2008 had seen Wilco in places like New Zeeland (and will see them in New York City opening for Neil Young), and had come to Palma to see themselves almost lonely with me on a gigantic balcony in the Auditorium, while the ground floor was filled with about 500 people.
