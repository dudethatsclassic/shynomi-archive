An excellent show from Jeff Tweedy & co in Vicar Street. Thanks to Jim Nolan for supplying 
the set list.

Songs from the Sukierae sound great in a live setting and Jeff's solo set is great.

This one turned out pretty good - the change from standing to unreserved seating meant
I started off in the very back row of seats. By the time the band took the stage I was
in the 5th row, directly in line with the rigth stack. Crowd around me were quiet too.

For the old fashionded amongst us who still burn to disc I'd suggest splitting after I Am
Tryin to Break Your Heart

Remember to support Tweedy - buy their albums, got to their shows and buy their merch.

Enjoy the show!
