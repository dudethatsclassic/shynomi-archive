Notes: 
This was a free show as part of the summer concert series put on by LL Bean right at their main campus store in Freeport, Maine. The venue is a temporary one with a decent size stage and a small grassy area where people set up camp chairs to enjoy the show.

Lineup:
Jeff Tweedy (guitar, vocals)
Spencer Tweedy (drums)
Jim Elkington (guitar)
Darin Gray (bass)
Liam Cunningham (keys)

The crowd was Very well behaved for a free, open air concert attended largely by locals who go to every show, and aren`t necessarily there for the band that is playing.

There is some minimal wind noise, crowd chatter, coughing, some environmental stuff like car alarms, moving lawn chairs, etc. The more annoying issue is that there is also a slight digital "tic" that seems present. I am not sure what it is and it is only audible during the more quiet acoustic parts. I am not sure if it is an equipment issue or an environmental thing!

I believe this to be the only audience recording of the event, so I am releasing it even though it is less than perfect.
