Show Notes: Second show of the tour and another nice setlist with a few different songs, including a surprising but great version of One Sunday Morning. When Jeff asks the audience to sing along, someone makes up a story that there were signs up telling them not to sing. This becomes a running joke at the rest of the shows on the tour. 

Recording Notes: Just like I did, you're going to have to put up with a very loud guy yelling and singing badly who was sitting next to me unfortunately. It was so bad during Heavy Metal Drummer it made Jeff laugh, and his comment about maybe the audience shouldn't sing was clearly directed at him. Not much I could do about it, he was clearly a huge fan and really enjoying himself, just wish he could have enjoyed himself not sitting next to me. But if you can overlook him the recording sounds excellent. Just imagine me having to sit next to him - unlike this recording I couldn't turn down his volume. Anyway enjoy!
