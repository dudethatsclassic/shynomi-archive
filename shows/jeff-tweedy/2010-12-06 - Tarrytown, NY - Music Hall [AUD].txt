Jeff Tweedy
Music Hall
Tarrytown, NY
December 6, 2010

The Music Hall is an old venue, the rows are small and the seats are packed 
tight together, I guess people were much smaller back then.  Some of the seats 
are what you might call a little squeaky, during quite points you may hear a 
squeak, as someone gets up.  There are two points in the show where the taper 
actually had his microphones knocked over, the beginning of Via Chicago and the
end of Late Greats, so there is some noise on the tape.  Editing was done with 
Audacity, for the most part is was only used between songs to bring the audience
down and make the recording more enjoyable.  However during the two songs where 
the microphones where knocked over some editing was done, I isolated the loud 
"thuds" and adjusted the volume to manageable levels.

Original taper - Josh Sarner
Editing and packaging - John Bosco
