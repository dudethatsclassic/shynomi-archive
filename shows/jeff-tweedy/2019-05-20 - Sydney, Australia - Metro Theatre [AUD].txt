Show Notes: Jeff's first ever fully solo show in Australia was a real treat, full of brilliant songs, hilarious stories and nice audience singalongs. Apparently the Metro never usually has seats in it (this is my first time visiting), but for this show it was seated on the floor with GA standing from half way back. Jeff makes mention of this during the next show in Brisbane - apparently a lot of people were unhappy with there being seats. No complaints from me though.

Recording Notes: Sounds excellent. I was sitting a few rows back directly in front of a speaker and it came out really well as a result. Crowd was enthuasiastic but nice and respectful. Jeff encouraged singing from Jesus, etc. onwards but the guy next to me only took him up on the first verse. Enjoy!
