MISC NOTES:
First time seeing Ben Gibbard. Very good show!! I was somehow caught off guard by the extremely low volume
level, so I had several changes of gain throughout the first two songs, which I tried very hard to level-out
in post production. I feel good about the balance, however the EQ was a little rough and the low levels
resulted in a fairly sizable noise floor. All in all, I'm pleased with the tape, but I will know for next time
to expect these far lower sound levels!
