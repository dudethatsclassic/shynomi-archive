The Lone Star Cafe stood at 61 Fifth Avenue, at East 13th Street in Greenwich Village. Despite the name, it was a New York club specializing in country, roots and Texas music.

WHN-FM should remain on the cover as the broadcaster, but I would describe the circulating audio as a radio-transcription source, not definitively as an off-air FM capture.
