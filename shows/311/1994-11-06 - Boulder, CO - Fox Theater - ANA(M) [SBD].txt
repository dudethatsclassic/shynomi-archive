NOTE FROM ME: The exact date of this show is supposedly unknown to fans and for 
some reason 311 management as well, therefore it is known amongst 311 fans as the 
elusive "Secret Show - Crater Club - Boise, ID 10-xx-94". However, after doing some research and speaking with 
a former member of Billy Goat, I have come to the conclusion that this show took 
place on 11/06/94 at the Fox Theater in Boulder Colorado. Near the end of Disc 1, Track 07(Homebrew), 
Nick mentions that they had just finished playing 30 shows with The Urge and that this 
was their last nite with them. He then also gives respect to Billy Goat, a funk band out 
of Texas that opened for 311 and The Urge that night. I have spoken with Billy Goat's former 
drummer and Steve Ewing of the Urge and they both recalled that Billy Goat only opened for 
311 on Nov. 6th and Nov. 8th 1994, that was the only time Billy Goat ever played with 311, and that The Urge played with them on Nov. 6th but not on Nov. 8th. 
So, by process of elimination, this is most likely the correct date.
