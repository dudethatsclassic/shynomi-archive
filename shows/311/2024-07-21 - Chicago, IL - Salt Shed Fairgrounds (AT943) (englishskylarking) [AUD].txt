Notes:
Included extras are the sticker design by Garett Watt and some quick pics I took during the show.

This was my first 311 show and I had a good time. The band was having fun and so was everyone closer
to the front. Everyone was singing nearly every word of every song and otherwise having a good time.
There's persistant woo-ing and some whistling near to me between tunes that I ducked thanks to Steve's
manual duck plugin for Audacity, and a lot of louder clapping that I painstakingly enveloped. It's
not perfect, but the sound is otherwise good, and I just take it as the sometimes-reality of being
at a concert. Hopefully you can enjoy it, too!

Manual duck plugin for Audacity: https://forum.audacityteam.org/t/manual-duck-fade-down-and-back-up-again/24037
