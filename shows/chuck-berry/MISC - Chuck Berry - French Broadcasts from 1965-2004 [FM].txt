And now the sources.

All recordings made on cassettes, except for the 2004 set recorded on MD (Sony net MD MZ N710).
All are from radio FM, except for 10 and 16 (AM radio) and  14 and 15 (on line sound from TV).

The live shows took place in:
Paris, Olympia (01, 02) 1965, Musicorama show Europe 1, rebroadcasted in the 90's
Paris, Olympia (03) 1967 february 21th, Musicorama show Europe 1, rebroadcasted in the 90's
Paris, possibly Hippodrome de Pantin, (04 - 10), 1977 april, from France Inter
Nice Jazz festival (11 - 17) 1979 july from France Inter and TV (A2 or FR3)
Nice Jazz festival (18 - 20) 1981 july from France Inter  (featuring Ingrid Berry)
Deauville jazz festival (21 - 27) 1995 july 6th from France Inter
Antibes Jazz festival (29 - 37) 1995 july 23th from France Inter
Vienne jazz festival (38 - 48) 2004 june 30th from France Inter

Olivier (Lyontapes) made an upload from a Nice 1979 broadcast here; the songs were recorded on TV, my sources are not the same (see above).
