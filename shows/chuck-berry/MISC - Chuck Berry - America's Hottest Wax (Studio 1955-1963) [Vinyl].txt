CHUCK BERRY
AMERICA'S HOTTEST WAX
Remastered Edition

Originally issued on LP, Reelin' 001.

Lineage: LP > 32-bit .wav recording > Usenet > me > Audition 1.5 > data CD > FLAC > you

I restored this album as a project with a collector on Usenet in November 2004. I jumped at the chance, because this album is so rare, most of us have never seen it, and probably never will. He recorded his album in 32-bit and posted it for me in an empty binary group. I removed all the scratches, the sound of the turntable and the stylus passing through grooves, corrected the pitch and phase, and then reposted it on Usenet. Now I'm sharing it with you.

Artwork is included.

The contents of this album are all alternate takes of Chuck Berry songs recorded at Chess studios between 1955 and 1959.
