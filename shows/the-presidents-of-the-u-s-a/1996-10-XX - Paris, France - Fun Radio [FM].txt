I made a major investment in my audio equipment lately: I bought a 5 meter cable for a whole 11 euro and finally plugged my tape deck to my computer. Then the question was: what was I going to transfer? I am a maniacal bootleg collector but I never was into tape trading. So just for fun I decided to have a look in my old cardboard boxes in the attic, where all my self-taped things were resting in peace for years until now.
The first interesting thing I came across was a tape gathering several live radio broadcast from 95-96. This is a first generation tape on which I copied several broadcasts I taped on different cassettes. Unfortunately, I most likely taped over on the masters: I didn't know about generation loss and all that stuff back then - I was 15 and didn't even know what a bootleg was, let alone tape trading and such things. I used to tape a lot of live shows broadcasted back then and other tapes include complete gigs of Oasis, Pearl Jam, Jamiroquai, etc. I had a 90min tape of a radio show with The Cure, long interviews and a few songs played in the studio, unfortunately the tape is long lost (never lend mastertapes to supposed friends!!!).

About this release: All songs were recorded from "Fun Radio" (France), all soundboard recordings. Fun Radio was (and I guess still is) a very active sponsor and partner of live concerts and therefore used to broadcast the first 3-4 songs (or sometimes all songs) of shows they sponsored, live or afterwards.
Alas, the quality of those soundboards is often varying, as you can judge from this release: everything has been taped on the same brand of cassettes, with the same recorder, same recording levels (I had one of those stereo where the rec level is default), and still there were huge differences in levels and of course sound quality. Moreover my stereo signal was not perfect and some bits are plagued with radio static. Still the overall quality is not too bad, the static is sometimes distracting but not too annoying. The only mastering I did is balancing channels and normalising the sources before splitting into tracks. No other processing has been done. You get it raw :-) 

This tape also included two acoustic songs performed live in the radio studio by Soul Asylum, but there the signal interferences was really too important and the performance not enjoyable at all. There was also a filler of Pearl Jam live in Berlin, but I should have the complete broadcast of this on another tape. All in all, what remained fitted on a standard 80min CDR.

It's always big fun to hear American artists speak French to the audience when the play in a Frenchspeaking country. Apart from the usual "Bonsoir Paris", the RHCP remembered the few words of bad language they knew - just listen to Dave Navarro asking Anthony Kiedes "Suce ma bite" (Suck my dick). Chris Ballew (I think) of PUSA did an excellent French spoken interlude during "MAch 5", describing the car of his dreams en français dans le texte. Only Dave Grohl of FF seems to know very little French. 

Enjoy!
	- Furry Animal (16 Feb 05)
