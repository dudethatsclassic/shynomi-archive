-- Notes --
The Carolines and The Village Green opened. Debut performance of the unreleased song 'Will You Be My Girlfriend?' PUSA's taping/trading policy at the time did not allow the use of external microphones, so this recording was kept out of circulation. In 2023, the band held a Reddit AMA, where I asked and received permission to post the recording online.

A disc swap for the CSC source occurs at the beginning of Dune Buggy, so the recording temporarily drops to mono. The CSC source was retransferred from the master MiniDiscs using an MZ-M200. The original digital transfer from 2005 has been used for the CSB source (all attempts to retransfer this source later with the MZ-M200 contained read errors/audio dropouts).
