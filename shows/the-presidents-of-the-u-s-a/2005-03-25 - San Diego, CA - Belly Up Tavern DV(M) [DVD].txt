The Presidents of The United States Of America
March 25, 2005, San Diego CA, Belly Up Tavern - DV(M) to DVD
Source: Filmed on Sony Handycam DCR-HC32 NTSC in LP (90min) mode. MiniDV played back through that camera to Panasonic DVR-210-s DVD recorder via firewire.

An epic performance by PUSA! Unobstructed balcony-shot video with great audio. Very little audience chatter. If you like PUSA at all, then you should download this DVD.

This is the first concert that I ever filmed. I filmed from the balcony through glass for most of the concert. The glass was mostly clean, except that there was a little smudge that sometimes blurred Chris's face. Toward the end of the show, I stood up and filmed above the glass. This upload a copy of the original DVD that I made. This DVD contains the entire PUSA performance that evening.
The included pics in the folder were downloaded from the following website: http://www.dotphoto.com/ViewAlbum.asp?AID=2353453. If you look very closely, you can spot me in the background of a couple pics with my camera.
