The Presidents of The United States Of America
Dec 9, 2003 - The Old Farmers Market, Los Angeles CA
Source: Sony D8(M) to DVD received from the filmer. 

The filmer is right up front near Chris, so the video is great - just a little shaky sometimes. You can see Chris and Dave very well, but Chris is often in front of Jason. Unfortunately, the audio is pretty distorted for much of the show. It doesn't sound terrible, but you may not want to turn this one up very loud. This is the worst-sounding of the 3 known-source PUSA DVDs that I have, but it's still a good DVD, overall.

Very fun show with a great Froggie and hilariously botched Kitty. At one point, Chris breaks one of his guitar strings. He finishes the show with 1 string.

Support the band, share this recording freely with fans, and never sell this DVD!
