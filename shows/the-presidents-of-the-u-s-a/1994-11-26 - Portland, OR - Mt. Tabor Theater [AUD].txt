The Presidents first Portland gig. I was very psyched, as I had been listening nonstop to their demo tape and the previous show I recorded. I was psyched it was in the Mt. Tabor Theater, as it was close by. I don't really remembering the room sounding bad, but listening to the tape again...reverb-o-rama. It gets a little nutty when te volume gets up there. The band has some nice commentary and fun with it.

Not as "close" as the Crocodile show, but better than I remembered.

enjoy, and again I say: If you can't appreciate the Presidents, then your fun muscle is seriously atrophied.

don't bootleg this. just don't.
