Sound Comments/Faults?: Good quality matrix. Neither of the individual sources were ideal. The CA-14s was a little flat probably due to me taping it with a non-sound transparent hat*, the B3s source was quite distant. I decided to matrix the two, the CA-14s was the better sounding source but by adding the B3s I’ve added some more bass and richness into the recording. A raucous show – I had to move to the back of the GA floor after getting hammered in the mosh pit that I wasn’t expecting. Audience noise is moderate, most near microphone audience noise are just screams and a bit of chat, the audience singing is significant but away from the microphone and quite complementary. 

Show Comments: Track 26 which has multiple teases goes for about 5 minutes. This venue no longer exists, but it was quite unique for Melbourne. It was a standing venue but over 3 levels [capacity around 2000 I think], so people would stand all along the stairs up to the other balconies. Thus, a bit like in a church, audience singing is amplified considerably by the nature of the space, as demonstrated in this tape.
