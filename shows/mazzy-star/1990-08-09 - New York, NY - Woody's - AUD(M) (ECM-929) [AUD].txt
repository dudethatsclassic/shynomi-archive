Mazzy Star - 1990-08-09 (Aug. 9)
Woody's, NYC
Audience recording
Sony D6 analog cassette recorder/Sony EMC929 mic/Maxell EXII-Sm90 minute master cassette

FLAC conversion from WAV file taken from tape master kindly 
supplied by original taper Scott Simpson, who made a number of other excellent 
Mazzy Star recordings in the 1990s: Maxwell's 1993-11-6, CBGBs 1994-03-17,
Mercury Lounge 1994-05-21, Supper Club 1996-12-01.
Recordings of the Maxwell's & CBGBs shows taken directly from Scott's tape masters 
have previously been posted to Dime.

As far as I know, other circulating boots versions
of this show are all derived from Scott's recording but, unlike this one, others are at least
one analog tape generation away from the original. This one derives directly from the
master tape. An approx. 0.5 second long audio error (loud noise burst on right chammel
caused by a mic jack) between 00:01:57 and 00:01:58 
during the first song "Be My Angel", & audible in other circulating 
boots of this recording, I repaired with Audacity software.

This Mazzy Star show's a valuable document as there don't seem a lot of 1990
shows circulating, & this one's the best quality 1990 recording I know of. Plus, it boasts 4 
unreleased songs (see track listing). At least one of the 4, "Where Did You Run To" was a 
song Hope also sang with her early duo Going Home. Hope's unusually chatty with the audience
at this show & what she says is, intentionally or unintentionally, mostly hilarious.
This recording also has the only live version I've heard of "Before I Sleep".
