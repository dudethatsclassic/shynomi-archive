Artist: Mazzy Star
City: Antwerp (Belgium)
Venue: Pacific
Date: 22 June 1994


Recording equipment: Sony Digital Micro Recorder NT-2 + Sony NTC-90
Transfer (June 2014): Sony Digital Micro Recorder NT-2 > Creative Sound Blaster X-Fi Xtreme Audio > Sony Sound Forge Pro 10.0
Re-editing (November 2019): iZotope RX 7 Audio Editor (see lineage file for more info)

Setlist sheet scanned in 600 dpi, no descreening, scale 100%. Some colour correction was applied to correct the yellowing of the page.
Flyer scanned in 600 dpi, no descreening, scale 100%.


As far as I recall there was no support act, despite what the flyer says. Note that the set list taken from the stage after the show differs slightly from the one that the band actually played. They didn't play "Halah" and 
"That Way Again", but instead played "Into Dust", "Hair and Skin" (a Green On Red cover) and "Blue Flower" (a Slapp Happy cover). Tape flip occured during "So Tonight That I Might See" so there's a slight glitch for a couple 
of seconds (3:19 to 3:22).
