Mazzy Star - 1994-03-17 (St. Patrick's Day), CBGB, NYC

Audience recording by Scott Simpson who recalls:
"The CB’s show was done entirely with the lights down and them
facing away from the stage."

Sony D6 analog audio cassette recorder/CDIV metal tape/
Audio-Technica stereo mic (model unknown)


Track Listing:                                
1) So Tonight That I Might See                     
2) Rock Section (The Colours Out of Time cover) 
3) Bells Ring                                   
4) Fade Into You                                
5) I'm Less Here (unreleased until 2014)        
6) Flowers in December                          
7) Halah                                        
8) She Hangs Brightly                           
9) Blue Flower (Slapp Happy cover)  

62:12 mins. total

"I'm Less Here" remained an unreleased song until 2014 when the 
band finally released a studio recording of it as a single. 
This is one of only two live versions of the song I've heard. 
The other is from Copenhagen, 2000. The song may have been 
originally slated for Mazzy Star's 1996 album as the song's 
lyrics contain the mysterious line "among my swan", & that 
line's the title of the 1996 album, "Among My Swan."
