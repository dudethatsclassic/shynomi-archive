A member of a Facebook fan group discovered Dime member vacantrush had shared this recording at another
torrent sharing site, echoeshub. Though vacantrush has shared many Mazzy Star shows via Dime,
I'm not aware of him having shared this one via Dime. It may have been an oversight on his part,
so I'm sharing it on Dime now.

*often incorrectly titled "Where Did You Run To." A set list used on stage in 1990 gives the correct title.
It's a song originally played by Hope's 1980s duo with Sylvia Gomez, Going Home.
Opal with Hope singing played the song live some times (1987-88) and Mazzy Star played it live on their
1990 tour. This 1993 show may be the only post-1990 show I know of where Mazzy Star played the song live.
It was never released in a studio recording, although an unreleased studio recording of Going Home performing
the song exists 

**"I'm Less Here" did not get released till 2014 in a single. 
It was a song dating from the 1990s the band played live only occasionally. This is the earliest
live recording of the song I'm aware of. 

Hope at many shows barely spoke to audiences. At this show she speaks more than usual, 100+ 
words, a lot for her, which does include her complaining a couple of times about talkers in the audience.
