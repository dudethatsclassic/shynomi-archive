NOTES

Nice set from them before a nice (though at a few times distracted) crowd. They came on very late on a Sunday night so some folks were a bit surly at the beginning.

This was very early in my taping 'career' and I hadn't yet dialed in on the sweet spots at the Cat's Cradle. As a result that band sounds quite good but the vocals were very distant (even for Mazzy Star). I experimented with Izotope to try and raise them after a few tries I was able to get something I think is an improvement over the original. A few songs are somewhat marred by a distracted crowd but overall this sounds very nice.

I traded this on DAT with multiple people back in those days but as far as I can tell it hasn't made it's way online until now.

Enjoy,

mrpember, March 2021
