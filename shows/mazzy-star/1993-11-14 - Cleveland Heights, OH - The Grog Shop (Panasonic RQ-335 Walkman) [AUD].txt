11a (Crowd + MC) The crowd waits, then the MC announces: 
"Hey, we're sorry,but the band can't come back, it's too hot*. 
We'll cancel now. We're very sorry. Thanks a lot.

*The temperature in the club was reportedly swelteringly hot. 

Thanks go to Facebook fan Group member Ray Sferra who attended and recorded this show in 1993 and shared
his digital audio file he made from his cassette tape master. He used a Panasonic RQ-335 walkman-style 
cassette recorder. https://www.facebook.com/groups/6071129794/ 
I'm sharing his recording further on his behalf. 

Hope's voice sounded quite muffled on the source file, so I made some EQ adjustments to try to bring 
her voice out more clearly. And I reduced the volume of crowd noise between songs.

Ray also found in library archives a press review of the show from 1993, and a venue print ad from
1993 that menitioned the show. He also took a 2017 photo of the building with the address where The Grog Shop club
used to be in 1993. Copies of those are included in this folder, plus Ray's ticket stub from the show. 

You may notice in the middle of one of the later songs where the taper had to interrupt recording to flip his tape.

Thanks go to Ray for also sharing the 1990 Cleveland Mazzy show he also recorded. 
That one has a real rarity on it: a Mazzy Star song I'd not heard before.

Ray notes the club's stage in 1993 was located behind a window you see in the 2017 photo, the
one with the tree in front of it.
