Artist: Mazzy Star
City: Bruxelles (Belgium)
Venue: De Vaartkapoen
Date: 28 October 1993


Recording equipment: Sony Digital Micro Recorder NT-2 + Sony NTC-90
Transfer (December 2009): Sony Digital Micro Recorder NT-2 > Creative Sound Blaster X-Fi Xtreme Audio > Nero Wave Editor
Re-editing (November 2019): iZotope RX 7 Audio Editor (see lineage file for more info)

Setlist sheet scanned in 600 dpi, no descreening, scale 100%. Some colour correction was applied to correct the yellowing of the page.
Ticket scanned in 600 dpi, no descreening, scale 100%.


This was Mazzy Star's first concert in Belgium if I'm not mistaken. Not many people in attendance (you can hear the echo of handclaps and the most annoying sound of plastic cups being crushed). 
I think that little digital recorder from Japan did a pretty good job, the sound is nice and clear. A better microphone probably could have improved the sound a notch. But this wasn't mine, I just borrowed it and had to order the tapes
from the Sony shop in Brussels' city centre.

I wish I could make a new transfer of this tape but the recorder doesn't work anymore (it was a second-hand one that I bought in 2009 just to transfer the tapes to my computer). I'm not sure if I'll ever have a chance to redo the 
transfer so enjoy this one.
