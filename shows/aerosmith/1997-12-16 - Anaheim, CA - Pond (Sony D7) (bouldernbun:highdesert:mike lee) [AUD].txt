Aerosmith
12/16/97
The Pond
Anaheim, Ca

Uncirculated Aud Dat. Recorded @ 32 kHz/16 bit. This is lossless.

Taped by BouldernBun/Highdesert/Mike Lee
Floor section 101 - Row 11 - Seat 1 (20-30 feet from stage-right aisle)

Recorder : Sony D7. Mic : Sony Ecm 959A.
Transfer : Tascam DA 40, Soundblaster Elite Pro, hard drive
Post production : Sound Forge adjust volume, bass roll off, cut encore/ending audience deadwax, resample to 44.1

There is a source for this concert listed on etree though I don't think it is from my dat. For one Last Child was played between Stop Messing Around and The Farm. 
Last Child is not present on any etree source 12/16/97 setlist. I only ever did two transfers of this from the master dat and both were/are complete. 
One immediately after the show in 1997 (given to my sons who went to the show) using a crappy soundcard and this 2015 transfer. The 2015 transfer kicks
the 1997 transfers butt if this is the etree show. The sound gets muffled for a few seconds due to security during SOS. 
A really good tape and a fairly great Nine Lives performance. Tape has not been tracked.
