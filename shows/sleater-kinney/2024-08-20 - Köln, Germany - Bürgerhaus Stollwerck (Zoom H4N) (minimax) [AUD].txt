concert-info:
here's Sleater-Kinney at Cologne. A not sold-out show. For me it was the first time I saw this band and I wished I knew more of the songs as
they played so many nice songs.

It's always a bad decision to record bands from the front row in that location as the speakers are far away on both sides and the sound goes 
to all the rows in the back. So, this is a not that high quality recording, but better than nothing!!?!
But, as I hate standing in the back and seeing nothing, I prefer to see - with the risk of a bad recording. Sorry, folks
