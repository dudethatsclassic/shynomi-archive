Sebadoh - 2011 02 16 @ Record Bar, Kansas City, MO

Show note:
With most Sebadoh shows, things happen for no apparent reason.  The band starts off with On Fire but stop, pause then play Sixteen, then play On Fire Again in its whole form.  After Careful, an audience member sings Its So Hard to Fall In Love, then Lou launches into it acapella. The band ultimately plays for so long, the bar tells the crowd that they have to come collect all the alcohol as it is nearly past curfew, and the band then launches into its final song of the night.  Sounds like it was a fun show to be at. There's 5-6 shakey cam videos of this show on YouTube, just check this users's videos: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ehK8hoAQ__Q
