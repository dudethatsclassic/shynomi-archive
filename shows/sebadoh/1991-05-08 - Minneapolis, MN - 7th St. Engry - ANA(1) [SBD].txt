NOTES:

Tracking might be a bit off, but I did the best I could. Sometimes it's hard to know if they're playing the next song or are merely tuning up.

I think I've shared this earlier, back in 2005 or something, but that one was EQ'd and NR'd (oh, the horror). This is a raw transfer without any tinkering. I was tempted to fix the minor phase issue or fold down to mono using one of the channels (suggestion: use the right channel as it's cleaner, the left one has a low hum throughout) but decided not to since I'm too lazy and couldn't be bothered. I just wanted to get this out there before throwing away the CDR. 

The cue and log files are included for reference.

It's time to move on and get a life. I think I've actually listened to less than 3% of all the live recordings I've collected throughout the 15 years I've been into this stuff. 

Enjoy the show!
