Foo Fighters - 2023 08 12 @ Golden Gate Park, San Francisco CA

Show note:
Outside Lands 2023. It was cold and foggy, and winds started up, so there is some phasing due to the wind.  The Foos stated at 8 PM, and at about halfway through a lot of the crowd went to catch Lana Del Ray, so we were able to move up. During the band intros, Dave talks about how Josh drummed on Michael Buble's songs when he spots someone holding a Michael Buble sign in the audience and invites him on the stage to sing "Haven't Met You Yet" - of course it was Michael Buble.  Shame Shame was a last minute change, with Violet Grohl on vocals.  Monkey Wrench was on the setlist, but dropped due to time considerations - curfew was at 10 PM, and the band literally played the last note of Everlong then set clock hit 10 PM.
