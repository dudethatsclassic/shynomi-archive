Recording Notes:
[1] I managed to not unpause the R-09 at the right time, so I lost the first
    minute or so of Let It Die.

[2] During the beginning of this song, the band moved to a smaller stage in
    the middle of the arena floor.  They switched to a different set of
    speakers, which I was almost directly beneath.  This explains the change
    in sound quality for the next several songs.

[3] Dave starts this song alone on the smaller stage while the band moves to
    the main stage.  Dave will join them on the main stage for the conclusion
    of this song and the sound will move back to the main stacks for the
    remainder of the show.


Other Notes:
[a] "Marigold" is an old Nirvana B-Side, written by Dave Grohl in 1990.
    More info @ http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marigold_(song)

[b] "Keep the Car Running" is a song by Arcade Fire
