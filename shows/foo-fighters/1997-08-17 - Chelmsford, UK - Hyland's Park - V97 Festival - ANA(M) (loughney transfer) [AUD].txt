Foo Fighters - 1997 08 17 V97 Festival, Hyland's Park, Chelmsford, UK	

Dave Grohl - Guitar, Vocals
Nate Mendel - Bass Guitar
Taylor Hawkins - Drums
Pat Smear - Guitar

Transfer note: Sounds like it was recorded far from the stage, the crowd is usually louder than the band. I needed to raise the bass a little, reduce some of the crowd noise, limit the clapping volume and then run a 2:1 @ 15 db compressor to raise the music level.  Quality is still quite poor, but I figured I'd still share it since I went to the trouble of transfering and editing it.  Maybe someone likes it, but probably not.
