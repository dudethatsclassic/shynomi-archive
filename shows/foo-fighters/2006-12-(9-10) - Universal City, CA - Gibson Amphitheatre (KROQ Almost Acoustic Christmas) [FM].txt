NOTES: This is a recording of the Encore Broadcast. It aired on Monday, the day after the second night of the concert. It began at 6:00 PM and ended sometime around 11:20 PM. Since this is from an FM broadcast in the United States, the profanity has been scrambled out. In putting this together, I left out the DJ chatter between each set but left in the station ID at the end of the interviews because it would have been rather cumbersome. Since the Foo Fighters played on both nights of this event, I do not know whether the broadcasted songs were from one night only (possibly the second) or were a mashup of both nights, so I left the date blank for both their sets. The first song in Gnarls Barkley set (track 13) ends suddenly and contains a few seconds of silence. This is just how it was broadcast. My reception is not the greatest so there may be some noise/fuzz in the recording. However, it's pretty loud most of the time so if it even is there, it is hardly noticeable (to my ears at least). There are no fades. I did the best I could with tracking. I put the sets in the order that they were broadcast. I found the set times below online. None of the sets are complete.
