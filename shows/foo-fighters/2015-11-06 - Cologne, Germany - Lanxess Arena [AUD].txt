Had pretty good seats and a good position to tape. Think the recording reflects the sound in the venue. This is the raw file. No equalizer, compression or anything else.
It was a great show. Saw them in 1995, 1997 and 2000. Think I won`t wait another 15 years before I see them again. Dave`s throne is kind of funny and it doesn`t stop him from rocking as hell.
Wish they had more songs from "Wasting light" in their setlist. Anyway….you can`t have it all.

Enjoy & Share, but never sell this recording.
Please go and buy their records, go to their shows and support them in any way you can. They deserve it.
