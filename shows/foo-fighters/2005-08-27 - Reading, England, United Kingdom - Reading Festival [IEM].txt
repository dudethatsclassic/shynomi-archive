Info
----

Thought it was about time I shared this one.  Been in my collection a while now. Personally my favourite Foos boot.  Dave plays drums on Cold Day In The Sun and notes that it was 13 years sine he last played drums at Reading (at the iconic Nirvava show).  I flicked around the different monitor feeds programmed into the receiver a few times looking for the best one before finally settling on what I think was Dave's feed.  I recorded the same feed at the Millenium Stadium in December of the same year when they supported Oasis, a copy of which I think is already in circulation.  Enjoy!
