
Artist :- Foo Fighters
Venus :- International Arena, Cardiff, Wales
Date :- 20th November 2002


This is the complete recording from the above mentioned gig. I'm uploading it as I noticed on the http://www.foofighterslive.com/ website there is no known recording, so decided to remedy that fact. After taking advise from some of the foofighterslive forum members, I've left the recording in it's 'raw' form. I consulted the forum as the recording is in need of some tweaking, I recorded it using Sony Minidisc recorder MZ-R55  and Sony Headphones, using the headphones as a mic left the recording sounding quite tinny. I've uploaded an mp3 sample, so if you're not a FF completist, you may wish to hear the sample before downloading. 

http://www.sendspace.com/file/r094g9

During the first track 'All My Life' you will hear crowd members singing (me & my mates!!!!) :-), but other than that there's minimal crowd singing during songs.  Inbetween songs, there are several amusing moments (maybe annoying to some).....my brother telling me about a girl hearing him comment on her camels toe and me and my bro singing 'Onward Christian Soldiers' and 'Enjoy The Silence'....I don't know why!

MZ-R55 and Sony Headphones - transfered via line in on soundcard - track marked using Goldawave - flac
