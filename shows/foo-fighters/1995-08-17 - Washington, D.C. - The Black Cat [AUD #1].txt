Foo Fighters

August 17, 1995

Black Cat

Washington, DC



FOR TRADING/SHARING ONLY



Foo Fighters fans might find this show interesting for several reasons.  First, as of December 2008, it doesn't appear that a copy has surfaced (according to www.foofighterslive.com).  Second, Dave Grohl's tease and comparison of Purple Rain to Big Me is worth a listen.  Finally, Craig Wedren of Shudder To Think joins the band for the encore to sing Lovin' Touchin' Squeezin'.  There are a few sounds problems, explained below, but this is otherwise a pretty nice recording.

Thanks to the taper, a person I traded with many years back.  We lost contact a long time ago, so I have no way of knowing if he'd want to be named here.  With that said, I'll just leave the thank you as an anonymous one.
