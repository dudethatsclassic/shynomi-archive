Review
This is a complete video of the performance but it has two small problems. First of all there is no audio for the first two minutes of the video due to technical difficulties. Secondly up until around three and a half minutes in there is a an issue with the interlacing of the video, either caused by the broadcast or the capture and encoding of the DVD. Up until that point horizontal 'lines' can be seen in the picture. After these problems are rectified in the first song the rest of the video is very nice with great quality video and audio. Video shots are clear and crisp and the audio has a nice clear mix with good punch and there are just a few further audio dropouts, usually affecting only one channel for a second or two.
