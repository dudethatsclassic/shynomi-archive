Crowd was rough the first 3-4 songs on the floor and then everyone calmed down. 
You will notice the sound improves as hands and bodies were not being tossed
around in front of me. Please note I removed the 5 minutes of crowd noise 
during the encore break (after "All My Life"). Dave, Pat and Taylor were 
on camera backstage taunting the audience with how many songs they would 
come back out and play - 6 ended up being the number! Great show, check it
out if you are a fan! Im pretty happy with how the recording turned out.
