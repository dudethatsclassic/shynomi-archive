WOW what a band!! So glad I decided to go along on a Tuesday night. Well worth the lack of sleep for work today. One of the loudest bands I've ever heard, and Dave sounds amazing live!! Just before Foo Fighters came out I decided to join my friends in section 136, but got bumped about a minute into Everlong. I got so lucky and within a minute I found a spot in the handicap section at top of section 101. The sound was epic and I had a very cool crowd in front of me, aisle behind me. A few close clappers here and there, but otherwise zero distractions. I did no editing other than normalize to 95% and tracking. Crank it up!!

Note: During band introductions, the audience and the band sang happy birthday to Chris Shiflett's son, Liam, and Taylor Hawkins led a round of "Day O."
