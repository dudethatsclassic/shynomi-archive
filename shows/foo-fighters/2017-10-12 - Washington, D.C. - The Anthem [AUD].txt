MISC NOTES:
This is a recording of the sold-out Grand Opening of The Anthem!  Guests entered to an unannounced set from DC Go-Go legends, Trouble Funk, who played a 45min set ahead of The Struts.  I did not tape anyone but Foo Fighters.  The venue is beautiful and the crowd was PUMPED, with some folks queuing up outside at early as 3am for the 6:30pm doors!  Foo Fighters are among the most consistently awesome bands I've ever seen and this show is certainly no exception.  Dave Grohl makes several references to Seth Hurwitz, who is the co-owner of IMP, the promotion and production company which owns and operates several large and prestigious venues across the region, including The Anthem.  I would also like to document that in the lead up to Seth's accompanying the Foo Fighters in a rendition of a Stones song, the object of Dave's curiosity was a wireless microphone which Taylor was handed by tech off stage left. 

Enjoy!
