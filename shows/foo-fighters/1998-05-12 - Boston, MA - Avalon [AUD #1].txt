NOTES
---------------
Great energy coming from the band and Dave is making the funnies like normal. Before "See You" he talks about
how much he hates "The Smiths" and says that he imagines that "See You" sounds like a Smiths song.  He also
apologizes after it that the song sucks so bad. Then before "Up In Arms" he says "I'm gonna try to sing this
one like Morrissey" then laughs. Talks about having a bean bag fight the last time they played there too. 
The master MD was sped up about 10% so I had to slow it down in Audacity.  All analog copies of this were always
recorded at the wrong speed from this MD. This will be the only circulating copy of this show with that corrected.
