Foo Fighters - 2026 05 02 @ Starland Ballroom, Seyerville NJ 

Note:
Show was announced 3 days before it took place, tickets had to be purchased in person. I didn't attend, as I was at another show, but a friend did, and this is his recording. The venue holds 2500 and tickets were $30. Crowd noise, but that's what it is. Animal was  dedicated to a fan in a W.A.S.P. shirt, Signs was a nod to Tesla's cover, Aurora was dedicated to Taylor Hawkins.
