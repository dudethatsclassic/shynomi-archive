NOTES
---------------
Really fun show here! Not the best sound quality but to me this is a FF classic. Dave and the rest of the band seem to have a
great time and Dave is very talkative. He dedicates See You to Robert Smith because he thinks it sounds like a Cure song. He 
also talks about how they got kicked off of TFI Friday the day before because they tried to make them look stupid and they 
didn't take it. This is also a noteable show because it is the first time the Foos ever played with the guys from Queen. 
During the encore Roger Taylor comes onstage to play the Queen song Sheer Heart Attack with the band. Really good cover of 
the song if I say so myself. This was also the last performance the band did in 1997. Enjoy!!
