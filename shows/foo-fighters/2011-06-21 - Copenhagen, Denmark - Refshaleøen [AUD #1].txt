NOTES
---------------
First of all a big THANK YOU to Glenn Nielsen for recording this for me. This recording is VERY OVERLOADED
therefore it is for completist FF collectors only I would say. For a first time recording though I think
Glenn did a great job and I'm happy at least any copy of this show exists. I'm really sorry it took me 
this long to get it posted. I can't believe it's been an entire year since I got this MD back in the mail.
The gear used was not the best as you can see but it was the best "loaner" gear I had at the time. I recently
updated my own rig however so I have better gear to loan out if anyone is ever interested in taping the Foos.
If you are interested feel free to contact me and I'm happy to loan something out for someone to tape. 
FF tapers seem to be dwindling these days so I am always stoked to have a show covered. The taper accidentally 
hit stop during the recording so the last part of Let It Die is missing.
