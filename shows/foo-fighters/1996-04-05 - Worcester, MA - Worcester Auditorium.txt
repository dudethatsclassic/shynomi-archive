NOTES:
--------------
This was the tapers first time recording a show so I think it came out great all things considered. He did not want to be 
credited but did want it shared. There are spots throughout on the master tapes where the tapes had degenerated due to the way
they were stored over the years. That's where the warbling comes from in spots of the show. For the most part however it sounds
great and it's always nice to hear a "new" Foo Fighters source. Please thank the taper for sharing this. I haven't been able 
to contact any of the other tapers from this show as they have stopped trading or passed away. If you have any information on
where we could locate other tapers or you have a low gen copy of another source from this show please contact me or one of the 
other guys at: www.foofighterslive.com Enjoy!!
