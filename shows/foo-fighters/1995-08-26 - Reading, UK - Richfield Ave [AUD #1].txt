NOTES:	This was the first appearance of the Foo Fighters at the Reading
	Festival.  They headlined the second stage tent on Saturday while
	Bjork was on the main stage.  Mean Fiddler, the festival's organizers,
	realized they had made a mistake putting the Foo Fighters on the
	second stage instead of the main stage, but the Foo Fighters refused
	to switch stages.  So the second tent was PACKED!  Throughout the
	performance Dave kept reminding the crowd to be careful and  to take
	care of everyone and he asked a couple of times for some of the
	members of the audience that had decided to climb a pole to get down.
	At one time he even stated that they were being kicked off of the
	stage because of the unruly crowd.  This show was taped on a Chrome
	TDK tape and according to the taper came out a little muffled, hence
	the raise of the high end.  Unfortunately the master tape was thrown
	away after the first transfer, so this will be the only transfer
	available.  After Podunk, there is a quick tape flip cut; no music
	is missing.  Please enjoy this recording and try to tape and share
	other FF shows.  MANY THANKS TO PETE!
