NOTES:

The original plan for Sunday was to get their early, grab a bite to eat, stop by the Zenni truck to grab some free sunglasses, and then chill at the main stage waiting for Jimmy Eat World. But all that changed right before we left the house, as the Foo Fighters tweeted out that Dave Grohl and Greg Kurstin would be performing the "Hanukkah Sessions" live at the small acoustic stage at 2:45pm (half hour before Jimmy Eat World were to begin). Well this worked out perfectly and completely changed our plans. I had to forfeit the sunglasses, and instead we grabbed some $30 Crab rolls (expensive, but definitely the best food I ate all weekend there, hands down) and then made it over the small stage right when Bottlerock pinged the performance to their app, which let the general crowd know about it, which means we luckily avoided the massive crowd flocking there, as they all arrived after us, allowing us to get a prime front row spot. We of course had to sit there in the hot baking sun for an hour and a half, but that's festival life right? Oh how I've missed it!

I really didn't know what the "Hanukkah Sessions" were, as I don't follow Dave or the Foo Fighters online much, but to my surprise (and delight), Dave was on drums for the show, and Greg was on keyboards. It turns out it's a cover set of different Jewish artists (all different ranges and genres even), and I was quite impressed with the show. Dave and I made eye contact a few times, so that was cool too (being Nirvana has always been, and always will be, my favorite band). Also originally being from Napa, I ran into an old school buddy behind me (figured that was bound to happen sooner or later). 

For being front row at a small stage with okay (at best) speakers, this turned out pretty good I think. The vocals are a little quiet in the mix, but I think that's really just due to the fact that Dave wasn't only singing, but pounding away at the drums, and being so close, that was picked up a little more than usual I suspect. In any case, I think it sounds great, and if you're the mood for some great covers (I recommend Elastica's "Connection" personally), then download and enjoy!
