REVIEW:
This is a compilation containing all of the UK broadcasts of the V Festival in Chelmsford, England. The first three songs are from the very first broadcast and features three songs. The next two tracks are from a second broadcast, featuring one different song. The last substancial section contains PRO #1c which features all of the songs of PRO #1a and PRO #1b but has a completely remixed audio track and features a few different camera angles, which is why all sources are needed. All three sources have very similar video and audio quality having been recorded in the same way. Video quality is usual for a DVB-T broadcast, good clarity but some macroblocking is present. Audio quality is excellent on all three sources, with a different mix on the third.
