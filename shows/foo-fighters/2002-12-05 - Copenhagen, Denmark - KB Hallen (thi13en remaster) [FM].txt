Big thanks to Milkysunshine for bringing this here, then  
requesting one of my overrated remasters. So to the dedicated  
fans out there I say:

"Hey, stop enjoying yourselves so much, would you please?  
You're really bugging a couple of the negative creeps out there with your zealous attitude towards a good sounding live show. I think you should be using that energy on finding all those  
other remastering guru's out there, and convince them to redo  
all my stuff to finally put me in my place."

Honestly, I'd love it if someone would do what I do to all the  
shows I've done, and please please please do it better too, by  
all means. Then I can go back to just leeching and listening to 
quality shows, and spend my spare time doing some things around 
the house that need work, like my leaky roof in the sunroom, or 
patching the stairs out front. Don't even get me started on the 
paint job the inside needs...


OK, I had some Foo to Fight myself, I guess.

So here's the Foo Fighters from a really good show in 2002. To  
me, One By One was the pinnacle of this band, though strangely, 
it doesn't get the setlist attention here that I think it  
deserved. But you do get a whole bunch of the hits, which is  
cool too.

The original had an inaccurate setlist, and a whole mess of DJ  
interruptions. This has all been fixed, but no show was cut at  
all; stage banter, applause, story telling, and awkward silences still remain intact.
