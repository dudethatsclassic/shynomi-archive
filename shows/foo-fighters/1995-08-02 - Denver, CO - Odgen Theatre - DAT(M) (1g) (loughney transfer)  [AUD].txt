Foo Fighters - 1995-08-02 Ogden Theatre, Denver, CO

"This requires alternative tuning. Uncomfortable pause."

I'm transferring all of my tapes.  I have a lot of early Foo Fighters tapes, all were recieved shortly after the shows - so mostly low-gen, no MP3 or compression.  Quality is mixed, and probably most of these are circulating. I'm transferring everything and sharing them in case I have something rare / unique, etc.  If you already have a digital copy of a show, you might want to check before you download. I'm providing samples.

Notes:
Sound is a bit distant, but not a lot of crowd noise. Tape flip caused a small cut in Exhausted. Sounds like the same source as https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zsywY5dDpCw
