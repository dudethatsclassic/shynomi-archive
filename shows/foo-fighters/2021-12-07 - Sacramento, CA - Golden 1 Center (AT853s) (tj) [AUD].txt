My second time seeing Foo Fighters this year and my third time seeing Dave Grohl perform in something and all I can say is holy shit that man is a beast. I don't think he stopped moving once the entire show. Even when he stands still he's chewing gum. FF put on an incredible show, playing a mix of songs from across their nine albums. Dave did a fantastic job of interacting with the audience and the entire band was in fine form and sounded great. Was a real pleasure to see them and I hope they come to the Bay Area again soon. I grabbed the setlist from the incredibly nice cameraman in the back after the show and included it in the directory.

I met up with several tapers before the show and it was a real pleasure! Looking forward to taping another show together again. You never know what is in the works...
