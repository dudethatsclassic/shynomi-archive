Foo Fighters
07/24/95
La Luna, Portland, OR

Source: Audio Technica Lapel Mic -> Unknown Aiwa Deck
Generation: ANA(M) -> DAT(1) -> Normalize -> FLAC
Notes: I borrowed a bunch of masters from the taper several years ago, including this gem. Originally I transfered the analog stuff using a Kenwood CT1050 -> Tascam DA-20MKII. About a year ago I transferred the DAT to FLAC and have been meaning to torrent it and finally had the chance. The digital transfer was done with the same Tascam DA-20MKII -> Echo Mia midi -> Adobe Audition. I removed the loud pops from the tape flip and normalized the whole thing before downsampling from 16/48Khz to 16/44.1Khz. Split the whole thing up in CD WAVE.
