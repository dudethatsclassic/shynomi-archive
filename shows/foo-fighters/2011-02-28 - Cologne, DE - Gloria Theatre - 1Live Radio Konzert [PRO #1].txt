1Live Radio Konzert
GLORIA THEATRE
COLOGNE, DE

Exclusive concert, organized by 1Live, a radio station 
from Cologne. This show was limitted to only 600 people. 
Tickets weren't sold. You could only win them during a 
lottery in the radio or a few websites. Too bad a lot of 
people were unlucky enough to stay at home, including myself.
Fortunately, the concert was broadcast live. Still great.
I connected my radio with an instrument cable through my guitar
Preamp into my PCs soundcard. No equalizing, no effects.
