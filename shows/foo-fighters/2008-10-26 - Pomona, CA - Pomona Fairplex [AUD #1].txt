NOTES
--------------
This was recorded 2 rows in front of the sound booth using my iPod. I left my USB power 
supply in the car thinking I had plenty of power to record just the foos set.  I was wrong.
The last song "The Pretender" is the only song missing from this recording. I have also found
out the down side to using an iPod to record shows as a result of this as well. When the 
battery dies while in record mode, the WAV file is saved with errors. In this whole recording
there are split second drop outs about 7 or 8 times in each song. I would only recommend this
if you are a completist foo fighters collector.The Sound quality is good but the dropouts can
be quite annoying. I hope another version of this show surfaces as I would love an upgrade.
