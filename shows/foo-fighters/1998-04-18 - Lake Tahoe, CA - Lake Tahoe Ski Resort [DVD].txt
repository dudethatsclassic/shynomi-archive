REVIEW:
This show took place at the base of snow covered mountain and the taper shot the video from a gradient towards the back, slightly left of stage. The shaky for the first couple of minutes but quickly steadies and for the most part shots of the stage are clear and steady. The video quality is quite good with little sign of generation loss, although the picture is slightly 'foggy' with washed out colours, likely because of the conditions. Audio quality is surprisingly clear and crisp for an outdoor amatuer video. During the last song 'I'll Stick Around' the fans start throwing snowballs at the band and the venue lifts a plastic curtain to protect them, but Grohl immediatly tells them to put it down and sees the fun side of it, trying to dodge the shots. They continute playing the song before surrendering to the deluge of snow and call a cease. Fun show.
