Foo Fighters - 1998 01 22 Akasaka Blitz, Tokyo

I'm transferring all of my tapes.  I have a lot of early Foo Fighters tapes, all were recieved shortly after the shows - so mostly low-gen, no MP3 or compression.  Quality is mixed, and probably most of these are circulating. I'm transferring everything and sharing them in case I have something rare / unique, etc.  If you already have a digital copy of a show, you might want to check before you download.
