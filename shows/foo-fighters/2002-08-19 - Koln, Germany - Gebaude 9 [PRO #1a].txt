REVIEW:
This DVD is the most complete of the various broadcasts of this show. The video itself is 16:9 widescreen but was broadcast in a letterbox 4:3 format with the broadcasters logo overlapping the image in the top right corner. The video is quite good quality but can get a bit fuzzy and blocky at times, thanks to the lower quality broadcast format. It also doesn't help that the small venue wasn't brilliantly lit, so it gets quite dark in places with just a few spot lights around the stage. Generally though it's a very nice, watchable video and audio quality is also very good, typical for a modern digital TV broadcast.
