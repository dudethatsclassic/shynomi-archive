Source 1: Icom IC-R5 > Edirol R-09 (44.1MHz 16 bit) Left Channel

Sources 1 and 2 converted to mono and SOurce 2 converted to fake stereo using mda_stereo.  Mixed and mastered in Adobe Audition CS5, Har Bal 2.3 and Izotope Ozone 4.0

Info
----

Two source mix of this show.  Location was directly in front of a repeater stack so a good quality audience.  I was a little disappointed with the IEM feed I picked up.   I recorded Chris Shiflett’s monitor which had almost been a full and balanced mix in previous shows.  Now it is just his guitar and drums and a tiny bit of Dave’s vocal.  I mixed them anyway and it sounds ok but not as good as I had hoped.
