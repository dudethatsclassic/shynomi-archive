NOTES
---------------
Here is another Foo Fighters show that really hasn't circulated in any form. Robert Bowman sent me a copy
of this on CDR a year or so ago. Since then he was able to track down the taper and obtained the ANA(M) from
him. All of the legwork and praise goes to him on this one. I only transferred it for him. Better sounding 
and more complete than the AUD#1 of this show. There are 2 or 3 split second dropouts in the recording but
they are in the master tape. Nothing significant is gone though. Only a second or two each time it happens.
Even though this came from the taper there is no gear info attached. I'm assuming the taper didn't know otherwise
Robert would have given me that info. I hope you guys enjoy this. New shows from The Colour and the Shape era
are always new and exciting to me.
