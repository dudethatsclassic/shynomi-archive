NOTES
---------------
Good hometown show for Dave. Before the encore someone throws a tape onstage and Dave asks the guy who
threw it if it sucks because there were a bunch in their dressing room too. He thinks about throwing it 
to the sound guy and having him put it on but doesn't want to hit anyone in the crowd with it so he says
he will save it and listen to it later. Pretty funny. The tapers levels were off for the first 2 songs and
it's very distorted. After that things seem to get corrected and only get better as the show goes on. Seems
to me that this is a DAT sourced recording. If anyone has an upgrade please let me know. Thanks.
