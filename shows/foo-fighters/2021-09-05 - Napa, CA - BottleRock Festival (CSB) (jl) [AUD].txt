Foo Fighters - 2021 09 05 Jam Cellars Stage, Napa, CA

Show note:
BottleRock Festival. I decided to split the band intros.

Recording Note:
I decided to go stealthy as this was my better half's first concert since pre-COVID times, and we wanted to take it easy.  We were about 100+ meters back, on the left, agaisnt the VIP section.  I had the mics clipped on the VIP wall about 6 feet high, pointed up and to the stage. Unfortunately, the VIP-ers were more interested in drinking and talking than listening. Then at one point, a drunken couple pinned themselves right in front of us, and I expected that they would start going at it. Next time, I think I will smuggle in an actual recorder and battery box ....  iPhone video of Somebody to Love here: https://youtu.be/e-TGzNvFa1c
