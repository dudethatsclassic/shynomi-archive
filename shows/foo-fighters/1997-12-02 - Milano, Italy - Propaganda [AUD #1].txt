NOTES
---------------
Nice sounding DAT recording from the late 1997 tour in support of TCATS. Wattershed is present here which was a rarity to 
hear on the fall/winter '97 tour recordings. I think they only played it 4 or 5 times over those couple months. Most of the
shows they played "Walking After You" in that encore spot instead. Pretty much none of "I'll Stick Around" is present here 
but the master was cut so no copies out there have the complete song on them. Hopefully someday another source of this will
surface and we can get a full recording of this show. If anyone has any leads on a complete copy please let me know. Enjoy!!
