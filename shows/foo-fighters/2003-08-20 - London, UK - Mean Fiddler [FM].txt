This is a really good tight performance, with a few tracks that (at the time) hadn't been played in years - which in turn means a good lively

audience.  The show is mixed beautifully, with a sharp clear stereo separation so thanks to the BBC for that.  The brief radio intro & ident

have been left in, & unfortunately the BBC were only able to broadcast an hour or so of the show live before they had to cut the broadcast as 

required.  This means that there's only an intro really to 'Monkeywrench', & then an ident which I've faded using Audacity - no music removed.
