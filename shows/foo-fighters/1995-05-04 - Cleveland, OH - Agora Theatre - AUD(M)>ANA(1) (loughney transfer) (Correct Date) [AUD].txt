Source note:
Got this tape in 1995, but noticed there was another show listed as 4 May 1995 Cleveland, so I figured this was the wrong date. After a large search, across many continents, it has been determined that the existing 4 May 1995 Cleveland show (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bx81oL1Z9TE) is in fact 29 May 1995 Lupo's Heartbreak Hotel but just a differnt rip / transfer of the existing show. Working with the fine people at foofighterslive.com to get things updated.
