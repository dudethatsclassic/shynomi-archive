NOTES
---------------
This is the first show that was recorded from the tour supporting The Colour and the Shape. Before this the band had only done
a few promo things for TV shows and 2 secret shows in LA. The show took place in the parking lot of a Tower Records store in
Rockville, MD. The band seems to be excited to play new songs and this is the first full show recording with Taylor Hawkins
on the drums. There are 2 private shot videos of this show circulating but this DAT is new to most of the community.   

No thanks go to me on this one either. An old Foo Fighters DAT trader wanted this shared along with a bunch of DAT upgrades to
help out the Foo community. Thanks also need to go to Mike Zeigler for transferring this. All I did was upsample and track this 
so I could put it up for download. This is one of many new sources/upgrades that will be torrented in the next few weeks.
