Show Notes:
Rescheduled show from original September 4 date which had to be postponed due to illness. 

Show featured plenty of covers and teases through the night.  For "Breakdown" Brothers Osborne joined the band on stage.   
  
Recording / Editing Notes:
Some song transitions were a challenge to cut.  For "Run" and "The Sky is a Neighborhood" the backup singers did their false start, followed by 20 seconds of crowd applause and then Dave kicked the song off properly, which is where I started the track. 

During the Band Introductions/covers extravaganza I split this based on how they were listed as single tracks on setlist.fm for past shows and not as individual tracks.   

Settings on the CA 9200: Gain +10db, Volume 100% and Edirol R-09HR: Line Input: 38, Plug in Power: Off, Limited: Off, Low Cut: Off, Mic Gain: Low.

Adobe Audition channel adjustment to balance volume in channels, right had average RMS value that was 0.43 dB higher. My reference for calculating RMS in Adobe Audition (because I never remember) is here: http://bit.ly/2ooIg4e.

My final EQ adjustments were: -2dB Bass, +1dB Treble, 0dB Amplification
