NOTES
---------------
This is the second night from the two night run the Foos did at the Astoria in early 1997. They played exactly the same songs
as they did the night before, just in a different order. Dave does say that the crowd on the 2nd night was better than the 
crowd from the first night. Nice early performances of songs from The Colour and the Shape here and this recording does sound
better than the recording from the first night. There is a lot less crowd chatter heard on this one. The taper must have been
in a better location for night two.
