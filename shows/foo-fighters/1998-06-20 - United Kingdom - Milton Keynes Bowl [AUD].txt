Milton Keynes Bowl, UK 

Lineage
=======
Recording : CoreSound Stealth Cardioids -> Sony D100 DAT
Transfer : DAT Master -> Sony PCM-R300 -> Optical out -> PC (Creative X-Fi Optical In) -> Adobe Audition 2.0 MultiBand Compression & EQ -> flac conversion with dBpowerAMP (level 4) -> files verified and tested for SBE's with Trader's Little Helper

This one was taped by myself and has not been widely traded. Almost complete show, overall very good sound quality - some wind problems blowing the sound around and the mix wasn't great but decent enough.

Most of the opening song is missing. I did tape the whole set but on revisiting the tape to transfer the recording, I found the tape was badly damaged at the start of this set. Don't know how but it was completely unreadable. So I've faded in from the first clean part of the recording. Be assured, there are no problems with the rest of the recording. 

Transferred direct from the master DAT but had to do a lot of work to get it sounding as good as it does now. The original mix was appalling but I believe I've made a big improvement on it. Will post a sample.
