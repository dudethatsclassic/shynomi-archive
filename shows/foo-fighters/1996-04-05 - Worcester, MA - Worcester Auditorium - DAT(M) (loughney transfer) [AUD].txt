Show note:
Foo Fighters Live shows 2 analog recordings, not sure which one this is. 2nd live performance of My Poor Brain. Good Grief has the Pixies intro.

Transfer note: 
Tape flip after Podunk and cuts the intro of How I Miss You. There was bleed through on the leftchannel from something that had been previously recorded on the tape, so this is a dual mono show, just using the right channel.  That made the quality significantly better.
