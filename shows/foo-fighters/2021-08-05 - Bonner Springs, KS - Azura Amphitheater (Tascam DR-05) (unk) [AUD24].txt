This was something of a last minute decision to go (even if I wanted to), but went to help a buddy not get lost trying to get out (he drove from about an hour away).
I stopped the final note after about 90 seconds on my recorder and cut most of that off.
I did not split Walk on its own track because of the way it came after Medicine at Midnight.
Past this, I do not have too much else to say about this. I am glad I went and it was a good show, but I am unsure if I would go again.
I hope you enjoy this, as it seems there are not too many documents available (as of September 2021) from this tour. Perhaps other recordings will come out later.
