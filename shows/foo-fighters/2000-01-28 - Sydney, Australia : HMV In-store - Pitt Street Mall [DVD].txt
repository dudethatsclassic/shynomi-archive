REVIEW:
This amateur video is shot from towards the back of an HMV store. The person filming is clearly doing it stealthily as they hide the camera at points and nearly every song slightly cuts in, probably as they were a bit late pulling the camera back out as songs started. 'Everlong' was played after 'Aurora' and is missing entirely but most songs are missing just the first couple of seconds. You can see how much is lost as there is a clock on screen constantly. The video is unknown generation but thought to be no worse than a VHS(2) so the quality is generally quite good, with just a bit of fuzzyness to picture. The taper gets fairly good shots of the band through the crowd, mainly of Dave, but does focus on all band members when given the chance. There are a few obstructions to the view every now and again but for the most part shots are clear and steady with good levels of zoom. The audio quality is adequate, not distorted or thin and generally quite pleasent to listen to.
