NOTES
---------------
Pretty nice audience DAT from the May 98 shows. Don't know if this is the same recording the Foo Factory bootleg was made
from or not as I don't have it to compare. There is a little bit of diginoise in Monkey Wrench and Hey, Johnny Park! After 
that it seems to subside and you don't hear it anymore. If anyone has an upgrade to this please let me know as I would love
to obtain a copy without the diginoise if there is one out there. I have the soundcheck from this show that was taped by an
analog taper that I will put up when I find it. It was tacked onto another tape that I have misplaced at the moment. They
jam on Exhausted and some other random stuff during soundcheck if I remember correctly. During the show they jam on Baker Street
in the middle of Alone + Easy Target which I believe was pretty common for them to do around the time of this show. Enjoy!!
