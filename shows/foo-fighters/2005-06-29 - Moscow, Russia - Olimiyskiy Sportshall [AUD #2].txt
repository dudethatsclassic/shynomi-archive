Notes: 
The other bands playing at the festival were: Garbage and Queens of the Stone Age. This was a great festival - the first time 
we had 3 bands of such a caliber playing together. The sound at the fest was indeed very loud. The recording sounds a bit distant, 
I was unable to get the floor tickets, but anyways, the sound is clear and nice. The 20,000 capacity venue was half full, so it 
also marred the overall sound a bit. The FF had imho the best sound and delivered amazing setlist with some very rarely played tracks.
