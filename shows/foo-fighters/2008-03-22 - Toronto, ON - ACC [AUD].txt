Notes:
The Foo Fighters were joined by Against Me! and Hi-Fi Handgrenades for the Canadian leg of their Echoes, Silence, Patience, & Grace tour. The venue holds roughly 17,000 people and was sold out. Foo Fighters went on stage at 9:30pm. They were joined by their extended band for most of the set and Geddy Lee and Alex Lifeson for YYZ.


The Foo Fighters consist of:
Dave Grohl - Guitar/Vocals
Nate Mendel - Bass
Chris Shiflet - Guitar/Backing Vocals

The extended band includes:
Jesse Green - Strings
Drew Hester - Percussion
Rami Jaffe - Keyboard/Accordion
Pat Smear - Guitar
