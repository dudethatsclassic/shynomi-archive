NOTES
---------------
This is a combination of sources to hopefully create a final DVD source for this show. The video comes from my 
VHS(2) tape that I have had since 1997. I got a dub from someone who caught it off of tv when it was broadcast. The audio
comes from a DAT clone sourced from a DAT recorded during the original broadcast. The DAT was only recorded at 
32khz so I converted the sample rate to 48khz and synched it with the video. Hopefully this will be a good enough
source until someone with a VHS(1) copy can come through and get us one to transfer. The audio quality is upgraded
quite a bit on this one compared to what it was in the past. There is no cut in "I'll Stick Around" on this copy 
either. The video plays the whole broadcast through from start to finish. I think the results are pretty great. 
I hope you like it as well. Enjoy!
