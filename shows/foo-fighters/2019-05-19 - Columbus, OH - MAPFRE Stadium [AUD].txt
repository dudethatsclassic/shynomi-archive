Notes:

% = Originally Recorded By Queen,
    featuring Luke Spiller [The Struts] & Taylor Hawkins on lead vocals. 

This was the inaugural Sonic Temple Festival, formerly known as Rock On The Range. The
Foos closed things out on Sunday evening and due to gale force wind advisories and rain
all afternoon, most bands were cancelled from 4pm on. One of the side stages was 
completely shut down the entire day due to damage suffered from the wind. The Foos and 
Joan Jett (side stage) were the only ones left when we were let back in (after an 
evacuation) at 730. I almost decided not to record because the winds were still very 
strong at 9pm when the Foo Fighters took the stage. However, it wasnt as bad as it
was during the afternoon -- thats for sure. So, when listening to this, please keep
that in mind. You will hear wind muffle all throughout, but once you get used to
it and you get used to the stadium's PA, its a fun listen. No real surprises in the
set. They only got 2 hours (when they normally play 3). I was hoping for "La Dee Da", 
that song still escapes me on the C&G tour! Pauly Shore was brought on stage during
"My Hero", as he had just lost his father the previous night. Dave dedicated the song
in his memory. Pauly had introduced the band before they had taken the stage. Enjoy!

I did not record any other bands earlier in the day.
