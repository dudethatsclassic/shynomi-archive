Notes:
Livestream from the November 14th Foo Fighters show at The Roxy. Once the livestream ended the show was available to replay on Vimeo, and this file was taken directly from there.

Pretty fun show, you could tell the guys aren't used to playing shows at an empty venue (and let's be honest... Who is?).
There were a few teasers of songs from their upcoming album, Medicine at Midnight, mixed on some classic songs.
During "My Hero" Dave filled the gap left by the lack of audience by making the crew sing along, making for a nice and Foo-ish moment.

The video includes ~30 min of pre-show, which I decided to keep in order to have an untouched video file.

Enjoy!
