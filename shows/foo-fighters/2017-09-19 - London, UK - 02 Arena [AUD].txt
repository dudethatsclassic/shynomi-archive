NOTES

Not my best recorind but still listenable, one the day of the show all ticket holders recived
an email informing them of more stringent security checks, including walk through detectors and
all phones and cameras etc going through on trays etc.... I didnt want to have my mics, battery
box or extraleads flag so I went with just the Zoom. I recorded from the floor just to the left
of the mixing/sound desk.

ZOOMH2n>CD WAVE>TLH>DIME>YOU
 
A very long and enjoyable show, although if they had played for much longer I would have had to
have left to catch my last train home, Rick Astley came on for a very enjoyable rendition of
his big hit from way back when. I dont mind the odd "extended jam" but there seemed to be a lot
of those here, in my opinion a couple too many. dave mentions a couple of times about this show
being their London album launch party and it was basically a party atmosphere for the 2.5+
hours.

If someone is able to clean/tidy this show up through some software magic/TLC please feel free
If that person wants the original wav files more then happy to send them over.

Maybe a better capture will eventually surface, its still very listenable just not as good as 
it could have been if I had taken my full rig in.

Share freely and use wisely.
