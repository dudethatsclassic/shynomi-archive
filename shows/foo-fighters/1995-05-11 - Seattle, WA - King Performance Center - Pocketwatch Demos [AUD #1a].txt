Great early show here - could easily pass for a soundboard, but I think its an audience recording. Correct me if I'm wrong. Excellent setlist- Good energy overall.  Be aware that "Exhausted" is cut at the very end.  The final chord plays and rings for about 2 sec, then the recording cuts.  This is how it was on the CD. For those of you who own this boot, make sure you know that the song listed as "Floaty" is actually "Podunk". But I'm sure you already knew that. ;-)
