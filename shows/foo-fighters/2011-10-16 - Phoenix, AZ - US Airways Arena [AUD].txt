Comments: This was our first time seeing the Foo Fighters and they played quite loud. We
were in the front row section 219, aimed at the stage right stacks! The crowd was totally
behaved during the recording, which came well (IMHO). They played for over 2 1/2 hours 
and really appeared to be enjoying themselves this Sunday evening in downtown Phoenix.
Enjoy!
