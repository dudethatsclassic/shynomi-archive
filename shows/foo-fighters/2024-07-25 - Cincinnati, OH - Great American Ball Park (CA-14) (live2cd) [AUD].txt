Notes:

#  Dave on acoustic guitar, Rami on accordion.
^  Dave solo acoustic.

The Foo Fighters first trip back to Cincinnati in nearly 3 years to the date.
Their prior performance was on July 28th, 2021 when the The Andrew Brady Music
Center opened. Sadly, since that performance, we have lost Taylor Hawkins, so
this was the first time we were seeing Josh Freese behind the kit who joined
the band last year. I was very impressed, but then again - he's Josh Freese,
who wouldn't be? The setlist did drop in some rarities for the hardcores, which
I appreciated. "Generator" was really nice to see as was the following song,
"La Dee Da". They were doing "La Dee Da" for C&G back in 2017/18, but I had
missed it. The new tracks sounded great with "Under You" (acoustic) and "The
Teacher" (for Dave's mom) being highlights. "Aurora" is another I will never
get sick of and it meant even more as it was played for Taylor. The stadium
was sold out (40k?), but there was plenty of room in the pit, so I did have
some space around me. I still had to remove some screaming and some yelling
in RX7, so I did spend a fair amount of time making this sound nicer. Please
note that during a few songs when Josh would hit his kickdrum there was a 
loud crackle that came from the PA. This did not happen very often, but when
it did, I used the de-click feature in RX7 on those hits. It definitely helped
smooth them out. The show was pretty bass heavy, so I did EQ a tiny bit. I
left most of the bass in. Please adjust on your home stereos accordingly.

Mammoth WVH and The Pretenders opened. [both recorded].
