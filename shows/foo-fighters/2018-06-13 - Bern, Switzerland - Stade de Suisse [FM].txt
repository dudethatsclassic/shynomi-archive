Notes:
This show was recorded from a broadcast of Swiss radio station SRF3 in December of 2018.
I removed all DJ talk and station IDs. Because of that, a few songs have a fade-in (e.g. The Pretender or These Days)
or a fade out (e.g. Everlong). Because of a small technical problem I didn't catch the first couple of minutes of Monkey Wrench which started
the second hour of the broadcast. I'm really sorry about that and I hope it doesn't diminish anyone's appreciation of this recording.
The sound quality is excellent as usual.
