This show is NOT ACOUSTIC although the concert is called KROQ Acoustic X-mas.  I got this CD-R from my best friend that worked at KROQ (LA) at the time.  I asked him for a copy of the Foo Fighters set and the OASIS set.  I never got the OASIS one.  I am not too sure if this has been floating around but I have not seen it on any of these sites yet.  I was actually there for this one.  Tori Amos played Smells Like Teen Spirit right before the stage revolved and the Foo Fighters were doing their intro into Monkey Wrench.  Which was really cool to see.  Also, Pat Smear comes out and plays Stacked Actors with Dave's guitar.  You can tell he didn't really know the song by the way he played.  And since Dave was not playing he had his hands free to use a bullhorn towards the end of Stacked Actors.
