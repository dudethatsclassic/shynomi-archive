The Foo Fighters - 2023 08 10 @ Lake Tahoe Outdoor Arena, Stateline NV

Show note:
Really excited for this show. After Taylor's death, we wondered if the band would continue.  Seeing them in Fresno might have been the last show, or so we thought.  Arrived as the band was soundchecking "Aurora", "The Glass", "The Teacher" and "Still Haven't Met You Yet" and met up with TJ before the show. We were in line with the right (or stage left) tower, next to the soundboard.  Unfortunately, people did chatter a bit, and one lady kept throwing her headback and screaming into my face - which I had to tell her to stop.  Aurora was dedicated to Taylor & was really happy they did "The Teacher." Great lighting, see photos.
