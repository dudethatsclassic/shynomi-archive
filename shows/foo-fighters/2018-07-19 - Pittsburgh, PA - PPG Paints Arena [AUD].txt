Notes:

^ = Originally Recorded By John Lennon/Van Halen.

% = Originally Recorded By Queen,
    featuring Luke Spiller [The Struts] & Taylor Hawkins on lead vocals. 

Chris Chaney (Jane's Addiction, Alanis Morissette) filled in on bass for Nate Mendel,
who just had twins on the day of the show.

This was a fun show, but I left slightly disappointed. For some strange reason,
the Foos took the stage at 845 (when PPG Paints Arena uses an 11pm curfew). This
puzzled me a bit when I knew their show was 3 hours. Well, around 1045 word got
around to Dave to start finishing things up. He made mention of this on stage
and they cut the encore break (which is roughly 10 minutes) and a couple of songs
from the encore. One being "This Is A Call" from the debut and the 2nd being
the nightly audible. Lately those have been "La Dee Daa", "Generator" & "Big Me".
They also cut a few jams during the band intros. All in all, the show was roughly
35 minutes shorter than most on the tour with only 16 original Foo songs played.
They did end up playing past the 11pm curfew, but only by about 10minutes. No
reason they couldn't have gone on at 815 like every other city! OK, end rant.
The crowd was great around me all night, a few loud claps that I removed with
the clip restoration tool in Audition. Even tho this set was a bit abbreviated,
I hope you enjoy it!

The Struts opened. [recorded]
