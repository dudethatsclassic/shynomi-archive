REVIEW: 
 Taken from a Channel V promo DVDR this is a very good professional shot DVD. The video and audio are both of very good quality as expected for a pre-broadcast source although the video was only transferred via a standalone recorder with a lower setting as the video bitrate averages 4Mbps. This means at times, particularly later in the show when it gets dark there is quite a bit of macroblocking but it's not too bad. Picture clarity otherwise is very good. Audio is of excellent quality with good high end and a nice clear mix. The promo DVDR was of the second broadcast, not the original world premiere broadcast which means 'Skin & Bones' and 'Hey, Johnny Park!' are not featured but 'Young Man Blues' is.
