2024 Remaster Notes:
I got this from dime or the den back in 2008. I listened to it once then forgot all about it until a week ago.
While preparing it for upload I realized something could be done to improve it. I've never messed with
anyone elses tapes before but I figured something is better than nothing and somebody should do it.

So first I will list the diagnosis:
-There is some haunting print-thru of Joe Walsh and GnR in track 1.
-The left channel was almost 5db lower than the right.
-Most of the music was limited to the <8khz range.
-Excessive tape hiss was prominent.
-The overall sound was flat as a board and lacking character.

What I did in Audition:
-Increased the left channel by 1db.
-Decreased the right channel by 4db.
-Applied "High Hiss Reduction" to both channels.
-Used graphic equalizer to drop off all frequencies above 8khz and increase the lows and mids.
-Applied hard limiting to -.1 with a 3db input boost.

I was actually at this show when I was 13. I just remember it being loud. Bloody loud. I could feel the kickdrum
through the floor. It was exciting to see Pat & Dave from just 25' away. The Amps opened up for them and they
killed it as well. I looked for a ticketstub or flyer to include but nothing has turned up. If you find something of the
such please post in the comments. I want to thank the taper and everyone else involved with the sharing of this
special evening from my youth. It was awsome to find this after 12 years of assuming a tape did not exist.
