Notes
Here is another master upgrade from one of Master8s videos, this one is their
stand-in set at Ozzfest 1998. This is again from a digital MiniDV clone and
thankfully this one is pretty much error free, no major nasty digital glitches.

The raw video as transferred had two clear issues - firstly, owing to the fact
it was shot on a sunny British evening the sun was very bright, this led to
shots directly at the stage to look pretty overblow in brightness, some old VHS
copies looked almost fully white in some angles. With this new master transfer
I have toned that down the best I can, it's not perfect but far nicer to watch
now with more natural tones and colour.

The other issue was the sound syncronisation was off, due it being filmed a long
way from the stage. I have applied a delay to the audio to correct this, so it's now
correctly in sync.

The taper mostly shoots directly at the stage but does sometimes pivot to the big screen.
Those shots are also in sync thanks to the natural delay on those screens exactly for this reason.

There is a small cut during the encore break, no music is missing.
