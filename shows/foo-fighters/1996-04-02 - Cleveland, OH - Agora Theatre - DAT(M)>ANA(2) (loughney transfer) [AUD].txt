Show note:
Weenie Beenie has the Outshined intro. Good Grief has the Laverne and Shirley theme intro. Petra Hayden fromo That Dog sings Floaty.  The overall sound is a bit distant. Not sure about track 15, Dave quips that the audience is hearing a new song being created, but he might be goofing.

Transfer note;
Enough Space fades in, with really low volume to start. Right channel was quite low, so normalizing added some hiss.
