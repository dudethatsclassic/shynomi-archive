Comments:  Surprisingly good audience recording from the first of two nights at Chicago's Wrigley Field.  Dave Grohl always seems to be amped to play in Chicago, particularly when he's at Wrigley Field (across the street from the Cubby Bear, where he attended his first concert as a teenager).  He ran around like a madman when he was on vocals, and his between-song banter made him sound like he'd just downed a half-dozen Red Bulls.  I was standing next to the taper, and assumed the recording would be ruined because of the commotion around us in the audience -- however, this turned out to be one of those shows where we joke about the "crowd filter" being turned on, because the audience disruptions on the recording are minimal.  

Performance-wise, this is the only date I saw on the tour so I can't compare with others, but the band did seem "on."  The setlist is pretty similar to the rest of the tour (I think they may have changed one song the second night) with the exception of Rick Nielsen's guest appearance on "Ain't That a Shame" mid-set.
