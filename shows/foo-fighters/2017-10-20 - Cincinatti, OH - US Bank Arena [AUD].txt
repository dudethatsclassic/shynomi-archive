First time seeing The Foos since 2011 as they have not returned to Ohio for a proper
show since. Great, high energetic show. Crowd was loud - VERY LOUD. If you listen to
"My Hero" or "Best Of You", you'll get an idea. Dave even made sure to mention it.
The new material from "Concrete And Gold" sounded excellent live, especially 
"The Sky Is A Neighborhood" & "Dirty Water". 
2 strange notes on the setlist - their 2014 LP, "Sonic Highways" was completely 
ignored during this show even though 2 songs did appear on the typed setlist, 
"Something From Nothing" and "Congregation". They were passed over with the latter 
being swapped for the Petty cover "Breakdown" paying tribute on his birthday. Finally,
this setlist contains 18 repeats (of 24 songs) from the 2011 set I last saw in Columbus.
This band has such a large extensive catalog, there is no reason why they cant play
some album cuts from the first 3 records. Being a hardcore fan myself (moreso in my 
teens and twenties), I would very much welcome that. I have heard "Enough Space" has 
been getting some play lately. Thats a good start! Time to fire up "Alone + Easy Target",
"Exhausted", "New Way Home", "M.I.A.", "My Poor Brain", "Headwires", and so on...

The recording went nearly perfect with my neighbors. All were quiet and respectful,
no screamers, talkers or loud clappers. The sound in US Bank is what you would expect
from a 40+yr old hockey arena. I tried to find the best sound on the floor and stood
there for the entire set. IMO, it paid off! Crank this one! Long live the Foos!!!
