Foo Fighters - 2024 08 13 @ Toyota Pavillion, Concord CA 

Note:
Really good show from the Foos and the first time in Concord. Good selection of songs and they are mixing up the setlist from show to show. I'm happy that they have shortened some of they jammy parts in songs that they had been doing for the past few years. I'm in favor of more songs, less jams. Recording side - the battery on my battery box was a little marginal (it had been fully charged) meaning I needed to turn up the gain to get a decent level. On the quieter parts there is some air/hiss and the bass can be a little more dirty than normal. Probably not too noticeable, but just putting here for completeness sake.
