On this occasion rather than starting entirely from scratch
with new songs, Grohl instead primarily spent his time going
back over the instrumental tracks recorded earlier in the 
year as Allister Lob. Vocals were recorded for all 5 songs
recorded in April and with these, the tracks were given their 
final titles : ‘Podunk’, ‘Let’s Make A Bet’, ‘I Don’t Want Your’,
‘How I Miss You’ and ‘Watered It Down’.

The 5 tracks from April 1992 were book-ended on the November 
reel by 2 new songs, entitled ‘Pretty Girl’ and ‘Slacker’s Password’.
