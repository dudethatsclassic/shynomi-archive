Notes: I am relatively sure that this source has never been released but there is a chance I gave it to someone over a decade ago. As it is, a proper release should suffice instead. I sat at the very top of the Orbit Room as I went into the show with literally no expectations but left impressed enough that sometimes I will still slide on the MD Masters to have on in the background. Decent enough capture, definitely ambient and heavy on the midrange but the bass has just enough of a punch that it is appreciated. To me, this seemed like a good performance and the band, if I remember correctly, was engaged with the crowd. During the song 'Glycerine', the lead singer ran up to the top of the venue (into the seats) flanked by security guards and basically allowed the entire section to swarm him like locusts - I stayed put. During the last song, I moved position in order to get out of the venue as quickly as possible. After years of operation, The Orbit Room was permanently shut down by the city as it failed to meet minimal safety codes.
