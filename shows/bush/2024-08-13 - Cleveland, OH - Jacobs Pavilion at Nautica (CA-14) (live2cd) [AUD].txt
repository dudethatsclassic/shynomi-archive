Notes: 

*  Originally Recorded By The Beatles.

First time back at this venue in 5 years. I remember the sound being very
good back in 2019, but unfortunately that was not the case this night. The
sound was much lower than I felt it should have been for an outdoor pavilion
and the mix was very muddy and distant. Bush was the loudest of all the bands,
but still did not sound as great as I expected going in. This is still a very
listenable capture, no doubt, but I wish it was better. I'm not really sure
what I can say about Bush's live show that I havent said in the past - most
of the songs have a very loud backing track that Gavin sings to and when
you hear it - you can't UNHEAR it, if that makes sense. Take a listen to
"Bullet Holes", "Identity", "The Sound Of Winter", "Heavy Is The Ocean",
"Flowers On A Grave", "More Than Machines" and you'll hear what I mean. FWIW,
Gavin's actual live vocal is much louder on the older songs. However, there was
definitely a track running on "The Chemicals Between Us" and the rarely played
"Prizefighter". Bush has been one of my favorite bands for 30 years, so this is
a very difficult thing to witness. It has been getting progressively worse in
their live shows over the past 10 years. I still enjoy their albums, but its hard
for me to want to support something that is not authentic. This is why I have sat
on this recording for more than 4 months, I honestly was not looking forward to
going back and hearing it again. Having said that, the band was fantastic and
that is the positive note I will end on.

Tim Montana, Jerry Cantrell and Candlebox were all on the bill. [all recorded but Tim].
