NOTES:

I can't believe it took until May of 2026, in Napa, CA of all places, to finally see Bush live. I've been listening to them since Sixteen Stone first dropped back in 1994 when I was only 10 years old. Razorblade Suitcase was the first cassette tape I ever owned (both that album and the previously mentioned Sixteen Stone would soon follow in my CD collection). I wish I had gotten to see them back in the '90s when they had their original lineup (if I only had been born 10 years earlier), but eventually you reach the point of beggars can't be choosers, and I'm sorry it took so long to finally see them. They just never seem to be booked for festivals I would attend, or play any decent/close venues around my home.

Anyway, skip forward and I see they get announced for the Bottlerock 2026 lineup. I think, well, that's where I'll first see them finally, 30 years late. But then set times came out and I realized that seeing Bush at the festival meant I'd have to leave the main stage area (where all the other bands I wanted to see were playing) to go see them on the second stage and then head back to the main stage. This typically wouldn't be a big issue, but I ended up in the front row on the rail at the main stage and there was just no way I could give up that spot to go see Bush and then come back and expect to get back on the rail.

BUT, because Bush had been announced as one of the bands with a post-Bottlerock aftershow (along with LCD Soundsystem, Chevy Metal and Rilo Kiley), I prepared for this and purchased a ticket to the aftershow on Sunday night after the festival ended. So knowing I was seeing Bush post-festival, I decided NOT to give up my front row rail spot and did not catch them at the actual festival. In a perfect world, they would have just played the main stage, and all would have been right, but we don't always get what we want...

Anyway, this show was FAR better than any shortened festival set could be. It was my first time in the venue (I had always heard about it) and wow is it tiny! It probably holds 200 people max I'm guessing. The one issue is the show was listed as starting at 10pm. Who the fuck planned that? This is marketed as a post-Bottlerock "aftershow". The festival itself ends at 10pm, so why the hell would you schedule a show to then start at 10pm in downtown? How do you expect people coming from the festival to make it in time? Well you might be thinking, "It's doors at 10 pm and the band starts at 11 pm." Yeah that would make sense, but this is Napa we're talking about, so no, doors were not at 10pm...the fucking show started at 10pm. 

LUCKILY the last set/act I saw at Bottlerock 2026, Cut Copy, ended their set at 9:45pm, so I had 15 minutes to leave the festival site, cross over the third street bridge, redo my recording gear to sneak it in, and then head into the venue. I made it into the venue about 5 minutes to 10pm (the fastest 10 minutes of my life getting into a venue), and then the show probably started about 10 minutes later around 10:05 PM. I'm sure there were plenty of people coming from the festival who ended up missing the first 20 minutes or more of the show. What terrible timing/planning. Do better Bottlerock!

Anyway, the show itself was fucking awesome. Seeing Bush in a tiny 200 person venue? Yeah, that'll never happen again, so I'm glad I took advantage of it. I also thought the sound was pretty good in the venue. Loud and clear for the most part (some of the vocals sounded a little muffled), what else can you ask for? I also just have to say Gavin Rossdale looks fucking great for a 60 year old. He has found the fountain of youth, so good for him.

I was worried they weren't going to play some of the songs I wanted to hear, like "Little Things" as Gavin said the promoters asked them to play different songs than they had played at the festival (and according to setlist.fm, they played "Little Things" at the festival). I started freaking out and worried they wouldn't play "Little Things," which was the song I wanted to hear live the most. What the fuck promoters? Telling the band to play different songs!!?? THANKFULLY, Gavin gave some fan service and played all the big hits off both Sixteen Stone (including "Little Things") and Razorblade Suitcase. RELIEF fell over me. This truly was the perfect Bush show. 30+ years in the making, and worth the wait and then some. The only set I ever heard/listened to that was better than this was the Woodstock '99 show, so that's saying something.

Some dude did get tackled by security during "Little Things" (of course), near me...I think he was trying to start a pit and threw some elbows and someone pushed him down and he started rolling around and running into people. Security didn't like that and chased after him. They must have had a talk with him, as I saw him come back in the crowd and apologize to people. Never a dull moment, even in little tiny Napa.

Shoutout to John and his friend Frank for the ride home after the show (saving me some Uber money), it was very cool to hear your stories/adventures of seeing Nirvana back in the day. This recording is for you!

Peace <3
