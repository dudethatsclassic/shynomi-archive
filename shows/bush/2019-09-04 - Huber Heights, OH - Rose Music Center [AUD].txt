Notes: 

The ALTimate tour featuring co-headliners Bush & LIVE with special guests, 
Our Lady Peace [not recorded].

Had great luck with this one. The sound was good (not always the case w/ the
Rose) and quiet neighbors. Well, there was a guy down to the row to my left
who belted out stuff between songs every now and then, but it doesnt deter
from the show. Gavin was in great spirits and was very thankful to the crowd
for the support. My only gripe with seeing Bush these days are the backing 
tracks. I understand most bands use them, but there is no reason why they 
need to be higher than Gavin's live vocal. "Winter" and "Bullet Holes" sound
like a live band playing to Gavin's studio vocals. Annoying. Great songs not
being heard properly live. Having said that, the performance was top notch
and no, I did not turn around once to watch Gavin run around the amphitheater
during "Little Things". I stood my ground!

Bush were the first co-headliner on stage at 8pm sharp.
