Notes: 

The first Bush headlining tour since 2018. My first time seeing Bush headline
since 2017! Since then, they have released 2 records: "The Kingdom" (2020) &
"The Art Of Survival" (2022). Due to covid limiting their ability to play, this
setlist was stacked with material from both. 5 from "The Kingdom" & 4 from "TAOS".
The rest were from Sixteen Stone (as to be expected) and the lone song in between, 
"The Chemicals Between Us" from 1999's "The Science Of Things". I was very happy
with the setlist, the only songs I would have added would have been "Slow Me", 
"Send In The Clowns" or "Kiss Me I'm Dead". Maybe next time. The recording turned
out really nice. I always have good luck at this venue. It generally costs roughly
35% more than other venues (on the same tours) to see a band here - but when you
factor in the benefits (their sound is top notch), its worth it in the end. Plus,
shows in casinos are ALWAYS more expensive. MGM Northfield Park Center Stage holds 
1,820 seats and was completely sold out.
