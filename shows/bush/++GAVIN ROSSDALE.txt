Notes:

* = Originally Recorded By Fleetwood Mac.

So, a taper's worst possible nightmare scenario occurred right before Gavin 
was to walk out on stage @ The House Of Blues in Cleveland to me. For some reason 
my left channel was not receiving a signal from my DPA 4060 microphones. So, I 
had a dilemma on my hands. I only had a few minutes to decide what to do. After
messing with the input & the filter box, I decided to go handheld w/ the Edirol.
Thank god this unit offered this, or I would only have captured a monaural tape.

I never have attempted a tape with the Edirol's external mic (no need to, really)
so I did not know what to expect. I held the unit at chest level pointed towards 
the right stack away from any heads. I was quite surprised when I checked the 
recording out when I returned home. It actually sounds damn close to what the 
DPA's would have given me. There are noticeable differences, but its a really good
tape. Now, to figure out what went wrong my left channel so I will be ready to
tape future concerts. Its not the Edirol, thats for sure.

Enjoy everyone, Gavin was really firing on all cylinders in Cleveland.
