Notes:

Yes this is the real deal....newly discovered Stunning Master Stereo SBD of Bush from 1997...full performance, unreleased until now! 

Mercury Lounge was a small club (about 1500 ppl) inside the flashy Casino building in Melbourne Australia. The band was on a whirlwind promotional tour and only stayed in the country for a week. Gavin does not sound particularly impressed that they are playing the epitomy of "Corporate" gigs, he says "whatever" a few times nonchelantly in between songs, and "how many conversations can be going on at once" in a break suggesting he is non-plussed by the lack of attention. At the end he says "We've had a LAUGH...See YA!". Its a powerful performance from a pissed band, Gavins voice is young, strong and piercingly clear in 1997. Special stuff!

Note the introduction message from the band to Dave Grohl...interesting

Audio Note: It seems the tape was recorded a little hott for the first 5 or 6 songs...then things settle down. Thankfully its not overly distorted, its more of a tighter crunhy sound at the start that might be noticed.
