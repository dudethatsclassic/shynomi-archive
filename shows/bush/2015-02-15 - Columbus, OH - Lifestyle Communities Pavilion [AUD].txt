* = Originally Recorded By The Talking Heads.

Bush have decided to change up their setlists on this current run. Columbus got a 
decent setlist, but I really wanted to hear "Surrender", "Prizefighter", "Swim", 
"Insect Kin" & "Loneliness Is A Killer"! I should have went to the previous night 
in Indy!!! Mix was a bit bass heavy so I performed a slight EQ in Audition to correct.
It still has a PUNCH to it, so please adjust your stereo system accordingly. 

Gavin ran out on the floor during "Little Things", so the screaming you hear is 
coming from his wireless mic as he was going from one side of the floor to the other.
