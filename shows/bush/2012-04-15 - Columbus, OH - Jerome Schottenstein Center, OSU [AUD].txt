The "Here & Now" tour featuring Nickelback, Bush and Seether. 
Bush followed Seether, preceding Nickelback. 

Thanks to Gavin for getting me into this show! He ran a twitter contest
"60 DAYS | 60 PAIRS OF TICKETS" which I entered... and won! Crazy. I was able to 
attend & see one of my favorite bands without having to pay the asinine ticket 
price required to pull a decent recording. No ones taping from those $40 seats!

Notes on the show: Bush gave it their all and sounded terrific. I was reminded of
seeing them back in 1996 and 1997 watching Gavin command the arenas... 
it was something else. The highlight was during "Come Together" where he ran out
to the crowd and eventually ended up in the high 100's in the back of the arena
finishing the song. Insane -- but he's been doing things like that since the 
"Science" tour. Recording is decent for an arena, not many talkers, etc.
