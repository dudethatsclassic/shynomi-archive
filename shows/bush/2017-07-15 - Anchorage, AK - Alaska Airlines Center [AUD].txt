Show Notes: 
It'd be an outright lie by saying that Bush is even on the ol' radar; they're not (always lumped them in with Pearl Jam clones STP and Creed). But I had business to tend to in Anchorage, and being already there, why not throw a 700 mile road trip in the mix (down and back on 2 hours sleep)? Bush played a solid, hits-based set, and there was minimal crowd around me (the venue was maybe half-sold). Near the end of the main set, Gavin ran all around the arena.

lo-res video here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bRgINoQA_X0

He's no machine head, but he is an uploading machine. His name is Dennis, and if you like this recording, throw him a "thanks", as without him, you'd not hear this stuff.  Save the "fuck off's" for the likes of me ;)

More to come (Tim Easton (x2?), 36 Crazyfists and Ben Harper are next week, Jewel is in early August, and the Doobies are on tap for Sept., along with some The Magpie Salute shows.
