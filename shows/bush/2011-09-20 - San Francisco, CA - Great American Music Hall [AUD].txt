BUSH
September 20, 2011
San Francisco, CA, USA
Great American Music Hall


I was never a huge Bush fan, but they were very popular when I was in high school and I always liked what I heard. I'd never seen them, but I thought I had to when I heard they were playing a place that holds 600 people. It was a really good time.

Heart Of The Matter is played live for the first time. 

I was right up front on the rail, and during People That We Love, Gavin came over and hopped down into the crowd directly in front of me. He quickly put his arms around the girl next to me, his head on her shoulder, and sang part of the song in this position. This was inches from me, so you may hear his vocals direct for a bit in addition to what was coming out of the speakers. I wasn't sure if I could. This is also the rare recording where the taper (me) made physical contact with the lead singer while taping. Kind of uncomfortable. FYI - the man sweats like nothing I've ever seen. He was SOAKED. Jesus Christ. But anyway, the girls around me go a little crazy during this song. You'll hear it.

Finally, the right side of the recording was garbage, so I dumped it and doubled the left side. Oh, and I recorded this maybe two feet from the speakers. Samples below.
