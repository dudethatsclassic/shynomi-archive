Awesome night in a small venue .
the show also featured MENEW ,Thick as Thieves (local band) and Powdered God (local band).  
sadly it was a small turn out but a very energetic night .

MENEW played before FILTER took the stage and blew me away .
my camera only records 2 hrs so i usually only film the headliner .
they were so good i filmed 2 songs and bought their cd at the end of the night 
so check them out.

I had never seen FILTER before & only had their 1st two cd's from back in the day .
gotta say they still rock !!!
the singer jumped onto the barricade all night , a lot of times right on top of me .
so a bunch of footage that is dark especially when he was crowd surfing above me .
one cool thing was during "Trip Like I Do" singer Richard Patrick takes my camera and films the
song himself ! I got a big kick out of it to say the least :)
during Richard Patrick's political rant i wasn't exactly thrilled (being a conservative)
but I am also of the mind FREE-SPEECH isn't just for those you agree with .
over all a really cool show !
I hope you all enjoy :D

as always NEVER SELL !!!
GIVE IT AWAY !!!
