Notes:

My first Filter show in over 10 years. Ironically, the last time I saw them
was paired with Local H (who opened this show) and Helmet in 2014. Richard
whined and complained on stage that night when people started to leave (tons
of local support on a weeknight, they went on late, etc) so I got a pretty
bad taste in my mouth not wanting to see them again. Especially when they 
cut their set short by about 5 songs due to his bitch fit. Anyway, that is
now water under the bridge. This show was quite the opposite. This was not
only a rare headliner (on a tour where they are supporting Jerry Cantrell),
but a homecoming for Richard, as he is from Cleveland. His mom was actually
seated right above me in the balcony. It was packed, too. I couldn't move
much on the floor and there was crowd surfing!! It felt like 1996 again.
It was a tremendous show and I left pleasantly surprised. The new songs 
sounded great slotted in next to the classics. The bass was extremely heavy,
tho, so I did my best in post to EQ it down. The first third of the show
was the worst, so I spent considerable time on that section. Local H's
set sounded much better, but this is still quite good. 

This recording is dedicated to the memory of fellow Cleveland taper (and
Filter fan), Jason "nameloc01". He was gracious enough to send me CD-Rs in
the mail over the years including a show we were both at (Filter at the Grog
in 2010) when my rig failed! He was on my mind when I was taping this. RIP.

Local H opened. [recorded].
