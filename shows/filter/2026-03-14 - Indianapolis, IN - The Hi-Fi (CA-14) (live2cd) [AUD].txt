Notes:

I caught Filter in 2025 headlining in Cleveland, and they put on a tremendous
show. I was excited to see them a year later and see what changes or surprises
might be in the set. While they did add 2 different songs: "All The Good" &
"Be Careful What You Wish For", they omitted 7!!! Meaning, this show was 5
songs shorter. They removed some of my favorites too - "American Cliche",
"So I Quit", "The Take", "For The Beaten", etc. Also, the energy in The Hi-Fi
did not match the hometown crowd Filter had in Cleveland last year. Honestly,
people started leaving before and during their set in a room that only held
400. So, there was noticeable open space when they were on stage. This was
still a very enjoyable show and I felt quite lucky to had been there as all
3 of these bands could sell out The Hi-Fi on their own. Still can't believe
this tour got booked there! I caught another show on this same tour in
Columbus at The KING of CLUBS on March 29th but did not record Filter as the
sound in that place sucks and knew I would always come back to this recording,
anyway. Columbus was (to no surprise!) the same exact setlist and length.

Local H and Finger Eleven opened. [both recorded].
