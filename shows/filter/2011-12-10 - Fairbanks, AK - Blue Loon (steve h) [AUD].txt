show notes from Steve: this was a tricky endeavor, and a first for me...cutting it close. I actually attended 2 shows this night, there was a Big Bad Voodoo Daddy show at the Hering Auditorium from 8:00-9:30, then I broke from tradition of heading to the Loon early to set up and get a prime taping spot, and instead rushed out there to catch Filter. this caused the filming to suffer somewhat (further vantage point), but the audio came out crystal clear (imo). Richard was funny. the songs pretty much rocked. it was a good time, though the promoter was bummed as just over 200 people showed up (capacity 450)...sometimes shows in the middle of winter do that to ya! anyhow, big thanks to smores for the upload, and you never know what may come next!
