Notes:
First time I've seen GBV in about 20 years. Short (for them) festival set that included a great selection of older stuff as well as some excellent newer material. Pollard's voice was a little rough but he was entertaining as always. He drank two Miller Lites and a couple of shots of tequila - an order of magnitude less than when I saw them in the '90's. His liver could teach us a few things.
Recording is okay. The first song has some distortion. Outdoor gig so there are some talkers and wind. Sample of one of the better songs in comments.
