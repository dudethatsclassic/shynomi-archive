Notes:
The short-lived Guided by Verde lineup had been touring for a couple of months at this point. I'd seen one of the lineup's first appearances in Chicago in May and was pretty disappointed, but by this point they sounded better. I had tickets to see them again in November, but that show wound up being cancelled after Pollard let it slip that he was going to ax the other members.
I don't think this show has circulated much, if at all. GBVDB.com doesn't have a listing for a recording. I got the tape as a trade in 1997 and Dime member clownmaster transferred it for me a few years ago. 
The recording is off the soundboard, but the sound is weird - very trebly, somewhat distant, and with some hiss despite being a low gen tape. There are also a number of skips in the encore. I gave a try at editing it in Audacity but in the end just decided to put it up as is. mp3 sample in comments.
