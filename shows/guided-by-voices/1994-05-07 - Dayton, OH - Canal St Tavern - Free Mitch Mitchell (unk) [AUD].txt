Canal Street Tavern was a popular place downtown - bands, beers, and food pretty good for such a setting. Was open 1981-12-07 to 2013-11-30.  Pretty good list of shows played there:
https://www.setlist.fm/venue/canal-street-tavern-dayton-oh-usa-73d6ca31.html

This show is of early gbv in a home town setting.  No historicism here, blah blah. Shorter show than some later on, or could be incomplete.  I guess the hit here has to be "I Am A Scientist", but "Weed King" deserves mention clocking in at 3:09 min. So much for the cruel criticism that all gbv songs are less than 2:30. As with some other things these guys are reported to get better with increased alcohol consumption, both by the audience and the band. Don't know about that but they are real good live.
