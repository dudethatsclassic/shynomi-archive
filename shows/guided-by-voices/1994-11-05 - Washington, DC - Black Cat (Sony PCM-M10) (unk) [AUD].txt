Notes:
Per gbvdb.com, "Gig listed in Guided By Voices Fall Tour 1994 itinerary. 
GBV were to play at 12:15am for 60 minutes. Had a note about Michael Azzerad + 1, 
Aaron Blitzstein + 1, and Jim Romeo + 1 being on the guestlist." (Aaron Blitzstein
is a TV writer, but I have no idea who Jim Romeo is or why his presence was notable.)
Nice long set list with some rarely performed songs; the band invited a fan onstage
to sing "Big School". The tape was hissy and ran a little slow, so I tried to clean
 it up in Audacity. mp3 sample is in comments.
