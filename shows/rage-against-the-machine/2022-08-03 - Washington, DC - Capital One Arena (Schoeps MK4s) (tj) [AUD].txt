Night 2 of DC. I was on the floor 40-50 meters back in front of the soundboard left side. Prior to Rage Anthony and I were talking to a guy in a full suit who had scalped a ticket and somehow made it onto the floor. When Rage came on the floor exploded. I had a full pit form around me during Bombtrack and I had to run out of it to avoid getting crushed. However, there were multiple pits that were around me so I got ping-ponged around the floor quite a bit. I saw the guy in the suit jumping and running around like a maniac which made my night. Great setlist although no FTP which we were hoping to get. Interestingly, the printed setlist said "V2" and included Vietnow so I'm curious to know what was on the V1 setlist. I filmed as well but since it was so chaotic the footage is chaotic to say the least. DC had the roughest crowds both nights. Next up, MSG!
