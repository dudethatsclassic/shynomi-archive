Rage Against the Machine performing in Washington DC as part of a Mumia Benefit Concert.
Rage would debut their live cover of Fuck the Police, which is unfortunately not included due to official release on the Live and Rare album.
They also debuted Wind Below at this show, which makes it one of the few times they performed it live.
I’m not sure if this is a soundboard or a glorified audience recording: I’ve seen sources list a taping rig so its possible there’s two sources in circulation.
Either way, Rage only played 5 shows in 1995, so enjoy!
