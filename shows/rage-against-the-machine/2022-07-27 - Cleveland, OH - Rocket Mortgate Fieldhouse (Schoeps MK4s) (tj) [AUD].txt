BORN OF A BROKEN MAN!! Another amazing set by Rage Against the Machine, this time featuring the tour debut of Born of a Broken Man. Rage moved a few songs around which was a nice surprise tonight. Close Your Eyes was done with Run the Jewels and sounded incredible. During Killing in the Name the band displayed images of Tamir Rice, a young child shot to death by a Cleveland Police Officer, on the scren.

I was mostly dead center 30 meters back or so and filmed as well. I did get knocked around a bit but hey, it's a Rage show and I was near the pit. I didn't monitor the recorder since I was trying to film and not get crushed so it looks like the levels might've been a bit hot. Good times!
