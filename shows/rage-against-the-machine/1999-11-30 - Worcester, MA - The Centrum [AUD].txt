Rage Against the Machine performing in Worcester, Massachusetts on 1999-11-30 at the Centrum exactly 21 years ago.
This was the Boston stop of the Battle of Los Angeles tour, and from a review it sounds like the backdrop said "The Battle of Boston" but I was unable to find a photo.
According to the Associated Press on-duty police officers had to be present for the event since off-duty cops boycotted the show, who normally provide security for concerts at that venue.
Sadly, no video has ever surfaced from this show - if you have a video please get in touch!
