Rage performing in Philadelphia as part of the Battle of Los Angeles tour.
Pam Africa of International Concerned Friends and Family of Mumia Abu-Jamal gave a speech about Mumia before the concert began and is included on the recording.
This is an incomplete, but good sounding, recording of them playing that night.
Bullet in the Head, the Ghost of Tom Joad, Vietnow, and Sleep Now in the Fire were played after Calm Like a Bomb.
I do not think a complete source exists, I've only ever seen this 58 minute version listed on various trading sites.
I've also always seen the source listed as "Machine Guns in Philly", so it's possible a bootleg CDR was sold afterward.
