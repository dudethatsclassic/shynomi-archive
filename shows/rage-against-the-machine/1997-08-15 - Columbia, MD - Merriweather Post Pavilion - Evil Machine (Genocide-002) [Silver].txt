Notes: Ripped straight from the "Evil Machine" Silver CD, which historically did not circulate much.
The tracks are 1-1 with how they're pressed on the CD: Darkness and Without a Face are the same track on the CD.
I have heard that there is a complete audience recording of this show that includes Freedom and part of Township Rebellion after Killing in the Name, but I've never been able to find a copy.
This is the only known recording of Wake Up in 1997, and the last known recording prior to that was in late 1993, so this is a very special treat!
