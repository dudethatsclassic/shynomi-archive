**remastered by exene**

Contrast clause:
This is not  from a DVD rip, or from the silver CD, which harrymerkin shared here:
http://zombtracker.the-zomb.com/details.php?id=25189

Its an audio transfer of my low gen VHS copy.  
Of all my old VHS tapes, this one actually has decent sound quality, so I cleaned it up to share.
The sound is also excellent on the silver CD, so I would not call this an upgrade, just different.

All tracking and remastering done in Audacity.
A few seconds of static are audible, mostly in track 6.  This is present on the silver CD as well.
I smoothed those out best I could but its still noticeable. 

From what I can tell, it appears to be the full pro-shot show with 
all between-song noises and guitar tuning intact, no profanity edits, etc.
The total running time is 3 minutes longer than the show on the silver CD,
but I think its just missing crowd noise.

Amazing show.  Rage plays the very first Coachella.  
Zach mentions he is recovering from laryngitis, but he sure doesn't hold back.

Source: Soundboard > VHS(x) > Sony SLV-D360P > Audacity > wav > flac
