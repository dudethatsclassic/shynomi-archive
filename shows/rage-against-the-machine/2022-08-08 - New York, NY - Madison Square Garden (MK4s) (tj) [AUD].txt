RAGE AT THE GARDEN! The first of five nights at the Garden over the course of a week. First time Rage has done a residency like this anywhere since their five night stint at the Roseland in NY in 1996. All of the shows are sold out which is wild. I got off the subway and bumped into Trackstar the DJ before the show - what an incredibly nice guy! Definitely very star struck so if you're reading this sorry for being awkward lol. 

I was situated higher up towards the back of the floor but the recording still came out well. The band wasn't entirely on it tonight and the sound could've been improved I think but hey, they have four more nights to figure it out. The band was definitely amped up to be performing and the crowd was into it. Fun show overall. Can't wait for the other four!

Also filmed as well and came out pretty decent. Should be sharing that as well at some point
