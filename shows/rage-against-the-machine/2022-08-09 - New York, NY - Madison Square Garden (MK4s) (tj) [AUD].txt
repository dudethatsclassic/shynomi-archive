First of all, a fantastic time meeting so many great Full Senders ahead of the show. Was great to finally meet the king of Full Sends in person and shoot the shit ahead of time. Let's do it again soon! Rage played another fantastic MSG set and sounded much more dialed in than they did Monday night. The sound was more clear as well; couldn't believe the difference. Solid setlist with Tire Me and Take the Power Back rounding out the first rotation slots and probably the best performance I've seen of Vietnow for the second.

Recording came out solid for my position, no complaints. No talkers or anything around me either. Also filmed in 4K as well and was very happy with how that turned out.

This was my brother and I's first Rage show together in over 15 years, having last seen Rage together at Rock the Bells on Randall's Island. We'll be back at it Thursday!
