An early recording of Rage Against the Machine performing at the Tivoli along with Tool.
This historically has not heavily circulated.
It is somewhat distorted so its a bit of a collector’s item, but it is still a great sounding performance by Rage!
Someone has shared this on Youtube as well and notes that they bought it from a dealer on the “Dublins O’Connell Bridge”, so its possible this is from the same (shady) source!
