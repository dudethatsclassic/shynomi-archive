Notes:

I really never planned on releasing this show as I wasn't happy with the end result. But there has been 1 person out there that has been persistently requesting me to release this for the past 10 years. I'm going to apologize for being really lazy. I never had any intention of hoarding this show. This is for you, finally! 

For the first 6 songs, we had to avoid the crush, hop over a barrier, and deal with something that disconnected my cables. I got it sorted as Bullet in the Head cuts in and the sound is significantly better from there.

I’ve been looking for decent video of this gig for years. If you have any, please post or PM me.

Lastly, this show was recorded for my enjoyment purposes. You may hear some yelling, screaming and amp adjustments during the set. I always try to keep them at a minimum.
