This was easily the roughest and most intense crowd of the tour. Mega pits the entire time, crowd surfers, jumping around, this show had it all. The band seemed especially fired up, with Zack giving a speech during Bullet in the Head. Was great to have so many friends at the show - can't wait to do it again!
