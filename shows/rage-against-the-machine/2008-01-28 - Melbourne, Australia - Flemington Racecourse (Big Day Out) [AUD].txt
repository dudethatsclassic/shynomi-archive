Recording Notes:
Show was recorded in the D-Barrier on the elft side of stage. The volume may change due to not having a clear sight of the 
speaker stacks: and from my insane dancing around. It wasn't my best attempt at recording but to be honest, I was more concerned
about having a good time at possibly the only time in my life I'll ever get to see Rage.
Microphones were clipped to the brim of a hat about 6ft from the ground and spaced 6 inches apart.
