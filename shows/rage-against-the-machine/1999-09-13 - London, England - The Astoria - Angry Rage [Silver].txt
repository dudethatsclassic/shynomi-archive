Rage Against the Machine
1999-09-13
The Astoria
London, England

Lineage: CSB -> MD -> "Angry Rage" silver CD -> FLAC

Setlist:
1. Testify
2. Guerrilla Radio
3. Bullet In The Head
4. Sleep Now In The Fire
5. Calm Like A Bomb
6. Ashes In The Fall
7. People Of The Sun
8. Born Of A Broken Man
9. Know Your Enemy
10. No Shelter
11. Bulls On Parade
12. War Within A Breath
13. Killing In The Name
14. Freedom

A smaller club show to promote the Battle of Los Angeles.
This is the first recorded instance of several of these songs, including Guerrilla Radio.
Ashes in the Fall was only played a handful of times before the 2008 reunion, so it's nice that we have a recording of this performance.
They did play at an even smaller venue the night before - would kill to get a recording from that show!
