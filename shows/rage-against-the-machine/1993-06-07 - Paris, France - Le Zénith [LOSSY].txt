Rage Against the Machine
Le Zenith, Paris France
1993-06-07

Setlist:

1. Take the Power Back
2. Bombtrack
3. People of the Sun
4. Know Your Enemy
5. Darkness
6. Killing in the Name
7. Bullet in the Head
8. Freedom

Full audio (lossy) can be previewed here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nPEGI5BbVKo

This is an infamous show.
I'm not sure what happened before the show, but for whatever reason Zack De La Rocha was upset with MTV and yells at them a couple of times throughout the night (notably during Know Your Enemy), culminating in the entire band performing Freedom naked for the closer.
Aside from the PMRC show (which they didn't actually play anything), this is the only time they did that.
The energy in this show is pretty intense, definitely a great recording.
