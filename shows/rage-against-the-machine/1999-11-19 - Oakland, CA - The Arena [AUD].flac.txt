I got this CD-R back in the CD-R trading days (early 2000s). Unfortunately, I don't remember the source or who I got it from (sorry!). I can speculate that it was recorded with high end mics because it sounds good. I was hoping that a direct-from-master transfer would show up, but I haven't seen anybody else share this show. I had to share this since it has been 20 years. Maybe sharing it will bring out more information about it.
