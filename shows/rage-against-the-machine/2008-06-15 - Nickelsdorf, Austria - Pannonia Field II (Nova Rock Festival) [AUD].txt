Show Notes:
From the muscician point of view a nice show, from the audience point of view it was 
disappointing. While it was almost impossible for RATM to meet the high expectations that 
most of the present audience had it still was disappointing to see such a bored and tired 
crowd stand around yawning most of the time. The band itself played an excellent set I 
found, the spark just didnt hit the audience. 

I stood far away and to the right of the stage due to the masses of people wanting to see 
RATM. The sound is surprisingly good even though some phasing occured reason being that I 
was far away from the stacks. Still, think it is well worth it if you want RATM 08 shows. 
Enjoy.
