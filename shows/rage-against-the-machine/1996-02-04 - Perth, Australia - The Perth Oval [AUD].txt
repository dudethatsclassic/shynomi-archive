Rage Against the Machine performing at the Big Day Out festival in Perth, Australia.
This was their final show in Australia until 2008, where they headlined the same festival.
At these concerts Rage would play Evil Empire songs in their finished forms for the first time.
Snakecharmer was debuted at this show and features slightly different lyrics.
A jam which has been titled "Machine Gun" by fans was also performed here as well.
This is probably the last concert that they performed that Bulls on Parade was NOT performed at.
Only two full recordings exist from their 1996 set of shows, so enjoy!
