Rage Against the Machine's full performance at the Whisky a Go Go in Los Angeles, California from 1992/12/14.
This is the first time they performed People of the Sun - it's a slightly different version lyrically.
This is a fairly rare show, please enjoy!
I previously uploaded a mp3 of People of the Sun on the old Downrodeo site; I believe that has been circulated a bit.
The tape has noticeable hiss and some clicks - to the best of my knowledge, all circulated tapes of this show are like that.
