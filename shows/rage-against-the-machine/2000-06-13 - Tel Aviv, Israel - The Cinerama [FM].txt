A rare recording of Rage performing in Israel.
Rage would only play in Israel three times, and this is the only recording from any of those dates.
I have no clue what's up with this recording: it sounds like it might've been broadcast on a local radio station because there's something talking between songs.
I'm guessing someone did a really poor radio capture because the sound is pretty bad - lots of hiss.
Still, this is the only source to surface, so you take what you can get!
During the encore, which was unfortunately not recorded, Brad Wilk sang a prayer while Zack de la Rocha played the drums.
