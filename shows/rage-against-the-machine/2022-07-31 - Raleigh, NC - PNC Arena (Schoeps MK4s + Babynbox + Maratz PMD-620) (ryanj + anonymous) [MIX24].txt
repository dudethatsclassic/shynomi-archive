Notes:
A really good day all around meeting up with some people before the show. Was nice to see everyone. Had a few people taping this one, so here is the second source to be released. Thank you to TJ of the Rage Archive for hanging out during the day and being able to meet some new friends along the way! Show was fantastic. Even with Zack sitting for the show, the energy was there. Floor looked absolutely insane from where I was sitting. I can't wait for El Paso and the Detroit shows.

Recording sounds good, I am happy with the results and I also added some low end/vocals with the feed I was given. This was my first time running the Marantz modded by Doug Oade. I am looking forward to using this unit more.
