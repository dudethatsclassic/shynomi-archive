Ahh, yes.  Kind of an odd one here.  Summer of 1996, and this is one of the first shows I would ever tape with a half-decent rig, cobbled together from 
fund working part time at a record store.  Sadly, my old used WMD6C crapped out for "People of the Sun" and "Know Your Enemy", the first two songs. 
In order to make the show complete, those two songs are spliced in from a 1st generation video recording of the show.  I don't 
know anything more about it than that.  In any event, mine is the only audio I have ever been aware of for this show, 
and IMHO, the rest of the audio is pretty darn good for an arena show (and recorded by a relative newbie, no less). The show started late 
because the crowd was so out of control the barricade couldn't hold them.  The band had to play the whole show with the lights 
on in order to placate the venue.  Needless to say, these guys were relentless as always.  Enjoy!
