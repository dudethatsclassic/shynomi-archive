
Rage Against the Machine
1992-10-10
Elysee Montmartre
Paris, France

Opening for Suicidal Tendencies

Taper: RM
Lineage: Sony WMD3 + micRO
Transfer: Gen 1 DAT / ANA  > CDR > WAV
Length: 37:10

Setlist:

1. Bombtrack 
2. Bullet in the Head
3. Killing in the Name
4. Know Your Enemy
5. Settle for Nothing
6. Wake Up
7. Freedom

A huge, huge, HUGE thanks to TM for sending this over my way. This is a real treat - I had no idea this existed till very recently! This is one of the earliest audio recordings we have of Rage Against the Machine and it is the only known full recording of them on their 1992 tour where they opened for Suicidal Tendencies. I have no idea if the taper knew who they were prior to taping them or if they were there to only record ST but holy shit I'm so glad they got there early to record Rage. The recording sounds great, sounds like there might have been a minor level adjustment during Bombtrack but otherwise things are very consistent. Huge thanks again!
