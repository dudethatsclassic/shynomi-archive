This is a previously uncirculated recording of Rage playing at the legendary Fillmore in San Francisco.
I believe that this was announced super shortly before that night and might've been a semi-secret show.
This is the last known performance of Wind Below, which is the least performed song out of all the Evil Empire shows.
We are super lucky that this show surfaced.
If you ever visit the Fillmore there is an upstairs room with photos from performances over the years and there's a super intense one of Zack on stage.
I always wondered if we'd ever seen something surface, and now we have!
