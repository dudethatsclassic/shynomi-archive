What a great day with so many great people: Ryan of the Nine Inch Nails live archive, Meghan of War Within a Breath, Anthony, GW, Shane, and of course Sunny. It was so awesome to spend the day with fellow Rage and music fans. This was hands down one of the best sets of the tour and I think this might be their longest set of original songs to date. No Shelter was performed on a black Gibson guitar which might be a first. Wake Up was performed on Meghan's request which was wild as well. A truly unforgettable day - let's do it again in DC soon!

In terms of the recording it sounds great. Small channel dropout in Bombtrack that I left, that was from me adjusting the recorder. Nbox Platinum was used tonight and it sounds killer. Also filmed (and monitored!) in 4K as well. There were at least two other tapers tonight so this show should have some killer tapes emerge from it soon :)
