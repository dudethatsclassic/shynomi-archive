Rage Against the Machine performing at the “Medicine Show” in LA in 1992.
This is not a great sounding recording, but it does include their only performance
of “End of the Tunnel”, which sounds like it features lyrics from Wake Up at parts.
This is one of the few recordings we have of them in 1992, so enjoy!
