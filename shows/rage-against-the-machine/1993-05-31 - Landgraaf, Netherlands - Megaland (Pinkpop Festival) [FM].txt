Disclaimer.
As noted this tape was originally dubbed from another tape (this was done two months after the original recording had been made). I taped various parts of the (live) broadcast and made a compilation of the aired music for my own use. After transferring the tape to WAV, all tracks have been cross-faded to get one contineous live mix, except for tracks 8 and 9, as track 8 was aired incomplete and fades out on this compilation. So, if you want to burn this on CDr that makes a 'natural' point to switch discs.
Also note that this is a real FM capture, no cable, but straight from the airwaves. And this comes from a tape that was almost 16 years old at the time of transferring. This means that some FM and/or tape anomalies or degeneration may be present. Most has been fixed to the best of my abilities and most of this stuff is of very good quality.
