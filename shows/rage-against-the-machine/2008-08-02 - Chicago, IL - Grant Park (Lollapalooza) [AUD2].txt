This show was one of the main reasons I attended this years Lollapalooza.  This show got pretty crazy though, with a lot of people getting injured up front.  Zach stops the show on three different occasions to ask everyone to calm down a bit and take a few steps back.  I was toward the middle of the crowd and everyone, including me, were moving back each time.  But it didn't look like any of those up front were moving back at all...  So the setlist is probably a little shorter than other shows due to the breaks they had to take, but all in all, Rage put on another great show.  Hopefully we'll see a new album soon...

I hope everyone enjoys!
