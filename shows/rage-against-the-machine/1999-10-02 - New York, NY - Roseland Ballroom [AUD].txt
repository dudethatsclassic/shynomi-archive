Rage Against the Machine performing at the Roseland in 1999 to promote their upcoming album “The Battle of Los Angeles”.
This was a secret show announced very shortly before they played, and they would do the same in a few other cities as part of this promo tour, including Washington DC, London, Los Angeles, and Toronto.
Zack doesn’t talk much between songs - he was struggling with Laryngitis for these shows.
This show hasn’t circulated much historically, so enjoy!
