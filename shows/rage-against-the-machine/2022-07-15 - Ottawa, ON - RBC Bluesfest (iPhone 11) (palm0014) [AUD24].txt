A huge thanks to palm0014 for sending me over the raw WAV to track! I reached out asking if he had taped Rage since he had gotten some other bands from this festival and indeed he had! This recording sounds excellent. I'm shocked by how good the iPhone mics sound; would definitely use them in a pinch knowing what kind of recording you can make with them. Zack sat this show dead center like he did in Chicago and he gave a speech during Township Rebellion which he hasn't done probably since before the reunion. During Freedom the band displayed images on the screen specific to Canada which read:

"An indigenous person in Canada is over 10 times more likely to be shot and killed by a police officer than a white person is."

"In Canada, Indigenous women and girls are 16 times more likely to be murdered or to disappear than white women are."

"SETTER-COLONIALISM IS MURDER."

"LAND BACK"
