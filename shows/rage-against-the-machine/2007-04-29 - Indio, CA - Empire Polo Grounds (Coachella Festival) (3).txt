.I was in the pit for this bad boy with 60,000 pissed off Rage fans behind me trying to smash closer.  The entire section directly out from stage left 
	was so packed around me that when Rage began playing I couldn't help but jump because the crowd was lifting me of my feet...everyone was jumping.
	The entire section almost immediately became one huge pit that didn't dissipate until after the show.  I have a few slight issues with clipping
	because I started recording about 40 feet out from the stacks but with the mosh pit, I was being pushed almost up to the stage and back repeatedly.  
	The pit was so rough that it really started clearing people out because they were too scared, or too hurt to last the entire show up front.
	I was worried that I was going run out of batteries for the end of the show because somewhere along the line, my battery stick got smashed and 
	Rage was the last show of the day...Yeah, the pit was rough.

.My left mic shorted for Guerrilla Radio, Renegades Of Funk & Calm Like A Bomb after being blasted by a water bottle grenade, thus drenching my left mic.
	Therefore I copied the right channel over to the left channel for Guerrilla Radio, Renegades Of Funk & Calm Like A Bomb to make up for the short.
