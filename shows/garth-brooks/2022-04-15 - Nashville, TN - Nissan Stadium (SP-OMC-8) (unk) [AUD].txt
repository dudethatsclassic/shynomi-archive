Nashville, TN
Nissan Stadium
April 15, 2022



Recorded from field, row 12


SP - CMC-8 microphones > Sony PCM-M10 > SoundSaver for INport >
Trader's Little Helper > FLAC

The initial show was scheduled for July 31, 2021 but rained out due to a severe storm.  The show was canceled and rescheduled for April 16, 2022.  The second show (this one) was added just two weeks before the show took place with tickets going on sale April 1.  There were only about 10,000 people in the stadium but Garth worked hard to make the night happen and it had a certain kind of magic to it. The crowd was small, but it was mighty.
For a stadium show recording I think it came out excellent.
