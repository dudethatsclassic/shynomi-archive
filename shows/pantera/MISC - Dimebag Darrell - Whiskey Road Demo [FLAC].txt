01 Whiskey Road (Demo)

This demo was given to Guitar World by Rita Haney for the February 2015 Dimebag Darrell "10 Years Gone" Tribute edition. In addition to the internet stream from Guitar World site a 7" Flexi-Disc is included in the collectors edition of the magazine which is avaiable for purchase from the website.

This demo features Dimbag Darrell on all instruments and vocals. It was tracked in 2001 during the Reinventing The Steel tour.

Enjoy!
