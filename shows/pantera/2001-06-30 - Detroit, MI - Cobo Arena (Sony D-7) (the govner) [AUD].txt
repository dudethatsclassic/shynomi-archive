Yeah....I know I'm a day late posting this.....it just means I will post 2 Pantera shows today! 

This would be the last time I would see Pantera as a band. At the time I had relocated to Tennessee after the factory I worked in closed it's doors and gave me a hefty "severance" check for the 10 years I had put into the place. When I found out about this gig I had to make the trip up to Detroit....I mean it was Slayer, Pantera & Static-X on the same stage! It was also the same day that a close friend of mine, Paul Manzo had some problems with his teeth hurting which his dentist realized it was something else...the early signs of Lukemia which he has managed to keep in check. I wished that there wasn't an echo to the recording but all tapes from Cobo can't sound as good as "Live Bullet" or "KISS Alive" but it is still a great performance that also happens to be Phil Anselmo's birthday! I may post the Slayer & Static-X at a later time but for now here is some more Dimebag for you musicial pleasure! 

Enjoy!
