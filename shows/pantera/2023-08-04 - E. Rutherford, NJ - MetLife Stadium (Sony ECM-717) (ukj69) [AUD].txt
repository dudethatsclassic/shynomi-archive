Our 25th release is an excellent quality capture of Pantera's opening set (following Mammoth WVH) for the first night of Metallica's "No Repeat" weekend shows at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, NJ.

Sound of course can be horrible at this size of show and this particular stage set up, based on a number of reviews from the tour, but ukj69 had very good seats and pulled it off nicely.

Ukj69 was on hand for both nights and we'll be sharing more of the shows from this weekend here and in the case of one particular band...elsewhere.

Thanks to ukj69 for the continuous flow of show, I've been enjoying stepping out of my usual genre of releases. 

Tapers/collectors don't let those archives fade away, if you need assistance releasing Masters or known generation tapes there are plenty of us here that can assist! 

Samples and artwork are included.
