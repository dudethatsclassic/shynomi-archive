Video: PAL; 4:3; 720x576; 8264kbps CBR; YUV4:2:0; Top Field First Interlaced

This is the second transfer of this particular tape, using superior equipment.

This transfer is from a studio VHS.

This tape has a 30 second gap between tracks 3 and 4, I have left this on the
DVD. The drum solo in Bullet with Butterfly Wings is edited out on the tape.

The audio track has quite a bit of hiss, this is especially audible during
Perfect. Due to the amount of noise it is difficult to reduce it without
introducing worse artefacts, so the audio track remains unedited. It should be
noted that this noise is recorded onto the tape itself. The noise is present in
both the normal and Hi-Fi audio tracks on the tape. The higher quality Hi-Fi
track is used in this release.

The video was transferred using the JVC deck as it has superior image quality,
however it failed to track the HiFi audio track, but the Panasonic succeeded.

Thanks to Cool As Ice Cream for sending me his tapes to transfer.
