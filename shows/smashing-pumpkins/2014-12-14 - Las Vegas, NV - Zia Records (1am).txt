Notes:

Published Feb 2022 (yes, I've had a lot on my plate to process).  This was a 1am
surprise acoustic show after the Brooklyn Bowl show on 2014-12-13.  I forgot to
adjust my recorder level in preparation for an acoustic set, so much gain (and
hiss) was added.  Fortunately, I managed to get a good spot for recording thanks
to a friend I met at the Bowl who got us quickly to Zia after the show.
There's a few pops and crackles from failing mic cables noticeable during
"Being Beige" and possibly other places, but it's fairly minor.
