**Remastered** by thir13en


Smashing Pumpkins
2000-05-13
Aerial Theater
Houston, TX, US 


Redone for the reseed. I was never too happy about the splice of "Today-BWBW" on my first go round with this, and there was still transfer clips from the original mastering which was done a bit hot for digital. I did some tasteful de-crackle on those break-up spots, really toned down the excessive mid-to-high range and gave the bottom some definition. Much warmer and fuller than the original, and now just a bit cleaner and bigger than my first RM of it. 

*I did a fast fade-out on "Today" and a fast fade-in on "BWBW". 
*The two overly extended crowd passages for the encores were shortened a bit but all show material and banter remains.
