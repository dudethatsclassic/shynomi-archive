Notes:
The shorter (but no lower quality) sequel to the original Fruit Bat Salad, this one has a different format - the entirety of Siamese Dream in sequence, picked from several different video recordings of shows. In addition, there's a fifteen minute clip from Lollapalooza 94, where Billy guests with his friends The Frogs (including one who is - unfortunately - wearing blackface) for a goofy performance.
