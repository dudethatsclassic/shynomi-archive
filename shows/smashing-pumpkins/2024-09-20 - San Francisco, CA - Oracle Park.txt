Smashing Pumpkins - 2024 09 20 @ Oracle Park, San Francisco CA

Note:
The Pumpkins went on as the sun was setting. Somehow, the sound was a bit muddy, probably due to the acoustics and the fact they were playing loud - there was a lot of echo bouncing off of the back. There was some wind as well, I had mic covers so the wind noise isn't noticeable, but there is some phasing from the hanging speaker stacks moving a bit. People also kept getting up to get beer or walking through our row to get to the floor entrance, which started getting on my nerves. There are 11 pictures included, that I took. Hopefully this isn't too much for anyone.
