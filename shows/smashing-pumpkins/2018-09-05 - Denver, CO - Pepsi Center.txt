Show notes:
Wow, what an excellent performance, and damn it was loud in the Pepsi Center! For the first few songs I had to lower recording levels to avoid clipping, but despite all that and a bit of chatter between my wife and I and others who were sitting next to us, I think the recording turned out nicely.

Billy skipped some lyrics in the first chorus of Cherub Rock. The old man's memory is not what it used to be, I suppose :)

Enjoy!
