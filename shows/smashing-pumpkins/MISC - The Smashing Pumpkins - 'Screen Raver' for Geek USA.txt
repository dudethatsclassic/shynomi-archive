SCREEN RAVER

This Screen Raver is designed to be played in conjunction with the
Smashing Pumpkins CD "Siamese Dream". This Screen Raver will play
only one song on the CD: "Geek USA". Make sure that your CD-ROM
drive is set up for playing audio CD's and that your speakers are
turned on. Some external CD-ROM drives must be hooked up to external
speakers or headphones in order to hear the audio CD. Refer to the
drive's documentation to determine if audio CD's can be played in 
your drive and to obtain instructions on how to set it up for playing
audio CD's. If you do not have a CD-ROM drive, the Screen Raver will
still play but it will not be accompanied by music.

To quit the program, type "Q". If you quit the program by any other
means, you will exit but the audio CD will keep on playing. If that
happens, you will have to stop the audio CD manually.

Typing "B" will restart the song. The song will automatically restart
5 seconds after the song is done.
