Recording Notes:
- Security was a little tight and kept hovering right next to me, so I had to 
record “extra stealth” (I was unable to check and adjust levels at all). This
unfortunately resulted in a bit more bass during the full band sections and 
some extra crowd noise. Overall not too bad, and if you use equalizer to drop 
the bass a little, it’s actually pretty decent I think.
- There is a glitch in track 2. One of the few times I was able to (thankfully) 
check the levels the recording had paused, so I had to restart. I believe only 
about 30 seconds or so of audio was lost due to this.
