Another Jackal Production Vol 17: Smashing Pumpkins 2018-08-28 Golden 1 Center, Sacramento, CA

A very nice Smashing Pumpkins show.  This was a sprawling three hour set heavy on Mellon Collie & Siamese Dream, along with some fun covers.  Most shows I've attended at Golden 1 Center have had decent sound... at least for a basketball arena.  Much better than ARCO Arena (Echo Arena) ever was.  There were a few nearby talkers but mostly just an isolated comment here or there as opposed to lengthy philosophical discussions spanning full songs.

Includes a copy of the show advertisement & some crappy pics taken with my crappy iPhone 8.

Enjoy!!
