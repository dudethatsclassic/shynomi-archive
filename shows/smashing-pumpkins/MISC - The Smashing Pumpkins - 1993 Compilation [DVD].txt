Smashing Pumpkins

Footage from three different shows. Until a few weeks ago this footage was totally unheard of, a friend of mine received a VHS compilation from the taper of these shows, this wasn't even listed on the tapes contents and was discovered by accident after going through his archives. There's a few interesting things to note about this footage, firstly that no information of these shows were known to of existed of these shows before now, not even the setlists. Secondly, all the footage in this compilation is shot from the side of the stage and at one point from behind Jimmy during Geek U.S.A. in Detroit.

The video quality is great, the sound quality is excellent!
