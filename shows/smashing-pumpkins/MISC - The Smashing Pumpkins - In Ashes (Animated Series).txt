The Smashing Pumpkins
In Ashes (Animated Series)


Episode 1: The Color Of Love
"As The Crow Flies": The Smashing Pumpkins' first episode of the five-part animated series, In Ashes, and official video for "The Colour Of Love." Written and created by William Patrick Corgan.



Episode 2: Confessions Of A Dopamine Addict
"Inspirations, Aspirations": The Smashing Pumpkins' 2nd episode of the five-part animated series, In Ashes, and official video for "Confessions of a Dopamine Addict." Written and created by William Patrick Corgan.



Episode 3: Anno Satana
"I'm In Love With Your God": The Smashing Pumpkins' 3rd episode of the five-part animated series, In Ashes, and official video for "Anno Satana." Written and created by William Patrick Corgan.



Episode 4: Wyttch (Official Animated Video)
"Owl Wait": The Smashing Pumpkins' 4th episode of the five-part animated series, In Ashes, and official animated video for "Wyttch." Written and created by William Patrick Corgan.



Episode 5: Purple Blood
Lost IN The Dream Factory": The Smashing Pumpkins' 5th and final episode of the five-part animated series, In Ashes, and official animated video for "Purple Blood." Written and created by William Patrick Corgan.
