**Mastered** by thir13en

This is another one that I'll just call a master, not a RM. 
These were fine sounding demos, many of which could have easily 
been used as a professional release (except maybe the Batman songs). 
So I gave them the full mastering treatment, which basically means 
I used some slight mastering compression at the front of the chain. 

Great tunes here, some interesting different versions, 
and Billy trying out a new vocal styling. 
In preparation for the recording of Adore, 
he used a vocal coach. On some of the versions here there's 
a slight gravel to it (ie, My Mistake), almost as though he had a cold. 
Luckily he abandoned that idea and did it his usual way, 
though you can still hear a slight mellowing to his attack 
on some of the lighter pieces on Adore, like "Dusty..." or "Behold...". 

"...Unhook my lights to peek behind the flash..."
