Notes

This was the 1st show @ the Fillmore that was pro shot. so we might see some of these songs later. Billy was in a very good mood on this Friday night. a lot of short banter. The last banter was the only one long enough that i felt could use its own chapter. I have some wonders about the audio sync being perfect during Drown - 1979. It feels like it goes in & out of sync. some people don't notice it, the rest of the show feels like it is in sync perfectly.

Highlights: This show was pretty smooth. lots of guitar changes. Nice 1979 full band. Last performance of Daydream & The Crying Tree of Mercury @ the Fillmore. lots of panning in this footage to see a lot of the band
