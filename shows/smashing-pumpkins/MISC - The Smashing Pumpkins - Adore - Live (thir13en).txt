I started this compilation a couple weeks ago, as a tribute, and a big 'thank you' to Mixwell. 
Unfortunately, I ended up having to ask for his help since I didn't have (13)s of every major album track. 
You can expect to see the rest of the major albums in this format very soon. I will be seeding them one at a time.

This 'album' I was able to finish without Mixwells help, so here it is. Adore, in it's completion. 
Composed entirely of thir13en remastered tracks from various concerts. 
I really wanted this to fit on a single CD, but that just wasn't going to be possible.

The only modifications I have made from the original tracks is recompiled the album as one wave, 
crossfaded the tracks for continuity, then split them again.

The Adore-era shows were some of the best performances SP ever did, in my opinion... you should really check this one out.
