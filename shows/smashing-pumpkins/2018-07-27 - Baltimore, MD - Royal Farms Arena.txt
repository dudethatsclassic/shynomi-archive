Notes: First time seeing Billy since the Oceania tour in 2012, which was an amazing tour but this was the reunion! Bummer no Darcy but name a rock band without drama? Overall, this was a killer performance. Billy screws up the lyrics during Muzzle and laughs it off. That's what a professional does. 

I don't enjoy transiting to this venue but the amenities were top notch. Vendors walked around selling beer all night. During Zero you can hear a guy say "13." I used sign language to pay $13 for it. Other than that the only other recording note was during Bullet with Butterfly Wings I switched seats with some seat poachers that roamed up next to me and proceeded to sing and talk. I didn’t want to be annoyed anymore by them so I moved. Overall the recording kicks ass!
