Smashing Pumpkins
2007-08-22
The Carling Academy, Glasgow, UK

An Evening with the Smashing Pumpkins, recorded live at the Carling Academy in Glasgow on August 22nd 2007.
Recorded from the Balcony, 4th row centre.
Stereo mics with battery box onto Sony MZR-500 in LP2 mode.
Transferred optically to PC and EQd in Soundforge.
Incomplete as unfortunately my MD player failed approximately 19 minutes in (near the end of Hummer).
Taped & edited by Jambo.

Downloaded as a 119MB FLAC file.
Track splits with CD Wave Editor.
Files renamed, tagged with Winamp 5.35.
Added checksums and info text.

Source: Stereo mics (WM-61A) > battery box > Sony MZR-500
Transfer: Sony MZR-500 > optical out > WAV
Edit: WAV > EQ in Soundforge > FLAC > CD Wave Editor (track splits) > file rename > Winamp 5.35 (tags) > FLAC
Lineage: MD(m) > WAV > FLAC
