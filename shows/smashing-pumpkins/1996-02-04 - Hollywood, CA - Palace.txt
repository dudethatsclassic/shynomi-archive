NOTES:
This is the first show we are sharing from Dave Asselin’s collection

Dave sold his tape collection back in the day, and part of it eventually ended up in a seaside town in the northeast of England. We managed to secure the tapes last year and have spent time going through them to identify what’s what

Whilst much of Dave's DAT collection has been transferred before, this particular tape was never located as it was actually a DAT to ANA transfer

Some notes about the show:

We've had an apparent setlist for almost 30 years but the second encore has actually been noted incorrectly as it kicks off with Frail and Bedazzled, an undocumented performance. We had no record of the band playing the song after 1995, so this 1996 show now makes it the last known performance by the original lineup

The recording also includes a really great version of Silverfuck and Billy comments during the show that this was the best show of the 3 performances at the Palace

The recording is incomplete as it only contains the electric set. The taper also appears to pause the tape between encores unless there has been some editing between the tape generations
