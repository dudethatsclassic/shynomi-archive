Video Source - Most likely a PAL-sized version of the HD feed used for Mojo. Source was 720x576x32 native widescreen MPEG.
Resized and encoded to NTSC Widescreen DVD standards at 8300-CBR.

Patched in 13 mintues of websource video for "Gossamer" using my webstream cap.

----

Audio source - pTV from Obertu (complete show in high quality audio, dude you Rock!)
Patched in some missing audience portions using the webfeed.
Upsampled to 48K.


Big thanks to the usenet flooder and Obertu for the sources to make this happen.
