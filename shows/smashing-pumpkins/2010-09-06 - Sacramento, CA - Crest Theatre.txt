The Crest Theatre was an interesting venue to see The Smashing Pumpkins as it is an old time vaudeville theater converted to a movie theater.  Therefore, it has movie theater style seathing throughout.  So you don't have that same dynamic of people moving about since the seating is in the way.

I taped from the right side approximately 20 feet back from the right stack.  There were two girls that were so excited to see Billy that they needed to talk a lot about it for the first few songs.  It's not overly obnoxious and goes away after a few songs.  Sound quality is pretty darn good.

Please to enjoy...
