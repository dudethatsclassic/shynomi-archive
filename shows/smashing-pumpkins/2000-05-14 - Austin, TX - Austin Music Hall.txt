MY FAQ:



Smashing Pumpkins
Live at the Music Hall
Austin, Texas
May 14, 2000

This is one that I meant to get out over a month ago, but the switch happened and it was sort of forgotten. JB upped the original recently, here's now the RM of it. 

Another great Machina show, their second night in Texas. Something about SP in Austin doesn't quite seem right, they don't really fit the scene there. Maybe they should have done a SRV song, or even a Butthole Surfers tune. Either way, they came, they rocked, they moved on. And this is what's left.

I re-spread what the FM crunched, created a nice even spectrum for it to breathe in, then lit it up to the top, as always. No editing...

Like it, download and enjoy it.
Don't like it, that's nice too.
