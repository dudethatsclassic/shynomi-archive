Taper's notes:
- The venue (PalaMalaguti) is actually located in Casalecchio di Reno (a small town close to Bologna)
- It was my first recording with my new digital equipment (thanks to Antonio for suggestions)
- The recording is imho enjoyable but audience chatting/singing/screaming is sometimes annoying
- The main flaw is an unwanted/unexpected stop of the recorder during "Tonight, Tonight". About 50 seconds were lost, but fortunately I could promptly restart the recording minimising the "damage". For this reason track 04 ("Tonight, Tonight") has been faded-out and track 05 ("Mayonaise") has been faded-in
- At 1:01 into track 05 ("Mayonaise") you can hear me hit unvoluntarily the cap where the mic was mounted... of course, I was still confused after having discovered the recording stopped 1 min before...
- A possible microgap could have been generated by the recorder since Edirol R-09 do not support track longer than 2 GB. So I have manually split the recording at the beginning of the encore (I have used the very same point to split tracks 33 and 34).
- Summarising, the recording sums three big WAV files. No part of the recording has been cut; no remastering has been done, only a significant amplification and some fades as follows:
	WAV_1	[0:24:53]	tracks 01-04	track 04 faded-out
	WAV_2	[1:57:44]	tracks 05-33 	track 05 faded-in
	WAV_3	[0:14:14]	tracks 34-37	track 37 faded-out
- Splitting the 11-min Cash Car Star segue has been challenging. I hope no one (or few) will be disappointed about the result
- Classic taper's wishes: no E-bay, no mp3-to-wav (mp3 only for personal use!), share preferably in torrent trackers (preserve file integrity!)
