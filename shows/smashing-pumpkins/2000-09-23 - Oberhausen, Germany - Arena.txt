Notes:
I left an untouched copy of the original info file as I received it.
I added tags in Winamp 5.34, created ffp and st5 checksums, and added a spectral analysis.
This is one hell of a piece of work. From a good source, despite being a little on the thin side, it now has vitality and range. The beef has been turned up and the highs are crystal clear. Listen for Jimmy's cymbals throughout, and gain appreciation for what the thir13en remasters can do for your listening pleasure. This is a treat, amongst the finest remasters around, no question.
