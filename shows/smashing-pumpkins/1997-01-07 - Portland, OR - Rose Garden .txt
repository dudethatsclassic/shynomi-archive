Notes: I traded for this recording back in 2001 and have not seen it online on a tracker or on lists for a very, very long time. It's definitely a surfaced recording, but it isn't a very common one. This is a shame, too, as the show is outstanding. Highlights include a fantastic "Porcelina" into "Rocket" combo, a spot-on "Rhinoceros", a fabulous "Drown" and another crazy "Silverfuck" which features a unique and very cool outro unlike any others I've heard from this tour. As to the lineage I have little reason to doubt that this is completely digital, though it would not surprise me too much if there was an analog step in there somewhere either. Remember, this was back in the day when people didn't keep track of that stuff as closely. Maybe somebody who specializes in spectral analysis or something can check it out? I don't know. Regardless, I hope you all enjoy it. It's a great show from one of the last great eras of the mighty SP.
