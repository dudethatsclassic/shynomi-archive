Concert Airing:
Marquee Sports Network, Sunday, October 9 at 9:30 PM CT.

https://www.marqueesportsnetwork.com/smashing-pumpkins-concert-to-air-on-marquee/

Longtime Cubs fan Billy Corgan and the Smashing Pumpkins are coming to Marquee Sports Network.

Last month, the Smashing Pumpkins performed in front of a sold-out crowd at Metro in Wrigleyville, and the full live concert will air on television for the first time on Marquee Sports Network, Sunday, October 9 at 9:30 PM CT.

The concert took place as part of Metro’s 40th anniversary celebration in partnership with Chicago alternative radio station, Q101.

During the two-hour set, the band performs tracks from across its extensive catalog, while premiering new songs from its upcoming studio album, ATUM.

On the Marquee: The Smashing Pumpkins Live presented by DSV will air on Sunday at 9:30 PM CT, only on Marquee Sports Network

ABOUT ON THE MARQUEE: Marquee Sports Network’s music series, On the Marquee spotlights the biggest local bands and artists, as well as acts performing at iconic Wrigley Field. Past episodes of On The Marquee have featured Umphrey’s McGee, Chris Stapleton, Zac Brown Band and the Dead.
