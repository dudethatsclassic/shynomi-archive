Opening night of "The Rats In A Cage" Tour. Billed as "Two unique sets
celebrating Mellon Collie at 30 & nearly four decades of unruly hits &
dark treasures." Each set lasted roughly 85 minutes and weighed heavily
on the deep cuts in both. Please check setlist.fm for song liberation
detail and live debuts. There were quite a few as this was the opening
night of the tour. I have been fortuante to see TSP many times going
back to the original MCIS tour (in 1997) and this was without question
one of my favorites. The band sounded terrific, the setlist was full of
surprises and the sound in the Schottenstein was incredible. It was one
of the best sounding arena shows I've heard and I feel the recording
reflects that. I didnt even have to EQ!! I did remove a bunch of screams,
yelps and whistles (mostly in between songs) in RX7 as they did start to
get pretty annoying during playback. The set break (intermission) was
around 10 minutes (maybe 15?) and the crowd noise was cross faded for a
seamless listen. Some level adjustments were made during quieter sections.
