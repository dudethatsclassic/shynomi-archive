Smashing Pumpkins
1993-03-15
Center Stage
Atlanta, GA



Thanks to the supplier man, haylo.

Video is quite good, once the folks around the taper settle in it's a great view of the stage. Killer set but unfortunate that "F&B" is cut just 20 seconds into the song. I filled in the audio with the AUD source and I did a quick-fill of video for that song. Basically it's a few cut up pieces in slow-mo crossfading once in a while. Better than a black screen or incomplete set. Also the very beginning of "Geek" is cut, I think the taper thought the banter would go on longer as they were switching guitars for that song. NBD though.

Transferred the same way as always, capped through my HD-cam and down-converted to DV, edited in Vegas and rendered in Main Concept MPG2 and Dolby Digital AC3. Sorry though, no 5.1, it's just stereo. I played with a bunch of different audio setups on this one and decided the straight stereo sounded the best to me. Audio was worked over, sounds ok, I think it's a little better than the straight audio, but not all that much. That's AUD's for ya.

Built a small menu for it, DVD begins with a title screen then the main menu appears, and song selections can be made from the track menu. Plus it has some nice soothing tunes and a built-in SP heart-shaped glitter lamp for your extra enjoyment.

Have Fun! I did.
