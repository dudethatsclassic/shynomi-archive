Technical Info:
Audio One - This is the matrix release and is pcm.  It had 
to be stretched in order to sync it to the video. The first 40 
seconds of this track are from the vhs audio (before 
compression) to fill in the silence.

Audio Two - This is the original vhs audio and is in ac3.  
I chose to do ac3 so that the video quality could remain
higher and so that I did not have to re-encode the video.

Video - Captured with an ati 8500dv.

The vhs is an unknown gen.  Brian Muehlhaus had this to say
on netphoria

I have the complete Storytellers video, even though it isn't really complete. 
I have been told that I have one of the lower generations around. Don't know 
if that is true, but I got mine from a guy named Jonathan Hunt in 2001 or so. 
If I remember correctly, some of the songs they played again aren't on the 
video I have, so technically it isn't complete, but I have heard that the 
complete video with every song that we have on the audio versions isn't in 
any circulation. Don't know if that is true.
Brian


The chapters of this release are messed up because I encoded
the video with open gops.  Some chapters start a little into 
the song.  It doesnt effect anything too greatly.
