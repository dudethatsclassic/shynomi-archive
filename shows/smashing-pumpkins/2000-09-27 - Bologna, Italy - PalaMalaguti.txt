Notes:
This show features the band changing up the setlist a bit from the opening week of the tour. Raindrops + Sunshowers replaces Blue Skies Bring Tears, and Cherub Rock and 1979 feature spontaneous ending jams. This is the second, more complete source of this show. Buzzing noise in starts of Thirty-three and Blank Page, end of Cherub Rock, and during band introductions. Porcelina Of The Vast Oceans and Blank Page cut in.
