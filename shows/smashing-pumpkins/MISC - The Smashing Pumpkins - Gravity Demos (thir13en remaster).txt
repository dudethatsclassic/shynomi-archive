**Remastered** by thir13en


Billy's Gravity Demos get the thir13en treatment. Bigger, badder, better...the way they should be heard.


So many excellent instrumentals and some great ideas that weren't totally fleshed out, but the inner workings of a great band and it's wonderous music is pleasantly displayed none-the-less. Must have material for any true Pumpkins fan, it's worth many repeated listens.
