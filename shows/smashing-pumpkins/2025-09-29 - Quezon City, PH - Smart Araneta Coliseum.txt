TAPER NOTES:
This was a very messy and difficult recording. I didn’t buy the seats myself so apparently I was right down the middle in the lower box area of the arena, which is very good for viewing purposes but horrible for taping (it’s extremely visible - the venue staff and photographers kept passing by and lingering. I tried moving a few times but it didn’t really work out). There are various volume and quality fluctuations throughout the recording, and it’s a little muffled and very boomy. It’s also incomplete because the recorder fell down during Bullet With Butterfly Wings, so a part of that song is missing. But at least there’s countless versions of that on YouTube. Some chat is also cut. The concert itself was amazing and very well played but I was disappointed with the lack of variation of the setlist, (unlike the first time in 2012 when they came to Manila) but oh well, that is a nitpick and one for the whole tour itself.
