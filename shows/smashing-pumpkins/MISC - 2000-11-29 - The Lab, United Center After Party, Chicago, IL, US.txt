This is the full, uncut, raw footage of a few impromptu interviews
with some of the guests at "The Lab" in Chicago, IL, USA, following
the Smashing Pumpkins concert that evening.

This event was organized and hosted by Smashing Pumpkins fans, for
Smashing Pumpkins fans, held right after the second last concert ever:
November 29, 2000. It was a chance for all of us in the community to
finally meet face to face.

Thanks go out to all for making that night so memorable, and to The 
Smashing Pumpkins for bringing us all together one last time.

Enjoy.
