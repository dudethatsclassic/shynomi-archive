I bought this show at a record convention in OKC in around May 1994 for $25.

Audience shot

This show has some flaws in the tape, and the camera tends to stray here and there. The cameraman is primarily filming Billy most of the time. The very beginning of Geek U.S.A is cut, the very end of Soma is cut as well as the very beginning of Cherub Rock. However, there is some very interesting conversation going on b/w Billy & the audience, eventually leading to Billy walking offstage in the middle of Silverfuck. They come back onstage, but the first 1:30 of Bury Me is silent. Despite the flaws the show is very entertaining. Sound quality is pretty good. Enjoy!!
