Smashing Pumpkins
All Hallows

All taken from the "Machina Acoustic Demos", here's some refurbished versions of a few of my favorites. Unknowingly done with a spooky twist, spirits obviously guided the flavor of these tunes, fittingly for an All Hallows celebration. 

So, a little "Rawktober" music if you will:


01 - Glass & the 13 Ghosts
02 - 13 Kills
03 - 13 Brings Tears
04 - Autumn 13thwww


1 - A slightly trippy, drippy pyscho-psychedelic version of "Glass & the Ghost Children".

2 - A textured, drony version of "Speed Kills"

3 - Heavily phasy-tremor-terror version of "Blue Skies Bring Tears"

4 - The-Opposite-Of-Acoustic version of "Autumn"



Absolutely everything in these versions are from the orginal versions, all additional noises, samples, loops, etc.,were generated by using samples of the original pieces.
