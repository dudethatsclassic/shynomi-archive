COMMENTS:

I began recording shows in 2007 when Genesis decided to reunite and go on tour.  From there I was amazed at the number of "bootleg" shows of them and the quality was astounding.  To that I bought an olympus LS10 and found this website.

My first show was Boston in Greenville, SC.  It was such a crappy recording but I listened to it nonstop.

From there, I learned from many great minds here on DIME.  This became such a drive and addiction to where I wanted to do what others were doing.  I wanted to create or at least record the perfect show (knowing well that will never happen).

I have gone through many progressions in recording and mixing.  However, it is nice when a show lands in your lap and is SO good, you just have to keep working with it until you think it can't go further.
This show is one of them. 

Several years prior when I started recording wireless, I had NO idea of what I was doing. I attempted the Smashing Pumpkins at the Road Runner Wireless amphitheater.  The recording was just enough to listen but not the quality I had wanted to achieve. I vowed I would get the Smashing Pumpkins the next time the came around, if they ever did.

I am quite pleased with this show and wanted to share.  To those few key people (you know who you are), sincere thanks for assistance.  Nuff said, you know the time spent and committed.

I am providing the soundcheck as an added goodie.........

Please respect my show by not trading, sharing on other sites (unless asking first), remixing, and especially selling it for profit.  Please support the Smashing Pumpkins by purchasing their music and merchandise.

Wireless may not be your thing, but please keep comments civil.  It will not change my mind as to this medium of recording.
