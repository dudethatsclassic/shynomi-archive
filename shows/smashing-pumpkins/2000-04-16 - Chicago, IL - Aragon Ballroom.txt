No easy way to say this, so I'll just blurt it out:

-----it's a different remaster than the one I did last spring. There, I said it------

So, I re-did this for a couple of reasons:

1. It was one helluva show, a personal favorite and I felt the first RM could be improved on a bit.
2. A friend who wanted my version of this show also happens to like the banter. He never asked me to put it back in. I did it as a favor. Even though he loves the RM's, he also wishes I wouldn't cut the banter. So for him I left it all in. Being respectful of each other pays off. 


Edits: 

Imperfections in Everlasting Gaze were repaired. There was an odd jump in volume from disc 1 to disc 2, now corrected.
All XRT mentions, DJ v/o's and mindless babble was cut, a good 7 or 8 minutes of just total blah-dy blah-dy blah...gone. All show material remains, all banter, intro's, false starts, bad story telling...it's all there. The show has only been edited to smoothly run from song 1 to 23, like one long uninterrupted show.

I saw when this was posted at DIME that one person commented about liking the DJ banter too. I was dumbfounded, appalled even. But I quickly realized, there it is, the true fact that you'll never please everyone, ever. Why try? Not gonna.
