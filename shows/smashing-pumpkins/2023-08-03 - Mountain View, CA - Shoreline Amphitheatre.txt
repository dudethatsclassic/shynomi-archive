The Smashing Pumpkins - 2023 08 03 @ Shoreline Amphitheatre, Mountain View CA

Show note:
Good show line-up with Rival Sons & STP playing long sets. Pumpkins seemed to be having fun, with Billy joking that James was standing just where Jerry stood.  Good lightshow as always, photos included. The night was windy, luckily I had mic covers to keep the wind out, but there is a little bit of phasing from the house PA at times. It seems like it took a couple of songs for the sound to get dialed in correctly.
