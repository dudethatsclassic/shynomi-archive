--notes--
* this sounds noticeably better than previous versions and does not contain skips in the encores, but still suffers from distortion and brickwalling

* taper pauses between encores

* first performance of Bullet with Butterfly Wings, although Billy has mentioned that the verse riff floated around as far back as the 1992/93 Siamese Dream sessions in Atlanta

* Red Red Meat opened

* infinite thanks to BLG for this recording

* this is a terrific show from the band's space-psychedelia period [listen for James' raygun], highly recommended if you can deal with the below average sound quality
