ZODEON CRYSTAL HALL (Limited Edition)

The official album release of The Smashing Pumpkins’ Zodeon At Crystal Hall is finally here!

Recorded during the pandemic and inspired by ’65 and ’66 British psych influences. This 12-track album is officially released on gorgeous 180g vellum clear vinyl. Tracks from Zodeon At Crystal Hall were originally available as 5 singles in the ATUM: A Rock Album in Three Acts boxset - this 12-track release includes 3 unreleased tracks and is sequenced the way it was always meant to be heard in stereo. With stunning art by Katelan Foisy and Chloé Mendel Corgan, this is a must-have for any SP fan.
