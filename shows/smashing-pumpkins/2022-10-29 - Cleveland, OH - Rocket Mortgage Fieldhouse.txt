Falls performance in 2019, but thanks to a cancelled flight in NYC, I had to
miss it. So, this was exciting for me as it had been the first time in over
4 years. They are currently prepping "Atum: A Rock Opera in Three Acts" over
the next few months and 2 songs were performed from it here. I had a really
annoying girl 2 rows in front of me yelping every 1 to 2 minutes throughout

when I could in RX7.
