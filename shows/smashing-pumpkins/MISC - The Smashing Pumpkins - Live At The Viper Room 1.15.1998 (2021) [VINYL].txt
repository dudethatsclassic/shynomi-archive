this vinyl rip was obtained through the band's fan community as 4x 16-bit/44.1kHz .wav files (one file per side) of unknown lineage.

after comparing with multiple audience bootleg sources of the same concert, and with the band's official marketing and promotion clips, it was determined the vinyl was recorded while spinning at a slightly incorrect (slightly fast) speed.

processing performed in audacity:
- import into 32-bit float
- reduce speed by 3% via 'change speed' effect to match existing other recordings
- split into tracks
- shaped dither applied via 'best quality' sample rate conversion setting for export to 16-bit flac

all the above was detailed to OPS staff for approval before uploading.
