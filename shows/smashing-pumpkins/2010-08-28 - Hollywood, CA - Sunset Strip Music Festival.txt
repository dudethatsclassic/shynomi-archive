Shot from the left side of the stage about 6 "rows" back, the video can get a bit shakey at times but quickly becomes steady.  The shots are well framed and the focus is crisp.  The audio is good but some songs are slightly overdriven by the bass.  Overall it is a pretty good show.

I had a problem playing this disc in my computer but it played just fine in a DVD player.  

The Smashing Pumpkins embrace an open taping policy.  I've been a big fan for many years and this is my first public video contribution.  Enjoy.
