Ray Charles
September 28, 1976
Liederhalle, Stuttgart, Germany

This show apparently was first uploaded by ubu and then reseeded several times by theocrite (my sincere thanks to both of them!). I got it as Torrent 204820 and found this version to suffer from a lot of DC offset which moreover varies between tracks, causing nasty clicks at the start and end of files during playback and unnecessarily reducing headroom. I corrected this (using Sound Studio) and made a few very short crossfades during the applause to compensate for the remaining unevennesses.
