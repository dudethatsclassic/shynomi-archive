I'm by no means an expert on Nirvana, but I don't let that stand in the way when it comes to 

transferring and sharing tapes that come my way.  I did some research on this recording.

This particular date has never been on the TTD tracker until now.

There are 3 known audience sources.  Source 2 and 3 both have the last song cut.

I do have source 2 and will be uploading it tomorrow.  I have not heard source 3.

What I'm offering here is the first and most complete source.

The original tape flip on the master tape is after 'Stain', so there is a tiny bit missing at that point.

The tape flip on this 1st gen copy was after 'Been a Son'.  I've pieced that back together seamlessly.

The only adjustments I made to this was amplify the volume an additional 4 decibels and balance channels.

I've included a scan of the j-card and cassette in the torrent.
