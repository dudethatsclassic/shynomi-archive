These are all of the tracks left off from the original compilation, either
because they didn't fit, weren't classed as song or weren't available at 
the time.

way, they're as they were originally. They only thing I've done is two break
the six minute home demo into two tracks - the jamming, and "Old Age".

What is included is the home demo of "Old Age", including the jamming that 
preceeds it, and the two unknown songs/jams from the 1991-11-25 radio session.

Also included is corrected artwork, the original artwork had a couple of
errors. Sorry about that!
