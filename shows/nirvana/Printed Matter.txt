This is the FULL log of the 3-hour chat with Jim Griffin from
Geffen Records. He answered a lot of questions - some of them several times.
He tried to explain why many Nirvana sites were 'shut down' lately,
by himself and Geffen and what the real reason for this was.
Jim joined us about 90 minutes before the chat was scheduled to begin,
so it was pretty lucky I got it all logged. My nick is "NirvClub" -
and Mr. Griffin's nick is "Griffin" and later "JGriffin".
This is the full, unedited, uncensored log so it might contain
some pretty irrelevant stuff, and things will get repeated a lot.

Enjoy!

Rasmus Holmen
www.nirvanaclub.com
