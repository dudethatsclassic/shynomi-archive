The exclusive interview of Kurt Cobain for 
Helter Skelter et Hyacinth fanzine November 21st 1991.
Hotel Royal Fromentine, Paris, France.

It's so hard to write anything about Kurt Cobain. Everything 
has been said. I thought it would be easier after all these 
years (this interview was made 10 years ago). It was only 
yesterday. But i don't want to dwell more on that subject. 
It took me quite a long time to overcome it. 

This meeting was planned for Aligre Fm & a fanzine named 
Hyacinth. We were following that band & the Seattle scene 
since 1989 and it was obvious for us to keep on. We met when 
they were in Paris for the first day of their promo tour for 
the release of Nevermind. A late thursday evening in a hotel 
in Pigalle in November 1991. Kurt Cobain had prefered to talk 
to a french fanzine than to the parisian corporate rock radio.

I would like to thank Naïa without who this interview wouldn't 
have been the same. She asked all those non-musical questions 
that take all their depth because of what have happened. I 
thought it was better to leave this interview unedited, so 
i was sure not to betray the thought of Kurt Cobain. We have 
always refuse to sell it and the best way to show our 
gratefulness is to put it online for free. take care
