In October 1992 two British writers, Victoria Clarke and Britt Collins, were writing a book about Kurt. Apparently they were going to accuse Courtney of drug abuse during pregnancy in the book. To prevent the story from getting public Kurt, Courtney, Dave and even Kurt's aunt left a series of threatening messages on Victoria Clarke's answering machine.  The tape with the messages is 36 minutes long and contain 9 messages from Kurt, 1 from Dave, 1 from Kurt's aunt, and several more from Courtney.
