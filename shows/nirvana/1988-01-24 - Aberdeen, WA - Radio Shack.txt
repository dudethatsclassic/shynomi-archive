This was originally broadcast on "American Journal" in 1994. American Journal is a TV program that has aired exclusive NIRVANA content several times, stemming from favorable and close ties to Courtney Love. In this segment, Eric Harder, a producer of this early NIRVANA video shoot, is filmed giving his tape to a grieving Courtney, accompanied by Kat Bjelland. 

It is unknown whether this tape in a more complete form will ever see the light of day.  If so, it would likely be on a future NIRVANA release and not through unauthorized fan distribution.

In the segments here, Kurt and Krist mime along with their guitars to the 1.23.88 demo recording.  Dave Crover plays live drums and those can be heard in several segments.
