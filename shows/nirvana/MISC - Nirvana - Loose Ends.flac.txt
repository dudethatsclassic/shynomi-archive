NIRVANA 
"Loose Ends" compilation

This compilation is every studio and radio session currently available 
in the Nirvana trading community that has not had an official release.

This, along with "With The Lights Out", the recently released box set, 
present every known Nirvana recording.

Every track on this compilation is taken from the best available source. 
In addition to this, each track has been remastered to ensure the best 
quality sound possible.

This compilation was compiled and remastered by John W. Busher. He gave 
up hours of his free time to kindly do this compilation for us. Please 
show your gratitude by distributing this freely, and not converting it
into mp3, or other "lossy" formats.

If you have any questions, email him at mudshark@ptd.net
