==================================

This set collects the best of Nirvana's early years that has yet to appear on an official release. The sound quality and performance quality were taken into consideration when choosing the best versions. All of the material presented herein is from the lowest generation underground sources. There was no brickwalling, compression or noise reduction applied during mastering...only the speed, EQ and occasionally the balance was tweaked to achieve as close to a "perfect" version as possible. You won't find these tracks in better quality.
