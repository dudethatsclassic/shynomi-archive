Video surfaced online March 2020.


Single camera PRO-shot video, shot partially from an elevated position from the back of the venue, partially from the right/front and partially from the right/left/back on stage. Has timecode box covering part of the screen.
Includes only "Smells Like Teen Spirit", Breed (cuts in and cut), "Lithium" (First attempt cuts in), "Lithium", "Love Buzz" (cuts out), "Come As You Are" (cuts in and has a lot of cuts), "Been A Son" (has a lot of cuts and cuts out), "Negative Creep" (cuts out), "On A Plain" (cuts in), "Blew" and "Dumb" (cuts out).
After "Love Buzz" the taper disconnected the microphone from the soundboard and moved around the venue leaving the camera recording only with the built in microphone ,it lasted until the start of "On A Plain". The start of the song has built in audio, some secongs without audio, then the taper connects again the microphone into the soundboard.
During "Breed" and "Negative Creep" it's only footage of the crowd.
Clips appear in the "You Know You're Right" music video and have been broadcast on MTV.




Show footage is 23mins:
01: Smells Like Teen Spirit (complete)
02: Breed (cuts in, cut)
03: Lithium false start (cuts in) / Lithium (complete)
04: Love Buzz (cuts out)
05: Come As You Are (cuts in / various cuts)
06: Been A Son (various cuts)
07: Negative Creep (opening riff only)
08: On A Plain (cuts in, contains an audio drop-out)
09: Blew (complete)
10: Dumb (cuts out)
