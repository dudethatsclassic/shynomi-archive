Extract from The Herald concert review:

One last time then. On Saturday night, the members of the band Garbage descend on a cold and grey Edinburgh, the haar so low it’s a struggle to see Edinburgh Castle from the esplanade, with a sense of gratitude and a purposeful sense of occasion.

The gratitude is for the weather. They’ve just spent six weeks sweltering around Edinburgh, after all. The occasion? Possibly the final ever appearance by the band in Scotland. Or so front woman Shirley Manson had suggested beforehand.

And if she doesn’t say as much onstage this evening, the importance of this evening is never in doubt. “For me tonight is the culmination of 40 years in music,” Manson tells us, “which, as you can imagine, feels both sobering and f****** fantastic.”

Playing this hometown gig, she will later add, is the highlight of her musical career, on a par with the time Garbage played the opening of the Scottish parliament.
