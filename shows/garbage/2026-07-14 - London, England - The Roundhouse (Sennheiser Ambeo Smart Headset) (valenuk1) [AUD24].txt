Source, Location & Transfer:

Source: Recorded with iPhone 12, with Sennheiser Ambeo Smart Headset
Location: Main arena area, approx. 10m from the stage, started on the right of a sound deck and then moved several times, further right.
Transfer: Raw Flac 24/48 via Motiv Audio > Tags, Track Splits, Fades & Tags added via AVS Audio Editor > Dime

Notes:

First time using this recording set up in a inside venue, and initially I struggled with the high end distortion, as well as chatters, clappers, drunks and difficult trying to find an empty-ish space to try and avoid them all. I progressively moved further right from the sound deck , further right until I find a spot where there wasn't a great deal of chattering or clapping going on. My Smart Watch flagged that on a number of occasions the sounds levels had hit / exceeded 85 & 90dB, however the sound quality of the captured recording was okay, not brilliant due to the distortion in the first few tracks, but that improves as I moved around the arena. still some nattering and noise though, but at least its another recording obtained.

All done for recoding shows from this tour now, hopefully someone else will record the final two shows in Ireland. There may be more garbage shows recorded in the future years to come, but who knows if/where or when.
