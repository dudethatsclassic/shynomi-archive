What a wonderful night. This was my first time in the Kings Theatre and it
makes me wish I lived back in NY! (I grew up on Long Island). What a venue!
Not only was it quite beautiful but the acoustics were tremendous and every
seat (from what I could tell) had a great view of the stage. Shirley was
very talkative. Im not sure if shes been doing this every show or if she
had more to say to us being in NY on the final night of the tour. If
you notice some track times are longer than the song should be, thats
her! "#1 Crush" was not on the setlist and was played as an audible
when the band abandoned (an almost complete) "No Horses". I did have some
clappers near me, so I ran the automatic click remover between most songs
(not all) in Adobe Audition during the worst parts. On a final note, please
be aware during the first 1:10 or so of "Afterglow" my line in cable
was not plugged all the way in on the SP-SPSB-11. Therefore, it only
recorded one channel. That section appears as a dual mono. I was checking
both L and R levels before putting the deck away and resolved it before
Shirley even sang a note. Thank god! Always check your gear, tapers! This
could have been mono the whole way through had I not been thorough.
Mistakes DO happen. Enjoy everyone! =)
