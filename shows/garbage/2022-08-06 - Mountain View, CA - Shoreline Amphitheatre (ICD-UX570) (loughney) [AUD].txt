Garbage - 2022 08 06 @ Shoreline Amp, Mountain View, CA

Show note:
Second time seeing Garbage this year at Shoreline. Last show of a 3 month tour, the band seemed really into playing, but at the same time seeming happy to be at the end of the tour. This was a loser show, with lots of shout outs to the crew and team. Taping location was in the seated section which improved the sound, but there were some chatty folks around us who get picked up on the recording at times.

Recording notes:
Backup capture, using the Sony's on-board mics. Picked up a bit of talkers around me since the mics weren't isolated.
