Tape story:
The venue is the main arena in Padua 
I have located myself in the centre, 20 m from the stage
Few people around me (for most part of the concert I had 1-2 m of free space in front of me)
The recording is from very good to excellent with only occasional, distant background chatting and short clapping episodes near mics

What about the show? 
It was my third Garbage show (the others were in Vigevano 2012 and Milan 2016, both taped)
Band in top form with a setlist mainly based on the first and the last albums
Butch Vig was not part of the tour due to illness, replaced by Eric Gardner
Shirley Manson was as usual very talkative and long talks appear at the end of tracks 07 ("American election day"), 11 ("the postman and Italy") and 15 ("Cherry Lips or Bleed Like Me?")
