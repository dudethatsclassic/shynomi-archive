Notes:

The last few seconds of Cherry Lips is cut, as I ran out of disc space on my minidisc (Damn you, Sony.... if you can't do a 
2 gb disc due to technical limitations, put out a 1.5 or 1.3 gb disc!!!).  I had taped the intro music (Hurt by Johnny Cash), 
thinking I'd have enough room on the disc since previous recent Garbage shows had been in the 75 minute range. 
But with some audience interaction and the extra Hammering In My Head being played due to the screwup on I Think I'm
Paranoid, I ran out of space.  A shame, since I've ended editing out Hurt to be able to torrent this on Dimeadozen.org.
Also cut out a bit of the encore break so that the main set fit on one 80 min disc.

The girl who spoke regarding the E-Tix problems didn't come out too clearly on my recording, although I heard pretty clearly
what she said during the show.  Some Garbage.com fan club members had ordered tickets for the show, but they weren't ready 
when they went to pick them up.  The girl mentioned having to wait in line since 4:00 (in the morning?).

Shirley's cut off shorts had "For a good time, call...." written on the front.
