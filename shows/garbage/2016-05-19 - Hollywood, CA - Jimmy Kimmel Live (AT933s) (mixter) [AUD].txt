Performance / Recording Notes: 

garbage make their long awaited return to the Jimmy Kimmel Show, some 3 1/2 year after their last appearance. The band 
performs their latest single, "Empty" . It's pretty typical garbage fare with the driving beat and chugging guitars. To 
me, it's not a standout track like "#1 Crush", "Stupid Girl" or "Only Happy When It Rains" (the latter of which is 
performed here). Perhaps it will grow on me over time. Nevertheless, it's still garbage, so it's still good. That's not 
to say that they haven't recorded a clunker before. This isn't one of those. I'm just happy that they're releasing a new 
album. Shirley vocal performance here is solid, though she sits in the middle of the mix at times. The band overall put on 
a great show.

The recording is a solid pull off the left stack. As such, there isn't much in the way of stereo imaging here but vocals and 
instrumentation are clear. With that being said, there is very little crowd noise. It's brief and for the most part between 
songs. However, the majority of it is in the right channel. That's where all the people were. I only had one tiny girl to my 
left and she didn't clap very much. Furthermore, there's no wind noise but the high frequencies do shift around a bit and the 
other instrumentation gets quieter when Steve rips into his leads (especially on "Push It"). Strange. Also, Eric's bass playing 
is a force to be reckoned with. It creates its own weather pattern. Oddly, this recording did not capture as much bass as I had 
expected / heard. Quite simply I was positioned too close to the stack. The slow moving lower frequencies passed through me and 
were not picked up by my mics that were some 4 feet above the sub cabs. Because it's not quite perfect, it's an EX-.
