Garbage - Loreley Festival, Germany 1998-06-20

Was looking for some Wildhearts CDs for a member on here when I came across this silver CD I bought ages ago.  Can't see it on here so thought I would share.  It is a great sounding FM/soundboard show from the 2.0 tour it is sadly not complete when you see the full setlist but has the better tracks on it I think.

The silver is called Push It Germany 1998 for those that want to know.
