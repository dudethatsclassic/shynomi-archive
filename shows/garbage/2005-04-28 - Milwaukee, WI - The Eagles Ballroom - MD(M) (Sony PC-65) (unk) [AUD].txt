If you are careful you can smuggle a Glock 9mm into the show, and given the surrounding neighborhood you probably should. Special thanks to the fan nearby who occasionally announces "Garbage" to remind us of who we are listening to. Shirley also received a request to bear children for an enamored fan and When I Grow Up is dedicated to Shannon, a longtime fan. Butch's High School Alma Mater is also referred to as VHS (Viroqua High School) and this is explained to Shirley. Shirley takes the Lord's name in vain at least once, and uses the "F" word 29 times. Shirley wore a peach colored dress, but I was unable to ascertain which shoes or underwear (if any) she was wearing. This is the complete show. Garbage takes the stage at 9:25pm...
