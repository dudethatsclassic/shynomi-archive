Garbage - 2022 06 02 @ Shoreline Amphitheatre, Mountain View, CA

Show note:
Opening concert for the 2022 season at the Shoreline. Lullaby from Rosemary's Baby was the intro music. PA system sounded a little off - sounded like the volume fluctuated a bit, but otherwise nice to be back. Wicked Ways had a couple of verses of Personal Jesus in the middle, Only Happy When It Rains started with quietly before the band kicked in.  You Look So Fine ended with a verse or two of Dreams.  After Happy When It Rains, a young fan held up a sign about Shirley's dog - Shirly pulled her up so the could get a photo together. A fun set.
