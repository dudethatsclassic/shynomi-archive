NOTES

First time I've been to the Masonic (Full name: Nob Hill Masonic Auditorium)...light-moderate
patdown, must more for looking for weapons than portable digital recorders, which I didn't even
try to hide.  Not a huge fan of the place, as it didn't host rock shows until a couple of
years ago, but think the sound was actually OK, though I could have used more power driving
the mics, though ended up having to bass-bost as what came out sounded really shrill and
tinny.  Other reviews I've read of the joint indicate people actually think it's too loud
and the place can't handle the volume.  *shrug*

Shirley was in a talkative mood, her reason being that they would have a month off after this 
show, but it seems other 2016 have her similarly chatting it up, so I've tracksplit her digressions 
individually, coming up with a novel numbering scheme for them.

Had to take a break a little past midway towards the set so the end of 'Why Do You Love Me'
got missed, as well as the entirety of 'Night Drive Loneliness'.  Didn't see either of these 
up on YouTube, oh well, that's how it goes sometimes.  

My alma mater's broadsheet has a decent write-up of the shindig:
http://www.dailycal.org/2016/09/29/garbage-revitalizes-90s-zeitgeist-authentic-masonic-performance/
