Sorry, couldn't edit it down to fit on a single CD though I'll leave it up to you to decide where the best disc split is. After Stupid Girl or after Trip My Wire seem to be good places.

Finally getting round to transferring a lot of my old DAT's to CD, so thought I'd start offering a few up. This one was taped by myself and has not been widely traded. Complete show, very good sound quality in my opinion although the performance isn't great. It was early in the tour and they make quite a few mistakes and really didn't seem confortable on stage at this one. There is some shifting in the sound evident as I got closer to the stage than I intended and was pushed around a bit so couldn't keep the mics steady but I don't think it detracts too much. The quieter soudns sound really good. Very little crowd noise.

Transferred direct from the master DAT, subtle EQ added to give it a bit more punch and balance but apart from that it's untouched.
