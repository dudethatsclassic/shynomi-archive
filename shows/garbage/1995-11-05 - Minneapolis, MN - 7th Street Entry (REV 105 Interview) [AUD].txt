Garbage
7th St. Entry
Minneapolis, Mn.
5 November 1995

REV 105 Interview

This is an audience recording of their first ever show done in a very small room attached to the First Avenue club in Minneapolis. 
Also included is a short interview they did at a local radio station the day of the show. 
I have no recollection of where I got this recording, but I most likely got it on cassette. 
Transferred from cassette by me using a Nakamichi MR-2 to a Tascam CDRW-700. Cassette > CD > EAC > FLAC 6.  
It is a very good audience recording, altho they obviously took out almost all the space in between tracks, for some unknown reason. 
So there is no between song banter. I would rate it an 8 overall. No artwork. The show runs 63:08, the interview 6:40.
