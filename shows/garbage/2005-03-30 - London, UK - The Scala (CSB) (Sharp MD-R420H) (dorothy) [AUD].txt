Resulting recording is a little bass heavy but vocals nice and clear and improves as it goes on. Taped about 30m from the stage from the raised sextion above the main floor next to the desk. They came on to Johnny Cash'd version of 'Hurt' which was ironic what with Nine Inch Nails playing just down the road at the same time.
