Notes: Sold out show that was originally scheduled to be at the Wiltern, but was moved to the Palladium due to ticket demand.  It was my first time seeing Garbage since they opened for the Smashing Pumpkins in 1996 and I thought the show was excellent.  The sound at the venue was loud and extremely bass heavy from my position, but I think the recording came out good and sounds better than it did at the show.  Garbage was much louder than the opener so there are a few level adjustments during the first song.
