Recorded from GA almost central in front of the sound desk, just under the edge of
the circle overhang.

Another great performance from one of my favourite bands. I first saw Garbage at this same venue in
early 1996. I had seen comments from other shows about constant talkers and to and fro-ing to 
the bar etc. Maybe I was lucky or maybe everyone around me was just there for the show, apart
from one clapper inbetween songs but I have no complaints about that. I definately benefited 
from being over 6" and with the sloping floor here, direct line of the hanging stacks is a great
place to record from. Another very solid capture.

Yes on a personal note I would like to have had Starman at one of the 2 London shows but, it is
what it is.
