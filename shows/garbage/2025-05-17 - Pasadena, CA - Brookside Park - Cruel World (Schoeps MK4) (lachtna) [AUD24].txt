Garbage - 2025 05 17 @ Brookside Park, Pasadena CA

Note: 
Cruel World 2025 - the 4th year. Unlike previous years, this was not a hot and sunny day. In fact, the skies were filled with goth clouds that cried on us for most of the day. I really enjoyed Garbage's set. I was right next to her during Siouxsie's set in year 2, and she seemed to be ecstatic to be playing the festival.
