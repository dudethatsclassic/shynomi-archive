REVIEW FROM "accordionbreath" on the www.garbage.com message board:

garbage was over the top, way invisible what i expected. shirley manson: white, energetic, beautiful and me close to her.
concert close to perfection. shirley manson's shirt was torn since the first song and that seemed for an excuse for her to
talk more with the audience, which she did a lot of while someone taped it to her skin or something. she was dressed plainly,
i thought she would dress a bit more designer like or something but it seemed comfy to dance and jump with. she moved from
the right to left side of the stage all the time. she picked up all the posters made for the band, read them, including one
that was posted the tours dates in texas and she said she didn't get it "is it an american joke?" :) she even signed autographs
while being on stage! :D after all was over my knees felt like my ears.. they're still buzzing, i feel i am still over there,
it's what i unconciously think of, shirley's focused eyes on people, her voice notably exploding on giving all garbage fans
want, having contact with them.
