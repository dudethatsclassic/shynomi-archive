Notes
This was a seated venue, no general admission. Shirley Manson jumped into the crowd for most of Hammering in My Head. I dropped the recorder during Vow and the audio is slightly muddy for a few seconds. 

The only audio editing I did was to amplify the audio slightly (without clipping) and split the tracks. You may want to turn the low end down in your equalizer when playing. I find the bass drum to be too loud, but I didn't want to mess with the mix, as I'm not an audio engineer.
