^ = Features "Personal Jesus" snippet [Depeche Mode].

Wow, what a set. I love Garbage. They have been a favorite since their debut
landed in the summer of 1995. They were 50% of the reason I went to this show.
Having never seen Alanis, getting to see Garbage at the same show was a huge
selling point. For the casual fans, this hour long set had it all. They played
all of their 90's singles and even threw in some new tracks from the new LP,
"No Gods No Masters". One surprise (for me) was the inclusion of the title track
when I thought they would be playing the lead single, "The Men Who Rule The
World". Checking setlist.fm, they have been alternating them between shows. The
only problem I had with their set? It was over too damn fast. I hope they
continue to tour for this release in 2022. Ohio needs some Garbage headline shows
soon! I know Im not alone! Shirley was very vocal chatting with the crowd between
songs, moreso than I've seen in the past. I loved her intro leading into "OHWIR" -
dont miss that one.

This turned out much better than the Alanis set. Not sure how or why, but it
sounds cleaner and more up front and I stood in the same exact position for both.

Opening for Alanis Morisette [recorded].
