Notes:

Garbage announced a full scale headline tour supporting their new album,
"Let All That We Imagine Be the Light" earlier this year. I had planned
to attend my home show of Columbus but when Shirley announced this will
be their FINAL headline tour of the States, I decided to hit up Newport
the night prior, as well. Why not? I was very excited to see they were
doing "Empty" and "Parade" when the tour began as I have never been able
to catch those songs live. Roughly 10 or 11 dates into the tour, Shirley
started feeling ill (cold or sore throat?) and started struggling on stage.
She then changed their setlist in New Haven removing the following songs:
"Empty", "Parade", "No Gods No Masters", "Godhead" & "Why Do You Love Me".
They added "Queer", "#1 Crush", & "When I Grow Up" in their place. I was
hoping she would be feeling better by the time Newport and Columbus rolled
around a week later, but the setlist remained. She did tell some fans in
VIP she was feeling better, but I'm not sure if those songs will return or
not. Shirley and the band still sounded amazing, and I'm still very happy
I attended both nights. "Empty" & "Parade" will have to remain on my list
to see, I guess. I had a great respectful crowd around me and I thought
the recording turned out good to great. I'm still unsure if I will share
my Starcrawler recording as their sound was extremely poor both nights.

Starcrawler opened. [recorded].
