Garbage
May 10, 2002
First Ave.
Minneapolis MN

Fresh digital transfer from master DAT May 23rd 2008

Taper:  "The Fridge" aka: alzeppelin

Location:  DFC resting against the soundboard railing

Source: Coresound High-end Binaurals > Bass Roll-off Box > Sony D100 > Master 48 khz DAT

Transfer: Master DAT > Sony D8 > 7 Pin > Coax Digital Cable > Echo Mia Soundcard ->
Cooledit Pro (downsample, normalize levels) > CDWAV (track splits) > FLAC Level 8

My first concert at this famous venue and I have been a fan ever since.  A
great show, even better than the 2005 show at this same venue with the band
very energetic.  We got the traditional story from Shirley about the band's
first ever show at the 7th Street Entry which is located in the same building.
Gwar played the same night and she tells the story much better than me.

The 4th show I recorded via stealth and the sound is excellent though the bass
was very high mainly due to Garbage's obsession with pumping the bass up to 11.

There is another source recorded by a friend of mine closer to the PA.  His
tape has a little more audience noise than mine.

Enjoy, and release more midwest shows from ANY band!
