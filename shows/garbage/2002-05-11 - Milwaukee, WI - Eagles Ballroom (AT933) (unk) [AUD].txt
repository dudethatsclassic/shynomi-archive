Source: 

The source for this release is a CDr (which is an Audience recording) that has recently been obtained as part of the circa 200+ private collection of Garbage Live Shows, which im presently indexing, converting to Flac and then sharing.

As such I have no other information to work with. I have no info on the original source, the tapper, location or their equipment. Spectural anlaysis for several files are provided, for information.

If the native Info files are available on these CD's, then that data will be captured in the info file that are generated, for each release. If this is your original release, thank you for recording, and please advise of any further data that you may have.

There are also a number of audio cassettes to work through to, which will be addressed once the CD's have been concluded.

A huge thank you again to the original collector Jayson Kaap who gathered up all these shows over the years.

Another huge thanks again to the https://www.garbagebase.com website, which has proven to be a valuable resource for helping out with show dates, locations & track listings etc.
