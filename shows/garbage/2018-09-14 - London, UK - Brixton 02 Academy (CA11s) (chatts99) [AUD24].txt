Recorded from GA left of the sound desk, in line with the hanging stack just under the edge of
the circle overhang.

A great performance from one of my favourite bands. I first saw Garbage at this same venue in
early 1996. This was the first show announced for this 20 years Paranoid Tour and I think this
recording is worthy of such a special occasion. I had seen comments from other shows about
constant talkers and to and fro-ing to the bar etc. Maybe I was lucky or maybe everyone around
me was just there for the show. I definately benefited from being over 6" and with the sloping 
floor here, direct line of the hanging stacks is a great place to record from. No talkers around
me and a very solid capture.
