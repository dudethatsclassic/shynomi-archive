Notes:

I started out with really low expectations, given the open air venue and the chattering going on around me I'm really quite pleased with how this recording had turned out and the recording settings within the Ambeo Smart Headset App (noise reduction on, ambient environment off). I had to move relocate in between Its All over and Special, as I had a loud happy clapper to my right, and a loud chattering party to my left. 

I also recorded this show with a back device (iPhone 16 Pro & MV88 mic) which has turned out a lesser sound quality then this recording for some reason (need to check the recording settings). I did contemplate trying the make a merged version in Audio Audition, but that's a new skill that I need to learn (and a project for another day maybe??). If there is any interest in the back up recording, let me know and I'll cut / index / tag / share.
