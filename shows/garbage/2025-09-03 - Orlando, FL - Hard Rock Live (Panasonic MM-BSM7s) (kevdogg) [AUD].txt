Notes: 
First show of the Happy Endings tour. The band is well rehearsed and the performance was incredible.
They pretty much trainwrecked Only Happy When It Rains but that was the only fiasco that I noticed.
I've never pulled a good recording from this venue being somewhere other than right under the p.a.
Now this recording was going smoothly until a group of screaming females moved in behind me right
before (what else?) Cherry Lips. In Audition I had to manually decrease their 13 atrocities by 6db and
I also slightly lifted the highs with the E.Q. and did a hard limit to -.1db with a 4db boost. Let's stay
hopeful that Garbage will be back in action once the music industry sorts out a way to keep artists paid
what they rightfully earn. Cover art is included in the torrent and I hope you all enjoy the show.
