Garbage - 2025 10 23 @ The Mountain Winery, Saratoga CA

Note: 
This may be the last Garbage North American tour. Shirley explained they will still play shows, and make music but the business has changed so much that tours have become unfeasible. With that, the band put on a hell of a good show. Shirley introduced "The Trick is to Keep Breathing" in honor of the great Clem Burke, Garbage played with Blondie at the Mountain Winery in 2016. Shirley went into the office during "Hammering My Head" and band intros after "Stupid Girl."
