TheLibr8r's Notes:
Okay folks, this is probably my favorite Garbage bootleg.
First of all, the quality is so good that you'll swear it's
an official release. This is the infamous performance where
Duke's amplifier catches fire mid-set during "Shut Your Mouth".
It's really not a big deal and is pretty funny but you can tell
Shirley is incredibly nervous and quite upset. After the techs
got a back up rigged, the band continue for one last song.
Tracks 12-15 are an FM broadcast from the Chicago radio station
WXRT at the Virgin Recordstore on a previous date. Covers are
included in the torrent. This was a pain in the ass to FLAC so I
hope you enjoy it:)
