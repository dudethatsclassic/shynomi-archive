Notes:

Second night in a row catching Garbage on their headlining tour. The setlist
was exactly the same, so nothing different. However, between songs, Shirley
was way more talkative and animated in Columbus. They were both Promowest
venues and both sounded awesome, so when it comes down to preference, I would
pick this one based on her overall attitude. The crowd here was also bigger
and more into the performance. Listen to the sing along on "Only Happy", wow!
Garbage have stated (and do again during this show) that this will be their
final headlining tour of the States, I just hope they continue to do package
tours and one offs where I can catch them in the years down the line. They
still sound incredible live. So happy to have seen them two nights in a row.

Starcrawler opened. [not recorded, they had extremely poor sound].
