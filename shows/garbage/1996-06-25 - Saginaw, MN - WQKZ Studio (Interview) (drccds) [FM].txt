Garbage
WQKZ Studio
Saginaw, Mn.
25 June 1996

I was lucky enough to have a friend working at this station at the time Garbage was opening for Smashing Pumpkins when they started their tour in Saginaw. 
He told me to come to the station and meet them, etc. The first track is a phone interview Shirley did with the morning show DJ's from her hotel. She was not at the radio station for the interview with Butch on Steve.
The main part is the on-air interview Butch Vig and Steve Marker did at the radio station. The last track is all the calls they took while doing the interview, whether they made it to air or not. 
They took calls from fans during songs and commercial breaks and broadcast the ones they liked, but I have all of them here. I was able to record it directly from their board, altho onto cassette. 
I transferred it to cassette myself from a Nakamichi MR-2 to a Tascam CDRW-700 standalone CD recorder. Cassette > CD > EAC > FLAC 6. All commericals and songs have been taken out. 
It runs about 42 minutes with the edits. No artwork.
