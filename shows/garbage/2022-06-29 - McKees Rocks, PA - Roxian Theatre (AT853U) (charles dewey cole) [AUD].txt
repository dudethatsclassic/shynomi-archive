In November 2021, Garbage announced plans to tour this summer with Tears For Fears.
While this was great news, it was one more year without any headlining shows. I really
loved their "No Gods No Masters" record last year, so I was eagerly anticipating a 
headlining tour in 2022. Well, in the spring they announced 7 special headlining shows
on some days off of the TFF tour. The closest to me was in McKees Rocks, Pennsylvania!
(wait, where?) Turns out, its essentially Pittsburgh. I just didnt know that during
the initial announcement. Once I figured out this was a 3 hour do-able drive on a 
work night (ouch!), I decided to take the plunge and buy a ticket. The only other
headline dates were on the east coast. To say I was excited was an understatement.
Well, the show dates arrived and the first 3 headliners went off without a hitch in
Richmond, VA, this show in McKees Rocks, PA and the following show in Wilkes Barre,
PA. Unfortunately, at this point, someone in the touring camp (or band - it was never 
announced) caught covid. They ended up cancelling the remaining 4 headliners and
resumed the TFF tour in Canada a week or two later. So, this means that Garbage only
performed 3 special headline dates all year and this was one of them. Were the others
taped? No idea. I wasnt real happy with the sound in the Roxian Theatre. I felt like
it wasnt loud enough. Either that or the crowd was just too loud overpowering the band
at many times throughout the set. When I got home and checked the wave, it was very
clear that the crowd noise/applause was LOUDER than the PA was. So, I had some work
in front of me. Not only did I have to reduce the crowd noise manually every time they
spiked higher than the music, but I had to remove unwanted screams, claps and whistles
as the crowd was very rambunctious around me. Seriously, I cannot recall a more excited
crowd in some time. Turns out, this was also their first visit back to the Pittsburgh
area in 20 years. Shirley was very engaging throughout the entire show and even acted
as our "safety officer" providing hearing protection for some little ones who were with 
their parents and stopping "OHWIR" for a girl who had fainted in the crowd. They then
restarted it from the beginning and made sure to tell us to sing louder. It was
without question the loudest singalong of the evening! What a way to go out!!! Lets
hope Garbage add more headline dates in 2023 and beyond. While they do make a great
opener for larger bands, they truly shine on their own. As you will hear, their fans
could not get enough. It took me 2 months to finish working on this one. I wanted to
make it as perfect as I could given the sound issues. "Godhead" was a favorite of the
night. Be sure you turn this one up!
