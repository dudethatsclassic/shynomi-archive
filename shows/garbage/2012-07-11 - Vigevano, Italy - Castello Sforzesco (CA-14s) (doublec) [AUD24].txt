Tape story:
The venue is an amazing historic castle (Shirley had very good words for it).
I have located myself in front of the right stack, about 25 m from the stage.
Few people were around me and also very quiet.
The sound is very very good. Imho one of the best recordings I have ever done.

What about the show? 
It was my first Garbage concert and I enjoyed a lot even if I had to fight with dozens of mosquitoes from nearby ricefields.
Shirley was surprised that a girl in the first row awaited 15 years to come to see them... I bought the Subhuman CD single in 1995...
The gig was part of the third edition of a ten-day summer festival (10 Giorni Suonati) held at the Castello di Vigevano
A summer storm hit the area few hours before the concert with no effect on the show and the crowd. 
"Beloved Freak" is just an improvisation because was not rehearsed for the tour (and remains the first and unique rendition of it).
"#1 Crush" contains a snippet of Madonna's Erotica at the beginning
Shirley Manson was as usual very talkative and more-or-less long talks appear at the end of tracks 05/08/09/12/15/17/19
