Notes:
Many factors explain why this recording came out the way it did.
-I arrived late. Being a 4 hour drive for me, I wasn't stressing the
clock as I was under the impression Blondie was the opener.
Am I really such a fool for thinking Garbage is more popular than
Blondie??? It still confuses me to this day.
-I had to set up in a rush in a bathroom stall and my input level
must have been set to 25 or more when I hit record.
-I was only 6 rows from the front and everyone was sitting quietly
in their seats as security hovered around like bees. There was no
safe way of adjusting my levels without catching my neighbor's or
security's attention.(You never know who might freak out or snitch)
When I got the file opened on my pc, I realized just how bad it
turned out. The low frequencies were clipping so I just used the
EQ to roll off everything below 350hz. I left everything else alone.
Cover art is included in the torrent. Please check the sample first.
Please release any Garbage gigs you may be sitting on, as I have.
