Here's something you don't see very often.  The group based out of Minneapolis, Golden Smog.

By 1997 the band consisted of Johnson, Murphy, Louris, and Perlman, 

along with two new members: Wilco frontman Jeff Tweedy and Jody Stephens drummer of Big Star.

In 1998 Golden Smog released their second full-length studio album 'Weird Tales'.

By mid 1999, the band took a hiatus and only performed random gigs in their Minnepolis area.

I believe this is the lineup for this radio appearance.

Kraig Johnson - (Run Westy Run) Guitarist, singer
Dan Murphy - (Soul Asylum) Guitarist, singer
Gary Louris (The Jayhawks) Guitarist, singer
Jeff Tweedy - (Wilco) Guitarist, singer
Marc Perlman (The Jayhawks) Bass
Jody Stephens - (Big Star) Drummer

Emmylou Harris walks into the studio near the very end and the band speak with her very briefly.
