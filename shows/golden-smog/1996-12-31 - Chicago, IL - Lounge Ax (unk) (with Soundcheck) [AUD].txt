Unsure of the title to "Winter Song." I used the free web version of Moises (nice tool!) to transcribe the lyrics and could not come with any hits on them in Google. But it's not "Winter" by the Rolling Stones or "Long Cold Winter" by Cinderella; I suspect it's a Golden Smog original.

The last track of the soundcheck section is with an audience. I suspect it's the final song of the encore. The show through "Guitar Man" runs just under two hours, so if the show was recorded to a 60meter/120minute DAT, it seems very likely that the taper would have popped out the nearly full DAT and finished off with the DAT they'd used for the soundcheck.
