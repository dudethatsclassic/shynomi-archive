NOTES

Fun set from the first real Golden Smog tour.  Jessy Greene from the Geraldine Fibbers (who opened the show) guests on four songs.

There were brief right channel dropouts on the audience recording in several songs. During those passages I copied the left channel
onto the right channel before mixing with the board tape. Sound overall is very good.
