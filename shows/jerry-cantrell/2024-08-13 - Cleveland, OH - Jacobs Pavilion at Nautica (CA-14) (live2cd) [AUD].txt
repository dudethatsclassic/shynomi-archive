Notes:

First off, Jerry had the poorest sound of all 3 bands I caught this night.
It was muddy, distant and lower than most outdoor concerts I have attended.
Because it was so low and because the majority of the crowd only knew the
AIC cuts, there were tons of talkers during his solo selections. This is
still very listenable, I just wish I got a better pull. I was 35 ft from
the left hanging stack, there is no reason why people around me should be
able to carry on a conversation without raising their voice! Ugh! Anyway,
Jerry was fabulous and I thoroughly enjoyed getting to hear his solo works,
especially the new track "Vilified", the 1998 single "Cut You In" and
"Atone". The AIC tracks were amazing, too (of course)!

Greg Puciato is featured on lead vocals throughout the set.

Tim Montana, Candlebox and Bush were all on the bill. [all recorded but Tim].
