Notes:

It was fantastic to experience live music once more, after almost 3 years since my last show.
After I passed the security check, the guard glanced at my Zoom and mistook it for an e-cig.
He told me not to smoke it inside or something hahaha.
Before the show began I had a brief chat with the guitarist and founding member of Subterranean Masquerade.
It's an Israeli oriental/prog metal band that I really like and taped a few times some years ago.
I reminded him about an email I've sent requesting to do a soundboard recording.
Of course he didn't remember any of it, but told me to contact him about it before their next show.
Jerry's new album is truly great, in my opinion, so it was wonderful to hear some of it live, as well as many AIC songs.
This lineup deserves the credit for quite a performance.
The recording is very good from the beginning to the end, despite the venue's bad sound, the people moving around me and my own (stupid) actions.
I tried to stand like a statue most of the time and as close as possible to the speakers in front of me.
Check the samples and decide for yourselves.
Enjoy!
