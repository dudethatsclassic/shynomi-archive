NOTES:

After baking in the sun for a 5 hour block at the main (Orion) stage, it was finally time to pack things up and head across the venue over to the smaller (though now larger) Stardust stage. I had met a cool crew at the soundboard during my time at Orion, and as it turns out, they all were here to see Slowdive, so I said well let's head out, and two of them looked at me kinda funny and asked why, and I said Slowdive was playing on the other stage, which was a complete shock for them, because they were about to get comfortable thinking Slowdive was up next, so I said noooo, we gotta leave and head to the other stage for Slowdive into Rilo Kiley. I'm glad I had told them I was heading out, because if I had just vanished silently, they may have stayed at the stage hoping to see Slowdive but never getting to. It pays and helps to sometimes talk to your neighbors and help them out if possible.

Anyway, so off we went, with me leading the way through the crowd and over to Stardust. It wasn't very crowded when we got there (as expected), so we set up shop about 20 or so feet from the front of the stage and ended up with an absolutely fantastic/perfect (imho) view of the stage/band. And I didn't have one talker around me the whole time, what a miracle! The band, who I hadn't seen since FYF in 2017 (and did not record that night) was on point and sounded like a dream, it was unreal. EASILY the best set of the day, without question, nothing else even came close. I only wish they would have had another hour to play because I never wanted that set (or night in general) to end.
