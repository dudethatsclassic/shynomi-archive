Slowdive - 2023 10 16 @ The Warfield, San Francisco CA

Note:
Second night at the Warfield. This was scheduled after the first show sold out. They had wanted to play two consecutive nights, but Canabal Corpse had already booked the Friday slot, so the band returned after playing in LA, for the last stop of the 2023 tour. We were treated to different opening act, Sun Colony from LA, a few different songs and one extra songs. The other treat was that the volume was turned up to 11, when it was turned up to 10 on the previous SF date.  You could see from the band's faces how happy they were to be playing - it seems that they had a really good US tour. Looking forward to seeing them again, hopefully without a 5 year gap.
