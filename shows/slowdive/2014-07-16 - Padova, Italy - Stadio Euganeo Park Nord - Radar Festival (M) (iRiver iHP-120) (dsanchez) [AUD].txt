Notes
-------------------
This was the fourth time I saw Slowdive during their 2014 comeback, and I can only say it was perfect -again. The sound was really, really good and "40 Days" and "Golden Hair" (again!) were the highlights of the night for me. I also have no words to thank enough Rachel for dedicating me "She Calls" and I only can say that I will never ever forget that moment. 

About the recording 
-------------------
I was in the first row, left side of the stage.
