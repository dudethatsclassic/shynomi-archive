concert-info:
it must have been one of those hundreds of Slowdive-cover-bands this evening. This band looked like Slowdive, they played many many Slowdive-songs,
let me say: all the songs they played were Slowdive-songs. The recording comes out really fine, but the sound in the hall was so crappy that
f.e. a friend of mine left after 30 minutes. And it all depends on the sound when this band plays live. I really enjoyed the first three ones 
of them I grabbed earlier this year, but this one - nothing left. It was like not having been there. What a big crap!!!
Even last years' one at the Troxy/London seemed to be better which is not true as the sound at the Troxy was much crappier than it was here.
Anyway: I really love this band, but not at this evening.
