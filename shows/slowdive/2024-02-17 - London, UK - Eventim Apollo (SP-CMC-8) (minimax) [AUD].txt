concert-info:
one more from Slowdive, a day after my Brighton-show:
what to say about this one? Slowdive on the peak of their career?!! 
Do I really have to say that they blew me away, again.
My faves were (again): star roving, catch the breeze, souvlaki, golden hair - and the winner was again: when the sun hits.
So much must have happened between their last tour - then the pandemic came - and now their recent one: they now fill all these huge halls as five years before they played
mostly in small clubs or much smaller venues. Now I am probably the oldest visitor as most of the audience are about 20 years old or so. Strange!!
Anyway: a fantastic concert.
