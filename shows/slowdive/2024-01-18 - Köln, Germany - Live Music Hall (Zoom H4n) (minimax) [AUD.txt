concert info:
after last years' concert at The Troxy in London I was a bit sceptical about this one. The Troxy was just a big crap due to the awful sound
which made all the magic go.
Here the setting was much better - means: no big bass-speakers on the ground in front of the stage. So the sound was amazing: loud, very loud and clear.
All the songs the band played came out fantastic, let me say: the hearts were filled with warmness as a big contrast to the cold cold snow outside!
My faves were: star roving, catch the breeze, souvlaki, golden hair - and the winner was: when the sun hits (why do I always have to cry a bit on their concerts??)
Thhe only thing I have to critisize is the encore-set which I don't like that much.
Anyway: a fantastic concert.
