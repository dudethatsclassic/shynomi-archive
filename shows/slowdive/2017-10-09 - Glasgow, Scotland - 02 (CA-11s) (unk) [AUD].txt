I have no idea why I hadn’t heard anything by them before this year considering I was a Cocteau Twins and My Bloody Valentine fan back in the day. Thanks to Spotify, they appeared on a suggested playlist earlier this year and I’ve been hooked ever since. As a result I’ve been looking forward to this gig for a while. The band didn’t disappoint, a strong setlist, with selections from all of their albums. If I’m being critical (when am I not?), I think the show was maybe a song or two too longOverall I’m happy with the recording but would have preferred to have been a little closer to the PA. --K
