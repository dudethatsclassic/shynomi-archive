This was a great show and I'm really glad I was able to record it. I had a great spot near the back, raised up above most of the crowd and close to the center. The sound in the venue was superb and it comes through very clearly on the recording. I did next to nothing to process the show; I didn't even need to compress or otherwise reduce clapping or anything. There's hardly any audible audience chatter, too.

I wrote a review (including a picture) available here:
http://patricksmusic.blogspot.com/2017/10/slowdive-isan-live-20171003.html

Enjoy!
