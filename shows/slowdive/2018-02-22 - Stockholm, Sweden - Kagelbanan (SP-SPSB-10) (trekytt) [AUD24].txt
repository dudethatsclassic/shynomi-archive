****NOTES/EXPLANATION :*****


Excellent by "Slowdive". First time I had to leave a concert before it was
finished, because I´m not kidding you, as hot as in a oven. I thought something
was wrong with my eyesight, turned out I started to see "2 bands on stage", 
which ofcourse was not the case. Probably reasons - concert was sold-out and
packed to last centimeter of the venue, and perhaps the air-system wasn´t 
circulating as much as perhaps would have made it easier for our taper
this concert. 

I tried to move to another spot yet there I saw "2 bands on stage"
once more and realized I had to get out of the venue he-he. Never left a concert
before, and yeah stoked because the sound was really good in the venue and what
I managed to record sounds really great. Not much audience-interfering and crisp
and punchy sound. Too bad, yet s**t happens.

Yeah, I know it sounds amusing yet stoked that I had to leave after half the concert.
I didn´t count on it being so hot. If I would have known, would have smuggled
5 liters of water with me. When I finally got home, had to drink 2 liters of
water to get the balance right(!) At least I didn´t see "2 wifes" when I arrived
at home, that´s the good end of the story  :-)

Who knows, it could have been another reason to why I had to leave earlier. 
My wife is pregnant with our first one, so within 1-2 months we shall expect
our newborn. I always has my phone on the loudest volume and vibrating, in
case I recieve a urgent "It´s time to leave for the hospital"-call from the wife.

If you listen carefully after the first song, even the band realizes that it´s
very hot in the venue, quote:

- "It´s warmer in here than it was in Australia". 
(**They just finished their Australian tour a couple of days before)

Anyhow friends, recording turned out well, if yet incomplete!
Lesson learned to always bring some extra water with you and something
to chew on. 

This was probably my last recording for quite a while, as I
don´t want to risk too much and being far away from my wife as our little
one can decide that it is time to bless the world with it´s birth!
Hopefully I can raise our newborn to become a taper, and that we will
see a father/son-recording-team in the future! That will lead to a
3rd generation of tapers within the family, as sometimes I lend my
taping-rig for my father (Who has taped 3 concerts too!)
For sure a new era will start for us soon, as we will become 3!

Be blessed and please send some blessings for our little one!
