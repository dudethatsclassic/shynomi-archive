Review:
Slowdive started their touring cycle for 2023's Everything Is Alive with an Australian tour back
in July of that year, and they've finished it with another run here in May 2025 - and in the
meantime, they've jumped up to another level of rock and roll stardom. In Perth and Sydney, they
added second dates to keep up with audience demand, while in Brisbane and Melbourne they doubled
the size of their venues; in this case, moving from the ~2,000-capacity Forum to the
~5,000-capacity Festival Hall (or "Festering Hole", as some unkind patrons refer to it). It was an
all ages show, too, but thankfully I was in the 18+ general admission standing (which didn't help
the tenor of the crowd, really; at the start of 'Shanty' you can hear someone in the crowd tossing
out a racist slur. Apologies), ready for my second round with the band.

Apart from the crowd being pretty rowdy between songs, I'm happy with how well this recording came
out - credit to the sound guys, who must have been pretty locked in for a big boxy venue like this.
The setlist was pretty similar to when I caught them last time around, although I found it a little
harder to connect at this show - I'm not in love with their newest stuff the way I am with the old
material, so when they're skipping 'Avalyn' to play a bunch of new songs I'll be a little bit
bothered. 'Machine Gun' was a lovely addition, though, as part of the "hits" section later in the
main set, and they closed out with a brilliant 'Golden Hair' again. Obviously it's not included
on this audio recording, but the projected video on the backdrop was hypnotic as well.
