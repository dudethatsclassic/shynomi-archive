Notes: 
This was not taped by me, so not much to say on that end. I was blessed to trade with DAT tapers in the 90's (I was analog), and they were kind enough to rewind a bit for each side so this should be a seamless production. This is a true DAT > 1G > you.  Soundgarden was in rare form in '96, methinks Chris was drinking and saying some funny stuff.  See if you agree. My understanding is that this tape has been sought out by Soundgarden collectors for quite some time...I guess the download count will reflect that.

Huge kudos go out to TallTaper for capturing the proceedings, and the inimitable Mr. Orr for his uploading and mastering skills. I did EQ this when it was transferred back in '15, but I have no idea what the settings were. So that should generate a nice round of upraised middle fingers in my direction.
