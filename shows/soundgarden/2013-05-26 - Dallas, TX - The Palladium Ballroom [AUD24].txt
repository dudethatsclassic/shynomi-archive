Notes:

Took a last minute trip from my hometown of Philadelphia, PA to catch the Austin and Dallas shows over Memorial Day weekend. Solid show. Not a legendary one like Austin the previous night, but it had its moments. Happy to hear "Tighter and Tighter," "Fresh Tendrils" and "Kickstand" for the first time. "Beyond the Wheel" made a roaring return. Raymond Cameron did a nice job on "Eyelid's Mouth." Disappointed in the one-encore ending of "Slaves and Bulldozers," but it still clocked in at over 2 hours total. When they came out from the encore break and led with that, I knew that was it. Oh yeah--no "Spoonman" this night. Pretty rare not to get that one, no?

Place was packed again like Austin, but not nearly as rowdy. The dope smoke was far more intense than the other 4 shows I saw on this tour, so I guess that explains why. On my little excursion from Philly to catch this and Austin, I'd say Austin was the entree and this was the dessert.

Recording came out pretty well especially considering that I was farther back than I would have liked. Decent amount of punch to it.

I will not be posting a 16 bit version. Any of you are free to create one and do whatever you'd like with this recording since I can't stop you anyway. No need to ask permission.
