Following my upload of the Sydney show from the week before, here's the only other known 1997 live Soundgarden recording which is also the last circulating live recording of them before their 2010 reunion.  There is apparently a recording of the final show in Honolulu a week after this but I've never seen a copy.

Sound is pretty similar to the Sydney show although maybe a little less distant.

Enjoy!
