Wow, this is a special one.  This is my first DAT master (although not my first master of any kind).  Soundgarden was the first band I ever saw live, back in '94, and although I didn't think anything could top the level they were at in 1994, they were still pretty great in '96 - and this is a massive, epic, 100+ minute show.

The quality of this recording is pretty mediocre. It's distant, it's boomy, and the "collar method" is not a good one, even if you're tall. It has all the things I hate about stealth tapes - phasing here and there, boomy, etc. But hey, cut me some slack - I was pretty inexperienced back then.

Huge thanks to Mike Ziegler for his hard work over the past year transferring many of my old masters.  My intention is to seed them all here as time goes on.  If you happen to remember an old one of mine that you really want, feel free to PM me.  Anyway, especially after his awesome recording of the reunion Soundgarden show, this one goes out to Mike.
