Soundgarden
1991-10-06 
Hollywood, CA
High Voltage, Rip Magazine Awards

A weird source, unfortunate that it has an odd flaw in that tracks 7 & 8 are inexplicably different than the rest; flimsy, weak, mono. A real shame as the first 6 songs are capped in a full-bodied blaze of stereo. Hard to say if it's the out-of-phase of a squished stereo source or if it's just one sided-mono, but it really kills the excellent mood that this had going. I did my best to bring it closer to the rest, and it works, but still unfortunate.

Anyway, short rockin' set from the early Soundgarden years.
