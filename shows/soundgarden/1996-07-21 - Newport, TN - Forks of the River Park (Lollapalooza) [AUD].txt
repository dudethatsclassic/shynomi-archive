Taper Notes:
The Newport, TN Lollapalooza show in '96 was a really good outing for the hard rock version of Lollapalooza. I got to see Soundgarden, The Ramones, Metallica, Rancid, and Rage Against the Machine, to name a few of the main stage acts. I also caught part of The Melvins show at a side stage.

It was a hot middle of the summer TN day. Not enviable by any means. It meant shirt off after about the first hour. For a pale skinnned, bald white boy, that wasn't the smartest thing to do. I got there around 10 AM and left around 11 PM after the Metallica set ended. It was the worst sunburn I've ever had.

This was my first time seeing Soundgarden and when they opened up with Spoonman you could feel the sonic onslaught vibrating deep in your chest. These guys were there to bring the goods, and that they did. Though it wasn't a headlining show for them, they played as if they owned the place.

You see recordings of this show every once in awhile, but for the most part they are wrongly credited to being in Knoxville. It wasn't in Knoxville, but 50 miles east, in Newport.

Earlehead
