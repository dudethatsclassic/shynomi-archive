Matrix notes:
This is a mix of source 1 and 2 above.
Its a rough one, sound quality varies in each source.
Source 2 begins near the start of Room A Thousand Years Wide.  
Beyond the Wheel source 1 is cut halfway, patched with source 2 to complete the track.
Jesus Christ Pose is all source 2, missing entirely from source 1.
No noise reduction or compression was used.
Source 1 remaster consisted of speed corrections and eq.
Tape flip between Drawing Flies and Incessant Mace.

This was carefully pieced together to make the full show, 
a great performance and not widely circulated.

Gun has additional guitar from guest Pepper Keenan from C.O.C. 

Cover art included.

Thanks to gornandez for sharing the tape, 
and thanks to Mr. Cassette for the solid transfer.

And thanks to the tapers! 

enjoy!
