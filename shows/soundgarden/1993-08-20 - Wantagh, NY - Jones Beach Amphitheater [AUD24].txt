SOUNDGARDEN
JONES BEACH AMPHITHEATER, WANTAGH, NY (USA)
AUGUST 20, 1993
taper: SMORES
transfer: KRW&CO
tracking: SMORES

TRANSFERRED USING A NAKAMICHI DR-3 CASSETTE DECK INTO A CREATIVE SOUNDBLASTER SOUND CARD > Adobe Audition 5.5 (level boost/hiss reduction) > CD Waveditor (tracking) > flac8
Olympus ME1S mic > Aiwa J505 cassette recorder > Maxell XLII 90

setlist:
1. -intro-
2. Gun
3. Spoonman
4. Kickstand
5. Outshined
6. Let Me Drown
7. My Wave
8. Rusty Cage
9. Fell On Black Days
10. Jesus Christ Pose

notes from Smores: I remember rain being predicted this evening and rather than chance ruining my relatively new DAT machine, I decided to tape with my analog recorder. As I listened back to this show you can hear the rain just pouring down especially during Let Me Drown and My Wave. By the time Soundgarden had finished the rain had finished and I don't remember any during Neil Young's set. I know there are alternate source recordings of the Neil Young set, but I am unaware of any alternate Soundgarden tape. If anyone has an upgrade of this show, please post. This was the first performance I saw of Superunknown songs.

bill: Neil Young/Soundgarden/Blind Melon
