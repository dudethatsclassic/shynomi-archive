Remaster Notes:

I was at this show, my 2nd and last time to see them.  Also brought my then-13yr old daughter, it was her first big concert.  
This show means a lot to me.  I am forever in Lou's debt for taping it.  A nice recording, now sounds even better.  

Original Notes:

Took a last minute trip from my hometown of Philadelphia, PA to catch the Austin and Dallas shows over Memorial Day weekend. I go to a lot of concerts, SG and otherwise. For my favorite bands I'll travel--I've already made long car rides to Atlantic City, West Long Branch and Wallingford to see SG this month. Sometimes I wonder if the stress and money spent to go to all these shows is worth it.

This show was worth every penny. Absolutely nuts.

Started with a line that went straight back a quarter mile just to get into the venue. It was moving but I never in my life saw a line that went that deep just to get in. Place was absolutely packed and by far, the rowdiest of the 7 SG shows I've seen between 2011 and 2013. I have no doubt the band fed off that energy. I almost wonder if they were too rowdy as it seem like Rusty Cage was not the planned end to the show. Usually they do four encores and I have to wonder if they made the decision to shut it down before someone got hurt. One guy was body surfing--first time I've seen that post-reunion--another guy randomly offered me a joint, and a another threw a beer from off the balcony at the end (which hit me).

Anyway, even though the end was abrupt that was a special setlist, for die hards only. I got 6 songs I hadn't heard this year, three I hadn't heard period (Incessant Mace, Never the Machine Forever and New Damage), and of course those last two are rarely played. I also heard them sound checking Tighter and Tighter before the show started, which would in fact be played the next night in Dallas. With the deep cuts, and the sound quality (great, clear, punchy acoustics) I was smiling all night. 

Unfortunately, as good as it sounded in the venue, I don't think the recording sounds that great. It's too distant and boomy, in my opinion, for where I was standing, which was maybe 10 rows back from the stage. Who knows why. Not a difficult listen by any means, but I was hoping for better. 

I will not be posting a 16 bit version. Any of you are free to create one and do whatever you'd like with this recording since I can't stop you anyway. No need to ask permission.

Hello to anyone I happened to talk to in line or inside the venue.
