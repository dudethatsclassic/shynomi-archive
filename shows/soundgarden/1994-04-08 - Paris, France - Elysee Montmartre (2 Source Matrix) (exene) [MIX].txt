Matrix notes:
Lineage: flac > Audacity (mixing, rendering, speed correction, level adjustments) > wav (16/44) > flac
Tracks 01 and 20 are source 2 only: track 01 is only present on source 2, and track 20 is just crowd noise.
At first I thought these two sources were from the same DAT, but close listening
reveals these are different sources. The audience noise and applause gives it away.
This one took forever, as the speed fluctuated widely between the sources.  
Worked on it during 2020 pandemic lockdown, finished it up in 2021.
After this concert, the band first heard the news about Kurt Cobain's suicide.
Rolling Stone mentions the show in their article:
https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/soundgarden-into-the-unknown-96743/
