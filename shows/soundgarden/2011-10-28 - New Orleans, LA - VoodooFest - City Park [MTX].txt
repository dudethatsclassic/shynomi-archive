Soundgarden
Friday, Oct 28, 2011
VoodooFest, City Park
New Orleans, LA


*********************
2 Source Mix
*********************


Source 1: DPA 4061 (matched pair) > 9v batt box > Edirol R-09 (WAV @ 24 bit, 48 kHz)

Source 2: Church Audio AT831 > 9v batt box > Edirol R-09 (WAV @ 24 bit, 48 kHz)

Location: Front of stacks, stage left, 30 yards back

Lineage: SD card(s) > card reader (USB) > WAV > FLAC

Mastering: Wavlab 3.04 (EQ, dynamic compression, matrixing, dithering and resampling to 16 bit, 44.1 kHz) > CD Wave Editor (track splits) > FLACfrontend


Recorded, mastered and matrixed by Adam


Note:  Final matrix is approx. a 60/40 mix of the two sources, in favor of the DPA source (with some variations mixed in throughout.)

There is some slight wind noise at times, and some slight phasing at other times, as I often found myself trying to move out from behind taller folks in front of me, or away from people who were more inclined to have an in-depth conversation than to actually pay attention to the show.  

It's not a perfect recording, by far, but I'm pretty happy with the results, considering I've been waiting 15 years to see and tape these guys.
