Soundgarden - November 21, 1996 - Electric Factory, Philadelphia, PA

Re-seeding a classic show that I've loved since I received it from the taper back in late 1996. This show has a few rarer performances in it, and was one of my favorite shows to listen to back in the day.  Still love the performance now.  Mastering was tricky, it's a tape that sounds decent, but very hard to polish a good sound from.  I tried 10 years ago, and put this aside as a project to get back to. Unfortunately it had to be following Chris Cornell's passing.  I believe the remaster does the show justice, but I also included the raw tape transfer because it is so heavily remastered.  RIP Chris Cornell
