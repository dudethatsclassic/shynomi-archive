Notes: Thanks to gornandez for the fresh rip from the original CDs. This is, in my opinion, the best show and best bootleg of the Superunknown tour. The band is very energetic in spite of extremely hot conditions inside the Armory. The OJ Simpson car chase played out during this show, leading to numerous references to the incident throughout the show. "Hand of God" is played for the first time since 1989. Natasha from Eleven joins the band for "Fresh Tendrils". An amazing show.
