Soundgarden
Fox Theatre
Detroit, MI, USA

Chris Cornell's final concert.  

recorded by gornandez
location: halfway up balcony
lineage: audience master > flac (16/44) > Audacity (track splits, EQ, levels balanced, edits) > wav > flac
remastered by bluejane99

Notes:
Well... here it is.  Final performance of Chris Cornell.  An intense show.  
I got misty-eyed more than once while working on this.  
His voice on Slaves and Bulldozers, my God.

Edited out the recorder drop in Spoonman best I could.  
The eq was minimal, -2db of low end.  

Huge thanks to gornandez for sharing this important recording.

RIP Chris Cornell
1964-2017
