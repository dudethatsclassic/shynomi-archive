Notes:
Soundgarden's 2nd ever gig in Ireland, the first since 1995. 
I was standing to the left (Kim's side) approx 10 people back from the stage,
so I was perhaps a little too close, as you can hear from Matt's kickdrum which was very loud.
the Edirol tends to be quite boomy which is why on stood on Kim's side and not Ben's!

Limo Wreck was played for the first time since 1994 and the band seemed really into it, Matt
singing along to himself. Like Suicide was also a bit of a rarity, on this European tour at least.

The sound was a little off for the first few songs but gradually improved throughout the night.
Pretty decent sound overall on this recording, if you don't mind a very evident kickdrum!
