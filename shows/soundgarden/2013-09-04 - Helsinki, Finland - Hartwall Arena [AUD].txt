	Show notes:
First time seeing them live and despite the absolutely mindblowingly bad sound at the venue (reflected on the recording as well, sorry about that), really enjoyed this 2 hour long set. The show was on Kim's birthday as well, and a member of the audience was called up on stage to sing the finnish version of Happy Birthday to him. Opening act was Graveyard from Sweden.

	Recording notes:
Recorded approximately 2 meters in front of the soundboard, center of the venue.

	Mastering details:
Equalizing used to reduce the icepick cymbals quite drastically, as well as some of the excess boominess of the lowest bass frequencies. Still not 100% happy with the sound of the recording, but it is what it is. Outside that just bringing up the volume. 

- Borlag on 2015/07/03
