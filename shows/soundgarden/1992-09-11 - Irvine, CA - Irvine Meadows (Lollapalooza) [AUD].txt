Notes: An extremely tight, extremely intense show-one of the best Lollapalooza shows. Chris' vocals are phenomenal here, particularly on an unhinged, unbelievable "Beyond The Wheel". Guests abound; Eddie Vedder joins the band to do backing vocals on "Outshined", like he did on 7/22/92; Somebody from Ministry joins the band to do horns on "Drawing Flies" and "Room A Thousand Years Wide"; and two guys from Body Count join the band for "Cop Killer". The recording is really nice, too, though there is a bit of static at times and Chris sounds slightly muffled at times. Still, if you like Soundgarden at all, grab this one! I guarantee you will absolutely LOVE IT.
