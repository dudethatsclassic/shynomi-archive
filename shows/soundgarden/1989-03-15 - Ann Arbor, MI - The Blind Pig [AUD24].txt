“Signed to A&M Records with Louder Than Love in the can to be released later in the fall of that year, Soundgarden played a very intimate show at Ann Arbor’s legendary club the Blind Pig. My friend who I went to the show with recorded it on a portable cassette recorder. Though not packed, the audience was pretty energized with some serious moshing going on at the front of the stage. This was the first of several times seeing Soundgarden and they blew me away with their musicianship and just shear HEAVINESS. The setlist was heavy on their Sub Pop material and the most recent Ultramega OK record on the SST label. A couple songs from the yet-to-be-released Louder Than Love were presented along with a great cover of Sly and the Family Stone. The recording quality is OK, certainly listenable in my opinion.The performance more than makes up for the recording’s deficiencies, though. No EQ-ing or other sound processing used. If anyone would like to try and improve the sound quality and do a mixed version, be my guest.”
