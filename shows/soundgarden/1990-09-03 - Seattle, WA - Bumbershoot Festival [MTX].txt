Notes from aud #2:

For many years, the annual Bumbershoot Festival in Seattle provided a host 
of taping opportunities for JEMS. Given its search-free, choose-your-own-seat 
approach, Bumbershoot became a testing ground gear we could normally deploy. 
Hence the use of Nak 300 shotguns for this Soundgarden show which we also 
shot on video. Our seats at the rail gave us mounting capabilities and clear 
sight lines.

A couple of curious things about the show, the first being that Soundgarden 
was actually pinch-hitting for the Psychedelic Furs who were scheduled to 
headline. Second being that the opening act were local power-pop purveyors 
the Posies, which seemed odder at the time than it does now. The PA cut out 
several times during the Posies' set but I think it held solid for Soundgarden.

The show includes a cover of Spinal Tap's "Big Bottom" as the set closer. When
we get around to posting the video, you'll see why it was such a highlight.

Samples provided. Hope you enjoy it. And if you are interested in the video, 
let us know, it just might motivate us to post it.

Butterking for JEMS
