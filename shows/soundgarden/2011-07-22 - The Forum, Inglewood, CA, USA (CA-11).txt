Notes:

I'm continuing my Spring Cleaning and finally mixing and releasing some old shows. Have tons to go!

This was my 3rd time seeing this band live and first time since 1996. Really happy to see these guys back together and what a phenomenal setlist they played. Lots of old shit in there! This was an awesome reunion tour for sure. 

I have been messing around with this mix for a few years and finally settled on the one presented here. I think its a happy medium in the end. It may come off a little hot in areas, however I'm really happy with the end result. Hope you guys enjoy it too!

Cheers!
