2020 transfer notes: I've been wanting to get this retransferred for at least 10 years, so there's no time like a pandemic to make it happen! I took the raw files and EQd them, and then normalized them to -0.5. I think the quality is a little better, and it's nice to remove the CDR step in the lineage. Crank this one up loud! Thanks to Rob for the transfer! 

Original notes: The recording is incomplete, missing at least the first two songs. First show of the European leg of the Down On The Upside tour. Best setlist ever! They serve out many songs that were seldom performed on the Down On The Upside tour such as "Never Named", "Head Down", "Waiting For The Sun", "Mailman", "Drawing Flies", and 
"Drop out Boogie" (one of only two known performances), as well as "Full On Kevin's Mom", performed for the first time since 1990. A fantastic performance, amazing setlist, and excellent sound make this one of the best Soundgarden bootlegs in existance.
