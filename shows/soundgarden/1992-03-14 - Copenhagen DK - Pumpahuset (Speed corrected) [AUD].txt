Notes:
To my knowledge, this is the first speed-corrected version of this show to be shared.
The tape speed fluctuates throughout, it tripled during Flower.  That's all fixed now.
No noise reduction was used, some tape hiss is still present.  
The performance is awesome.  The sound is not great but its a big improvement.
Hopefully a lower gen copy will surface someday.
