The first show of Soundgarden on german ground since 16 years! Of coure mainly the same setlist as on their other european appearances, with two numbers dropped due to the
curfew time of 10. pm for the Zitadelle. But an high energy show from both the band and the crazed up very enthusiastic crowd, who cheered every song like an good old buddy. 
Hope this will be not only a short lived reunion, so I can probably catch them again...as it's benn just about excactly 20 years ago ago since my last SG show at Pinkpop Festival
in the Netherlands...Enjoy! SB.
