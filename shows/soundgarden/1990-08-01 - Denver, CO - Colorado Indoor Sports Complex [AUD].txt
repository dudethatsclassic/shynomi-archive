Remaster notes:

Awesome performance, Chris' voice is insane.  
But the audio had thin and low levels,
lots of crowd noise and many cuts/drops throughout, 
especially in Flower and Ugly Truth.
Sounds like the taper is getting clobbered in the pit, 
but managed a great recording under the circumstances.  :)
I cleanded this one up best I could.
Fixed the numerous drops and cuts.
Edited some crowd noise.
Re-did the track splits.
There is noticeable hiss remaining, as I don't use NR.
As with most audience recording remasters,
its not perfect but its an improvement.

"You seem like a happy crowd"
- CC

Hilarious crowd talking @2:40 during Ugly Truth.
