Notes:

Performance was very good and Chris' voice sounded great. I thought the setlist was a mild disappointment compared to the sets they were doing this past winter--none of the old songs were anything they weren't doing in 2011.

This was the first show I've recorded with my new SP-SPSB-11 12 volt battery box. The wiring for the left channel on the SPSB-8 9 volt box that I had for close to six years gave out during the Steven Wilson show I recorded on April 19th. I decided to go with a battery box that had a detachable cable, so in case this happens again, I'll only need to replace the cable. 

This is an OK recording but I was disappointed with how it came out. I can't help but feel paranoid that somehow the new battery box is to blame. I know there are a lot of variables that go into this but if anyone can confirm or deny that this battery box would affect the quality of the recording please let me know. I think this could sound very good with some post-production editing however as it's a clear take, but a little distant sounding, given how close I was to the speaker stack.

Speaking of that there will not be a 16 bit version. Any of you are free to create one and do whatever you'd like with this recording since I can't stop you anyway. No need to ask permission.
