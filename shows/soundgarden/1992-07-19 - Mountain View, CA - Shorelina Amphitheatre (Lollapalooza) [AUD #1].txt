Soundgarden
July 19, 1992
Shoreline Amphitheater (Lollapalooza)
Mountain View, CA, USA

*remastered by exene*
AUD #1: analog audience audio [Sony 909]

Setlist:
01 Jesus Christ Pose
02 Face Pollution
03 Outshined
04 I Can't Give You Anything (The Ramones)
05 Ugly Truth
06 Rusty Cage
07 Slaves And Bulldozers
08 Searching With My Good Eye Closed

Notes: Only known performance of Ramones cover "I Can't Give You Anything".

Remaster:  The original ran a little fast, and needed bass.
This one is speed corrected, EQ'd, normalized.
Thanks to milocampo on archive.org for sharing this,
and gornandez for sending it my way.

Note from mikocampo:
"I got the cassette in a trade through the mail years ago because I was at that show. It didn't have a generation listed on it, so I don't know the generation of the cassette. I digitized it by plugging in my digital recorder to a tape recorder and recording it as a wav file then using audacity to separate the songs into tracks and then converting to flac with traders little helper. I have recorded many concerts, but this is not one of them.  glad you are enjoying it."
