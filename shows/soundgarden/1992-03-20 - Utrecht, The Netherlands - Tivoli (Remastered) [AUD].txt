Soundgarden
March 20, 1992
Tivoli
Utrecht, The Netherlands

**Remastered by exene**

Another great audience tape of an awesome 1992 headlining show in Europe.  
The sound quality was nice, but with the 2nd gen it could use improvement.
Even better would be if the master tape surfaced.  Until then, this will have to do.
It ran a little fast, common with copied analog tapes.
Slowed down -1.0%, eq'd, fixed several drops, channels balanced and boosted in Audacity.

For the new lineage, see original source lineage below and add Audacity > wav > flac 

Thanks to Johan W. (hooligan70) and Phil H. (phil183) for sharing!
