show notes:

this is an unknown DAT>unknown mics (most likely Core Sounds or Sonic Studios) 1g (XLII) I obtained in a trade with Brian D. got a good chunk of Soundgarden from him, nearly all of it spun from DAT clones to 1g analog.

this tape had a layer of fluttering hiss at around -38 to -40db that I found annoying, so I gave Audacity's "noise reduction" a spin, and I think it turned out pretty fantastic. 

normally, I don't mess with noise reduction, but when I do, it's always at -10db, vs. after the boost.  this seems to work much better than applying it when the levels are near 0.

the other fortunate thing is that there was about 10 seconds of 'dead air' at the beginning of the analog (most of my tapes spun from DAT have this, I assume from the slower startup time for the heads of a DAT vs. analog)

with that dead air (minus the click at the beginning when the safety tape ends), I was able to isolate ***just the hiss***, sample that, and apply it to the entire file.

and by gum if there was no flutter at -60db pre-boost...yet I think the music retained its 'shape', if you will.

as always, there are samples to see if you agree or disagree.
