We've been asked about this video many times and I'm happy to finally get it out there for those who care.

JEMS' audio recording of this legendary Soundgarden show has been around for many years and was torrented in 2011 from our master DAT which is still on the tracker:

http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=361293

A matrix of the JEMS master and another audience recording is also available here:

http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=452927

I guess that's my contrast clause for this master video recording of the show.

JEMS was set up on a railing, about halfway back, stage right, with tripod, to capture the show, and the recording is extremely steady with few moments away from the action. There is some zooming and the image can be dark at times, but otherwise it looks pretty good for a 1990 audience shot. 

The reason we hadn't torrented the video until now was we were waiting (even seeking volunteers) to sync better audio to the video. What had escaped me until tonight was that this was one of the shows where we actually patched our Nakamichi 300 shotguns into the camera, so it already features the same audio as our DAT recording. Silly me.

This show was authored for DVD by our pal KS and he did his usual stellar job with the menus and such. Thanks K. In fact, his mastering of the audio, I would argue, is better than our audio-only torrent. The only rub is that our camera battery died after a lot of use that day, so we only got the first 45 minutes of set. But what's here will hopefully satisfy those of you who have been asking for this. Screenshots provided. Trust me on the audio.

BK for JEMS
