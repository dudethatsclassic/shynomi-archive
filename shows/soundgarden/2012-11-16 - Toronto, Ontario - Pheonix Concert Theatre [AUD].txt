notes
Thanks to Rivals for recording and giving it to me to work on. The main source is incomplete as the batteries died during Rowing at around 3.20. I have patched in the remaining 20 odd minutes of the show with the SP-CMC-4>SP-SPSB-1>Edirol R-09 source. The CMC-2 source was recorded from directly behind the soundboard, while the CMC-4 source about 25 feet from the stage.
There is a huge amount of chatter on the main source that at times drowns out the music playing on stage, kind of surprising to me as I would have thought people would have shut the fuck up and enjoyed Soundgarden in a small venue. The patched in source had alot of clapping which I have tried to clean up as best I can, and is probably a little chrispier than the main recording, but figure I'd share this anyway, never know someone may find it handy to have.
