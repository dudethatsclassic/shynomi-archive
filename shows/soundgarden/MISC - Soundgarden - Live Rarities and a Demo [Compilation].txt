Firstly, here is the first (or at the very worst, second) known recording of Soundgarden.  Note that the drummer here is Chris Cornell.  Sound quality is pretty alright. Could be much much worse. I took out some of the hiss, as this particular recording is from a 7" vinyl boot, "Incescent Mace/Loco Mosquito" (yes, the boot has 'Incessant' spelled wrong).  Cool little piece of history here.

Another noteable song here is the live medley "Working Man/Communication Breakdown/I'm Eighteen" from the Bumpershoot Festival in Seattle, Sept. 5, 1988.  Significance of this particular recording is that it has been remastered so that it can actually be easily listened to. 

"Say Goodnight" is a "lost" Soundgarden song, along with "Christi" - they were never released anywhere and were only ever performed live once. 

In "Cop Killer", Chris rants about the police and about a bunch of stuff that had been happening blah blah, you probably know the story. But he gets pretty political here, which is interesting. Thats really the only reason I added it. The sound quality is sub-par.

The other live recordings are some hard to find live covers.
