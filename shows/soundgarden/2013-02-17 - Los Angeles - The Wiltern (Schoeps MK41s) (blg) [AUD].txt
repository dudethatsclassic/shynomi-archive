Notes: Another great show with Blind Dogs being played live for the first time.  I went to this show solo and didn't have my wife with me to hold a spot on the floor.  This meant that I didn't quite get the location I wanted, but all things considered, I think the recording came out ok.

After night 2, I went back to using the "Output Transformer" output on the Tinybox and I think it improved the overall sound a bit.  I also went with the mid gain setting which may have helped as well.

For the entire 3 night run, there has been issues with the PA at the Wiltern not being able to handle the bass that SG puts out.  Any distortion occurring on any of the recordings originates from this.

Resampled/dithered to 16/44 via Adobe Audition.
