We walked in as the Interrupters walked on stage, so I was too late to record them. I really enjoyed their set, though. I got my gear ready to record the Hives, only to find out after their set that I had not clicked "record". Enjoyed that show as well... So on to Green Day.

We were seated in section 122, in line with the right speaker stack. The recording is just OK, no singers/screamers/clappers nearby, but the sound was not too good where we were and there was a fair bit of boomy echo in the hall. They put on a good show with lots of energy, which sort of wore off toward the end. I would have cut about half an hour and reversed the order the "American Idiot" album, so they would end with the title track and go out with a bang. Oh, well... Opinions are like assholes: everyone's got one.
