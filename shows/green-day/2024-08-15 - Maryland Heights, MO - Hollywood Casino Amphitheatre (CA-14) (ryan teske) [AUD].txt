Notes:

^ truncated and sped up with incoming storm to get people out of the venue.

I received the raw wave file from Ryan and EQ'ed it a tad to bring down the
bass, hard limited the pyro "booms" that were going off throughout the show,
normalized, tracked and encoded to the FLAC format. It's a much nicer listen
now where you can crank it and not worry about scaring your dogs or cats when
the booms hit!! Seriously, they were loud!! Huge thanks to Ryan for taping,
he upgraded day of from the lawn to the pavilion to avoid the rain and in
turn got a much better recording! I really like the way this one turned out.

"Bobby Sox" was on the setlist before "Good Riddance (Time of Your Life)" and
was cut due to weather.
