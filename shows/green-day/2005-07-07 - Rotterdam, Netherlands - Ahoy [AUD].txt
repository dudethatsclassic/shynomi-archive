Well, this is my second attempt to record a show. It's something completely different from my earlier seed (Anathema).
This is not exactly my kind of music, I was there with my daughter, but what a show it was. Amazing. So much interaction with the audience. Fireworks, fires, great show, if anyone has the chance to see them, go for it.

In general the sound quality in Ahoy is not the best you can get, and I did not have the best position, but I think it's all right. I had a seat at the right side, somewhere in the middle.

A small part is missing because I had to change the disc. It was at a part of the show when Billy Joe Armstrong invited three people from the audiende to play a song.

The girl who was invited to play the guitar, was allowed to keep that guitar. When Billy Joe tells her so, that's the part that's missing. 
The bass-player had to do a stagedive.

Of course there's audience noise, but I think it sounds quite ok.
Enjoy.

Corry
