Green Day 
Saturday July 25,2009
Times Union Center
Albany,New York

Audience Recording
Taped from section 118(right side of stage.)
Source:H2 Zoom(low gain,internal mic,recording level at 50%)>SD card>computer>Flac>CD Wave to split files>Cool Edit Pro 2.0 to normalize>YOU
Taped by Harry
Pictures by Rosario

This is my first attempt at recording.Thankfully security was cool.My wife,son,girlfriend and I drove from Syracuse to Philly to see them on the 21st.
Then drove from Syracuse to Hartford on the 24th.Then from Hartford to Albany on the 25th.We were like Deadheads only with soap :)
Green Day consistently puts on the most enjoyable fun, rockin,crowd participating,comical show around.If you're not exhausted and sweaty when the show 
ends you must be dead.
   Recording Notes; Not much I can say.Jason's guitar is low in the mix.Overall I give it a solid B+ Maybe somebody can do some magic with it.I'm extremely happy 
with the way it came out though.
