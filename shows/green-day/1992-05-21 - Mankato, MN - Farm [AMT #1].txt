"The show in question here took place in St Peter. It started out in Dan Fisher's North Mankato backyard with "My Friend Stu" and "Loin Groove" opening. By the time Loin Groove finished, the cops had shut down the show. So in true punk rock spirit, the entire show relocated to a house on 5th street (i think) in Mankato. All of the audience traveled there in a caravan of cars. Before Green Day even started, the show was shut down yet again by Mankato cops. So the crowd made a caravan one last time to a farm in St Peter. (I don't recall whose farm it was) Green Day set up and played on top of a giant wire spool. That is until the St Peter cops came and unplugged them. Good times."
