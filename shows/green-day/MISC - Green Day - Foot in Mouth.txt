Green Day
Foot In Mouth

Foot In Mouth, also known as Explosion Live! + 5 in Japan, is a live EP by Green Day. It was released only in Japan on April 25, 1997 through Reprise Records. The songs were recorded on various dates from the tours for Dookie and Insomniac.
