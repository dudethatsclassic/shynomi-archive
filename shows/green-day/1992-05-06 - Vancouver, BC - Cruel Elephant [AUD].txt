Here is the third Boss Pop Green Day recording.It seems a bit harsh and overdriven, possibly reflecting the state of the PA system at the club. This gig was held at the "new" Cruel Elephant, a club in the Gastown district of Vancouver with two levels (the band played upstairs). The improvements - more space, no "mystery meal" - did not entirely overcome brutal sightlines. Still, at the time it was a much appreciated venue for independent bands of an alternate bent.
