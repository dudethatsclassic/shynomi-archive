notes from the taper: My daughter asked me to take her to see Green Day.  Not my preferred type of music, but I recorded it since I was there and for the gig to live on. Hopefully someone in the DIME community is a fan and wants this. I've meant to upload this for a while but time is scarce and I was about to shelve this deep in my hard drive. Most likely lose it and forget about it forever. I read on one of SMORES' uploads that he was willing to help anyone in my situation with no time to bring this to the community. Best to share the gig than to let it die alone in a hard drive and get lost in time. 
The concert was full. The Toyota Center is gigantic and getting a perfect recording is about as impossible as it gets but it's decent.

Cool Stuff: 
1) The lead singer started insulting President Trump towards the end of the concert so there are a few boos here and there (Texas is a red state) and people insulting Green Day back.
2) The concert was very long so you get your moneys worth. 2 1/2 hours. Y&T and Udo Dirkschneider are known for long gigs, but Green Day beats them hands-down.
