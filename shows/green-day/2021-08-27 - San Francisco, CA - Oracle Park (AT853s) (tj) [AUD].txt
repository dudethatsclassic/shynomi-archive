My SO bought us tickets to the Hella Mega tour with pretty high up seats so I brought my equipment and rolled the whole night.
I had a lot working against me: bad seats, chatty / drunk neighbors, strong wind, shit AQI, 16 dollar Hazy Little Things, and pretty bad phasing / volume shifts, but I still ran the recorder anyway.
Haven't seen much really pop up on Dime from this tour so thought someone would appreciate it.
It reflects what I heard, so at least that's accurate.
I aimed the mics at the right repeater since that was the closest speaker to me.
I also taped Weezer, Fall Out Boy, and the Interrupters, although the Interrupters I only captured with onboard mics.
The wind dropped for this set so out of everything I taped this sounds the best by a long shot.
There is a CCM4 source of this show - please get in touch if you are the taper!

In terms of the actual performance Green Day was great.
Been hearing their music for years so it was nice to finally see them play!
Always fun when a band plays to the hometown crowd.
Billie Joe's mom was in the crowd for this show.
