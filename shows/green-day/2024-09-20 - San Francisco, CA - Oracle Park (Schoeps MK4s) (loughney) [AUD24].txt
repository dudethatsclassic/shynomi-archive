Green Day - 2024 09 20 @ Oracle Park, San Francisco CA

Note:
Hometown show for Green Day on the 30th Anniversary of Dookie and the 20th Anniversary of American Idiot (which was released on September 21, 2004). Lots of shout outs to different parts of the Bay Area, lots of rants about the Oakland A's manager moving the team to Las Vegas. The band seemed really happy to be home, and really put in a killer set. Lots of (literal) fireworks that went off at various times. Lots of silly things like a Bad Year blimp flying above the audience, people in animal costumes and the Linda Lindas coming on stage and blowing bubbles and taking Billy Joe away in a wheel chair. Oh, yeah - I took a bunch of pictures of the various shenanigans - mostly of the screens since we weren't close enough to the stage, which are included here.
