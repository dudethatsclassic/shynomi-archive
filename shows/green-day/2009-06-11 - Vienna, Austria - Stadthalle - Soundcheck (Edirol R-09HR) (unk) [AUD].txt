GREEN DAY
SOUNDCHECK
Vienna (Austria), Stadthalle
6th November 2009

Lineage:
Edirol R-09HR (internal Mics @ 44,1/24) - Kingston SDHC 8GB Class 6 Card - USB 2.0 - PC (Ubuntu) - Audacity (Normalization)

Setlist:
01 Mass Hysteria / Modern World
02 King For A Day
03 21st Century Breakdown

Notes:
I found this unreleased Recording on my Computer. Via a lottery from a local Radio Station I got the Chance to visit Green Days Soundcheck in the Afternoon before their show in the Stadthalle a few hours later.
At the beginning we were standing in the Entrance area because local Security don't wanted us to be inside. After the first song Billy Joe said we should come closer. After that we were directly in front of stage.
