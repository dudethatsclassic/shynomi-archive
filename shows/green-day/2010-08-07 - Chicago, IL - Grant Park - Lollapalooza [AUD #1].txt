Remaster Notes:

This was a pretty damn good show from what i've heard and this recording is no less. I actually think it sounds better than shows recorded with good external mics! There were a few things that needed to be fixed though. I took the bass down a good amount, left it just a tad higher than normal professional recordings. I brought the rest of the band closer and put the vocals just a bit closer than the rest. Bass and vocals were centered in the mix a bit and the drums and guitar were widened. EQ'd, lightly compressed, excited and topped off with a small pinch of reverb. It's not without it's faults though, the enhancements to the rest of the instruments help bring out the tinny, PA-esque sound of the vocals. This was originally masked pretty good by the really loud bass, but now that its not as loud and there were boosts to the highs and mids, it really brought out that quality in the vocals. I tried to reduce it as much as I could without affecting the rest of the mix but it's still somewhat noticeable. It's not bad though, I just thought I should mention it. Put on your iPod, or burn to CDs, play it in your car, play it at home, turn it up loud!
