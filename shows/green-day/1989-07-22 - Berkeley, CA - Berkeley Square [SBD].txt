Notes:
Early Green Day show that I obtained as a cassette trade sometime in the mid 1990's. This is one of the earliest circulating recordings of the band, shortly after the release of the 1000 Hours EP.
The band opened for Killdozer at this show. The set consists mostly of songs from their first two EP's and the debut 39/Smooth LP. At one point, one of the band members jokes that the audience consists
mostly of their families. There are a few songs on here that I think are unreleased.

I got the tape from a West Coast trader who got a copy directly from the sound man. I digitized it at some point (don't remember the equipment, unfortunately.) 
I may have traded it with a few people back then. A lossy version of this show was posted on Dime several years ago, 
and immediately banned. The show is sometimes misidentified as a radio broadcast; they did an interview on KALX on the
same day, which I'll post separately, but this is a soundboard recording. 

There were a lot of recordings from the late '80's/early '90's East Bay punk scene in circulation in the cassette trading days, but they seem to 
have disappeared. In particular there was a guy named Jason Duncan (I think) who advertised in Maximumrocknroll who had tons of shows
recorded at 924 Gilman Street. I've posted what I have here, but if anyone has any of those tapes, please dig them up and get them out there. I'm particularly interested in 1991-1992 Green Day shows, 
anything by Operation Ivy (or related bands), or Jawbreaker.
