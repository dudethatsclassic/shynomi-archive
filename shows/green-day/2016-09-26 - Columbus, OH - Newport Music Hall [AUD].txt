Green Day's first date supporting the forthcoming "Revolution Radio" LP due out
October 7th, 2016. They announced 11 warm up club dates in advance of their
world tour early next year. The shows sold out in minutes, some even seconds...

Personally, Ive never been to a club show quite like this one. The crowd sang
along to nearly every word of every song and were (at times) louder than the PA.
You will notice this right from the first couple of songs, thankfully they turned
the PA up shortly thereafter. These guys could have easily sold out one of the
2 arenas down the street (or in any city), but opted for the Newport. We got lucky,
and it wont happen again, thats for sure. If you were there, crank it up. If you
weren't, grab your best pair of headphones, crank it even louder, close your eyes
and picture yourself in the middle of the complete and utter madness you will hear
from the 1,700 screaming fans in attendance.

"at least we're not home watching the fucking bullshit debates..." - BJA
