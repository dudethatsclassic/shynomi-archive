Here's Green Day early in their career performing for a not always friendly Vancouver audience. 

The Cruel Elephant existed in two separate locations over its few years of being an "alternative music" venue in Vancouver. This set was recorded at the first location, up on Granville Street - a narrow room with a central dais overlooking a sunken stage at the back - and memorable because its liquor license required food sales, and so the cover at the door also gave you a ticket for a plate of rice or a "mystery meal" which would be delivered from the adjoining restaurant. Eventually the management found a better location in Gastown. Still, the first Cruel Elephant hosted a few memorable gigs.
