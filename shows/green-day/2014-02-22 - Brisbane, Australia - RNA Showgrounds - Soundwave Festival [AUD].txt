22 February 2014
Soundwave Festival
RNA Showgrounds
Brisbane, QLD
Australia

Green Days first show on their Australian tour for Soundwave Festival. This leg concludes the '99 Revolutions' world tour.

Recording Equipment: Sony HDR-MV1
Lineage: Raw .MP4 file (48,000 Hz PCM)> Sony Movie Studio Platinum 13.0 > .WAV (48,000 Hz PCM) > FLAC (48,000 Hz FLAC)

Comments: Recorded from within the "D Barrier" toward the right side of the stage. Therefore the mix is interesting in that you can hear the band in the right speaker and the audience on the left. Actually sounds pretty cool.
Unfortunately the quality is severely degraded by the large amount of wind noise throughout the recording.
