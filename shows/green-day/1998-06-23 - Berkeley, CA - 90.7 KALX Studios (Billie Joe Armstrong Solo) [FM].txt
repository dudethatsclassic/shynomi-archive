A couple of years ago, some idiots on the Green Day Community message board tried to argue with me that this didn't exist. Here's proof that it does. 

On August 21, 1999, I set up a tape recorder to record Bracket live in the KALX (UC Berkeley radio) studio, then went out for the evening. When I got home and listened to the tape, I discovered that this had been broadcast at the end of the show. Believe me, if I'd had any idea that it was on, I'd have found a way to tape the whole thing (I don't know how much longer it went on for; if a complete recording exists outside the KALX archives, I'm unaware of it).

The song "Life During Wartime" was released on a KALX benefit comp called "Bored, Lonely, and a Little Pissed Off" in 1999/2000; for that reason, it's not included in this torrent.
