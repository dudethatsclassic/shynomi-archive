This is a really good audience tape, with a good solid presence all round - everything on stage can be heard clearly, with a decent sound mix

as well, & the audience isn't really intrusive even around the taper.  The 'Move Festival' itself only lasted a few years, but it did produce

some excellent shows in it's time...  


Taken from a no label silver I acquired maybe 10-12 years ago, the original cd was cut into 22 tracks, with reprises of tracks 5, 16 & 18 - 

in other words, they'd been split where the 'audience participation' bit begins - hence Audacity in the lineage to put them back together, &

there's no EAC log simply because on it's first pass EAC managed to rip 21 tracks before Track 22 caused it to 'freeze', requiring a program

close prior to saving the log - & Track 22 required one more pass before ripping successfully...


Ffp & md5 included...
