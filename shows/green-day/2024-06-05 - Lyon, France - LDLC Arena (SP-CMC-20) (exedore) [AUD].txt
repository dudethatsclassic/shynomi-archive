Notes:
First time doing a big show with the Olympus and handling the EQ in Neutron. Overall, the EQ helps a lot as the original sounded fairly hollow which, from the front of the dead-on seating area, makes a bit of sense. A solid show, no surprises in the setlist, but some glitches including BJA's mic dying mid-song. Minimal banter and endless EYYYYYY-OOOOOOOOs (though I lost count of "let's get crazy" calls) - all rock, little talk. And Tré is still best boy.

If there's proper demand for the non-EQ'ed version or for the Interruptors' opening set, they can be posted as well.
