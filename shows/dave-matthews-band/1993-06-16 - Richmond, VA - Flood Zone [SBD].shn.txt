UPDATE:

*	Mark Lynn notified us that he taped this show at the Floodzone and
	that the encore was missing from most DATs that spread around.
	He had actually put the encore for this show on another tape he
	made back then (5/19/93).  I have added Cry Freedom from the 5/19/93
	SHNs to this show and updated the MD5 files.
*	Here are Mark's notes:

"There was actually an encore performed on this night I just recorded it
on the end of my 5/19/93 master. So Cry Freedom is missing from this as
it circulates. Take the Cry Freedom from 5/19/93 and put it at the end
of this one and you have the complete show(See notes on 5/19/93.) I
think this is one of the best sounding boards from The Floodzone. Man
did they have the mix dialed in on this night!"
