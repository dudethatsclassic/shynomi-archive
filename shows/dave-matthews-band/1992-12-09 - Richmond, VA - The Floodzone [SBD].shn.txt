Notes:
----------
*	The original CD source was DAT > CD by Jeff Travitz and he sent the
	audio discs to Allan to convert to SHN.  The original DAT came from
	Mark Lynn.  Somehow, PFWYG and LIOG were not included on the audio
	CDs Jeff sent to Allan.  For years, this show has spread without
	these two songs.  I took a DAT I had of this show that included
	PFWYG and LIOG and joined this splice together with the rest of
	the original second set.  So, the SHNs from the first set are the
	same as the old set, but the whole second set is new (due to do the
	shift I had to apply to fix SBE issues).
*	My DAT conversion was done direct to SHN as follows:

	Conversion:	Tascam da20 > M-audio Firewire Audiophile spdif in
			Sound Forge 7.0 (record/resample) > CDWav > MKWact
			Louie Rendek [ljr7@yahoo.com]
