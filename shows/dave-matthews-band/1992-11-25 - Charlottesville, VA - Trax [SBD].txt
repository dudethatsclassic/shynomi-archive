* = Dave & Carter only

There is a LOT of tape hiss on this one and some analog warble at the begining 
of LLD and Jimi Thing.

Dave mentions the upcoming NYE show in at the Omni Hotel in Richmond.

Before SMTS Peter dedicates the song to a friend in the crowd. Yes, Peter 
actually speaks. That's even more rare than hearing Tim Reynolds speak at a D&T 
show.

People People is cut off.

The very end of Billies is missing. OSW is completely missing. 



These rare older shows have been collected and converted through a collaborative 
effort to get them into circulation. Sometimes these shows do not have the best 
sound quality (and maybe that's why they aren't circulated much to begin with), 
but people should have access to them.

UPDATE:  Received the wav files from Adam to review. First off, Lover Lay Down starts abruptly and may or may not be the opening track. Dmbalmanac.com lists Spotlight as the opener, however it cannot be determined if either track is the opener nor can True Reflections be deemed the closer.  Using Soundforge, I normalized the right channel on all tracks of disc 1 to -8db due to clipping,did the same with the left channel only on disc 2. Applied 10 band Graphic EQ to 0db on all tracks to as well to enhance this source a bit. Using the Paragraphic EQ,cut the hiss on all tracks above 8kHz by 6db. The hiss is reduced but the tone had to be preserved so I couldn't do a radical hiss removal. It is clear that this came from multi-generation cassette recording as the hiss and tape warbling is heard throughout the show. Retracked all wav files and created new flac files and fingerprints via Flac Frontend.
