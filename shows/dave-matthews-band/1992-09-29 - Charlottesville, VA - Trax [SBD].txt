NOTES:
----------
Entire show with Miguel Valdez on percussion
Stefan did not play during this show either except for the last 3 songs with a *.

Carter hurt his hand so Miguel filled in for this show and the Floodzone show the next night. These were special acoustic shows.

This is not a great recording and it has a lot of flaws, but it's a sweet show that was difficult to find. 

This recording has a lot of hiss. 

Dave mentions a show in Washington D.C. the past weekend (09/26/92 - possibly at the Bayou although the exact venue is unknown)

There is no segue between Minarets and Blue Water.

DIDO has a 1 sec cut near the end of the song, then the end of the song is missing. 

Most of the Watchtower is missing.

Two Step is cut off.

These rare older shows have been collected and converted through a collaborative effort to get them into circulation. Sometimes these shows do not have the best sound quality (and maybe that's why they aren't circulated much to begin with), but people should have access to them. 

Thanks to Dan Pyrik for the CD source used for the conversion.
