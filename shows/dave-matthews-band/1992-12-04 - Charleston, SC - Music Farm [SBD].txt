UPDATE by Henry Hart on 10.12.06:
There is definitely more to this show than what is in this recording.  Halloween was probably played next, but no complete copies of this show are currently known to exist.

Recently, One Sweet World, and Rhyme & Reason were originally in this conversion, but they were clearly not played during this show, so I removed them and the tail end of Pay For What You Get's track. I also lowered the right channel volume 3dB, and retracked.ed the right channel volume 3dB, and retracked.
