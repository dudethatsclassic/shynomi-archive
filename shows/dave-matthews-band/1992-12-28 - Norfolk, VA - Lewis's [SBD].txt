NOTES:
----------
* = Dave & Carter only


This recording has some flaws and a lot of tape hiss (see UPDATE notes
below), but it's a sweet show with a really long set.

There is some feedback during the begining of Minarets.

The very end of The Maker is cut.

Set II has less hiss, but also higher levels. The levels are actually too high for set 2, so there is some clipping, especially during Halloween. 

Dave mentions that Metallica is pretty lighthearted compared to DMB when they are in their Heavy Metal mode because they have Carter Beauford.

The Grace is Gone tease is just Dave (chords only). (see UPDATE notes
below on what this tease may actually be)

SMTS has a slight skip in it at 01:43.

Warehouse is aborted. Dave mentions it was a false start...."the condom broke".

These rare older shows have been collected and converted through a collaborative effort to get them into circulation. Sometimes these shows do not have the best sound quality (and maybe that's why they aren't circulated much to begin with), but people should have access to them. 

Thanks to Dan Pyrik for the CD source used for the conversion. 

Compiled By Adam Nelson on November 9, 2006

UPDATE by Henry Hart on 11.25.06:
I cut everything above 10kHz in Set 1 to remove some of the hiss.
No music was present in this high frequency range. I left Set 2 as is.

There is DMB lore of a Grace Is Gone tease before So Much To Say.
I think this is actually Dave teasing Norwegian Wood... it actually
sounds like he is just figuring the song out. The two interpolations
just before and after this tease supports my theory.
