^ = John Prine Cover
* = Dave solo

This is a really sweet show, as are all of the other July 1992 shows in circulation. I am not sure why this one was not converted until now because it's a treat to hear.

Dave introduces Satellite as a song by Elvis.

Boyd says "I love Dave Matthews passionately and I don't give a fuck who knows."

Dave's sister Jane is at the show. 

Dave mentions the 07/31/92 Trax show (a rare Friday evening show) and how he wants 1000 people to show up for it.

After almost every song, there is a cut in the crowd noise. 

Seek Up is cut off at the very end, but seems to be only a partial
performance and not the entire song played. 

These rare older shows have been collected and converted through a collaborative effort to get them into circulation. Sometimes these shows do not have the best sound quality (and maybe that's why they aren't circulated much to begin with), but people should have access to them. 

Thanks to Dan Pyrik for the CD source used for the conversion.
