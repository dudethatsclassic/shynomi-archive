NOTES:
----------
The date of this show is not known exactly. It is often traded as 07/15/92 Trax, but that is certainly wrong given the presence of Granny & Halloween in the setlist. An approximate time period for this is late November or early December just based on the setlist, but it is most likely either December 1 or December 8 at Trax. 

Dave mentions Tuesday night, so it's almost certainly a Trax show. Plus he says "Merry Christmas". It seems unlikely he would say that if it was still November (like 11/24 - around Thanksgiving), and there are only two Trax shows in December that are not already recorded (12/01 and 12/08). It may be more likely Dave would say Merry Christmas if it was later in December, possibly around the time many UVA students are finishing up with their finals exams and heading home for Christmas. That is why I picked December 8th as the date but it may never be 100% confirmed.

Me & Julio is cut at the start of the song and also at the end of the song.

There is some noticible tape hiss on this recording but it's not overpowering.



These rare older shows have been collected and converted through a collaborative effort to get them into circulation. Sometimes these shows do not have the best sound quality (and maybe that's why they aren't circulated much to begin with), but people should have access to them.
