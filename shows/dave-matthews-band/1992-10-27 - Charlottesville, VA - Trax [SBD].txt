* = Dave & Carter only

This is a cool show. It has some flaws, but some unique oddities that make it worth a listen.

Blue Water fades in at the start. This is probably not the start of the show.

Dave mentions the upcoming Halloween show and promises 5 new songs.

Dave thanks Gwen Stefani and No Doubt twice. They must have opened for DMB at this show.

This is a sweet Watchtower. Dave is very talkative and mentions his time playing the song while he was in Amsterdam. He beings with improvised lyrics of "Since I got a haircut I don't smoke too much weed, but every now and then I put on a wig and blow my mind away".

WWYS is played very fast..."California style".

Dave asks the audience what they want for songs during the encore.

There is a lot of hiss in this recording. Plus on disc two the levels are really high.

There is a skip in WWYS.

LIOG has an 11 sec drop out.

The begining of Jimi Thing is missing at the start of Disc 2.
