Dave Matthews Band pulls no punches in this 2007 performance. "A Dream So Real" opens the amazing setlist followed by a mix of classics and rarities unique to 2007, including "Eh Hee" and an extended "If I Had It All". Rashawn Ross on trumpet has by now joined the band as a touring member and adds some extra heat on the "Sweet UP and Down", not played on the DMB stage for a few years.

The rest of the set is all energy, closing out the show with "Louisiana Bayou" and a crushing version of the familiar classic, "Ants Marching". This one of a kind show is a "must have" addition for any fan’s collection.
