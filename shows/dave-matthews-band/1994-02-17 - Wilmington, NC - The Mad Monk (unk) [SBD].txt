This recording is incomplete as most of the second set and possibly some of the first is missing.

This original conversion circulated with the Minarets> Typical, Jimi as the encore, which it obviously wasn't, so it moves those songs to their standard position in the set. There are several cuts between songs which suggests the taper intended to fit as much of the show on a 90-minute cassette as possible and these songs were simply placed out of order for some reason.

This 2025 reconversion also increases the speed/pitch of the tape by almost a half semitone.
