Dave mentions that he has given up smoking. He also says that Stefan is from Switzerland and guesting for Boyd is "Dr. Smith from Lost In Space." Before Dancing Nancies, he introduces the song as "Dancing Katie's", after a friend in the audience. Most of Jimi Thing is missing, with only the remaining minute or so recorded.
The pitch/speed of this song is just a little off, but is close enough
to be left as-is.

These rare older shows have been collected and converted through a collaborative effort to get them into circulation. Sometimes these shows do not have the best sound quality (and maybe that's why they aren't circulated much to begin with), but people should have access to them.

UPDATE by Henry Hart on 10.12.92:
This show was originally sent to me with April 15, 1992 as the date.
I was sure that was not correct. Why would Dave play
Pay For What You Get with such structured lyrics for the first
time in April, then shelve it until September? And Miguel playing
with them for half the set and Dave mentioning how he will play
with them soon, but not again until the end of September... it didn't
add up. Well, the Minarets Digest has a reference to a show on
September 15, 1992 with Miguel as a guest performer and starting with
some Dave solo songs. It is just too likely that this is that show considering these qualities as well as the fact that the day is the 15th for both. If anyone can prove me wrong, fell free to contact me
via dreamingtree.org.
