* = Dave solo

Dave mentions the Fridays After Five show the next day, so this is an unusual Thursday night Trax show. I recieved the source CDs dated 05/12/92, but it should really be 05/14.

Dave also mentions the Saturday 05/16/92 Trax show.

Boyd tells the crowd it has been nine days since he quit smoking.

The entire show may not have been recorded.

These rare older shows have been collected and converted through a collaborative effort to get them into circulation. Sometimes these shows do not have the best sound quality (and maybe that's why they aren't circulated much to begin with), but people should have access to them.
