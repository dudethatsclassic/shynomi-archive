Mammoth on the spring headlining trek for their most recent album, "The End".
Unfortunately, while on tour, Wolf came down with the flu and had to postpone
a couple shows. Since the tour has been back up and running the show has been
shortened a bit to help him get through. Missing from last fall are as follows:
"Same Old Song", "Like A Pastime", "Optimist", "Mammoth" & "All In Good Time".
Wolf has added "Something New" to the spring setlist, so we lost 5, but gained
1! I was still very pleased with the show given his health and thought he still
put on a tremendous set! The April Fools prank at the encore was hilarious and
made all the rock news sites the next day. Funny stuff. The recording went well,
this was the 2nd show where I have used the SP-SAPM-1-USB-C adapter into my 
Galaxy S21 and outside of some very minor hiss during the quiet parts, it did
the job! I still prefer my M10 or A10, but this works in a pinch when venue
security have started asking to remove your belt and look through your things
at the door as soon as the walk thru metal detectors go off.

James and the Cold Gun [not recorded] and 10 Years opened. [recorded].
