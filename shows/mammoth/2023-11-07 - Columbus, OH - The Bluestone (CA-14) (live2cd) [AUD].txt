Notes:

^  Wolf solo acoustic.
#  MWVH fan Trace Tackett brought up on stage to play guitar.

Mammoth WVH's first official headline tour. This was the 3rd gig of the tour
and the setlist featured a great balanced split of 8 from each album. The sound
in The Bluestone is never great, but this is a fun listen. If you go into this
expecting to hear a loud rock band playing in an old church, thats exactly what
it sounds like. I stood in direct line with the right hanging PA and pointed the
mics up from the brim of my hat! This show was sold out and packed in tight! In
fact, Wolf mentioned it was the first sell out of the tour and how proud they
were. I removed some unnecessary claps and screams with RX7 when needed in post.

Turn it up!

Nita Strauss opened. [not recorded].
