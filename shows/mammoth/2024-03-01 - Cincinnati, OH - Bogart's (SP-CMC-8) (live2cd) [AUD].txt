Notes:

^  Wolf solo acoustic.

Mammoth WVH's first official headline tour. The show featured a great balanced
split of 8 from each album. While this turned out pretty good, it sounded very
flat to my ears and thats because I was extremely close to the PA using my
cardioids in a relatively small room (rookie mistake). However, I did not have
my omnis on me - so I had to make due. I used the stereo expander feature in
Adobe Audition (at 200%) to give the recording a little space and breathing room
from the "flat" capture that was bothering me. To my ears, it sounds slightly
better now. I have been tinkering with this for 2 weeks and I think this is
the happiest I'll be with it. I also had to add some bass in EQ as there was not
much in the original recording. I also removed some unnecessary claps and screams
with RX7 when needed in post. This show was sold out and packed in tight!

Nita Strauss opened. [recorded].
