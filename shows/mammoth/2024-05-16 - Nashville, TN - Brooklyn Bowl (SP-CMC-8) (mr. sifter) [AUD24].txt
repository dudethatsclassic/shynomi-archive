Mammoth WVH Brooklyn Bowl Nashville, TN 5/16/2024

Recorded & mastered by Mr. Sifter  Source: Source: SP-CMC-8 pair > battery box > Zoom H1essential > EQ/mastering via Izotope Ozone > 16/48 FLAC

Notes: 
This show was predictably loud, but also a tough show to master, as the kick drum was the loudest thing in the mix from where I was standing, and the drummer has zero dynamics to his playing even before it hits the PA. And it's that clicky, modern-era Lars Ulrich bass drum on top of it, really distracting at the show and even more so on the recording. Was able to move back right in front of the soundboard for the encore, which sounds a bit better for the last two songs, at least. Tried to temper the kick using Ozone, while also boosting the drowned-out vocals and guitars a bit, while also tempering some excess audience noise. (There's one awful, isolated woo girl next to me, I hope she has a woo girl in her own ear at the next gig she goes to)

All that said, a solid recording of a killer show. Wolfie is starting to get better at being a frontman, and the material and guitar playing is just killer. Hopefully he'll do a proper live album at some point, but until then, enjoy this one.    I'm Alright
