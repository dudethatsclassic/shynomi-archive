Comments:

Something REALLY rare here. A full 2 disc set of backstage footage, crew interviews, 
setup, band interviews (dan, adam, jim) and a whole lot more. 

The disc starts with an interview of Graham Colton who talks about touring with the crows, 
and what it means to be a part of a tour with CC, and the influences on his band, 
Graham Colton Band. Then come some interviews with the crew (Cory, Doug & Billy). The
interview with Doug is particularly hilarious and contains HEAVY sarcasm and during which
he calls Dan Vickery a side project.

He talks about the dynamic of a crew that has been together almost 10 years, and what keeps
them all together (booze, money!!). 

Then we move into some soundcheck footage with GCB and band member interviews. 

The interviews with Adam, Jim and Dan have all (in very small parts I believe) been on 
the official from one time to another. This is verified when the taper talks about the 
footage being filmed for cc.com - however as yet it hasn't turned up in its entirety.    

Adam starts off by calling the fans a buch of whiners and telling them to stop complaining.
He talks about being a fan of music in general, balancing life on the road, the evolution 
of rock n roll, supporting community causes, interpreting songs and politics. A very very
interesting sit down interview with Adam.

Dan talks about how great (and long) the tour has been. He goes on to talk about the role 
of Art in society, musical influences, touring and writing, writing his own songs, the 
relationships with other bands. Again, a great interview conducted expertly. 

Jim talks about joining the band, and how he has slotted in with the other members and 
crew. He thanks the fans for their support, talks about playing with other bands, meeting
the fans.

Disc 2 starts with Immy and Bryson playing backgammon and also features some backstage 
footage of GCB performing. There are some more interviews with Dan, and then 3 songs 
being performaed by the crows. 

I found this video very interesting, and as it came to me in 2 discs, I have kept it 
that way. Date and location are mentioned numerous times throughout the footage.    

hope everyone enjoys this rare gem

-unknown, 2008
