06.12.02
London Astoria, England

Taper: Willie Thompson

Recorded Using: Sonicstudios DSM-6SM binaural microphones (hat mounted) > Sony TCD-D100 DAT

Mastered using: Tascam DA20MKII DAT deck > Delta 2496 soundcard (coax) > Cool Edit Pro 1.2 > CD Wave > mkw > CDR(n) > dBpower amp > WAV > FLAC(Level 5)

Recording Position: Recorded from the little podium about 20 feet back on the left hand side, level with the soundboard. I was in the middle of the audience, so be warned that you occasionally hear an annoying little twat who thought we were there to hear him. 

A note about the sound: Generally, it is very good.  The first couple of minutes of this are a bit rough.  The soundman had his levels way too low when the band started.  Occasionally, there is the odd bit of digitial distortion right at the end of a song (i.e the clapping). It only affects a couple of songs, for a second or so at a time, so I just left it in. It's a little bit bassy perhaps, but that's just the way I like it !

Sound Quality A
