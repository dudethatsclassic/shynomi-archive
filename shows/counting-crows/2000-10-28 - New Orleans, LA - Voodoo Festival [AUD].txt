Comments:

Another previously “unreleased” audience recording, this time in New Orleans. Crows had just finished (or were about to finish) touring with Live, and Adam talks about how much he just loves New Orleans. 

The band are in great form, with Immy and Dan trading riffs like they were born to do just that. It is the guitars that really stand out on this show for me. 

Cutis Watson sits in on “Round Here”, “Anna Begins” and “Hanginaround” playing trumpet, and seems really into it. “Anna Begins” has some crazy alt that I had never heard before, and is truly mesmerizing. Adam gives his gf (Courtney Cox) a big kiss at the beginning of Hanginaround. 

The video itself is shot from somewhere left of stage, and considering the obstructions (the proshot rig) is very good overall. There are some out of focus parts, but on the whole, this is a great video of an amazing show. 

This show was also recorded as a Proshot, but as yet, only a couple of tracks (Round here & IWIWAG) have surfaced (on Live Vol 8).
