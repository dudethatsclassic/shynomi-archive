Comments:

Shot during the AAEA tour, this one landed in my letterbox as a VHS. It is shot from above right (mounted) and is positioned pretty much to only see adam and dan for the whole show. There was a lot of “snow” on the tape which I assume has been from degeneration of the vhs. 

The show itself was surprisingly low key. Only points worth mentioning are a stunning AMO1 alt and a really cool PBB alts also. Everything else was pretty much stock standard. 

Another unearthed treasure.
