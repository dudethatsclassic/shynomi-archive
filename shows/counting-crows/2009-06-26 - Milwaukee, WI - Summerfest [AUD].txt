Adam was singing an entirely different song when they began "Richard Manuel".  Could it be a new alt?  Anyway, it didn't seem to jive with the music and he didn't seem to like the way it worked (or didn't work) so they stopped and started over. 

This was a phenomenal set for such a short show (10 songs total).
Unfortunately, I had battery issues from the start (and some amazing luck).
I turned on my recorder and it said the battery power was too low to record.  I tried turning it on and off because I have had issues with this before, but it was not going to work.  Then I looked to my right, on the ground under the empty seat next to me was an Energizer battery.  So I said to myself, might as well try it.  I put it in and the power problem seemed to be resolved.  The show had already begun, but I was happy to be able to record it.  Throughout the show I was checking to be sure it was still recording and I was happy to see the power and record lights on the whole time.  When I got home and put the recording on my MacBook, I found only about five songs.  Here they are.
