This post is a tribute to my friend Johnny Legs who was a major player in the live music recording world since the late 70's and who sadly passed in 2006.  He helped to bring tons of live music to fans and counted among his friends the Counitng Crows, who were huge fans of his, and of the live recordings he got for them.  He began as a guy who got them boots, but soon became their close friend and the day after he died, they spoke about him at this show, in tracks #3 & #11.  He will always be missed, but I will always remember him and the live music he loved so much.

R.I.P. my brother
