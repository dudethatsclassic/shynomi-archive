COUNTING CROWS
April 6, 2008
Apple Store
San Francisco, CA, USA
free in-store performance

I'm going to start by saying this recording is probably just for the collectors and very patient fans. This was a free show for a couple hundred people, and what I expected to be a basic 40-minute set that you usually get at in-stores ended up being nearly two and a half hours of music and Adam Duritz talking about songs or taking questions from the crowd. As you'll see if you look below, the recording is about 45 minutes shorter than the show. I started recording before the band came out and stopped after they left. The problem is that random bits are just sort of missing. There are no blank spots I edited out - some parts just didn't record, and I ended up losing a total of about 45 minutes throughout the show. Oh, and it's mono (don't ask). So, what got recorded sounds decent, though some of it's a little quiet (especially Adam's talking), and it may be less frustrating if you think of this as just a very long sample. There's a sample below, and towards the end is one of the "skips" I mentioned.
