 2014-09-01
Here is the radio interview and performances from Acoustic Cafe in 2014.  The original source is MP3 (lossy) but I used audacity to transfer it to FLAC to prevent further audio degradation. I then cut the unbroken audio into individual tracks and added album art and tags.  I hope you enjoy.


The setlist is 
1. Intro 
2. Interview "Writing for a Play"
3. God of Ocean Tides
4. Interview "The Writing Process"
5. Scarecrow
6. Interview "Inspiration"
7. Richard Manuel
8. Interview "Palisades"
9. Cover up the Sun
10. Interview "Who I Am"
11. John Appleseed's Lament
12. Outro 


*All songs were recorded during soundcheck for their show at the Greek Theater on August 17th of 2014.
