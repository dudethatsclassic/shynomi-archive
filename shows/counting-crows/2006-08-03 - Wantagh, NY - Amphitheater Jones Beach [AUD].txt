Counting Crows

Jones Beach (Wantagh) NY
08.03.2006



This is from the Crows' first of two nights at Jones Beach.  The audio is not very good.  If you are a casual fan looking to grab one or two shows from the New Amsterdam Tour, this is probably not the show for you.  If, however, you are a fan who feels that bad audio is better than no audio, this is an opportunity to grab a poor recording of a good show.  

This is one of two shows I attended and recorded on the New Amsterdam tour. Prior to this, I have never recorded any of the dozens and dozens of concerts I've been to. What a waste, I know. Had I only known what I was missing by not capturing these unique moments in time. Ah well.

Anyway, for these two shows I did not get any special equipment. Doing it more as a lark, I just used the recording function of my MP3 player. As a result, the recordings are really bad.  If I could do it again, I'd get some real equipment and do it right. The shows were excellent, and being able to listen even to the terrible audio and relive those shows makes me yearn to have good copies. Needless to say, I am a complete convert to the incredible value of boots.

My MP3 player records to 16 KHz WAV format.  From there I used Audacity to save each track and Trader's Little Helper to convert to FLAC.
