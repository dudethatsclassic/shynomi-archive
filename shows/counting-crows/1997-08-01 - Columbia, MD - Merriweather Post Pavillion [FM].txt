COMMENTS:
Given that Adam's birthday is the origin of all his music (August, and everything after) I feel like this show is quintessential for any Crows collection. It's in his hometown. It's on his birthday. And it highlights the music that served as the foundation for their success. It just seems 
to sum up the heart of all I love about the band. Two recordings exist of it (one complete, one not). However, to my sheer frustration both versions were severely speed-altered... rendering them unlistenable. The FM broadcast I discovered was sped up 5% from normal. The soundboard copy was slowed down at various speeds throughout the show.  In both instances, the band - and especially Adam - sounded ridiculous. I couldn't take it seriously. So I decided I would experiment with the two recordings... ultimately paving the way for this remaster.



Clarity and strong bass was present in the SBD version, although dry with a less "live" feel to it. The FM version was brighter tonally, with far more audience noise and detail. I thought they would compliment each other well if I could speed-correct them both and carefully line them up. I'm not a professional; all my work was done with free applications. And additionally, I applied very little EQ and used no compression tools at all. All I did was (painstakingly) speed correct, synchronize (which I had to continually adjust to avoid phase issues), and then blend using panning and level adjustments. My goal was for it to sound as close to the actual concert did as possible. Both tapes came lightly panned to the left, which I more-or-less left as such to preserve the coloring of the original recording. But I did adjust the mix when needed to ensure that they compliment each other. It isn't perfect, as both versions had various flaws which I couldn't avoid. But I tried to iron out 
all that I could. And to my ears, it sounds astonishing. It's now one of my favorite shows; there's an energy and character to it that very few bootlegs possess, and I'm proud of that result. I hope you feel the same way. Trade it freely. It's a show that's meant to be cranked!!! Enjoy!!


Tip - Given that both sources utilize the SBD recording, when mixed down to Mono there can be some phase issues at various points. So this version sounds ideal in stereo.
