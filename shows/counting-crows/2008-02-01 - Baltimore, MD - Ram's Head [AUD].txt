The mics on the Zoom H4 are too close together and make the recording sound as if it were in MONO, (this IS a STEREO recording). Recorded from center stage, 2nd row. 

The incessant conversation of much of the crowd detracts from this recording.  The record levels were much too high as well.  (Don't buy a Zoom H4)

I am NOT the guy who is singing.  Chris pounds his foot on the stage floor to keep time, this is not a flaw in the recording.

I traveled about 2,500 miles to see this show, (two 5 hour bus rides, two 5 hour plane rides and two 3.5 hour car rides) and it was entirely worth it.  It was a very unique and special show.
