Comments:  

Here we have the Crows acclaimed performance for VH1 Storytellers. Previous versions 
have circulated without (but not limited to) round here, anna begins, the full version 
of a long december (and their introductions). I believe the introduction to catapult has 
also not seen the light of day as yet. 

I don’t particularly know the history of this video, other than I received it as a VHS. It is 
certainly not a complete unedited master reel, but is a more complete version of the show already 
circulating. It has some editing on it (notably, snippets of tracks that are, I assume, to screen 
after a commercial break) and is not nearly as good video quality as those already in circulation. 
Having said that, all introductions are complete (with curse words). 

This is one for the completists, and is another “underground” tape that has likely never to have 
surfaced beyond “the inner circle” – whoever the fuck they are………….
