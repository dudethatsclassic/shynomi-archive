08.23.94
RPM Warehouse Toronto Ontario, Canada

Comments: 

Here we have the Crows during the 1994 AAEA tour. Adam and the boys appear to be in a very good mood for this show, with Adam particularly shooting the lights out. 

Cox family sit in on a number of tracks (violin and dobro) and really make an impression. Adam talks about how he is looking forward to playing a lot of new songs (elisabeth, margery)

Video only get a B- on this one due to the constant obstructions and shaking. When it is focused and unobstructed, the actual picture is fine. There is a lot of shaking and a lot of “black outs” that are audio only. The taper spends a lot of time getting into position during the first song.
