The Counting Crows & Augustana
07-29-2009 Red Rocks Ampitheater - Morrison, CO

Recorded from Row 51, near center.  Lineage: CA-11 > Zoom H2 > PC (CD Wave and TLH)

Ok, this recording is actually pretty decent about 75% of the time, but it has some nearly fatal flaws as well.  Let me explain.  Taping actually isn't that much fun, and it is hard on the people you go to a show with.  Talking, clapping and moving are all things that have to be severly restricted if you want to pull a great recording.  I was with my family at this show, 11 of us total if you can believe it, and I had to promise them that my little hobbie would not interfear with their fun.  I can't blame them - they went to have fun.  At two points during the show, large portions of my group leave - with the obligatory goodbyes.  Now, on top of that, you need to know that it rained hard prior to this show's start and then at least two times during the show.  An environmental issue that caused problems all its own (wet mics, wet Zoom - BAD, raingear blocking rain - GOOD, blocking sound - BAD).  You get the picture.

However, the biggest issue of all occurs during track #6 and #7.  I had to leave my gear with my future son-in-law. A straight "A" student on a college tennis scholarship.  Drug free....but you'd never guess that based upon his performance holding my gear for those two songs.  He didn't understand that the mics were on his head (on my hat I had stuck there) and didn't understand how to keep the Zoom dry and still allow it to record.  His cousins, realizing things were messed up, decided it would be a great time to chant about genitalia.  Gotta love it...  Oh well, it was a great show!


CD Disc info:  The way I cut the tracks in CDWave resulted in a nice break for disc 1 (tracks 1 - 14).  However, disc 2 would most appropriately include tracks 15 - 29.  Unfortunately, that ended up being 80 minutes and 21 seconds. If you can burn disc 2 using an overburn setting, I recommend doing that and then disc 3 can be just the encores.  If not, disc 2 should have tracks 29-33, and the break there is loud.  Oh well, you can figure out what you want to do.  I will provide coverart that allows for either option.

Counting Crows Audio Recording Policy

Audience taping is permitted at all Counting Crows shows. 

When Counting Crows are performing at a festival or other event featuring multiple bands, that particular event's policy may override Counting Crows' customary taping policy. All taping
is limited to audio only - no video is ever allowed - and with microphones only (no soundboard patches).
"Tapers" tickets are NOT required. A special taping section will be located behind the mix position in
each venue when possible. Tapers are only permitted to bring ONE audio recording deck and ONE microphone
stand/set of microphones into the venue. Anyone found taping in violation of the above policy will be removed from the venue and unauthorized recordings will be confiscated.

Since there will not be designated tapers' tickets, space in the taping section is first-come first-serve.  Taping will be permitted on a space-available basis with ANY ticket. When the
section is full, no additional recording equipment will be allowed into the show. If you wish to tape at a show, you have the best chance of getting a spot in the section if you are in line to enter the building when the doors open; this time is always printed on each ticket. If all of the space is taken before you enter the venue, you will not be permitted to bring recording equipment in with you.

The enjoyment of the audience in attendance at a concert always takes precedence over recording efforts.
This means that at no time should tapers ever require other patrons to be quiet or otherwise interfere with their enjoyment of the show.
