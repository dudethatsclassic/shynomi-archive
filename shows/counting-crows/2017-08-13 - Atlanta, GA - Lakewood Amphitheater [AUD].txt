Counting Crows
A Brief History of Everything Tour 2017 (with Matchbox 20, opening act Rivers and Rust)
Lakewood Amphitheater, Atlanta, GA
August 13, 2017

Recorded by Corrsfan, just left-of-center, uncovered section 205, front row (last seated section before the grass seating area 205)

CA-14 omnis > CA9200 battery box > Tascam DR100-MKII > 16/44 WAV > SD card  
Edited by Psychocaniac:  Audacity (amplify/leveling) > CD Wave Editor (track splitting) > TLH (SBE Fix) > dBpoweramp Music Converter (wav to L5 flac)
I followed Corrsfan's lead on the MB20 show and normalized individual tracks to bring up the overall volume and make it easier to hear acoustic and quiet
numbers.   

Taper's notes:  I had not used my new CA-14 omni mics yet, so I decided to give them a run, knowing they would pick up more of the audience 
than the cards, but, hell, I decided to give everyone the full concert/party experience. 
This was by far the biggest party-like concert we had ever been to. Even being in the last seated section and not on the floor, there was 
still a lot of activity in the crowd, with lots of dancing and singing (you will definitely hear it).  
I was seated the entire show (that's why I picked the front row of the section, as my wife can't stand for long periods), with the mics 
clipped to the bill of my cap. Getting the gear past the bag checks and metal detector was easy, as we just put it in the bottom of my wife's purse, 
with all her other "purse stuff" piled on top of it (the Tascam is not small).
