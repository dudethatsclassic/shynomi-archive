NOTES: This was a stealth recording made in far less than ideal circumstances.
My location wasn't great, there were some *very* talkative fans nearby, and I 
was literally right next to some of the crew, who yelled quite a lot.  Anyway,
only minimal editing was performed, including normalization and amplification
of an otherwise quiet recording.  Additional EQ and removal of some loud claps,
etc. would no doubt enhance this show, but I'll leave that to you.  At minimum,
you'll likely want to adjust the bass depending on your playback device.  In
addition, please do not distribute or trade this show without explicit permission 
from me (either you know who I am or please simply keep this to yourself). It 
should follow that I would also kindly ask that you not reveal your ownership or 
knowledge of this boot, because going down that road can only end badly. A DVD 
is planned for release, and the band did not allow taping at this performance.  
Sorry to sound so overbearing, but I don't want to trigger a repeat of the so-
called Disney-gate crisis ;)  All that aside, hope you enjoy the show!!!
