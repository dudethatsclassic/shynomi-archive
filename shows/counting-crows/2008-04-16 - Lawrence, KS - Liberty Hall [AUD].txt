This is the second time I've seen and taped Counting Crows, both shows had a freaking PA failure!
It was very cool to see this band in a 1000 seat venue like Liberty Hall, which IMHO, is the best venue in the USA!
While this recording is far from perfect, it deserves a listen. Hardcore CC fans should enjoy.
