A Show of firsts for me, the first time I've seen the band, the first time in Belgium 
and the first time I've ever tried a taping of this sort. The Concert Hall itself
was a perfect venue to see the band for the first time, I find arena's & stadia lack
atmosphere. I was apprehensive at first being so close to the stage whilst trying to 
tape the show not because on any over zealous security person as was a distinctive lack
of their presence during the concert (anyway I'm sure Adam, Dan & Immy saw me recording
during the show) but of surges from the crowd pushing, knocking into us, to the point
I thought of joining Anton's entourage on the balcony. I needn't have worried as the 
audience was great despite a virtually packed venue, everyone seemed to respect each 
other space even when going crazy. I was flanked by Simon on my left, Kasper on my right
and more by luck than judgement I ended up dead centre of the stage 5 rows back. The Dutch
girls farther to my right and little forward of us, I had been pre warned by Sandra not
to stand to close to her as she sings all the songs out loud.

The whole experience was magical, meeting Simon the evening before and both getting a
little tipsy on the local ale, meeting everyone else prior to the concert, Christine
who I've known online for a few years connected with the compilations, Sandra & her punk
loving husband Johan, Young Kasper his enthusiasm was so infectious, Adrianne whose totally
crazy, Nelle whose nearly as crazy & Maartje, Then of course Anton (whose the same in real
life as he is online) & Camille. 
Then standing around after the concert in the rain, while everyone was looking around
for band members for photo's and autographs, It was great of the band that they gave
their time for the fans. After having a drink with everyone who could stay, unfortunately
Sandra & Johan, and Maartje & friend had already gone, Chatting with the Benelux girls
prior to them going off in the rain, bound for Werchter.
Meeting the following day with ones who were left, for a coffee, guiness, iced tea, getting
to know Anton & Camille, and having a laugh mainly at Anton's expense.

So many highlights, 
Thanks to everyone
I'm so glad we have a record of it through the photo's & this recording.
Enjoy
Graham.
