Comments: Entire show was acoustic. This was the same day ‘Hard candy’ was released in the US. Only 500 people, who reserved a copy of ‘Hard Candy’, were allowed in to the show. The band was on a balcony on the second floor above the crowd, who stood in the rows of CDs on the first floor. The entire show was recorded for WXPN's ‘Live @ the World Cafe’ it was broadcast on 07/10/02 in Philadelphia and 08/23/02 on the nationally syndicated show.
