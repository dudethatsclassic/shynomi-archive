Taper notes:
Digging back through some recordings that I'd previously transferred (this one back in 2012!), I came upon this great Counting Crows show.  This Hammerstein Ballroom show was the first time I ever saw them live, despite the fact that I'd been listening to their albums for a very long time.  I recall Eric Vandercar inviting me to join, and I thought "why not?!".  Hammerstein Ballroom is certainly not "intimate", but it's certainly smaller than a lot of other places that they tend to play.  I also remember, at the time I recorded it, being kind of disappointed with the way my recording sounded (I generally prefer to record from the floor section at Hammerstein).  Upon listening back in 2019, I could tell that the raw materials on this recording were strong, but it just needed a touch of EQ to really make it shine (even though I generally prefer NOT to EQ my recordings if I can avoid it).  Sure enough after just a little tweaking, I present to you BARN190, which now REALLY shines (note that it takes a song or 2 for the band's sound engineer to really get everything dialed in well).

It's not just the recording quality that inspired me to push this one out quickly, but the setlist and performance too.  Opening with a subtle mid-tempo number like "Mrs. Potter's Lullaby" is a gutsy move -- not exactly a song that gets the crowd up and jumping, but it's a beautiful rambling bit of poetry.  This "Goodnight Elizabeth" takes a long detour through a "Christmas Coming Down" interpolation with a touch of "So You Wanna Be A Rock and Roll Star".  Other highlights for me are "Anna Begins", "If I Could Give All My Love" (such an amazing song!), a wonderful version of "A Long December", "Holiday In Spain" with a little intro describing how it came together, a rocking version of "Have You Seen Me Lately", and outstanding versions of "Rain King" (with a lengthy interpolation of "Oh, Susannah") and "Round Here" (with an interpolation of Fountains Of Wayne's "Radiation Vibe" -- the first time I'd heard heard of the band or their music), and finally a perfect show-closing version of "Sullivan Street".  This was a major pleasure to listen back to, now, 17 years later...

What a band!  What a show!  Thanks to EV for getting me out to see and record this one.  I miss him....

Enjoy!
