Toazted.nl

May 2002 

I think this interview took place in May, 'cause back than CC was in Holland, touring with Santana. 
05.20 they played the Gelredome in Arnhem and 05.21 there was the recording for 'Denk aan Henk' (3FM radio show) in Amsterdam. 
The interview took place in a taxi direction Amsterdam, so it's likely the interview was hold the same day as the FM recording, 05.21.
Later the same year CC came back to Holland to play the Heineken Music Hall, Amsterdam. But at that time 'Hard Candy' was already released.


Adam Duritz chats with Toazted in a taxi to Amsterdam. 
The album 'Hard Candy' is about to be released and Adam tells how he puts all the songs together. 

"I wrote some of the lyrics when I was drunk on a holiday in Spain. It seems really cheap but it turned out great!"
