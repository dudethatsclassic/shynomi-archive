Adam's words: 
Dated October 8, 2006 Evening -
"Somewhere in the air above America"
(http://adam.countingcrows.com/journal.php?uid=2310)

"So I'm flying home from Las Vegas. We
played a charity gig there last night. I 
thought it went really well. We walked out 
onstage in this massive arena filled to the
brink with a black tie clad crowd and 
without a word launched into a 10 minute
version of 'Round Here' followed by a 7
minute extended 'A Long December', then I 
said, 'I'm sorry I'm not very talkative',
and we left. I think they were shocked. I 
thought it was very cool."
