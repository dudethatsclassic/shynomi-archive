Here we have yet another VHS conversion. This is possibly 2 camera mix (either that or the person filming moves – could be either). This one has a lot of drop outs which were on the original that I received. 

Audio from this show is also circulating as “Staring At The Sun” bootleg.  

The show is pretty high energy, with notable performances of “Children in Bloom” and “I’m not Sleeping” (which has some bad drop outs).
