Comments:

There is a fair bit of echo on this DVD, probably because the video 
looks to have been shot from directly in front of the band, right at the 
front of stage. As such, there is also some moderate distortion at the
louder moments, although the shitty speakers I am listening to this through
may have something to do with that. Of course, it would also be the venue. 

Video itself is great quality, and if I were to guess (which I am) I would say
this is a hand held DV cam, or something very similar. The picture looks great
full screen and does not seem to have been compressed (again, I am guessing). 
There's no pixelation and no dropouts. 

Performance is three songs and is typically high energy, Hard Candy is especially
tight although AD does point out Brysons fuckup on the ending.  

another year, another FTU release.
