Comments: A very nice 2002 gig from the Irving Plaza, NY. Hard Candy had just been released & it was “doing really well” according to Adam et al. The video on this one is pretty good, which makes me think that VHS is definitely NOT the original source, rather a DV Cam or something similar (I don’t have all the details). The sound is also very good on this one too. It is shot from somewhere above left of stage, and (like all audience shots) has some period where it is either not in focus, blocked or disturbed somehow. All in all, a very watchable show. 

Highlights are “Round Here” with amazing electricity alts, and the closing tune – Sullivan Street. This is another one of those video’s only watched by those on the “inner circle” or who are part of the underground.
