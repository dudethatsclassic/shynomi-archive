General thought about festivals, they are a lot of fun but not great for recording. A lot of talkers and vendors screaming out to sell beer. Is what it is. I moved around a lot trying to get away from some of it but there was no escaping it. Inside the stadium it echoes a lot. Outside the stadium there was quite a bit of wind. 

I sat in the stands for this show. I was getting tired by this point as the weekend was about to wrap up. It had been a long time since I had seen the Counting Crows and I enjoyed the show. There is a part of me that wishes I had gone to the B52's instead but I am ok with the choice. The vocals start out a bit muddy but do clear up a couple of songs in.
