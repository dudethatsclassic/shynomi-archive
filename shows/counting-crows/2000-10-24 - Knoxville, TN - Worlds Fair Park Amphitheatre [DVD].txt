Comments:

I received this VHS with the Live gig on it also. I am guessing that this is an incomplete show as the taper either ran outta tape, or couldn’t be bothered with the whole Crows set. 

This one is a little tedious to watch. The filming is not always great, the sound is not always great – but if you concentrate you get the feel of the show in any case. 

Only really point worth noting is the Dolphins’ Cry alt in “Round Here”. Also, Adam & Ed do a duet on “Dolphins Cry” during the Live set.
