The Covers Album Compilation

Comment: That is a CD bought from a shop in England and it is a series of cover versions of tracks, recorded in the studio at the time they were recording Hard Candy.  Some of the tracks ended up as B-sides., most didn't. It was going to be released but in the end was shelved, but presumably shouldn't have been allowed into the hands of traders. It is also circulating as a boot called ''Under The Covers'' or something similar.  However, they are rating it as only B to B+. This Copy is a studio version quality.
