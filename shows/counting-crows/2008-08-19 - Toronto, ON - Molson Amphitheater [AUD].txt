Great show.  Very special, rare all acoustic performance.  Counting Crows opened for Maroon 5, but these guys definitely stole the show with this great set.  Thunder Road isn't a teast, it's the full song, smack dab in the middle of Rain King.  Very cool indeed.  The show is a tad quiet because it's acoustic, and the crowd can be loud at times, especially those around me, but shit happens.  It's still a very enjoyable listen.

Feel free to clean this show up, I just ask of you two things:
1. Share it, 
2. Send me an email so I can give your work a listen!

This is a pretty special show to me.  Sorry it took so long to release.
