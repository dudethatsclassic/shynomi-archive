Titeltrack taken from Counting Crows album "Hard Candy" and contains 2 versions of "Big Yellow Taxi" and to live and acoustic tracks. The enhanced video isn't included in this release.

Counting Crows is a rock band originating from Berkeley, California. The group gained popularity in 1994 following the release of its debut album August and Everything After, which featured the hit single "Mr. Jones". The band's influences include Van Morrison, R.E.M., Nirvana, Bob Dylan, and The Band.[1][2][3][4] They received a 2004 Academy Award nomination for the song "Accidentally in Love".
