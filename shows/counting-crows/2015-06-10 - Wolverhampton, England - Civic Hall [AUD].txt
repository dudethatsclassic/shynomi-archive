Counting Crows
Wednesday June 10th 2015
Civic Hall
Wolverhampton
England

Taper: Adam Grant / Adamness (adamness@live.co.uk)

Source/Lineage/Equipment: Taped from 3rd Row Center Balcony seats using an iPhone 5s & Zoom iQ6 Stereo Condenser Mic. Recorded to WAV using Zoom Handy Recorder App. WAV File transferred to Laptop using USB via iTunes. Tracked and Fade In/Out added using Audacity. Converted to FLAC (L8) with TLH.

The only UK show (if you exclude the Isle of Wight Festival performance) that Counting Crows are scheduled to perform in 2015. When the show was announced I was determined to go and as a treat to myself, I purchased a Meet & Greet package for before the show.

From my home in Newcastle, I've taken a 450+ Mile Round Trip, with an overnight stop at my mother's house in Burton-upon-Trent the night before the concert (being drafted in to help put together a couch whilst there).

The show itself was brilliant, although the setlists are seemingly becoming a little stale as of late. Highlights of the show being Hard Candy, Miami & A Murder Of One.

Hopefully you lot will enjoy the recording. I've now got just under 2 Weeks until I fly out to Dublin to see them again

In terms of Sound, I'd say this would be an A-
