Here is a nice compilation that was put together a number of years ago and circulated around the Counting Crows trading community. These four cd's have a number of performances from 1994-2000 all of which are SBD/FM. The overall quality is very nice with the weakest sounding show being on disc two. It's still listenable but is a little bass heavy in my opinion.
Also Sullivan Street on disc 4 has some static issues/digi noise that is present on all circulating copies that I know of.
You can decide if you want to leave it off your final comp. Even for the couple of small flaws here and there I think there are enough nice performances to make this a worthwhile download.

Enjoy!
