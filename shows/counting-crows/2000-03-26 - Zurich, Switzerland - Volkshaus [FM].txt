I was at this show myself. Swiss radio broadcasted the concert a few days later. That way I
was able to tape it. This is the whole show (although a little short in my opinion)!
Before the encore the crowd went "ooooh" like they usually do in Switzerland. Adam was a
little freaked out by this, as you can hear in his comment. Counting Crows were back in
Switzerland in the summer of 2000 at the St. Gallen Festival. I have that show too - so if
anyone is interested in that recording, I can post it as well. Just let me know.
