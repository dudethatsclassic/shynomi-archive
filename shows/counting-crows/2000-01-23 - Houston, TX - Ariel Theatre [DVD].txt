Another audience shot here, this time from the TDL tour. The opening of this show is really high energy, and adam comments how he has ripped his setlist by the time they got to “high life”. 

The video is a little tedious. Sometimes it is terribley out of focus, pointed at the roof or to the back of soeones head. Other (rare) times it is a nicely focused close up shot. For this reason, I have the video a B- (it could easily have been a C+). 

Not a whole lot to mention about this one.
