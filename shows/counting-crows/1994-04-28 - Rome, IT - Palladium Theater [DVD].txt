VideoMusic 94 Broadcast

Note from the source: I converted the show from a vhs I've recorded from tv in 1994, but you can guess how many times I've played it since that time, even if I've preserved the cassette in a case from light and dust...
I think this is considered as "master tape". The audio also in vhs is not great probably due to the recorder I had at the time and cannot do anything better (at least not with the software I have) . I've used Nero Vision 4 and converted in PAL at 5073 kbit/s and resolution 720 x 576; audio settings were on "automatic" but should be Dolby Digital 5.1
