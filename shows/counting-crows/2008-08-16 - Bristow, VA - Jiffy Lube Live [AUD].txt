MISC NOTES:
This was a co-heading tour between Counting Crows and Maroon 5 featuring Sara Bareilles opening the show.  I taped all three acts.  For Sara's set I was allowed to fly my mic stand at the full 12ft. height, however Nissan Pavilion's Production Manager asked me to lower it for the two main acts.  I raised and lowered the stand constantly in responce to the audience's reaction to the band (when the audience stood, the stand went up to about 6-6.5 feet, when they were sitting, it was lowered to be about 6 inches above the tops of their heads).  All in all, I'm very happy with how it came out; I hope you enjoy it as well!
