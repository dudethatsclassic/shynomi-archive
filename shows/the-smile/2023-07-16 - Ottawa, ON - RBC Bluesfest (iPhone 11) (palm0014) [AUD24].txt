Recording notes:
Quite the respectful crowd during The Smile.  Didn't run into too many issues other than someone trying to get by me at the beginning of 'Read the Room'.  

Show notes:
I didn't listen to any other recordings of The Smile going into this concert and I was blown away.  I swear I was in some trance at one point and my bud tapped me on the shoulder and it scared the bejesus out of me!!

What a great choice for the closer of Bluesfest.  I look forward to their new album after hearing a bunch of new tunes.
