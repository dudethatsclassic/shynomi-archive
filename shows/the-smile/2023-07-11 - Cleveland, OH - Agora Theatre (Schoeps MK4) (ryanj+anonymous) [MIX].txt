Notes:
Been trying to see The Smile their last few tours. Missed the Atlanta show, missed the Detroit show, missed the Charleston show but finally hit a show in Cleveland! Had front row, dead center seats in the balcony. It doesn't get much better. Also our first time at Agora. Great venue. The sound was amazing inside. Just what I would expect from them live. I have been holding off seeing/hearing anything until I saw them. Simple stage design and loved seeing all of their synths and modular setups easily on stage. The Same was probably my favorite track from the night. Sounds so incredible. 

Very happy how this turned out. The video is very good too. Enjoy.
