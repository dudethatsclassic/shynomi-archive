Great gig by an amazing band! I tried to record the entire show using three different sources, but unfortunately, my primary recorder stopped right after the first set. While the recording could have been better, it's still an enjoyable listen. Apologies for the boomy bass and the loud crowd, but they were really into it tonight (and who can blame them?).
