Keane
Bexhill-On-Sea De La Warr Pavilion
9th March 2012

Roland R05 / Sony ECM-125 mic 16-bit WAV recording, levels increased in Pyro Audio Creator LE, trackmarked in CD Wave Editor, converted to FLAC in FLAC Front End

A nearly hometown show for Keane (just down the road from their home of Battle in Kent), this was the first of two shows to preview their forthcoming Strangeland album.  Ten new songs feature in the set, eight of them being played for the first time, the other two for the first time in the UK

Even The Stars music blog described the show in their review - https://www.eventhestars.co.uk/2012/03/keane-bexhill-on-sea-de-la-warr.html
