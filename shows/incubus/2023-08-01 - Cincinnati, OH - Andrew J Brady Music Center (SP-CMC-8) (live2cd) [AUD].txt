Notes:

*  = Originally Recorded By The Beatles.

Stellar set from Incubus, no pun intended. I last saw them in October, 2021 and they
changed out about 10 songs from then. Awesome to see. I wish more bands who have been
around as long as they have would do the same every year or two. Made for a completely
different show and while we didn't get "Warning" or "Megalomaniac", it made the last
show all that more memorable for different reasons. I think I liked this one more, but
thats mainly due in part to getting to hear "Make Yourself". The people around me were
pretty respectful, no loud screaming or whistling nearby. Turned out pretty decent.

Paris Jackson & Badflower opened the show. [all recorded].
