Incubus
Riverbend Music Center
Cincinnati, OH
7-29-2007

Incubus played a pretty mellow set last night with not a whole lot of their older songs in the mix.  They did rock though.  This is the fifth time I have seen them play and, although the set didn't compare to other shows I have seen, they still were very entertaining.  This is my first recording that I have been able to share with you all, I hope you enjoy it.  The sound quality isn't as great as many shows that are on here.  It was recorded with an MP3 player's internal mic.  The bass hits are a little heavy and it sounds a bit muffled but I have heard a lot worse sounding recordings than this.  This one didn't turn out all that bad.
