Notes:
Death From Above 1979 and Deftones opened for Incubus to what seemed like a sold out crowd. My first time out with these mics and I do love the card pattern already. Great pickup of the stack and I think it’s a good tape. As I haven’t bought anything from Incubus since ‘Crow Left of the Murder’ I’d say this setlist was evenly distributed with old/new and the band sounded great for the first night of the tour! Worth the price of a pavilion seat! Enjoy the tape.
