Thanks to some production holds getting released the day before this concert, I 
decided to go. I had been checking for tickets for a couple weeks leading up to
the show and did not want to sit in the back of the theater. I got lucky and one
opened up 11 rows back from the left stack. The ticket was pricey, but considering
they were playing a full album in an intimate theater setting, I decided to bite 
the bullet. Glad I did. What a show! This was also my first time seeing them as
they had cancelled the previous show I had a ticket to (Toledo, OH 1999). They
never returned to Toledo, and I never wanted to drive 2+ hours to catch them. 
That may have been a mistake. What a live band. I have to see these guys again
in 2020 or 2021 because there are so many songs I still have to see live. Their
Morning View album has always been a favorite, so perhaps I will catch a 20th
performance of that! If you are a fan, you will want to check this one out. The
sound was pretty decent. 

"Cheers Aye-kron, cheers!" - BB
