Incubus
Roche Motel
Pocatello, Idaho
December 6, 1998

SHN size 370 mb

Source info/warning:  This was the first show that I recorded and I didn't have real mics so I used my headphones.  Surprisingly enough, It actually works pretty well but I found much greater fidelity with my core sound budget cardioids.  Also, being that this was the first show I recorded, I had yet not learned that one should not drink and scream when one is recording a show.

Sharp MD-702>minidisc>(Kenwood rack minidisc unit)>soundblaster via coax digital cable, normalized and cut and burned to cd-r


No setlist info, if someone helps me with it I will post a new info file.


Show notes:  This place was a dive, which is why Brandon asks 'What did this place used to be?'  A couple of months later Incubus was playing the Delta Center in Salt Lake City with Ozzfest and the rest is history.  It is kind of cool to catch a band in small venues like this before they hit it big and hopefully fans will enjoy this early recording.

Opener this night was 2 Skinnee J's and I can torrent this if anyone wants it.  There are much better quality 2SJ shows circulating but it may be good for the completists.
