Notes:  No EQ was done to this recording, other than converting to 16bit/44.1kHz and tracking, this is as raw as it gets.  The original 24bit/48kHz version is available for trade.

Crowd around me was very well behaved.  Lots of ambient 'sing-a-longs' picked up from the floor and lower balcony, similar to my 10/10/06 AIC recording from the same venue.  Nothing too distracting.  Basically a good old FOS CSB recording.  For as far back as we were in line, I'm more than happy with the results here.  Special thanks to my woman, Suzanne, for putting up with me and not talking too much during the show.  

Rowdy crowd on the floor...kind of.  For the first 3/4 of the show, I don't think there was enough room to even get a mosh pit going.  We got a kick out of the people crowd surfing during 'I Miss You.'  Suzanne turned towards me and started head banging, it was all I could do to not bust out laughing.  You'll hear her at the end of the song say 'I thought it was appropriate.'  I'll take a FOS spot in that upper balcony any day.  If you ever have an opportunity to tape a show at this venue, check out the upper balcony, up 5 flights of stairs from the entrance.  You might be 50' off the floor, but it feels like you are hanging over the stage.  Great view and great sound!
