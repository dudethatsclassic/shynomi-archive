Notes:

* Features extended jam intro.

This show was originally scheduled for Sunday, September 5th. The night before
Labor Day. Sadly, a touring member tested positive for COVID-19 and the show was
moved a month later. Luckily for me, a few good seats opened up because of some
ticket refunds. I happened to see a pair in my preferred location and snatched
them up week of. We lucked out with weather as you never know what October may
bring. This was an outdoor (but covered) show, so we were never worried about 
rain, but thunder/lightning could have been an issue. Thankfully, the rain held
off and we had none. Just cloudy skies with temps in the low 70s/high 60s at
showtime. The recording went surprisingly well. I had 2 occasionally loud girls
to my right and did my best removing their "whoo's!" & screams when needed in 
iZotope RX 7. You may hear Brandon's microphone shorting out during "Circles".
This was quickly corrected by "Wish You Were Here".

Not a bad show for my final outdoor recording of 2021. Can't wait for the next
Incubus tour.
