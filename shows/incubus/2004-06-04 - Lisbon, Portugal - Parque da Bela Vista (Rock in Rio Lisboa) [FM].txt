Notes: Complete recording of the full concert re-broadcasted by Mega FM on 
             the 10th of July 2004. There are plenty of station IDs throughout the 
             recording which I chose not to remove... still, if you feel like doing it, 
             go ahead.

             Regarding the show... I attended it, and IMO the band put on a great
             show that night. The audience did not seem much into it though... I
             think that was mainly due to Incubus being some kind of "outsiders"
             that day (other bands included Sepultura, Moonspell, Slipknot and
             Metallica). Besides, Incubus played before Metallica, which I think didn't
             help either. On the other hand, judging by the setlist, I think the band 
             took into account the fact they were playing along heavy metal bands...
             A really nice set, including a rare, powerful performance of "Take Me To
             Your Leader" (Incubus always include a surprise when they come to 
             Portugal!). This show also features my favorite version of "Sick Sad 
             Little World"... I love Mike's guitar work on that one. Enough said... hope
             you enjoy the show as much as I did!
