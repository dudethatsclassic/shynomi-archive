This recording turned out quite nice considering we were sitting behind the soundbaord in the first row of the seats/bleachers. The DakotaDome is just that, a huge dome arena/glorified gymnasium used for football games, and not designed with acoustics in mind. That said, this recording is nice. This gig was a bit of a rarity considering Vermillion, SD has a population of less than 15,000 people and this was the biggest concert the University had ever hosted.
