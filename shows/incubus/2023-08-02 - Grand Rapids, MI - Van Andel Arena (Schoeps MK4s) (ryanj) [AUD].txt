Notes:
Initially was going to GR to see Orville Peck at Frederik Meijer Gardens. But he sadly cancelled his whole tour. Incubus shortly after made an announcement of a tour and stop on the same day in GR. So, my wife and I were thrilled! We made the trek after work from Detroit so we missed the first opener and caught some of Badflower which we really enjoyed! Incubus put on an excellent show! From the last time in Charleston, they have really upped the lighting for their shows. Sound was great for the show too. We stood at the back of the pit and got some good film from the show! Really happy how both tapes turned out. I think I have seen Incubus almost a dozen times in the last 15 years and finally heard them play my favorite song, "Make Yourself!" It was nasty! During "Drive" Mike's guitar seemed to have broke right before the solo. He had to walk off stage to get a new guitar while Brandon kept the melody going by humming.
