Comments: The recording was made from Section 6 Row O, dead center few rows back from the soundboards. Opening act for the show was Bew Kweller. Interestingly, it turns out that Brandon had a very eventful day. He was arrested while boarding a plane from New York to North Carolina because he had a knife in his luggage. He arrived in Raleigh and had a police escort to the Alltel Pavilion. He arrived at the show just in time to walk on stage and start the show. Included with the show is a news article that reports the incident.
