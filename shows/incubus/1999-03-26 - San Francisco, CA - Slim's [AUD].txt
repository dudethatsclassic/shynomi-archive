Incubus - 26.3.1999 - Slim's San Francisco, CA 

taper unknown

Set:  Favorite Things / Shaft! / Magic Medicine / A Certain Shade Of Green / Nowhere Fast / Hilikus / Glass / Idiot Box / Pardon Me / Vitamin / New Skin / Redefine / Summer Romance (Anti-Gravity Love Song) / Calgone / Color Me Badd Mixing

Halfway through Calgone the song is stopped, a verse of The Jackson 5's "I Want You Back" is played, and they finish up the rest of Calgone.
