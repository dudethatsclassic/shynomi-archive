NOTES
I suppose it's worth mentioning that Nirvana played the Paradiso 8 years later to the day. 
Circulating recording cuts in during "BRO JAMES" intro, so it's possible there were more opening songs
("WORLD" + "BAD MOOD" perhaps?).

ANOTHER NOTES
Cassette tape > Total Recorder > .wav > LP Ripper > Flac Frontend > .flac

This must be the recordig mentioned by Chris Lawrence in his Concert Chronology on the official Sonic Youth site.
The recording starts during "Brother James" and probably one or two songs before this are missing. I was there, but i
didn't know SY at all at the time and the set was well on its way when i entered the Paradiso.
A memorable night of madness though! SY playing so earsplittingly loud that most of the audience choose to appreciate
the show from behind a heavy curtain door in the lobby. And then a completely crazy PIL set with John Lydon attacking
the audience with fire extingishers and having his clothes torn off by an overenthusiastic fan (i guess he got a
little bit more than the shirt as a souvenir as John Lydon managed to break his cordless microphone on the blokes head ...)
