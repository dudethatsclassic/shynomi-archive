Sonic Youth - 2002 02 22 Logos, Fukuoka, Japan

Show note:
Great recording! Song '1234' was only played during this leg of the Japan tour.  Kim Gordon & the Arthur Doyle Hand Cream was called Mariah Carey & the Arthur Doyle Hand Cream at this point.

Ripping note:
