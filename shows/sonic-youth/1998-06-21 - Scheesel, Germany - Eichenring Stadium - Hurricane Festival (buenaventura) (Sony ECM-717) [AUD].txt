Show note: 
Thanks to Buenaventura for recording and sharing this show. As far as I can tell, it has never circulated. It is a bit boomy sounding due to the outdoor festival. 

Transfer note:
Tape flip during Sunday is at an unusual time, because the tape was used to record a short set by a different band before. There was a cut in Wildflower Soul as well. Some digital clicks removed through Audacities default click removal settings. A high-pitch whine on the tape was removed.
