unknown recording / unknown transfer


01. Intro

04. I Love Her All The Time
05. Ghost Bitch
06. She's in a Bad Mood
07. Green Light
08. Brother James
09. Inhuman
10. Expressway

A pretty interesting set. Not sure how it was recorded but the file I received was LOUD - so I don't know if the slightly
distorted sound is due to a brickwalled transfer or just the way the tape sounds. In any case, I reduced the amplification
by 50% and that is the only change I made, aside from inserting the splits to encode to flac.

Last known show of the fall '85 European "Bad Moon Rising" tour. Steve Shelley on drums. The first and only performance
of "She's in a Bad Mood" since 1983 (and with Steve).
