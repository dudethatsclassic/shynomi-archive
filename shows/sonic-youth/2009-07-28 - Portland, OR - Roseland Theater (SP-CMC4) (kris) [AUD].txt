Despite being the hottest day of the summer(105 degrees in portland), this was a much more relaxed show than the crazy seattle fest a few days earlier. 
I thought about doing on-stage mics like last time but the roseland decided to put up the barricade for this show.
I've never seen anyone sweat as much as Thurston did here. Total drippage the entire time..awesome.
The crowd was pretty much standing still until they played silver rocket. 
They also thought they were too cool to hear Thurston's michael jackson joke.
Would have liked to hear cross the breeze again. Skip tracer was a nice surprise.
I think this went alright in capturing the overall sound, but the roseland has never been known for it's great acoustics, so take it for what it is.
There were at least 2 other AUD sources and a couple videotapers.
