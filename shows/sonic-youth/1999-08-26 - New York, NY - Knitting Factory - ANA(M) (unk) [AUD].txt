Sonic Youth - 1999 08 26 @ Knitting Factory, NYC, NYC

Show note:
This was billed as a Thurston gig, but the full band played. Early instrumentals, but Lightnin'.

Transfer note:
THis is a strange one. Sound quality is a little odd as well, listening to "StreamXSonik Subway" you might think this was a show from 1981 not 1999.
