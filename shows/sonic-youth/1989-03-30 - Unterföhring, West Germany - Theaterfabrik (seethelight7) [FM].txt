An unlabled tape of SONIC YOUTH in Muenchen made me first think of another 1st gen. from Christian Z.'s recording activities. But the sound was definitely no audience source.
A call on the Lightseer proved, that this is an FM-Master culled from BR2. This is maybe around the circles, but the quality itself here leaves nothing to be desired. A strange
thing indeed is, that given the fact that this was a delayed broadcast, why they (at Br2) not cut down the extensive tunings in favour of the "missing" first track, "The wonder",
as it was stated to be played as opener. Maybe the quality was too bad? There are a lot of trouble going on during this rather short show, and a lot of cursing by Thurston, about
the radio transfer marred their equipment and the tunings...Hmmm...Aren't most early days SY concerts are a feast of feedback??? Anyway, think you will love this, and for me this
show has two of my alltime favourites, too. Cross the breeze and Teenage riot back to back from their grande album "Daydream nation". Enjoy, or leave. SB.
---
