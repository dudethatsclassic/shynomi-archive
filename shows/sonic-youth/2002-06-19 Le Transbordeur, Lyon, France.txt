Sonic Youth - 2002 06 19 Le Transbordeur, Lyon, FRA

Show notes:
Decent sounding MD audience recording. Noticed that the left channel was somewhat weak and picked up a lot of crowd noise. I put an envelope around some of the clapping, to lower the volume, then normalized the left channel in pieces. Raised bass and guitars to brighten the sound a little bit. Finally, some light compression to raise the music compared to the crowd. Finally, a removed fade after Radical Adults.
