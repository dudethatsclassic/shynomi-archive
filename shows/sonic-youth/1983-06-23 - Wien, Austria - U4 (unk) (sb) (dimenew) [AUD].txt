unknown gen. audience tape, but judging the sound, it's a very low gen., maybe 2nd or 3rd gen.
This cassette (TDK SA90) -> playback on Harman Kardon CD491 -> ZoomH2 (at WAV 16/44) -> SD card -> HD -> Audacity (edit/remaster) -> WAV 16/44 -> TLH8 -> FLAC -> DIME ->
YOUR EARS AND MIND...on a sonic noise trip... Another recording that suffered from the automatic mic gain control of those 80's recorders, where the in-between song tunings/chatters
are almost louder than the music taped. I tried to balance that a bit, as far as it was possible without disturbing the music. Another great find from DIMENEW's endless tapecollection!


---
After a short pause - due to personal taping activities - another one from the small 80's SONIC YOUTH flood our team has to offer. So this is the #6, if my rotten brain can remember?
This is a real weird one, from, what I think, their first visit to continental Europe. As I am not really well into Sonic Youth's very early stuff (joined in with Daydream nation and
left after Goo), this was a very difficult show to edit. Hope I got it - at least - almost right. The great Concert Chronology Site lists "I wanna be your dog" as the last song, maybe
the encore, but I can't figure out that it is here on this recording which just spans one side of a 90 minute tape. So, if that song was played, I'm sorry, it is not here.
Bummer in the summer? Ok, but if this tape does not circulate very much, be happy with what you have to be happy with. And now let the tape get rolling...SB.
---
