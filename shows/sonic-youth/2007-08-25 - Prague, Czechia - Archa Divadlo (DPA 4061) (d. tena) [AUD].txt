Comments: 

Second Sonic Youth ever in Prague, great and energetic show. The crowd (most american than czech) was really into the show and enjoyed a lot. Band also seem to have a good time.

Sound quality is ok.
I planned to tape with UA5 but same day checked and batteries were not functioning so this was an emergency rig. 
The cheapo SP saturated the sound (I think this is a defective unit because I was SURE it was not clipping), but the recording is still enjoyable, specially for those who were there or SY completists. 

*I had many problems during first song, while trying to find out why the preamp was not running as it should. I tried and tested many settings on the fly so the fist track is affected by this and during the could sound more or less consistent. I tried to make the sound as smoother as I could.
