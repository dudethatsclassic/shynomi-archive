Taper's Notes:

i started checking my boxes of  tapes for some live recordings i did back in the days. the first to come up with is a stellar show of SONIC YOUTH i taped in my hometown of Düsseldorf in 1989.

so this is a so far uncirculated recording of SONIC YOUTH appearing at the NOISE NOW FESTIVAL in düsseldorf in march 1989. it was my first time to see them and i was absolutely blown away by the sound dripping of the walls and ceiling of the only half packed PHILLIPSHALLE.

i taped the complete show. but while transferring the tape to computer i was browsing for some cover art, thinking i might find jpg of the NOISE NOW FESTIVAL ticket or poster or something. what i found instead was that three songs from this show were used for the "deluxe" reissue of DAYDREAM NATION. so i had to remove SILVER ROCKET, THE SPRAWL and CROSS THE BREEZE from this torrent. this is sad because the show is like one massive sound collage with tape snippets in between the songs. still i decided to keep going. 

the recording is pretty rough with quite some hiss and the sound changing. but you get a good idea of how great SONIC YOUTH were that afternoon (if i remember it right they were on at ca 5pm.)
