We had some pretty abnormal July temps for this show (low to mid 70s) and
a nice wind coming in off of Lake Erie. I decided to run the omnis with
the fear of wind and it was the best decision. Thankfully, the wind is
barely audible, however, slight phasing does occur here and there. I did
lower the bass a bit in post (it needed it!) and raised levels before
normalizing. The solo performances needed a slight volume boost. I also
raised the levels when Taylor spoke. When Taylor performed on the B and
C stages, I stayed put facing towards the main stacks. I definitely got
some stares, but it was all worth it in the longrun as she performed her
own penned Sugarland hit, "Babe" for the first time. I was actually very
(very!) fortunate I had no loud screamers next to me or around me at all.
I taped Taylor in 2015 and was very lucky, as well. I hope if I see her
again my luck continues! Taylor was a little under the weather with the
sniffles and runny nose, but she persevered and put forth an incredible
show. Without question, one of the biggest productions I've ever seen!
The recording turned out good for a stadium recording. Enjoy.
