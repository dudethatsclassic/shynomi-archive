This is night number two in Chicago.  I had tickets and planned on taking my daughter to both Friday 10/9 and Sat 10/10 shows.  
I ended up selling my tickets to the Friday night show at the last moment.  No problem, we still had tickets to Sundays show 
back in Minneapolis.  This was an excellent show.  Crowd was of mostly young girls who screamed like crazy.  My tickets were
at the opposite end of the arena.  Great view of the entire show, just a bit of a distance.  Sound I think turned out good.
You get a good feel of how loud the crowd was. Headliner Taylor Swift was also Recorded and will be uploaded also. 
Special Thank you to my Daughter for helping me with the song titles. 

My Suggestion is to split into two disc between Tracks 15 & 16.  
That way if you wish, you can eliminate the 4 Minute scream track.  
I will say it was amazing to hear all the young girls scream for so long.

This is my MASTER RECORDING.  I did not do anything to the recording other then split the tracks.  It is raw as I hear it from my seat.
