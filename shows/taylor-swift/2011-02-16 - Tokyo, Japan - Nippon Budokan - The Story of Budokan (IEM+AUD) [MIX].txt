Liberated bootleg from Japan. The IEM kicks in about half-way through Sparks Fly, disappears for a while during Mine. From 'The Story of Us' onwards it's consistent.

Audio quality isn't as good as the Red Tour recording but a decent enough listen. The audience source obviously had a few issues with screamers near the taper.
