Notes

Opening night of the 1989 tour. IEM / Audience matrix from the Xavel label in Japan. The audience is set quite a way back in the mix but no matter, the IEM sources have a great balance to them. Audio is quality is a little down on the 2nd night from the same label, but still an excellent listen. Occasional static interference. There is a cut in Enchanted / Wildest Dreams, the first half of the song is missing along with some chat.

If anyone is sitting on a complete Red Tour video (or audio) other than Tokyo would be great to see it posted!
