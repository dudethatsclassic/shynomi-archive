Notes:
Went into this penultimate show of the Eras tour expecting to be impressed, and I wasn't
disappointed. Absolutely left a fan.

From a recording standpoint, this is an interesting one. We were side stage, right in
front of the band, but also with an array directly in front of us. Makes for a nice direct
PA tape, with a bit of direct band signal (leads to a bit of flam from the snare in the
more rock songs).

Taming the stadium and crowd proved to be a little tough, especially facing the stage from
the side, meaning that the bulk of the audience was to one side of the tape. Ended up
pulling down the sides a bit, and using some EQ to tame the screaminess of the crowd. Came
out decent for a giant stadium tape IMO.
