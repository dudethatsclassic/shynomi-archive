Alanis was in a good, comfortable mood and really did it this night for me (4th time seeing her in 25 years).
The audience "overdid" it a bit for me as she's just a girl and not a god so to me the applause was more annoying then
being just an honnest appreciation given to the just played song. 
Maybe it was the complete setting of the venue and the accoustic set that draws a different type of folk to this kind of shows.
One thing I was pleased about though was that most of them didn't talk to much or to loud during and in between the songs
so that makes this a nice and clean recording of a very impressive performance.
Because of the audience being so loud and Alanis being a bit talkative inbetween songs I had to make some of the splits
in places I usually don't. I did think about seperating the chats inbetween but then thought you can skip to the next song any
time you like so most is just right from the start of the first notes of the song where I placed the splits.
Alanis played a new song called Reasons I Drink and unfortunatly you can hear a little buzz in the recording what I beleive is a
rather shitty connection between the mics mini jack and the recorders input channel. It stops just after Mary Jane begins and isn't
really noticable during the songs but some (like me) might think it's pretty annoing. Have no clue how to get it out so it is what it is.
And yes, I am looking out for new gear and am thinking to put the good old PCM-D50 up in the cupboard and replace it with a Roland RO7 (better sugestions appreciated).
