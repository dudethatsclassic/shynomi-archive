The next destination on the trip is Bergen Norway for Bergenfest.  The first
two days the lineup looked interesting so plans were to hit the festival hard
then the last two days were going to be gametime decisions which became sightseeing, 
eating and drinking.  The festival is always well run and easy to navigate.  Some
rules are annoying but that is more a result of local customs than general stupidity.
The first day had 3 artists I wanted to see and 3 unknows.  Alanis Morrisette was
headlining and was very good.  My first time seeing her.  Lucy Dacus had a 45 minute 
set as a preview of 2 Amsterdam shows for me and Lee Fileds who played late at 
Blues Peer and I missed.  I enjoyed the other bands too so it was a great day of
music and the weather was amazing.  Considering it had rained 11 straight days before
arriving, sun and sunlight til midnight made for a great day.
