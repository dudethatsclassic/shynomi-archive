The tape was originally marked as (early 1974), but it was quickly verified as the May 28, 1973 show.

In the past there were higher gen copies floating around.  This 1st generation cassette is clearly a major upgrade.

She only played about a dozen dates on this tour, so it was not extensive by any means, and throughout the show she talks about playing new songs from the new album ("Fantasy") which was released in June 1973. Had it been early 1974, I don't think she would be referring to it as the recent or new album, because that would be a half year or more later. Her next album was released in September 1974.

At this show, she starts off playing "Tapestry" songs and some older material, introduces the band, then plays tracks from the new album, then returns for encores (older songs). That pretty much sums it up. She doesn't mix up the setlist by alternating new material with the old. 

David T. Walker and his group opened, then were the backing band for Carole. The band included Charles Larkey on electric bass, formerly of the Myddle Class, the Fugs, Jo Mama, the City with CK, and who was CK's husband at this time.
