Notes:

THE BUENAVENTURA LIMITED SERIES, VOL. 25

With much gratitude to the generosity of buenaventura, we continue to bring you this limited series with more wonderful material to counterbalance the so far terrible '20s.

This limited series is being so coined because there isn't a deluge of tapes coming through for this right now, and the scope of it is still undetermined, but that could potentially change at any moment. There seems to be enough on tap at present to keep all following the series with enough new sounds to take in for at least the next few weeks. 

Switching it up just slightly this time, still staying in Köln, but this time at E-Werk for a set from the Breeders.

This show finds the band promoting the "Last Splash" album, and although they were performing a similar set of songs each night, they would change out the placement some. "Pod" gets a substantial amount of representation here, with "Only in 3's" performed live, which didn't make that much of an appearance in their live 1993 sets, or maybe those shows just don't circulate much. Maybe you can all fix that...

Sound is of buenaventura's usual excellent standards, although this one is slightly anemic in the bass, but it is clear. "Lime House" doesn't make it past the tape flip, and "Painter" cuts in, but otherwise, you get every bit of Kim and Kelly fidgeting around on stage between numbers, and Kim's dependably entertaining audience interactions. 

Enjoy,
elegymart
