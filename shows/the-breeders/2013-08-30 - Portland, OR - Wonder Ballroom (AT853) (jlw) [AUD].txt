NOTES: Sold-out show.  I had wanted to get to the Wonder Ballroom for some time and it's a great venue situated in almost in a neighborhood of PDX and appears to be an old church with hardwood floors, a tall ceiling and even a balcony.  A really great place and it even has a bar and restaurant in the basement.  Unfortunately they don't have a huge sound system which seems customary in even small places now and it wasn't quite as loud as the other Breeders shows we went to this year.  This one was the best by far of the 3 northwest shows we went to (PDX, Van, Sea) just in terms of the crowd response and how much fun it looked like the band were having on stage.  Been a Breeders fan since Pod in 1990 and seen them in several incarnations of the band ('93, '08) and this was the best...they are really playing great this tour.
