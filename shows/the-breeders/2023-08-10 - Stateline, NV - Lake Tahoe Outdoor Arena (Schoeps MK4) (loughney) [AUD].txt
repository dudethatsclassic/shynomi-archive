The Breeders - 2023 08 10 @ Lake Tahoe Outdoor Arena, Stateline NV

Note:
I thought the Breeders were a good choice for an opening band. Coincidentally, I saw the Breeders open for Nirvana 30 years ago, so it was a nice loop. Had to move a little bit as there was a group of some very drunk dudes behind us, but luckily they aren't too disruptive.  Cool to have Dave join in on Gigantic as Nirvana cited the Pixies as an influence - it was pretty loose and you can hear Kim break into laughter at Dave's clowning.
