Notes:

I have been taping shows now for 28 years (hard to believe) and when I look
back at some of my earliest recordings, this is top 10. I was absolutely
enamored with the "No Name Face" album in 2001 and was so looking forward to
their headlining concert in Pontiac on September 12th that year. Sadly, due to
the 9/11 attacks, the show got postponed 3 months later to December. Because
of this, I actually went to see their show in Cleveland in October, because I
didn't want to wait any longer! However, my CSB mics were being repaired at
the time, so I was unable capture that one. But, I had them back by December!
This show had everything, no pun intended! The full NNF album performed in its
entirety + 2 brand new songs then unreleased. "Take Me Away" later appeared on
"Stanley Climbfall" in 2002, whereas "Climb" was never released. Such a killer
song, so that still makes no sense to me. The sound was decent for CC (better
than it was when Lifehouse returned in 2003, anyway), and the crowd (while
loud at times) did STFU during the music. My friend Ryan recently discovered
my master minidiscs in storage and did a fresh transfer for me. To my ears,
this sounds even better than the 2001 transfer and I wanted to reshare.

Nailing Betty opened. [not recorded].
