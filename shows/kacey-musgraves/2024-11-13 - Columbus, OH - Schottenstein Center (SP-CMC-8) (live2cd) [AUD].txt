My first Kacey show in 5 years. Blame COVID and her only booking 14 shows
for "star-crossed" in 2022 and there you have it. I really did not connect
with that album, so I was OK missing the tour. However, "Deeper Well" is a
welcome return to form for Kacey (in my opinion), so it was with great joy
getting a tour announcement in my city earlier this year. The setlist was very
heavy Golden Hour and Deeper Well with a few other songs (and covers) sprinkled
in. She only performed 2 from her first 2 albums, which was kind of odd, but I
was more than happy with only getting 1 from 2021's "star-crossed". There are
so many gems from those first 2 albums I hope return someday. Some haven't been
performed since 2016! Crazy! This was recorded from the GA floor and for the
most part I had great neighbors. I did use iZotope RX7 in between songs removing
some loud whoo's and screams when needed. However, for an arena recording of
quieter, mellow music - you really can't ask for a better capture. I still
think she belongs in larger theaters (and not arenas), but thats a discussion
for a different day. If you love Kacey (especially the new album), get this!!!
