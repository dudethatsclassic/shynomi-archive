Notes:

*  = Originally Recorded by Gloria Gaynor.
+  = Originally Recorded by Brooks & Dunn.

This show included the full performance of the "Golden Hour" record sprinkled
throughout the set. She only gently touched on her first 2 records performing 2 from
Same Trailer and 3 from Pageant. This was my 2nd show on the tour and it was just as
exciting as the first - even with the identical setlist. We had a polar vortex going
on outside as temperatures starting dropping down into the single digits (and were
negative by morning), so Kacey made mention of that at one point. She ended up
cancelling her next show in Chicago (scheduled for January 31st) due to the extreme
temperatures. For this show being GA and sold out, I was fearing it might not turn
out the greatest with a bunch of people crammed elbow to elbow in the pit - but I
got lucky with some very nice neighbors. I also stood slightly closer than normal
to ensure the quieter songs were not drowned out by crowd noise. One funny note
on the performance, Kacey's cover of "I Will Survive" was intended to be a duet
w/ Liza Anne, (much like it was with Natalie Prass earlier in the tour), but,
Liza never came out. It was her first night out with Kacey, so perhaps she forgot,
but Kacey ended up butchering one of the verses Liza would have sung. She covered
it up pretty well with some laughs, but you'll definitely hear it!
On post - I did raise some levels here and there to create a better listening 
experience when it came to the quieter performances. Turn this one up and get out
there and support Kacey in 2019!
