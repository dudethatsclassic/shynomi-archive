Notes:

*  Originally Recorded by Texas Tornados.
+  Originally Recorded by George Strait.

Kacey was last in Columbus at The Schottenstein Center just 2 years ago in November
2024 promoting her then wonderful album "Deeper Well". So, it was a bit of a shock
to see her back so soon with the follow-up, "Middle Of Nowhere", which has Kacey
returning to her country roots. Another shock was seeing her completely disregard
the "Deeper Well" material playing none of it in this current setlist. We did get
11 brand new MON tracks, so I cannot complain there. She also completely ignored
her "Pageant Material" record, which was strange considering those songs sit very
well in line with "Middle Of Nowhere". This concert was "in-the-round" with the
stage in the center of the arena (which featured the band) and a circular conveyor
belt-like stage on the outer rim for her to play from. Quite the production and
nothing like I've ever seen before. I went to this concert not knowing where the
stacks would be, so I found an empty in direct line with one of the 4 corner facing
stacks in 122. While the sound in The Schott could have been better (I did have to
run an EQ), the recording is fairly enjoyable and sounds much better now. I also
removed a few faint screams and yells in RX7 to improve the quality of playback.
