BBC Radio 2 Country is a four day pop-up digital radio service, which will run from midday to midnight from Thursday 10th to Sunday 13th March, centred around three days of live coverage from the C2C: Country to Country Festival in London

"Sporting giant earrings and a tiny skirt, Kacey Musgraves introduced herself as “a dumb little girl from East Texas”. Nobody was fooled for a second. Made-up like a beauty queen and, not uncoincidentally, promoting an album called Pageant Material, Musgraves brought a hint of art project to the evening, without undermining country’s innate sincerity. The 27-year-old swore; she joked that nobody would come to see “my saggy skin” when she’s performing as an eightysomething; and in the jaunty Biscuits she reminded us that “mind your own biscuits and life will be gravy”. Wise words indeed. 

Tellingly, when she covered Crazy, it wasn’t the Willie Nelson weeper but the more recent Gnarls Barkley hit. She delivered These Boots Are Made for Walkin’ sporting flashing boots and A Spoonful of Sugar with saccharine-free glee, but her own songs were smart, sweet and catchy. Her breakthrough hit, Merry Go Round, was a wise heartbreaker, the closest the evening came to providing a traditional country standard and it enticed a mass singalong."
