*  = Originally Recorded by Gloria Gaynor.

This show included the full performance of the "Golden Hour" record sprinkled
throughout the set. She only gently touched on her first 2 records performing 
2 from Same Trailer and 2 from Pageant. This was my 3rd show on this tour and
4th overall supporting Golden Hour and it was by far the best. Since Ive last
seen Kacey, shes won all kinds of awards and begun playing larger venues. You
can tell everyone in the crowd knew every word to the GH songs and listening
back its very evident. Earlier this year, (while the shows were still top
notch), this one had something extra special in the air. We had a beautiful 
night in the low 70s and some light wind. Thankfully, the wind doesnt interfere 
with the recording too much (altho you will hear some at the beginning of "Slow
Burn"). I had bought a ticket in the pit earlier this year when they went on
sale and decided it was too close during the opener's set. I watched the seats
and found an empty a few rows behind the pit in row H directly in line with
the left speaker stack. Luckily, no one in the seat to my right and no one in
the large aisle to my left. The crowd was very loud (applause and sing alongs),
but it doesnt ruin the recording in any way, since I had open space around me.
If only taping all shows could go this well. The show had been sold out for 
months in advance, so finding an empty in the venue's sweet spot was exciting. 
I offered my pit wristband to a girl in front of me, but she chose to stay in
the seats with her husband. One final note on the show, it appears Kacey has
ditched "Die Fun" from the sets since earlier this year. No idea why, but its
worth noting. Hopefully when the next album cycle comes around, we get to hear
more from Same Trailer and Pageant Material, as those records have largely been
looked over in favor of the popular Golden Hour material this run.
On post - I did raise some levels here and there to create a better listening
experience when it came to the quieter performances. Turn this one up and get out
there and support Kacey in 2019 and beyond!
