Show Notes: Kacey's second tour of Australia and her second time at the Palais, despite her claiming early in the show she'd never played the venue before (it was 6 years and a pandemic ago but still you'd think her people would have reminded her). Her second to last show of the Deeper Well era, and 2 songs shorter than the Brisbane and Sydney shows that preceded this - no SZA cover or an extra unplugged song those shows had for some reason. It was still an excellent show, with one of the most elaborate stage set ups I'd ever seen - The Palais looked like it'd been turned into a moss forest, and the balls dispersed into the audience during High Horse was particularly fun - this was definitely not a Garbage concert! While Kacey hasn't quite catapulted into the next level of stardom as I thought she might when I first saw her here in 2019, she still has a very devoted passionate fanbase. It'll be interesting to see where she goes in the next phase of her career.

Recording Notes: I was a few rows back in the balcony of the Palais and was a nice spot for a good capture. A quiet audience around me makes this a nice one to listen to. A few photos I took also included. Enjoy!
