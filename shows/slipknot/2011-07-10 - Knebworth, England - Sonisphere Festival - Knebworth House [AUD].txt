Headlining the third and final night of the UK Sonisphere 2011. This was the last night
of of a short tour to celebrate the life of Paul Gray who died in 2010. The band 
assembled at the front of the stage at the end of the show for an emotional show of
respect to him. They were joined for this by Donnie Steele, one of the founding members
who had switched from guitar to fill in on bass but had played the show from backstage.
