I've known about Slipknot for the last twenty years but I've never listened to a single song of theirs. I think in my head I was envisioning a lot of drumming and indistinguishable throat screaming and my buddy did as well so we agreed that if we weren't feeling it we'd head out early. We had been at the festival four days already so we were pretty beat. The second the curtain dropped I realized that every assumption I had about this band was wrong. I couldn't believe what I was seeing: they had a crazy LED stage setup with two drum-towers mounted on each side and pyrotechnics. I felt like I was at a Muse concert. I was completely floored: they were beyond entertaining and I wish I had given them a shot years ago. The lead singer had a great voice, each instrument came through very clearly, and the rest of the band was very engaging in their various roles. The lead singer had a lot of positive things to say about the crowd and the Midwest in general and seemed genuinely stoked to be there. Originally Nine Inch Nails was supposed to take that headlining slot which is the reason I bought tickets to the festival but I'm glad I got to see something new as I would have never gone to see them otherwise. If they ever roll through the Bay Area I'd definitely buy a ticket!
