Notes:

* "Pinhead" & "Blitzkrieg Bop" (Ramones) intro.

The first tour to feature Mark, Tom and Travis in over 8 years has certainly been
big news. Tom announced his return to Blink in 2022 with an arena tour following.
They are also working on releasing a new studio album this coming fall, which is
still yet to be officially announced. The song "EDGING" performed here will be
featured. Tom has mentioned to fans it will see release in October.

So, where to start? Toms back! I guess that was my draw to the show and a ton of
others, as well. It had to be, to damn near sell it out at a 15k cap! The last time
I saw Blink 182 was in 2017 on a stop in Columbus, OH with Matt Skiba on guitar.
The venue they were playing was a third of the size of PPG with GA pit and GA
lawn. It was still a fun show, but it was not the same without Tom. The last time
I saw the band with Tom was in 2001 on the TOYPAJ tour in Cuyahoga Falls. So, this
show for me was 22 years in waiting and did they ever deliver! The setlist was
nearly everything anyone could want from Blink focusing on the big 3 albums and
touching on others. My only (small) gripe would be absolutely nothing from 1995's
"Cheshire Cat", not even "Carousel", which used to be a concert staple! I do wish
they played one or two others from 1997's "Dude Ranch", but beggars can't be
choosers. I was hoping we would get "Not Now" (as they played it in Detroit the
week prior), but I don't think the band was happy with it. Maybe later in the tour
it will pop back up. Without question, one of my absolute favorite B182 tracks.

On the recording, I bought a single aisle seat a few weeks before the show as TM
opened up a row out of nowhere in direct line with the side facing stack. Section
121 is my favorite section to sit in at PPG, so it was hard to turn down the ticket.
Plus, the seats were slightly lower priced than the standard lower bowl as they were
side/rear stage. They were still expensive, just not $250 expensive. I got lucky with
an aisle seat closest to 122. This way, I eliminated possible direct noise to my left
and could step a little out into the aisle if I had anyone loud to my right.
Thankfully, the female to my right was very gracious and remained quiet after having
explained what I was doing during the set break. The only real issue I had was a few
fans in the row behind me, but thanks to the cardioid mics, they arent intrusive on
the recording. I also used RX7 to remove some "whooo's", random claps and a few
pyrotechnics that went off during the final 3 songs.

Mark gave a very emotional speech before "Adam's Song" and it was the most emotional
part of the evening. I did not expect to tear up at a Blink show, but I did. Please
share this with as many Blink fans or friends as you can! It was great getting to
see them again and hopefully they'll be back after the new album releases.

Beauty School Dropout [not recorded] and Turnstile [recorded] opened.
