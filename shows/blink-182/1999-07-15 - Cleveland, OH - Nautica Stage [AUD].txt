Notes:

My friend Kevin invited me to this last minute and I grabbed my gear (I was then using MD
with a handheld Sony mic) & we went on our way. We were mainly going to see Zebrahead and 
Blink. We arrived 10 minutes after ZH finished, so that was kind of a bummer. They had a 
noon set on Nautica Stage. I also remember seeing Ice-T, Eminem, Black Eyed Peas and some
other acts I was never really a fan of, but will never forget. Sevendust were on after 
Blink and I have their recording as well. I did not tape any other acts. 

I lost this recording to a bad CD-R about 5 or 6 years ago. My friend Michael Rainey went 
into his storage and pulled out another CD-R that actually played with no diginoise! I am 
grateful and thankful to him for both this and the Sevendust sets.
