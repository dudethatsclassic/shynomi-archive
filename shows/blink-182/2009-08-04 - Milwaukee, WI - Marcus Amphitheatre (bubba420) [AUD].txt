Notes:
This was my second time taping wih my core sound mics. I went to this show becuase my 13 year old son really wanted to see Blink 182 so i thought what the hell...i would take him and give the mics another "test run". The sound in the venue wasn't the greatest, it was pretty bassy for the other acts but not to bad for Blink 182 which i thought had the best sound of the night. At times the levels are a bit hot, mostly inbetween songs from the crowd. After mastering this i think overall this still sounds pretty good, better then the old Sony ECM-719's i used to use before this. I am pretty sure nobody else taped this show, if they did they haven't stepped forword and said they have or circulated it. A big thanks goes out to brad for transfering my DAT master.
