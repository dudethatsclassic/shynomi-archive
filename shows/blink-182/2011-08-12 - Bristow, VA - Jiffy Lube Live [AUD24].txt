MISC NOTES:
I finally saw Blink 182!  Although I had lawn seats, I think that this recording came out fairly well, considering.  There is some strange sound mix up's towards the end of the set and to be quite honest I'm really not quite sure what caused it... I'm suspecting that it might have had something to do with batteries since the problems seem to get worse as the show goes on, but even at their worst I don't think that they seriously disrupt enjoyment of the recording.  

Blink 182 was the headlining act on this tour.  Before them, My Chemical Romance played for about an hour (I also recorded those guys), and prior to MCR, Manchester Orchestra played (I missed them due to a late arrival).  All in all I think that Blink definitely put on the better show of the two acts I saw, and they were definitely the crowd favorite.  

Thanks to the users of setlist.fm for posting an accurate setlist so soon after the show ended!  Thanks also to Scott for bringing me along!
