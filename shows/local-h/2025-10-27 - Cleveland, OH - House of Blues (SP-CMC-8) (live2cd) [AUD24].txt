Local H is second billing on the big Everclear Sparkle And Fade 30th
Anniversary tour and are being given 45 minutes each night. To say
they are killing it, is putting it mildly. Forgoing most of their 90s
material (at least in this set), they focused on a lot of the up-tempo
songs from the best of their 00's output. It's great to see a band play
a "90's tour" (lets call it what it is) and then watch them blow people
away with what they've done long after terrestrial radio and MTV cared
to pay attention. Without question, one of the best opening sets I've
seen from the band - high marks!

Ronnie DiCola was featured on drums.
