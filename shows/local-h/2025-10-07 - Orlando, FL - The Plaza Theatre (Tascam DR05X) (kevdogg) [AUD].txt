Notes: 
Opening for Everclear on the Sparkle & Fade 30th anniversary tour. Sponge opened for Local H.
All were recorded. After encountering some mic connection static during Sponge's intro music
I decided to switch to the internals for Local H set. The deck was placed on the rail/shelf thing
that runs along the length of row M and it's about waist level while standing. In Audition I gave a
subtle increase in the 2khz range with the EQ and a hard limit to -.1db with a 9db boost. I am quite
pleased with the results. This was sadly my first Local H show and I was impressed. Better late
than never though. The guys were hanging with the crowd right after their set and were signing
and doing photos at the merch table after the show. Good peoples for sure. Don't miss a chance
to see them, it's a great performance. Cover art is included in the torrent. Enjoy the show.
