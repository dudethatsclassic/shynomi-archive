Scott performed solo acoustic prior to the taping of the Local H LIFERS Podcast
taping on New Years Day. They scheduled 2 tapings, one on the 1st and the 2nd
on the 2nd. I honestly did not know how many songs he would perform, so getting
9 in 30 minutes was a pleasant surprise. I only expected 2 or 3 to be played.
I held the A10 up and pointed at the right stack for the entire 30 minutes, so
my arm was a little tired by the time this was over. I thought it turned out
fairly good. I did hard limit the audience applause in between songs as it was
spiking higher than the performance. Playback is much smoother now.
