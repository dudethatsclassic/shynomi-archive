Before heading back to Chicago to perform their Dumb & Dumber themed NYE show,
the band treated Milwaukee to a warm up NYE "preview". This was the second of 
two nights at Shank Hall. Night 1 was the "encore" (final) 20th anniversary 
performance of "Here Comes The Zoo", which they had toured on all fall. To my
ears, night 2 was a tad bit more bass heavy, so I did the best I could in Audition
to reduce, but its still fairly heavy. Please adjust accordingly!
