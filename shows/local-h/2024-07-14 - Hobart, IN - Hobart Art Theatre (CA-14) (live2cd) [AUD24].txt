Notes:

*  Originally Recorded By Oasis.

After getting rained out 50 minutes into their set the night prior,
Local H played thier 2nd show this weekend in Hobart in the 83 year old
Art Theatre. Thankfully, this was inside, because - yes, it did rain again.
The setlist featured some great stuff from the WHTPJS record including
"Mellowed" (which was cut the night prior), "P.J. Soles", "Weather" &
one of my favorite closers they have, "That's What They All Say". The only
song that out of place in the set was their cover of the Oasis B-Side,
"Acquiesce". I happen to love the tune, so it was exciting to see, but I'm
fairly positive I was the only one who did know it. Also great seeing them
start the set with a heavy rendition of "User". That song is not played
enough. The sound was typical for an old theater - boomy with some
reverberation, but it sounds good enough. The show was sold out, so we were
packed in tight. 

Killium & Atonement opened. [not recorded].
