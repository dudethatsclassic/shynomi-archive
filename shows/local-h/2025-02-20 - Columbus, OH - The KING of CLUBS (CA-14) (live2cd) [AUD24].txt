This venue generally does not sound too great (the sound reverberates badly off
of the walls), so I recorded this fairly close in line with the left stack and
it turned out good (listenable). IMO, The setlist was amazing. Getting to hear
"JTBB", "Buffalo Trace" and "TWTAS" in one set is always a treat not to mention
"Scott-Rock" making an appearance. The tags during the 2nd part of the set were
real fun to hear, as well. Thanks to TKOC for being so accommodating with the
recording and thanks to the band, as always.

Ronnie DiCola was featured on drums.

This Day Forever and Cream Camino opened. [both not recorded].
