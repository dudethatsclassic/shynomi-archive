My 2nd chance getting to see Local H with the Toadies on their 2026 tour.
Local H played the same setlist as Cincinnati, which was the AGAD set.
While we did not get any tracks past 1996, I'll never be upset at the
chance at hearing songs like FDF, Lovey Dovey and MD2. Those are not
played all that often, so no complaints from me!! Plus, you know, its
been 30 years. The recording went well, but Local H got absolutely hosed
on the sound. This happened in Cincinnati, as well. If you listen to my
Toadies recordings (from Cincinnati or on this same night), you'll wonder
if they were in a different venue - the difference is really that vast.
From what I understand, Local H are using the house guys on this tour
and the Toadies are brining their own crew with their own board. I guess
that makes all the difference! Is this total shit? No way. Local H
sound amazing and powerful as ever. It is fairly muddy and due to the
majority of the sound coming from the stage (not the PA!?!?), the
recording came out very left channel heavy facing the right stack.
I did my best to balance this in post. Scott's vocals are also pretty
drowned out at times - but that "MD2" closer? It's worth cranking to 11.

Ronnie DiCola was featured on drums.

Opening for Toadies [recorded], Olive Vox were also on the bill. [not recorded].
