Notes:

Local H in Chicago for their annual New Years Eve show. This time, it was
Pee-wee's Big Adventure themed, so Scott came out dancing as Pee-wee and
things got started from there! This was also a special show as Local H
featured both drummers: Ryan Harding & Ronnie DiCola (who has been playing
the majority of the shows since late May) in place of Ryan who was on
paternity leave. It was fun watching them play while watching each other to
stay in sync. Local H also brought back "Blur" for the first time in 13
years as well as "Skid Marks" for the first time in 6 years! Incredible!
During "Wolf Like Me" I had to move the stand about 5 ft over to the right
as a mosh pit broke out in the center of the floor. I moved it back and it
happened again during "High-Fiving MF". The sound changes slightly when
this happens, but its not all that noticeable. Thankfully, the stand did
not get bumped at all. Also note, at the end of "Cha!", Scott had some
issues with his rig and spent a few minutes trying to repair it. He later
told me it never got fixed, but I honestly couldn't tell during the show.
