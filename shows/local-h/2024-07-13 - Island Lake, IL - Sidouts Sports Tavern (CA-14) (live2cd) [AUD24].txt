Notes:

Local H performed here in 2023 and the show was so great, Sideouts decided to 
bring them back! The only problem was, we had some rain - actually it was more 
like a downpour. The venue was outside (behind the tavern) and sounded great!
They had a large firepit on the ground that I was able to use to mount my stand
behind. Made it super easy to tape with no threat of people bumping into it. 
This rousing version of "John The Baptist Blues" may have been the strongest 
version I have heard since 2017 or so. Very impressive. The rest of the set
was very single heavy with "Bakesale" and "Buffalo Trace". The rain started
during "Buffalo" and started getting really heavy midway in. By the end it was
a full on downpour with some running for cover inside. The band quickly left
the stage and said they would be back with an extra long encore. Sadly, it kept
raining and they called it about an hour later. The songs on the set that were 
cut were as follows: "Mellowed", "I Saw What You Did", "Hands On The Bible", 
"Bound" and "That's What They All Say". Glass 1/2 full, we got a great sounding
capture with a ton of energy. We had some fireworks going off early on, I used
RX7 to remove those pops when necessary.

Radar Waves opened. [recorded].
