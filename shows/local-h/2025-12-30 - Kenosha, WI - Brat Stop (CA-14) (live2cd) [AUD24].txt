This place did not sound the greatest, so this is what it is. I mounted
the stand to the right of the soundboard area and raised it to roughly
8 ft and hoped for the best. Due to the size of the crowd, I could not
get any closer if I tried. Local H started the show with 3 straight
Cheap Trick covers paying homage to the band who had gotten their start
on this very stage playing there many times in the 70s, 80s and 90s!
During "Bound", we had a guy jump on stage and stagedive (with nobody
catching him) and it ended up going viral a few days later. This is only
the audio, so you may want to look up the video of that, pretty funny
stuff. I loved this show, just wish it sounded better! 

Thanks as always to the band for the taping permission and for the media pass.
