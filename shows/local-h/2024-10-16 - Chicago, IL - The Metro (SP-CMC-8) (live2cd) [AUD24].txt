"WHTPJS 20th" tour grand finale at Chicago's fabled Metro. Sold out. No opener,
all Local H for nearly 3 hours. They did break after the acoustic set for 15
minutes. Just an incredible show! One of their very best. The tour that preceded
this final performance did not include the additional musicians who guested here,
nor were the sets as long. The album performed this way is the closest live
representation of the album there has been. If you're a fan of the band, this
is a must listen. Because I had the mics high above the floor and out of ear
shot from the crowd in the balcony above, the sound is quite good. Huge thanks
goes out to Ryan Teske for assistance with setting up the gear (we ran 2 rigs)
and for grabbing the center balc spot when doors opened!

Thanks as always to the band for the taping permission and for the media pass.
