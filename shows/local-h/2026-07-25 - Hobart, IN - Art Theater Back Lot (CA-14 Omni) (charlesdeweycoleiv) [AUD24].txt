The Art Theater Back Lot is located in a parking lot on the Lake George
waterfront in downtown Hobart. This was H's 2nd year in a row headlining
the Art Theater's Lakefront Series. We had cloudy skies, but thankfully
no rain and temperatures were around 80, so all in all a nice night to
see the band. They only have 4 (FOUR!) headline gigs booked so far in
2026, so this was a rare treat - which is why I made the drive. I had a
fan in front of me with a huge sign that read BMW MAN!!! Scott must've
seen it because they did in fact play it (or attempt it, rather) as it
has not been played live yet with Ronnie. The song was cut short by
about a minute, but it was for sure the surprise of the night and you can
hear him and his friends going nuts when Scott launched into it. Gotta
love those moments. My favorite part of the show was opening with JTBB
into BITD and then ISWYD. What an all out assault to start the set!!!
Also - the "Shock The Monkey" interlude in HFMF was also awesome and just 
as surprising as "BMW Man" earlier on. The show came out a little bass 
heavy, so I did EQ quite a bit in post to improve the overall sound. We 
did have a loud crowd, so Im glad I had the omnis high above the crowd.
Huge thanks to the band and to the Art Theater's amazing staff for being
so welcoming with the stand and bag full of gear at check in.

Ronnie DiCola was featured on drums.

Chasing Allura, Fear And Grace & Gerhart opened. [none recorded].
