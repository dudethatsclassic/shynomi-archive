+  = Features a "T.V. Eye" tag (The Stooges).

Before their tour with the Toadies began, Local H announced there was a
chance they might play "As Good As Dead" in select cities for the 30th
anniversary in their opening slot. While they did not truly play the album
in it's entirety (missing was "No Problem" & "O.K."), they did play everything
else in Cincinnati. They did this the night prior in Cleveland, as well.
Not sure how many other cities will get the AGAD treatment, but it was
certainly a pleasant surprise. Unfortunately, Local H's set was riddled with
sound issues (it was at it's worst during the first 3 songs), and once the
weird ringy bass sound went away, while better, it was still very muddy
compared to The Toadies set. It's still very enjoyable, I just wanted to
make a note of it. The Toadies set was also quite a bit louder as I had to
turn down the gain on the PR-2 during their first song. Dont worry, there
was no clipping or distortion of any kind on either recording.

Ronnie DiCola was featured on drums.

Opening for Toadies [recorded], Vandoliers were also on the bill. [not recorded].
