Notes:

Local H in Chicago for their annual New Years Eve show. They did a few weeks of
dates in September and October performing their 2004 album, "Whatever Happened
To P.J. Soles?" finishing at Chicago's Metro with a bunch of additional musicians.
The show was easily one of the band's best, so they decided to do it one more
time for New Years Eve! So, this show starts with WHTPJS, then there is a 15
min set break (removed here) and continues just before midnight with the 2025
New Years countdown. "Hey Hey, My My (Into The Black)" makes its debut in a
Local H setlist and "President Forever" returns for the first time since the
drive in shows in the summer of 2020.

Thanks as always to the band for the taping permission and for the media pass.
