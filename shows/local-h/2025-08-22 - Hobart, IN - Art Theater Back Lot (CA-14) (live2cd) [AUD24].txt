The Art Theater Back Lot is located in a parking lot on the Lake George
waterfront in downtown Hobart. This show was a nice change of pace from
the year prior which was inside the art theater and HOT. It felt like it
was 100 degrees in there! We had beautiful weather with temps in the mid
70s at showtime. Couldn't have asked for a better night. There were some
douchebags in the crowd up front, but thankfully they are not audible on
the recording. I guess I had the mics high enough! We had some minor mosh
pits but they never came near where I had my stand. Got lucky. This was
the live debut of "Rip This Joint" in a Local H set. Scott and Ronnie
sounded great. Turn this up!

Ronnie DiCola was featured on drums.

Chasing Allura, Seldom Told & Atonemento opened. [none recorded].
