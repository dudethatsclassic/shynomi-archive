Local H in Chicago for their annual New Years Eve show. This time, it was
DUMB & DUMBER themed, so Scott and Ryan played their parts as Harry & Lloyd
(quite well, I might add). It definitely makes it a fun listen. They also
cherry picked some great tunes from the OST, leaving out the most popular
Deadeye Dick song to much dismay. The venue was PACKED, not sure if it was
sold out, but the crowd went all the way to the back and wall to wall. There
was also a circle pit in the center of the floor (Im not kidding), but -
thankfully, I was far enough off to the right, I did not get bumped (or taken
down), which did happen to me once years ago. It was loud in there and I was
close, so I went with the omnis and thought it turned out fairly good to
great. Turn it up and enjoy the place where the beer flows like wine & where
beautiful women instinctively flock like the salmon of Capistrano. Enjoy!
