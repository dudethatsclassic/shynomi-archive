Opening for Everclear on the Sparkle & Fade 30th anniversary tour. Sponge opened for Local H.

and side fills along the walls and ceiling. I'm looking forward to catching some more good bands
at this place. In Audition I gave a subtle increase in the 2-6.3khz range with the EQ and a hard limit
to -.1db with a 8db boost. I am quite pleased with the results. Cover art is included in the torrent.
