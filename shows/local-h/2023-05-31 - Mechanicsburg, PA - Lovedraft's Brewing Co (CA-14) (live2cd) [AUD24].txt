Notes:

Local H are primarily taking 2023 off to allow drummer Ryan Harding time
to be with his family. However, there have been a few dates here and there
scheduled. Ryan has played two of them so far in Chicago (Metro and
Liars) and will be performing in Island Lake in July. Local H booked
this 2 show trip (headlining in Mechanicsburg and opening for LIVE in
Bethlehem) and Ronnie DiCola filled in for Ryan at both. Considering he
picked the setlist, I have to give it to him. Most fill in drummers wouldn't
dare pick songs like "Valley", "Defy", "Leon", "Cynic" or even set opener,
"Patrick Bateman". He did a tremendous job and makes this show that much
more fun to listen back to. Great job, Ronnie!!

Anthrophobia opened the show [recorded].
