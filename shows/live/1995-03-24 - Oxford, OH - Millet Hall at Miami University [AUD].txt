Notes :
This is a pretty rare audience recording from the US leg of the "Throwing Copper" tour.
It sounds very good for it's age. (The very first song doesn't sound that great, but the rest of the show is VG+ quality).

I've been an avid Live (the band) concert recordings collector in the 90's and this one has always been one of my favourites because of the amazing preformance the band delivered that night !
