This is my first time seeing the band since they reunited and they were great as expected although I'm disappointed in their setlist, and them playing 2 covers and then not playing a single track off their debut album which is my absolute favorite of theirs. 2 acoustic tracks back to back derailed the flow of the set too, they need a pick me up song before LC. They played second on this night so not sure if the set usually has something else in the mix if they go last, I never bothered to look. I would have liked a track from The Turn but LOL, that was never going to happen. 

BTW, the place does have some stupid corporate name for the stadium, but I will never call it by that name. The Wisconsin Timber Rattlers are a minor league baseball team for the Milwaukee Brewers (and the team was called the Appleton Foxes in the past and was once an  affiliate of the White Sox and the Mariners and possibly some others)

I never have much luck in huge outdoor shows for sound quality but this is about as good as I could expect to pull, pretty happy with it.
