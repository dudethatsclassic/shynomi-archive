Notes:

The taper has his own method when he records and tracks live during the 
recording on the iPhone 4, so there are cuts in between almost every song. 
This is why you will hear fast cuts after songs are finished and some missing
crowd noise. He then distrubutes the recordings in mp3 format only. I asked 
him to send me the master wave file and instead received 27 separate tracks 
with tracked intros and talking tracks. I decided to merge all 27 and track 
song by song as I normally would. I also cleaned up some loud "whoo's" and 
screams in RX7 for him. They were spiking the wave files so much, it was hard 
to listen to during playback while turning the volume up. I really do think this
is a wonderful capture and wanted it to sound the best it possibly could in
lossless. Thanks again to Josh for providing the capture!
