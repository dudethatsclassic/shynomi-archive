Notes:

^  Includes Band Introductions.

Recording wise, Live's sound was slightly better than STP's and the recording
definitely reflects that. However, one minute into the first song "Top", we
had some row shuffling going on with the usher checking everyone's ticket with
a bunch of people arriving fashionably late. I had to walk out into the aisle
and back in again. By the time "All Over You" starts, I was back into my seat
and the recording sounds normal again. This only really goes on for a minute
and a half but please keep it in mind when listening! One of the highlights
for me was hearing them perform "Lady Bhang (She Got Me Rollin')" as its a
brand new track and breathed some life into the set. Ed's band is really
firing on all cylinders and they really do put on a great show. I wish the
original lineup were still together, but this is the next best thing. Try to
keep an open mind if you do go to see them! Turn it up and share!

Co-headline with Stone Temple Pilots [recorded] and opener,
