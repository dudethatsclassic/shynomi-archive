Taping Position: Dead-Center Mezzanine Seating
Gen: 0
Taper: MITAPER78

Notes 2025: My buddy and I were not fans of Live and we were a bit disrespectful to the band as we sat waiting for Jane's Addiction. Myself and my friend openly talk and laugh a few times. Truth is, looking back and listening to this I really wish we would have just not been talking and just appreciated the music. But hey, we were young and stupid. The recording is distant and certainly not an audiophile recording. Again, the dumbasses talking really made this a very amateurish recording and the only reason this is being released for the first time in 2025 is because I wanted to release the entire capture, warts and all, of the Jane's Addiction show 24 years ago. If you are a fan of this band, much apologies. I would say that *this* source would be for hardcore collectors of Live fans only.
