Originally, I seeded this recording incorrectly tracked, due to my error tracking in the Samplitude stage of my work flow. I corrected this by splitting tracks 07 and 08 in cdwav, renaming the new files tracks 07a, 07b, 08a and 08b. Because many people had already downloaded most of the original fileset, and no downloads were finished, I did not change any of the tags, so now some track names and numbers will be inaccurate; I recommend immediately stripping the tags in a media player (which is usually as simple as a menu command or keystroke). The reason I left these erroneous tags in place is so this source will be a quick download for the initial peers; when joining this seed, point your client to save at the same location as the original source, and you will only need to download tracks 07a, 07b, 08a, 08b, this .txt file, and the new fingerprints. I apologize for the inconvenience, but I thought it best to pull the source before it got out, and I have tried to make it as painless as possible for those that were first to be keen on listening to the music.
