Gotta love Brummie humour - at the end of Can't Get You Out Of My Head, someone shouts 'Show us your arse' at Wayne in tribute to Kylie Minogue.

As you'll hear, the crowd were seated and very quiet between songs, not the usual raucous atmosphere of Lips shows. Towards the end of the show, Wayne seemed to become increasingly frustrated by this.
