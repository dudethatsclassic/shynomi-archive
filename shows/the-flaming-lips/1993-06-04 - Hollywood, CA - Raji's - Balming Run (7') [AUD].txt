Tracklist:
1. Turn It On
2. She Don't Use Jelly

Notes: A bootleg pressed EP of 2 live tracks pressed in 1994.
Strangly, this record was made by "Skin Graft Records - Poland." Skin Graft Records is a legitimate business here in the US and the Official Flaming Lips webpage's discography had it listed for a few months. Sounds to me, like the Lips taped their show and tried to make an extra few bucks on the 2 promo'd singles.
This is on a really vile pink vinyl, and from the Raji's show that does the rounds on the tapes. The sound quality is kind of variable but it's a good performance none the less. Not sure I would've bothered making a record out of it, but there you go - it's got great guitar noises on it, so you get over the sound!
