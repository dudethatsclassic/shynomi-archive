The Flaming Lips - 2023 08 20 @ Stern Grove, San Francisco CA

Note:
Stern Grove Festival. Warm, sunny day in San Francisco - an excellent way to close out the Stern Grove Festival for the year. Had a big group together to enjoy the show from the lawn. Lots going on and off stage with pink robots, balloons in the audience, confetti guns, etc.  Got bumped a few times by baloons during "She Don't Use Jelly" which are slightly audiable.  Before Alan Palomo's DJ set, SF Mayor London Breed declared "Flaming Lips Day", which I included at the beginning. Wayne sings along to Okie from Muskogee - the line about San Francisco ...
