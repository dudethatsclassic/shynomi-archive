Another one from the early FLIPS. Guess this doesn't circulate too much, as it is a real raw
audience recording. The overall sound sucks, as it is very muddy and every attempt to increase
the higher levels resulted in higher hiss. So, I didn't do much on this. Hope you like it 
anyway, cause it's a good one, stating from musical view. This is the last one of that little
series, I hope you enjoyed it. SB. Oh, almost forgot...this was made at one of my favourite
venues (still active) in Germany, the lovely Fabrik (Factory) in Altona, Hamburg.
