This show was held on the same night as Brisbane's Riverfire Festival - our annual night of corporate fireworks and military flyovers.
Hundreds of thousands of Brisbanites flock into the city and pack the riverfront areas to watch this.
Why? Good question.
Wayne mentions at the start how the lads had seen some of this happening outside beforehand.
The venue is close to part of the Brisbane river which is the backdrop of the fireworks.
As usual, Wayne repeatedly prompts everyone to scream thoughout the show.
Thankfully i managed to avoid being too close to those that partook.
You'll hear lots of balloon pops and the hissing of the confetti machines though.
