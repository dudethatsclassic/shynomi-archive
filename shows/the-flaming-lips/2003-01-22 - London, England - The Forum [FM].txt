THE FOLLOWING IS A REVIEW OF THE SHOW POSTED BY THESTEREOEFFECT.COM

The Flaming Lips 
Live @ The Forum, London 
22 Jan 2003
 
"You are about to witness the most spectacular thing you have ever seen..." proclaims the flickering projection behind the stage, overstatement possibly, or even irony perhaps, but over the next 90 minutes it's hard to disagree with Wayne Coyne's endearing statement of intent. A statement that's been slowly growing since 1999's "The Soft Bulletin" was taken to the road. First there were nun puppets and fake blood, then the big screens and glitter. When the Lips played the Astoria last year the odd animal suit was spotted on the stage but nothing too untoward. This time round though it's such an overwhelming assault on the senses it's almost too much to take in. Oh, and it's a freak show, a flamingly overwrought freak show.

"Race For The Prize", quite possibly the most emotion rush inducing and just damn exciting track to open a live show you could imagine explodes across the Forum, glitter flies, giant Pandas dance. Wayne seems so excited swinging his giant flashlight above his head he barely makes it to the mike for the first line. Fischerspooner can be heard whimpering in the corner, twenty fans who'd turned up to watch the show are now part of it, on stage and dancing like E'd up goons. Just another night for Wayne Coyne then. Oh, and is that the Easter Bunny? And, er, Father Christmas? Yes, and yes again. 

Much of "Yoshimi Battles The Pink Robots" and "The Soft Bulletin" gets aired, Wayne's strap-on strobe lights get donned during the aural cacophony of "Lightning Strikes The Postman", the usual Teletubby loops get projected during "A Spoonful Weights A Ton" - the bass led freakout as trainwrecked as ever.

As with support act British Sea Power however, this is all merely show. Nothing but eye-candy, visual popcorn to be consumed during the performance. Take it all away and The Flaming Lips would still be one of the most arresting and exciting live bands operating on the planet and although "Yoshimi..." may be a more subdued recording than the unsurpassable "The Soft Bulletin" it's best offerings produce no-less of a reaction from the foaming crowd - "Do You Realize?" has already become a staggering live favorite. 

When they finally leave the stage after a startling encore of "What Is Light/The Observer" it's like the Ark is about to depart; Pandas, Dogs, Elephants, Kangaroos, Rabbits and Tigers traipsing off into the darkness. As the tour wraps up what we wanna know it just where the hell do they go next? With the live show already resembling a Las Vegas traveling circus the mind boggles at Coyne's next move, foam machines? Roller coasters? Free hallucinogens for all comers? The thinking does indeed go on, you can almost hear that mans brain ticking over, calculating the next move as he leaves the stage.
