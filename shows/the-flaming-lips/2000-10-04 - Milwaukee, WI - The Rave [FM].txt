Last show of the American leg of the Soft Bulletin Tour.  Recorded from the out of the headphones set the band handed out to audience members (so the audience can listen to a perfect soundboard mix while they watch the show) into a minidisc recorder.  We only rarely traded this, but I did upload 128 kps mp3s to one of the two Flaming Lips FTPs ten years ago.  But here is a rip of our master CD.  

A great performance, including a rare Zaireeka track closing the set.  During the middle of Spiderbite Song, the entire audience was completely quiet to give Wayne full attention.  Just as Wayne steps up to the mic, my friend (we were in the front row) plaintively says "Wayne, you're bleeding." and Wayne starts to laugh just as he starts singing, which is audible on the recording!  Side note, this was just when the band began to spread the word to dress up to the shows, and our friend indeed did dress as a duck, making him one of the very first to dress in animal costume for a Flaming Lips show.
