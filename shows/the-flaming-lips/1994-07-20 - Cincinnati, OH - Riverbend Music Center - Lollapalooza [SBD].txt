This is an excellent quality, rarely circulated soundboard recording from the Lollapalooza tour.  We all owe rOCKY a debt of gratitude for donating it to the Brain.  His CD version came directly from the original soundboard DAT.  This show features a rare taped performance of Slow Nerve Action while Ronald was still in the band, as well as a unique ending to the set, with Chewin' The Apple Of Your Eye segueing into The Process.
