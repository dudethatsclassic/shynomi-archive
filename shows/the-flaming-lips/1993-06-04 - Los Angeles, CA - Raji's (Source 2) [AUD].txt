Taper's Notes:
This was an interesting show and a privilege to attend.  I had just gotten back from a tour & 
had gotten an advance copy of the "Transmissions from the Satellite Heart" album, which we 
played constantly for the last few days of our trip.  The night after we got back, I went out to
Raji's to see my friends the Lazy Cowgirls, and was told when I got in that the Flaming Lips were
going to play, unannounced, at the end of the night (ended up begining around 2am).  I'm sure I 
didn't believe it until I saw the giant road cases being loaded through the tiny back doors.  
They didn't go on till almost 2AM and they let 'em keep going for about 40 minutes.  Luckily 
(anonymous) was taping and the results are in your hands.
