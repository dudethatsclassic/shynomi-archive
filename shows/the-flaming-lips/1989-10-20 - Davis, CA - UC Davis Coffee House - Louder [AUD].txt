Notes:

There's a tape flip between tracks 8 and 9.

Incredible show. The Coffee House wasn't a particularly large room, and there weren't very many people in attendance at this show (I'm guessing in the neighborhood of maybe 50 people). The band quickly filled the room with the product of their fog machine(s), which was helped by the low ceilings in the room. They also had a few painfully bright white lights set up behind or next to the drum kit pointing back at the crowd. What I remember most about this show is being immersed in fog so thick you practically had to chew it (I was smelling that stuff for days afterward, and to this day when I encounter "fake fog smell" I think of the Lips), being hit by blinding bright lights occasionally, and it being loud as fuck. It was heaven.
