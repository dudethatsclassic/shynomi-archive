This is an excellent quality, rarely circulated soundboard recording from the end of the Transmissions From The Satellite Heart tour.  We all owe rOCKY a debt of gratitude for donating it to the Brain.  His CD version came directly from the original soundboard DAT.  The show includes all 7 commonly played Transmissions songs during this era.  Turn It On, Moth In The Incubator, Bad Days, Superhumans, She Don't Use Jelly, and Chewin' The Apple Of Your Eye had previously circulated from this performance from an FM broadcast on Westwood One on February 12, 1996.
