Show Notes: 
The sun was down and made a perfect setting for Wayne and the guys to start the show. 
At first Wayne came out as they were setting up the 30'x30' video screen to test the mics. 
The crowd showed their appreciation with gracious cheers.  Then the balloons began to fly.  
About 300 or so 4' diameter balloons came out from behind the stage and were floating all 
around the crowd.  There were even some 8' diameter balloons floating and wobbling around 
the crowd.  Next were the smoke and the people wearing stuffed animal costumes from the 
‘Do you Realize’ video.  Wayne really got the crowd amped and singing along to the words, 
especially when he got into his bubble ball and rolled into the crowd.  I'd have to say 
that these guys stole the show for the whole weekend ATP festival.  They played a great 
set and their theatrics didn’t end till the show was over. 

The show was excellent and it sounds excellent, it was a good venue for recording.
 
Hope you enjoy this as much as I have!
