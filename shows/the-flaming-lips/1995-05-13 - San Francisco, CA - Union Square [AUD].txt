I don't know the original source for this show.  I have only seen it in circulation from one person on the internet, with whom I traded for it.  He says that the person he got it from received it directly from Ronald Jones.  Good audience recording sound quality, with some distortion on the lower end.  The show is a fun performance and the setlist has a few rarely played songs/arrangements, including an incredible version of Everyone Wants To Live Forever.  

This was a free show put on by Live 105 in San Francisco's Union Square.  Photos of it can be seen in the Waking Up With A Placebo Headwound book from pages 104-106.  Steven Drozd comments, "Free shows in a park are usually a bust, but this one in downtown San Francisco turned out to be quite fun.  I think we were surprised so many people came and stayed."  

A user on Youtube videotaped the show but has never traded it.  A video playlist of almost the entire show can be viewed at http://
