I believe I've seen the Flaming Lips every time they've played in Indiana, as far back as the Patio in Indy in 1994.  This was the first time I taped them, and the first time I'd seen them since that '94 show and the major part of their rise to huge success.  Hence, I didn't know that the animal-costumed dancers were part of every show - I thought it was all an Easter thing, since this show was on Easter Sunday.  Very fun show, overall, and I'm kicking myself in retrospect for not having stopped and talked to the guys on the way out - Wayne was standing there right by the door.
