Danish Radio have always been present at the Roskilde Festival, 
and brought us many good live recordings. In the late 90's they
introduced at backstage area called "Ko-stalden" (aka "The Barn"),
here they would invite bands to come and play a song (or 2 or 
maybe 3) ... often these performances would be acoustic and 
something completely different from their main set. Most of these
performances was later broadcasted on Danish Radio.

I had an old friend who worked as an engineer in the 1995/96 edition,
who was kind enough to lend me his master cds, I copied them back then,
... so here is another chance to get "clean" pre-fm copies.
