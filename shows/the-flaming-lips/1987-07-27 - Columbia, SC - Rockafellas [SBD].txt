unknown gen soundboard tape -> this cassette (Maxell Xl2) -> playback on Harman Kardon CD491 ->
ALCrec. -> WAV -> Soundengine (edit/remaster) -> WAV -> TLH8 -> FLAC -> DIME -> YOUR EARS AND 
MIND, singing the blues of Charlie Manson...This cassette was at very low volume levels, so
after remastering, there's also some more hiss. Alsao I think that this recording was done
using one of the most useles things ever invented: Dolby noise reduction. It just cuts off all
of the higher frequencies. To those who invented this shit...I'm gonna get you and send you
straight to hell, iof you're not already there...LOL.

quality is not what I usally prefer to do here. But these are old tapes, and definitely not 
1st gen or masters. If someone out there got a better source, feel free to post. I'd be happy,
too, as this is a very very cool show. I dig those early FLIPS much more then their recent
musical journeys. Have as much fun with this as you can, and maybe the master of this will
surface here sooner or later...SB.
