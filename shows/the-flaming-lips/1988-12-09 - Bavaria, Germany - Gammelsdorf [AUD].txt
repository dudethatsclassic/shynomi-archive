Oh, were to start with the lines about this show? 
Recorded in the middle of Nowhere, a small village west of Landshut, the freaky Flaming Lips
were greeted by a nothing less freaking audience. The recording is not up to nowadays standarts,
but given the equipment used, it came out amazingly well. This must've been a helluva party!
I'm not too deep into FLIPS, so there's surely one out there for the missing pieces in the
setlist. I really enjoyed listening to this (while transferring in real time), and I hope
you enjoy this, too. Got some more to share...soon...over the rainbow...
