Notes:
This recording came from the estate of Guy Gimbrone (RIP).  Further source info is unknown
at this time.  I am guessing that this is a 2nd gen. cassette because the tape I transfered
flipped between tracks 11 and 12 but an apparent tape flip existed within track 12, leading
me to believe that this was originally on a 100 min. tape (perhaps the master tape?).  
Internet research only came up with this show existing as an incomplete (70 min.) analog, 
so this seems to be the closest to the original source that has surfaced thus far, which is 
why I decided to seed it.  If anyone has more complete information on this show or a source
closer to the master tape, please contact me at revmike101@yahoo.com.
