A complete short support set at the now closed down London Astoria. 
Crowd was loud & talkative as per usual at this venue. Very annoying at times. 
I believe this was one of the NME Brats shows that they put on at the start of the year. 
Flaming Lips were third on, opening were Bis, followed by Drugstore, with Headliners Teenage Fanclub.
