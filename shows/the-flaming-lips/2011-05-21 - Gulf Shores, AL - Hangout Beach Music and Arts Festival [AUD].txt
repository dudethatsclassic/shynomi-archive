  This was one of my favorite shows of the Hangout Festival. Audio is great but it doesn't do the Flips justice. A video would have been great.   It's a good thing the band played loud because we were by some chatty people where were there to talk to their friends and not pay attention to the band. There is some audible crowd chatter during the quieter passages, but it comes to go.
  Overall, it's better than I thought it would be. I originally wasn't going to tape the set because of location but decided to experiment. I think it was successful.
