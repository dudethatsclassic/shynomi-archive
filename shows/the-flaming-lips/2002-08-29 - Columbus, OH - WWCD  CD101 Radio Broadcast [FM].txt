Notes

CD101 has a lousy transmitter, so reception is lousy just about everywhere in Columbus. For the recording, I hooked up a rabbit ear antenna and found a setting with minimal static. As noted above, the broadcast was recorded through a Mackie board (VLZ preamps) and patched out post gain to the M-Audio Delta 1010 soundcard. My editor was Sonic Foundry's Sound Forge 6.0. The only adjustments made were the fade-in and fade-out, then I dithered to 16-bit. No compression or EQ was applied. CDWav was used to easily edit the one file into individual files for each track.
