I have noticed a lot of Be Here Now era shows are being uploaded lamenting Oasis’ demise so I thought I’d add an absolute corker!

The Be Here Now tour was one of the shortest yet most intensive jaunts around the world undertaken by the band. After over six months on the road the tour wound up in South America. The set list had changed considerably with the songs sung by Noel increasing from two in 1997 to six by the end with much of the ‘Be Here Now’ material being dropped from the set reflecting the public’s and maybe the band’s disillusionment with the LP.

It had been rumoured from the start of the tour that it would cumulate with a series of Stadium gigs in the UK including Bolton’s Reebok and Wembley Stadium.  It would be another two years before these rumours became reality.

Instead Noel took to supporting Paul Weller unannounced around the UK that summer with Wolverhampton being one of the first if not the first of the dates on that tour.
