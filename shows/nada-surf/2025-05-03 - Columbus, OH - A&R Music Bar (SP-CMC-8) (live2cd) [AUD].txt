Notes:

*  Originally Recorded By The Pixies.
+  Acoustic, no amplification, sung with crowd.

This was my second time seeing Nada Surf on their Moon Mirror tour,
the first being the previous fall in Detroit. It was a fantastic show /
peformance and I am always floored by how great Matthew still sounds
after all of these years. Not to mention, Daniel and Ira (who do provide
backing vocals throughout the show). I felt the show leaned a bit too
heavy on the new record (9) when they have 9 other albums to pull from.
I appreciate bands playing their newer material, I really do, however
sometimes I think if overdone, it can impact the feel or vibe of the set.
I felt like that happened a few times during this show. I favor their 
first 5 albums (I hold them in high regard), so its always a bit of a
let down when they skip important tracks from those eras or even more
recent favorites like "Something I Should Do". Thankfully, they played
that and a few other different tracks last October. I have to give a
MASSIVE thanks to Matthew (and Nada Surf's tour manager) for bringing
my gear in for me when the venue told me I couldn't record. With the
help of a friend (Chip!), they came to my rescue and literally brought
my gear in past security. Another reason why I love this band so much!
