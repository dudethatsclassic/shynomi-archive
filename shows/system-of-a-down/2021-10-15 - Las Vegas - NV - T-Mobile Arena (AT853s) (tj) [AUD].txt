After seeing the Stones the night before at the new SoFi Stadium I got up early, grabbed a coffee in Pomona, and then drove out to Sin City to see System of a Down's first performance in 2021. This was a tour I had been looking forward to a long time: they rarely play shows and when they do it's often in Europe or somewhere remote. I got to Vegas with plenty of time to spare so I killed some time at the beer garden near the venue before getting in line early. This venue does not allow bags of any kind (a recent policy change) so I had to be creative to get my gear in. I was able to get everything in without an issue and set up shop dead center in a good spot. Russian Circles and Korn played great sets, but then there was about an hour wait due to technical difficulties. It sounded like they were having issues with one of Daron's guitars.

When System came on the place absolutely exploded. I was not expecting to hear X as an opening song: took me a minute or two to recognize it! System then plowed through 30 songs, bringing back favorites like Holy Mountains as well as performing the full version of Soldier Side, which according to setlist.fm looks like they've only done about 10 times or so. Vegas was given the live debuts of Genocidal Humanoidz and Protect the Land and they both sounded great. They introduced each of those songs as well, and Daron and Serj seemed pretty chatty which was nice to see. All in all a fabuluous performance and I'm glad that I was able to catch them at this show!
