Lil Tendies and I made the trek down to Vegas to see one of my all time favorites, SYSTEM OF A DOWN, perform at the first Sick New World Festival. Got in line early to get inside to see Scowl perform bright and early and then spent most of the day up against a rail dead center facing the two main stages with the relay towers right in front of us. It gave us some shade as well which was great because it was 90 degrees+ most of the day. We wanted to get up closer but there were these massive VIP sections that cut the floor of each stage in half. That meant that people crushed up in the GA sections and we did not want to deal with that. Korn and Deftones both asked people to take a step back or they would stop the show so we made the right call.

At 10PM System walked out and played one of the best sets of theirs I've seen. They opened up with some songs off the first album, Soil and Darts, and then proceeded to play a solid set of songs spanning all their albums. It was a treat to hear DAM, Spiders, and Forest. Everyone onstage seemed to be in a good mood and I'm glad we made the trip down to see them.

Taping notes: hella windy, the line arrays at the stage and relay towers were lowered for a few songs at the beginning. Tons of of phasing and it is very visible in this recording. Wind died off toward the end of the set so it sounds better the second half of the tape. Some audience chatter but nothing major. I'm not thrilled with how this recording came out but given the crowd crush / VIP bullshit up front I don't think we could have gotten a better pull short of sitting in front of a relay tower at the expense of not being able to see anything.
