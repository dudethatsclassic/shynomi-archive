NOTES
 
I WAS LICKY TO FIND OUT ABOUT THIS LAST MINUTE WARM UP SHOW AND GRABBED A TICKET, I SAW LUSH
MANY TIMES DURING THE LATE 80'S AND EARLY 90'S AND WAS VERY PLEASED THAT THEY HAD DECIDED 
TO GET BACK TOGEATHER, I KNOW IT MUST HAVE BEEN HARD TO DO SO WITHOUT CHRIS.
 
THE SHOW WAS SOLD OUT BUT NOT PACKED OUT. THE RECORDING TO MY EARS IS VERY GOOD AND THERE IS 
LITTLE OR NO CHAT AS WE WERE ALL THERE TO SEE THE BAND. MIKI WAS QUITE TALKATIVE AND HAS
LOST NONE OF HER SHARPNESS AND SHARP TONGUE.
 
THEY SEEMED TO REALLY ENJOY THE SHOW, MAYBE THERE WAS SOME RELIEF AT IT BEING THEIR FIRST
FOR 20 YEARS AND SOME RELEASE OF TENSION AT FINALLY BEING ON STAGE.
 
PLEASE GO AND SEE THEM ON TOUR, BUY THE NEW CD AND PURCHASE THE MERCH.
