This video more than any other gives the feeling of really being *at* a Lush show.  You get the whole experience: the lights, the sounds, the atmosphere, and yes, the heads always getting in the way.  Just like real life!  But our intrepid cameraman does his best, capturing many great tight zooms, and somehow maintaining a remarkably steady hand throughout resulting in a nice clear picture and very good sound.  There are some tracking issues here & there from the source tape, most regrettably during 'Lovelife', but overall a great video document of the band in support of their best album.
