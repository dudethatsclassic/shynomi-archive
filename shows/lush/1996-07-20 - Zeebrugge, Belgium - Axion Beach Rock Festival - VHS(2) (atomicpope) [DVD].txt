Notes:

This is another rare video.  But come to think of it, most Lush shows are  :)  From a 2nd generation VHS, here we have Lush playing the outdoor festival circuit during their final months.  Also on this bill were The Sex Pistols, Manic Street Preachers, and Sleeper.  I'll lay it on you straight: this video can be frustrating so it won't please everyone.  Why?  Well it pretty much captures what it's like to actually attend one of these outdoor festivals - heads get in the way and lots of jostling about.  But this filmer was dedicated and when you can see the band, the images are great.  Very crisp for a 2nd gen, and rather close zooms.  Mostly focused on Miki (who looks very cute in pigtails and short summer dress) with occasional pans to the other members.  Furthermore, the sound is among the best I've heard from a Lush audience video, good enough to extract and make an audio boot if the video proves too annoying.  But most of you won't mind!

Buy their albums, share this freely, and enjoy!
