Notes:

This is yet another rare video.  In fact, almost certainly recorded by the same taper who captured the Trenton show 4 days later.  Witness the slow pans, steady hand, the tight focus problem, and the overall fine work on display - clearly all director trademarks.  :-)  This time they throw in some avant-garde camera angles in a couple spots, typical of their early work.  We also get a similar atmospheric, colorful light show which I find enhances the music.  Simply put, whatever your reaction was to the Trenton show will be very similar to this one.

But there IS a difference.  This time the camera location is 20-30 degrees off stage right so we get many more shots of Emma and closeups of her guitar than probably any other vid.  Which makes this a must for Emma lovers!  Sound is pretty good too, vocals a little hard to pick out but a cinch if you already know the words.

There is a cut during For Love where several seconds of the song are skipped.  (Pause button accident?)
