Notes:

One of the earliest Lush shows available on video, ths comes from a 3rd generation VHS.  Check out that setlist, some great lesser-played gems sprinkled throughout that night!  My source for this video listed this as May 15th but most sources say March is correct, including  Miki's tour list.

Pretty good picture & sound.  The venue 'The Venue' (how droll) doesn't seem to offer much in the way of lighting so our heroines are suffused in red and blue for much of the show.  Camera holds a nice medium-close fix on Miki with Chris in the background for most of the night with a few pans over to Emma's side.  From time to time the camera wanders to the wall & ceiling so luckily the audio quality is decent enough to entertain during those meanderings.
