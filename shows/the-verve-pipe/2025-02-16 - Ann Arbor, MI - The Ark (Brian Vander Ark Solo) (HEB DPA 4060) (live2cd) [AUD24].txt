Notes:

*  Originally Recorded By Soundgarden.

First time taping Brian (solo) since July, 2014. Pretty crazy to think about as I
used to tape him solo multiple times a year between 2003 and then. Reason being is
he has mainly focused on The Verve Pipe since. So this was a real treat to get to
see him play his solo material again. One setback on the recording, tho! I made a
rookie mistake plugging my mics into the headphone jack of the M10. When the show
started, I noticed my levels were much higher than usual and extremely sensitive.
I looked over the settings on the back of the M10 and I was getting a recording, so
I put the M10 back on the table in front of me & thought "well, I must be imagining
things." Roughly 50 minutes into the performance ("Medicate Myself") I looked down
at the M10 and it hit me like a bolt of lightning - ITS PLUGGED INTO THE WRONG JACK!
Why, oh why, did I not catch that when he started, I have no idea. Like I said, it
was a rookie mistake. So, the first 50 minutes of this was recorded from the on board
stereo mics on the M10 and the final 40 is from the stand mounted HEB DPA4060's. You
will notice a fairly large quality difference, but atleast its all taped. Of course
all the great songs of the set were during the first 50 - so Im kicking myself, esp.
given how far I traveled from Ohio (through the snow) for this show. Having said all
of that, I hope you still enjoy! It was a great performance from Brian.
