Notes:

! = Features Nate Bearinger on flugelhorn.
* = Originally Recorded By David Bowie.
+ = Originally Recorded By Fleetwood Mac.

Jeffrey Niemeier (violin/fiddle) makes a few guest appearances throughout the
show on select songs. Very special show as this was my 42nd birthday and got
a shoutout before they went into "Myself"! They also played my request - the
Bowie cover, "Life On Mars". Apparently, when they played it a couple of months
back, it did not get much of a reaction from the crowd, but I really wanted
to hear their take on it as I knew they would nail it - and that they did.
To my knowledge, this is the first full band performance of "Blow You Away"
in over 18 years. I remember hearing it the summer of 2004, but since then
its been MIA!!! Such a treat! You will also hear the drums panned a little to
the left as that was the way the kit was positioned from where my rig was set
up. I boosted the right channel a tad to make up for it in post.

There is no break in between the sets. I cross faded the crowd noise
for a seamless listen. The actual break was around 20 minutes.
