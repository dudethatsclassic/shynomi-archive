*  Originally Recorded By Soundgarden.

The Verve Pipe on the 2nd show of their 2nd leg (west coast) with Our Lady
Peace. The setlist has remained the same swapping out the newer song here
and there. I was very excited getting to hear "Hollow Bones" instead of
"Dust", which I saw twice this past April. The sound was good to great,
however, whenever Daine would hit the kickdrum you could hear a small pop
over the PA. Its not too annoying, but it is audible at times during the
set - especially when the guitars were low. I used iZotope RX 7 to remove
some unnecessary screams and yelps in between songs.

Opening for Our Lady Peace. [recorded].
