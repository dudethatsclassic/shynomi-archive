This was an interesting show. First, it was a free to the public concert
celebrating the grand opening of Element Eatery. The concert was held
outside near the rear of the property in a large open area. The show was
only announced a week and a half prior, so the turnout could have been
better. Eventually, everyone inside (who was eating), came outside to
check out the band. Second, TVP were without their drummer Zach Dubay,
their keyboardist Randall Sly and a violinist. The drums were filled in
by Brian Vannieuwenhoven. He always does a great job. However, the absence
of Randall was quite apparent. Channing did a nice job playing a pre
recorded bit on her "pad" for "Photograph", but with other songs he was
sorely missed. He had a prior obligation with another band, so Im sure
he'll be back for all future shows! The third interesting tidbit was that
the band decided to come down (OK - Brian, Lou and Channing) and perform
sans PA right in front of my stand to the crowd covering the Lindsey 
Buckingham classic "Holiday Road". The kids in attendance were really
getting into it and the crowd harmonized towards the end and made it
truly special. One of the best moments of the night. Speaking of covers,
the band opened with "Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band", which I
had never seen before! That was really surprising. So, this show (while
lacking in some areas), excelled in others. Made for a unique performance
and one thats worth listening to. We had perfect weather (70 at showtime
with the sun setting) and no wind, so I went with the DPA's from dead
center at PA level. I think it turned out real nice. I'd also like to add
I really enjoyed the performance of "What Might Have Been", the one song
they played from their 2021 LP, "Threads".
