Notes:

* = Originally Recorded By Fleetwood Mac.

Due to on again off again rain this evening, the set was cut short by 
25 minutes. Their original slot was 7-8pm, they ended up taking the stage
at 730 and playing til 5 after 8. The entire amphitheatre was evacuated
at 640 and we were sent to a nearby building to await the weather to pass.
It never got really bad, but the radar showed more storm cells heading our
way. They made the decision to wait an additional 20 minutes before letting
us all back into Timberwolf to make sure the skies would be clear. When we
finally got back in and got to our seats, TVP were taking the stage at 730.
So, we (probably) missed "Cup Of Tea", "Hero", "Never Let You Down", and
1 or 2 others.
