My second night in a row seeing TVP. They switched out a few songs and added in
"No One's Going To Break", "Penny" and "All Of You", which was cool to see. Without
question, "Penny Is Poison" was the highlight of the entire show. That one does not
get played enough! I have only taped inside The Woodward Theater once before back
in 2016, so I set up my stand in the same place I did then, 20ft back from the
right stack and all turned out well. Thanks to the band for taping permission,
as always.
