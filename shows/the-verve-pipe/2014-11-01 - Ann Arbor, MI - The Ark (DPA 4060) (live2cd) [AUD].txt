Notes:

* = Originally Recorded By Fleetwood Mac.

The band stripped back their typical full-on electric show and performed 
acoustic/electric to give the songs a bit more of a softer edge. The drum 
kit was slightly left of the mic stand, so they are more prominent in the 
left channel. I amplified the right channel a tad to even out the mix. 

I have been seeing this band for over 15 years and this was one of my 
favorite performances. The band sounded incredible together and they 
included 8 (of 10) tracks from the 2014 release, "Overboard".
Channing Lee is also a featured guest backup vocalist. 

Huge thanks to Kris Jaussi for making this recording possible.
