TVP seem to like playing The Ark on my birthday as this is the 3rd time in
the past several years. 2024 will mark the 10th year they've been performing
at The Ark and every show is unique and different. Compared to usual tours
or even other headliners in their home state of Michigan, they always save
something special when they play here. 2023 was no different. I'll run them
down. We got a very brief cover of The Eagles "Seven Bridges Road", which 
served as a perfect prelude into "Cup Of Tea". "Somebody Someday" is a song
that got played very little when it was released in 2017 and this is the
first time it was performed at The Ark. "Roulette" is a brand new song
written earlier this year that also made it's Ark debut. They have yet to
record it. Both rare cuts: "Out Like A Lamb" (1993) and "Nothing Like Your
Love" (2017) benefited from having Nate Bearinger guest on trumpet. Not
only are both songs extremely rare to see live, getting to see them performed
with Nate is a real treat. I believe this was the first time he guested on
"Nothing Like Your Love". "Real" (1996) is another rarity that saw the
most play during the "Villains" anniversary shows 6 or so years back, brought
back out here and on other dates this summer. "The Freeze" (2021) is still
a somewhat new song that was played very little when released. Brian and
Channing have brought it back recently, and this is the first time it was 
ever performed at The Ark. Without question, this was one of my highlights 
of the evening. "Bridge Over Troubled Water" makes it's in concert debut 
as it was recently recorded live at a charity event in Grand Rapids. TVP's
version is equally chilling as it is beautiful. So, was this a special show?
What do you think? I always tell everyone who loves TVP to try to catch a
show at The Ark. You will not regret it. If you missed this year? Turn it
up and go next! Im sure like all the others, it will be special. You will
also hear the drums panned a little to the left as that was the way the kit
was positioned from where my rig was set up. I boosted the right channel a
tad to make up for it in post.

There is no break in between the sets. I cross faded the crowd noise
for a seamless listen. The actual break was around 20 minutes.
