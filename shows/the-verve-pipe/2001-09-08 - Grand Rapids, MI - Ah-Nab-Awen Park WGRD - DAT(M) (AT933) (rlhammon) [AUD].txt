Notes:

I had some help getting the correct date on this one as I knew it was a radio festival 
taking place over a long weekend. Almost 20 years after the show I found some references 
online to the dates of the festival being over September 6,7, & 8th. A random post and 
connection on DIME brought me to "TheOilman" and eventually BVA himself checked on his 
notes to confirm the date was indeed the 8th of September!

Any TVP show with Griff on the harmonica is a great one, but I really like the additon 
of the horns to this particular show.

Overall the sound quality is nice, especially for a recording that sat around for 20 
years before I even rewound it and listened to it. The stage was a temporary setup 
for the festival and open air, so there's certainly people talking around me, but the 
music comes through clear. This is the entire recording from start to end, just a raw 
dump from the original master nearly 20 years after the show.

Encore break is complete, and "Snack break" is some short talk with the band handing 
out some of the leftover perks they had to the crowd. No music in either of these 
tracks, but they are part of the complete recording.
