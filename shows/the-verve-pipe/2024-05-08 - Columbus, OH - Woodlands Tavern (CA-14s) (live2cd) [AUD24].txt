TVP returned to Columbus for the first time in over 6 years. Generally when
I see them, I am traveling to Michigan (or somewhere else), so this was a
refreshing change! The highlights here were the brand new song, "Dust", the
show opener, "Supergig" and the cover of "Strawberry Fields Forever". I have
a feeling when I go back to this show years later, those will be the three I
will want to playback the most. Brian's guitar was having a cable issue
throughout "Cup Of Tea" and "Veneer", so you will hear some distortion here
and there while the stagehand (Neil Patterson) tried to fix it. A minor fight
broke out at the start of "Medicate Myself", so you may hear some scuffling
between 2 individuals take place. When the band launched into the heavy part
of the song, it had ended. Now lets hope it wont be another 6 years before 
they return again!
