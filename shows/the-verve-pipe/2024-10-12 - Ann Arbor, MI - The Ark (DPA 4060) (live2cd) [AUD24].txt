Yet another Ark show in mid October, right around my birthday! The last 2
years were actually ON my birthday! (the 14th). They always save special
setlists for The Ark and once again we got some great stuff. "Reverend Girl",
the new track "Hollow Bones", "Myself", "She Has Faces", and the fan fave
classic, "Martyr Material"! Sadly, they dont do that one all that often
anymore, so its always a treat when they do. Brian Vander Ark on playing
The Ark: "When we go to The Ark, it's different. We can play something new.
We can play rare tracks or deep cuts. We can play stuff from our early,
early days before we were signed to RCA. So it's, it's one of those places
that you don't go play for the paycheck. You go to play because you know
you want to stretch out a little bit and you want to have some fun and go
down memory lane as a band rather than being forced to just play the hit
songs and get off the stage." "La La" was on the printed setlist after
"She Has Faces" but was skipped due to time. You will also hear the drums
panned a little to the left as that was the way the kit was positioned from
where my rig was set up. I boosted the right channel a tad to make up for
it in post.

There is no break in between the sets. I cross faded the crowd noise
for a seamless listen. The actual break was around 20 minutes.
