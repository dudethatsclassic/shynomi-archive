Notes:

* = Originally Recorded By David Bowie.
+ = Originally Recorded By Fleetwood Mac.

The entire venue was seated the entire show. Because I flew in for this
gig, I did not bring my mic stand. I used my hat mount for the CA-14's.
No real surprises in the setlist, but it was fun getting to hear "First
Fire Of The Winter" & "Life On Mars" during the second set. It has atleast
been over a year since they have done FFOTW! The STL City Winery is brand
new, it just opened on the 18th of March. This very well might be the 
first full recording to come from there!

There is no break in between the sets. I cross faded the crowd noise
for a seamless listen. The actual break was around 20 minutes.
