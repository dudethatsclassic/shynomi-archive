The Celestia Theater recently underwent a $1.5 million dollar renovation
by the Wadsworth Square Foundation and this was one of the first concerts
held since! My seat was in the 2nd row, 3 seats over which was right
underneath the right stack. Because of limited space, my stand was not an
option, so instead I used the hat mount and tried my best to stay still
for the entire hour and a half. The setlist featured the new song "Tattoo",
which was exciting for me and a great solo rendition of "Black Hole Sun"
by Brian. The rest of the setlist was pretty standard if you have seen
them in recent years. Still, an incredible show! The show was very well
attended and with how well it went over, I would expect them back in the
next year or two. Some volume adjustments were made on the quieter songs,
applied some hard limiting on some loud clapping and removed some yelps 
and whoo's in RX7 when needed. I was seated directly facing Lou Musa's
amp, so that is the loudest with Brad's bass and Brian's guitars seemingly
only coming from the stage with the drums and vocals coming from the PA.
Still, a good mix regardless, but its very Lou heavy!!!!
