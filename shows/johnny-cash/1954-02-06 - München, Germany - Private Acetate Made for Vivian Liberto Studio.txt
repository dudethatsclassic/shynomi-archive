Date: ca. Feb 6, 1954
Location: Unknown, Munich, Bavaria (West Germany)
Label: Private Acetate
a.	Acetate	MESSAGE TO VIVIAN LIBERTO - 1:22(J.R. Cash)
b.	Acetate	DARLIN', AM I THE ONE? - 1:22(Carl Smith)
Both titles unissued.	
This recording, noted as being from a private acetate, has been passed around the internet for a number of years.

Vivian Cash's 2007 book, I WALKED THE LINE: MY LIFE WITH JOHNNY, states that this acetate was recorded in Munich, Bavaria (then West Germany) on February 6, 1954. All thanks to Kurt Rokitta for emailing me to state that the date is referenced on page 254 of the paperback version of the book.

This is technically not a Sun Recording, but I am including it in this sessionography for it's novelty and for the inclusion of a short version, as performed by Johnny, of "Darlin' Am I The One" written by Carl Smith.

The August 22, 1953 edition of "The Billboard" magazine lists the Carl Smith recording of "Darlin', Am I the One" (which was the b-side of "Hey Joe!") as one of its best selling folk records of the week. Therefore, its safe to assume that Johnny likely had heard the song at some point in the fall and winter of 1953 and it stuck with him when he recorded his letter home.

As it has been mentioned on various web sites, this acetate appears to be the earliest known recording of Johnny Cash in circulation.
