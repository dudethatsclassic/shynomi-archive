Sugar - 2026 05 02 @ Webster Hall, New York, NY

Note: 
If you had asked me about the changes of a Sugar reunion last year, I would have rolled my eyes. So, I was super surprised when these NYC dates got announced and had to get tickets. This is the first Sugar show since January 16, 1995. High energy show, Bob was in quite the form. For the first show in 31 years, it was a solid show. Clownmaster ended prematurely when drummer Malcolm Travis fell back off his drum seat, and there was a missed cue here and there, but great to have them back. All photos are mine.
