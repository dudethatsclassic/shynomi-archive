Notes:

The Goos returned to the road for the first time since 2019 as their 2020
"Miracle Pill" tour with Lifehouse turned into the 2022 "Chaos In Bloom"
tour with Blue October. Obviously, the 2020 tour was rescheduled twice
with Lifehouse dropping off as support. The setlist featured 3 brand new
tracks from the aforementioned "Chaos In Bloom" record and they sounded
really great live. Especially, "War". The recording went fairly well. The 
sound - while good, was not at it's best (I've seen many shows at the Rose).
I did EQ this a bit to bring it to life as it sounded fairly flat to my 
ears. I also removed some unnecessary clapping near me during the music.
I was really looking forward to the Goo's cover of Tom Petty's "Runnin
Down A Dream" (they have been ending their shows with it), but for some 
reason, they skipped it in Huber Heights. It wasnt a curfew issue, but it 
may have been the heat and or exhaustion. It was over 90 degrees throughout 
the entire concert and its possible it was taking it's toll on the band after
an hour and a half straight with no encore. Still, 22 songs in 96 minutes, 
I cant really complain. I'll head over to YouTube for the Petty cover.

Blue October opened [not recorded].
