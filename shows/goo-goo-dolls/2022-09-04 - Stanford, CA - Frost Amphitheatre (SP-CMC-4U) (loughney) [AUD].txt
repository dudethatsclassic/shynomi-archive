Goo Goo Dolls - 2022 09 04 Frost Amphitheatre, Stanford CA

Note:
Was a pretty hot day, maybe hitting 98 degrees during the day, but cooled off by the time the Goo Goo Dolls played. The reason why I mention that is because the crickets were deafining after the show, and you can hear them during the quiet parts of Name adding a nice touch of atmosphere. "Running Down A Dream" was on the setlist but dropped due to time / curfew considerations. Blue October opened, who I also taped.  Props to the lighting director, who did a great job.
