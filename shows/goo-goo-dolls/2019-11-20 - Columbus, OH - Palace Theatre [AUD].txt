Notes:

The Goos returned to Columbus supporting their brand new LP, "Miracle Pill". The set
featured 5 songs from the LP and they also brought back a few gems they havent played
in awhile like "Let Love In" & "Home". One of the highlights was the new track,
"Autumn Leaves" -- thoroughly impressed with that one, dare I say better than the 
album version? You'll want to check that out if you're a fan. I bought my seat directly
in front of the left stack (maybe a row or two too close), but it turned out good. 
I did raise some volume on some songs (ie: "Sympathy"), and EQ'ed slightly to bring
down the bass. During "Black Balloon", I got hit with about 15 black balloons, but
was able to take out those small spikes in Adobe Audition. There was also so much 
confetti at this show I was finding it in my shirt the next day. You will actually
hear the fans release the confetti twice during the show. Insane! One final note,
right before Johnny started "Name", a fan yelled from the crowd that he wanted to
play it. Johnny jokingly replied "No!" but then invited him onstage anyway. The
fan (who's name was Dan) ended up performing the first 1:30 of the song and Johnny 
took over the rest with the band. It was pretty cool to see and Im sure thats a memory
Dan will never forget. He didnt do that bad of a job, either. As always, a great 
performance and had a blast seeing the Goos live. Still great and I've been seeing 
them since 1996.
