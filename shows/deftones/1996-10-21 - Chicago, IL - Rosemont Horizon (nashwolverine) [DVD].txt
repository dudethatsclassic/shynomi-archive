Deftones
Rosemont Horizon
Chicago, IL
October 21, 1996
Support To Kiss


Here is an interesting pairing if I've ever seen one. Deftones and Kiss. Deftones played on about 9 different dates during the Kiss Alive Worldwide (Reunion) tour in late 1996. I'm not sure how they were received at the other gigs, but at this final gig in Chicago, things weren't going over very well. There was a lot of booing and the band was defending themselves by saying Kiss wanted them to be there. One of the best quotes was when he said "The coolest thing about this? Your kid is going to go home and buy the record and you have to pay for it. Bye Bye."
