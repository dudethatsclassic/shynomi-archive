I am not fan of Deftones but recorded this almost 25 years ago :-)First Deftones concert in Portugal and it is the first time i am sharing it and the first time i transfer my master tape to digital.
It is the full broadcast but not the full concert because the first two songs, Root and Engine No. 9 were not broadcasted. Limp Bizkit were the headliners that night and Fred Durst makes an appearence on 7 Words. There is a gap in sound on track 4 but that is due to a loss of transmission by the network it is not a flaw on the master or even of the transfer. The gao was 13 seconds long but i edit it and left a 2 second gap. Track 11 is a short post show interview with two members of the band.
