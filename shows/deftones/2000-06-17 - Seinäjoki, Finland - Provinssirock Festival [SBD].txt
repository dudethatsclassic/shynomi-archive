Notes:
Soundboard recording that starts out a bit rough, but improves by the second song. 
Sadly, there are quick fades between songs. 
This isn't as distracting as TAO, but it's there. 
There is also a bit of analog presence, but it's not distracting. 
The best draw here is a really nice performance of Damone.
