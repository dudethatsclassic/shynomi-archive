Notes: It was a good show. I got to GR with about 15 minutes to spare. The line to the bathroom was way to long so I had to setup in the crowd. It was a little awkward. I taped about 20 Ft. from the soundboard. Unfortunately, the sound was absolutely HORRID for the show. This is about the best that you could possibly do for this situation. Even though this setup is not the best, the sound was just terrible. Very boomy, the guitar levels would sometimes go up and down, and Chino's vocals were mixed low. I thought maybe it was just my ears, and that the mics would pick up the sound better like they sometimes do. But no, not for this show. Still the energy at this show was amazing! So awesome to see Deftones at such a small venue. 

Ryan
