Artist : Deftones
Venue : Carling Academy, Bristol, England. 
Date : 16th April 2007


I went to this gig as a favour to a mate, he paid for my ticket, and I recorded the show for him. I didn't know one song by this band, a little hardcore for me. Surprisingly, I did come up trumps, with one of my best recordings, probably a 9/10. I was standing on the top balcony, left of stage. The venue had speakers suspended from the ceiling, so I was just a few feet away from the hardcore sounds!!! This show was re-scheduled, we'd arrived at the venue about a month earlier, to find the show cancelled, the singer, Chico??, had throat problems (still had 'em by the sound of things) :-).  My verdict....... I'll never be a fan, but they sure know how to hardcore rock!

Recorded on Sony Hi-Md MZ-NH700 using Sony  ECM-719 mic. Edited using GoldWave Digital Audio Editor (fade in/out)
