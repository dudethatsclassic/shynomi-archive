DISCUSSION:

This was just a side recording that day.  Primary focus was Incubus.  This band was SO loud.  I have never heard a band in my life this loud.

However, it was a pleasant suprise.  Really good show.  I recorded 7 feeds and used 4 on the mix.  These is some slight white noise inbetween songs but I think it should not distract from the show.

I mixed this to my ear.  Sincere thanks to some very close friends for their assistance in my progressions.  This show has been on the shelf for over 18 months, and I hope Deftone fans enjoy!

The true show is SO loud that the vocals truly hide in the music.  This may be  noce option for the true fans of this band.

Support the bands, buy their music and merchandise.
