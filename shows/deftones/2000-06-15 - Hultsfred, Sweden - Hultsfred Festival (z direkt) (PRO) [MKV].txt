20 years since the day of this upload, the band headlined the Hultsfred Festival in Sweden. The whole broadcast was captured and transferred by an unknown source.

The broadcast does not feature the full set. "Be Quiet and Drive" and "Engine No. 9" did not air on TV on that specific broadcast.

This show was already on the channel, we decided to reupload it with enhanced framerate and the VHS statics removed.
