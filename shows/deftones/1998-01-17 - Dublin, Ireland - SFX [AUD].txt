My part:
Sony Type I cassette(2) > R-09HR (16/44 WAV) > Audacity (WAV, slowed by 4% and fades added) > CD Wave Editor (WAV) > TLH > FLAC level 6 
*I played the tape on a Technics RS-TR232, through a Yamaha RX-V365 amp, into a Roland Edirol R-09H*

Notes:
Deftones first ever gig in Ireland.
I was at this show with friends, but wasn't taping back then. A friend bought this cassette from a Dublin shop the next day.
Presumably this was a minidisk recording, transferred to a cassette which was then used as the master from which multiple copies were high speed dubbed.
Got this tape from my friend a few days ago (May 2017) so this is a new transfer.
The tape ran too fast (a tell-tale sign of high speed dubbing), so I used the Live EP as a baseline comparison and slowed the audio by 4%, which seems ok.
There are a few dropouts during 'MX', and there is an annoying girl who screams "Chino" non-stop throughout the show.
The tape-flip was between 'Lifter' and 'Mascara'.
