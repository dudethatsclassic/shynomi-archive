Notes:
Arrived at DTE a little late due to 2 hours being in rush hour traffic, so I sadly missed DFA 1979. They go on early (6:30) if anyone is wanting to tape them, and they only play for about 25 minutes. I got to my seat about 10 minutes before Deftones went on. I also picked up some AT831s from Darktrain with a 4.7k mod. I wanted a pair of cards and these certainly do the trick! Great pickup and really concentrates on the forward sound, unlike the DPAs with their omni pattern. Don’t know why I didn’t pick up a pair of these earlier! Great mics. 

This was the first night of the tour opening for Incubus. A few hiccups with mics dropping out or keyboards not being plugged in by the techs but other than that Deftones brought it! Chino’s voice sounds great! As we all know, by the end of tours his voice is really starting to hurt. Tape sounds great, I was adjusting my mics during the intro so I mainly removed the first 30 or so seconds so you didn’t hear the bumping. Enjoy the tape!
