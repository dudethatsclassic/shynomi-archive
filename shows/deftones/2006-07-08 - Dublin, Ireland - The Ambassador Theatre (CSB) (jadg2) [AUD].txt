Notes - The second of two great nights in Dublin by the Deftones.  This time the venue was a bit empty, probably due to the Oxegen festival which was also on that day, so that made for an extra intense mosh pit because of the space.  A fairly mixed up setlist kept things interesting, with a shorter and looser performance than the night before.  Kudos to my friend who was the first person up crowd surfing during the night, right when Chino came down to the front.  
Listen for the Gorillaz cover during Bored.  

- Moved back following Elite, and then moved closer to the stacks again later on, so there's a bit of a change in the sound. 


Venue Notes - the venue is a small 1500 capacity theatre venue.
