Sketchy seats.  Close to stage, but looking across stage, with partial obstruction.  Basically, this is a recording of the sound coming from the PA speakers directly in front of and above me.  Sound quality nothing special, but not bad.  Listen to samples.

Spectrum Center was packed, and maybe sold out.  If you listen carefully you may hear the squeals of teenage girls.  I bet if you told Chino, Steph, and Abe in 1995 that in 2025 they would be playing to teenage girls in sold out arenas, they would have demanded to know who your weed supplier was.
