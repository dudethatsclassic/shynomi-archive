SHOW INFO:

The second leg of the self-titled album tour is notorious for its super heavy, atmospheric performances. This set is a perfect example. I have yet
to re-listen to the whole thing and give all the setlist extra details, which I will update this torrent with later on. I can already say it is a
beautiful, super heavy set, with probably the heaviest performance of "Elite". Chino's vocals were harsh but not bad in my opinion, with his screaming
capacity being the wildest during this very specific tour.

A great, clean recording by an unknown taper. This bootleg was signed by a "GL" on the original .txt file, this may be the taper. I would appreciate a confirmation.
