Courtesy of the Stonecutter Archives is this early Ride audience recording promoting "Nowhere," supported by Levitation. 

Even though sonic swirls are part of the band's sound, there's the occasional bit of that going on here at times, but unfortunately it seems to originate from the taper or the mics. For the most part, it's a brilliant recording, but those spots may be audibly maddening.
