Great setlist and just about two hours long! I stood in the back, raised up above the crowd a bit, but unlike when I saw Slowdive at Huxleys, the sound was not particularly great. I don't know if it was just due to that location, but I was too scared to move elsewhere and risk it only getting worse without being able to get my original spot back.

The obvious highlight was Anton Newcombe of The Brian Jonestown Massacre coming out for a rare performance of "Grasshopper". His guitar is the noisy one kind of in the background. It's hard to distinguish, unfortunately, but especially near the beginning and end it's more obvious.

The opening band, Dead Horse One, was really good, too, but unfortunately I didn't think to record their set. That was a mistake!
