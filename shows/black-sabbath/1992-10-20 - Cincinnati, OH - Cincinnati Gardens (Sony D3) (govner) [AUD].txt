Here it is.... my last Tony Iommi recording!
 This one I'm not very proud of as the quality is not up to my usual high standards as this one was plagued by all kinds of problems with people rushing the main floor (as ticket sales was poor) and my lack of hours (and experience) on my new DAT recorder. It was a losing battle to keep my 4th row seat and in hindsight I should have just stepped back and enjoy the show (and got the whole show on tape!) but live and learn. I did what I could to bring up the volume on "Computer God" but didn't have muck luck. It's not going to get any better than this so enjoy it for what it is and not for what it is not!

Enjoy!
