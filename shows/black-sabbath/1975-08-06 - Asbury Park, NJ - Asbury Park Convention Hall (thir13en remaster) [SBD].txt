**Remastered** by thir13en

Black Sabbath
1975-08-05
Asbury Park Convention Hall
Asbury Park, NJ
SBD

Sabbaths' early albums just didn't capture the full on energy of the band, it seemed "canned" and somewhat light-weight compared to their live shows. Unbridled and raw, the band live had so much more impact. This is an excellent representation of that fact. Once again, I took a good recording and made it bigger and badder. So here is Ozzy's Sabbath in their prime. 

Enjoy!
