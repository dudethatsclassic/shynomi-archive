"Stratcat58" notes;
Yet another recording from Swedish trading partner PEJ as part of a 1990’s era tape trade.
This tape came to me dated as 8-22-75 but I am pretty sure that is not the correct date.  
The recording is definitely from a Chicago concert as Ozzy mentions the city just before “Symptom of the Universe”.  
Their original tour itinerary had them in Chicago in July (July 16th with the possibility of another show on the 17th?).  
However, Joe Siegler and Robert Dwyer’s “Black Sabbath online” site tour dates page https://www.black-sabbath.com/tourdates/sab_tour/ mentions the possibility that the Chicago July date(s) were postponed until August(?)  
But the 1975-08-22 date conflicts with the reported date for their San Antonio TX show.  
August 22nd was supposed to be the start of the southwestern leg of their ’75 US tour so it seems unlikely to me that they would be playing in Chicago on that date.  
Maybe someone out there can provide some more insight on this. 
The “Sabotage” 1975 US tour is fairly well represented recording wise.  
In addition to the now famous Asbury Park NJ recording (I was there!) and the Don Kirshner TV show appearance, recordings of the Baltimore, Providence, Long Beach and Boston shows also circulate.  
A recording of the San Bernardino show has recently popped up on YouTube as well. 
Every recording of this show that I am aware of is incomplete.
This tape originally played back quite fast/sharp and required some work to get it back in the ballpark speed and pitch wise.  

"blackchevy" notes;
Here are MY notes that I got from the taper himself. 
I had a 2nd gen copy & the person who gave it to me gave me the person who had the master tape. 
I got in touch with him & he sent me an upgraded 1st gen. 
Here is the story he gave me. 
"He told me he was a truck driver & decided to stop in Chicago for an overnight stay to rest. 
He saw Sabbath was playing & decided to go. 
He had 2 blank tapes with him. As the story he told me goes, while he was on the 2nd tape , a security guy caught him & asked for the tape. 
He gave it to him & the security guy literally pulled the tape out of its shell right in front of him. 
Then the security guys says, "Got another tape". The taper replied, No, that was it. 
So that is why there is only half of the show. Beleve it or not, that is the story he told me. 
I had no reason to doubt him. And he said the date was 8-22-75. 
That was the date on the tape he sent me & to the person who I first got the tape from (the 2nd gen copy)

"looney99" notes;
As for the date question, the old Sabbathlive entry from 2005 or so stated: "The taper dated this one as August 22nd, 1975. 
This information conflicts with the original scheduled date of July 16th (and 17th), as well as the San Antonio gig on August 22nd, both confirmed by ticket stubs. 
However, it is possible that the original date could have been rescheduled.
