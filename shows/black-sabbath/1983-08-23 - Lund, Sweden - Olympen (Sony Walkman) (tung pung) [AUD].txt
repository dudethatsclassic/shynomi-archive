Notes:
A friend gave me a box with a lot of tapes.
None have been in trading before, the taper have been holding
on to these recordings and he wasn´t sure if he ever would let them go.
After a few years of talking and asking he did say yes to do this.
It will be about 15 - 18 shows in total and they will come to you!!

So, what do we have here?
"Black Sabbath" live in Lund on the beginning of the tour with Ian Gillan on vocals.
The taper did record 2 songs with the support band, Pretty Maids, I put them on as a bonus.

The sound in the venue was very loud and the sound on the recording didn´t turn out
that good, and you can hear someone next to the taper say something about this during the show.

* = Cut due to tape change, some music is lost, but not that much
** = Drumsolo is most likely cut.
*** = The end is cut.
**** = The beginning is cut and "The Dark" was not recorded.

The taper have stopped the recorder after each song, and therefore there are
very little talk between the songs from the singer.
On the really bad side, when he turned on the recorder again,
it made the mic go sky high, and we had to cut that away.
He have done this in most of his recordings, you will notice this.


Enjoy!

D.M.
