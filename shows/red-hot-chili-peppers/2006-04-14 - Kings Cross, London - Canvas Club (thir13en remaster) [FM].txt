**Remastered** by thir13en


Red Hot Chili Peppers
2006-04-14 Canvas Club
Kings Cross, London


Nice FM cap of the "new" Chili Peppers. To me, and it's not a bad thing, they sound about the same as they have for the last 5 or 6 years now. Here it's a decent set of mostly new, but some oldies too, like "Me and My Friends" which is real cool to see in the setlist, and even better to hear them play it now. It's heavy and tight.

Source was good, but a very low and compressed imprint. I decompressed some, rounded out the EQ and maximized. I like it a lot.
