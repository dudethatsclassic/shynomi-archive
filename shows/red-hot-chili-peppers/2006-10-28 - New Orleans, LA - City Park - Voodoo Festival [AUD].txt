Notes by Adam: 

You can't beat a free VIP ticket to VoodooFest and a chance to see a great band like the Chili Peppers.  I didn't want to fight the crowd up front, so I decided to tape from the rear satellite speakers, and I think it turned out to be a great decision.  I hope everyone enjoys this show, including track 6 on disc 2 where The Meters join RHCP onstage for a 15 minute jam song.  Please note that this recording is slightly incomplete.  I'm missing a little bit at the beginning of d01_t01, d02_t01, and finally d02_t07.
