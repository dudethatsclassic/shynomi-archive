Notes:
This show is the 15th of the "Unlimited Love Tour" of the Red Hot Chili Peppers and the last of the current tour leg through Europe. 15 years ago I had seen the band at the same place, the stadium was called AOL Arena back in 2007. I'm glad I had the chance to relive the line-up with John Frusciante and I'm not the only one, as the crowd FOS was chanting his name a couple of times. Also they showed some dancing in circles at the appropriate songs. Just like at the other 2022 shows I attended, the Chili Peppers spent a lot of good vibes, each of their shows has something unique. This time I got the idea that they switched the order of the songs more than before, and after "Aquatic Mouth Dance" I was even afraid they might have skipped "Snow". But then I decided to just embrace what the band had to offer. The switched order might have even confused John at one time, when he (after "Black Summer") went to Flea in order to start "The Heavy Wing", who assumingly told him it was "Soul To Squeeze" to be played next.

People around me on the grandstand were having a good time, but only a few stood up to dance or cheer. The sound at my place was good, so here comes a decent recording, nevertheless with some cheering of the people next to me or even talking. But hey, it's an audience recording, it's nice to hear the audience once in a while, their singing during "Otherside" gives me a shiver at least. One weird thing happening during shows in Germany is the clapping of the audience, even at parts of songs where it does not fit at all, unnecessary in my opinion - we have Chad to keep beat.
 
The Red Hot Chili Peppers played 5 songs off their new album "Unlimited Love", and pleased longtime fans with "Nobody Weird Like Me" or "Sir Psycho Sexy". The latter was for the encore, a nice surprise regardless of the fact, that I was sure they would play "Under The Bridge", because it was the last show of the tour leg and this summer they did not play it very often. Anthony, you owe us another song. One highlight for me was "The Heavy Wing" (from the recent album "Unlimited Love") and songs I haven't seen live for a while (or ever), like "Throw Away Your Television" or "The Zephyr Song". Again the stage show with lights, videos and effects was amazing. If you get the chance to see the Red Hot Chili Peppers live, go ahead :) And if you like their music, buy the official albums.
