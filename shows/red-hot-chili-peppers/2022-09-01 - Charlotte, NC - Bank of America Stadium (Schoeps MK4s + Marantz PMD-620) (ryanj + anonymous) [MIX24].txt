Notes:
First time seeing RHCP. It was an amazing show. Fruciante is an absolute beast. It was great hanging out with Mark from LPLive at this show and thank you to GW for hooking us up with pit tickets! We were in front of Frusciante at the rear of the pit, which was great for stack taping. Parallel Universe was my favorite song from the set. This was only my 2nd stadium show I have ever seen. 

This was matrixed with a friends feed source. I am really happy with the recording.
