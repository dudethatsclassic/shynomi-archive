Notes:
Almost 10 years after I had seen the Red Hot Chili Peppers with Josh Klinghoffer, they returned to the Goffertpark with John Frusciante in 2022 and spent a lot of good vibes. The place is huge and it was filled with about 60,000 people. As I wasn't willing to stand too close to other people, I took a place very far away from stage on the right side. People around me were dancing and having a good time, but they were way to chatty in my opinion. I changed places twice, but it did not get much better, folks had too much beer and talked too much, you can hear them particularly at quiet parts, please excuse. The sound of stepping on plastic cups is also annoying.

The setlist was really good, you don't get "Soul To Squeeze" and "I Could Have Lied" in one show often, I guess. In return they skipped "Under The Bridge" which was on the setlist, I think Anthony saying "we owe you another song" at the end of the show refers to this. The Red Hot Chili Peppers played 4 songs off their new album "Unlimited Love", and pleased longtime fans with "Suck My Kiss" or "I Like Dirt". One highlight for me was "Hard To Concentrate" (from the album "Stadium Arcadium"), which had its live debut played by John Frusciante. During "Black Summer" something went wrong with my recorder, so part of this song and the following "Scar Tissue" got lost. The stage show with lights, videos and effects was amazing, it would have been even better at night, but we got a lovely sunset. If you get the chance to see the band live, go ahead :)
