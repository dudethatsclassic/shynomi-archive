Sarah McLachlan – KCRW Studios, Nov 4, 2003 session is a true live FM broadcast, captured direct-to‑digital by KCRW’s in‑house engineers and aired on Morning Becomes Eclectic.
She played a six‑song acoustic set—mostly Afterglow material—plus a brief interview segment. This was a live in‑studio FM broadcast, not a tour stop, and is one of the cleanest, best‑documented 2003 promo appearances.
