Notes: This is the very first show on the Afterglow world tour. The band hadn't played live for several years,
and hadn't rehearsed in weeks, so while the performances aren't perfect, their enthusiasm more than makes
up for a little sloppiness. The crowd is great and the band really responds to them.

There are two edits in the show. The first occurs in Last Dance, due to mic problems. It is a noticable edit,
but it isn't a "real" song so isn't a problem. The second edit is due to a disc change during Black & White.
Missing from the song is a verse and chorus, but the edit is so clean you wouldn't notice unless you were
singing along.
