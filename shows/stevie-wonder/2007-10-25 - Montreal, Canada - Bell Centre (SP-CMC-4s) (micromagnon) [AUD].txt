Stevie Wonder
Centre Bell, Montreal, Quebec, Canada
October 25 2007

Taper:  MicroMagnon, the stone-age taper.

Source:  Source: SP CMC-4s (AT-853s) > SPSB-6 battery box (107 Hz bass roll-off) > Creative Nomad JB3 @ 44.1 KHz  Normalized in Sound-Forge, split with CDWave.

I went to this concert mainly to please my girlfriend, because I always identified Wonder with his cheezy MOR ballads from the 80s,  but I had forgoten his wealth of groovy material from the 70s.  Needless to say, I was impressed enough to call this show one of the best I saw in recent years.  I’m not 100 percent familiar with all his songs, and there may be one or two omissions in the set list, because he jammed a lot, playing a few lines from one song, then moving to another.  I’d love to see a true fan fix the set list, and create some nifty artwork.

The show was nearly three hours long, so it has to be split on three discs.  My split is 1-12 disc one, 13-22 disc two, and 23-29 disc three.
