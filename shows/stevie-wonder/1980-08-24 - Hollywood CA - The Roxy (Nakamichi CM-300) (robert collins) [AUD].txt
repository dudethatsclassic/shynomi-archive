Musicians:      Stevie Wonder: Keyboards, Vocals; Ben Bridges, Rick Zunigar: Guitar; Nathan Watts: Bass; Isaiah Saunders: Keyboards; 
                Dennis Davis: Drums; Earl Derouen: Percussion; Hank Redd, Bobby Malach: Saxes; Nolan Smith, Larry Gittens: Trumpets; 
                Wendy Barnes, Alexandria (Alex) Brown, Melody McCulley: Vocals; 

Guest Artists:  Teena Marie: Vocals, Piano; Betty Wright: Vocals, Piano; Jermaine Jackson: Vocals, Bass; Buddy Miles: Vocals 

Notes:          A woman named Eula Love was shot and killed by the LA Police.  Stevie decided to hold a $100/seat benefit concert for 
                her small children @ the Roxy.  The Roxy only seats 450 people -- it's a very famous small night club in Hollywood.  
                I was sitting dead center in the Roxy.  There were stars all around.  Actor Tony Curtis announces Stevie Wonder at 
                the beginning of the concert.  I saw Lavar Burton, Bette Middler, Mayor Tom Bradley; and of course the guest 
                artists:  Teena Marie, Germaine Jackson, Betty Wright, and Buddy Miles.  There's probably a few people I'm 
                forgetting...but you get the picture.

                My master tape was stolen in 1987.  With help of some friends, I managed to track down a 1st Generation copy 
                of my master.  I used this copy for CD Master of the show.  Unfortunately, the last side of the last tape had 
                speed variation problems, which I believe I successfully fixed on the computer.  If the last 20-30 minutes sound 
                a little weird, you'll know why.
