Notes:
The heat:
The concert started an hour late.  While waiting, we sat in a sweltering heat.
By show time, it was still 102 degrees, and by show's end, it was still 91
degrees.  This made sitting, taping, and everything except listening very
uncomfortable.  As a result of the heat, Stevie played shorter for us, than
others on the tour.

The sound:
Sitting 10th row center might have been a little too close.  The bass was
overwhelming, the highs were lacking, and the mix seemed bad to me.  What
happened to the days when Stevie hired his own dedicated mixer -- such as Gary
Olozabal?  I was able to fix the highs, but left the lows for the listener to
adjust out -- if they so desired.  However, sitting so close had it's
advantages too.  You'll notice from the recording that the trumpet sound is
right off of the stage, and sounds REAL crisp.

The mistakes:
It was so hot, my microphones kept slipping away from their perch on my
shoulders.  Therefore, you will hear some microphone muffling -- but mostly
in between songs -- rarely during a song.  So my apologies for the mic
muffling.
