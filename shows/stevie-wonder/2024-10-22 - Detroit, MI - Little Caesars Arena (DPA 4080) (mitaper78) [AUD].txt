Source: DPA 4080 > DPA MPS 6030 > Sony A10
Position: Stage-Left, Lower bowl, Approx 50' from speakers
Taper: MITAPER78

Notes: Stevie Wonder is not just rock n roll royalty but Motown Royalty. Up until this most recent tour, did not have the opportunity to see/tape Stevie. Generally, when an artist starts a concert by speaking prior to performing a song I find it somewhat annoying but, hey, it's Stevie Wonder let the man speak. There are a couple times when the actual PA/speakers basically short out - you can hear a loud crackle on one of the songs (I edited the end of a track so you can hear it and how the sound went quiet other than people acting confused in the crowd). What happened was the sound engineer's console/board had to be shut down and restarted. It was, something. Did my best on the song tracking, some songs (to me) sounded better just as one file rather than tracking every song as some are very short. Hope you enjoy this recording.
