T's notes

This show was recorded by patpier.  He asked me if I would edit and master his recording.  His original WAV files were recorded at 16 bit 88.2 kHz.  He provided his recording to me in 2 FLAC files.

I cleaned up the heads and tails of the recording.  I removed the official Michael Jackson recordings which were played through the PA except for "The Way You Make Me Feel" which I left in place because Stevie Wonder sang over the recording for almost half of the song.

I used the equalizer in Roxio to boost the high frequencies.

I reduced the volume of some of the loud clapping and increased the overall volume.  A couple of the songs and also when Stevie talks were at a very low volume.  I boosted those sections to acceptable levels.

I resampled from 16 bit 88.2 kHz to 16 bit 48 kHz, spit into individual tracks, converted to FLAC and identified some of the song titles.
