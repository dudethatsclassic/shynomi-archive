Notes from the Husker Du Database:
"Audience tape. Venue was for many years listed as Ritz, but the poster states that Ritz was the backup venue in case of rain. An attendee has confirmed that the weather was fine that day, and that the show took place in the courtyard of La Rocca (The Fortress) as scheduled."

This recording has this airy-ethereal, almost dreamlike feel to it, which is probably due to the fact that the band is playing in the courtyard of an old fortress. One of my favorites for sure! Thanks to the Italian taper who made this recording in the first place.
