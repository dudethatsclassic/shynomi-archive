HUSKER DU - September 8, 1984 @ 7th St. Entry, Minneapolis, MN

Another cool one for you guys. This one has the only known live versions of "Watcha Drinkin'" and "Plans I Make", as well as a ton of other rarities that you can see for yourself in the setlist below. This is supposedly one of two sets played that night, but I suspect they actually played one, since 70+ minutes seems a little long for that kind of setup.

Once again, no gen info, and I would call this one a B or so.
