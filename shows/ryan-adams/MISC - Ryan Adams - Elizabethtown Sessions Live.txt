Ryan Adams
Elizabethtown Sessions Live

A live compilation of songs from the Elizabethtown Sessions (aka Darkbreaker).

01. Ryan Adams & The Cardinals - Cemetery Hill (2006 soundcheck - Bedhead 6, Disk 5)
02. Ryan Adams - Clementine (2005-08-09 - Commodore Ballroom, Vancouver, BC)
03. Ryan Adams & The Cardinals - Everybody Knows (2007-05-16 - Ulster Performing Arts Center, Kingston, NY)
04. Ryan Adams - Dreams Of A Working Class Clown (2001-04-25 - Sodra Teatren, Stockholm, Sweden)
05. Ryan Adams & The Cardinals - Lions Of Broadway (aka Steve Miller And Weed) (2004-09-14 - New Daisy Theater, Memphis, TN)
06. Ryan Adams - Who Were We (2006-02-21 - Colston Hall, Bristol, UK)
07. Ryan Adams - Lighthouses (2006-02-21 - Colston Hall, Bristol, UK)
08. Ryan Adams - Everything Dies (2006-02-24 - Apollo Victoria Theatre, London, UK)
09. Ryan Adams - Two  (2006-02-24 - Apollo Victoria Theatre, London, UK)
10. Ryan Adams - Maps (2006-02-18 - The Philharmonic Hall, Liverpool, UK)
11. Ryan Adams - Don't Get Sentimental On Me (2006-02-18 - The Philharmonic Hall, Liverpool, UK)
12. Ryan Adams - Elizabeth, You Were Born To Play That Part (2006-02-21 - Colston Hall, Bristol, UK)
13. Ryan Adams - Lighthouses (2006-09-18 - Bush Hall, London, UK)
 

All tracks are soundboard sourced. Saturday Night is not included, since the song from the Elizabethtown Sessions has nothing to do with the one Ryan played live in 2001/2002. I made simple fade ins and outs and volume adjustments with Audacity. I also edited Maps a litlle bit to take out one of Ryans mistakes before the first verse. I couldn't pick one version of Lighthouses so I included two very different ones.

Thanks a lot to the people that recorded and shared these tracks!

Enjoy!
