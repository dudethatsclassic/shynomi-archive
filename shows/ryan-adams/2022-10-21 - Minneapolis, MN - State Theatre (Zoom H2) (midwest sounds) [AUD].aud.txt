Tapers notes: This was my first time seeing Ryan solo, I had seen him with a band a few times before, so I was looking forward to this one. It didn't disappoint, the setlist included a bit of everything including some local flavored covers. I will also say, I love Ryan Adams, but he may have some of the most obnoxious fans I've been around. Constantly yelling out songs, yelling random things at him, etc. One thing I did find funny is someone yelled for "Midway Park", and a few people yelled back "that's not even one of his songs!". Well, it is, it's a Whiskeytown song...made me chuckle a bit. I took a few pictures (included in link above), but this was a "no camera" show, so I did the best I could with my Galaxy S9, which didn't go great in the low light.
