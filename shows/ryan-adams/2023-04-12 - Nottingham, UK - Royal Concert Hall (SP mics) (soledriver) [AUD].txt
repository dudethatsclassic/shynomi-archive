Ryan Adams
Royal Concert Hall
Nottingham, UK
12th April 2023

A Soledriver Recording...
I've never heard such a quiet show in a 2,500 seater...or less light either, come to that.
I've done a basic rework of the show so that the music comes through, 
but the talking parts are still lost in the ether somewhere.

Lineage: Sound professional mics > CA 9200 Pre-amp > Roland R-07 recorder > 
         Audacity (tracking, processing) > Traders Little Helper > Flac8 sector aligned
