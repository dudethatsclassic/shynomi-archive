Ryan Adams
2023-04-22
Phil, Haarlem,The Netherlands

Dutch Masters Audience Recording

Lineage: Edirol R-09 + Sound Professional SP-CMC-8 Mics. 
Editing with Adobe Audition. Encoded to flac level 8, aligned on sector boundaries. 
This recording is compiled from two different sources. Due to a cable issue, I had to call out for help from Dutch Masters' Apprentice. Thanks man.
Track 18 up to 27 were inserted from his recording, which was done with the same recording gear. Still the sound differs a little, and we sat side by side.

Quite a challenge these solo acoustic DRA shows. Low volume sound from the speakers, a lot of volume fluctuations and very loud applause.
A lot of effort went in the editing. That took some time
