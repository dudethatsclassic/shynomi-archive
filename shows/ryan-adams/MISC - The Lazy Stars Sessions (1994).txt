Unreleased demos of one of Ryan Adams early bands with great garage sound. 
 
This material showed up on E-Bay in 2001, described as:  "Before Whiskeytown, and somewhere between one of the Patty Duke Syndrome's break up/make ups, there was Lazy Stars. Bassist Tom Cushman recorded them on a tape he stole from a car, and it has magically been transferred to digital. Anguished post-punk power drone rock, like Crazy Horse, Wipers, Galaxie 500. 11 Songs. Rare, raw, nice. Yes, this is Ryan on all vocals and guitar."
