Ryan Adams
Sing You A Song (1998-2014)
A Live Collection of Jams & Improvs  


This is the updated final version of my compilation. Some of the fun for me was to listen to how the ideas for the improvs came up, so there's a good deal of banter included. Hope you enjoy the stand-up comedy-esque moments. In my opinion it also paints quite a good picture of Ryan Adams' relationship with the audience. Many of the songs on disc 3 & 4 have been originally on disc 7 & 8 of the first version of my Rough & Mellow collection a couple of years ago.
The last track on disc 7 is an unreleased song from the scrapped Glyn Johns Sessions.
