Ryan Adams
Rough & Mellow (1994-2014)

A collection of live recordings of unreleased original songs by Ryan Adams, of which no demo or studio recordings are avaiable. 
A lot of these were only played or recorded once, if not I picked the one that sounded best to me. Edits made with Audacity.

This an updated final version of my compilation. The Cardinal Jams (originally Discs 7-8) and some tracks from disc 6 have been removed and are now included in the "Sing You A Song" Jam & Improv Collection. Although "Petal In A Rainstorm" was released by now, I found it to be too pretty to be taken out. I included "Testy, Testy" and "21st Century Wars", since unlike the available demos the live versions have lyrics.
