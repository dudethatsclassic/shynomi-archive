Matthew has an incredibly large body of unreleased songs in demo form. Most of these circulate among fans in reasonable quality. However a few of the sets I have never been able to track down in lossless quality. These include an entirely different set of songs on the similarly titled Unearthed (Earth Demos), another set called Pre-Girlfriend Era Demos and finally two or three songs (Terrible Dream & You're So Young) that were circulated as Superdeformed Vol 3. If anyone has these holy grail demo sets in lossless quality please share them.

An even bigger grail item is an enormous list of In Reverse demos from 1997-99. To my knowledge those haven't circulated among fans even in lossy quality.
