Notes, rambles, etc.:

If I still lived in LA, I would have been there.  McCabes is always a good time.

Gotta hand it to ijwthstd for an great capture.  The dynamics are significant - there’s (sometimes off-mic) between-song chatter that’s quite low, but the signal during is perfect: solid and undistorted.  Some kind of normalization could be nice, but I left remastering for someone else.

Also because it was a one-off benefit, there’s a bit of space between most songs - people coming & going, telling stories, etc..  Since most were over a minute, I tracked them separately for the option of a more seamless listening experience.

Any help with unknown titles, or who was playing what would be greatly appreciated.
