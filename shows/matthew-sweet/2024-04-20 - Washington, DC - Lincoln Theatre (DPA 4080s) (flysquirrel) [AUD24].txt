Comments:

Recorded from the rear left balcony approximately 125 feet from PA stack. Not a great location for recording considering the hall's acoustics. The FOH engineer struggled during this show and the mix was poor for the first 4 or 5 songs before it stabilizes. The show included long stretches of silence between songs as Matthew was also trying to troubleshoot significant noise/interference coming through his amp for most of the set. Remarkably, he did not seem to have a guitar tech to help.  Abe Partridge opened.
