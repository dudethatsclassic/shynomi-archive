Notes:

Huge thanks to Ryan Teske for lending me my old pair of Core Sound Binaural
microphones I gave him in 2005. Im in the process of replacing some gear, so 
he was a life saver! Going into this show, we (the fans) all thought we would 
be getting a normal headlining set. Once the show ended after only 53 minutes
we all looked at eachother puzzled. This was a very small club and most fans 
in attendance were the absolute die-hards. I guess it was in the original
contract to play 60 minutes (or less, in this case) due to their obligation
with The Wicker Park Street Fest the next evening. I dont know the specifics,
but it definitely put a slight damper and early ending to an otherwise awesome
evening. The band mentioned it was a "weird" gig the next day and listening
back, you can tell there are a few mistakes here and there. During soundcheck,
they were testing playing with earplugs, without earplugs and even 
repositioning their amps to better hear eachother. The band didnt trainwreck 
anything, and the performance was actually quite good in retrospect. Having 
said that, there were some bumps along the way. Specifically during the first
3 songs. 

The energy inside the Beat Kitchen was through the roof and it had sold out 
in under 3 minutes. It will always be one of those shows you had to be at 
and I was very happy to have been so lucky. They made sure to reminisce about
the first and last time they played there... in 1993! Turn this one up!
