Veruca Salt - 01.25.95 - Studio 105, Paris, France - FM

This is from a cassette I purchased over 10 years ago from Mystery Train Records in Harvard Square, Cambridge, MA.  The cassette itself is a TDK SD90 (Type II).  I couldn't tell you what the generation is, but it doesn't have a lot of tape hiss and such crap- it's pretty smooth.  Conservative A-, strict B+. I put the cassette onto disc from Onkyo deck TA-RW313, onto Maxell CDR Music Pro with Pioneer cd recorder PDR-509.  I created the wav files with Roxio Cd Creator 8, and the flac files with Flac Frontend.
I love this band, and this is a great example of them as they were gaining popularity.  There is dj talk in between songs quite frequently, and the last track gets cut short on the broadcast.  I hope y'all dig this.
