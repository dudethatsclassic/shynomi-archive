* = Originally Recorded By Screamin' Jay Hawkins, featuring Scott Lucas 
    (from Local H) on guitar.

My 2nd show seeing Toadies touring in support of their new album, "The Charmer". 
The setlist was almost the same from Cincinnati in May, but we did get a couple
swap outs, most notably "Dollskin" (as voted on by the audience versus "Pressed 
Against the Sky"), and fan favorite (and single!) "Backslider". Both were at the 
encore and both were surprises to me! IMO, the sound in Columbus was a tad better
than in Cincinnati, so this sounds good cranked! Oh, did I happen to mention they 
did "Backslider"!?!? They did! Now, if they'll just bring back "Quitter"... !

Olive Vox [not recorded] & Local H [recorded] opened the show.
