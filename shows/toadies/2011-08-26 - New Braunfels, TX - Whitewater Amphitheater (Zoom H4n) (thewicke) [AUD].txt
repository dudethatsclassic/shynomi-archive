Notes: I set my recorder on a power box about 10 feet to the right of the soundboard. 
I wrapped it up in a t-shirt I had just bought, and I would like to personally thank 
the photographer that came by and tried to steal said shirt. The recorder got knocked 
to the ground right after Tyler, and i reset it within a few seconds. I left this in the 
recording because I couldn't really find a reasonable way to edit it out, although I did 
reduce the peak caused by this. I also added gain to both channels, and a little more so 
on the right channel. There is also a slight cut in the audio during the opening of Send 
You To Heaven. This was due to the end of the file limit size (2gb) on the recorder. This 
should have been seemless (as it was for the show Saturday night)...not sure what went wrong 
as I've never had this problem before.

Todd messed up a few lyrics during the show..not sure if this was caused by alcohol or the 
sound guys (as it was on Saturday night)...we'll just call it warts and all!

Despite all of that, the recording turned out pretty well. Hope you enjoy!
