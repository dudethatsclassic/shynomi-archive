Notes: I set my recorder on top of an ice machine about 40 feet behind the soundboard 
which caused a rumble throughout the recording, as well as, a bit of reverb in the bass.
The rumble is mostly audible during breaks between songs, and is hardly noticeable during songs.
I left all of this in the recording, but reduced a few of the peaks caused by the sound guys.
I also edited out most of the announcer intro as I hadn't set my recorder yet and the sound guys 
overloaded part of the audio before the Toadies came out.

Todd complained about a monitor problem during their first crack at Possum Kingdom, which the band 
abandoned. There may have been other problems as well as Todd messed up a few lyrics on other 
songs too.

Despite all of that, the recording turned out pretty well. Hope you enjoy!
