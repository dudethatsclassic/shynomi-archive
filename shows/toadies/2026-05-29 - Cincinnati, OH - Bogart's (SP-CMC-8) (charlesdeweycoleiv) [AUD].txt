Notes:

* = Originally Recorded By Screamin' Jay Hawkins, featuring Scott Lucas 
    (from Local H) on guitar.

Touring in support of their new album, "The Charmer". My first time getting to see
The Toadies since 2017!!! So, I was very excited. I have been listening to the new
album a ton since it's release, so I was very happy to see 10 of the 12 songs on
the setlist! Ironically, my favorite song from the album, "Come To Life" has not yet
been played live. Maybe later this summer when they return to Ohio. I really cannot
complain with a 24 song setlist at over 95 minutes. However, it was really weird not
seeing "Backslider" on the setlist as well as "Quitter". They also completely ignored
their last album, "The Lower Side of Uptown". I would have loved to have heard 1 or 2
tracks from that. But, those are minor gripes, this was still a tremendous show. They
will be touring all summer (with Local H), so please go see them and support!

Vandoliers [not recorded] & Local H [recorded] opened the show.
