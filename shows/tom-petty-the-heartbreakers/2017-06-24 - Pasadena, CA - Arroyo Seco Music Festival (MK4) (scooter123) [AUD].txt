Tapers Notes:   

Brutally hot day and overflowing crowds made this one of the tougher shows to tape in
the past few years.  It basically involved 6 hours of standing in 90 degree heat shoulder
to shoulder so I could muscle my way into a tapers position, just behind the pit.  

But we were rewarded with a nearly two hour Tom Petty show.
