08/31/2005
Tom Petty & The Heartbreakers
Red Rocks Amphitheater
Morrison, CO


Uploader MikeMono note:
This is one of the last shows on Tom Petty & the Heartbreakers' 2005 summer tour, which would end a few nights later with two nights in a row at The Gorge. The crowd can be a bit loud at times, especially during "It'll All Work Out," one of the few things that makes this boot unique. But the band sounds great playing this mostly greatest hits setlist. Enjoy!
