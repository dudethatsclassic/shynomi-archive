Tom Petty and The Heartbreakers 
Santa Barbara/CA - County Bowl

First Night

I dedicate this tape to my Dad. May 24 is his birthday. He turned 22 when Tom had this gig. He Loves You Don't Know How It Feels and It's Good To Be King. When I listen to It's Good To Be King, I think about the times he told me about the hideouts he had all over the place when he was a kid. He met HIS sweet little queen, who can't run away, my mom, back in 1998 and they both got married in 1999.
