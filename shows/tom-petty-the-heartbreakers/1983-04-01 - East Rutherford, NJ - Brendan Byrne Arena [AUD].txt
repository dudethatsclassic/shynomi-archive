Tom Petty & The Heartbreakers - Lowe - Carrack : The Lostbrook Tape Series - Volume 20

I was so obsessed with progressive rock that I paid little attention to the rise 
of Tom Petty and foolishly missed opportunities to see him perform in smaller venues.  
Petty’s genius could no longer be ignored as he continued to pile up the hits, finally 
dragging me out of my “one story town” for the Long After Dark tour.  Recorded and 
photographed from fifth row center.


April 1, 1983
Brendan Byrne Arena
East Rutherford, NJ
USA
 
Recording Equipment: Internal Mic - Sanyo tape deck - TDK SD C90 - Alesis TapeLinkUSB - Audacity - WAV
Taper: Lostbrook
Mastering: CQ
