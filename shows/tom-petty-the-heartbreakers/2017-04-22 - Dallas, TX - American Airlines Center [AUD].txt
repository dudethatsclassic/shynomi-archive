Nice show from Tom for his 40th Anniversary tour. I was hoping for a few more rarely played songs but
still it was a good setlist. I did have some talkers and a few drinks around me so their presence is felt on
the recording but it is what the experience and this show was like. But I will never get why someone talks about
wanting to hear American Girl all night but gets so drunk they can't stand up or enjoy it by the time it gets played.
Some of the quiter parts could use a boost in volume but I let it intact here and people can adjust to their own tase.
I just thought it might raise some of the talking aroubd me so I left it alone for the posting.
