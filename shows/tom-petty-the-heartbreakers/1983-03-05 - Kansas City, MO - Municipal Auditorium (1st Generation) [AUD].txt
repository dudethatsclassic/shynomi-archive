This share comes from my 1st generation tape, it's a nice capture of the Kansas City stop on the "Long After Dark" tour. There is just a bit of distance to it but it's very clear and clean with nice instrument separation. There were two dozen or so instances of what sounds like mic bumps but I've repaired all of these and 99% of them are seamless now.

Typical set for the '83 tour, always nice to here "Route 66" get a slot.
