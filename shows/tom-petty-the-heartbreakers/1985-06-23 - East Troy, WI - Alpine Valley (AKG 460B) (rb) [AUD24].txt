Tom Petty June 23rd 1985 Alpine Valley Music Theater East Troy Wis USA ** POSTED BY ANALOG **


Mics: AKG 460B w/ ck1 capsules
Recorder: Sony PCM-F1 Digital Audio Processor w/Sony SL2000 Beta video recorder 2 channel digital recorder
Master Beta Tape played back on SAME GEAR (not exact machines/calibrated) analog out > Naim NAC 52 Preamplifier >
Recorded to Nagra Seven @ 24b/192khz
Location: About 10th row floor right center
Sony Sound Forge just to track. 



Here is another PCM recording. After three years I hung it up. LOVED the head room, but the sound being a tad dry (new tech stuff) wore on me after a few years... 

I was too close to the stage at this classic venue. This still has the power you would hope for! RIP Tom! Very glad this came out well. One more archive success. No dropouts BTW! let me know how you enjoyed this. Enjoy, RB
