Tom Petty & The Heartbreakers
October 6, 1995
Coleman Coliseum
Tuscaloosa, AL

Source: pre-FM Album Network Cassettes > FLAC. 

Edited to remove annoying announcer comments & remastered by Mr. Sifter, July 2024. 

I'd been looking for years for a better quality source of this show I remember taping off the radio myself in 1995, a cassette I played innumerable times the next year or two. Finally, a pre-FM source appeared, apparently the show was distributed on cassette instead of CD, it's a great listen but not the most refined mix, likely due to the short turnaround time from recording to distribution. Interesting that nothing from this show was used for either the Live Anthology or the 'Wildflowers Live' set that came with the 2020 reissue. 

Tom Petty's only ever show in this town, presumably more songs were played (certainly "Love Is A Long Road" and "Learning To Fly") but these were the only ones broadcast.
