Notes:  After all these years this was my first time seeing Tom and the band, and they sounded amazing.  
I didn't have the greatest spot for taping since I ended up in section 225 which is in the upper deck and
off to the far right if you're facing the stage.  The sound wasn't bad up there, but Steve's bass drum was
a little over-powering at times.  As can be expected, there were a few moments of crowd stupidity, but overall
it turned out fine.  Enjoy!
