Notes: Not the best capture sound wise, nothing an EQ can't help, but after five years I've only seen a couple shows from this Petty/Nicks tour, so I guess it will have to do. Also, this was a very rowdy crowd - talkers, singers, clappers, screamers and even a drunk getting kicked out and dragged past us by security! Luckily, they all come and go and don't ruin the recording, but they are there at times. It truly was a crazy scene at MSG that Tuesday night. Can't imagine what would have happened if the show was on a Saturday night. Trey Anastasio opened the show, but we did not tape him. Enjoy!
