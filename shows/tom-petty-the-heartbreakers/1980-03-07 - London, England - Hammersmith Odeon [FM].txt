Notes
Tom Petty And the Heartbreakers were on the road for their 1979 breakthrough album, Tom Petty And The Heartbreakers - Damn The Torpedoes, when they recorded a handful of shows between 1979 and 1980 for the King Biscuit Flower Hour. These three sides capture one of those shows recorded at London's Hammersmith Odeon on 7 March 1980. Another Tom Petty King Biscuit show was released in April 1980 on Tom Petty And The Heartbreakers - King Biscuit Flower Hour.
