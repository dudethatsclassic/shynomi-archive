LUCIFER BURN Presents A New Series On DIME. 
"The STEVE JOBE Master Series" 

Steve Jobe is a personal friend of mine. 
Steve is from the Dayton, Ohio area, like Myself. 
I have known Steve since the late 1990's. 
We both were at one time heavily invovled in "tape trading circles". 
Steve started recording Live Concerts as early as 1984. 
But with early primitive equipment that wasn't his, he only recorded a few shows. 
Steve got more serious latter on once he purchused a Sony D6 in 1996. 
Steve continued to record concerts up through 2003 before he finally stopped. 
Steve recorded concerts in many different parts of the U.S.A. 
Steve recorded shows as far away as East Rutherford, New Jersey, Memphis, Tennesse & Chicago, Illinois. 
Steve mostly recorded shows here in Ohio in Columbus, Cincinnati & Dayton. 

So let's get started with this next one !
