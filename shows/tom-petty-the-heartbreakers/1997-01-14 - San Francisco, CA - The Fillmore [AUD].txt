Tom Petty & The Heartbreakers
Live at The Fillmore
San Francisco, CA
1997-01-14

Traded this audience recording (via 2 CDs of wav files) over a decade ago and so don’t have the information. Please do not sell.

Converted from WAV to FLAC.

Though the recording isn’t the greatest, it’s notable for many reasons: first, it’s one of the first few live performances of “Walls,” and in contrast to later Fillmore shows which would do acoustic renditions of this song, this one is electric; second, “Climb that Hill” was only played at this show; third, the appearances of rarities like “On the Street,” “Trailer,” and “Keeping Me Alive.”

Enjoy!
