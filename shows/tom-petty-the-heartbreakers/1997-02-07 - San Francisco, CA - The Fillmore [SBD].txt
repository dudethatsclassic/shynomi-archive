This excellent set was uploaded by klynch86 back in April. It is a superb-sounding boot 
(spectrals/frequency analyses look perfect!), from a KSAN broadcast of the entire final 
concert of TP's sold-out 20-night stand at the Fillmore in February 1997. 

Unfortunately, the original upload was as shn files which had been imperfectly extracted 
from a CD which was burned with SBEs intact. This resulted in very audible gaps in between 
each track, as well as a bit of the beginning of the following track showing up at the 
end of each track, thus making it difficult to remove these gaps. I used a 
lengthy method, described briefly below, to remove these silences 
**AND ONLY THESE SILENCES**. I could have done the same thing manually, but that 
would likely have resulted in the loss of a few bits from the originally uploaded files, 
which would not have been acceptable to me. What you get as a result is perfect, 
seamless transitions in between tracks, without any gaps, audible or otherwise. 
I have not removed even a single bit of the non-silent parts of this recording--only 
the silent parts have been removed, making a true, lossless, non-SBE, non-TAO, gapless 
recording. **NO BITS HAVE BEEN HARMED IN THE MAKING OF THIS RECORDING!!.**

Here's the dilly: If you have like this upgrade, and have the old files on the hard drive, 
please consider seeding discs #2 and 3 for a while, until I can get to 100%. Then, I will 
do the same fix on those, and you'll have this great concert in the manner that the 
good Lord intended you to have it. I'll put some links below, as well as a sample. 
Artwork, md5, SFV are included.

Thanks to klynch86, the original uploader of this great boot, and to Andre at EAC for 
the free tools which made this fix (painful but) possible. 

Enjoy!

--Doc
