Great show from Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers. It has been several years since I last saw him
so I was really looking forward to it. The new CD is really good and I like the songs they played from it.
I had not seen him perform A Woman in Love since the 80s so that was a nice treat. I have always felt Mike 
Campbell was one of the most underrted guitarist in rock. and again tonight he did not disappoint. There 
were a lot of talkers around me so you can hear them alot on the recording.

Enjoy!
