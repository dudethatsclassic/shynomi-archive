This is the second night of the "Southern Accents" Tour, it appears to be under-circulated in the torrent era. 
The setlist didn't change much from night to night and this shows it was locked in from the start. 

It's a solid show but the band seems to still be getting its legs underneath it, they don't really attack the songs like later on in the tour and at times approaches them somewhat conservatively which tends to create a lack of energy.

Quality is good needing a pretty healthy pitch adjustment and some subtle mastering to bring out some buried frequencies, recorded fairly close to the stage with very little audience noise in the taper's vicinity. The master would be quite good if it was to ever surface...thanks to my old trading partner of 30+ years MR for the files.
