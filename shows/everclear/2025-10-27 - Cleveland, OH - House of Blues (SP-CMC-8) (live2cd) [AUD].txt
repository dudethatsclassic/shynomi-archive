Everclear are touring in support of their "Sparkle And Fade" record which is
celebrating 30 years in 2025. They are playing the full album interspliced
throughout the set. Art Alexakis has been pretty vocal about his MS diagnosis
9 years ago, so its a blessing he is still going out and playing live. I have
been fairly hard on him over the years (I saw a few of their Summerland tours),
and haven't seen them since 2017 because - well, they bored the hell outta me.
But, lowering my expectations (greatly) for this while knowing we were going
to get a lot of deep tracks from one of their best records had me a little
excited. They definitely delivered a good show (not great) and Freddy Herrera
did a wonderful job (no pun intended) giving Art some breaks every few songs
to speak to the crowd and thank the opening bands, etc. Some of it was a bit
repetitive and monotonous, but I get why they were doing it. Art's vocals
were a tad high in the mix, so keep that in mind when listening. I also had
to boost the bass (which is rare for rock shows) to get this sounding better.
iZotope RX7 was used to remove some screams, whoos, and yelps when needed.

Sponge and Local H opened. [both recorded].
