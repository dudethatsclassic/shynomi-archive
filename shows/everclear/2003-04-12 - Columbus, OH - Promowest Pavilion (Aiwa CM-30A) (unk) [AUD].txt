This one has a couple issues. This was the first time I saw a show inside at Promowest Pavilion (later renamed The LC, or Lifestyle Communities Pavilion). We ended up going upstairs and were at the back of the club. Thus, the sound is a little distant. I also had a chatty neighbor so you can hear bits and pieces of their conversation. There is also a few second drop near the beginning of "Learning How To Smile", and a poorly timed but extremely necessary bathroom break during "Father of Mine". Finally, the Sony was acting up a bit, or I had a bad tape or something, but there are a couple spots where the tape drags slightly. Other than that, it was Art's birthday and the band really had a good time. Enjoy this one!!
