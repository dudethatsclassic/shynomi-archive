Notes: 
The 26th show of the Sparkle & Fade 30th anniversary tour. Sponge and Local H opened and both were recorded.
Everclear played a great show. Scott from Local H joined them for the LedZepp snippet and Vinnie helped out on
Santa Monica. I don't know why they're doing a SM snippet early in the set but I left that a part of track 5. I like the
setlist but they shoulda/should drop Father and New Life and play some deeper cuts instead. They have so much
great stuff in the catalog. This was my first visit to this venue since the major remodeling and I think it turned out
well. The new p.a. is small but it sounds great; a pair of L-Acoustics L2D's and various center and side fills along
the walls and ceiling. I'm looking forward to catching some more good bands at this place. In Audition I gave a
subtle increase in the 2-6.3khz range with the EQ and a hard limit to -.1db with a 9db boost. I am quite pleased
with the results. Cover art is included in the torrent. Enjoy the show.
-kevdogg
