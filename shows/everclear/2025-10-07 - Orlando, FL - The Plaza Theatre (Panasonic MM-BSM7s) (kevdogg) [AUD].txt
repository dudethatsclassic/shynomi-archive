Notes: 
The 22nd show of the Sparkle & Fade 30th anniversary tour. Sponge and Local H opened and both were recorded.
The band were in good spirits and the show was loud but well mixed. I saw only a couple empty seats so I'm pretty
certain the place was sold out by showtime. Scott from Local H joined them for the LedZepp snippet but no Vinnie
on Santa Monica this night:/ I don't know why they're doing a SM snippet early in the set but I left that a part of track 5.
I like the setlist but they shoulda/should drop Father and New Life and play some deeper cuts instead. They have so
much great stuff in the catalog. In Audition I gave a subtle increase in the 2-6.3khz range with the EQ and a hard limit
to -.1db with a 7db boost. I am quite pleased with the results. Cover art is included in the torrent. Enjoy the show.
