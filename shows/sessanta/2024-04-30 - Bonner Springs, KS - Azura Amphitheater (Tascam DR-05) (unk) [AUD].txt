There was some concern on the day of the show as to if this would start on time or not happen at all (tornadoes and severe weather were spotted on the radars). Ever since my Greta Van Fleet debacle, I have been approaching taping somewhat differently with regards to urgency. I bring this up because my younger daughter had a strings concert that night at Memorial Hall (yes, that Memorial Hall in KCK that is no longer used for concerts). I had suspicions that if the show couldn't start by 9, it wasn't going to happen at all. We had dinner plans at around 7 and with the show not starting at the time it was scheduled to, I decided to live my life. I did leave for the show once we arrived home, but all I was able to catch is what is here. Too bad, because what I saw was very good. On the other hand, it was a condensed show anyway so even if I had seen the whole night it was not going to be a full show (I wondered if the band was going to do the entire show, but with an appearance in Chicago the next night I suspect it would have been a logistical nightmare). I am glad I saw some of it, but bummed that I didn't see it all. This is probably the last recordings of those currently available (June 2024 as of this writing), but it may be a nice keepsake or document if you were there.
