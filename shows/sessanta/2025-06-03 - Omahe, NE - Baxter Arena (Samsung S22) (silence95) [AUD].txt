COMMENTS:
I'm just gonna say that this tape is not great. I brought my H1e to the
venue but left it in the car I was dropped off in as I had never snuck
gear into a venue before and I didn't know what the security would be
like - metal detectors? A patdown? Even worse? Fortunately it was only
a metal detector which I'm pretty sure I could have snuck my H1e through
but unfortunately my ride was already long gone by the time I got in.
So I had to settle for a phone recording, which turned out about as I
expected - somewhat muddy and unclear. I'm sorry I couldn't get a better
tape, folks... Anyways, this was my first time seeing Primus, and my first
time hearing anything by APC and Puscifer. Primus was fuckin' awesome,
although I would've appreciated one or two more deep cuts. APC were great
too, didn't like Puscifer as much though. Definitely will be checking
them out. Apologies if the lineups for APC and Puscifer are wrong. For
acts one and two my phone was in my pocket, which turned out more
listenable than expected. For act three it was in a cupholder pointed up
at the ceiling, which turned out less listenable than expected. I was
trying my hardest to not get kicked out and turned into SPAM so I couldn't
hold my phone out in front of me, even if that would've resulted in a
better tape. Samples of the "good" parts in comments.
