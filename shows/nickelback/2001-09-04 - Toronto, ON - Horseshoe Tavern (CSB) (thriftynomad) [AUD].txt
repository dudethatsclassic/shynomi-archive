NOTES: This was the "pre-album release" show they did at the Horseshoe in Toronto as part of Dave Bookman's (RIP) Nu Music Night. This is a great tape from a fun, small show and I forgot about the MD for 20+ years because there are a few skips on the master MD that I haven't been able to transfer cleanly. They show up mostly in the first 3 songs. If anyone has an MD deck capable of directly ripping the ATRAC file, message me on dime (thriftynomad) and maybe we can get a clean rip.

Anyways, it's been long enough! Here's Nickelback just as they were about to get really big in a room with about 300 people, a week before they released 'Silver Side Up' on 9/11/01. The internet also has another show in Cleveland listed for this date but Chad mentions that the album comes out in a week so this is definitely the right date and they were in Toronto, not Cleveland.
