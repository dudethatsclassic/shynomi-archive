Pavement
1999-06-22
Cotton Club, Atlanta GA

An ACIDJACK upload (acidjack@acidjack.com)

AKG 414-TLII>Oade M248>SBM-1>D8>CDR (Marantz CDR630)>FLAC(8)

Special thanks to KindTaper for the clone.

Great memory here.  Part of a 3-night run that my buddy Cory and I covered,  driving from NC up to DC for the 9:30 Club show and then down to Atlanta to cover both shows there.  We joined up with KindTaper in Atlanta who was nice enough to let us patch out of his rig.  The AKG 414-TLII's are extremely accurate mics.  Who knew a ramshackle lo-fi indie band like Pavement would ever inspire folks to record them with such fine equipment?
