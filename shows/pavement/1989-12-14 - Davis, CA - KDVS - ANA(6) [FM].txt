Pavement - 1989 12 14 @ KDVS, Davis, CA

Show note:
I think this is just Spiral Stairs, S.M. and Gary Young. Tracks 2, 4, 6 and 13 are just records played while the band changes strings. Tracks 15 and 16 are the band jamming to the songs which are mixed in from tapes.  Played in the studios at KDVS, University of California Davis.

Tracks 2, 4, 6 and 13 removed as they are officially released versions.  You can hear them on SoundCloud here: https://soundcloud.com/john-loughney/sets/pavement
