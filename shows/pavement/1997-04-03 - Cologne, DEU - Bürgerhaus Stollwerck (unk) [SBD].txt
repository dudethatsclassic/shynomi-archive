Pavement
4.3.97
Koln or Cologne, not sure which, but definetly somewhere in Germany.
Venue Unknown.


Source: SBD (Most likely DAT w/o any digital frontend)

Lineage: Trade CDr > EAC > WAV > FLAC

Commentary...
The board feed sounds okay (not spectacular, but entirely listenable).
Also, the taper misses the first couple verses of Stereo.
Some pretty interesting stuff, especially a remarkable "Gangsters and 
Pranksters", the extended ending to "Shady Lane" (which didn't quite
make the album), a fairly early version of "The Hexx",
a mostly raging "Conduit for Sale", with "Fight This Generation" and 
"Zürich is Stained" to round out the set. 

Sound is A-/B+, show is A, imo.
