After getting skunked in the presale months ago I had little hope of attending this show as the resale ask was 4 times face.  But as fate would have it I was given a Ticketmaster gift card for my birthday back in August and the week before the show TM released a bunch of tickets behind the soundboard.  A little far back from the sweet spot but we do what we can.  So the recording is a touch boomy.  Maybe you can twaddle your knobs and filter some of that out on playback.

If I could have requested one song it would have been Grounded, so I was happy right off the bat.  The band seemed relaxed and playful which was nice to see in a group that has been plagued by whatever sort of angst took place back in the day, they certainly appeared to be enjoying themselves.

I thought I had pissed away all my chances to see this band so I was really excited for the occasion. I was not alone.  The crowd was very much into the show and many songs had full audience participation.  Not ideal for taping but the energy it created was palpable.

So go see Pavement if they come to your town.  You should have all the albums by now, if not they can help you with that too.  Splurge for the autographed poster and display it proudly for your incredulous teenager.
