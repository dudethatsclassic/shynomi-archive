Pavement - 2022 09 17 @ The Paramount Theatre Seattle, Washington.

Recording notes: Recorded from front row center directly in front of Ibold. Weird spot because the bass was booming and I was in front of the stacks. There were smaller monitors on the front of the stage facing the front row audience but, I wasn’t directly in front of those. There is some clipping that starts about three quarters through because at one point I was tired of holding the device and placed it on the stage. Dumb move I know. Doesn’t really sound bad to me though. Anyone interested in trying to reduce clipping drop me a comment and I’ll send you the file. Light compression and limiter from the built in Motiv app on the Iphone. SM’s Benson head and amp sounded amazing hearing it directly from the stage. Spiral seemed low in the mix compared to the other shows I recently attended. 

Show notes: Premiere of Painted Soldiers. SM seemed like he may have hit up a weed shop before the show. Could have been an exaggeration of his spaced out stage persona.
