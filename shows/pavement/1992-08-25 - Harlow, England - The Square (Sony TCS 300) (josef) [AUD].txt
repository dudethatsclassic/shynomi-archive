I think you're going to like this......

A secret warm up gig before the band's Reading Festival appearance. The venue was a small and unlikely club in a distant town. It was quite a trek from where I live. I went with my best mate Nigel in his old Morris Marina (as usual) and Matt came along too. Matt was completely off his tits on speed - but that's another story. We'd already seen Pavement a few times since they'd hit the UK earlier in 92 (Clapham Grand, The Old Trout at Windsor and ULU spring to mind - maybe others?). By this stage my taping exploits were infrequent but Nigel and I had been to the Square earlier that year to see Stereolab playing a low key gig (June 92?) and knew what a great venue it was. So, I dusted down my old TCS 300. Here are the results. Not bad for 1982 technology. At the time I gave copies of the tape to Matt, Nigel, his brother Andrew and another mate and big, big Pavement fan Jerry. None of us were tape traders but it may have dribbled out to a wider circle. To be honest I'd love to know if that's the case or not. Does anyone already have this from a tape source?

Anyway, a cracking gig with bags of atmosphere, a crunchy sound, two drummers and a bitchy singer. Looking back now, Gary was coming in for flack even back then. Poor bastard - this gig was the day after he got married! As you can see, much of 'Slanted' and some earlier tracks. 'Here' is best described as the 'John Peel' version. Lovely bass work in 'Summer Babe'. TRINITY TITANS is a corny sports chant thing - but still nice. If anyone knows what track 8 is then please let me (and the band?) know.

I'll put a sample in 'comments'. Trust me - it's a great recording.

Enjoy
Josef
