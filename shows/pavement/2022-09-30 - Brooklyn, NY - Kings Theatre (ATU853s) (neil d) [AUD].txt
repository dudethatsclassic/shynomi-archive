I wasn't initially going to go to this show, but a friend had an extra ticket and I couldn't pass up Pavement within walking distance of my apartment. Our seats were way in the back of the Kings Theatre orchestra section, which normally isn't great in the cavernous former movie palace, but the U853 cardioid mics did a good job of zeroing in on the sound from the stage to where this recording sounds significantly better than it did in the moment. (The guy in the row behind me who brought his own tambourine, for example, is inaudible here.)
