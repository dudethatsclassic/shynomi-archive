A complete headline set at the now closed down London Astoria. Crowd was loud & talkative as per usual at this venue. 
I believe this was one of the NME Brats shows that they put on at the start of the year. 
Great supporting bill of Gorkys Zygotic Mynci, Mogwai and Broadcast.
