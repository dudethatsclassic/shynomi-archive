Pavement - 2022 09 13 @ Masonic Auditorium, San Francisco

Show note:
Caught San Francisco nights 2 and 3.  I think I enjoyed this most, probably to the strong string of songs that they opened with.  Night 3 might have a slight edge on recording quality, based on my recording location, but both came out well enough. Pavement returned with their classic line-up, but augmented with Rebecca Clay Cole on keyboards and whatnot.  Oh, and met up with KingCactus during the intermission, which was nice.
