Liz Phair at the Sinclair, Cambridge, MA, June 06, 2018, audience recording.

This was recorded with Zoom H2 with internal mics, on the low mic gain setting.  H2 lined into computer by USB, wav sent to Magix Audio Cleaning Lab 12.  Magix program was used for track markings.  Flac created with Trader's Little Helper, SBE and torrent work also done with TLH.

This recording came out great.  I recorded from in front of the soundboard, and stayed put for the whole set.  There are moments of singing along and hooting and hollering (not by me), but nothing too annoying.  I went with my ex-wife - that's how you really get the most out of "Divorce Song."  My only complaint about the set is that it was short, just over an hour.  There are a bunch of ok cell-phone photos included.  I did not record the opener (Soccer Mom).
