This is a decent recording, but not great, mostly because of the running commentary from the people sitting next to me. I wish I'd told them to be quiet! I also missed the first few seconds (and there is some noise from handling the recorder after I turned it on) because Liz Phair walked on stage and began playing with no announcement. The lights weren't even dimmed before she came out.

And no, "Quiet" was not a tribute to the headlining band!
