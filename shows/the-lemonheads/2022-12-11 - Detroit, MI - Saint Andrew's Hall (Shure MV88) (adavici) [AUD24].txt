Detroit, MI
Saint Andrew's Hall

Opener: Juliana Hatfield (also recorded) 
And On Being an Angel (not recorded)

Master recording from the left of the soundboard, about 3/4 back. 
 
Sure MV88 Mic - iPod Touch/Motiv-Audio app (24/48) -  
ProTools (mastered/EQ/Widen) - SoundForge (Splits, fades, FLAC).  
 
Sound quality is very good - I'd rate it a B/B-. It sounds pretty good
but there is a lot of crowd contamination that detracts a bit - nearby
people talking, joking, singing, etc. I tried to move around a bit to 
avoid it and it worked on and off, but you'll hear it for sure. Saint 
Andrew's was packed and real estate on the floor was limited, the crowd
was rowdy and loud.  

Great set from Evan Dando and The Lemonheads. Evan was with it and sang
and played very well. This is the It's A Shame About Ray 30th anniversary
tour so tracks 12-23 are that record front to back. I saw that Juliana 
Hatfield came out and sang on a couple songs on previous dates and was 
disappointed that didn't happen here, but a great performance nonetheless.
