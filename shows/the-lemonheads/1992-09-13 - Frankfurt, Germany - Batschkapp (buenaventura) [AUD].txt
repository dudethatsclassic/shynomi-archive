Show notes:
Another great show from the Buenaventura archives. Despite some tape issues with the tape flip, but this was an awesome show. There are currently two 1992-shows on the tracker, one from about a week earlier on this tour, so the setlist is similar, but this was really Evan Dando at his best. There's a sweet short clip of him doing an acoustic song, allegedly after this show, but guess it would haven been before, rather than after https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lFFYsJZgmM0

Transfer Notes:
Tape slips a bit at the beginning of Die Right Now, and there's some speed issues during the song. The song is cut also by the tape flip.
