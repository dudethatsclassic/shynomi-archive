Eels
2008-04-27
An evening with Eels
Enmore theatre
Sydney, Australia
Last show of 50 date world tour

record: CA11(Clipped on collar, DFC 20m from stage) > CA-9100 > Tascam DR-1 24/48
transfer: DR-1 > USB > Audiocity (Dithered to 16bit, normalised and track splits, fade in & out of encores) > Xact convert to flac
taper:  Exileded


Great show, being the last show of the tour I was hoping for a longer set but what the Band (E and the Chet) held back in quantity they more than made up for it in terms of quality.
As a fully seated show my location was almost perfect for taping, 5m infront of SBD right on the isle. 

That first 20 seconds of Magic World suffer from lots of handling noise, I wasn't expecting the band to hit the stage as the lights went down, got fairly caught out as I hadn't clipped up yet... 

Please support the band by buying their CD's and merchandise, E's book "Things the grandchildren should know" comes highly recommended
