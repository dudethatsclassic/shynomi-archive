As a fun little project I wanted to try something different. I merged the audience recording of EELS at the Lowlands 1997 festival with the FM broadcast as posted here on Dime ages ago by user 'frankvink'. The results of this matrix recording are quite good. This is now the complete show. 

I used as much of the available FM-source as possible. Sometimes the transition between AUD and FM is hardly noticeable (Not Ready Yet, Mental) and in other cases (especially Looking Out The Window With A Blue Hat On) you can hear quite clearly where the AUD recording ends and the FM version takes over, as the song was cut in on live radio back in the day after a news broadcast. But all in all I'm quite happy with the results. 

The audience recordings were made at the center of the GOLF tent of the festival, in front of the sound booth. The other songs were broadcast by 3FM radio in The Netherlands.
