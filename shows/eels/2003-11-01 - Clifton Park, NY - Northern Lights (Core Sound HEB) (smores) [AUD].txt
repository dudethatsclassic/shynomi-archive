notes: I am not the biggest Eels fan. In fact I don't "get" some of it. According to online setlists from shows around this time there was only slight deviation from the standard setlist. That said, once the recorded version of The Star-Spangled Banner came on over the P.A. I left, and my tape had run out. He had done three encores and the P.A. was playing a pre-recorded song. The next day I read a review of the show in the local paper and they said that the show ended with a cover of Whiskey River. At some shows the last song listed was The Star-Spangled Banner, while others did show a fourth encore that included Whiskey River. Anyway, by this time, out of tape, tired, and really thinking the show was over- I was gone. This is all I recorded of the Eels set. Also, for some reason, my levels were very high on this, bordering on distortion. Show is listenable, but not as clean as it should have been.
