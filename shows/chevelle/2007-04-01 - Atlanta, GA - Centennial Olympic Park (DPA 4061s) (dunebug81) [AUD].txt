Music festival titled MyCokeFest 2007. Great name. This was a free daytime concert
celebrating the NCAA Men's Final Four, which was held at the Georgia Dome the
following day. Other artists performing were hellogoodbye, Maroon 5, LL Cool J and
Sugarland. I have the Sugarland set and will be sharing it. It sounds like the taper
moved to a better location after the first 2 tracks. The rest of the show sounds better
thereafter. Get Some was cut short after Pete noticed security "threw that kid down
like a sack of potatoes". Remainder of the set went without incident. Enjoy the show.
