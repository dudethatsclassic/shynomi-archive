Notes:
"This is from a copy of a cassette purchased many years ago at Mystery Train Records  in Harvard Square
in Cambridge MA.  The cassette (which my friend bought and was some sort of Type II tape) was dubbed
onto Maxell XLII (same speed) with Onkyo TA-RW313 deck.  The tape went from the Onkyo into the
computer with Audigy Soundblaster SE.  It ran a little fast, so I "adjusted" the speed as much as possible
with Magix Music On Cd And Dvd program (I stretched it out by a little under a minute).  Same program
was used to make track markings, and burn the disc.  Disc converted to wav with Roxio Easy Media 8,
flac with Flac Frontend.

The sound quality on this gets a B+.  Very cool show.  There is an edit between tracks 16 and 17, probably
due to a tape stop before encores.  The last track ends abruptly with the show being disrupted by a fight.
I'm not sure if there is a longer version available, but I hope y'all enjoy this.  The other side of the tape has a
show from Westlands in New York from 1994-09-24, which needs to be drastically speed corrected, and I'll
get that one up shortly.  Turn it up fuckin' loud!"
-original seeder
