Notes:
"This is from a cassette purchased by a friend of mine at Mystery Train in Cambridge, MA.
The tape (a Type II, though I'm not sure precisely what kind) was dubbed in real time with
Onkyo TA-RW313 deck, onto a Maxell XLII.  That tape went from the Onkyo into Audigy
Soundblaster SE.  Tracks were made, as well as speed correction and burning the disc,
with Magix Music On CD And Dvd program.  The disc was converted to wav with Roxio
Easy Media 8, flac with Flac Frontend.

The sound quality on this gets a B (maybe +).  It was stretched out by about a minute with
the Magix program, and still may sound a touch fast.  There was a break during track 17,
which I removed mostly, though it's detectable.  As for the show itself, it rules.  There are
occasional technical difficulties with the guitars (Lars, I think), and they royally fuck up
Salvation for a few.  My Life is a Sick Of It All cover.  Let's Go!"
-original seeder
