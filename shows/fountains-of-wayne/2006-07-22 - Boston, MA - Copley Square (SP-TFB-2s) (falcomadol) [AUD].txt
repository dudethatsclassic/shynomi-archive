This concert was outdoors on the lawn at Copley Square in front of the library in Boston (back when it had a lawn). It was a beautiful day as I remember it, with an audience full of the kind of people who fill a city in the early summer evening on a weekday. I was off to the right side of the audience. 

Forgive my fiddling with the earpieces in the first song, it gets better. I think this might have been the actual first show I recorded.

Around this time I was recording a bunch of concerts on Minidisc but I only transferred about four of them, and two I already posted on archive.org years ago. The last one (a Shonen Knife show at Middle East Upstairs) is still MIA but I know it came back into my possession after being loaned for a while. The Minidiscs are probably all gone for good now. I found my mics and the battery box but not the MZ-S1 which is a good indicator that they were disposed of at some point that I can't recall, but it's always possible that they're in a box somewhere in the basement.
