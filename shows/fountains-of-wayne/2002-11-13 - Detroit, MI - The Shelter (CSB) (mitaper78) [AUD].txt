Notes: I went to this show being a fan of the song 'Radiation Vibe' from the 1990's. Was not a huge fan or anything like that but decided to make the trek to Detroit from Michigan State University in East Lansing, MI to check it out. Performance was strong but also loose. Back in the day, the Shelter was a tiny little venue underneath a slightly larger venue called St. Andrews Hall (both still there) and both were sketchy on the outside as well as the inside. Fountains of Wayne absolutely rocked the venue. The crowd, sold out, but we are talking maybe 400 people max, were crazy for the performance and band. Recording-wise, I stood directly in front of the left speaker and did not move thus this is a solid stack recording with minimal crowd noise. As a note, during the break between encore, I walked up to the sound engineer and asked him for a setlist. He said he would (you can hear us talk). This was transferred and tracked by me ages ago so if it is not perfect, oh well. Until 2025, this source has never seen the light of day. Hope you enjoy.
