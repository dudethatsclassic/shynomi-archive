notes: so this one Sunday, I wandered into the Smokehouse for brunch...
Jen, one of the owners asked me what I was doing later that day... "why, 
what's up"??.... "oh, the Old 97's are playing here at 3"... "say what"??? 
turns out that the Old 97's, who had played a sold out
show at the 930 Club in DC the night before, were going to play a small
private acoustic show for listeners of WRNR, the local radio station.... as part
of a contest, the station had people submit band tshirt designs, with
the band themselves deciding the winner.... the winner then got to pick where
the special show would take place, and that person chose the Smokehouse... 
I asked her about taping the show, she called the station manager, and I got
the go ahead..... the sound system wasn't much in terms of loudness, just a couple
of monitors up on stands, and people crowded all around.... so the music was not 
the loudest part of the recording, the crowd and applause was.... did the best I 
could to reduce that so I could raise the levels on the music, but it ain't perfect... 
and the beginning of "Time Bomb" is a good example of how invasive a single clapper 
can be on a recording if that person is near the mic's, but overall, this is a decent 
recording... and we all got free tshirts with that winning design!!... so, all in all, 
a pretty nice day that started simply because I wandered into my local tavern thinking 
about omelets....
