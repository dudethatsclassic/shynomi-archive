Notes:

* = Originally recorded by The Beatles. [Tease]

The third of 3 consecutive shows I was attending, this was by far my favorite. 
The atmoshphere was very laid back (nobody pushing eachother) & it was just a
very enjoyable time. Even tho the setlist did not compare to the first 2 I 
attended, this one was something special. Unfortantely, the band had all
contracted some throat virus during their days off, so the set time was cut
short. Dan was drinking cough syrup w/ a gin & tonic onstage. Strange. 

Recording turned out phenominal, I taped directly below the left speaker stack
and had my mics positioned perfectly the entire 81 minutes. 
The only audio notes to mention, I removed the 4 minutes of crowd noise during the encore. 
I removed/cross-spliced this at the end of t12. This was to make sure the recording would
fit onto one Cd-R, which it now does.
