Notes:

The second of 3 consecutive shows I was attending, this show definitley had the
most energy of all 3. The crowd was very much into the show, and you can tell
by listening to their responses to the songs on this recording. The band sounded
great & this version of "Tuna In The Brine" bested the previous performance in
Columbus. Such a treat getting to hear that one live.

Recording turned out great, I taped literally directly in front of the "NO AUDIO
RECORDING" sign underneath the right stack. I got bumped a few times during
"Reflections" & "Insomnia" & the mic movement can be heard on the recording, but
its nothing too bad. No static or clicks are present. God, I wish this band would
tour the states more...
