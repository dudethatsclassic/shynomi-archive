Silverchair 
Summerfest-Lazer 103 Rock Stage 
Milwaukee, Wi. June 29th, 1999 
Broadcast Live On Lazer 103 FM 

FM->Mitsubishi 4 Head Hi-Fi VCR(High Grade Video Tape)->Sony Mini Disc(For any editing and track placement)->Pioneer stand-alone cd recorder(via optical cable)->CDr->WAV->Flac FrontEnd(level 6)->Flac->You 

What seems like an odd set-up to record actually provided me with may years of good results for these FM broadcasts from Milwaukee's Summerfest. The reason I did it this way was because I wanted to be able to record straight through without changing mini-discs or cd-r's. Around 2000-01 I was also just buying my first PC so doing any sort of editing there vs. mini disc was out of the question for this then newbie. I think the overall quality is excellent and only the best equipment and media was used year in and year out.
