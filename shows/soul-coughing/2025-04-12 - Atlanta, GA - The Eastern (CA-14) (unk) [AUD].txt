This was a "I never thought I'd get to see them" show for me, considering I didn't know who they
were until after they broke up and how well-known the acrimony between the members was. I'm glad
they were able to put that all behind them and do these shows, because they were awesome. I was a
bit disappointed that we didn't get "Rolling," especially considering it had been played at every
other show on this leg of the tour. The rest of the set was perfect though, so I can't complain
too much. The "down in Marietta" line in "Soft Serve" got a big cheer from the crowd, and after the
song Mike assured us he was talking about Marietta, GA and not Marietta, OH - although he does
tell Ohio the opposite...

I recorded from up in the balcony, just right of the center, and I think it came out near-perfect.
The only issue is that for about 20 seconds of "The Idiot Kings" starting around 0:38, there's some
crackling. Not sure exactly what happened there, but it's fairly brief and not too distracting.
