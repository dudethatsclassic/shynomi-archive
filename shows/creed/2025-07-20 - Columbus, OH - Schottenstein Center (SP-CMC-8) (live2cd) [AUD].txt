Notes:

First time seeing Creed since October 15th, 1999 - so, its been awhile.
The set was primarily focused on the first 2 albums with a few from the
third. I was hoping they would be playing "Weathered", but it was removed
from the setlist this year. The band sounded incredibly tight and if you
can stomach Scott Stapp's motivational speeches in between songs, this is
a great listen! I guess thats what the next track button is for. I did use
RX7 to remove some screams, yelps and unnecessary clapping when needed.

Daughtry and Mammoth were also on the bill. [both recorded].
