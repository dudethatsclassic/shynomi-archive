NOTES: 
Ok, took me a bit, as this didn't come out as great as other shows I posted from the cruise, its a bit rough but since nothing else showed up, I figured I should share. It was 
on the brink of storming, it was VERY windy, and the seas were rocking the ship quite a bit (even more later that night). Not sure why the band had to take a 5 minute break in 
the show but I'd think it has something to do with it seeming like a storm was gonna break at any minute. It literally was electric. 

During Say I, the SpaceX launch went off mid-song, very, very cool indeed. I had sent this recording to two different
friends that are more talented in cleaning things up than me but neither had a result they felt was worth sharing, so I did my best with the RX7 AI tool and did my best. It not
as bad as old bootleg cassettes were, you'll still be able to crank this up and enjoy it, and probably get the feeling like you were there next to me under the stars. Wanted to 
get this out before the tour starts which is in 2 days, of which I'm attending (my 5th Creed show of the year (4 on the 2 cruises)). Cheers.
