Notes:

*  Late Show with David Letterman appearances.
   "One" taken from February 3rd, 1999, "Higher" from November 16th, 1999.

When I used to record shows in the late 90s, I would transfer the MDs to my Gateway
PC (in wave) and burn them onto SONY CD-R's. I used to record a ton of late night
appearances and would sometimes add those to the CDs as fillers. That was the case
here. I never intended on trading out the audio from those Letterman shows, but
I kept them on here because they sounded really good. They were directly from my
HI-FI VCR recorded in SP mode from cable broadcasts. As far as the main set goes,
considering this was in the early days of my taping career, its very listenable.
I had to hold the microphone the entire show at my chest, but the results are quite
good from the rear lower bowl at The Palace of Auburn Hills. This album went on
to become certified diamond and was one of the biggest rock albums of the era. They
were exploding all over radio and TV at the time.
  
Also on the bill were Oleander and Our Lady Peace. [both recorded].
