Uploader’s Notes: 
This is a previously uncirculated concert recording that recently turned up after. The last song (You Gotta) Fight For Your Right (to Party!) was not recorded. The quality is pretty rough during Slow and Low and Brass Monkey - and yet not bad elsewhere. 

'Slow Ride' is not the entire song - it is just the following eight bars:

I got money, I got juice
I got to the party and I got loose
I got rhythms, I got rhymes
I got the girlies with the def behinds
I got ill, I got busted
I got dust and I got dusted
I got gold, I got funky
I got the new dance they call the Brass Monkey

Then they start into 'Brass Monkey'.
