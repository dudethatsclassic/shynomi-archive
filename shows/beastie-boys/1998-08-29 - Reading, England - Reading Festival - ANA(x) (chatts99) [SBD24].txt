So, after a recent Xmal Deutschland upload by 10thFlrClown, some off-site conversations have been had.

There should be a few more Xmal uploads coming over the next few weeks, however it has given me a bit
of a proverbial "kick up the back-side", I have been collecting live tapes since the early 80's, many
of these will have been previously digitized and will be in general circulation. Hopefully some of what
I will choose to upload from 2 boxes of tapes will be rare/new to circulation or maybe just upgrades
to generations already in circulation. All these from the "Box of Tapes" will be relatively low
generation as they would have been purchased at the many bootleg tape stalls from Camden Market or
Portobello Road Market or the various Record Fairs around London at the time, some may have been purchased
or swapped from/with like minded fans/collectors from the classified ads of NME/Sounds/Melody Maker etc.

In the comments of this torrent I will list a few of what I hope to upload next so maybe people can
