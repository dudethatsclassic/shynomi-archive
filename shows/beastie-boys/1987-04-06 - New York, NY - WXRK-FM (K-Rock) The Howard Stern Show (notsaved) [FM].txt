Here’s one fer 9thbeastie and some others who may be interested…no music here, just antics.

Fer some reason, the quality on this isn’t the best, considering K-Rock was one of the biggest stations in the NYC market, and it is from a 1st gen cassette, but it’s still very listenable. Quite hilarious too. It is missing the first segment, but still runs over an hour as the boys sit in fer Robin’s news segment.

Ad-Rock was secretly dating Molly Ringwald, so he gets busted on to reveal it on-air, to no avail.

Inside jokes about the “sneaky pouch” and the like leave ya kinda perplexed, but it’s pretty funny anyways.

I have never seen this one out and about. Figgered I’d share it, since it’s strewn across three CDs in my collection and I don’t want to lose it….
