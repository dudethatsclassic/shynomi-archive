Legendary first Def Jam hip hop show in Holland: double bill with Run DMC and the Beastie Boys. Beasties have been seeded before, but I don't recall seeing Run DMC here. Six months later, a second Def Jam tour would hit Holland: Public Enemy, Eric B & Rakim and LL Cool J (all three of which I will seed later). All of these were broadcast by VPRO radio.

There's a short snippet on the VPRO site currently, which has one additional track: Brass Monkey. You can get it here in 192kbps mp3: http://3voor12.vpro.nl/luisterpaal/playerpage.program.14002723.html
