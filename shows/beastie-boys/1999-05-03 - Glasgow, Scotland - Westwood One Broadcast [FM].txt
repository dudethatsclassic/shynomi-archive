Beastie Boys 
May 3rd, 1999
Scottish Exhibition & Conference Centre
Glasgow, Scotland
Liberated Boot CD-Wonder Boy Records #013
"Glasgow 99"

Another one dug out from the old Boot Box.
This was the first show of the "In the Round"
tour, which took place on a specially configured 360-degree rotating turntable stage and through a circular P.A. designed to literally surround the audience with sound.  Don't know if this
has been on DIME before--the boot CD is tersely entitled
"Glasgow 99" and is documented on many a BB boot site.
Sound is Very Good, audience is definitely present in the mix. This was originally a Westwood One/MTV broadcast and was 
apparently mixed very "live" in an attempt to capture the full
gestalt and craze of the first show of a radical worldwide tour.

Tested with Traders' Little Helper and confirmed as lossless.
Research confirms that this recording is not of the complete show-they played closer to 30 tunes in the full performance.
