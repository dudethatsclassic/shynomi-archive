Since my last Beastie seed was so hot around here, I figured let's spread some more B-boy love. I really enjoy coming across their boots, they are always a joy to work with as they are rather predictable and easy to fix. The vocals always dominate and the music gets lost, but bringin them back together takes very little effort and makes such an imporvement in the listening experience.

Another fine Rockpalast capture, the "undetermined" source shows very nice integrity in the scope. With little evidence of a video tone I'd say it's probably a pre-broadcast source. It was clean and primed for the remaster, which has made it bigger, badder...ill'er?

I would also guess that since this source adds up to 74 mintues that the last two songs were cut to make a single CD. Anybody has those other two tracks, you know where to find me. TIA.
