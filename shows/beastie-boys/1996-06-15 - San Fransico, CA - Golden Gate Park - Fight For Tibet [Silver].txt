


Artist: Beastie Boys
Title: Fight For Tibet
Venue: Polo Fields, Golden Gate Park, San Fransico, California
Date: June 15, 1996
Label: Blizzard Recordings
Catalogue No: BLZD148

COMPLETE SHOW – SOUNDBOARD


At the first Tibetan Freedom Festival in San Fransico, there were over 100,000 people there.  Now that’s a big show.  This is a recording of the Beastie Boys from that very show.  I believe it comes from a pre-FM source and is a soundboard show.  Although it is a soundvboard show, the sound is not wonderful.  The mixer, I am thinking, was most likely high.  This should please all Beastie fans as much as it does me… enjoy!

Other bands at this festival included:

The Smashing Pumpkins, Chaksam-pa, A Tribe Called Quest, Pavement, Cibo Matto, Biz Markie, Richie Havens, John Lee Hooker, Red Hot Chili Peppers, Rage Against the Machine, Sonic Youth, Beck, Foo Fighters, Björk, De La Soul, Fugees, Buddy Guy, The Skatalites and Yoko Ono/Ima
