Beastie Boys live in Coloseum (Warsaw, Poland), 17.02.95

Recorded on tape from radio broadcast by Krzysiek, transfer by PmF; cheap-ass MC -> Philips MCM390 -> Planet Waves Cable -> Creative Sound Blaster Audigy 2 -> SoundForge 7.0 -> Acid 5.0 (track splitting) -> dBpowerAMP FLAC (Medium Quality)

So here it is - a fragments of rare performance by Beastie Boys, never seen or shared on the internet before, containing the first and only Polish concert by BBoys in Warsaw. The concert was once broadcasted by radio, and thanks to one of the fans, he recorded the portions of the show onto the tape. The quality even though it was a radio broadcast is not so good, because the tape was really old and in bad condition; Anyway, this is a REALLY rare gem and it is your first opportunity to have it.
