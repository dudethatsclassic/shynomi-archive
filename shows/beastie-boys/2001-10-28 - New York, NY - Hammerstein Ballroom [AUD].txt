This recording showed up on eBay shortly after the performance for around $100.00. As I recall the B-52s set was also recorded and sold in a separate auction. Other acts the played this night include: The Strokes, Rival Schools, B-52s, Cibo Matto, and Rahat Fateh Ali Khan. The second night featured the Beastie Boys again along with NERD, Mos Def, Jon Spencer Blues Explosion, Moby, Michael Stipe of REM, Bono of U2, and Rahat Fateh Ali Khan.
