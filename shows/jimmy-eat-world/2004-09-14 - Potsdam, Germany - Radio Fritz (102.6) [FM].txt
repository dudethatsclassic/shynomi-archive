Full band electric in-studio concert in front of just a few dozen people, broadcast live. Includes two photos from the gig.

I was lucky enough to attend this small gig myself (check out my crappy phone camera photos!), but this also meant I had to set up the tuner/computer in a hurry, and I made a mistake and sadly only recorded one channel of the broadcast. So this recording is mono, I'm very sorry.

This recording hasn't circulated as far as I know. There is an MP3 version going around, which is in stereo, but (a) it's lossy and (b) I think it was recorded from the Fritz webcast, not from FM. I think this recording here has better sound, even though it's only mono. If anyone has a stereo FM recording, then please share!

There's pretty much nothing above 15 KHz in the spectogram for this recording, but this is just because of the FM compression. I recorded and edited this myself and am sure that, apart from the FM compression, there is no compression (mp3 or otherwise) in the lineage.
