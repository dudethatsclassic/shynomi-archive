Notes:
If I tape a show and it's incomplete, I usually don't post it. Even if it's like 30 seconds missing. But this had been sitting on my desktop since the festival and it sounds pretty decent, so why not? I think Jimmy Eat World put on a great show, and their festival set did not let down. We flew in just for the day to see Nine Inch Nails and we didn't get in to the festival grounds until Jimmy started. So, we missed the first few songs. Still bummed the Nails tape came out blown out when this came out the way it did from the same stage.
