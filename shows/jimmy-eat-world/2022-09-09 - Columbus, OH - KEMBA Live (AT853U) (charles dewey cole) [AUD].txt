Notes:

The last time I taped JEW at the same venue (on the outdoor stage), I got
busted 45 minutes into their one hour set opening for Third Eye Blind. Then,
it rained (heavily) during the entire Third Eye Blind set. It was not a good
night. I vowed that evening the next time this band returned to Columbus and
headlined, I would get the full thing. 3 years later and here we are. This
time, the show was inside Kemba (previously known as EXPRESS Live!) and I
had darkness on my side. Not as conspicuous as I am outside under the sun.
The sound could have been a tad better, but the recording turned out fairly
good. I had a lot of yelling and clapping around me and tried to fix as
much of that as I could in post. I also brought the bass down a touch.
This is a terrific live band. If you get the chance, I urge you to go see
them. If they come back, I'll probably go again.
