Review:
This was the first time the opening act for a show has been an artist I've heard of! Jimmy Eat
World, Arizona's finest, returned to Australia as the opening act for My Chemical Romance's reunion
tour (not the first time these two bands have played together here - back in 2005, both had opening
sets for Green Day).

Most of MCR's 2022 shows had had two or even three opening acts, but Jimmy Eat World were the only
one for their Australian leg, meaning that they got a full hour to perform, and they made the most
of it. I'm only passingly familiar with the band (they only played one song from Clarity, wisely
sticking to the two big records after it), but they were amazing - and I have to say it, full
credit to the sound engineers at Rod Laver, the venue's sound was really good. Was an ideal opening
act, and my recording is pretty nice as well (although a few people start showing up near me in
the second half of the set, so it gets worse).
