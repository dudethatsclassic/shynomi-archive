personal note: (original file)

After the impact of the first Sex Pistols show on June 4 1976
in the Lesser Free Trade Hall, they played 2 more times in Manchester.
This is was their 3rd gig (and last) overthere.
A typical Sex Pistol show. Playing mistakes (Steve 1 note off in the 
intro of 'Anarchy'), lots of tuning, arguing with the audience, 
Johnny complaining about the monitors several times. 
on track 9, Steve threatens to unplug if the audience won't stop gobbing at them.
One of the first live versions of 'God save the Queen' a couple of weeks before
this show, it was new on the setlist. Small break mistake during GTSQ.
Johnny is well voiced this night.

enjoy!
