Early 70's copy with -3 stampers turned out to be the best sounding pressing for all four sides of the finished vinyl mix. The earlier mixes were used on the very first US pressings and were quickly replaced with the finished mixes. The finished mixes were the only mixes released in most countries, while the early mixes were used for the British LP until it was reissued in 1982 with the proper mix.
