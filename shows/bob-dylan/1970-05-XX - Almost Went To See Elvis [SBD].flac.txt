Review:  
CBS Studio Nashville, TN May 3, 1969 and 
CBS Studio B, NYC  May 1, 1970 

The first 12 songs, from Nashville, are wrongly  identified 
on the cover as being from NY 1970.  The sound quality is 
very good. The 1969 material includes Carl Perkins, Johnny Cash, 
and Billy Wotten (guitars), Marshall Grant (bass), and 
W.S. Holland (drums). Produced by Bob Johnson, this session was 
three days after the 'Nashville Skyline' LP was recorded in the 
same studio, with different musicians. The 1970 material is 
available through the CBS  Studios Reference Recording archives. 
It was produced by Bob Johnson as well. This session also 
produced the "If Not For You" version that was released on the 
official CBS LP 'The Bootleg Series 1-3" The musicians include 
George Harrison (guitar), Charlie Daniels (bass & backing vocals), 
Billy Mundi (drums), & *Bob Johnson (piano)
