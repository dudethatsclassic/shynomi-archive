Notes:
This bootleg release combines the quadraphonic mix of Bob Dylan's album "Desire" (taken from a vinyl source) with studio outtakes from the album sessions in July 1975 and a recording session with Bette Midler in October 1975.

Due to bad pressing/faulty source material the outtake of "Joey" (2-1) has several digital glitches.
