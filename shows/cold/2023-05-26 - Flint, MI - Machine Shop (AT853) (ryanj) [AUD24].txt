Notes:
My brother is a big Cold fan so I went to this show with him. We actually saw them two years ago and they do put on a great show. This was the 20th anniversary of their album, "Year of the Spider." As my Schoeps are out of commission for a while, I went with the ATs. One point I had a screamer next to me, but thankfully it was in-between songs. Tape came out decent. We were pretty close to the stage, so not sure how much I picked up on the PA. I felt like Scooter's mic was mixed pretty low.
