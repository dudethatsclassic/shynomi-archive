This was the first act we caught of the day and I'm glad we did. We were torn between Fishbone and them and we definitely made the right call. I've been listening to their music a long, long time and it was a treat to see them live. They had a great sounding set. I didn't know it at the time but this was their first public show back, having done a livestream earlier in the year. Would love to catch them again!
