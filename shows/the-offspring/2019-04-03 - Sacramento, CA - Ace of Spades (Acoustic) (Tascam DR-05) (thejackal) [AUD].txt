Another Jackal Production Vol 9: The Offspring 2019-04-03 Ace of Spades, Sacramento, CA
My first time seeing The Offspring.  This was an acoustic mini-tour that hit a few small West Coast venues.  Ace of Spades is a great place to see a show but the recordings made there always have a lot of crowd noise.  It's shoulder-to-shoulder standing room only on the floor; there are a few elevated tables on the perimeter and also a small balcony area above the bar.  But of course, the floor is where it's at!  This is one of my favorite places to see a show.

Enthusiastic & chatty crowd for a fun high-energy acoustic set with lots of unexpected (at least for me) covers.  Not my best ever recording but it definitely captures the energy in the house.  Before Self Esteem Noodles tells us a long story about his grandfather's ukelele that he brought back from Hawaii after WWII.  He proceeds to bash it Pete Townshend-style after the song to end the show (see the included pics).  I guess his grandpa must have brought back a half dozen ukes, since I'm sure they ended the other shows on this mini-tour the same way!

Includes pics from the show taken with my crappy iPhone 8.

Enjoy!!
