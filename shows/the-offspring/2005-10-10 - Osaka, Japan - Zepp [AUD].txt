Recording Info & Sound Quality
Almost perfect recording for the small venue like Zepp Osaka. 
 We recorded the show from the furthest place at the house, the last row on the balcony seats.  
To stay away from the crowd surfers and secure the place to set up the microphones, 
we could not find any other spot in the house.  Aiming the microphones at both stacks, the highs and lows were captured well.  
The guitar and vocal are pretty much up-front.  
Not much audience interference (at least annoying ones) on this recording, 
you can crank up the volume and get lost in the blistering sound.   
The Sound Forge or other fancy softwares were used.

Note
I have not much to say about this band and their music.  
We were kind of asked to record the show by this Japanese woman who could not make it to the gigs.  
She gave us free-tickets and made arrangements for the transportation to the house.  
Thanks a million
