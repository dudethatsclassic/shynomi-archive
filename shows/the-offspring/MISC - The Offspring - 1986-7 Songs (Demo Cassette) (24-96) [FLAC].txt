Their 2nd demo tape containing seven studio recordings. None of the tracks are listed on the tape. 

The 7 song tape is the last recording to have the song "Prophecy" on it. The band was unhappy with the recording of the song and it was removed from the other version of demo tape they distributed. It was later changed and recorded under the name "Demons" on their first album.
