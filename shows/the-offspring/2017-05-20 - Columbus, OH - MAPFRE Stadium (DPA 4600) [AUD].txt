Notes:

I loathe festivals. I really do. But I made an exception for this year's ROTR.
So many great bands. We unfortunately lost our Friday headliner (Soundgarden)
and had poor weather all 3 days, but ROTR persevered & the show went on!
Because this was recorded in a huge soccer stadium, please do not expect
perfect sound. To avoid crowd surfers on Saturday, I decided to stand further
back in the field near the soundboard tent. I positioned myself against the
rail to the right of the board, which happened to be in direct line with the
right stack. I will include an image. While the sound is a bit more distant,
it was much easier to tape and made it much more enjoyable. The Offspring were
my favorite performance on Saturday and this is well worth checking out.

RIP, CC.
