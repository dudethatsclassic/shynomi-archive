I'm a pretty casual Primus fan but really love the Claypool Lennon Delirium, so I was a bit disappointed
that not only did they have shortest set, but one song was a cover that at other shows on this tour was
part of Primus' encore. I would have loved some more stuff from either of their first two albums, but oh
well. The show was overall a good time though!

For the first two sets I was kind of in the middle of the pit area, but then moved to the back of the pit
for Primus. I think because of this the Primus set sounds a bit better than the other two, in terms of
mix and less crowd noise. There were some especially chatty people near me during Delirium's set, which
was made more noticeable by the fact that their songs were generally a bit more chill and less loud than
the other two bands. I still think it's a solid recording regardless. I did run into another taper in  the
pit, so I'd be curious to hear how his came out. I can't remember the guy's name though, sorry!
