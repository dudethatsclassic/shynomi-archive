Hi all - so having missed Phil’s show in LPR in April (with tickets in hand!!) I was so relieved he added Brooklyn to his August run here… While RADIOHEAD as a whole are being in their usual painfully slow mode, I’m quite thrilled to see them all on an individual basis! Phil is touring his second solo album “Weatherhouse”- now, I always stay away from saying which record is better because we all have our personal opinions; but in this case, however, the response to the 2nd albums from everyone I heard was a pretty uniform ‘wow!!’ I guess there is some growth into a solo artist that just happens - which is why his live delivery of the earlier songs changed quite a bit as well. 

This was a great show - 90 min straight, basically a complete performance of both records. Phil (like everyone else in his band) appears to be a multi-instrumentalist, and everyone seems to play everything. Quinta plays instruments which I wouldn’t even know how to describe! With respect to the tape, this is not the easiest show to capture because some songs were very quiet, while others had enough drum power for me to watch for level indicators. My only concern here though is that in parts with heavy drums they overwhelm the vocals. I can’t say whether this was intentional or whether it sounded different elsewhere in the venue. I get a sense that Phil is aiming for a pretty electronic sound live, so the ‘dissolving’ vocals can be a part of that. Anyways, I loved the show - hope you like it too.

One other thing: Phil is the absolutely nicest person you can imagine. He came out and chatted with the audience before show, very laid back. He stayed on to sign everyone’s stuff and letting people take as many pictures as they wanted… I actually spoke with him for quite a bit. and - my god, these were like…. the cheapest tickets… In these little venues, it’s hard for me to imagine he’s making any money at all! I grabbed the vinyl :D and he signed it of course… Go see him if you get a chance!
And YES, RADIOHEAD next record is in the works…
