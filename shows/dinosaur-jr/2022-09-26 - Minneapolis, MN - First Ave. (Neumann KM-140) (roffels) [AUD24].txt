Dinosaur Jr. - 2022 09 26 @ First Ave. Minneapolis, MN 

Show note:
Show was rescheduled from September 14th 2021. Lou was very talkative through out the show, and really seemed excited to be playing First Ave, and talks about the Replacements, Husker Du and running into Greg Norton. Nice jame after Out There that starts off with J tuning a bit, but the rest of the band joins in. I wasthinking how much these fall shows seem different than the two nights I caught them back in Februrary, but then I remembered Lou was sick with COVID and couldn't play when I saw them, so that's why ... 

Mastering notes:
roffels reached out, asking if I'd like to prep this and post it, as they were busy, but wanted to get the show out to fans. Of course, I was more than happy to do that. The recording is nice, and I really enjoyed listeing to all of Lou's stage banter. Saved as 48KHz / 24 Bit as most of roffels' other torrents seem to be use that format.
