Dinosaur Jr - 2007-12-13 @ The Voodoo Lounge, Kansas City MO

Note:
First off, this sounds like it was a LOUD show. Second, if this was an analog tape, I swear it was running fast - the band sounds like they are hopped up on caffeine. The first 15 seconds of Almost Ready started off a little too loud, so I adjusted the volume to try to even out the volume.  There are a couple of places where there's some additional noise (which sounds funny to say, with all of the feedback) maybe either from the PA or Mic, but otherwise a loud and energetic show.
