I have been sitting on this recording for exactly one year.  Plans to track and share it were undone when the world went off the rails less than
a week later.  I didn't know at the time it would be the last show I would attend for more than a year. It may also be the last the band has 
played aside from their superb webstreams.  Whatever the case, what was at the time simply another outstanding Jayhawks show has become a document 
or sorts.  Of the band trying out one from the then unreleased new record.  Of Gary's birthday.  Of a time when live music was something I most
definitely took for granted.  Listening to it was bittersweet, but definitely more sweet than bitter.  Enjoy and look forward to the days when
there will be new shows to attend and share.
