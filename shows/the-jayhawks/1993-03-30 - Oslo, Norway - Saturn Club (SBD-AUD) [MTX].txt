The Jayhawks had just released their album Hollywood Town Hall and they were at an "in store" at Tower Records promoting the record and would be playing a live show too. I had purchased a copy of their first album on vinyl and was at the instore and asked Gary and Mark to sign my LP. I ended up having a conversation with one of the guys in their entourage as to whether they had any live recordings and if maybe I could get one somehow. Well I really didn't have much expectation but totally out of the blue, weeks later, I received a package and it was a cassette of this performance. I really didn't know what to expect quality wise but I was blown away. I gotta say this live performance shows a band at the apex of their game. And yes, my cassette does start after the first song begins. I wanted to share a little bit of the history of this recording. I have no idea if anyone else got a copy of this. And I agreed not to share the cassette but after I saw the Jayhawks nonchalance towards these live recordings I did share the recording because it was just too good to keep to myself. So crank it up and close your eyes and you are at the Saturn Club in 1993. Thanks guys for a lifetime of great music!
