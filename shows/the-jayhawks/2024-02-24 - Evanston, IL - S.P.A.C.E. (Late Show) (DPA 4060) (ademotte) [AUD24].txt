Comments:  This recording captures The Jayhawks' second of three shows at Evanston's S.P.A.C.E. in their first performances of the year. The band sounded fantastic and were in good spirits; since they were playing three shows in a two-day span they attempted to vary the setlists and there were some eclectic picks as a result. Gary gave a shout-out to Robbie Robertson (who passed away in 2023), mentioned that the Jayhawks' name comes from The Band's original name, The Hawks (who knew?) and they did a beautiful cover of "The Night They Drove Old Dixie Down." Overall, the set was a really nice cross-section of their career, including a song so new that it doesn't have a title yet.

In terms of sound quality, the sound was as good as I've ever heard at the venue. I was lucky to be in the sweet spot in front of the left PA (exactly where I stood for The Baseball Project last year, for those who downloaded that recording). The only downside is that a group of young women were chattering in front of me for the first half of the show, so they are audible during the quieter moments. 

Can't wait to see them again.
