JEMS obtained master tapes from DB, a prolific SoCal taper whose work spans four decades, and the first cassette in the box that jumped out at us was this performance by one of our favorite Washington bands, Screaming Trees.

It is a short but sweet set and, we believe, a new addition to the but handful of tapes of the band from this early period. Quite listenable sound, too. Samples provided.

We can't quite identify one of the tracks, so if you can, please let us know. Includes spirited covers of Love and Jimi Hendrix. I would tape the band at Bogart's myself five years later.

Thanks to DB and to BLG for his work in mastering and prepping this show for release. We appreciate the help immensely.
