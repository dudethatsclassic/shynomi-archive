Notes:
So, it being 10 years removed from their hit album "Give Up" when everyone was in High School, in their awkward teen years listening to this record and I can say that nothing had changed with anyone in the crowd… Ha! I am really happy with how the tape came out, but still a bit of chatter and now because all of these high school kiddos are now of legal drinking age some were shit shows around me. And really it is at the end of each song you hear most of it. The sample gives you a very good indication of each song.

The paragraph above was supposed to bring some hilarity to this. But really, it was a fantastic show. The whole band was amazing. Jenny Lewis and Ben Gibbard killed it. "Nothing Better" was probably the highlight, and "Natural Anthem." The sound was spot on. Really glad they hit the road again. I saw them back in 2003 and always kicked myself for not taping it. Enjoy the tape.
