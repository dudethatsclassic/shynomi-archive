2013-06-10

The Postal Service
Detroit, MI, USA @ The Fillmore

Source: IEM: Sennheiser G2 B @ 642.800 > Sony PCM-M1 / DNTEL Mix
Transfer: DAT-M @ 48kHz > Tascam DA-20 > UX 24/96 > Goldwave (edit, resample to 44.1kHz)
Position: Recorded remotely 
Taper: MITAPER78

Notes: Hard to believe I saw the 20th Anniversary performance of Give Up by The Postal Service last year; this recording was from their 10 year anniversary of their album which they toured off of. Wife and I hit the show and, at the time, it was a big deal for us as we actually had a babysitter to watch our three young kids for the evening and anyone who has had young kids - a night away is worth it's weight in gold. Anyway. Super stoked for this performance and I really wanted to nail it. Out of the handful of sources pulled, this is the best one and it is DNTEL's mix. Very nicely balanced throughout and minimal RF issues with the exception of the first song. During the first song, WWBS, the frequency jumps through the mixes briefly as the band did not perform soundcheck at this show and I chose to verify the mix I wanted to grab. I believe I gave copies of these to some friends back in the day on CD-R but this is the first time this source has ever been 100% widely shared/torrented. Hope this makes a collector, somewhere, happy. Even better than that, I hope someone who was at this particular performance finds this and it brings a smile to their face. Enjoy.
