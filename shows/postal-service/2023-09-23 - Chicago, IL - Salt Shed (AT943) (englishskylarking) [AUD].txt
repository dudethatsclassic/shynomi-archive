Notes
First time letting everything ride on the Sound Pros adapter and my phone. I'd tested this
adapter/phone/app at several shows with an extra set of mics, so I was aware of its limitations
and quirks, and I'm thankful that everything went off without any major issues at this show.
This recording turned out better than I expected, and it's totally listenable after the help of
a couple iZotope modules with the caveats noted. Definitely recommend checking out the adapter
over at Sound Professionals and probably the attenuator they mention in the description, the
SP-ATEN-1, for an extremely low profile setup, particularly if you can't take advantage of the
Shure offerings because you have an Android rather than an iPhone.
