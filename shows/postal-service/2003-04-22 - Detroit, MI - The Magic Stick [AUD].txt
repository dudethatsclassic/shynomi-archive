Postal Service
The Magic Stick
Detroit, Mich.

This isn't a great sounding show and is a tad bass heavy, but it isn't that bad. The Magic Stick just wasn't built for the kind of techno-y bass riffs they tried pumping through those speakers. Anyway, it's a rare recording as I think I've only traded it to two other people.
