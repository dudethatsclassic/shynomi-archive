I'm honestly not super thrilled with how this recording came out, mainly because the vocals are pretty washed out in 
the mix, especially Les Claypool's. I was recording from up front on Sean Lennon's side of the stage, so I'd guess that's
the reason. Regardless, the show itself was really good and I figured it might be worth listening to for someone. The
guys were pretty chatty during the set, and I've split those longer moments into their own tracks. Sean informed us why
we should eat food we've dropped on the floor, while Les gave us the background story behind "South of Reality." He also
interrupted the jam during "Breath of a Salesman" to talk about Moog synthesizers, which originate from Asheville. I would
have liked to have heard some more stuff from the first album, but otherwise the show was a great time.
