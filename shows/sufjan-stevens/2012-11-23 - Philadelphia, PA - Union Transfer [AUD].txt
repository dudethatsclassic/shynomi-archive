Season’s greeting, dimers! I recorded this one a year ago, then did not get around to seeding it, so this is my belated 2012/early 2013 Christmas present to you. 

This was the opening night of "The Surfjohn Stevens Christmas Sing-A-Long: Seasonal Affective Disorder Yuletide Disaster Pageant On Ice" 2012 Tour, and the concert is pretty much a disaster as billed; it seemed that Soufie and Band were winging it most of the time, as he readily admits on stage. That actually made it very personal and a lot of fun. If you listen carefully, you can hear my daughter “call” Sister Winter before Sufjan plays a note. Sound quality is A-minus; I’ll post a sample below.

Enjoy!

—Doc
