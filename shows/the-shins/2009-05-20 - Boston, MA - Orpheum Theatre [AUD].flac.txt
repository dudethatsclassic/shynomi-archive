The Shins
Know Your Orpheum!
2009-05-20
Orpheum Theatre
Boston, MA


Lineage...

Zoom H2 (stereo wave 16 bit at 1411kbps) >> Audacity (for normalizing
and track splitting) >> Trader's Little Helper >> Flac level 8


Unfortunately I propped up the microphone too high and it was making
contact with my seat. This resulted in what sounds like microphone
distortion but you're really hearing are vibrations from the chair
transferring to the H2. The Shins played a few new songs but did not
announce their titles. Apart from normalization, nothing has been
done to modify the original sound of this recording some EQ-ing may be
necessary to achieve a more natural sound but I will leave that to
the listener.
