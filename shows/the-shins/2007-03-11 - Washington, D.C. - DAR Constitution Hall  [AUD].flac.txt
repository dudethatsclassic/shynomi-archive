Comments:
There was a big sign on the door saying no recording equipment, so I spent about 
10 minutes working up the courage (and surveying the entry process) to try and 
get in with my stuff.  After getting in it took me 5 good minutes to get my stuff 
ready (there was security everywhere), so I missed the most of the first song 
(Sleeping Lessons).  Other than that I think the recording came out solidly for 
such an echo-y venue.  Please share freely and in lossless formats only.  Contact 
with questions or comments: subpop12@hotmail.com
