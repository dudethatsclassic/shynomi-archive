In order to get the full capacity of each burned audio CD, the order of performances was shuffled up. The above listing is just so there is little wasted space on the CD.

Saturday: Ray Barbee > Culver City Dub Collective > A Brokedown Melody (film) > Tommy Guerrero > Donovan F > Jack Johnson
Sunday: Tristan Prettyman > Matt Costa > Sprout (film) > The Mojave Three > James Mercer > Superwolf > G Love

James Mercer is the lead singer of The Shins. This is just a James Mercer (solo) performance.
Superwolf is Will Oldham and Matt Sweeny
