Notes:
This recording represents a true "MacGyver" effort--anyone who appreciates
'old-school' taping ingenuity will totally enjoy this.  We arrived at the show 
without a mic stand(don't ask) & couldn't score one from the sound crew.  
I held the ortf bar for the J. Mascis set & that was hell trying to keep everything 
in position so between sets I found two bits of wood 'floating' in the pool & 
fastened them together with some white gaff that the crew was willing to 
kick down.  The result was a perfectly suitable 'mic stand' that titled upward
toward the PA.  Taping was a breeze after that...and speaking of breezes, 
this show took place on a beautiful summer night in Brooklyn with a strong 
summer breeze that would blow through on occassion.  We had wind screens 
on the capsules but some of the stronger winds would still push through--
nothing too terrible, mind you but I just figured I'd give you a heads up.
Also, at a couple points during the show there is a bit of distortion/crackling
coming off one of the instruments--this is not from the recording as I remember 
it happening at the show.  This show was EQ'd to negate some of the inherent
bad acoustics of the pool setting & some 'loud clap removal' was performed to 
erase the loudest nearby clappers.  All things considered I think it came out 
pretty damn tasty & we hope you all enjoy it to the fullest!!!!
