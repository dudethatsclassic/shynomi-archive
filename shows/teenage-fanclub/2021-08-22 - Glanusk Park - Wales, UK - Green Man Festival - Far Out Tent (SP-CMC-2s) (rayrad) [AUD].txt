this is the first time i've seen the fannies since the creation shows - and gerry's departure

and i've been sort of dreading it

how weird is it going to feel?

no 'sparky's dream'?!

in the end, it's wonderful, of course

so many of these songs have been burned into my brain for 30 years

it's the final set of GM 2021 for me - and there aren't many better ways to round off what's been another reliably wonderous weekend in the welsh mountains

it's been a scorching hot day - and a full moon now hangs over the site

lighting us all up as we stumble out of the far out, trying to forget for a little longer that we have to go back to reality tomorrow

fontaines DC have pulled a huge crowd at the main stage, leaving the tent a little thinly populated for the fannies' set

but, up the front, walloped and having a properly marvellous time, i've no sense of this until people tell me about it later

if the band were looking out at a sparse crowd, they don't seem to mind

happy to be playing only their second show since things started up again

an hour and a half flies by

i just this morning shelled out a small fortune for next year's GM tickets

but honestly it's worth twice that

there's nowhere quite like it

enjoy
ray
