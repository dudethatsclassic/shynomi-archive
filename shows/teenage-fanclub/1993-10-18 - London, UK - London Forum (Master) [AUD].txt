Notes:

THE BUENAVENTURA LIMITED SERIES, VOL. 9

With much gratitude to the generosity of buenaventura, we're proud to bring you a limited series with more wonderful material to counterbalance the so far terrible '20s.

This limited series is being so coined because there isn't a deluge of tapes coming through for this right now, and the scope of it is still undetermined, but that could potentially change at any moment. There seems to be enough on tap at present to keep all following the series with enough new sounds to take in for at least the next few weeks.

Off we go to Kentish Town for a set from Teenage Fanclub once again, this time promoting their "Thirteen" album at the Forum. They were on a bill with the Poppies, Superchunk and the Posies.

Another grand performance, but the sound on this one, compared to the prior volume's recording, is a slight step down, probably because of the distance of the ticketed seating. It's still a decent recording, but doesn't pack as much of a punch sonically as the Köln '92 tape. The first few notes of "Metal Baby" are missed on the flip, and the show went pretty late so buenaventura decided not to chance missing the last tube, and left the venue at the tail end of the final song, where the band bring out Jon and Ken from the Posies (or as they like to refer to them, the then also current members of Big Star). A little of the walk to the tube station has been left in and tracked separately, which you can omit, thought it might be cool however minor, for those of you who want to be transported back to the streets of London 27 years ago.

Enjoy,
elegymart
