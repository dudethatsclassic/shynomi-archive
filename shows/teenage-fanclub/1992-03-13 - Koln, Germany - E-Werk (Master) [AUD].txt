Notes:

THE BUENAVENTURA LIMITED SERIES, VOL. 8

With much gratitude to the generosity of buenaventura, we're proud to bring you a limited series with more wonderful material to counterbalance the so far terrible '20s.

This limited series is being so coined because there isn't a deluge of tapes coming through for this right now, and the scope of it is still undetermined, but that could potentially change at any moment. There seems to be enough on tap at present to keep all following the series with enough new sounds to take in for at least the next few weeks.

We're in Köln once again for one of power pop's best by way of Glasgow, Teenage Fanclub, this time from E-Werk.

This is a typical setlist for the "Bandwagonesque" tour (but what a set of songs), and the performance is likewise TFC's usual energetic workout. After they finish off with a very short "Satan," someone in the audience throws some unknown item up on the stage. Based on Brendan's reaction, we probably don't need a visual. 

Cracks are made about 10cc and Duran Duran songs, but cute banter aside, nothing more needs to be said here. It's a ripping set, and another reliably well-recorded document from buenaventura.

Enjoy,
elegymart
