Notes:
This is a very rare and amazing recording.  It's not an "A" quality fidelity recording, but it's definitely an "A"
quality in music performance.  To this day, most of the pieces of music on this recording are still unreleased.

One of the highlights is Bunny Brunel's rendition of Ivanhoe three years before its official release.
