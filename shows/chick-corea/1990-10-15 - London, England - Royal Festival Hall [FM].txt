EDITING NOTES

I transfered this to CDr 3-4 years ago from my master tape that I recorded when it was broadcast in 1991. Musically, it was an awesome show, 
but it had a lot of tape hiss, which was a major distraction. Having just got Adobe Audition 2.0, I decided to practice using the hiss 
reduction filter and I'm really happy with the results. The vast majority of hiss has been removed without negatively affecting the 
nuances of the original source.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMENTS

Do I really need to sell this show?  Oh, alright then I will.  First, the sound quality is superb as it's from my master cassette.  It has
a wonderful, warm analog low end that will give your speakers a nice workout. Secondly, I've seen this show circlating on quite
a few lists, but they are often incomplete - this is the complete FM broadcast.  And then there's the show itself, which is just killer - 
especially with the 1970s Return to Forever medley kicking things off.  And finally - Frank Gambale :-) 

Enjoy!
