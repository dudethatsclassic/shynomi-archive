The three unidentified pieces are improvisations. In the Trio Music context,
the drum solo has been issued as "Hittin' It" (Roy Haynes) and the bass solo as
"Transformation" (Miroslav Vitous). In this particular version Vitous includes a
reference to "Alice In Wonderland" (Sammy Fain, Bob Hilliard). The piano solo
has been issued as "Mock Up" (Chick Corea). This version includes a reference to
"Some Time Ago" (Chick Corea, Neville Potter).
