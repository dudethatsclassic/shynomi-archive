I'm surprised Paramore hasn’t been more popular here in the Dime community.  I can count on one hand the number of shows I've seen of bands that I had little prior knowledge of that truly blew my mind.  This show comes close.  If you like No Doubt, Paramore is sure to please.  You can't help but get wrapped up in the infectious enthusiasm of the crowd when the whole room is bouncing to the rhythm.  I'm looking forward to catching them  again the next time around.
