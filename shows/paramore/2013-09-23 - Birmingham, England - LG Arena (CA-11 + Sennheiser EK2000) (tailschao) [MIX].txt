Paramore
Monday 23rd September 2013
LG Arena, Birmingham, England
FLAC Level 8, 16Bit, 48KHz

Source 1: Church Audio CA-11 Cardioid (2007 V1 Edition) > Tascam Dr-2D (Mic-In, Power On, Gain Low, Levels 80, 24Bit 48KHz)
Source 2: Sennheiser EK 2000 > Tascam Dr-2D (Line-In, Levels 98, 24Bit 48KHz)
--------: Adobe Audition CS6 > Trader's Little Helper

Location: Front row of general admission standing, extreme stage right corner,
underneath speaker stack, mics pointed upwards.


My pre-amp has a broken input jack and I do not own a battery box, so mics
powered by recorder. Not ideal, and I think there is some distortion in the
pure AUD in places as a result of the underpowering. There is certainly DC
offset visible in the waveform. I need to buy a battery box!

Decent mix on the Hayley Williams monitor feed. Click track in places - blame
her, not me. Unfortunately they use Sennheiser B Range and my B Range box has
something wrong with the antenna. Reception is abysmal. So there are some (not
too many) patches of radio static. Sorry. I need to buy a new Sennheiser B
range box, but they're so expensive!

Also in the monitor feed was some odd occasional switching of mixes. 95% of
the time it was Hayley's mix, and I was sure at Soundcheck that I scanned all
the frequencies in use and this was definately Hayley's frequency. I do not
think I taped a monitor engineer feed by mistake. Usually a feed with
switching mixes is a monitor engineer feed. But I do not think here. Not
sure what the reasoning was behind that, but I cut out all the sections
of alternate mixes. So in some places it is pure AUD.

Simple matrix in Audition. A tiny bit of EQ, and dropped the centre channel
on the monitor feed to bring up the stereo panned guitars a little.

Decent enough sounding recording all in all. A bit low on Bass Guitar though...


I don't think I've ever seen as much hair dye as in the audience of a Paramore
concert!
