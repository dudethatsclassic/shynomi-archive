#  Hayley Williams solo.
%  HalfNoise.

First time seeing Paramore (mainly on the strength of their current LP), and
was extremely impressed. They performed 8 of the 10 songs from the new album
and they all sounded incredible. I got lucky with decent neighbors and had
an aisle to my left. So, while there were screamers everywhere, I did not
have any in my direct vicinity - thank god. I also had the seat to my right
empty. My personal favorite of the night was hearing Hayley's vocals on the
track, "Liar". Wow! Phenomenal job. Paramore gave one of the best shows I've
seen in recent memory. I will be listening back to this one for years to come.

Note: 20 minutes before Paramore were to take the stage there were building
alarms going off and all guests were told to evacuate the building using the
stairs only. Roughly 3 minutes later, we were told to return to our seats and
that it was a false alarm. Pretty sure the entire arena's buzz was killed
during those 3 minutes! Turns out, someone was smoking in one of the stairwells.

Bloc Party and Genesis Owusu opened [not recorded].
