The Marked - Music Videos
=========================
This transfer is from a VHS tape I got from Ron Roesing via Ebay. The picture
quality is an improvement over the versions currently circulating from the
Psycho Tape. Unfortunately, the chroma signal isn't present for the first couple
seconds of each music video. This is an issue with the initial creation of the
tape, not the transfer.

I have synced audio tracks from the circulating "The Marked Live/Demo Oct '86"
tape as the audio is the same, but of far superior quality. The original VHS
audio is included as the second audio track.
