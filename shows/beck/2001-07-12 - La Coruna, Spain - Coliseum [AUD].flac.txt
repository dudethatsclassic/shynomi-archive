Beck

Date: July 12, 2001
Leg: European Summer Festival Tour 2001
Venue: Coliseum
City: La Coruna
Country: Spain
Notes:
	Noisy and annoying audience recording, though every once in awhile
	it is still decently clear (mostly near the beginning).; "Hotwax"
	and "Broken Train" are a funky and fun pair to get back-to-back.;
	The crowd must be getting drunker as the show goes on. By the time
	Beck gets to "Nicotine & Gravy" and "Loser" (cool combo!), the
	Spanish audience is clapping and chanting and singing along and
	having fun. They seem to be singing "Burro" while Beck is doing
	"Jack-ass"!;
