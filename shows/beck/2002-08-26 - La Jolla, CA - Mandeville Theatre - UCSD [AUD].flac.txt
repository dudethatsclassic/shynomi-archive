Notes:
Here's a little gem from Beck's "Sea Change" tour.  HP recorded this out in La Jolla
& brought it back east as beautiful raw material which was then 'beefed up' for the
enjoyment of all.  This was back in the day when I would just 'jack down' the loud
crowd at the end of a song instead of using the 'wavehammer' but it still was done
democratically & non-intrusively.  Also, a sweet EQ was done to boost the bottom 
and the music itself was jacked up around 28db!!  A nice show from this tour--the 
"No Expectations" is especially sweet.
 
The material from "MB Eclectic" was also EQ'd to bring out its true sheen.
****This Bonus material is in glorious MONO--if you have the sweet copy of this
in stereo, feel free to seed it & hook everyone(me, too) with an even better version****
