I got this in a tape trade many years ago, so many that I really don't remember facts at all. It's a good early Beck show that I transferred from cassette and cleaned-up.

A weird set starts with the soon to be indie hit "Beercan", then falls into early material noisy type jams, very rockin', wild and untamed. Then a couple of down home acoustic pieces and top it off with another noisy jam. 
A few of these songs I've never seen anywhere else, so it's got something for those looking for some rare tunes.

Enjoy!
