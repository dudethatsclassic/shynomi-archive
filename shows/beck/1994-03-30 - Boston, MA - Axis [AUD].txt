Notes: I chose not to apply any noise reduction to this reduction, to preserve the frequency range from the original recording as best as possible. The dropout in Pay No Mind, the pause button scratching in Loser, and the cut at the end of Sleeping Bag are all present on the original recordings. The only processing done was to add short fades at the beginning and end.
