I didn't realize that this one is pretty rare in the BECK community, and I certainly don't want to sit on a recording that needs to get out. I was at this one, and it was a matinee show that started at 5PM...a weird time, but it worked, and probably allowed Beck to stretch his set out a bit without having to worry about any curfews...

The set has a couple of oddities. (EDIT)This includes the only known performance of Everlasting Secret Jubilee (/EDIT) After the first listen in a long time, I can tell ya, the sound system DID suck this night, typical fer Roseland, and the recording is stealthy, but the performance does transcend this, and the jams are fabulous, especially the rare 'Little Drum Machine Boy' during Hotwax. My favorite BECK band too...
