-Recording notes-
Sound Professionals Deluxe Cardioid Stereo Microphones > Sound Professionals Mini Battery Module > Line In > Sony MZRH910 HiMD recorder, HiSP recording 

Transfered with SonicStage 3.4, tracks cut with Soundforge.  Fade in on Loser and fade out on E-Pro... no other modificatons made.  No EQ or level adjustment... this is a straight transfer.

I recorded this in the General Admission "dance floor"area.  I was about 25 feet from the stage, just right of center.  All in all, this came out well.  Check the samples if you're interested.

Once you download, feel free to modify it however you want- transfer to mp3, mess with the sound- whatever.  Have fun and enjoy it.
