Some notes about this show

One of the most disappointing shows i've ever seen. Am a huge fan and have seen many great Beck shows over the last 12 years but this one was just plain bad. The only redeeming feature was the cute puppet show and that before Movie Theme, Beck apologised for being sick and not being able to sing properly. 

His cold really affected the energy of the show and the acoustic set (tracks 10-13) just died big time. The festival crowd really gave it the jazz oddysey style thumbs down. Movie Theme and The Golden Age were particularly bad vocal performances - Beck just couldn't sing these with his cold. The former not helped by a guitar not working and the latter only made passable by Beck throwing in a few lines from the Pixies' Wave of Mutilation. During these acoustic songs you can slightly hear Basement Jaxx blasting away in the background - they were playing on the next stage at the same time and unfortunately their sound bled through.

To add to all this, the mix was pretty poor. I tweaked with the recording to make it sound more balanced and it's better than what the punters heard live (IMO). The Sydney show from the night before reportedly had a really bad mix and lacklustre performance as well.

When announcing Beck to the V Festival line up, the organisers stated in a press release, "Whoa. In his only Australian shows, Beck will be bringing his incredible catalogue of songs and simply astounding stage to Sydney and the Gold Coast V Fest. From the Odelay days to his latest releases, Beck's eclectic 75 minute set will dazzle all comers."

75 minute set my arse! It only lasted 56 and that included 1000BPM where Beck wasn't even on stage. That song had 2 people dressed as grizzly bears running around miming the song. 

In short, this show was the encapsulation of everything wrong with outdoor festivals. Short sets, bad sound, a restless crowd and performers playing way under par (take the money and run).

In contrast to this craphouse set, the Pixies followed and played one of the most amazing shows i've ever seen. 
(see http://www.dimeadozen.org/torrents-details.php?id=142932)
When my head finally stopped spinning after their set i could only ask Beck who?

ps: While i'm at it, is anyone else sick of Beck's designer jeans schtick during Where It's At? He's been saying these same words for at least four years now. How hard is it to rap a different dedication from night to night, yet alone year to year?
