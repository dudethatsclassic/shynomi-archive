Beck, 1994-12-04, Melkweg, Amsterdam, 52:49

Here's a description of this show from Hijacked Flavors:

"While the setlist doesn't contain anything terribly unique, this is one of the best shows of 1994.; "Fume" is much mellower than usual, until the end where it goes a little crazy.; Beck makes up some completely new lyrics for the first verse of "Soul Suckin Jerk."; Beck tells everyone "It's All In Your Mind" is only on a single, but will be on his next record. Eight years later on Sea Change, it was true!; Beck sings "Loser" in a goofy voice, and ad-libs all sorts of stuff.; Don't hear too many full band "Nitemare Hippy Girl"s, but this one is."
