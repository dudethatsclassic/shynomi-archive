Notes:
This was meant as a stand alone show to be aired, so that's how I kept it.  I deleted some of 
the banter in the middle, and tracked it together, the only odd part is when Beck starts talking
about how he named 'Odelay.'  At the end of the show, the DJ erroneously states the show was 
March 30th, when in reality according to Beck.com it was May 30.  The original CD was four tracks,
I split them up to make one continuous show (songs 01-06, 07-08, 09, 10 each had their own tracks).

These same performances of 'Where It's At' and 'High 5' are also included on Media America's
SonicNet All Access show #98-51.
