Beck
Zellerbach Theatre
Philadelphia PA
August 17 2002

All I know about the recording is thus:

It was recorded with a Sony MZ-S1 MD Player/recorder and a Sony ECM-MS907 microphone.

It was transferred to cd-r through a mixing board and using an audio burner.

A copy of this cd-r was given to Whiskeyclone.net by the taper, who FLAC'ed with FLAC frontend.

It's the best damned setlist Beck ever played.
