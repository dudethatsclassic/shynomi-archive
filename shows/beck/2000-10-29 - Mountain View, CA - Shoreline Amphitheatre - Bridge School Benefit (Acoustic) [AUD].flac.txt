December 6, 2004:

This show was transferred from DAT to Cool Edit at an incorrect sample rate of 44.1 kHz by the seeder. I have reset the sample rate to 48 kHz (the actual DAT sample rate) and resampled at 44.1 kHz to correct the speed. I have reencoded to FLAC using FLAC frontend 1.7.1 using quality level 8. So, the new source/conversion/lineage is:

Schoeps mk5 (omni) > Bill Rutelhuber Box > M1  (FOB) > Sony D8 -> MIT Terminator S/PDIF -> 		M-Audio DiO24/96 -> Cool Edit 2000 (44.1 kHz, same as DAT Master) -> CD Wav 1.71 ->            mkwACT v0.97 Beta1 > mkwACT v0.97 --> WAV --> Sony Soundforge 7.0a --> resampled -->
	FLAC frontend 1.7.1

I also made some changes to the tracklisting. O MAria was listed as Ovarie (laugh) and the John Martyn cover was left out.

Now, enjoy this recording as it was meant to be heard.
