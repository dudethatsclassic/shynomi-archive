Notes:
This show was originally on two CD's and three different tracks (01-03, 04-06, and 07-09).  
A fourth track with 'Nicotine and Gravy' and 'Debra' was determined to be from an earlier WW1 
recording according to the setlist archive at Beck.com.  Nothing was written on the cue sheet 
saying what show it's from, but listening to Beck he shouts out KROQ, thus leading me to
believe it's from that show.  According to Beck.com, 'White Christmas' was also jammed after
'Where it's At,' but omitted from the WW1 show probably because it aired in June.

Sound Notes:
The really annoying DJ couldn't be deleted because she talks over some music.  A couple of 
curse we edits are also included.  The 'Annoying DJ Outro' track is done over 'Swampsolo,' or so
how it's listed on the cue sheet.
