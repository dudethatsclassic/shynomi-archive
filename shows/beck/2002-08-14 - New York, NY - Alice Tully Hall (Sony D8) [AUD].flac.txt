Only time I have seen Beck.  He's cool, but I'm a very casual fan.  I recorded this right after getting this mic setup for stealth recordings.

I had a perfect taping spot.  Up front, dead center, with a rail in front of my seats that i was able to secure my mics to.

Big boomy opera hall, so a good and boomy recording.

Given the casual fan status of my Beck knowledge, I have no idea what the names of most of the songs are so no setlist listed here.  If someone listens and wants to let me know, I will update this info file and my post.
