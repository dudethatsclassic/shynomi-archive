Show Notes: My first time seeing Beck and it was a highly entertaining show! A great mixture of songs and a fantastic band, Beck is a great entertainer. Despite Beck claiming this was the first performance of Seventh Heaven, setlist.fm shows they played it once in Japan last year.

My main complaints about the show were the crowd, surprisingly this show sold poorly, with the back section of seats behind the soundboard curtained off, and tickets being sold for half price in the last week before the show, so despite the seated sections that were sold being full, and the GA section of the floor bursting at the seams (oversold cheap tickets to compensate?), the crowd was really lacklustre, not really getting into the show, despite Beck's best efforts - it took until Up All Night and E-Pro for the crowd to really get into it (you'll hear the results when Beck tries to encourage singalongs and gets muted responses early on). Nonetheless I really enjoyed the show, and would go see him again if he makes the trip back to Australia!

Recording Notes: Sounds really good, I had a couple of guys next to me who liked talking to each other occasionally which the recorder picks up, but it's not too distracting. It came out a little bass heavy, so I've slightly turned down the bass and upped the treble and sounds a lot better for it. You may also hear a creaking noise from time to time, which is the noise my seat made and not much I could do about it. Anyway enjoy!
