Two fabulous nights at the incredible Royal Albert Hall, a splendid time was guaranteed for all !
There are some truly great videos on the usual platforms, well worth checking out to get the feeling.
 
I've had two totally different seats for the two nights, and such are the recordings.
All photos included are by myself (except the posters), lots of them, including much RAH atmosphere.

For once, I've tracked the talking between the songs separately, hope that's okay with you.
This is the 24-48 version, as recorded, with very little EQing in low range and some edits to eliminate my obnoxious coughing.
