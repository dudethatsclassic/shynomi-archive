Show Notes:
I never liked STP.

To me, they sounded like they locked themselves in a closet listening to Pearl Jam.

And Bush sounded like they locked themselves in a closet listening to STP.

And Creed sounded like the locked themselves in a closet listening to Bush.

The nicest thing I could say about STP is "they sound good on the radio". Then again, so do most bands...

All that aside, I liked Weiland even less.

I saw them in Anchorage in '00, after the '96 NYE cancellation debacle. Scott woke up (came to?) 3 days before the show and changed his mind, it had been sold out (8750) for weeks.  four years later, same venue, they barely sold 2000 tickets (Alaskans *never* forget).

So with a new singer,  and since Stone Sour, STP and Billy Idol were all in Atlanta last night, Billy won out the night previous (also taped and shared here), and the trek to Knoxville was made to check out the new singer for a band that sounds like "corporate, paint-by-numbers rock to these ears. (sorry Stone Sour, you're liked even less)

He did an adequate job, and it was a solid 100 minute or so set (I think). Some deeper rarely-played cuts were played, and it was a sold out show.

And you should be really happy, yet again, with the sound quality

It will probably be another 18 years before I see them again, as the setlist was basically a carbon-copy of the night previous.

Nnyhow, not sure who likes these guys less, me or Dennis.

In that spirit, thank him and fuck me...
