A decent recording.  I have bad luck at the Fillmore with crowds.  The woman behind me had an ear piercing scream
(just like Velvet Revolver 1/26/08) and the guy next to me kept screaming "ST-muthafuckin'-P" so it got really annoying.

greatest work I've done, but it's something.  The band was pretty good, the vocals were really buried in the mix.  There
wasn't that much chemistry on stage; this show made me miss Velvet Revolver, at least they were good actors.
