Notes:

*  = Originally Recorded By Led Zeppelin. 

Just a forewarning, during the first 8 tracks (specifically d1t04, d1t05, d1t06, 
d1t07 & d1t08) I found myself in the middle of a most pit, stage left. I moved 
back about 8 feet from my previous position to avoid being hit, but I still had
the wind knocked out of me a few times, so you will hear phasing when this happens. 
I tried my best, but given the circumstances and the need to push & shove during
the songs "Still Remains" & "Cinnamon", the recording suffered. Scott eventually
calmed the audience down at this point and when "Big Empty" began, everyone 
started enjoying the show. It was really a madhouse, people on the ground hurt,
fights, security carrying people out, etc. Simply not necessary at a Stone Temple 
Pilots concert. I removed the 4 minute encore break, which was prior to "Dead & 
Bloated". I didnt feel the need to keep 4 minutes of a screaming girl in this. 

Quite pleased w/ the new setlist this summer. Please try to ignore the guy to my 
left singing along w/ "Dancing Days". He wouldn't shut up.
