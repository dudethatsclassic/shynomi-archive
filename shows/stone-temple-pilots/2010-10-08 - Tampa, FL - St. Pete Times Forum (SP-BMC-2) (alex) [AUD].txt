Horrible Audience. Rude, Drunk, Loud and Inconsiderate. Then again I probably shouldn't have expected anything better. I nearly dodged getting drenched from flying cups of mystery liquid. Drunk guy behind me shouts all throughout the recording; right into my ear and mics. Thankfully not during the songs. Crowd Noise was really bad.  The band however, really cool to  watch. They seemed to be having fun and it showed in the performance. Overall a good ambient recording considering the circumstances. I am suprised how distant this recording sound taking into account how close I was to the stacks. No battery box tonight.
