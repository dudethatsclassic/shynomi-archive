Show Notes: This was my 3rd tiem seeing STP since 1993(93/96/08) and definitely the strangest show.  They had a local highschool marching band open the show and play for the fairly drunk masses for 25 minutes to cheers and jeers.  Scott's voice was shot, and the icy wind blowing off the lake bringing the temperature down in the 40's did not help things.  Dean had guitar troubles during Big Empty; after restarting the song a couple of times they gave up and went into Wicked Garden.  Very enjoyable show overall, would have been nice if they skipped the marching band and just came out early as it was an unseasonably cold night played in an open-air ampitheater.   I took some pictures at the show that I used for the artwork included.
