Thanks again go out to the tapers, but a huge amount of thanks and praise should go out to my mate 
Donkyflip he deserves a huge protion of the credit for this project, because it was his idea that 
these two sources should be combined together because they would compliment one another.

I've been a fan of STP for years, and I was fortunate enough to have caught them in concert three 
times before they disbanded, I really miss them, hopefully they'll get back together someday, but I 
didn't collect their shows as much as I do with Zeppelin, now I'm really taking an interest in their 
bootlegs.


This was an incredible performance, the song that really shines on this show (for me at least) is " Down", 
lots of energy and power put into the song, at the end of the song it sounds like they added the intro 
to " Moby Dick", probably just trying to end the song, but it makes sense that they might do something 
like that since the group are Zeppelin fans.

Both these tapes are excellent on their own, this project was made though because we thought that these 
tapes are better together than being apart.

The first source has powerful vocals and instruments, but it does have too much treble, and didn't give 
any ambiance of the crowd, the second source has the ambiance and warmth of the crowd, and nice bass, but 
it does sound a little far from the stage, so both of them together would make a great recording that 
uses both of their strengths.

The title comes from comments Scott makes to the audience about getting naked during the show, as well as 
Chris' comments about the beach being a nude beach, lucky, lucky guy.

Artwork is included in the torrent, we hope you all enjoy this.

Cheers
