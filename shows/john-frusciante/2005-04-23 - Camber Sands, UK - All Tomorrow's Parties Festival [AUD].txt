Live At All Tomorrow’s Parties Festival - 23rd April 2005

This would be that beautiful, beautiful acoustic show held on 23rd April 2005 at the second weekend of All Tomorrow’s Parties Festival in Camber Sands, UK. It’s one of only two acoustic shows that John played after 2001; and, to many listeners of his music, his best live show ever. He was happy, his voice was at its best, he was crying together with his audience.

The show was taped by Gareth.
