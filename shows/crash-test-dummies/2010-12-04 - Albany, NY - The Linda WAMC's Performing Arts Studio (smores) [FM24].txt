Brad Roberts - vocals
Ellen Reid - back-up vocals (main vocals in The Ballad of Peter Pumpkinhead and You're Early)
James Reid - acoustic guitar

Brad's talkative during all the concert. There's a moment when an audience member asks for them to play Thick-Necked Man, a song from their first album "The Ghosts That Haunt Me" in 1991.
Brad replies that, funny enough, that was the only song from their first album that he didn't write (besides their cover of Androgynous by The Replacements) and that it sucked; therefore, he won't be playing it. The song was originally written by Benjamin Darvill, their harmonica and mandolin player who quit in 2000 and went on with a solo Project called "Son of Dave".
Also, he tells the story of how the song Mmm Mmm Mmm Mmm got made. Basically, he made a demo for the band to listen to and since he didn't have any chorus at the time, he simply hummed through the chorus section. When he asked the band what they thought, they told him that the song was alright as it was, so he didn't write a chorus for it.
