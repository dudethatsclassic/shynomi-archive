What a great concert, Liam's voice wasnt that bad at all which was my main
concerning about the show. They played a damn great gig from beginning to
end, and since i've seen them all the times they were here, this was their
best performance here and they really enjoyed it, since they were quite
talkative with the audience, thing they never did here and they seemed to
be in a good mood, which is something almost strange for Oasis...

The broadcast. Well, since i wasnt here to check properly as i was at the
gig... before i left home, i did some tests finding the right position for
the antenna and recording some bits, and it was sounding quite good, a bit
of noise in the back from the signal but nothing disturbing, i recorded
like 2 hours before the actual show and the quality is excellent, but when
the concert starts, the quality degrades, there's something with the
cymbals, with the high frequencies, that make it sound hissy. Anyway, i
think it is still enjoyable. And i have read various people complaining
about the same weird sound of the mix. This gig was also broadcasted by
another FM station, called FM100, but i have no information on that one.

Now, the DJ talking. Argentine's radio stations have this bad habit of
ruining all the live broadcasts talking over the beginning and the end
of almost every song, and playing a banter of the radio station here and
there. There's nothing to be done about this obviously, so don't blame me,
blame the system! lol.
