COMMENTS:
This is a very good concert. The band is in a talkative mood and you can pick 
up on some of their banter in between and during songs. The crowd is a bit rowdy.
Liam warns them to stop throwing cans. Inbetween some songs they begin to chant. 
Supersonic or Take Me Away are probably the high-lights of the CD. The packaging 
is nothing spectacular. On the back, Cigarettes & Alcohol is labeled as 
"Cigarettes and Alcohol, You Pissheads", Take Me Away as "Some Night", 
D'Yer Wanna Be A Spaceman as "Basement" and Whatever as "Shakermaker". It is the 
complete concert. | Reviewed by Gary Szymanski <grsski@discover-net.net>
