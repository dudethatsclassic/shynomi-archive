Notes : 
Some copies supposedly of this show that are in circulation are actually from the 2nd night.
During this show Liam dedicates "Live Forever" to "the Princess", referring to Princess Diana who died a month earlier. 
On the following night he dedicated the song to "The Band" - listen out for it to tell the difference between nights.
