Here's another beautiful sbd recording from Oasis.Both gigs
at the Earls Court Arena were very important for them.
The quality of the recording is very good but not so good
as this fantastic concert really deserves.
The original sound was a a little bit too bassy,the guitars
and the voice were a little bit lost in the mix,
the stereo separation was not so good 
and the recording levels were too low.
So i've adjusted the eq,brought transients back to life,
i've improved the stereo separation and maximized 
the whole mix with L3.
I've also corrected a couple of flaws 
that were in the original recording.
I'm not here to say that this is the definitive version or 
i'm the best audio mastering engineer.Not at all!!
(former mastering engineer i should correctly say..)
But i think that now this recording sounds 
as it should soundif it would have been 
officialy released as an official bootleg
in the same way Pearl Jam do for their live recordings.
I hope you'll like it!!
