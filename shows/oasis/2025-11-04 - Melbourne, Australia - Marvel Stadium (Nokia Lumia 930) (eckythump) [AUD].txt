Notes: Not sure what else I can say about this band and this tour that hasn't already been said - I've seen Oasis on every tour they've visited Australia (Adelaide at the Entertainment Centre in 1998, Melbourne at the Forum in 2002 and Festival Hall in 2005), and while those shows were a long time ago now while the band was in its "prime", this show blew them out of the water (though The Forum show in 2002 was legendary and one I will always remember). The setlist is the same every show so there's no surprises here, but the 60,000 people packed into Marvel Stadium on what was a cold wet miserable Melbourne day (a public holiday to celebrate a horse race as well no less! Thankfully the stadium has a roof they could close, otherwise I would not have been recording this) could not have cared less. And when you've got such iconic anthems that have become even more loved over time, why would they? No one who saw these shows will forget them for a long time.

Recording notes: I was in the level 1 stands half way around on the right side of stage about 3 rows back, and it wasn't the sound sweet spot, but I've managed to tweak the recording to get it sounding reasonably good through the wonder of Izotope's RX11 and Ozone 12. It won't be the best recording of the tour, but it won't be the worst. Not too much talking around me, though some guys came into the row in front of me during Rock'n'Roll Star, and you can hear them talking during the encore break, but they were too busy filming on their phones to talk during the music, which suited me. I really didn't want to get stuck in the crush getting out of the stadium and there was a specific train I wanted to be on which I would have missed if I'd stayed in my spot, so I started to make my way closer to the exit right at the end of Champagne Supernova, so the recording ends pretty quickly as the final chords of the song come to end, but no music is lost, just a bit of crowd cheering. I'm sure you can find it on YouTube if you need it. Enjoy!
