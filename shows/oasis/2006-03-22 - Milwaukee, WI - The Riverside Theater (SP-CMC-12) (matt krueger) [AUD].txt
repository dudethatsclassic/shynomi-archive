Notes:
This recording is pretty distorted/overdriven. Nothing was limiting the mics, and the 
show was very loud causing the distortion. I'd rate this a 7/10 at best. Certainly listenable, 
but probably not a show you'll listen to often. It was our first time taping with these 
microphones so we didn't really know what would happen. Next time we'll hopefully have something 
limiting the signal. Oh and a guy jumped on stage during Don't Look Back In Anger, that's why 
they stop the song for a few seconds.
