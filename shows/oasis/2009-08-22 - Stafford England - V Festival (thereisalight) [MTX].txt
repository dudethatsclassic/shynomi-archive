V Festival, Staffordshire 22nd August 2009 - THEIR LAST SHOW !!! [IEM/AUD]

Really love Petrock's recording from V-Fest last gig from an IEM Feed but i always find that the Audience was missing, specially during Don't Look Back In Anger.
After some trade, a real kind soul gave me an audience recording source (Thanks to you Mate)
From this moment, the idea of putting these recordings together grows on me.
Here's the final result (after 5 different unsatisfying mixes).

Here's the source used:

Audience source
Taper : AlexBuratti

Rig : 90 Degree Mic {Level 55] -> Zoom H2 

Sound Quality : Decent Audience Recording For A Festival !! 
Really thank you for recording.

IEM Source
Taper : Petrock2000

Source : Icom IC-R5 (ear monitor feed) > Edirol R-09 (44.1KHz 24 bit)

Mastered in Adobe Audition 3.0, Har Bal 2.0 and Izotope Ozone 4.0
Again, Thank you for it


Mastering:
Uncompressed wav for both source > Time Aligned with Cubase SX > WAV > Track Splitting > WAV

Only time aligning and volume adjusting, panning during the mix, no other filtering/altering of the recordings.
Note that it wasn't mastered track by track but during the play of the whole gig, as if it was done in real-time during the gig.
