The two Knebworth dates are regarded as the greatest gigs in Oasis history with 2.6 million people applying to get tickets, the biggest demand for tickets in British History (1 in every 20 Brits applied).  The band played to 250,000 fans over the two days the largest shows in the bands history.  

"Oasis wanted to set a new benchmark for an open-air performance; they hired the biggest PA, the biggest video screens. This moment could be seen as the last great Britpop performance; nothing after would match its scale."

If their is one defining moment of when Oasis reached rock super stardom this is it....

This is essential listening for any OASIS fan
