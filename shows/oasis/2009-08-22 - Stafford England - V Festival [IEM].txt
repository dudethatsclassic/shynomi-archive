Info
-----
Last show (ever?) from Oasis recorded from an in ear monitor feed.  Occasional interference in some songs, largely fixed using the auto healer in Adobe Audition 3.0 but you might hear some artefacts here and there.  Very good mix.  A slight gap of a couple of minutes in Champagne Supernova as my batteries ran out and I had to change.  Consider this my Christmas present from me to you and here’s to Oasis 2.0 and the Noley G solo record.  Onwards and upwards for 2010.
