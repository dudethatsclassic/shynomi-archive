Notes:
  
* = Originally Recorded By The Beatles. 

This was the first time I have seen the band since 2001, so I was excited to say the least.
They did not disappoint. Solid setlist & the band sounded wonderful. 

On the recording, it went pretty well throughout. I would like to comment that during 
"The Meaning Of Soul", a woman came looking for her children and rudely pushed me aside
wanting to check my ticket. When she realized she had the wrong row, she left me alone.
It does not affect the recording all that much, but you will hear it as it happens for 
a short period of time. 

Also note, I amplified the right channel by 0.5% due to it being slightly lower than the 
left. It may have been due to the way I was standing in accordance to the right stack.
