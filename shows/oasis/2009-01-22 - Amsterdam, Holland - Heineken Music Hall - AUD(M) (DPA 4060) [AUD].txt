NOTES:

As with my recording of the band's concert in Amsterdam the evening before, no mastering of this recording has taken place. You're listening to it exactly as I captured it.

It's a shame the band didn't vary the set by even one song, but beggars can't be choosers. Liam remarked just before 'I'm Outta Time' that, "Unlike America, we don't like to change."

Liam's voice sounded strained to me and he apologised in advance of 'Morning Glory', in case the vocals weren't good. Though strained, his voice held up well throughout the set.

The band as a whole seemed to enjoy their second night in Amsterdam more than the first. Perhaps that's because they hadn't had to travel and were better rested. Who knows?

Between 'Morning Glory' and 'Ain't Got Nothin'', you'll hear a girl ask me (in Dutch) to hold her beer, while she attempts to locate her boyfriend by sending him an SMS message from her phone. I could have tried to edit her out, but she unwittingly gives the recording a bit of extra character and also serves to uniquely identify it.

Once again, thanks to the wankers who threw beer and allowed me to share their cigarette smoke this evening. It wouldn't have been the same without you.

And don't get me started on the lighters that people held up during The Masterplan. Are Oasis an AOR band now? :-)

Enjoy!
