Notes:
Went to this show with my wife, my best friend & his wife. Great time; this was a recording where I was having at a show rather than recording a show 'only'. This was (I think) my 8th time seeing them. Recording came out rather well considering the variables. Vocals are prominent in the mix, guitars are clean & the bass is thick at times, especially during the encore.I shared a few MP3s of this show off of liveforever.us post-performance but did not do a proper transfer of this show until late 2006 where it was shared off of dimeadozen.org.
