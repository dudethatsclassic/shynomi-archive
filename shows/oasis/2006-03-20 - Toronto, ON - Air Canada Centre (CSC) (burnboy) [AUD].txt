I'm Albert Johnston (aka BurnBoy).

I've been attending concerts in the Toronto area since the 70's and in late 2001 decided I would like audio copies of the shows and began to record them.
They are all digitally recorded using various equipment with improvements being made along the way. 

Some of my recordings have been posted to dimeadozen.org  in the past but it is highly unlikely that these ones are the same file sets as before as a lot of them had been saved to cdr in audio form. At the time, hard disc drives were expensive and I thought this would be the way to go.

Some of my recordings were lost due to hdd crashes in the past, and some of those missing files have been posted at dime. I would love to get them back again if anyone has a copy they could re share.

My intention is to post all of my recordings that are allowed at dime starting at the beginning and continuing chronologically throughout. I have multiple recordings of many shows as I have taken extra gear in in case of failures. Some are 24 bit recordings which I can post if there is demand, but the 16 bit cd quality ones will be posted first. With multiple recording,  I will post the one that is in my opinion better, but if requested I can post the other versions of the same shows, 24 bit versions if applicable.
