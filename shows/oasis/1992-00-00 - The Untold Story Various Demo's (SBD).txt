Ok this is another silver disc I have entitled "The Untold Story" cat nos OACD006.

Once again I bought this at a record fair in Manchester in the mid 90's sometime.

This is what the information on the inside cover of this silver disc says.

"This new Oasis collection comprises recordings that fans have long dreamt
of owning - studio items of unreleased compositions from the days before Liam metaphorphosed
into the sneering, spitting bastard son of John Lennon! As a bonus, 
early concert takes of these songs are included.     

A glimpse of Oasis jamming freeely is provided in the shape of three great instrumental tracks
recorded at a US soundcheck.  

Things are brought bang up to date however with three contemporary performances from Noel
in solo and TV sessions.

Fittingly the sound quality of these recordings is very high for these recordings
and add missing chapters to the continuing success story of Oasis.

This cd is a true must for any serious fan."

Not much else I can add really that just about sums this silver boot up. Overall the sound is excellent.

Some of the songs are actually songs we all know and love but in an embryonic form "Champagne Supernova".
