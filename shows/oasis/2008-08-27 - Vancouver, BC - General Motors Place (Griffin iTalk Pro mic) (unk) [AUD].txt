Oasis
2008-08-27
General Motors Place
Vancouver, BC

Source: Griffin iTalk Pro mic -> iPod Classic at 16/44.1 -> Audacity (to split tracks)

This one doesn’t sound very good, my first time using the iTalk and was so disappointed I never used it again. Lots of clipping, it has a built in feature that auto reduces the volume when the noise gets to loud so there are plenty of volume boosts/drops, but at some points there is some decent sound worthy of a listen. If anyone wants to play around with it and try to improve it please feel free.
