This is a rare promo CD issued to US radio stations only although it is also listed on Discogs as an unofficial release here. 

It is a highlight show of the Oasis gig at the Hammerstein Ballroom in New York. 

Great sound quality on all songs. 

This contains probably my favourite version of Fade In-Out. 

Amazing intro and Johnny Depp plays slide guitar just like he does on the album version.
