OASIS 100% GENUINE 1ST EVER CASSETTE LIVE RECORDING.

This cassette was given to me personally by Noel Gallagher as a master copy to record demos from a recording 
of their first ever recorded live gig as Oasis (including Noel) at Manchesters Boardwalk, 14th January 1992.

The recording also includes their 1st ever studio recorded demo track.

Please note, to add to its value, none of these tracks were ever recorded & released except one track on the 
cassette, which was the demo to the intro of 'Columbia'.

The actual cassette (no CD's then) & cassette cover was drawn & written on by Noel himself, in my presence.

The recording was taken from the mixing desk on that night & I used it as the 1st generation master to record 
my demos for the band, that were then sent to the major record labels as their first ever demo. Ironically we 
had no replies !! 

I was asked by Sotheby's of London to auction this same cassette several years ago, but i decided against it.

So, if you want a piece of genuine rock history, here you have it.
