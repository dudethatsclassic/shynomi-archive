Oasis - Avenham Park, Preston 11th June 1994 (DAT Master)

Rig: Sony D7 DAT/ ECM 909a microphone

Postion: Stage right, about 10 feet from the stack

Linage: D7>WAV>Flac 8>You

Tracklisting

Shakermaker
Fade Away
Digsy's Dinner
Live Forever
Bring it on Down
Up in the Sky
Cigarettes & Alcohol
Supersonic
I am the Walrus

This was my first Oasis show and recording. I wasn't too fussed on them to begin with but this show really got me. A beer flew over my head during the first tune and haulted the first song. By 'Walrus' I knew they were going to be more than a flash in the pan indie band. 

Anyway enjoy as always!

Baggy!!!
