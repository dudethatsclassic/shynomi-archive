Notes:
This one has all naughty words edited, some rather clumsily.
Otherwise, this is a fine set of Zombie.  
Great sound quality, but both channels were low and unbalanced, distant overall.  Not anymore.  

I checked the dates for the live 1992 stuff on Sleeping Corpses box set, those are from 10/14/1992, 10/31/1992 and 11/30/1992.  So this one should be ok to share.
bill:  Pantera/White Zombie.  Pantera's show was also recorded by Westwood One, and is circulating.

I saw White Zombie in 1995, went alone, sick and running a fever.  Pit damn near crushed me. 
Got to the barricade and stayed there, made eye contact with Sean, so I saluted her... and she saluted me back!  :)
The front cover is a picture of my metal button from that concert.

enjoy!
