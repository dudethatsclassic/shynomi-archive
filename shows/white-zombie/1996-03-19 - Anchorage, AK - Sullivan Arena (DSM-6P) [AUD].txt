Show Notes:
This one should make my haters muddy their Michael Morgan's.

When this show was announced, Anchorage went nuts...the show sold out in days, and I believe it was the final date of their tour.

All that said, went with my gf at the time, and as my mics were on the glasses sans lenses, I thought to myself "what kind of idiot would wear glasses on this floor?".

"More Human Than Human" was playing.

About 20 seconds after that, this guy with his back to me flailing around, his fist went right over my girlfriends head and across the bridge of my nose.

You can hear it as a brief "pop" on the tape (at the 43:39 mark)...it sounds like the little plastic mallet on that "Don't Break The Ice" game (Parker Brothers).

The gf then handed me her thermal, and I blew about 50% of my septum into it, slid the glasses back into place, ***and taped the rest of the show*** (as a *real* taper would do).

The glasses kept sliding off due to the blood from the patch of skin ripped off the side of my nose by the right nose-rest of the glasses (the scar is still there).

And after the show, when you always point at "that guy" post mosh-pit...I was "that guy"...an easy 200 fingers pointing at me on the way out.

My eye was black for 2 months.

SICK version of "Children Of The Grave", btw...

The suffering we real tapers do for our art...hahaha.

Anyhow, THANK YOU Dennis...FUCK YOU Hagar! (you kids figure this out yet?)


Back to the salt mines....

---ballsdeep OUT!!!
