I had to do some manual track number renaming. Exact Audio Copy renumbered them incorrectly. I transferred my master cassette of this show a long time ago on a friend's equipment. I got a CDR copy and he got a CDR copy, and the wav files were deleted. These tracks were burned as bonus tracks on disc 2 of his recording of Oysterhead in Vancouver Oct 23, 2001. I have split my show out on its own by renumbering the tracks I ripped from my discs.

This was part of the Ozzfest 99 festival outside at Thunderbird Stadium in Vancouver, British Columbia. Black Sabbath headlined the show later that evening. Each act had a pretty short set time to play.

I tracked out several extra events in the show, including making the Crazy Train tease it's own track, and making introductions their own tracks as well. It all plays consecutively and gapless playback is needed. If you don't like it you can re-join some tracks for your own use (but, why, really???) .....
