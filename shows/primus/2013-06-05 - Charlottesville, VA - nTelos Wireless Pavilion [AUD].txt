Venue sound was outstanding! 3D was very cool! Great show!

Note: Battery issues with preamp lead to using mics direct into M10. With the lower
power they overloaded a little particularly at lower frequencies. I performed
a high pass filter at 80hz in Audacity and it reduced the distorted frequencies
but maintained decent low end.

At the end of the show Les offers us "a choice between two mammals for encore: 
puppies and beaver". As the crowd begins to voice their preference, somebody throws
a shoe and hits him. He says we won't get anything and leaves the stage. Damn.
