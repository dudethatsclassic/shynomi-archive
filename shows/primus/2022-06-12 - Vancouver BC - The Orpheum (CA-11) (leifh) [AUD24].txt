My 43rd Primus show.  This was the sixth and final show I caught on this tour, having flown out to Winnipeg and followed them back west.  Recorded from the orchestra section - RC, Row 9, slightly ROC by 1 seat.  

This was the first time they used Cage Claypool's music video for Follow The Fool as their live visuals accompanying the song.  

Primus played the Orpheum 10 years prior, almost to the day (June 15th, 2012) on the Green Naugahyde Tour.
