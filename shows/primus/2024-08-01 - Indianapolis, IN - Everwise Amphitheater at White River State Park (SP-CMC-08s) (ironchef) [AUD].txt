Notes:  Recorded from the GA pit.  It was too crowded in the middle, so I stayed
over to the far left side where I had some space around me to keep away from
the talkers.  I would've preferred to be in line with the PA stack, but again with 
the people and the talking, so I was a litle left of that.  The sound was heavy
on the bass, but at least it was free of crowd interference.   I don't normally mess
with EQ, but I thought this would benefit from the simple preset high pass filter in 
Audacity to take a little of the rumble out.  The Coheed and Cambria set was also 
recorded, but I missed the set by the first opener, Too Many Zooz.  Enjoy!
