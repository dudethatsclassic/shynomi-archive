Notes:
The Tabernacle is not known for it's acoustics, and we didn't have the best seats, so I lightly EQ'd this to bring out the higher frequencies. There were a fair amount of drunks, whistlers, and clappers around us, but they were mainly annoying between songs, so I used Audacity to selectively bring the noise down a few dBs wherever it was unbearable.

The reason for the title of this recording is thanks to my cousin, Chuck - toward the end of "To Defy The Laws Of Tradition", he stuck his face right in my mics to tell me about his damned green bag. Yes, a green bag. It was very frustrating when I listened to the playback, and he WILL get an earfull when I see him again. Anyway, I couldn't leave it like it was, so I had to tweak it a little, but none of the music is affected. Right before the final fadeout after "Pudding Time", we get to hear about the green bag again. It was amusing this time, so I left it in as it fades out.
