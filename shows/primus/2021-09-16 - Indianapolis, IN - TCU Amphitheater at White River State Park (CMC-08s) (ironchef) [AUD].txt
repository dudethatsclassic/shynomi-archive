Notes:  As I mentioned in my notes for the opening set by The Sword, this was my
first show at this new amphitheater that was built on the site of what used to be an 
open air venue with a temporary stage.  Even though I was about five rows back from the
pit, the overall sound was more muddy and distant sounding than I expected.  I was over to the
far right side of the audience, but not far from the PA.  

The crowd around me wasn't bad for a Primus audience.  However, I would have posted this days
ago if I hadn't obsessed over about 20 seconds during the quiet ending of Cygnus X-1 where 
someone was clapping.  I tried to do some editing on that without affecting the music at all.  
It's not as loud as it was, but it's still there.  Otherwise, it's free of crowd noise.  Enjoy!
