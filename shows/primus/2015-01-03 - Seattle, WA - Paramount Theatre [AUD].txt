15th Primus show.  This was the last of 3 shows in a row that I went to on my trip up the west coast.  Everyone lined up across the street before the doors opened.  The Paramount is a good venue, and the floor was general admission which made for a high energy crowd.  I was dead centre on the floor, about 15 'rows' from the stage if I remember.  

I got the setlist during the intermission.  Skerik sat in on the second set with the Fungi Ensemble, as well as Here Come the Bastards.
