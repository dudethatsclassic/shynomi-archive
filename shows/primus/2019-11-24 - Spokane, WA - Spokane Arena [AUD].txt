Third of four bands playing that night.  Taped from section 204, 5th row.  Mics pointed at right stacks.

This was my 36th time seeing Primus and 40th concert with Les Claypool.  Time flies.  I wasn't planning on attending this gig originally, having seen Slayer during the "final tour" in 2018 on the Vancouver stop.  But a last minute change of plans saw me going to the [Primus] show in Missoula the night before and catching this one as a pit stop on The Trek home.  By the time I got to buying a ticket for the show, the selection remaining was not the best.  I also got the whole set on video.
