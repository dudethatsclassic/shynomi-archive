Notes:
My 6th time to see Primus, and the first Primus show where I got to sit down.
Didn't get covered with bruises and other people's sweat.  :)
A few talkers but nothing too bad.  Amazing show, phenomenal lights and backdrops.  
Ler has technical difficulties (or as Les said, "testicle difficulties") after Fisticuffs
thus the improv bass solo and chat with crowd.
New stuff sounds great.  I will be buying their new album.

enjoy!
