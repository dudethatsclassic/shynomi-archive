Primus show #46.  Pretty last minute decision to go to this and the previous night in Redmond, WA.  When the tour was announced, I wasn't very enthused by the lineup (ie. Coheed & Cambria playing an hour before Primus every night).  However, after seeing the show in Highland a week prior, I just couldn't resist catching a couple more shows even if it meant enduring the support band.  I couldn't have done it without the help from esamirp and Grizzly Adams so a big shout out to both of them.  Since I bought my ticket only a couple days before the event, I ended up with a GA Lawn ticket, which I thought didn't matter, but as it turns out the venue has fenced off the pit area in front of the stage as a Premium GA ticket.  After some quick thinking from esamirp and a bit of sleight of hand, I was in the premium section and caught all three bands from the front rail.  

Guerilla Toss played first and their set was just as hot as the sun at 18:00.  The security team working the show went above and beyond to make sure everyone posted up front was staying hydrated.  Unlike the previous stop in Redmond, Coheed's pre-show acoustic set was only 1 or 2 songs, allowing us non-VIPs to enter the venue closer to the scheduled time of 16:30.  Their main set was still a dreadful 60 minutes spanning from roughly 18:45 - 19:45.  Apparently their drummer still makes the same ill-advised facial expressions while performing "unplugged".  

Primus took the stage right around 20:20 and their set was even better than anticipated.  The crowd was lively and there was a fair bit of crowd surfing happening.  Les sang a bit of Johnny Cash's Folsom Prison Blues before beginning Hamburger Train.  He also did an Is It Luck? fake-out before starting the encore song.
