The first set is a mix of sources 1 and 2 only.  Seth had consistent cable issues with the CA-14 mics throughout the first set so it was left on the cutting room floor.  Both of Seth's sources also had some issues at the beginning of set 2, so the song Hennepin Crawler is Bryan's recording only.  Many small clicks and pops have been meticulously removed.  

The crowd was pretty rowdy.  Claypool seemed fatigued or possibly hung over.  He forgets to switch off his envelope filter used in the song Hennepin Crawler before beginning the next tune, Last Salmon Man.  This happens again at the start of Lee Van Cleef, you can hear the delay used on Jilly's On Smack for a few seconds.  

There were 4 tapers in attendance this night and we all met up afterwards.
