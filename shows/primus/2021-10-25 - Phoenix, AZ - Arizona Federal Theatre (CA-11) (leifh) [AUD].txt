Final show of the summer/fall 2021 tour.  My 37th time seeing Primus.  Recorded from section 4, row 15, dead centre.  
I made 3 different audio recordings that night and also shot video of the whole show.  

Ler was noodling South Park before starting Welcome To This World.  Over The Electric Grapevine was last on the setlist (following My Name Is Mud) but Les nixed it.  A Farewell To Kings turned into a bit of a train wreck after the guitar solo, but they got it going again.  Stairway To Heaven tease before The Toys Go Winding Down.  

Vancouver band Black Mountain was the opening act.  

The Arizona Federal Theatre was originally named the Dodge Theatre, followed by the Comerica Theatre.  

Support the band, buy their merch and see them live.
