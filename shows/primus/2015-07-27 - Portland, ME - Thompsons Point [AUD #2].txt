My 17th Primus show.  The weather was great and the performance was too.  This is a brand new venue, I think this show was the second or third ever to take place.  Its sort of an industrial area and there's a retired warehouse on the property.  One thing I noticed right away is, you could easily sneak in by kayak.  There's also a grassy bank on the side of the highway (stage left) that would be perfect to film from (for free).  

I was there early and was one of the first in line as the gates opened.  On the way in there was a pat-down.  The dude who was doing the patting in my line was being very...thorough.  I actually brought 2 recorders and 2 sets of mics and 2 battery boxes to this show.  So I was bringing double my usual amount in for a stealth show.  The security dude was doing his job a little too well and touched one of the recorders.  He immediately stopped the pat-down and waved me through with an awkward look on his face.  I'm sure glad it turned out the way it did and I got in the venue without trouble or someone offering me a phone number!  Haha

Les sang a bit of Hello My Baby before Moron TV.  The night before this I went to see Weird Al Yankovic at the Maine State Pier.  Towards the end of that show, I saw Les Claypool and son Cage leaving the Pier.  He mentions attending Weird Al and eating a couple lobster rolls around Portland.  The setlist from this night also has a lobster roll reference.  During Over the Falls, Les introduced Ler's new Shark Guitar that they got while in Portland.  It turned into a pretty funny bit. Before Lee Van Cleef, Les teased I Am On My Way.  Les played some seriously messed up shit before Wynona's Big Brown Beaver.  At one point there was a drone flying around with a little camera attached to it.  

I would say this is the better of my two recordings I made this night.  For the opening bands I only used this rig.  The other Primus recording I did turned out pretty good too, but this one is better.  The performance this night was also above average by my estimate.  I noticed Ler and Shane LaLonde checking out GOASTT from the front-of-house sound tent.

I was staying on the border of Portland and Westbrook.  I walked 4 miles each way to and from the show.  Not because I had to,  I could have caught a bus or a cab.  It was just a nice day and I got a good look at some more of the city.
