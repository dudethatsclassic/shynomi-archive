Primus 05/03/89
Stanford University, Palo Alto CA
KZSU 91.9 Wednesday Night Live radio show with Ben Dee

Source: FM radio broadcast> cassette tape> stand alone CD recorder> CDR> cool edit 2000> CDR.

Notes: I received this little gem along with about 9 other Primus shows that were either unknown or new sources. The guy who sent them to me got them in trade from a taper who was new to CDR technology. So every one of them were burned as 1 big track on a stand alone burner with songs tacked on the end of CD's from this show and that show to save on CDR's. It was a nightmare! So I spent quite a bit of time editing and splicing back together the shows so they ran in order and flowed as a show should flow. 
This one stuck out to me as above average quality and completeness. I traded for this several times in the past and none are as good as this is. Most other copies of this are taken off an old grainy high gen. VHS tape someone filmed of this in the very small studio/radio station. And is not complete as far as I know. 
This had all commercials and talking between band members and DJ intact. I edited all but one commercial out (I liked the one that is on here) but left everything else as it was.
Dan Copenhaver (Zbird)
