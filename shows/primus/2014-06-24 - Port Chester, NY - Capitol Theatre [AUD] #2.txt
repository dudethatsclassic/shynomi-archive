11th Primus show for me.  Really liked this venue, and Port Chester was a breath of fresh air after being in New York for a few days.  They projected extra visuals on the walls, as well as this night's poster before the show.  I was directly in front of the soundboard, leaning on the fence, dead centre.  Audio turned out pretty good.  Got both setlists.  I took video of the whole show, some of it is watchable, but the second set might be kind of frustrating.  After the intermission, a taller guy was standing/dancing in front of me.  Okay, fine.  But he kept getting closer, and closer, to the point where he was pretty much grinding me and I was pressed against the rail with nowhere to go.  Then he gets pissed off because he was pushing his back into my elbow, (I'm holding a camera eye level) making the video un-steady at times.  Oh well.  There was a good amount of crowd surfing at this show, which was cool to see.  

Les referred to himself as, "Cocksucker Joe," during Over the Falls and blasted someone holding up a George Lopez sign they tore off the wall on the way into the theatre.  Someone yelled out Pork Soda, and he sang the line, "Grab yourself a can of Pork Soda, you'll be feelin' just fine," after Mr. Krinkle.  If only.  Les also teased PF Money/Sandwiches after Jerry.  Oh and the Mexican place just up the street is great.
