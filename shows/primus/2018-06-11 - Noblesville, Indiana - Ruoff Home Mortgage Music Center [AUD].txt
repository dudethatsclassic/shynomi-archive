Notes:  As I said for the Mastodon recording, this show was supposed to be the previous night at the 
Farm Bureau Insurance Lawn at White River State Park in downtown Indianapolis, but it got postponed 
a day and moved to a different venue because of thunderstorms.  I moved into the GA pit down front
for the Primus set and was glad I did.  After Mastodon I was a little concerned it might be too much
for the mics to handle, but it sounded great and wasn't as heavy on the low end as I expected.  I moved
a couple of times early on, but for the most part I was towards the back of the pit.  Great sound and visuals
from Primus on this one, so check out this tour if you get a chance.  Enjoy!
