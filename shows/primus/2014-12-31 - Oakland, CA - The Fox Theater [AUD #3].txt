This would be my 13th Primus show, and 4th time spending NYE in the Bay Area.  Lots of familiar faces inside and out.  I think I got to the Fox around 4:00PM and hung around until doors.  Good times.  

The first set was regular Primus.  There was a giant, inflated sperm whale hanging from the ceiling of the theatre which you'll hear Les call, "Spermy."  I was dead centre, on the first raised section, directly behind the pit.  I managed to get video of the whole show, but there are times when the crowd was very enthusiastic and bouncy.  Prior to the show and during intermission, there were ocean/whale/fishing sounds played over the PA.

The second set was when Danny Carey joined in.  It looked like he and Tim were having a blast playing together.  Lots of smiles exchanged.  I spoke with Danny a couple months before, after he sat in with Primus for a few shows, and he had nothing but good things to say.  

Some very cool noodling from Les between 'Last Salmon Man' and 'Southbound Pachyderm'.  Super long outro for My Name is Mud.  Ler played an EVH style strat during Hot For Teacher, which Bob C Cock sang.  Bob Cock then gave a Plant-style intro to Moby Dick, which Les played a bit of whamola during.  Tim had some nice words to speak through Ler's mic toward the end of 'Here Comes the Bastards'.

Hope you enjoy this recording and remember kids, stay off the yellow cake mix!!!
