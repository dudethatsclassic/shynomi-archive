Harro East Ballroom
Rochester   NY
Feb 22nd 2000


This was a very nice sounding show to begin with, and it had plenty of volume, so why is it here as a 13 RM? Most of you already know I'm nearly insane, but maybe you're wondering if there is a better reason as well...

Yes, it had volume, a bit too much as it peaked over 0.0 consistently. Most CD players and even some FLAC decoders would read this and clip, I bet some older CD players wouldn't play much music at all above the digital distortion. And it was a bit thin and brittle. 

It's a fine example of how even the best sources can benefit from a proper mastering job. I've been able to create more space so it seems less compressed, it doesn't go above -0.1 ever, and it definitely has more warmth. For me, much more enjoyable. It's not one of those "wow, what a difference" type RM's, but even the subtle smoothing of rough-edges can make a nice improvement to the overall listening experience.

All radio talk, ID's and mentions have been cut, it's just the show.
