My 41st Primus show and second time seeing them in Calgary.  

I was checking out Ticketmaster about 30 minutes after doors opened and there were still tons of seats available via the original broker (not secondary resale).  I was debating moving to a different seat following the opening set by Black Mountain.  Just as Primus started - boom - the venue was full.  What the hell happened?!!?  I don't know but I'm glad it was a full house.

During the first couple songs, security was doing laps - no photos or video, no drinks on the stage, etc.  It didn't take long for the audience to rush the front and the rules were out the window.  My row 2 seat became more like row 4.  

The band was ON this night, especially after witnessing the lackluster gigs in Regina and Saskatoon on the previous stops of the tour.  This was the first time they played The Ol' Diamondback Sturgeon since 2015-12-31 and before that it hadn't been played since 2004.  

The very end of Harold Of The Rocks is finished in a hurry, probably after realizing they were rapidly approaching the 23:00 curfew.
