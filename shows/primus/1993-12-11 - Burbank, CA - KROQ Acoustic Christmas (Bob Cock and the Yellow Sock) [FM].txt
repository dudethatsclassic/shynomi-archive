"I've had an erection for 5 hours in anticipation" - Bob Cock

Bob Cock and the Yellow Sock are actually Primus in disguise as kind of a jazz/lounge band, with Bob Cock fronting (Bob Cock is a friend of the band, and was producer on Suck on This). Bob Cock doesn't come into the set until the Disco Jam. According to my sources, Les is on an upright double bass, and Ler is on an electric guitar with no (or little) distortion. It's really cool to hear them play Primus songs this way, and the medley at the end is very interesting. Among the songs they cover in the medley are Stayin' Alive (The Bee Gees) and I Want to Rock and Roll All Night (Kiss). This is definitely a must have for any Primus fan, and at this small size would make awesome filler for another Primus show.
