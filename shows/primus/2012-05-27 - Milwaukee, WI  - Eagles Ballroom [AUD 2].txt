Stereo AUD master digital recording

CA-11C > MT II > WAV > Cool Edit 2000 > TLH v2.4.1 > FLAC 8 > transfer > you

Mics were physically separate left / right channel lines pinned over each shoulder facing stage.
I was just in front of the soundboard, slightly to the left, & that got a nice balanced stereo image.
I added fades for Set 1's beginning & end (Set 2 is hard-cut both in & out).
I brought down a dozen spots with bumps / loud claps / loud bass notes.
The overall levels were low (for me), & I gave it all a little 2dB boost, then cut it up into tracks.
I originally mastered this 2012-05-29, & finished putting the titles on tracks on 2012-05-30.

NOTES

Primus on the Green Naugahyde tour, for one of the "An Evening With Primus" shows.
Meaning, it was just them / no opening act, but they did two hour-long sets plus an encore.
A few Popeye cartoons were played between the two sets.

2 inflated rubber spacemen were in the back corners of the stage.
They projected moving faces on the "helmet glass" spot at times.

- - - -

This is a partial show, got the full first set & 45mins of the second, then ran out of battery power.
Shoot me, I wasted 20mins pre-show, not knowing when the lights would go down.
If I'd started right at the real beginning, I prolly coulda got it all - lesson learned.
I'd seen the power was low by the end of Set 1 / shut it off for the Popeye clips.
And so I missed just the opening notes of Set 2's Eyes Of The Squirrel, turning the unit on.

So anyway, it's missing ? 4 or perhaps 5 songs at the end, incl. 2 encore tunes:
* Groundhog's Day (I think) / ENCORE Wynonna's Big Brown Beaver (for sure)

- - - -

The Rave vs. Eagles Club vs. Eagles Ballroom

The building is the Eagles Club, & concerts there are run now by The Rave.
There's 2 places to play in it - The Rave (smaller) / Eagles Ballroom (larger).
This gig was in the ballroom.

- - - -

The venue is typically NOT a place where ya hear good sound, to the ear or on tape playback.
It's a ballroom from the 1940s big-band days, & tends to be echo-ey / boomy for modern music.

Given that track record, this tape turned out alright; maybe being right by the board helped.
I could barely make out Les talking when there, but what's on the tape is clearer than what I heard.

Usually I clap a lot, but I consciously tried to refrain for this one / only did a little bit.
Some other talkers in the area at times, but it's not too bad.
For the frequency I got bumped into by drunks, I expected lotsa mic bump issues - nope, just a couple.
At times ya hear the crunch of people walking on plastic cups on the floor.

- - - -

There's a few short 2-minute-ish bits that fit between legit songs.
As I couldn't identify them as being their own songs, I left them before or (mostly) after titled songs.
If some are legit, please correct me by posting to the comments.
Same if I fudged any of the titles themselves.

Similar bits also had Les talk a bit, & those I broke out as '[LES SEGUE]'.
