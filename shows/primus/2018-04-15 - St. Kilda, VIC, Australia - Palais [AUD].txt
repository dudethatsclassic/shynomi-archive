Primus
15th April 2018
The Palais, St. Kilda (Melbourne), VIC, Australia

Recorded from front of Lounge seating, level with right speaker
Source: Zoom H1 (in-built mics, level 15, low-cut off) > WAV (24bit 48kHz)
Processing: WAV > Audacity (top/tail) > WAV > CD Wave > WAVs > FLACs

Notes: 
My setup isn't ideal for this venue. 
I just put the recorder in my pocket, so the mics point upwards.
And in this case, the right mic points to the speaker, but the left inwards, mostly at the crowd, so it's a right leaning recording mostly.
The Palais has a very high ceiling, so the sound ended up very "big room" and echoy.
I've done minimal processing on this (just top/tail, no volume boost) in the hope someone gets enthusiastic and remasters?
