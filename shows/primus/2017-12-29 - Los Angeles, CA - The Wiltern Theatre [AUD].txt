My 31st Primus show.  The first of two nights at the Wiltern Theatre.  I was close to dead centre, at the front of the second section on the floor.  There was an opening band added at the last minute, Fade Up Fade Out Bye Bye.  

Les played a few bars of Kashmir before starting Mr. Krinkle.  The crowd got pretty rowdy in the latter half of the show, and when people started bumping into me, a switch was moved on the recorder causing two small drop outs.  One during The Storm at 6:45, and again at 2:00 during Welcome To This World.
