Comments:  I had been holding off on seeding this show, partly because I was certain that a better recording made with better equipment would surface.
Nothing has surfaced, so I feel inclined to put this recording out there for those who are interested.  I tried to smooth out the rough spots as best 
I could (there is some enthusiastic clapping and yelling near-by at various points).  The sound quality is pretty good considering the use of built-in 
mics and a hand-held device. This is a very decent and listenable recording. Until a better version of this show emerges in the future, 
I hope you enjoy this one.
