Arrived too late that night at the Fri-Son, i nearly missed the beginning, you can hear on the first track how i'm setting the microphone and stuff...
The venue was packed, i tried to get a good spot but it was impossible, so the first set is recorded 30m away from the stage. During the break before the second set i moved in front of the right stack.
Funniest thing was after Tommy The Cat, Les asked if there is any good bass player in Switzerland, of course there are and so David, a very young dude around 18, joined him on stage and played Tommy The Cat with the two other band members. Nobody really expected that he could play that good but he did.

Enjoy!
