Very good to see Primus again!  Same equipment, slightly better tapes.  It was as good or better than the first time.  I really like when bands like Primus vary their setlists instead of playing the same stuff all the time.  Also, 4 new songs, and they're all pretty cool.  Some poor kid passed out and hit their head before Primus started and was removed from the crowd by "Safety."  Les adds his thoughts on Tracy Morgan during the show, and seems to enjoy playing at this festival more than once.  During Electric Grapevine, there were some hang-gliders with spotlights on their helmets and they dispensed large amounts of a glitter like substance.  This made it look like it was raining fireworks.  As a result, you can hear the crowd reacting as this all happens to the left of the stage.  Ler cut his hair since the last time I saw Primus.  At the end, Les waved his hat and everyone wanted more, but it didn't happen.  This seems to be every show.  

There is some great official footage of this show, only parts are available to view online, but it is good.  I left this show and tried to get some sleep, but due to being in the closest "campsite" to the main gate, I was kept well awake by Lil' Wayne and everything else going on, especially at That Tent.
