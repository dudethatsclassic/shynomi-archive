Boss’s notes: The problem, if you could call it that, when I attend a Primus concert, I 
don’t sit with my arms folded. As such, I cannot contain my excitement. No fucks are 
given, I celebrate with great fervor! However, I’m hoping these episodes do not ruin 
your listening experience of this recording. It just so happens that Pudding Time and 
Southbound Pachyderm are a few of my top favorites… cover your kids’ ears, enjoy!
