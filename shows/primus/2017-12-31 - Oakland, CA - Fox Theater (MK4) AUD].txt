I wanted to go see music on New Year's Eve so grabbed Primus tickets.
No one else wanted to go so I went solo.  I arrived at 6 and discovered
a long line and the VIP scene so I figured getting a spot would be a 
challenge.  I also took public transit so I knew I had one shot to get
my gear in and had no place to leave it in the event I got denied.  I met
some cool folks waiting on line and after a long wait we finally got in. 
No issue navigating security and got in and had a choice of being way off 
center on first rail, dead centrer off the rail or dead center on the 
second rail.  I decided to do dead center on the second rail since wasn't
sure what crowd was going to be like.   The opener came out and they were 
not my thing.  The band had members of Tool and Mastodon along with the
guitarist from Primus.  They were very loud and actually had to turn the 
levels down during the first song.  Between sets I had the pleasure of 
having two drunk girls next to me that wanted to talk and squeeze onto the 
rail.  The guys they were with paid no attention to them and I could see why.
Finally Primus comes on and it was a fun set.  Not familiar with the music but
I liked it and enjoyed the visuals.  I did spend most of the set being pushed 
by one of the drunk girls who was trying to make room on the rail.  Eventually
she realized I wasn't moving. At 12 midnight they did the count down and ballon
drop.  Luckily not a single baloon hit my mics.  Overall the sound is pretty good
even though I was further back than usual.
