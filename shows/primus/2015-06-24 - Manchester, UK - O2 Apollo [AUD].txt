The first set was just performed infront of a black backdrop with minimal lighting. The
second set had a stage set full of candy and twenty foot mushrooms. A video screen displayed
scenes from the 1971 film. The band were joined by a cellist, a xylophone player and a couple
of Oompa Loompas! During the encores there was video footage of an Oompa Loompa trying to get
into Apsley Cottage, the pub adjacent to the venue, but his head was too big to get through
the door. He then visited Old Trafford and the Etihad Stadium provoking cheers or boos from
the opposing factions in the crowd when he held up the respective football shirts.
