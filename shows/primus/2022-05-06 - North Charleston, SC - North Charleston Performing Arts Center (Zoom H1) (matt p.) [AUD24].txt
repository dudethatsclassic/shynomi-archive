This show was a lot of fun, and the recording came out really good, I think. There's definitely some audience chatter
throughout, but nothing too distracting for the most part. Despite being part of the Farewell to Kings tribute tour,
they still played "a shitload of Primus" stuff, as Les says at one point. I'm really enjoying the new stuff,
especially "Conspiranoia," and it sounds great live.

Battles opened, and I recorded their set too.
