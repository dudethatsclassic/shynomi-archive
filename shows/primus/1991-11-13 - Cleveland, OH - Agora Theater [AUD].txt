Primus
November 13, 1991
Cleveland, Ohio
Agora Theater

This was an opening spot for Fishbone. By this time, it was almost truly a co-headline as Primus was waxing in popularity.
This is the only show I've ever been to that started right on time. Hence the reason I miss the first half of Sgt. Baker. Otherwise the recording is complete.
I used the mic that came with the WM-D3 and the recording is very clean but bass light. Rather than muck with in SF, I thought I'd just let you adjust your low end.

At one point a friend who did not realize I was taping, steps up and starts talking to me so there is a moment of conversation before I can clue her in. Oh well!!

This has been traded (and stolen) a few times, but here is a direct copy from the master source.
