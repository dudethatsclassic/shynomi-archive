Notes: A Fantastic/trippy crack of midnight 3D show from Primus at a beautiful venue and great festival! Been a Primus fan 
for a long time, so I may be biased, but they stole the festival from the Bigfoot stage.  Easily could have replaced a few 
of the snores over on Sasquatch.  Definitely one of the most interesting Claypool shows I've seen over the last 10 years (4 
with brigades, 3 for Primus [first non-Bonnaroo show] and 1 Oysterhead). Hard to say if this one was crazier than the blue 
glitter dropping skydivers of Bonnaroo 2011 or leaving the Dead show at 11:15PM to catch the midnight Primus set only to 
wait 2 hours at Bonnaroo 2004.  First thing Les says, now starting after 1AM, is you "just missed one helluva Dead show!"
Needless to say, Primus and Les always keep things interesting and now with the 3D show.  Not only that, but a Fight broke 
out during Last Salmon Man...kinda shady as I didn't know that still happened at shows.  Wearing headphones you can hear a 
girl screaming for help and people yelling for security.  Broke my glasses during the scuffle (not wearing them but in a 
backpack). 

Cheers to all the rad Canadians I met over the weekend! You all rock :)
