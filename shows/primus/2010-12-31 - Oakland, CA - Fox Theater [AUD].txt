Comments: I had been holding off on seeding this show, partly because I was certain that a better recording made with better equipment would surface.  Nothing has surfaced, so I feel inclined to put this one out there for those who are interested.  The sound quality is pretty good considering the use of built-in mics and a hand-held device. This is a very decent and listenable recording.  Until a better version of this show comes along I hope you enjoy this one.
