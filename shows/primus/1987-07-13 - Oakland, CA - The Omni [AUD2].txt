Primus
The Omni
Oakland, CA
July 13, 1987

Audience Recording

Earliest known live audio recording of Primus

Fixed version -- When I got this, it had a couple of dead spots between tracks. The "Eleven" fragment was lumped together with "Is It Luck?" on one track and "Temporary Phase" was lumped together with "Pudding Time" on one track. 

Lineage: AUD> ???> .flac> Trader's Little Helper (decoded to .wav)> CDR> CD Wave Editor (extracts & de-tracking)> PC> Audacity 1.3 Beta (removed gaps, re-tracking)> TLH (fixed SBEs & .flac level 6)

Tracks:

1. Jellikit
2. To Defy The Laws Of Tradition
3. Temporary Phase
4. Pudding Time
5. Tommy The Cat (cuts in)
6. Eleven (cuts out)
7. Is It Luck?
8. John The Fisherman
9. Sgt. Baker

Running Time: 36:11

Lineup:
Les Claypool - bass
Todd Huth - guitar
Tim "Curveball" Wright - drums
