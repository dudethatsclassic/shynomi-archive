Some great commentary from Les on this one including one of my favorite quotes of all time,

"and in about 25 minutes, that man right there, Tom Araya is going to come out and kick every one of your fucking asses"

It took me 9 years but I finally popped my Primus cherry at this show. I've included a low res scan of my ticket
stub since I was there. But, I was not the taper. It's always fun listening to a show you attended, even if you 
barely remember it. I do remember one of my friends getting hit in the head with a piece of the lawn during Mud 
though. Good times.
