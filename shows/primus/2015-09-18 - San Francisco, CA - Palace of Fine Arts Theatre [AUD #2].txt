Recorded from front row, center. I haven't really listened to much of it yet, but it should be a pretty clean recording, as it was at a seated venue, so there was no pushing or anything.  Very refreshing to be at a Primus show and not having to worry about being pushed!
The show sounded great live.  I was at the first Wonka show from NYE and it's cool to see it the 2nd to last time it will ever be performed.  Bring on future two set all originals tour, though, please!
