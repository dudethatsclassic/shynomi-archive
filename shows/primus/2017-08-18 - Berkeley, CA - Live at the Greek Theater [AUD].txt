Lineup:
Les Claypool - Bass, Vocals
Larry LaLonde - Guitar
Tim Alexander - Drums

Recorded stage right, on the barricade in front of Ler.  Great show.  One of the best sounding Primus shows I have been to, outside of the vocals being a little low at times.
I had to record it with the internal mics on my recorder because my CSBs are currently being fixed. The vocals were pretty low compared to the instruments for parts of the show, so there are points where the vocals are hard to hear. Otherwise, I think it sounds pretty good all things considered, though.

*As a bonus, I've included the recording I got of Les joining Clutch on stage to play Earth Rocker.  This was the only song I recorded from Clutch's set.
