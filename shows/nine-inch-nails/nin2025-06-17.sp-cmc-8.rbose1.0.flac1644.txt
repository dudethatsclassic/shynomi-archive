Boys Noize was the support act operating his turntables on a small stage behind the
mixing desk at the back of the arena. He was on for over an hour before Trent and 
Atticus were revealed on the B-Stage in the middle of the floor. For Act 2 the main 
stage had a transparent screen in front of it which allowed projections on to it from
live cameras.
