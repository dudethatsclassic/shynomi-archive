This is centre stage shot, with no obstructions. According to tour references
it was A and V recorded by the crew, which would explain why its
over everyone's heads but NOT shot from the balcony! (Knowing the Forum as a venue 
Id say it was shot from the mixing desk area from a tripod.) 

Sound is audience rather than soundboard, which is a shame! The camera is steady,
though it has a slightly annoying autofocus to it, which means after every blast
of lights and strobes it takes a second to refocus.

I got this show from Camden Market on VHS a dozen or so years ago, and only recently
converted it to DVD using a standalone recorder, so its not crystal clear, but well
worth adding to your collection!

No artwork Im afraid, and the chapters are just every 5 mins rather than track-marked.
