Notes:
Another one from Duke2004! This is a very clear recording from this date! I especially loved listening back to this one and hearing how clear Heresy's synth line was at the beginning and also Survivalism! Gary Numan coming out to do Metal and Cars must have been pretty special. Big thanks to Duke2004 for sharing this uncirculated recording.

Duke2004 had about 9 uncirculated NIN recordings from the London area ranging from 1999-2014. I am very grateful he wanted to contribute their recordings to the archive for preservation. If you have tapes that are not up on the archive and you would like to have them preserved, please get in contact at info@ninlive.com Thank you so much.
