Nine Inch Nails
13.13 Club, Charlotte, NC USA
February 25, 1990

A Doodee Music Master Recording

One of my masters new to circulation. Trent and the boys opening for the Jesus And Mary Chain.  Interesting to compare this earlier show to their current sound and hear how it's evolved. They didn't have the largest of crowds for this opening slot as the JAMC crowd were late arrivers but they seemed to do ok for themselves over the years in spite of that. Enjoy.
