Notes: This is the first time I have seen this anywhere and I assume the first time it's been circulated. Lots of firsts for this one, as it is the first source to come from this show as well! Thanks to ijwthstd pulling this out of his collection of tapes, his family member recorded this one. Although, as you can tell by the sample, is not that stellar. It's great to have a token from the night if you were there, or just to have a recollection of the performance! I transferred the master tape and it has a lot of hiss on it, even for a master tape. I can only assume this brand of tape is just no good. I also had to speed up the recording by +5.2. Tapeflip is in-between Hurt/The Downward Spiral and at the end of 'Head Like a Hole,' there is some sort of dropout that I can only assume is from the mic. After that point, there is sporadic popping on the recording. In any case, it's great to see after all these years I am still getting uncirculated tapes out to the masses! 

This is the 16 bit version. This can be burned straight to CD. If you would like the 24/48 version, you can find it on DIME or the NIN Drive.
