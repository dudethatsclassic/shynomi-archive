Notes: 


I had no CLUE as to a NIN concert. 
Because of the shows released by others on this website, I went to see what it was all about and decided to take my son.

He was not thrilled with the idea because he had no clue of the music.

NEEDLESS to say we are both now HUGE fans.

Show was electric to say the least.  
I cannot believe such energy can exist on stage.

No one sat down.  
The show began with the hot sun at our backs and facing stage.

That didn't even affect the incredible lights and smoke created.

NIN was the complete show!  
I am still blown away.

All I can say to the band is take your break and come back again.  
I know someone that will travel to see them.....





During the show I had mechanical problems with the 9 volt battery on my preamp.

The conncections could not stay flush.  
Thus i had to manually hold the battery in place throught the whole night.

The effect is minimal, but during some songs there may be a small "glich" or "skips", namely "Suck".

I apologize for this.

During an audience recording there are just some things you can't control.  The first is the crowd.
Chatter is present and sometimes distracting.  It does not interfere with show, except you can notice occassionally.
The second is mechanical.  As result I will be using an AC adapter for my preamp to prevent the "clicks" that I had to manually remove during editing.

On the lighter side, during "Piggy" the microphone was thrown into the audience and during the part of the song where the keyboards are being "banged" a member of the audience is singing, 
"nothing can stop me now....nothing.....nothing can stop me now....nothing"
and then a girls voice is heard saying "WE LOVE U!!!!!!!"

Again to the fine people who provide such great shows and wonderful website, this is my gift to you as thanks! 

Please enjoy......
