Notes:
TIMP told me that he recorded this show when he was sending me all copies of his old masters back in about 2009. At the time, he did not have this recording and I hope that one day I’ll be able to transfer all of the master minidiscs. At this point in time the masters are with someone else. But let’s fast forward to today, almost 20 years since the show.. He received this master recording again from a CDR copy he gave to a friend who ripped the CDRs so it could be archived! The recording was titled, “Sinful Pleasures.” At the end of the concert, there are a few bonus tracks that were included in the CDs. 

I believe this was TIMP’s first concert recording. Not bad for the first time. Great to preserve this on the archive!
