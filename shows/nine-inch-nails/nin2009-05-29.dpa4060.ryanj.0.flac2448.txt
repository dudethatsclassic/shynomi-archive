Notes: What a way to start off my NIN show binge. HANDS DOWN THE BEST SHOW I HAVE EVER BEEN TO!!!! The 2 shows after this one on the weekend did not even touch this show. The setlist was so balanced and there were so many classic NIN songs that were played on this set that you can't ask for a better setlist. Plus they played an extended set because of the fact that it was only NIN on this date (along with street sweeper). On the setlist they were actually supposed to play 3 more songs (Echoplex, Dead Souls, and In This Twilight) but could not due to crufew (dammit). Seriously if they played all 3 of those songs there would not be a reason to go to any other show on this tour!!!! Heresy just blew the roof off along with Mr. Self Destruct (the song I have been longing to hear live in person). Taped this from dead center about 30 ft. from the soundboard. I was really please how all the tapes came out this weekend. This probably rates 2nd best to Clarkston but I think all of them are really good. The I also taped the show with my video cam (Sony HDR-SR10) and the source should be up soon. I hope you enjoy the recording. 

Ryan
