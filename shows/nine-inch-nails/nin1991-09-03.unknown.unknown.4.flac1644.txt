Nine Inch Nails
9/3/91 
Le Bataclan 
Paris FRN                           

CS4     31min

Setlist:
Now I'm Nothing	
Terrible Lie	
Sin	
Physical	
The Only Time	
Wish	
Down in it	
Head like a Hole (Cut)


Part of NIN's Euro tour opening for Guns 'n' Roses. As with most of the 
shows from this tour technical problems/ poor audiences led to short sets.
This show in particular the technical problems start right away with vocals
cutting out on Now I'm Nothing. Wish ends in a feedback laiden tantrum 
leading to Trent repeating over and over 'Fuck you' during Down in it.
Trent's rant about how much he hates France during the opening of 
Head like a Hole closes the show in classic fashion.

From the recording point of view, this is one of the best NIN '91 recordings
and is nearly complete.
