This recording has not been circulated much, to my knowledge at least. I want to thank Polewka for taking the time to send a few of his master tapes to me to be transferred! He has made hundreds of fantastic tapes and I am very grateful that he let me transfer a few of them! This tape doesn't disappoint. I think it's probably the best source from this date, in my opinion. "Down In It's" intro is cut a bit due to the MD flip. Enjoy!
