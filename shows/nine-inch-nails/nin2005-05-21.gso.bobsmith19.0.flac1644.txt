technical:
- source: giant squid omni's -> giant squid battery box -> sony mz-nh1 (pcm mode) 
  -> usb 2.0 -> sonic stage v3.0 -> 44.1/16 wav
- matrix: mic source -> audacity v1.2.3 (wav file split) 
  -> adobe audition v1.5 (eq/dyncomp/amplify)
- encoded to flac using flac v1.1.0 on linux (fc3) with switches -8 --no-replay-gain
- recorded from the floor, right side (facing the stage) about 30 feet out
- mics on my shoulders pointing at the stage about 12 inches apart.

notes:
- well here's saturday night (first night) at the tabernacle in atlanta.  sorry it took so
  long.  i'm just not real good at this yet...i'm a unix guy, not a sound engineer.  i 
  always share my recordings, but my ass was just slack there for a few months.
- that being said, it's not the best boot, but not the worst either.  (headphones help!)
- the place was jammed fucking tight on the floor so i got pushed around a lot.  people 
  right up next to me on all sides made it difficult to not pick up some crowd noise 
  (or move).  one guy in particular comes through pretty good at times (dude, leave the 
  singing to trent next time!).  i thought about trying to edit the guy out, but decided 
  that was how i heard everything that night, so left it in.  not that i know how to do 
  that anyway...
