Comments:
I've had this tape for almost 3 months and was in talks about getting this tape for almost 3 months prior to that! My Nakamichi cassette deck crapped out on me and I needed to get it repaired. This was the first thing that I put into it. Skot emailed me back in August and told me he had a few tapes of his friends that had been sitting in a box for years. This was one of those tapes. The recording is fantastic for a 1990 tape! Full recording, and fades in just a few bars after the intro begins. The tape did run a little bit slow, which I sped up the tape to proper speed. This was taped by his friend Trevor on a Sony Walkman, this is one of my favorite finds for the archive! Thank you for Skot getting a hold of me and sending me these tapes over for the archive! I'm so happy to have added this, to what would have been just a tape rotting away in a box. Awesome! 

This is the 16 bit version. This can be burned straight to CD. If you would like the 24/48 version, you can find it on the NIN Drive.
