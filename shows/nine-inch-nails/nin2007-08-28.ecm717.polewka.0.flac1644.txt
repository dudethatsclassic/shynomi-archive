Notes:
First released April 2014. This recording has been uncirculated for almost 7 years. I want to thank Polewka for taking the time to send a few of his master tapes to me to be transferred! He has made hundreds of fantastic tapes and I am very grateful that he let me transfer a few of them!

This show was initially supposed to take place March 5th, 2007. But NIN has to cancel because Trent was sick and had problems with his voice. They rescheduled it for this date during their festival run of shows in the UK. Trent has some good banter this night about how shitty festival crowds are and how disgruntled he is that Carling was not big enough for NIN's light show segment to be allowed in. Meaning that they had to scrap their "computer segment" where they play "Me I'm Not" and other songs.

This is a great recording. Clear vocals and not much in terms of crowd noise. Very excited to add this recording to the archive! Enjoy the tape.
