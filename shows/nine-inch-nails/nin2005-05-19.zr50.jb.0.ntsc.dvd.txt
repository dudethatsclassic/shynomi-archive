Notes:
Most people know this as the infamous, "something's gonna get broken" show. During the club tour, there were some hiccups along the way. And as mics, guitars, PAs went out Trent showed his frustration by unloading on equipment. During Head Like A Hole, Trent's mic kept going in and out and at the end of the song just trashed the stage. Sadly, with this being such a great recording of the club tour (these Philly dates are probably the best visual representation of the show out there) the tape runs out right as Trent starts to destroy the stage. On the audio recording you can hear everything being blown up right on stage. Not sure if anyone actually caught this. But we would definitely love to see this if anyone has footage. Thanks to JB for taping this show. Epic stuff.
