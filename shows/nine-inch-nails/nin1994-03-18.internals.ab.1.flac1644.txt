Notes:  
First released March 2014. This has been uncirculated for a full 20 years. This was recorded by a local college student who interviewed Trent before the show for the college radio station. This is from a 1st gen cassette. Tape is incomplete because the girl who taped it must have used the same tape as the one she recorded Trent with (hence, less tape to record the 60 minute set). Tape flip is right after Sin and "March of the Pigs" is cut at the beginning. Recording fades out during "The Only Time." More than likely the rest of the concert was the last three songs. In terms of the tape, it's not that great. Being a microcassette recording, the recorder is made more for voice recordings. Many spots are blown out and unlistenable. But, having only one other source available from these warm up club shows, this is a special recording!
