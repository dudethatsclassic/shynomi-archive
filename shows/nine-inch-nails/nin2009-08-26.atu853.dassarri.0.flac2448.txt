Notes:
Fourth NIN show in less than a year for me. As will be apparent in the recording, T5 was packed wall to wall with fans and drunken morons. I managed to plant myself just forward of the SBD (against the rail) and was soon joined by other tapers, as this was definitely the prime location for good sound in the venue (I was the one in the grey RITC/AIR shirt with a Remington Firearms cap). During 1,000,000 until the first part of Dead Souls, a speaker malfunction caused an audible amount of crackling, which they thankfully managed to fix.  
I hope Trent's vacation from touring won't be too long and these excellent live shows will be sorely missed. Enjoy! - Dassarri
