Notes:
This is an upgrade for the archive with this source. The other transfer of this was from an unknown gen cassette and it had a lot of hiss reduction done to the tape. This is a raw transfer and the recording is actually quite good! 

Not sure if "Head Like a Hole" was actually played at this show. NIN was having troubles throughout the night and there was a slight pause before "Get Down Make Love" to make some adjustments. This could possibly be the whole set from this night. 

Big thanks to everyone who has been sending in cassette for me to transfer. Over the course of the next few months, I will have a handful of recordings that will be seeded here and on the archive.

This is the 16 bit version. This can be burned straight to CD. If you would like the 24/48 version, you can find it on DIME or the NIN Drive.
