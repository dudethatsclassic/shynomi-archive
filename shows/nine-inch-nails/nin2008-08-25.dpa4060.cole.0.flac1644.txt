Notes:

My first time taping the band since June, 2006 - this was a real treat. Trent 
commented that he was sick, but you really could not tell by the performance.
Apparently, a lot the set was cut short by a lot of the gear not being able to be 
fit into the Convention Center. This was probably the smallest place of the tour
& to top it off, not many showed up, either. Half of the seats were empty, with 
the floor being mostly filled. 

On the recording: My AA's decided to die during the end of "Terrible Lie". I did 
not notice this until "Head Like A Hole" finished. I quickly fixed the problem, 
and continued w/ the recording. Im probably more upset than anything at myself for 
not using brand new batteries, but you live & learn. The songs not captured were as 
follows: "Survivalism", "Ghosts 8", "Only", "The Hand That Feeds" & "Head Like A Hole". 

My apologies - please try to enjoy what I was able to record, regardless. I know of
at least 2 other sources, so we are thankful for that.
