Notes:
Next to a few other recordings that have been surfaced in the past months (like the KROQ 2005 MK4 source) this has been very sought after by me for almost 5 years. Alex contacted me in 2010 to tell me about the two Russian NIN gigs he recorded back in 2007. Sent me a few samples of his recording and was told he’d get to me in a couple days with the WAV files so I could add them to the archive/drive. Well, days became weeks, weeks became months and months turned into 5 years! I tried the email address that he contacted me from probably every 2 years to see if I would get a response. FINALLY, last week! I got a response and to make the long story short, I got the recordings yesterday. So excited to hear these and release them on the archive!

Two of the finest recordings (this and Moscow) of the 2007 tour. Never seen a recording on the Archos Gmini, but the quality is impressive! This is also the infamous show where Trent has his ‘steal my music’ speech. So again, thanks to Alex for getting back in contact with me to unearth these uncirculated recordings.

If you have any past recordings of Nine Inch Nails you would like to add to the live archive at http://www.ninlive.com, please get in contact with me at ryanj at reflectinginthechrome dot com!
