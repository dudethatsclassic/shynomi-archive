Notes:
Second night of NIN's two night stint in vienna. High energy with a plethora of songs from The Downward Spiral. The band was again high form and poured everything into the performance. Some reporters were saying in their reviews of the previous night that it was a hard set. Well, they were in for a surprise if they attended the second night because Trent Reznor and friends didnt give the audience a breather until La Mer basically. Intense, hard and rough. Aaron jumped into the crowd during March Of THe Pigs.
Recording wise this turned out great, I positioned myself closer to the stage than I did on the previous night and was rewarded with a richer bass sound and less mud. I can only speak for myself but listening to this recordign is a blast and thoroughly enjoyable. Hope you like it as well.
