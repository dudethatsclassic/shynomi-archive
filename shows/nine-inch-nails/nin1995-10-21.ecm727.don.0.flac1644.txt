Recording info:
Sony ECM-727p -> Sony TCD-D7 (Sony DG90M DAT) Sec 103 Row C Seat 16 (2nd seat in from aisle as I recall)
Tascam DA302->COAX->Sound Blaster Audigy->Sound Forge 8->CD Architect 5.0->Flac Front End level 8->you

This is the same source that was used for the HAWK 095 release "The Oddball Couple" however this is the complete show. I also taped Prick.
I taped hundreds of shows between 1983 and 2004 and this one remains one of my favorites because of the pristine sound and high energy captured on the recording.
I included the poster from the show along with a scan of my original ticket stub.
Hopefully everyone will enjoy!
