Notes:

This was my 2nd time taping NIN this year, and once again the 2nd time I ran into 
issues with capturing the complete set. This time, I blame it on the Schottenstein 
for closing my section [Section 121] and making me wait 30 minutes for a replacement 
floor GA ticket. Once I received my replacement ticket, I had to get a yellow 
bracelet to enter the floor. Upon entering the floor area, the 2nd song of the set, 
"Letting You" had just started. I had to find a good spot, and get my gear ready. 
Doing all of this, I somehow left the Edirol on pause for the first hour. This band 
just seems to bring me the worst luck when it comes to taping. Out of 4 shows, 3 of 
them I have run into problems. Very strange. Perhaps next time, I'll leave the mics 
at home!

So after my great seat in 121 had been taken from me forcing me to miss part of the 
set & having my recording not turn out as expected... what could have topped it? You 
guessed it, a totaled car on the way back to Toledo outside of Bowling Green thanks 
to some black ice on a 75N overpass. Luckily, my girlfriend & I are ok. 

Enjoy what I was able to capture! There were atleast very solid versions of "Echoplex" 
& "The Good Solider" at the show & on this recording.
