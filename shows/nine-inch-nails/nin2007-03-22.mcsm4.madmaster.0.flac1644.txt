Some audience noise can be heared during the more silence songs.
2 Americans, right beside me, were talking during the whole concert.
During 'Hurt' a guy asked me if the seat beside me was empty, you can hear him asking and after that there's some distortion for about 1 second (when I turned my head to him I probably touched the cables)...
And a special 'Thank you' to John, the guy behind me who knew I was recording, who also asked me something during the concert, straight into the mic's...

Extra info:
Second gig of NIN in Amsterdam during the 2007-tour.
After 'Suck' two barely dressed ladies came on stage with a cake for Aaron, whose birthday was celebrated for the 2nd day in a row.
The cake ended in Aaron's face and parts of it were 'shared' with the audience.
