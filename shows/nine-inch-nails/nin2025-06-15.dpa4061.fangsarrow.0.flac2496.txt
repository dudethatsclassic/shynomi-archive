Notes:
First date of the 2025 Peel It Back Tour, first Nine Inch Nails show since 2022 and the first Nine Inch Nails show in the Republic of Ireland since 2009! Incredible show that was in four parts, two on the smaller B-Stage then two on the main stage with the full band. I was situated in the standing area directly between the mixing tent and the B-Stage, Trent walked behind me through the crowd when coming out for the show. B-Stage sections were more sedate than the main stage ones so volume was quite different in the arena.

Thank you to Gambra for sharing this to the nin live archive. The archive has gotten a new makeover, including moving to a database. Which many have requested. Comments are welcome.
