This is almost a year late in getting this out. Sorry about that, hopefully people will still enjoy it. 

This was the first of two nights for the NIN club tour in Arizona. Unfortunately, I didn't really have a good spot to tape from that was protected from crowd scarring. So there are plenty of who-hoos, ya-hoos, and sing along. Somewhere along the line NIN became a sing-a-long band. However, I think you'll still enjoy the show as it came out pretty nice.
