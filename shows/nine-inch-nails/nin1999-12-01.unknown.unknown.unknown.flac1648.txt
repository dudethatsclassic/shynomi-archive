Notes:
This is a cassette recording copied to a DAT tape. Unknown gen and no taper information on the DAT. Decent recording, nothing worth writing home about. Hiss is not overbearing, but this seems to be a higher gen. This is also the same recording as "Finality." I originally had this on the archive in mp3 format. So this source is an upgrade.

On the DAT tape had the writing "You Fucking Promised Me." Probably just a title the taper gave it, but thought it was worth noting. Transfer is in 16/48, just as it was copied to the DAT. For archival purposes.
