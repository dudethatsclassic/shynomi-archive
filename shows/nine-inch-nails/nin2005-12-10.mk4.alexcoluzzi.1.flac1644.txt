Notes:
I’ve wanted to get this show for a long time since I first heard that there was an audience recording from this show. The webcast sounds good, but is very compressed. This audience recording is exceptional! Coluzzi has also made some other great tapes (see Fresno 03/2005 and Oakland 2005) so it’s nice to add this one to the archive. Thanks to all parties getting this one out there, you know who you are. :)
