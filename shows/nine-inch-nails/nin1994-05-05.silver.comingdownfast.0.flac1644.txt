Notes:
This Silver CD has been a pain just like the "Nails in my Head" Silver CD… This has been thought to have been one of the 07/29 or 30/1994 Atlanta dates. But this is FALSE. This show is from New Orleans 05/05/1994. Here is why, and big thanks to TheBang for figuring this all out:

FROM THEBANG:
The "Coming Down Fast" bootleg is labeled on the artwork as being Atlanta (no date given).  The only Atlanta dates are July 29 and July 30.  But most people think it is actually 5/5 New Orleans.  I don't remember all the details of why, because I evaluated the evidence presented at the time, and then filed it away in my mind that it was settled that this was New Orleans and not Atlanta.  So I don't remember exactly what all the evidence was.  But I do remember someone posting on one of the boards (ETS, I think), his first-hand account that he bought the Coming Down Fast in a record store BEFORE the Atlanta show happened (and he thought it was weird that there was a bootleg available in advance of the show).  So, it couldn't possibly be Atlanta.

And, in fact, I just found some evidence to back that up.  Here's a discussion thread from alt.music.nin, where people are discussing owning and listening to Coming Down Fast in early July:

https://groups.google.com/d/msg/alt.music.nin/KGOQPgHqOYc/M-22fM9aE-cJ

So, the recording could not possibly be Atlanta.  And for that many people to be owning it and listening to it in early July, it would have had to have been available for sale for several weeks.  I don't remember what evidence there was exactly that pinpointed it as the 5/5 New Orleans show, but it's plausible, and Atlanta is impossible.

Actually, looking over USENET some more, it looks like Matt Hartley, who was maintaining a full list of NIN bootlegs, at some point between 1994 and 1995, changed the listing info for Coming Down Fast from ??? Atlanta to 5/5 New Orleans.  Given that he was maintaining an information source about the bootlegs, I assume someone gave him info about the correct venue for the recordings; maybe the taper?

Update 05/06/2014: Found who the taper was and yes, KTS (the bootleg company) changed the date because there were so many NOLA bootleg CDs coming out. So they switched the date.
