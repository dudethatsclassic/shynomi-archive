Nine Inch Nails at Tabernacle, Atlanta, GA, May 21, 2005, audience recording.

This was recorded with Radio Shack CTR-122 cassette recorder by dime user deadlie13 (thanks lew!).  TDK D120 (Type I) master went from Onkyo TA-RW 313 into Audigy Soundblaster SE.  Track markings and cd-rs made with Magix Audio Cleaning Lab 12 program.  Wav made from cdrs with Nero Burning Rom, flac with Flac Frontend.

The sound on this one gets a B.  There is a mild hum, which doesn't affect the music much, but can be easily detected between some songs.  It's a steady recording, though, and a nice listen.  There is a tape flip at the end of track 12, so cd 2 starts at track 13.  There is a bit of "You're So Vain" during track 17.

I would like to thank the NINwiki, for supplying the set-list and for being fun and useful anyways.

Don't sell, don't circulate as mp3, give credit to crematia13 and deadlie13 when posting elsewhere.  Lew encourages you to remaster this recording, and to post positive results.  I can't remove the hum myself without compromising the sound quality heavily, but maybe some-one else can.  Enjoy!
