Notes:
I had released this footage before prior to it being added to the archive. This is private footage captured at the rehearsal concert from the now famous Nine Inch Nails "Lights in the Sky Tour." Arguably one of the best visual spectacles you will see for a concert. This is about 30+ minutes of songs throughout the night. This is the only source from the show to date. Only about 300 or so fans/friends/family were allowed into the arena and fans got in by winning tickets from a treasure hunt that was through The Slip album google earth download file. The sound is very good from the cam, and also the video was in HD. Back in 2016 when I uploaded the show, all I had was a standard definition DVD copy. Now I can present to you the full HD version of this show. 

Big thanks to thisoneisonus.org for hooking it up with this footage. You will notice that "The Hand That Feeds" visuals are very different from what you saw on tour. They scrapped the idea as they thought it was too confusing. It was ok to release this film. But the tapers wanted to remain anonymous.
