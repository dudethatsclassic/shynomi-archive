Notes:
Out of all the shows I have seen on this tour its very hard to rate which show is the best because there are so many good ones to choose from. This show in Wantagh was a pretty epic show. I have never been this close to the front at a concert before (7th row) and the sound here at Jones Beach was incredible. I have seen pictures of this place before but this place owns every Amphitheater on the east coast by far. This is my 2nd best recording on the tour, first is Bonnaroo then JB then Clarkston. There is little to no crowd noise and its sounds great on the headphones/monitors. NIN pulled out a lot of energy during this show, during Terrible Lie at the end the whole band goes nuts and TR just tomahawks the hell out of his micstand. During the Becoming Robin's guitar feed goes out throughout the whole song and at the end of the song he totally DESTROYS his acoustic guitar and just walks off like a badass. I like the Now I'm Nothing-Terrible Lie intro but nothing can beat the Somewhat Damaged intro. You can hear my Uncle and I at the beginning just so totally jacked for SD at to start the show off. We talked about hearing it all day and we got it!!! Anyways this is the last show I have to release... Enjoy.


Ryan
