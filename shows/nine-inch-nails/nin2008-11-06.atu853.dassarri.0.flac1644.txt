Notes:
What a fantastic show and an unfortunately poor recording to show for it. I'm not particularly bitter about this one, though. I managed to get a ticket for free (thank you!!!) and got just ahead of the soundboard on a very, very crowded floor. As soon as Love is Not Enough began I realized my recording would turn out poorly - everyone began shoving, shouting, pushing and yelling. But being so close to the stage and seeing the band having a great time onstage took away my stress from recording and I quite honestly forgot all about it. I had given up hope that the tape would be listenable in the least so I no longer made any effort to avoid packs of people and their lyrical shouts. I was very surprised to find that I could actually make out the songs afterwards, so I'm a little peeved that I didn't put more effort into the recording. But, oh well, I guess this is how I used to enjoy shows before I knew what taping was. Here's to a great night and the hope that NIN does this sort of thing again!
