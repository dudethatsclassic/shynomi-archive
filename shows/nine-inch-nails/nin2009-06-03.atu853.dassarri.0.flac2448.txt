Notes:

My third and last NIN show these past two years. I was able to experience them differently each time: first during LITS in a full-blown arena setting, then the intimate Atlantic City club show and , finally, the stripped-down amphitheater set. Although I must say that this experience has somewhat dampened my hatred for amphitheaters (the techs involved deserve a whole round of pats on their backs!), the typical drawbacks are evident in the recording: the audience. Stuck in a seat at an outdoor venue, you somehow always end up around several types of selfish idiots who either talk throughout the show, yell (incorrect) lyrics obnoxiously loudly, or decide to ask you loudly if you will be putting your recording up on youtube. 
All of that aside, the show was tremendous. Slightly less than half the songs played I had never seen live before and the energy Trent and Co put into the night was well appreciated. I also taped Street Sweeper Social Club, who put on a very unique (and quite funny) performance, but I left well before Jane's Addiction to make the five hour journey home.
