Source:

This was purchased from a seller, something I would normally never do.  However, I did so with the sole intent of releasing it to the Nine Inch Nails community.  When received, this release had the following issues:

-An introduction featuring the seller's logo
-The setlist had major continuity issues: went from 1,000,000 to Wish, cut into Hurt followed by In This Twilight, cut to Head Like a Hole through the Good Soldier, and finally cut back to Wish and ended with The Hand That Feeds
-A segment from Cheech and Chong's Next Movie immediately followed the program.  To drive home the idiocy of this seller, the interlude to this segment read: "The following is an officially released video track from Cheech and Chung it is not legal to upload it to any turrent site such as dimeadozen or tradersden if you remove this video track you will compress the original footage and end up with a lossy version."
