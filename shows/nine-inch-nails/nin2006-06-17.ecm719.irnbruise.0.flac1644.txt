Notes:
This recording has been uncirculated up to this point (Feb 2015). Big thanks to irnbruise for lending me her master MD tapes of shows she has recorded over the years. This was recorded in LP2 mode and my MD deck was not able to play these tapes. So big thanks to brianblank for transferring these MDs digitally for me. The recording is average, because of the LP2 mode the recording does sound a bit metallic at some points due to compression. But as this is the first audio source from this show (there is a video source available) it’s a great addition to the archive!
