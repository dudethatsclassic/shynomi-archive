Notes:
Show was out of this world. Arrived in Philly a couple hours before the show (said hello to the Tool Army pickup) and had plenty of time to get everything uncomfortably situated to get into the show. Does it Offend You, Yeah? was already playing by the time I got into GA and weren't half bad. By the time 8:20 rolled around, I thought I heard 999,999 playing (the lights were still on) and before I could ask my buddy if that was the case, the lights were shut off and the show was starting. Thus there are some pops on the recording as I attempted to put the mics into position as quickly as possible. I was very concerned about the mic position on my body and thus there may be some pops between later songs as I attempted to move them to more optimal positions. The visuals were so spectacular that I often nearly forgot I was taping and could barely stop myself from enthusiastically clapping. That having been said, though, the NIN audience was one of the worst I've ever encountered. I was often on the move to avoid packs of frat boys moving toward the soundboard to have obnoxiously loud conversations. 
I would very much appreciate an upload of the setlist, if anyone managed to snatch it. A big hello to ETS and I hope the wait didn't kill you guys. If you think you can make a nice remaster of this recording (I have no talent in this regard), please contact me to attempt remastering from the master files.
As always: NO MP3s. NO selling. NO torrenting without permission.
Enjoy! - Dassarri
