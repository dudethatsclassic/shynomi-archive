Notes:
To my knowledge, this is the first time an audience recording has circulated from the famous Nine Inch Nails Woodstock 1994 performance. Many have said this is the performance that put Nine Inch Nails on the map for their intense live show.

Big thanks to SH for sharing his master tapes with me. I know it took a little bit for me to transfer these but I wanted to make sure these were great captures. SH sent me an EQ sheet for his recording for the optimum listening. As the master tape was a bit bass heavy, and without the EQ the bass in the recording did a lot of burping. The EQ fixes all of that and the tape is great! SH used two tapes to record the show, first side was of Crosby, Stills, Nash and then the 2nd side was NIN. That is why the first flip for the 2nd side of the 1st tape was so early in the concert. Second tape started right after "Down In It." The song is not cut. Enjoy the tape!

This is the 16/44 version. This can be burned to a CD. The 24-bit transfer is available on the NIN Drive and DIME.
