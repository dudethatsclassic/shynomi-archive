Notes:
Went to these shows with my Uncle and had a BLAST! This set was full of firsts for me this tour (Last, Echoplex!) and the perfromance was great! The only problem with the recording is that during "Discipline" people were still getting to their seats which just amazes me that you could come late to a NIN show. The lady who was going past me had a purse and wouldn't you know it... her purse grabbed my mic cable and ripped it from my R09HR... My Uncle almost flipped on her. It was unfortunate it happened but whatever, I think I only missed like 10 seconds altogether. But enjoy the tape!

Ryan
