Notes:
Thanks to acc123456 for sending over this tape for me to transfer, along with a bunch of others! We did have another 2nd gen transfer of this but the other transfer ran too slow, heavy hiss and could have been tracked better. The intro to “Terrible Lie” was not recorded, and “Head Like a Hole” was not performed this night. You can hear at the end of the tape the PA music come on. So, this is a complete tape! For an early NIN show, this is not that bad! Again, bit thanks to ACC for sending the tape over.

This is the 16 bit version. This can be burned straight to CD. If you would like the 24/48 version, you can find it on DIME or the NIN Drive.
