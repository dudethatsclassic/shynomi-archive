Notes: 
Last show of the 2008 NIN Binge for my brother and I. It has been a great experience from us meeting so many nice people at each show and all the memories we will have of this fantastic show. This was also the last shows for Josh Freese and Cortini. This was by far the best performance I have seen and probably one of the reasons being its the last show of the tour!!! We came to Vegas all the way from Detroit for this one and it was an amazing journey! This audio recording is BY FAR my best one that I have ever done. The quality surpasses any show I have done in the past. Of course there are a few spots where there are screamers (2-3 from what I remember when I played this back on the flight home). But this is the best I have done. I also recorded the whole show on my Sony HD-DR10 Camcorder in HD and it looks fabulous. I just have to find a way to convert the files from ACVHD. Again, thanks to my brother for helping me bring in my gear and being a "blocker" for the show. This tour is very special to me and I will remember this tour for the rest of my life. Please support Trent Reznor and his work by buying his records and attending his shows. and trust me you might want to check it out next time as it will be the last time for awhile. Enjoy this recording, I basically am running on 2 hours of sleep cause I was so excited to get this out to everyone. Did basically all the editing on the plane back to Detroit. From the Jonik brothers happy holidays! 

Ryan Jonik.
