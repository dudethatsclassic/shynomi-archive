Big thanks to JL for sending me over his master cassette tapes to transfer along with a few other NIN shows that will be getting uploaded in the next few weeks. This is a fantastic recording from the early years of NIN at Lollapalooza. What a gem to get transferred. You don't find many master tapes from 88-95. A very enjoyable tape to listen to. 

This is the 16/44 version. This can be burned to a CD. The 24-bit transfer is available on the NIN Drive and DIME.
