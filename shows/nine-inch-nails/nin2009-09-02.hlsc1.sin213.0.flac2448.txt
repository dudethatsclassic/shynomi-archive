This was a great start to what would become quite a series of shows in LA.   The intensity of the band was met by the intenstiy of the crowd as Mr Self Destruct started...and with each song, everyone began to realize what was happening here....The Downward Spiral.  It was quite a treat to be able to hear The Downward Spiral played in its entirety.  The second set that followed was equally as satisfying, as NIN went through some of their best songs and brought out Gary Numan for Metal and Cars.  Unfortuntately, the Fonda show was cancelled immediately following this show and the Wiltern show not long after, but luckily the shows were rescheduled the following week, rather than being cancelled completely.  Trent said he was sick and was sorry if it did not sound good, but I thought it sounded pretty decent from where I was standing.
