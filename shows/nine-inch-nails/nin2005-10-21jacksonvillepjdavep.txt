
Nine Inch Nails
October 21st, 2005
Memorial Arena: Jacksonville, FL

Source:   DPA 4061s > MMA-6000 > M-Audio Microtrack @ 24/48

Transfer: Kingston Elite Pro Flash card > Lexar Firewire
          flash card reader > Cool Edit Pro > dED >
          (resampling done at highest rate, triangular dither
          at 0.54 bit depth) > CDWave > FLAC (level 8 encoding)

Notes:    Taped dead center, FOB, about 150' from stage.  Position
          varied based on whatever talking/screaming idiot I had
          next to me!
          The dED consisted of applying an FFT filter to lower 
          the bass from 55Hz to 35 Hz and remove bass under 35Hz.  
          Then the recording was normalized.  
          
          If you can fit it, disc 1 could include the first track 
          of D2 to keep the first set together.  That would make a
          disc that's over 81 minutes though...
