Recorded from the rear of the venue. This is one of the longest sets of the Downward Spiral tour. They played the full set and then came out with Adam Ant at the end for a few more songs. This is also another high gen video recording and I would love to get a new transfer/lower gen. Film, I think, would be pretty good if either of those two things happened. There is about 10 minutes of after the show at the end of the tape that also includes some other band at the end from another show I assume the filmer recorded.
