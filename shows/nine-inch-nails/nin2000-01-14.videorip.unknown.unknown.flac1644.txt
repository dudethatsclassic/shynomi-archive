Notes:
An average recording from the Fragility Tour. There is some clipping here and there from the audience cheers. The most notable part about this show is this is (to my knowledge at least) the only show that Trent sings the lyrics to "The New Flesh." Which is pretty cool.  

This is an unknown rip of the audio from the video recording of the show. Even though this is from a video recording and on the archive we don't like to have audio rips of video, we will keep it just because this was widely traded back in the early 2000s. Had to fix the sector boundaries on the 2nd disc as there was some gaps in the CDs I had. They should play seamlessly now. Looking at spectral analysis, this looks to be lossy. I would suspect it is because it is ripped from the video. Enjoy the recording!
