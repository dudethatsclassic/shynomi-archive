Notes:
This is a pretty elusive tape to obtain. I have reached out to both Richard (who taped the show) and Louis (who edited the show and possibly held the video recorder throughout the show as well) to get a copy of this for the archive. With a lot of back and forth that sadly has not gotten anywhere, this is all I could get the archive.

They got approval from Trent to film this show. Also on their youtube page was soundchecks of Terrible Lie and Sin, which are also included. This is the same source as source 1 on the page. The quality is significantly improved from the DVD version that was probably dubbed a few dozen times and then authored to DVD. I can also confirm that “Down In It” and “Head Like a Hole” were recorded for this source. Sadly, this youtube rip was partial of the show. But includes DII and HLAH. It misses almost the whole middle portion of the set though. 

Either way, I am glad to know that this is out there and I hope to one day have the full source (and raw video) for this show. For now, this will do.
