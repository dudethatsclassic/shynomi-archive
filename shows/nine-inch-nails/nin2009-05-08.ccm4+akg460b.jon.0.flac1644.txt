Show Notes:
- The Version of Piggy is the same as the one played on the "Outside Tour" the first time played since 10/28/1995 in Los Angeles..

- I'm Afraid of Americans is a David Bowie cover.
- Mr. Self Destruct's intro is the album version. Not the intro they used for the With_Teeth tour.
- First time Gone, Still was performed live.
- First time The Way Out is Through has been played since 01/15/2000 in Osaka. - No encore because someone turned off the PA system.

First show of the tour. This is a fantastic recording by Jon. Very crisp and just amazing quality! This is what you expect out of high quality gear! The only things done to this recording is I matrixed the left and right channels together, because the left channel was only schoeps and then the right was only the AKGs. So I matrixed both channels with each other about 45%. I then went on to amplify up to (just under peak) 0db. Nothing was done on the EQ side because there was no need. Here is the first night of the tour in high audio quality. If you are taping on this tour check out RITC for other tapers that will also be taping the shows and let us know what dates as we are trying to stay organized. Enjoy the tape.
