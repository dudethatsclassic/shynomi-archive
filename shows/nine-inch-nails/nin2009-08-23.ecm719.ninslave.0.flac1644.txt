Second NY show on the final leg of the 'Wave Goodbye' tour and what a treat
Trent Reznor gave to 1500 singing, clapping, devoted fans......The classic NIN
album "The Downward Spiral" played live in it's entirety and in order for the first
time ever. And if his comments are to be believed, this was the only time it will ever 
be played in this fashion.
This was never going to be an easy show to capture. The crowd intensity on
the floor was amazing and the band definitely fed off the vibe to create an
atmosphere that was truly amazing. This was easily the best and most 
memorable of the 16 NIN shows it has been my pleasure to have attended over the
last 20 years. It is a night that I will never forget, and one that I am sure a lot of 
people will claim to have been at even if they weren't!! 
Despite all this I think the recording has come out quite well. There are points 
where the movement of the crowd made for some phazing, and this was despite 
the fact that I had my mic mounted on my hat. I have not remastered it in any way, 
shape or form. If anyone would like to give it a try, be my guest. All I ask is that you
let me hear your final mix and leave this info file intact.
Finally...I would like to thank Trent for all the great memories he has left me with
from the last 20 years and wish him all the best for the future. Hopefully it will not
be too long before he makes a return. I would also like to thank him for the open
taping policy on this farewell tour. If only more artists had his vision.

This recording is dedicated to my wife for putting up with me and indulging my 
obsession without criticism or judgement.
Also, a big HELLO to our buddies Ben and Niha.
It was great to see you both again. Stay in touch bro.

ENJOY PIGS

NINSlave
