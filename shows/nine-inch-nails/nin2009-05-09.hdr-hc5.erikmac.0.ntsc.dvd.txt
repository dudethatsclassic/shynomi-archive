Notes:
This is a really good recording from the beginning of the NINJA Tour and the first time that Now I’m Nothing had been performed since 1991! Filmed from the back of the pit it seems, or even the first few rows of the seats. Great stable shots and close ups. I would love to have the HD footage from this show instead of the DVD.

If you have any more info about this show, or maybe you have some NIN live tapes sitting around that are collecting dust. I would love to add them to the archive. You can contact me at ryanj@reflectinginthechrome.com. Thank you.
