Notes:
First Night of NIN's two night stint at Vienna's Gasometer. Considered the more "hit friendly" set of the two played it was a high energy, full throttle show with an incredible Version of Hurt. The crowd was very much into it and sang along every line. Josh freese is a beast, just wanted to have that mentioned here :-)
Unfortunately I missed the first few seconds of Somewhat damaged since they started playing with the houselights still on which kinda surprised everybody in the venue. So I appologize, Its like 3-4 seconds after the song starts when I hit record. Nevertheless it turned out quite fine, even the muddy bass cant spoil it.
