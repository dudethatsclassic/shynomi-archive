Notes --------------------------------------------------------------------------
My recording levels were too low, especially on the MZ-R909, so there is some
background hiss that could have been avoided if I had checked my levels...
Recorded 15 or 20 feet from the left stack until Love Is Not Enough. Then moved
towards the back of the venue. Moved again to the left wall towards the back for
the last 6 or 7 minutes of the show. I don't want to hear any complaints about
the quality that are related to moving towards the back and to the wall. My
girlfriend wasn't feeling well that night, and I wasn't going to let taping
interfere with looking after her.
The fade tracks can be removed for a continuous recording, but they're nice for
burning to audio CD's.
