Comments:
Ok. I taped this cause I happend to live close by the festival area. I had seen NIN earlier this year already and it was great one. So why not this one. Great one again! What happened in this show, was that the festival organisation started the ending fireworks way too early and it interrupted Hurt. It caused quite a controversy after the show and not everyone liked it. At least Trent finished the song than leaving the stage because of the rude interruption.

As for quality, in my opinion it's great. Perhaps the best ever outdoor taping I have ever done. There are spots that make me think that is this even outdoor tape at all! Few phasings here and there, mainly cause people walked past me but it doesn't effect too much.
I could have worked this out earlier but I've had some PC problems lately.

Ninwiki and other official(?) sources say this gig took place in Helsinki, but that's wrong. Ankkarock is held in Vantaa and in it's smaller suburb area Korso. Vantaa is city right outside Helsinki.


BMR in September 2007
