Notes:
This recording has not been circulated much, to my knowledge at least. I want to thank Polewka for taking the time to send a few of his master tapes to me to be transferred! He has made hundreds of fantastic tapes and I am very grateful that he let me transfer a few of them! A very good recording, sadly, the 2nd master minidisc was lost. This is his one and only lost disc so it misses the last quarter of the set. Regardless, this is a great tape. Enjoy the recording!
