Notes:
This was added back on June 27th, 2009 as a shitty iPod video conversion. Remember that was a thing at one point? lol. Anyways, thanks to Frank G. for taking a drive of the archive's collection and editing the footage for me to something that I could present on the archive. This is my film from the show and there are a lot of great close ups and the video follows the music well. 

This was the 3rd day in a row of shows. Chicago > Indianapolis > Clarkston. Lots of driving in 3 days, but worth it. This show was one of the shorter sets during the NIN|JA tour and also pretty sunny out! I had a great seat near the soundboard and this was one of my favorite audio recordings I have done during this tour. There is a DVD version floating around as well. But that is in standard def. Enjoy the new edit!
