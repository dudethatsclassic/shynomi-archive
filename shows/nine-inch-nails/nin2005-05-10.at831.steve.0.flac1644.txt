Details:   Nine Inch Nails, live @ the Koolhaus, Toronto 2nd night, May 10th, 2005.
	I was 10ft infront of the SBD dead centre most of the show (tracks 3 and on), 
	during the 1st 2 songs I was about 20ft in front of the SBD in the centre, 	
	had to move due to the insane mosh pit.  This was an amazing show, definitely
	in my top 5 all time concerts.  I could only get tickets to this show 
	otherwise I would have done the 1st night as well, looks like it had a nice	
	setlist on the 9th.  Anyway this show was full of energy the band was nuts
	as was the crowd, seeing NIN in such a small venue (holds about 2500) compared
	to the arena shows of the past was unbelievable.  During "Piggy" Trent 
	jumped into the crowd, has he been doing this all tour?  During another song
	(can't remember which one right now) I think he through his guitar into the 
	crowd, and during "Suck" he through the mic in the crowd.  The show sounds 
	pretty good, not my best but still really good.  Big thanks to all the tapers
	that have already posted there shows here.  Highlight for me was "Reptile", 
	it just sounded so good, "Burn" was also a nice treat.  On a side note it 
	was insanly hot in the venue, I have never been in such heat before.
