Nine Inch Nails
May 22, 2009
Mountain View, CA, USA
Shoreline


This is just for the collectors. There's really no reason to download this recording unless you have to have everything - there are at least 2 much better recordings of this show up on Dime right now. I assumed it would be pretty easy to get a recording of the NIN and Jane's sets from people who actually know what they're doing, so I only brought my sad little Ipod/Belkin combo to record Street Sweeper. I hadn't seen any of their shows going around, and I wanted a "complete set" of the evening. Of course, I got to Shoreline a little late and missed almost the whole set and so I decided to record NIN just because I could. It came out a little better than I expected it to, but there are definitely some really bad sections and - again - it is much, much worse than the recordings already up. This does have some vaguely entertaining/informative crowd chatter, though. Did something go wrong on stage that can't be heard? Someone will tell you about it. Want to hear about the trashy blonde girl doing the wrong kind of dancing and flashing her ass while doing it? Well, you will. Is Twiggy still in the band? Nope, but he's still being discussed. And there you have it. Oh, and it's mono.
