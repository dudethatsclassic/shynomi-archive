Mastering Notes:
The taper sent me his master file over AIM and had me take a crack at the mastering of this. I hope he didn't make the wrong choice...
Although the limitations of the equipment are noticable at times, this recording shows that the taper truly knows how to use location to his advantage. I lowered the bass a tad, as it was a little overpowering, and brightened up the upper end to bring Trent & Co to the forefront of the recording. Overall, I think people will find this recording very listenable and enjoyable. Thank you, radiokid311!
- Dassarri
