Notes:
This was uploaded to YouTube about 10 years ago by AntiMusick. From what I can gather from the comments and description, this was filmed by DeAnza College students (I assume AntiMusick was part of it). It was broadcasted in the Bay Area on public access. I’ve tried reaching out to them to see if I can get a full recording, but it’s been very difficult to track them down as it seems the channel is inactive.

Also in the comments, it mentions that this was a surprise warm up show. Here is a recollection of a fan who went, “this was the unannounced concert before the 1990 Hate tour officially started the next night in SF. Just a few days before the show, djs on 89.7 KFJC were dropping hints like " if you like raw industrial music," (like was played on Mr Meanor's shows) "you better make sure and get to The Edge really early tomorrow night!" They wouldn't say who was playing, and there were no presales at all, just show up and get in line.”

This includes soundboard audio. Which I know circulates on its own to very few people. If you have this soundboard audio, I would swap this for the KROQ 2005 or Rome 2009 soundboard audio I currently have here on the archive.
