Notes:
Uncirculated up until August 2014. Almost 10 years on! When I uploaded the few uncirculated Crimson recordings a few weekends ago I asked if anyone had a few recordings from any of the shows I don't have on the archive of the club run in 2005. I received an email from Mike P. who happened to tape the first night from the two shows in Toronto! There has never been a source from the first night of the Toronto shows. There was one taper in attendance who had minidisc issues the first show and up until now, there had been no leads or any info on anyone taping the show. I had basically just stopped trying to search for anything. 

Mike was kind enough to send me the master DAT to transfer! I was very excited to hear/work on it. Indeed, the tape does sound great! A fantastic recording to relive this performance!  

There are now 4 shows from the NIN 2005 club tour that I am still trying to find sources for the archive. If you have taped any of these shows below, please get in contact:

May 03, 2005 - Denver, CO, Fillmore
May 06, 2005 - Chicago, IL, Congress Theater
May 07, 2005 - Chicago, IL, Congress Theater
May 15, 2005 - New York, NY, Hammerstein Ballroom

Again, big thanks to Mike for sending me his master DAT to transfer for the archive! Hope you enjoy the recording!

If you have any past recordings of Nine Inch Nails you would like to add to the live archive at http://www.ninlive.com, or are taping any concerts in the future please get in contact with me at ryanj at reflectinginthechrome dot com!
