FWIW, there are two other good audio recordings from this show so far, Kensey and Rjonik.
Their recordings have a little more bass in them and therefor carry a little more weight.  
Mine is a little crisper and slightly more treblely, despite being a few feet from the speakers.
I was fortunate to have no chatterboxes around me so there is less crowd noise which is good 
for really quiet songs like Lights in the Sky.  This torrent also includes the soundcheck.

Actually, this is the best recording of Lights in the Sky so far apart from the Noblesville soundcheck.

I also recorded video, but the camera shut down a couple of times briefly due to intense 
vibrations.  It will take some time to edit it properly with my audio but that will have 
to wait until I get back from vacation.
