Trent Reznor, Peter Murphy, Jeordie White, Atticus Ross: Live In Boston [6_23_06]

Radio Show 3

On June 23rd, 2006, Trent Reznor of Nine Inch Nails and Peter Murphy of Bauhaus, along with Jeordie White (NIN) and Atticus Ross, performed four songs for a small group of radio winners in Boston.  The unique set was broadcast live on several radio stations in the Boston area. These are the videos taken from NIN’s vimeo account. I assume filmed by Rob and the crew. All in 720p video and board audio.

Track listing:

01. Warm Leatherette (originally by The Normal)
02. Reptile (originally by Nine Inch Nails)
03. Strange Kind Of Love (originally by Peter Murphy)
04. Nightclubbing (originally by Iggy Pop)
