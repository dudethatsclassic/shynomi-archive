Notes:
Thanks again to FULLMETAL for sending me over his master DATs to be transferred. A good recording from one of the first shows of the Fragility Tour. A better recording than the MD source that was on the archive. No EQ'ing was done to the recording. The only thing that was worked on was the end of "Pinion" into "Somewhat Damaged" as there was some clipping from the record volume was too high. Used automatic phaser to clean it up and add some more dynamic range back to it. The show was recorded in 44.1, so this can be burned to CD.
