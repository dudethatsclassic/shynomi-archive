Because of the small venue, NIN could not bring in all of their equipment and the portion of 
the show from Ghosts to The Greater Good was skipped.  I switched discs during THTF in case 
they played a bunch of rarer songs before the encore to compensate for the shorter set.  
They didn't, although we got the debut performance of 8 Ghosts and the second performance of 
Meet Your Master.

A nice intimate show.  Thanks Trent, Robin, Justin, Josh, and Alessandro.
My 10th NIN show.  See you in Grand Rapids.
