Notes:
Big thanks to smores for sending me over this 1st gen cassette to transfer! This is the same source as the bootleg title “Hanging Like Jesus On This Cross,” but as a known gen of the recording. This version is a tad bit shorter than the unknown source. Leaving out the first 25 seconds of “Pinion” and about the first 15 seconds of “The Downward Spiral” where the tape flip occurs. 

After comparing both transfers, the 1st gen tape sounds much better. Not as muddy as the bootleg that floats around. This is why it took awhile to release this. But the quality different between the two versions makes it worth releasing this. Only thing I know is who taped the show, no equipment info is known. If you can provide more updated info on this recording, that would be fantastic!

This is the 16 bit version. This can be burned straight to CD. If you would like the 24/48 version, you can find it on DIME or the NIN Drive.
