Notes:
There was a FaceBook group where Dave posted about this show and someone spotted this and told them to get a hold of me about it. So Dave contacted me and I was ELATED to see this! This is the firs time I have ever seen this show/source. Basically 28 years on, it is finally released to the masses. The recording is great for the time and it seems like NIN is having trouble to start the night as you can hear Trent say something like “sorry for the delay.” Which is why there is probably a longer intro. 

Great to add this to the archive. And as much as I dislike social media, it’s a great way to find some of these tapes that would more than likely never see the light of day!
