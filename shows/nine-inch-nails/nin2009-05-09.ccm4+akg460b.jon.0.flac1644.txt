Jon put this up onto megaupload and it is an amazing recording!!!! The only things done to this recording is I matrixed the left and right channels together, because the left channel was only schoeps and then the right was only the AKGs. So I matrixed both channels with each other about 45%. I then went on to amplify up to (just under peak) 0db. Nothing was done on the EQ side because there was no need. If you are taping on this tour check out RITC for other tapers that will also be taping the shows and let us know what dates as we are trying to stay organized. Enjoy the tape.
