Notes:

recorded FOS on the left, around 5th-10th row, approximately. 
the audience around me was pretty "physical" during a few songs (like 'march of the pigs', naturally). there might be a bit phasing here and there in those places but nothing major, i managed to keep my footing pretty well. 
some occasional babbling here and there. luckily there was nobody close to me constantly singing or talking all through the show. 

a great show, as was berlin the following night. personally i was especially grateful and delighted to hear the 'fragile' material in this show. and it was good to see NIN for the last time with a NIN audience and not among some festival crowd. 

thanks for all the US tapers for sharing so much great material from the NINJA tour -   greatly appreciated. 
and if you happened to tape NIN on the current 2009 european summer/festival tour, please do share. thanks in advance.

enjoy.

-s
