Notes:
This is another tape from smores. This is the first time I have actually heard this recording from this Chicago show. The quality is amazing. I would have to say this rivals a soundboard source. Just listen to the sample of "Suck." Thank you to smores for supplying this awesome tape! D. Merz has taped many NIN shows over the years. If anyone has more of them, please get in contact.

This is a 16 bit recording. It can be burned straight to CD. There was no point transferring it, and keeping the 24/48 filetype since this is from a DAT recording. The recording does run a bit hot at the beginning of the tape (only during "Sin"). I suspect this happened when the taper was transferring this to a cassette tape and did not watch the levels. No brick walling, just a bit hot.
