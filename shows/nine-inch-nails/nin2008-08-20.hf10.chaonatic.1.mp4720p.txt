Chaonatic's video footage is some of my most sought after video recordings. I was never able to get the raw footage (or even the final product files) for the archive. His footage is some of the best from the 2008-2009 tour. I am not sure if he did film any more shows from 2013-2018. But if you're out there. I would love to chat about your footage!

Thank to fillow, this was ripped from Chaonatic's YouTube channel. Most of the videos were 720p, and only a few were actually in 1080p. I just kept all of the 720p tracks and merged them using MP4tools into one MP4 file for a sort of seamless video. There are fades for almost every song. But not too bad. Again, this is a great video and one of the only times that Love Is Not Enough was performed on this tour, also one of the last times it was played.
