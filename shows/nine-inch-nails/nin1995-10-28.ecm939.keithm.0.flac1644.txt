Notes:
JEMSHQ reached out to me about a Nine Inch Nails/David Bowie recording I have not seen. There is one other source I know about from this date, so it is very exciting to see another recording from the JEMS archive of NIN (and Bowie) as they are known for their quality of recordings. This one is no exception. A very good, clear recording of the end of the Outside Tour. Very thankful for the JEMS crew reaching out to share this one with the archive.

If you have any Nine Inch Nails recordings from past tours, even an extra source that isn’t on the archive. I’d love to hear from you and hopefully grab the recording for the archive. If you need help transferring it, I have all the means to transfer any sort of media. You can contact me at ryanj@reflectinginthechrome.com Thanks and enjoy this tape!
