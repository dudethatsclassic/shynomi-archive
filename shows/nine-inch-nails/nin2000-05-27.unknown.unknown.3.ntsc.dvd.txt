Notes:
Another very rare Fragility video. This one is quite a treat. 
This is one of the venues the band played in 2000 that had
video screens showing the performance, this video is a screen
shot, very steady and with few obstructions. The main issue
with this one is that the picture is a little dark. The sound 
is good but only in one channel. Enjoy!
