Notes:
I found this on YouTube and this is a VHS tape that was made by Trent Reznor for a fan named Michelle in St. Louis. She had written them a bunch of times and missed out on a show in Chicago. So Trent personally made a video for her to watch their performance from 04-11-1990 at the Ritz in New York City! NIN filmed this from the balcony, center. It’s good footage with the camera’s audio. He also included in the video the long music video of Down In It. Pretty cool to see that he made this for a fan. I have tried contacting nicolasflyer (to where this video came from) on YouTube, but I have never heard back. I would love to have this video from the raw source. But at the moment, this will be the best version that is available. 

To my knowledge, I have not seen it prior to it being uploaded to YouTube.
