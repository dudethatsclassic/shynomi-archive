Notes:
This is an unknown rip of this Silver CD bootleg. The source of this audio was ripped from mdvidguy's video recording. This was released back in the 90's and nothing better than a 3rd gen copy was available back then. So more than likely the source on this silver CD was a 3rd gen rip. To fit this onto one CD the bootleg company has omitted "Eraser" through "Suck" and also removed "I Do Not Want This." Decent audio from the video. If you can, grab the video because it is superb for a 94 show.
