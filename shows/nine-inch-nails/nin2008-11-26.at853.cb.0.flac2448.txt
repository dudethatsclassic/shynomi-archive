Notes: 

Incredible show - the best overall I've seen in my 40 years (and I was not a fan before I attended it!) This also marks the only time I've directly confronted a "talker" during a show. This really drunk guy was just babbling on and on to his friend during "Vessel," and I figured saying something to him wouldn't ruin my recording any more than he already was. As you can hear, he suddenly got very angry and theatening. At this point I realized I had made a mistake and could even get thrown out if a fight started.

So I quickly backed down and managed to squeeze my way a few steps ahead in the packed crowd. In my opinion, this incident really did not have a negative impact on my recording. In fact, I think it's pretty funny every time I hear it.

Trent also made a rather humorous speech before "God Given" - very unique from anything else I've heard from this tour.

Recorded from approximately the 20th row of the floor (general admission), directly in line with the left PA stacks. This was my best guess as to the "sweet spot" for this show, and I think I got it right!
