I brought the audio recorder and camcorder to both shows with no real agenda.  I was on the floor on the 28th and did audio with no 
filming except for "Atmosphere".  I started filming because Trent said they were going to play something they had never played before.  
I was in the balcony on the 29th and filmed from the center front row.  My footage will be torrented soon.  
I brought my head phones instead of my microphone to the 29th (dumbest recording mistake ever) but the audio on the camcorder is 
good enough to leave in.  

I was stuck farther back than I would have like for this show and was trapped near a group of frat boys talking loudly and doing shots.  
They seemed louder in person than on the recording, but there is a lot of backgroud chatter at times.
There are a couple minor issues with the recording.  I had to stop it for a few seconds at the start of March of the Pigs in order 
to switch from stereo to mono and there are a couple of drop outs at the start of Letting You.  

Location: right of soundboard
Recorder: Sharp MD SR60  Mic: Sony ECM-DS70P 
Lineage: MD --> Audacity --> WAV (44,100 Hz, 16 bit) --> dBpoweramp (compression level 8) --> FLAC
