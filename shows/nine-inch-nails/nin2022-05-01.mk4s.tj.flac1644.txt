I cannot state how much a great time I've had the last four days with Ryan and Adam from NIN Live and Mark from LP Live. You won't find more kind folks out there. I had an absolute blast and am sad that this leg of shows is already at an end. Let's do it again soon!

The last show of the southeast leg of shows and Nine Inch Nails did not disappoint. A great mix of bangers and rarities. Was nice to see some newer songs played as well. The band seemed in fine form and the light show was one again insane. Cannot wait to see them again, debating trying to swing Philly. Also filmed in 4K.
