Nine Inch Nails
July 1st, 2006.
first midwest bank amphitheatre Tinley Park, IL

Location: ga pit
Equipment: Sony Cybershot DSC-P100
Filmer: berlamont (www.berlamont.com)


Notes: I held the camera above my head most of the time, it can be shakey and a little off center because of that. 
I tried my best to hold it as steady and on center as I could. This is probably my last nin show for a while so I was trying to make it good.
At the end of down in it a bastard security gaurd came and told me to put my camera away (you can actually hear him telling me it heh) 
After he got a little ways away I brought it right back out, but right at the start of hlah he came back and I had to put it away again.
I was going to bring it right back out but he kept hovering around my area thinking he was going to get to take my camera. 
So all in all I got most of the show if you don't like it don't bother bitching, I don't care. 
I almost got my camera taken to get this, and my arms were on fire holding the damn thing for so long.
You can thank the hairy backed ogre for blocking the view quite a bit by jumping in the way constantly, but other than that this is the best recording I have done, so ENJOY!
