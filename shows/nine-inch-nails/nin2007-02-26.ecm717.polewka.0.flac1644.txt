Notes:
First released April 2014. This recording has been uncirculated for almost 7 years. I want to thank Polewka for taking the time to send a few of his master tapes to me to be transferred! He has made hundreds of fantastic tapes and I am very grateful that he let me transfer a few of them! This recording is very good, sadly this is another one of his recordings where the 2nd minidisc failed during the write. So the last quarter of the concert was not recorded. 

There was also some discrepancy between placements of a few songs in another tape from this show. This is the correct run through of the concert, which is the exact same as the track listing from the first source.
