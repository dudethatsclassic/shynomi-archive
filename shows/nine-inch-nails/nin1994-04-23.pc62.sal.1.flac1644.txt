Notes: 
Straight WAV transfer; no EQ. Big thanks to Alex K for the transferring his copy of this tape. To my knowledge, this is the first time this recording has circulated. A good recording and better than the other recording from this night. "Suck" is cut, as I imagine where the tape flip happened. 

This is also the same night that Chris Vrenna was hit in the head with a mic stand by Trent. This happened during "Sin."
