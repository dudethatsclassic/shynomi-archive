Notes: Last show of the 3 day stint of NIN. Best weekend ever. The first show I did not sit with my brother which sucked and unfortunately he did not see "Now I'm Nothing" into "Terrible Lie" which I still feel really bad about cause he was waiting to get in with a friend. The show... as always... WAS EXCELLENT! First off to hear Now I'm Nothing after almost 20 years of the song not being played is beasty!!! It sound awesome live and into Terrible Lie it just is awesome. I finally got to hear Sin live which just made my night!!!!! The set was shorter than usual and this will fit onto 1 CD. Many people were mad about the setlist but it was a very solid setlist. Nice to see Lights in the Sky on the tour. This show is dedicated to my brother. The only person who has been to every NIN show with me since the beginning. btw, this is my best recording ever. I knew why I got the DPAs and it was because they are really good mics, this seriously could put some competition on some soundboards out there. I was really pleased with how it came out, plus being right in front of the soundboard really helped. I also taped with my camera once again and it will be up here soon. Enjoy the tapes. 

Ryan
