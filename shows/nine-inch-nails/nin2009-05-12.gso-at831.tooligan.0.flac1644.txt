Notes:

This is hands down the BEST NIN show I have ever been too. The setlist was fantastic, and they rocked it from 
beginning to end. This matrix was done using my Hi-MD and regular MD recordings from the show. My mics were
not plugged into my batt box for my Hi-MD til the end of "Sin", so that is where the matrix actually begins.
I also filmed video with my CanonPowerShot A580 digital camera (which I may share whenever I can edit it and 
add audio). I did have to change discs on my regular MD, so "Gone, Still" is mostly cut do to this, but I did
my best to smooth it out in the matrix. There are a few drop out during "Not So Pretty Now" and
"The Fragile", not sure if it was mics or MD. I think this matrix turned out quite well in spite of some of the
issues I had recording and the sub par mics with my old rig. Enjoy!
