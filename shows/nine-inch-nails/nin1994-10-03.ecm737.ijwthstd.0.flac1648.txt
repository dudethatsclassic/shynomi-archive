Notes:
This is a different source than the "Slaughter In The Air" Silver CD.This recording was first transferred in September 2014 thanks to BLG. I had never seen it prior to this time. It may have very well been out there, but I did not have it for the archive. A very good recording, nothing less from ijwthstd. So big thanks for him taping the show and BLG getting this transferred.

This is the raw recording of the show. BLG was kind enough to send me over the raw transfer of the recording and the only editing done was to reduce the levels during the beginning of the show when the recording ran a bit hot, then normalize to -0.1 db.
