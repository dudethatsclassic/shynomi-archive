Notes:
GSP was kind enough to share his master with me. He made a few CDRs for me and I have ripped them with his master recordings on them. His recording misses the first two songs of the show while he got setup and I noticed in his tracking that “The Great Destroyer” was split between the beginning and the breakdown. I have fixed that but kept the rip logs from XLD that I did to confirm a good rip. Recording is good! This was one of the best LITS shows I went to on my run of shows and it’s great to have another source!
