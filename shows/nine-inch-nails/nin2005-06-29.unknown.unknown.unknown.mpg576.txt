Notes:
This really is for collectors only and pretty much is just here to preserve that this concert happened. It's not a very good shot. Recorded from the balcony on the right side with pretty much an obstructed view. If they were in the front row, they would have a really good vantage point to the stage. But you just see mostly the back of people's heads. It is very incomplete, only getting the beginning and the end of the show. If anyone has a better recording from this show, please get in contact!
