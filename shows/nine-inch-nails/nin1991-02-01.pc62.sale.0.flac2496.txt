Notes:
Back in 2014, Sal had messaged me on Twitter saying he had taped two SF shows in the early 90s. Took some time to find the tapes and I never followed up until I started going back through my emails during quarantine to follow any leads. Sal kindly obliged to send me the tapes to transfer for the archive!

This recording from 1991 was the same that is on the archive now, but from the master tape. I also transferred the previous one and I will say that my transfer methods have gotten better since then. The tape ran a bit slow, so it was sped up in audition by 4.9%. It sounds absolutely stellar. 

Big thanks to Sal for sending the tapes and allowing me to transfer them for the archive. If you have Nine Inch Nails live recordings that are not available on the archive, I would love to hear from you and help you transfer any sort of media, if need be.
