Notes:
First released April 2014. This recording has been uncirculated for almost 9 years. I want to thank Polewka for taking the time to send a few of his master tapes to me to be transferred! He has made hundreds of fantastic tapes and I am very grateful that he let me transfer a few of them!

This is a great recording and the first source to circulate from this date! The first night was riddled with problems with the Minidiscs. This recording is flawless. While listening through transferring this show, the band sounds like the were on it! "Getting Smaller" has always been a rare song to hear on the tours, and it never disappoints!
