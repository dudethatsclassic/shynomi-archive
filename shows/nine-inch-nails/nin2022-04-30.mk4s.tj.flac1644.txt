First off, another awesome night hanging with Ryan + Adam J. of NIN Live and Mark from LP Live but with my SO this time. Can't wait to do it again tomorrow in Franklin!

This was a wild show. They opened with Mr. Self Destruct and went HARD from there on out. No slow songs and no fucking around. They sounded incredibly tight and it was 90 minutes of chaos onstage. "Heresy" was played for the first time in years and there were a few fuckups of someone playing samples before the song started, most noticeably before "Sin" and "The Perfect Drug". I grabbed the setlist afterwards and have included a picture. One more show to go on this leg of the tour! I also filmed this in 4K.
