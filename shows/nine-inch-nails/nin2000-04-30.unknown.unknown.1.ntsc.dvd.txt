Notes:
MAD contacted me and told me that they had a DVD of the Montreal performance you see here, as it was not on the archive and actually it was the first time I had seen it. He told me that it was sourced from the master copy onto VHS. The filmer was a friend of a friend and they don’t have any more information than what was given at the time. 

You can tell the video is low gen. Very clean and clear! The beginning of the concert is recorded from the right side of the stage and a bit obstructed by the lighting. The filmer then moved after “Terrible Lie” to the rear-center of the venue and the video starts up again in the middle of “Sin.” The audio recording that you see on the archive I can now confirm is from this video. A great video recording and so pumped to have such a great copy on the archive!
