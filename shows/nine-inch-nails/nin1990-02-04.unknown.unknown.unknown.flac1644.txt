Notes:
This is the same recording/source that was initially on the archive. I did the transfer myself and it has definitely helped with the sound. From the tape, it does miss the beginning of "Terrible Lie" and starts at the first chorus. But I don't believe "Head Like a Hole" was played this evening because after "Down In It" PA music comes on. I believe that was the end of their set this night. You can also hear Trent say before "Ringfinger," "Is it quiet in here, or what?" Not sure if they ended early out of frustration, or that was all they were slated for.

Tape has been improved with the highs, and it's less muffled with no EQ/Noise reduction added. I have also corrected the speed of the tape as it ran a bit slow.
