Well, I'm finally getting around to digitizing my 700+ cassettes (at my wife's insistence--she wants them out of the house), so I'm going to start posting some goodies here. I taped many a show in the Toronto area from the late '80's to the late '90's, and also recorded a lot of radio broadcasts, mostly from CFNY, 102.1 in Toronto. Hope you stumble across something you like, or even a show you were at!

Nine Inch Nails--The Forum,London,England, May 26, 1994 soundboard

One thing about wading through boxes of tapes, you sometimes find things that you have no idea where they came from.  Case in point, this NIN tape.  Pretty sure it wasn't a trade, as my handwriting is on the card, and I know damn well I didn't tape it.  Must have just dubbed it off a friend, and it certainly doesn't appear to be CD sourced--"The Only Time" was split over the 2 sides, something I would never do! I joined the parts together, you can probably spot it if you listen hard enough. Anyway, it is a soundboard, Trent's in a pissy mood,and it doesn't seem to be on DIME at the moment, so here it is.  Any more info is appreciated.

Lineage: sbd>unknown gen cassette>M audio sound card>wav>flac.  Track splitting and gap editing with CDWave, track join done with Cool Edit Pro.
