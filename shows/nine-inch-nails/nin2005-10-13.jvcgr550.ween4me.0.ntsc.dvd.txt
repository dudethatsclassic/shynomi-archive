
Nine Inch Nails
Oct. 13, 2005
Veteran's Memorial Coliseum
Madison, WI

Filmed with JVC GR550 miniDV
MiniDV> Sony Vegas> Sony DVD Architect> DVD

Rainy night in Madison. Shot from the back of the arena upper level.  A little shaky but watchable.  The security was tight 
that night.  That is why the Line Begins.. is omitted and the other two songs cut.  During Terrible Lie I saw 3 security
guards come up the stairs towards me. About 3 rows in front of me they stop and bust a couple a people for I think smoking 
inside. But it caused me to hide the camera until they left.
