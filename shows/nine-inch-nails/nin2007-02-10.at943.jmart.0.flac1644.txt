Notes: First show of the '07 tour. I was expecting some new songs from the upcoming album "Year Zero", but no luck. It still was a great show ("Last" debut!). Around the end of Terrible Lie, the lighting board went off (the faces of the dudes at the board looking at each other was priceless!). Turns out it was the fire alarm (TR:"fire alarm turns off the fucking power") and the band kept playing the remaining of Terrible Lie in complete darkness. Trent said "Fuck it" and just kept going to March of Pigs and asked for the house lights to be turned on until the stage light was back. By the middle of MoP, the stage lights went on again and the show kept going normally. Great show, but SO short (can't say i didn't knew what i was coming for)! This was the only Lisbon '07 show i've managed to attend sadly, because i didn't have a place to stay for night 2 and 3. The recording is pretty solid. I ran the levels pretty hot but without exceeding the headroom of the recording. No close crowd noise whatsoever and I was about 10ft above the floor. The venue is from 1889 and by no means recommendable for such a loud show (the walls were pounding to the bass notes!). EQ'ing was applied to reduce lo-freqs and raise mid-freqs, but nothing big. No fades at mid-point of the show for enhanced listening versatility.

-j.mart
