Notes:
Released February 2014, this is the first recording to come from the Barcelona show in 2007. The video is average to below average. The view from which the recording was done is excellent! Just the camera has some trouble with the intensity of lights and frame rate. Recorded from the right side balcony, there is no obstruction of the shot and the stage is in clear view. No close ups, but seeing the full stage is really nice. I enjoyed watching the recording myself! I thank CP for sharing this with the archive. We also have the audio of it ripped even though it is not the best, and at some points it is blown out. Nice to have another show added to the archive!
