This is the first show I've ever tried to record and it shows.  
There are a few scenes where heads get cut because I was trying
to enjoy the show while taping (harder than I thought).  The 
camcorder jumps all over the place in the beginning, but it
does get a little better into the show.  Picture-wise there are a
lot of problems too as the lights were messing with the auto-focus 
and it would take a while for it to clear up (by which time there 
were MORE lights).  The strobe lights also messed with the 
steady-cam causing a lot of shaking in the picture.

I couldn't get the letterbox feature to work on my Mac so it's all
in 16:9.  It'll still play on a 4:3 TV, there is just cropping.


DO NOT SELL!!!
