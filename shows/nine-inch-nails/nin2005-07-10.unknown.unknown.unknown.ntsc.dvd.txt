Notes:
This is an unknown audience video recording that is an unknown generation and transfer. I assume this is from an ETS member back in the day because there is the old ETS message board from their computer screen at the beginning of this recording. Filmed from the floor on the right side, the taper was pretty close! Audio is expected, kind of blown out. Some good close ups and they were right in the pit for some of these songs! Pinion and Wish were not recorded, but every other song either was recorded in full or has snippets from it.
