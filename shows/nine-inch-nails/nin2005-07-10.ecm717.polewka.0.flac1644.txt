Notes:
First released April 2014. This recording has been uncirculated for almost 9 years. I want to thank Polewka for taking the time to send a few of his master tapes to me to be transferred! He has made hundreds of fantastic tapes and I am very grateful that he let me transfer a few of them!

This is a great recording and the first source to circulate from this date! Sadly, the taper had some problems with his minidiscs that night. There are a few dropouts during the recording. These are less than half a second, but there will be a skip during "Wish," "Sin," "The Hand That Feeds," and "Terrible Lie." Taper's 2nd MD disc was corrupt and only got a bit of "Love is Not Enough," and "Suck." 

If you have any past recordings of Nine Inch Nails you would like to add to the live archive at http://www.reflectinginthechrome.com, or are taping any concerts in the future please get in contact with me at ryanj at reflectinginthechrome dot com!
