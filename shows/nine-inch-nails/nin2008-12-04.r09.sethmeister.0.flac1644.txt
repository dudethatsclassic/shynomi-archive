I had just seen NIN for my first time a few months prior (2008-07-25) during the 1st Pemberton Music Festival and was very impressed. I was excited to be seeing them again but a large part of that excitement was completely destroyed the moment the supporting band took the stage. The Bug was by far the absolutely worst band I had ever seen before or since. It was a miracle that my recorder didn't break taping them and my ears didn't start bleeding. To this day, I still don't know what someone was thinking pairing up this disaster of a "band" with NIN. May I never see them again.

Surprisingly, I found that NIN's performance was a little lacklustre this evening. Almost as if they were tired. There was a lot of dynamic range and sadly, but as usual for Kelowna, the amount of respect given towards the band was minimal. 

The venue itself is never great sounding and tonight was no different. Somehow I had forgotten a cable so was forced to use my digicorder's internals. I still managed a pretty good recording and got lucky with not having too many talkers around me. 

All in all, this was a good show and worth the listen. Enjoy.
