Notes:
zman is an absolute legend in the taping community. Tapes everything and seems like he is at every show. Just pulled this out of his backlog of tapes. Glad to add this to the archive. Tracksplits were a bit off because he was not familiar with NIN. No problem, I was able to merge everything together and re-track it to it's proper positions. As zman said, "Toledo Sports Arena is notorious for terrible sound." He isn't wrong, but this tape is bad either. Just really hard to get a good tape in this place. 

This was my first show of the With Teeth tour and it blew me away. Life altering for my brother and I, a reason I started the archive and a need to see this band over and over. Thank you zman for adding another source to this legendary show. Getting Smaller was a highlight of this show.
