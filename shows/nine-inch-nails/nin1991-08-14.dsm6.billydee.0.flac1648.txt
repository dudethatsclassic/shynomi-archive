Notes: 
Released in April 2014. This show has been uncirculated for almost 23 years! I want to thank billydee for recording the show and sending this tape to ellaguru to be transferred! This tape was not playing properly on a few decks so I took it to be transferred and tried my decks that I have. It wouldn't play well on my DA-20 but it played perfect on my trusty D8! 

In terms of sound quality. This tape is fantastic! For an early NIN show, especially during the Lollapalooza shows, you are not going to find a better tape. There is some brick walling in a few spots, but that should not deter you from grabbing this show. It is arguably the best sounding NIN Lolla show that is available! Enjoy the recording!
