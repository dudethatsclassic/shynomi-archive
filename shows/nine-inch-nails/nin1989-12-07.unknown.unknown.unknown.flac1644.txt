The complete show is available in mp3, but not FLAC so far (Jan 08).  This rip has slightly better sound quality.   

In late 1989, Nine Inch Nails went on a brief club tour to promote Pretty Hate Machine.
Only two recordings from this tour have surfaced.  This is the first of those shows.
This show was initially circulated on cassette and a few tape speed problems are present, 
especially during "Kinda I Want to" which had the beginning cut.  This tour was the only time 
"Kinda I Want to" was ever performed live.
The other recording from this tour is a video source of the December 9, 1989 show at The Pipeline 
in Newark and has better and more complete audio.
