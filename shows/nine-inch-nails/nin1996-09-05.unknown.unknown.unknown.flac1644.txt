Animal and Tough are by Kevin McMahon`s band PRICK, played somewhat more aggressively by this NIN lineup.
R.S.V.P. features Clint Mansell of Pop Will Eat Itself. 

Also played ( but not on this recording were:
Wise Up Sucker ( featuring Clint Mansell)
Head Like A Hole ( Featuring Filter`s Richard Patrick )
Something I Can Never Have.

This was one of the three showcase gigs NIN did in 1996 to promote Nothing Records.

It`s an audience recording, somewhat distorted but an interesting show to add to your collection due to the
line up of the band on these shows and the special guests. I don`t know of the lineage, I got this in a trade a while ago
on cassette, transfered it to my PC, cleaned it up a little in Wavelab, and converted to FLAC using Traders Little Helper.
