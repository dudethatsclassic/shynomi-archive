Notes: 
Very interesting how we obtained this. Some guy came up to a group of tapers at Bonnaroo and said that he got this ALD for his wife and thought we could use it to tape. So of course we took it. I raced back to town to get a cable to run into my D8 for the show and came back to tape with it at 1 AM. Set the ALD at the beginning of the show and let it roll! Unfortunately I did not think that NIN would play over 2 hours, the tape ends at The Day The World Went Away and I miss that whole song and Hurt. But you get the rest of the recording in all of its glory. The only thing about ALD's is its a hit or miss. I think this is pretty good, the right channel was hardly there so when I boosted the channel there was a lot of noise from the noise floor of the recorder. So I reduced it in post-prod. Not bad. I like this recording a lot. Enjoy.

Ryan
