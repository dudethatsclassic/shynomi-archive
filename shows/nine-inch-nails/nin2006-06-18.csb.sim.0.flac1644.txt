Notes:
Sim was kind enough to let me borrow his master tape and transfer it for the NIN drive. I cannot thank him enough for doing this. His recording has been uncirculated up until Feb. 2011. This is the only source to ever surface from the SPAC show. He told me that he might have had some dropouts in the recording as his M1 was going bad. When I transferred the recording I listened to the whole thing as it was being transferred in real-time. No dropouts. I felt like this tour had the best energy out of any of them since 2005. This show is no exception. Great recording too, very clear and crisp. Please comment and give your thanks to Sim. Enjoy.
