Nine Inch Nails
Lights In The Sky Over South America Tour
Arena Movistar - Santiago, Chile
October 4, 2008
Opening Act: Vigilante

NINLIVE Notes:
This was given to me by ETS user fillow. Big thanks. I have not seen this and I am not sure if there is a lossless version out there, but I would like one! This is the complete broadcast (with a lot of commentary from the radio hosts inbetween and in some of the songs) but some songs were not broadcasted. The list of songs not in the broadcast were 999,999, 1,000,000, Piggy, Head Down, Corona Radiata/The Greater Good, Pinion, The Big Come Down, Ghosts 31, Only, THTF,  and God Given. About 15 seconds into "Letting You" is the FM broadcast spliced in from an audience source. Definitely searching for a lossless quality recording. If anyone knows the radio station, that would be great too!



————————

* This show was the first NIN show in Chile since the cancelation of the 2005 south american tour date. 

* During March Of The Pigs, Trent Reznor threw his microphone into the crowd, letting a fan sing the "and everything is alright" line. Trent replied "Thank you". 

* Before they played Closer, Josh Freese broke his kick drum, causing a short pause. Trent ask to the crowd wait one second for that. 

* Trent injured his right elbow during Only when he punched the front screen several times. 

* The front screen showed a black space during Only and for some songs during the rest of the show. 

* Down In It was on the setlist but was not played. 

* Before they played God Given, Trent spoke to the audience: 

Thank you! Thank you very much! We finally made it here. It's good to play for you, thank you for
coming on. Appreciated. I promise we'll be back. It wouldn't take 18 years. Besides, I'll be dead 
by then, so we'll be back soon, I promise. Thank you. This is a song from Year Zero. I wrote this 
because the country that I've been has lost his fucking mind. I personally apologize for our 
fucking president. He's a cunt. Anyway... here we go.
