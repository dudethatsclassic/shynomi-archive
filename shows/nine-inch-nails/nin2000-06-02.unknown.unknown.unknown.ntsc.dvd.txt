Notes:
Good audience recording from the center rear of the venue. The first 90 seconds of the DVD only has the audio with a frozen picture. The picture quality is great, but the audio has some issues with a lot of hiss in the right channel. I am not sure but to suspect this was not a great transfer from a VHS tape. Would love a proper copy of this one. It really is a great audience video recording. 

In a few of the shots, you can see a huge mic over the soundboard. I suspect this is one of the dates that they recorded the audio for the ‘And All That Could Have Been’ video release.
