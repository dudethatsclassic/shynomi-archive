2009-05-12
Austin, TX
Frank Erwin Center

Video: 1440x1080 h.264 .mov
Audio: 44.1kHz stereo PCM dubbed from tooligan (tooligan.infinitehosting.net)
Taper: OldManPolk

http://ninlive.com/shows/2009/20090512.html

Video was shot from the pit on the right hand side and about four rows back from the rail. 1440x1080 h.264 video shot with an Aiptek ActionHD. I only took about three seconds worth of audio off the camera's source and blended it in with an excellent recording supplied by tooligan (http://tooligan.infinitehosting.net). Thanks go out to him for letting me use this audio source.

The video is a bit shaky and out of focus in a few spots, but that's mostly during the first few songs as I had a couple of jump-and-pumpers right in front of me before they shuffled up closer to the front. A good example of the quality, minus the audio which was dubbed since, can be found at http://vimeo.com/4658275.
