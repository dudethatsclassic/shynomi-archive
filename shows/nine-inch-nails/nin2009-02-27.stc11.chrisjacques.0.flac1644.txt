Nine Inch Nails
Soundwave Festival
Melbourne Showgrounds
Melbourne, Australia
February 27th, 2009


Recorded by Chris Jacques (SeanceMascara)
http://db.etree.org/SeanceMascara
http://fanmaderecordings.blogspot.com/



Recorded with:
Church Audio STC-11 > Church Audio ST-9100 w/Bass Rolloff at 80hz > iRiver iHP-120 w/Rockbox installed

Transfer:
iRiver iHP-120 > Sony Sound Forge 8 (Raised Volume +15dB, Fade In/Out, Splits) > FLAC Frontend (Level 8) > FLAC


Recording Notes:
Show was recorded from the fence on the left side of the soundboard. I was standing on a little concrete pillar to get an
extra bit of clearance over the crowd.
Microphones were clipped to the brim of a hat about 6ft from the ground and spaced 6 inches apart.

I leave during Survivalism to go see another band. I let the recording run through and faded it out once NIN became inaudable.
