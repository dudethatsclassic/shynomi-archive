Tonic and Better Than Ezra were announced as the live entertainment for
2025's Red, Rhythm and Boom Festival in Mason, Ohio. Tonic took the stage
sharply at 6pm and ended just shy of 730pm. BTE were scheduled for 830pm
thru 10pm w/ fireworks following. This was one of the hottest shows I have
been to. It was in the mid 90s, but felt like it was well over 100 in the
sun. We had no shade and it was sweltering! Thankfully, I drank quite a
bit of water before Tonic's set & didnt deal with any heat stroke standing
still on the lawn taping. BTE was a bit easier as the sun started to
go down when they came on. The crowd just about doubled by the time BTE
took the stage, so there is more crowd noise on this than on Tonic. I did
spend some time in RX7 removing screams, yelps and even an air horn some
kids were messing with behind me. Thankfully, this only occurs for a few
minutes during the beginning of "Live A Little". The show was good, but
Kevin Griffin needs to learn how to wrap it up between songs. He goes on
for way too long before introducing songs and it kind of kills the show's
vibe. Not to mention, whats up with doing 9 covers and ending the set with
a cover medley? I would much rather hear their OG's first w/ 1 or 2 covers,
but to each their own. Ending with "Desperately Wanting" or "In The Blood"
(a song they didn't even play) would have been awesome! Last I checked,
BTE have 9 studio albums. Please focus on those!!!

Weird show, but great recording. Enjoy!
