Our taper was seated at the side of the stage in the VIP section, which is why at the beginning of the show someone mentions being "up on stage," and the audio is a combination of stage monitors and house sound.  He does not recall how he wound up there with a general admission ticket (scan included here).  But, he happened to sit next to Shannon Hoon's parents at the taping of Saturday Night Live about a month earlier, and maybe that's got something to do with it?

This recording contains an additional song compared to the bootleg CD versions that circulate, which end with Candy Says.  The tape flip before No Rain has been patched with audio from one of those bootlegs.
