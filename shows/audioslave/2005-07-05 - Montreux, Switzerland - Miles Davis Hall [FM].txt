**Remastered** by thir13en


Audioslave
2005-07-05
Montreux, Switzerland


Much thanks to Obertu for this. Got it a long time ago, finally did it up.

As far as the sound, the high end is slightly strange to me, but in scoping it I found nothing out of the ordinary, and an extremely solid 19k. When I encoded it, it was hard to get it to go lower than 70% which is a serious high-end signifier. I have heard similar effects from Swiss radio, most likely a high-end digital broadcast.

Otherwise, great show, lots of variety in the setlist showcasing some of the pre-cursor songs of AS members like Spoonman, and Bulls On Parade...
