This is a recording of Audioslave's Radio Takeover broadcast on 12/7/03 across the US.  I don't know if this was seeded here before, but I thought I'd share this show that I taped off of KXXR 93.7, 93X.  Hosted by Tenacious D's Kyle Gass, the show consisted of live performances by Audioslave with a couple calls from listeners across the nation.  All of the callers have been edited out as well as most of the talking.  

The setlist is as follows:
1 Gasoline
2 Super Stupid
3 I Am the Highway
4 (What's So Funny 'Bout) Peace, Love and Understanding? (acoustic)
5 What You Are
6 Shadow on the Sun 
7 Like a Stone (acoustic)
8 Show Me How to Live
9 Cochise
42:02
This was a great show and is highly recommended for Audioslave fans.  The sound quality is an A+.
