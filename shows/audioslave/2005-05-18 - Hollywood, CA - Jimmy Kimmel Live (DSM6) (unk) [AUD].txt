Free concert on Hollywood Blvd for "Jimmy Kimmel Live." I showed up early and recorded the soundcheck though the cops kept people moving so I walked up and down the sidewalk on Hollywood Blvd. Later on we were let into cattle pens in the middle of the street for the show. A few songs were broadcast but they played a longer set. During "Killing In The Name," some of the barricades came down and some fans rushed toward the stage.
