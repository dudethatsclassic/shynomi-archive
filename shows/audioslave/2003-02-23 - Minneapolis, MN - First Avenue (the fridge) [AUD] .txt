Audioslave
February 23, 2003
First AVenue
Minneapolis, MN

Taper:  "The Fridge"

Location:  10 feet to the right of the soundboard

Source: Coresound High-end Cartoids > Bass Roll-off Box > Sony D100 > Master 48 khz DAT

Transfer: Master DAT > Standalone burner > Master CDRs > EAC > Cooledit Pro (normalized levels)
> CDWave (tracking) > FLAC Level 8

Yet another unreleased show from Mr. Fridge's archives.

I orginally bought tickets for this show but ended up selling them since
I couldn't afford to go at the time.  Well, low and behold a week before the show
a stroke of luck hit me and I found some $ to go.

This was one of the coldest days I can remember being in line for a show.
I waited outside for about 2 hours in about 5 degree weather all the while
hiding my gear in the strategic places we all tapers utilize. 

The show itself is excellent and the band are clearly having a good time
which is something that obviously didn't happen as much as their tours went on.
I was afraid the crowd noise would be more of a factor but it turned out
to be one of my cleaner tapes.

I still like Soundgarden much better. ;-)

Enjoy!

Do NOT distribute as mp3!
