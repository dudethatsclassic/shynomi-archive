Notes
-----
This was a labour of love I can tell you.  A broken cable meant the IEM source was obliterated with static bursts.  Coupled with radio interference and mobile phone noise, it was completely unusable.  With the sad passing of Chris Cornell, I decided to look at it again and see if Adobe’s sound remover tool would be of any use.  Whatever magic spell those people at Adobe are using, it worked amazingly well and I was able to restore the IEM to almost a perfect source.  This matrix was made in Adobe Audition CC 2017 and used multiple Waves and Izotope plugins.  Enjoy!
