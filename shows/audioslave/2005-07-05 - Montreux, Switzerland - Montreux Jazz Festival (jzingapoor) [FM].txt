Audioslave
Montreux Jazz Festival
Miles Davis Hall, Montreux
July 5, 2005

COMMENT:
just came back from my trip to the montreux jazz festival, from the fantastic concert that audioslave gave last night, and YIPPEE!! my favorite live radio station, DRS 3 of Switzerland, already broadcasts this gem!
man, i've seen a lot of good live bands, but this was one hell of a rock show! the band was really surprised to find themselves playing on a normal tuesday night in front a crowd that rocked as there would be no tomorrow! whenever morello finished a solo, the listeners gave standing ovations like they were at a jazz concert (well, it's called Jazz Festival after all...) and cornell was accompanied by the giant audience choir during the accoustic Black Hole Sun. i think the band liked their playing there ;-)

hope to see soon the video of this fantastic gig! Soul Power to everybody!
