This is a pretty decent audience recording from the 2003 tour. It's somewhat muffled so I can't give it a higher rating. I'm sure a little bit of EQ could help this tremendously but I am leaving it as is.
Im not sure how it translated in the lineage but I had original FLAC files that I used for this. The above lineage does not match anything else in my list so I believe that I may had found the taper from the original SHN source and he sent me the FLAC files from his original CD set. Not certain on that but I recall something like that happened. Either way, this is a good one to have.
