Not necessarily my usual type of band, but kind of an interesting story.  I was at the venue to see the headliner, The Lemonheads.  But before they go on, this random pop-rock act from Orlando is on.  Orlando? Who the hell WERE these guys, anyway? Rob Thomas was being kind of a tool with the whole "DC baybay" thing he kept saying. I don't know what the deal was.  Maybe he was nervous.

But anyway, you know the rest of the story with this band.  Say what you will, but wow, these guys dominated the airwaves for the entire late 90s.  I think almost every song on this set was a radio single.  But here they were, still the opening act for the Lemonheads, playing their feel-good rock music to a crowd that mostly didn't know them at all. Little did those people know--if they owned an FM radio at least--how well they would come to know them.

Sound on this one is good - I was very close to the stack, and it sounds like it.  Obviously it has the usual weirdness of a stack tape (not much "stereo image" among other things) but who really cares when the tape is this clean? 

Thanks to Mike Ziegler for his herculean effort in transferring my masters.   These would not be out here but for his efforts.  ENJOY!
