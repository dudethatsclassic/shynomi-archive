Matchbox 20 - 6/29/97

This comes from my personal collection which is now the Matchbox 20 Recording Database. I picked this out to share to everyone else since is one of the top 5 recordings for '97 and hopefully this may help unearth any other MB20 recordings out there I dont have. So if you got something, let me know or send me a PM.
This rare tape was given to me from an retired collector which was transfered by me. The show comes from a fairly low gen tape as the hiss is quite minimal which is fortunate because the sound quality is pretty good. It just barely reaches and EX- rating (more like a B+ or VG+) but considering the age of the tape, I think its worth it. Although it is an audience recording and theres some crowd noise(mostly screaming girls) but mainly between songs. The sound comes off a bit "compressed" but overall everything is clear and loud. Certianly worth having in the collection, unfortunately the last song is cut (seems to be a common issue with these 97 shows) and only about one minutes worth is there before its over.
