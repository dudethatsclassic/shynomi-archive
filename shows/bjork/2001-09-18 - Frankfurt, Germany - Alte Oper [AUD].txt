*this recording was received via 2 CDR's directly from the person who recorded the show. The limited recording equipment information is all that the recorder could provide.
**The first CDR was extracted using EAC with no errors. The second disc would not extract using EAC, so Musicmatch was used. It became apparent when editing why the disc would not extract--there were 3 gaps in the second set, fortunately all during crowd noise. I used Soundforge7 to edit these gaps, and create a seemless recording. Soundforge pencil tool was used to smooth two bumps by the recorder into the mics during quiet moments. Fades were added to the beginning and end of each set. No normalization was necessary.
--danlynch 2004-06-16
