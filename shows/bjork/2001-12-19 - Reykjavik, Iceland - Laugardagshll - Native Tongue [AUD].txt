flac file and sound editing by danlynch 2004-05-25:
This was a very difficult wav file to edit via Soundforge.  The recording 
levels were at first quite low, so I normalized before any editing was done.  
Additionally, the crowd noise (loud clapping next to the recorder) in between 
songs was quite loud compared with the most of the music, so I manually 
lowered the volume of the crowd as best as possible.  Some minor artifacts 
remain from this process.  After the completion of the crowd noise volume 
edit, I normalized again.   Fades were done at the beginning and end of 
each set.
