This is most certainly not the FM broadcast of the Berlin 2003 performance,
but rather a soundboard recording with significantly greater frequencies than
the broadast version.  Frequency Analyis diagrams are included in this folder
to confirm.  This recording does not include the announcer ("Ansage"), but 
also does not include Generous Palmstroke, so that the most likely source
of this recording is from a pre-broadcast source.  Either way, its most certainly
an upgrade over the FM version!
