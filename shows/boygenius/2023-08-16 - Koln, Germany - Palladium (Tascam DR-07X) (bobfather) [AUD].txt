Source: Tascam DR-07X -> internal mics in x-position -> SD card
Lineage: SD Card -> raised the volume in izotope RX ->tracked in audio logic -> xact ->flac

* A Bobfather recording *

Notes: Impressive sold out show. Think I won`t see them again in such a "small" place (capacity 1500 pax). 
The crowd was 80% (or more) girls and women, knew the lyrics, we`re kind to each other.
I was scared about the recording when I heard the audience screaming and sing along during the support act (Muna). 
Standing 2nd row on the balcony I decided to lay down my recorder on the floor beneath the railing to make sure that I don`t capture too much singing of the girls around me. It turned out to be a very good idea.
The recording is well balanced. No disturbing noise. Almost excellent.
The band was in a great mood. Phoebes birthday was the next day, but the band decided to celebrate it on this evening incl. birthday-cake-smashing.
These three voices together just sound like angels singing. They know what they`re doing. If you want to know something about harmonies...listen closely.
