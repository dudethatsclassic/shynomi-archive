Notes:
Energetic show by Boygenius on the final night of the Re:SET festival. Their album is one of my favorites of the year so far.
Credit where credit is due - the crowd was attentive throughout with minimal talkers even during the quiet songs. 
There were multiple false starts in the final song thanks to a buzzing amp and apparently a laughing security guard.
Encore was not taped (had to work early the next day and wanted to get out before the crowd crush), but otherwise
the recording came out very well. mp3 sample in comments.
