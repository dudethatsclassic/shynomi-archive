Willie Nelson 1975-02-26  Panther Hall  Fort Worth Texas (T-476)(sbd)

I got the reel to reel tapes recorded at 7 1/2 ips for this CD-r release in trade from a DJ at a radio station in Arlington Texas.  I got them about 1978.  The DJ would let me go through their tape library and borrow tapes to make copies.  This show should not be confused with the 1966 show at Panther Hall which was an official release.
