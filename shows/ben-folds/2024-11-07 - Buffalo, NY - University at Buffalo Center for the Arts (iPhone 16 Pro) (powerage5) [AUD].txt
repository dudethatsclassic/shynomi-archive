This show was a lot of fun - my first time seeing Ben Folds, and I absolutely loved the paper airplane concept! Recording this show was a complete afterthought once I got to the venue and figured I could easily do it with my phone's internal mic. I think it sounds quite good considering - the crowd noise in between songs sounds a little harsh, but the music is quite clear. There's the occasional crowd noise, particularly in the second set. It was quite hard to scrub this or do much to lessen it with this show being Ben solo at a piano. But a very listenable recording all in all I think.

As always, don’t sell or convert to lossy.
