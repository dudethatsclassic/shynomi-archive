Notes
------
A show from Ben's Oz tour of 2005. He'd just played in a big theatre venue the night before, 
so it was fun to be part of this small club show. Many songs were fresh from the release "Songs For Silverman"
This show was seeded by another taper (whatboutbob) in 2005 at The Traders Den. This is NOT that version, 
and I have no idea which version sounds better. I'm happy-ish with the results here, but the levels 
do flucuate at times, and it's a bit bass heavy. I've left the EQ work up to your and your fingers....


Recorded, Edited and Upped by Franger. Enjoy!
