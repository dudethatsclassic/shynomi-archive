Notes:
Long time trader, first time taper.

I realized that we were sitting first row balcony, close to center, and with great line of sight to the speakers. So I hung my phone over the edge of the balcony and gave it a shot. Except the occasion sound of people hitting the balcony (which could probably be edited by someone who knows what they are doing), I think it sounds pretty good. The hall in general sounded amazing, so I think it translates to the recording.

This is in appreciation for all of the tapers, their efforts, and experience that will never have.
