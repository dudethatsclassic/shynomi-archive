Notes:

# Danny solo.

Also on the bill were Killing Joke [not recorded].

Due to my work schedule the closer dates on this tour (Cleveland and Cincy) were not
going to be possible to attend. The next date I had free was Pittsburgh. I purchased 
a ticket during the presale and scored a decent single in line with a side stack. A bit 
cheaper and I knew there would be decent sound having taped in this section
before. I actually would have liked to have been 10 seats to my right, but I did pretty
well using memory from a show I attended in 2014. Regarding the crowd, everyone around
me behaved for the most part except for this T.J. Miller lookalike mf'er to my left. 
When I sat down in my seat (20 minutes before set time), he put his arm around me and
wanted to be my friend. He was already wasted and it was only 825. He wanted to talk
Bengals Browns and I did my best to ignore him. When he noticed my clipping the mics
he turned and said to me (and I quote), "Dude, I AINT NO SNITCH, you DO what you NEED 
TO DO." Then, I decided to politely ask him if he could do his best to keep quiet during 
the show and there would be a free copy in it for him. He was so gone by this point,
I'm positive he forgot. Well, him and his buddy got into a screaming match during
"Parabol", so I did my best to move to the right (while leaning forward) literally over
the row in front of me and you cannot really hear them on the recording. If you put 
headphones on and listen closely, you might. But the cardioids did a great job, as 
always. I absolutely love the way these things sound in arenas and theaters. Its hard
to go with another pair. Well, until I can afford a pair of Schoeps MK4's - that is.
The show was great, loved getting to hear "Pneuma" and "Invincible" was a lot more fun
knowing it much better than I did in May. Please share this with as many Tool fans
as possible. Im looking at you, Tool Drive Project.

"this is for the ketchup..." - MJK
