Tool - Live in Berlin 5-21-2002   
4 Years ago I've been to this concert and since then I was desperatly looking for the Bootleg of this show. 
Some weeks ago I finally got it and now I want to share it here. 
The show took place in a relatively small club (Columbiahalle, approx. 3000 people) by a time when Tool already filled 
the Madison Square Garden in New York and the Setlist was nothing but adorable. 
Imagine mighty Danny Carey walking on stage, kicking you a hell of a drumsolo right into your face, 
breaking into the totally unexpected performance of "Ticks'n'Leeches"!!!!... as the opener!!!!...
and the crowd went totally nuts, as you can hear by Maynards comment after "The Grudge". 

The full set was:

Intro
Ticks & Leeches 
The Grudge
