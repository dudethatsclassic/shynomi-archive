I was in Amsterdam at the time of this concert.  I was able to lend some gear to a fan who wanted a recording of the show.  This was their first time recording a concert.  It turned out really good, however they were heavily intoxicated and there are some moments where they get a little over excited about their experience.  They were row 13 on the floor, in line with the right stacks.  

Maynard references the Trailer Park Boys during Opiate and speaks a few times in regards to politics.  Danny Carey's drum & synth solo is a highlight.  The Pot is tuned to C instead of D which is how they've done it in more recent years.  

The Crystal Method was the support act, and they also did a club show at Venue later the same night.
