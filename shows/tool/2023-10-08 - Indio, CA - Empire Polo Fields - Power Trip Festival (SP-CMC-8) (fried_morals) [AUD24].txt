NOTES:

"...and you all smell delicious!"

Finally, after God knows how many years, I can finally say I got to see Tool live! I mean I suppose I did see about 3 minutes worth (from the far back) of their Coachella 2006 set, but I really don't count that. And speaking of Coachella, it was almost 24 years to the day (give or take a couple of days) that they first headlined the inaugural Coachella festival back in 1999. Who would have thought 24 years later they'd be back at the same field, playing its sister festival, "Power Trip"? Strange world.

I recorded this set from the front of the GA rail (which was about 100 yards back from the stage maybe?) where I had been camped out since before 4pm. I thought the sound was great where I was at, and I had a decent view of the stage from where I was at (best you could get in GA practically). They of course didn't allow their set to have a bunch of video cameras on it, so the giant video screens on either side of us just showed a static shot of the stage. From where I was, you could barely make out Maynard's silhouette, but it was fine as I thought the visuals on that giant ass stage, plus all the lasers, looked really cool from far back. And again, the sound was top notch (imho).

Pretty sure this was the same exact setlist they played at the Aftershock festival a few days prior, except swap places with Rosetta Stoned and Pushit? So nothing groundbreaking of course, but it was still great to finally hear a few of these tracks live and in full force after all these years, including of course "Stinkfist", "Ænema" and "Swamp Song". What a great trio for an ending. Just wow.
