Notes: Transfered from VHS on 04-15-12
Here is one for the collectors. This is the third to last night of the first leg of the US, Europe And South Pacific Tour. The taper is located way up in the right hand side of the stands. 
Not much to see here. The camera is always going in and out  from black to a partial stage shot. Don't know what happened here. Maybe paranoid about getting caught? Audio is pretty good 
on this. There is some crowd chatter and yelling but that is expected. The tour is fairly young and still playing with a lot of energy. After Eulogy it abruptly cuts and that is it. Would of been nice to at least 
have the whole show. This was a damn good setlist they played this night. One thing I will bring up is that the review that Galen made is different that what I see here. You be the judge. Also he states 
that it is cut after Reflection and the length is 49:37. This show is cut after Eulogy and is 49:33. I think they are the same show. I could not verify the generation of the VHS but looks like a low gen to me.
