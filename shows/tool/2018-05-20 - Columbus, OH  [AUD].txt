Notes:

I brought my rig not knowing if I was going to record or not. Taping at ROTR is such
a crapshoot and its usually not worth the effort. I camped out at my favorite spot
all afternoon/evening (on the right side rail of the soundcage) towards the front
corner. This way, I can avoid (most) crowdsurfers and the sound is quite decent.
Unfortunately, during the first few songs I did have some talkers to my right, who
seemed more concerned with eachother than what was going on up on the stage. They
ended up leaving shortly thereafter, but listening back they are pretty annoying.
Who would rather talk than take in "Parabola"? Idiots. Tool opted to shut down all
video (no webstream/no jumbotron), so unless you were close-ish, you couldnt really
see the band. From mid-field, I could just make them out. But I felt bad for the
people behind me. Keep in mind, this is a very well attended 40,000+ strong festival
held in a soccer stadium. No big screens kinda sucks. Every other band used them.
Tool also refused the side-stage VIP ticket holders access to the side stage platform
they paid for. Those fans were shuffled into the soundcage area (to my left) behind
a fence. So they paid an exorbitant amount of money essentially to be told "sorry
you are going to have the same view as everyone else." Yikes! Im starting to see why
some people have issues with Mr. Keenan.

But one things for sure, the guy and his band put on one hell of a set. Problems
aside, Im glad I taped. Considering where this was recorded and the circumstances,
Im very pleased. It beats the last time I attempted to tape Tool (Detroit 2001) and
my gear got taken at the door. Hope you guys enjoy this one and please adjust your
home stereo accordingly, it can be a bit bass heavy at times.
