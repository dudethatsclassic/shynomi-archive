Notes:

Ozzfest 98 was a crazy time in music...I was 17 at the time and remember seeing some of the side stage bands (System of a Down, Incubus...) which obviously went on to create huge waves in music. The main stage showcased bands like Sevendust, Soulfly, Megadeth, Limp Bizkit, Coal Chamber, Deftones (not at this particular show) and of course Ozzy. 

This was a HOT day and getting the recorder in was super sketch. They waved a wand over my somewhat bulky recorder that was hidden in under a belt I was wearing and it for some reason didn't trigger a beep...I just remember thinking well this is probably going to get confiscated, so I'm taking the risk. I only brought it so I could record TOOL. They played right after Megadeth as the sun was setting and I was able to capture it from under the canopy about 300 feet back from the stage. For a pretty crappy Realistic recorder, the recording came out pretty decent. Maynard was in rare form as you can hear from the banter in between songs. He was wearing what looked to be a preachers suit and seemed very chatty. Right before Sober you can hear a drunk lady come up and speak into the recording. Kind of humorous but annoying all the same. Tape flip occurred during Pushit, but didn't miss but a few seconds and miraculously was in a quiet part of the song. Hope you all enjoy this!

This is NEW to circulation. Tape was found after years of sitting in boxes for 22 years, so I thought I'd share it. This is NOT the version currently circulating that doesn't have any source info available. Enjoy all!
