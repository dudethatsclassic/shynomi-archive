Notes 2024: Thinking back ~23 years (wow) to this recording… I am still surprised no other sources materialized. I recall that there was at least one other taper but maybe that was hogwash - don't know, never materialized. This particular performance was quite a cool experience for me as I literally walked to the show after class (Michigan State University) after meeting up with some friends. It was unique to see Tool in such a setting at a venue I had seen Rod Stewart, Tori Amos and John Mayer at; yes, Tool put on the best performance out of those artists. Until 2025, this recording had never been released as it just sat as Mini Discs for a couple decades. Spent some time EQ’ing this to give this source a proper introduction to the world of Tool collectors. I hope this will fill in a hole for you and/or a collector. Enjoy. More information at collectiveunconscious.org
