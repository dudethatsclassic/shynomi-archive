Notes:
Ironically, this was my first time seeing Tool live. Every time beforehand I was either not in the area, or had some other commitment. A good friend hooked me up with a ticket, SO BIG THANKS TO THEM! :) We sat absolute dead center about 15 rows back. It was ridiculous. The lights… The sound… Maynard coming out in full riot gear. I’ve been told that Maynard is usually not talkative, but he did say a few things throughout the night. “Great to be home.” The set was solid, and I am glad to have made the show. Traffic is always bad getting to DTE. And we were in our seats with 10 minutes before the went on. Sound was good from where we sat and I think the recording came out pretty well. Enjoy if you grab it.
