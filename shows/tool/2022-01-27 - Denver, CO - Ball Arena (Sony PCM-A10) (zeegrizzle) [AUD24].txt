Notes: Blonde Redhead opened, not taped. Show #8 and third recording ever for a show. Great crowd, great company, amazing seats and view, but there seems to be a pattern with bad sound for this tour. My seat was in the lower bowl one section down from being at Adam’s corner and on an aisle 3 rows up. Not sure what's up with the sound for these shows, but again I had to lower my levels all the way down to 1. Our seats were also pretty close so I'm sure that doesn't help. I just held the recorder in my hand and pointed at the speakers above the stage for the whole show, so no external mics were used, but I really love these stock mics on this recorder. Overall it was a great performance and we had a great night!
