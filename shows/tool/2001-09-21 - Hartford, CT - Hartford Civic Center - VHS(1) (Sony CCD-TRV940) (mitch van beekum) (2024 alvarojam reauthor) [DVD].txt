ORIGINAL NOTES
--------------
According to source gathered here http://www.collectiveunconscious.org/01arena0922.html
the circulating version of this is a DVD taken from a 2nd generation VHS in which
the audio is only in the left channel. This source was transferred by myself from
a VHS tape I obtained from Mitch Van Beekum, who alleged to have gotten it from the
filmer. I transferred a bunch of tapes from Mitch around 2003 and all his sources
appeared to be as he claimed.

The audio still has a wee bit of VHS crackle but it is very full sounding, as would
be if the Sound Professional mics that CU has listed as the source were indeed used.
All this leads me to believe this to be an upgrade over circulating versions.
I probably traded this a few times back in the day so it is not uncirculated.
