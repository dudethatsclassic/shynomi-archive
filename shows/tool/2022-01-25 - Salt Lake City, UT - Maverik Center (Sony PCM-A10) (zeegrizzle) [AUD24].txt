Notes: Blonde Redhead opened, not taped. Show #7 and second recording ever for a show. Great crowd, great company, amazing seats and view, but this venue didn't have the best sound. My seat was in the lower bowl one section down from being at Justin's corner and on an aisle 3 rows up. The overall volume of the sound in this venue was pretty damn loud so I had my levels all the way down to 1. I just held the recorder in my hand and pointed at the speakers above the stage for the whole show, so no external mics were used, but the stock mics on this recorder work great. I've never seen Hooker With A Penis, Pushit, Right In Two, Descending and Culling Voices live before so that was a huge treat for me!
