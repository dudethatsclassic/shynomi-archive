It's always a roll of the dice when it comes to assigned floor seating in a hockey arena.  It wasn't overly bad.  Had some chatters to the left of me but I did my best to mitigate that.

I don't usually add fade in/outs on my recordings...maybe I should at the very end at least!  Anyhow, I added one when I cut out the 12 minute intermission so it would be a little more of a seamless transition between The Grudge and Chocolate Chip Trip.

Show notes:
It's not often that I get a really bad view of the show but here I was with assigned seating on the floor and directly blocking my view of Danny Carey was a really tall bulky dude.  Ah well, what can I do?  I did my best to peer over his left and right shoulder from time to time.

The last time I had seen Tool was in 2002 in Ottawa and it was the most thrilling visual and audio experience of my life.  21 years later this statement still holds true.  Tool is on another level musically and visually and the show was incredible.
