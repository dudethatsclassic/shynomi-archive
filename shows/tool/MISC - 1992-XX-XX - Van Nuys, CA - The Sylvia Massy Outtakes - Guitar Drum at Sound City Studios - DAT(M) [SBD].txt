Notes 2025: Not a lot to say other than this DAT is what it is. No vocals, no 'real' outtakes, no insight simply a spattering of strange noises but it is what it is. It is a historical document, if you will, highlighting two core members experimenting. The guitar by Adam Jones is unsettling, at best, as it is an ominous distorted tone somewhat sounding like a box fan is directly next to the recorder/board. But, this is something that happened and it was caught on tape and sold for a considerable amount of money and here you are the lucky sonic recipient. 

I will make one simple request. Please, for the love of whatever deity, do not mess with this recording and 'sonically improve it by using X, Y or Z' just leave it alone and enjoy it as it should be: Something from the past you were never supposed to listen to.

Notes 2025 II: So, why release this now or ever? Ideally, one or more of the of the more than 50+ people who bought Tool stuff from Sylvia Massy at the height of the Covid Pandemic (i.e. November 2000) will realize that what you have is rare. Awesome. It would potentially be awesome to share it with the Tool community which is why CU.ORG released three Massy tapes which is, as of March 2025, three more than anyone else has. Come on, I spent a ton of money on stuff from Massy just like you did... And this is CU.ORG is offering for support for release of more Massy tapes... So?
