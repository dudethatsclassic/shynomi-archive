2024 Notes: This particular source is the best sounding of the AUD recordings pulled by a good measure. This is the type of recording that I hope to attain when I tape a concert; Tool or otherwise. Subjectively, the audio clarity I find to be pristine, the vocals (although buried in the mix at the show) do surface nicely throughout, the guitar is crunchy + clean, the cymbals are crisp whereas, like most DPA recordings, the bass + kick drums are a little thick. I removed a little bass from post and did a fresh transfer in 2024 complete with EQ. Not much more else to say other than, to my ears, this recording continues to sound more holistic, organic and better to my ears even in this age of solid state recorders. Unfortunately, I was using a 60m DDS tape and miscalculated the start time (based on previous Tool shows) so I ended up running out of tape right -which makes this source slightly incomplete- when Vicarious starts. Kind of a bummer but it is what it is. More information at collectiveunconscious.org
