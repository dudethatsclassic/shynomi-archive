COMMENTS:
The earliest Tool audience recording out there, back when they were just starting
to play gigs outside of California. This is different from the commonly
circulating recording that you can find on tooldriveproject. The lineage of
this tape is unknown outside of it being one of several uploads by,
"thethriftstorekid," on archive.org. I assume these are all recordings done
by that user in the Boston area so this could be the master tape. With a little
EQ work and balancing of the sound it turned out really good and there's not
that many tapes from this year that are easy to listen to so you'd better grab
this one!
