Notes: Blonde Redhead opened, not taped. Show #9 and first time doing VIP. Everyone in the first few rows seemed to be pretty energized. At first I was seated all the way on the end of Row B, but when the show started the guy next to me asked if I wanted to switch seats with his buddy which was way more centered and directly in front of Justin on Row C, so that was a win-win. The sound didn't seem too bad from where I was, but I still had to lower my levels all the way down to 1. I just held the recorder in my hand and pointed at the speakers above the stage for the whole show. No external mics were used. This was the first time I've seen Sober live so that was awesome and seeing the band that close was kind of unreal, especially during Culling Voices!
