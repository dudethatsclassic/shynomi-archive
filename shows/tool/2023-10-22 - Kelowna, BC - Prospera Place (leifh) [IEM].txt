I first saw Tool in this very building towards the end of the final leg of the 10,000 Days tour in 2007.  Happened on a free ticket just after the show started.  Even though it was probably one of the worst gigs of the tour and nearly turned into a riot, the 2007 concert blew me away and I would go see them a bunch more times.  Fast forward about 16 years (!) and Tool is playing the same little hockey rink.  I've upgraded my recording gear a bit since then but at the same time my interest in the band has waned.  The "new" album just doesn't do it for me, nor the ticket prices, nor the $500 CDs/LPs.  Don't get me started on the VIP thing.  But hey, good for them I guess...

Anyhow, this was a fun recording to do and I think it turned out reasonably well.  I hope you enjoy it.
