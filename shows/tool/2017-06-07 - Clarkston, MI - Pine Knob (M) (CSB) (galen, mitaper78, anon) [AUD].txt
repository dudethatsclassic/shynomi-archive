2024 Notes: Security is always rather abrasive at Pine Knob (then known as DTE Energy Music Amphitheater, the venue was Pine Knob from it’s inception until DTE paid a ton of money for the naming rights for 11 years turning back to Pine Knob in 2022, but I digress) and this evening was no exception. Thus, we decided on bringing in CSB microphones (with the metal housing for the 9V which always sets off metal detectors) for the sport of it. Also, to sort of compare from the same recording unit, Sony PCM-M10, between CSB this evening and the DPA 4061 used a week prior in Hamilton, Ontario (with the plastic housing which does not set off metal detectors). Maybe not surprisingly, the DPA4061 mics deliver a deeper sound but I must give it up for team CSB; they hold their own even though the bass has a bit of flutter to it. That said, CSB mics, at least the batch that Len released in 2002, were among the highest caliber. That said. as I was reminded, I am hyper critical of stuff pulled by The Collective. Other people, besides myself, that have heard this source dig it. With some EQ and fresh ears, this was reevaluated in 2024 and it really did turn out pretty sweet. Well, you be the judge.
