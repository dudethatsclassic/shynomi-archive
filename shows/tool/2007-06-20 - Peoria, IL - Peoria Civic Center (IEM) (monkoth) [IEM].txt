Notes:
First, a big thank you to that special little lady for loaning me the gear. 

Not the greatest sounding, but about 50 times better than the original raw recording. 
Somehow a loud layer of static got recorded underneath the sound. It sounded like an analog tape that had been run through 
about 10 generations of copying. That, and the sound was recorded very low. Not sure, but I think I may have accidently 
hit the phantom power switch on the Microtrack. 

So when mastering it, I took the Waves X-Noise Plugin to it, which is originally intended to restore old analog recordings
and vinyl records by eliminating the hiss, static and pops. It worked like a charm. There's still a little static underneath
the sound, but it is A LOT better than the original recording. Danny's ride cymbal got washed out in the static, so unfortunately 
you can barely hear it.  

The recording cuts out in the middle of the intermission. Just static from then on out, no music.
Also some good-sized chunks of Jambi are missing, likely due to me channel surfing during the song.
