Notes:

Another great show by Tool, but I'd have to say the show I saw back in October 2019 was a bit better. 
Adam made quite a few mistakes tonight, and even Danny was a bit off on Pneuma. We did get Descending, 
it was good but not as stellar as I was hoping for as this is my favorite Tool song. I can see why they
don't play it as often since it isn't quite polished yet. Invincible was probably the most solid song
of the evening. There are some volume drops at points where people had to walk by me. At the 2:05 mark
of Schism to about 2:11 of that song, my mics came unplugged when I had to stand to let someone by and
my recorder fell out of my pocket. I did manage to get them plugged back in quickly. Thankfully I had the 
Hold switch on, so the recording did not stop. I did manage to fix the shift in post, however the sound
with the onboard mics was not bad just at to high a level. At a lower level I might be able to get away
without using my external mics. I also did some volume lowering on crowd noise between songs. Enjoy!!!
