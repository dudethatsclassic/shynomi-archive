2024 Notes: This source was recorded at the top of the venue (specific reasons and story found at collectiveunconscious.org) and was if anything a hail-Mary. I was stoked to be inside the venue let alone taping Tool so this was a win. The recording is just what you would expect from an omni microphone, I don’t care what microphone you are using, but being too far away from the speakers is audible and not advised. This recording is rich with bass, semi-distant, vocals are muddy and the overall sonic enjoyment of this AUD recording leaves a bit to be desired - I say this even after extensive EQ and a fresh relook at the original file which was reassessed in 2024. But you can obviously be the best judge. As it is, until 2025, this source was not released so hopefully it fills a hole in a collector's heart I mean collection.
