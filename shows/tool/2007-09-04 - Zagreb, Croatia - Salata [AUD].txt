Here's my recording of the Zagreb show from the begining of this month.
I didn't really like the show. I thought the band were way too
much on the auto-pilot. Oh, well... Not every show can be a killer.

Recorded with: Core Sound Binaurals -> Sony MZ-RH1 MD (In Hi-MD mode)
Transfer: To .wav with Sonic Stage -> SoundForge (Normalizing, fade
in/out, track splitting) -> To .flac using FLAC Frontend (level 8) -> You

Tracklist:
01 - Jambi
02 - Stinkfist
03 - Forty Six & 2
04 - Schism
05 - Rosetta Stoned
06 - Flood
07 - Interlude - Encore Break
08 - Lateralus
09 - Vicarious

Note: Interlude is not complete. I opted to switch the disc during the
Interlude, as I was hoping they would play for more than 90 minutes.
They didn't. So I had to do a simple cross-fade of the two sources. It
turned out just fine. As far as the recording goes, it's not really a
high quality recording, but it's still enjoyable. MJK was not really
much present in the mix, and the acustics of the Dom Sportova seem to
be below par. I was standing some 6, 7 meters in front of the
soundboard.
