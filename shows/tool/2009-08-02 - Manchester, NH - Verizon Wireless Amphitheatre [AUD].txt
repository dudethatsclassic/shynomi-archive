taper's thoughts:
Heres the show I recorded in Manchester the other night, nothing close to AMAZEMENT. Im gonna go out on a limb for a moment, please hear me out. This was the sixth time Ive seen them in three years ( 5th show recorded ). Im so pissed off right now and I think other Tool fans would agree. The same songs now for two years, and nothing different at all on this tour. Cmon, anything off Opiate, Aenema or Undertow would be great. Instead of the long ass interludes between songs and the drum ext. in Lateralus. We saw it all in 2006 and 2007. Also when you listen to this, they wernt even in key with eachother, almost trying to rush through the show. You know what? I would much rather see them take the stage with no light show, no backdrops, just them playing some great songs that havent been played in a long time. My wife and I were so Pissed we left before Vicaroius was even played. Sorry heard many times, so this is for the collectors. I hope you enjoy, and for the people who saw them for the first time, I hope you enjoyed it.
