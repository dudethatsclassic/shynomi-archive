COMMENTS:
This matrix is made with a very uncommon type of recording, that being an ALD
capture of the show. It's basically a mono soundboard of the whole show, much
more balanced than an IEM but arguably lower fidelity. I attempted to make it
more enjoyable by overlaying three copies of the ALD and EQing them differently
to bring out the guitar, bass, vocals, etc. So it's like an AI stem splitter
without the AI part. This was then matrixed with the audience source. Someone
else once did a matrix of these sources but I thought it could use an upgrade
so I hope you enjoy this take on it!
