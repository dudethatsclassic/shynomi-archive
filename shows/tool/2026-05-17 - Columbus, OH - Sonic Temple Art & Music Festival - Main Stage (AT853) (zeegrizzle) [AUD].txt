notes from taper: On my way to union station to catch the light rail that takes
you to the airport and my uber driver and I run into the middle of a marathon 
downtown. I was definitely going to miss my flight for sure so I had to think 
quick. Told my driver thanks and jumped out the car to run my ass off and find 
the nearest scooter or else I was going to miss catching the latest possible 
light rail departure that'll catch this flight for me. After running for about 
three blocks like a psychopath I found one. Downloaded the app, purchased the 
ride and hauled ass with only a few minutes left to spare and got there with 
literally just a few seconds left before the train doors closed. I was dying 
on that train trying to catch my breath, but the running didn't stop there. 
Once I got to the airport I ran to security only to see the longest line I've 
ever seen. Yep, I'm definitely missing this flight.. But then some random 
person said the security line on the other side is shorter, so I ran to that 
one which was also long but not quite as bad as the first one. Still, it was 
dragging. After making it through security I ran some more all the way across 
the terminal to my gate and ended up being the last one to board with the 
attendants closing the airplane door right behind me. Honestly, it was a damn 
miracle I didn't miss my flight. If I would have hesitated for one second 
throughout any of that I would have missed this flight without a doubt. 
Easily the closest call I've ever had for sure. Anyways, after my damn near 
heart attack of a morning, I finally made it into the festival late around 
4pm. Went straight to the merch and after getting laughed at for asking if 
there were any posters left, I decided to just settle for a lousy shirt 
instead. Got a quick bite after that, checked out the rest of the festival 
then decided to go check out the main stage situation. This festival's main 
stage is actually inside a soccer stadium while the rest of the festival's 
stages surround the outside of it. Ended up finding a spot alongside the 
soundboard on the right side in the stands. Floor tickets were already sold 
out by the time I pulled the trigger or else I would have got a spot down 
there. No complaints though as my seat and the crowd around me ended up 
being really nice, plus there was barely any wind throughout the whole show. 
The sound was clear and not too loud so I had my levels at 5. A few hiccups 
from the band here and there but a great performance overall. First time seeing 
H. and Crawl Away so those were the highlights. A strong and solid festival set 
with a couple deep cuts to start out with these 2026 shows. Thank you Adam, 
Maynard, Danny & Justin. TOOL FOR LIFE
