Notes:
Taped from the side of the stage with the MK4’s pointing straight up at the stack. Decent tape. I am pretty happy with it. Minus the person who sat next to me and was screaming their head off at various points of the show. Honestly would have been a perfect tape, if that did not happen. There are a few points where they were so loud, I did cut the .5 second completely from the recording. These screams were usually in-between songs. So no music was cut. If you are downloading the 16 bit version, this has been converted from 24/48. EDIT: I decided to go back through and delete most of the .5-1.0 seconds of her screaming that almost clipped the MK4s. You will have no idea where in the recordings that this happens. I think now there were only 3-4 screams that I left in that I felt were acceptable and not overpowering/didn’t affect the songs. From the master file, it only removed 9 seconds total.

Was nice to meet up with GSP prior to the show. If he did not come down for the show, I probably would have stayed another day on my anesthesia rotation in Charlotte.

Big thanks to the Tool Drive and all the other tapers who share their tapes with the masses.
