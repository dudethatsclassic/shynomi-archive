COMMENTS:
The second of two nights at the Wiltern Theater, also captured on both an
IEM and AUD source. This night also has a circulating recording of
King Crimson's opening set, I highly recommend you seek it out and listen to
it along with this!

Just like the night before, I had also once attempted a matrix of this one,
but it was pretty rough. Nothing desynced during that attempt but due to
the roughness of both sources it just didn't sound very good. This time
around I tried my best to make them complement each other. The audience
source isn't nearly as good as the night before so it needed a lot of work.
One of the biggest issues I fixed was the heavy volume fluctuations during
The Grudge. I also attempted some heavy EQ to make it complement the IEM
well. The result isn't up to the level of a legit soundboard but given
the rough source material, I think I did good, and I hope you enjoy
listening!
