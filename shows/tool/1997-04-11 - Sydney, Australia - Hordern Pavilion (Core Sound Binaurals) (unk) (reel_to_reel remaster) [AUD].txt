COMMENTS:
This is a really good recording that not as many people seem to know about.
The unsplit master file has a glitch in it during Third Eye but otherwise it's
a really clean capture. I did some very conservative EQ and volume boosting.
The show itself is great as per usual with this period. Maynard does change
some phrasing on songs around this time because his voice must be a bit
worn out but you can hardly tell. This also has to be one of if not the longest
recorded Merkaba jams out there.
