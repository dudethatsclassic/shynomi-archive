Unbelievable performance by Tool. What a treat to hear Rosetta Stoned and Swamp Song in a festival set! I think Swamp Song almost got cut, it's scratched out from the setlist I got and before they played it you could see them getting together onstage to discuss something. Either way, glad that we got it and I think this might be one of my favorite sets I've gotten to see live. Visuals were on point and the crowd was great. Hope they come back through the Bay Area again soon!

Recording notes: sounds reflects what I heard. A huge thank you to the sound crew, they've sounded fantastic every time I've seen them regardless of the setting. I think you can hear talkers behind me that were between the rail and the soundboard who were quite chatty. Oh well! Filmed the entire set as well.
