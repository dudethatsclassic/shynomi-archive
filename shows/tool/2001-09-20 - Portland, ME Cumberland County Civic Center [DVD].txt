This is an excellent show with very good camera work imo. It was shot from the right of the stage and has lots of close-ups; taper definitely knew when to focus on whom. This show was the first time I saw Tool back when I was in high school! Gotta love Tool shows where all four hug at the end. There are a couple points at which parts of the crowd chant usa, including after Maynard's "positive" speech before Lateralus. 9/11 was only nine days earlier. I remember seeing someone behind me on the floor with an american flag. I missed Fantomas that night, so if anyone has audio or video of Fantomas from 2001.09.20, please share. Thanks and enjoy!
