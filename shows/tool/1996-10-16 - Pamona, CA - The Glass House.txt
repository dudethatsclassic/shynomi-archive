Tool
Glass House
Pomona, Ca
10/16/96

Taping rig - Sony D7 and Sony ECM 959A
Recorded 20-30 feet from stage center.
Recorded @ 48 kHz. This is lossless 
Tape and transfer by Highdesert aka BouldernBun aka Michael Lee

Transfer - Tascam DA 40/Soundblaster X-fi Extreme. 
Volume adjustment / bass roll off / resample to 44.1 kHz Sony Sound Forge. TLH to flac

A tape people have been after me about for years.It was a bear getting my rig into this show and 
San Diego the evening after. The wife and I were sweating bullits. SD was so tight and so bad I almost gave up while standing in line. As fate would have it
at 39 and 45, my wife and I were the oldest ones there, by FAR. We got to the head of the line, security took one look at us and 
"parents of the band??" Entrez vous. We still chuckle about that.  
3 versions of this (the dat, not the vhs) got out back in the day and all are rare.
A D7 to D7 dat clone using a mini plug mini cable (lame), a cassette version which someone transfered to cd and a cd version from the master dat via a shitty time period soundcard. 
All are inferior to this imo.
Commonly when you find this show it is the audio from a VHS tape of the show (not my source).
Recently , on Dime, I did run across the vid from this show coupled with my audio
w/o my permission and again, in inferior quality..
This is straight from the dat master. 
A great show and tape. However, the next night in San Diego was even better!
