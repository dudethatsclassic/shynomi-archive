<b>RECORDING NOTES:</b>
This Eugene show is my favorite Tool recording.
A different perspective, a different aspect;
I was down in the first set of rows right in front of the stage.
Fourth seat from center aisle, lined up with Maynard.
I always make sure to get good seats for Tool shows, but never this good.
My other recordings always had a bunch of crowd noise in front of me;
this time the vast majority of crowd was way behind in the distance.
Here, most crowd noise comes from those few around me by the stage.

Eugene was a special night -- an instant cult classic.
In contrast to the night before where Portland was a city show,
outstanding and all, but very business-like,
Eugene gave off a small-town, college campus vibe; noticeably looser.
Opiate blew everyone's mind, fractured like a stained glass church window.
. . . it found me peaking at that stage, what an experience.

If you don't have time to hear the whole recording,
please listen to just this sample of Opiate.
It has a trippy middle section, still gives me flashbacks to this very day:
