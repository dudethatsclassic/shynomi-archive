notes: 

Apologies for the overenthusiasm and talking - Took my 11 year old son for his first ever concert and first Tool concert - enjoyment and being in the moment came first! Recording came second.
The band / venue were super strict on recording and even phones out so I had to set and forget the recorder.

Seated in the Nosebleed section 2 rows from the very back centre - about as far away from the band as you could get - got a lot of echo/ reflection from roof so I am surprised the recording 
is audible at all.
