Notes by Adam:

My 39th TOOL show... 28th attempt at taping them... and it looks like the last one for awhile.  A couple of things to note...  They opened the show with a little video tease from the (at the time) unreleased Vicarious DVD... that blue and white TOOL logo that flashes on your screen when you play the DVD.  They also used the menu still shot during the intermission/set break.  Other than that there was nothing too different about this show.  It was nice hearing Flood again after so long.

Enjoy!
