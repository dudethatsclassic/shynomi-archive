It is what it is, this was a pre-911 Tool concert, second to the last, actually. Decided to keep the Charlevoix-crew post-show just as part of the recording because, why not, it was over 24+ years ago. There may be some bad language and an old friend of mine saying he ‘saw’ MJK’s dick onstage, but he did not.
