Tool
2001-07-19
North Shore Events Centre, Auckland, New Zealand

Bringing this recording back to life from the original MiniDiscs really brought back memories of the night. Just listen to Maynard's vocals on The Grudge, it sounds exactly like the album - as if it was playback.

The venue was unfortunately an indoor basketball court, so that's why it's echoey. The venue never meant for concerts. I was standing direct centre in front of the soundboard, leaning against it.

Security was incredibly tight for TOOL in those days. There were full body pat-downs and metal detectors at the gate. I wore jeans and a long baggy tee and hid my Core Sound Binaurals + Battery Box and Sharp MD-MT832 in my underwear and just hoped for the best.

I remember watching the sound guy at the end of the show carefully put away what I assume was a DAT recording of the show, so they were recording all their shows.

TOOL returned a year later to the same venue and by that time I'd upgraded to a Sony TCD-D8 DAT recorder which cost me an arm and a leg with bad New Zealand Dollar exchange rate and import duties. I was just 16 years old and working nights at a supermarket while going to school during the day to afford my equipment and concert tickets.

This is the first time the Masters have been digitally transferred.

I collect Marilyn Manson recordings, message me to trade.
