Notes by Adam: 

Not the ideal spot to tape with omni mics.  Cardioids would have been a better choice.  Maynard's vocals just didn't carry very well up to the upper levels of the theater, despite the speakers they had hung up in front of us.  Still, the results are pretty decent.  The fans around me were definitely into the show, but for the most part weren't too vocal, which is how I like it.  Overall this is a good recording.  The highlight of the show for me was hearing Right in Two... somewhat of a rarity it seems.

Enjoy!
