My 8th time seeing Tool.  The weather was nice, there was almost no wind.  This was on the Monster Energy Stage.  

Third Eye was a little rusty but still great.  Maynard had his big suit on as seen earlier in 2016.  Some guy in a wheelchair was repeatedly crowd surfing.  "Fuck these disfunctional, insecure candidates."  "Did you shit yourself?"  I didn't get to see any of the shows they played in January of this year, so it was awesome to finally hear Sweat live.  Not hearing Schism was a treat too!  At the end of Stinkfist, Maynard threw his megaphone down on the riser.  Unfortunately, these kinds of events attract drunken tools and as a result, several fights broke out during the show.  

I only taped Primus and Tool at this festival and did not attend the second day.  Huge thanks to RockTheMullet for driving up to Sacramento.  Nice to see a bunch of familiar faces in the crowd.
