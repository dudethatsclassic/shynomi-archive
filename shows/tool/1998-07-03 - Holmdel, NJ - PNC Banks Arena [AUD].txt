Notes - Many thanks to Mike for finding this, and sharing it. Good sound, perhaps recorded FOB / a bit further back. Sounds like a slight / brickwall distortion at times, but overall really very good. At the end of Eulogy is a pause, which is presumably orginates from a tape flip. This is the first performance on the 1998 Ozzfest tour, and thus is outstanding in quality. "I want what I want" ending on Sober.

About the source - This is probably the same as the previously uncirculated "Aiwa CM-30 > WMD-6 source" which was known to exist (a taper of the 5th of July show at the same venue, Jay B, was known to have a copy.) When asked about which microphones he used, the taper could not remember exactly, only that they were electric condenser mics. When prompted if it had been an Aiwa CM-30 microphone, while not 100% certain, he said that sounded about right.
