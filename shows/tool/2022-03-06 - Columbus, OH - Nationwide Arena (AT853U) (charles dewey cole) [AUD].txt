# Danny solo.

Also on the bill were The Acid Helps [not recorded].

I purchased tickets to see Tool in Toledo on May 4th, 2020 and had great taping 
seats. Sadly, that show got cancelled due to covid, so I had to make sure I caught 
one of their Ohio shows on their FI 2nd leg. The setlist has drastically changed 
from the last time I saw them in 2019. They have added some real gems and removed 
every single radio single from the setlist. Well, except for the few cities who get 
"Sober" on this run, that is. This show was really built for the hardcore fan in 
mind. Maynard also made mention of a Columbus favorite, Hot Chicken Takeover. He 
then used the word "spicy!" in "Opiate" and again in "The Grudge". Pretty hilarious. 
He also wore their shirt on stage from what I heard, but couldn't make it out from 
my seat. He must love HCT. One minor note, you will hear some static (most notably 
during the beginning of "Fear Inoculum") and at various times in between songs as
the show goes on. I tested my rig after loading the wave and as it turns out my
3.5mm male to male plug I run from my SP-SPSB-11 was going bad. I swapped it out &
the minor static went away. Bummer! But, honestly, you wont hear it once the band
turns it on. It could have been a lot worse! Strange enough, I used it the night
prior and had no issues. Fair warning.

"We both want... a spicy chicken dish!" - MJK
