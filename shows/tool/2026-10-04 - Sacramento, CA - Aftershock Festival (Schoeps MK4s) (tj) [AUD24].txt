Notes: Mother fucking TOOL at Aftershock. My third time seeing them at this festival touring on behalf of Fear Inoculum and they sounded as great as ever. This was easily the best sounding show I've heard all year and a huge props to FOH for making them sound massive. There were a few hiccups throughout the night including Danny's in-ears dying before Rosetta Stoned and Adam's talkbox going out during Jambi. He did the solo on his guitar. Either way, the set smoked and it was cool to hear H and Crawl Away and I hope we get to see a proper tour next year.
