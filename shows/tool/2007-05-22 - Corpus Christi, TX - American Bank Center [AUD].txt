tooligan's notes:

  First off I started off using Cool Edit Pro to combine all the separate tracks into 
a single wav file, and then amplified the entire show by 6dB. Then I used Wavelab to 
split the tracks and did some minor EQ adjustment (increasing the bass slightly, while 
lowering the mids and highs a bit to eliminate some of the brightness). Basically the same
EQ adjustments made to his SA show, but with a slight varience. I feel honored that he let 
me help master this show for him. Enjoy!
