Notes: A classic performance, including the new version of "Jeremy", "Star Boy", and the incredible "Little Wing/Maggot Brain", which is the only time this has been played by Pearl Jam. "Star Boy" is performed by Eddie Vedder along with Jimmy of the Frogs. "Little Wing/Maggot Brain" has Chad Smith (drummer for the RHCP) behind the drumkit. The Frogs set is complete, and there is some distortion at the very beginning which has been edited to improve it somewhat. "I Only Play 4 Money" is Ed playing with the Frogs during their set.
