Nice mix made from 3 sources available. Rear shot is a nice addition to the 2 front shots available since a few years, which makes this a great DVD.
Both audio has been equalized a bit. 

Audio 1 is sourced from Countryman Isomax II > Sony TCD-D7 (on 10' micstand, just behind Sounboard). Great quality, not much audience noise.
Audio 2 is a mix of 2 sources, using mainly the Country Isomax II version, and the Sennheiser ME-2002 used since is more ¨ambiental¨, giving you a more live sound experience.
