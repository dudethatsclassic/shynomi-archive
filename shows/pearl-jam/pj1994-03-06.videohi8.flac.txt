Technical Notes:

As mentioned above, this is the only known recording of this show.  Tracks 2-11 on Disc 1 had
excessive amounts of distortion.  This has been fixed with some processing.  The sound is still
poor, but it is far improved from the source.

Tracks 12-16 on Disc 1 have the best sound of the show.  The rest of the show is still very listenable though.

Other Notes:

This was the first show of the 1994 tour, and featured the debut of Spin the Black Circle.  There is a recording
of the next night (3/7/94) that will be circulating also.  The source is the same, but the sound quality is constantly
poor like the early part of this show.
