Notes : This is from my audience master DAT. This is my first upload of it. I doubt this version is in circulation, as the only other person I gave a copy to was a non trader. There are some problems at the beginning of Courduroy in the first minute and a half as I messed up the levels.
I don't remember too much about this show other than the crowd was pretty quiet. I was standing on the field to the left and in front of the sounddesk. The show was a benefit for various Seattle area charities, one of which I believe was the skateboard park across the street from the actual stadium. I've also included a scan of my ticket.
