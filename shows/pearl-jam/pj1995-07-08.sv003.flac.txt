Recording Notes:
The first song's levels were fixed, and the entire Pearl Jam show got a mids and highs boost. The Frogs' source was originally plagued with problems...most of these have been fixed but some minor pops, etc. still remain.

Show Notes (thanks to fivehorizons):
Security permitted cameras into this show. There is no pit at this venue—seats are bolted down. The mood was tentative, given the outcome of the previous show in San Francisco. After becoming comfortable with an extremely appreciative audience, the show became unforgettable. Ed quipped, "I gotta tell ya, my stomach hurts like hell right now. I think I got Cryptosporidium," a reference to a cryptosporidium outbreak in the Milwaukee water system in the early 90s. (Recall he left the stage early at PJ's previous show in San Francisco due to food poisioning.) A Neil Diamond act (Thunder & Lightning) joined PJ for 'Forever in Blue Jeans.'
