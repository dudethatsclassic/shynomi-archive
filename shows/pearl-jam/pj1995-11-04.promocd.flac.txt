Pearl Jam
November 4, 1995
Spartan Stadium
San Jose, CA, USA


For years, I worked in a record store and we sold used CDs. It was part of a big national chain, which really limited what we could take from customers, and one guy tried to sell me this Pearl Jam radio promo. We couldn't take it and, as often happened, the customer just left it at the store. I recently found it again after going through some stuff and decided to put it up here. I don't know why the promo/recording exist exactly - if the station recorded it from the live broadcast, or if it was recorded by the station for a later broadcast, or something else. But the recording is very nice and had pretty nice packaging, with a full artwork digipak and art on the discs. The tracklisting on the back was pretty bad, though, with some blank spots for the then-unreleased songs, some songs given incorrect titles, and disc 2 listing 15 tracks but having only 12 (as you can see below, there are three tracks with 2 songs each in the second half of the show). These are the liner notes:

"All Praise to Pearl Jam. Ticket sales of 34,818 make their November 4, 1995 concert the biggest event in the 62-year history of Spartan Stadium. Long live vitalogy!

Above all, we are obliged to the lsiteners of KOME for showing alternative rock that there is, in fact, a way to San Jose - and to the campus of San Jose State.

Many thanks,

Ted Cady, Events Director
San Jose State University

CD mastering by Tom Carr, Music Annex, Menlo Park, CA"
