Taken from Five Horizons:
Support act for Neil Young. Most of the songs are new at this point, as the band had just wrapped up a recording session for Vs. where most of them were written. Mike is running around a lot this show. Eddie plays guitar on Rearviewmirror, something that is common these days but was new at the time. Pulled Up is a Talking Heads tune and Tearing is by the Rollins Band. Eddie tells the crowd about the band's dressing room being broken into in Sweden the year before (6/25/92). Shot from the right side (facing stage) and is a little far away. The taper did a respectable job of shooting around the heads. I've been told that this show was videotaped by a lot of different people, but I believe this particular one is the most widely distributed.
