Show Notes (thanks to Five Horizons/TwoFeetThick):
Excellent show with the opening songs totally rocked out. Band looks really happy and playing great.
'Oceans' is a beautiful opener and 'In Hiding' is one of the finest versions played. 'Nothingman' is
introduced as a song about a man who "not only couldn't change the world ... he couldn't even take care
of himself." 'Institutionalized' is a Suicidal Tendencies cover ("I'm not crazy, you're the one who's
crazy ... institution.") 'Fuckin' Up' is a total surprise! Great crowd.
