Notes: This is the first time any recording of this show has surfaced and been publicly shared.  Many thanks go to SR for going through a tremendous amount of effort in tracking this down many years and getting it out.

The recording itself is decent and very listenable but does have a few issues.  It is a mono recording and there are many small mic bumps and/or digital errors that occur throughout.  The editing of the show involved a major correction in tape speed.  When this was sent to me, the speed was nearly 15% slow. I have never come across a recording where the speed was this far off and since I don't have any info about how the transfer was done, I'm not sure what would have caused it.  

Enjoy and Merry Christmas!
