BLG Notes: This is a mix I've wanted to redo for a long time. The mono FM source is thin, tinny, and almost unlistenable by
itself while the AUD source is pretty good overall. A mix was done many years ago, but it has some sync issues and in general
I thought I could improve on it.

The FM source does not run at a consistent speed, making it very difficult to align. After doing a baseline stretch of the entire 
show to get the sources close, I did another forty additional stretch points to really align things. 

I removed a siqnificant amount of applause from the CSB source and lowered the volume of some of the crowd noise, all before
mixing so it wouldn't be noticeable. In addition, I edited out the annoying DJ chatter so both encore breaks have been mostly
faded out.

As for the show itself, many consider this to be one of the best ever.
