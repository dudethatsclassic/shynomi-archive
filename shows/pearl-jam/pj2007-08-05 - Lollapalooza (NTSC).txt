Pearl Jam - 2007-08-05 Lollapalooza Festival, Chicago


Pearl Jam headlined Lollapalooza Festival making return here after 15 years. This performance was a subject of controversy when Ed's words 'George Bush, leave this World alone' for "Another Brick In The Wall" tag had been censored during broadcast. Thomas Young, a veteran soldier to whom "No More" song was dedicated, was invited to the stage for this song, joining Ed and Ben Harper. And for "Rockin' In The Free World" many guests appeared on stage with the band.
