Video is shaky and dark sometimes, but overall is better than the first night. Some nice close-ups, although sometimes with some pixelation.

Some minor cuts (less than 1 second) on both discs in Who You Are, Animal, Hail Hail, Tremor Christ, Not For You were patched with fade-out / fade-in.

Other bigger cuts (Footsteps and Black / Smile) were patched with shots from the show.

Video ends in the beginning of Yellow Ledbetter, so from there to the end of the show (Yellow Ledbetter and Rockin' In The Free World) there's a slideshow with shots from the show.
