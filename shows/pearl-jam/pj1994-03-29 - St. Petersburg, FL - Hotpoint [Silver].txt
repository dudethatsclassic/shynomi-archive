Pearl Jam
3/29/94
St. Petersburg, FL

Notes:
This source is ripped directly from a 90's CD bootleg called "Hotpoint".  It contains the complete 3/29/94 show, as well as 4 soundcheck songs that are also worth checking out, even if the quality is iffy.  The bootleg is a standard sounding audience bootleg from the Vs. tour, but the show itself is a piledriver.  The band is ferocious, and Eddie is particularly animated (some of his mid-song banter is hilarious).

Some items of note:
1) MONSTER versions of: Blood, Porch (well over 10 mins), Black (with a unique ending), and pretty much every song.
2) Pre-Vitalogy versions of: Whipping, Last Exit (labelled as "Three Days" on the bootleg, complete with a hilarious intro by Eddie), and Not For You (HOLY ANGRY!!!)
3) Unique "TYAAM" cover to end the show.

This is a great, great, great Vs-era show and I cannot speak highly enough of it.  I have had this on CD going on 25 years now, and it never ceases to amaze me.  The songs, the banter, the energy...it's a nearly perfect show showcasing the band at the height of their popularity.
