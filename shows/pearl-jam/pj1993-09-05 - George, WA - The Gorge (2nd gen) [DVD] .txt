The audio has been redone and added to the video along with menus and chapters. Tripod shot, dead center.

Taken from Five Horizons:

This video was tri-pod shot so it's rock steady, but it doesn't pan or zoom in much at all so Mike is almost always out of the picture, as is Stone. The taper was up high enough that we get a good view of Dave though, something that is rare in PJ concert videos. Dave screws up the beginning of Even Flow, thinking Dissident was next, and the band grinds to a halt to let him know he misread his set list. After the song Eddie, impressed with the scenery, says, "Maybe we should just stop playing -- we should all just sit here and watch the sun set." After Glorified G Mike strums the end of Yellow Ledbetter a bit before Stone begins Daughter. Eddie adds lines from Neil Young's Tonight's the Night to the end of Daughter, and lines from Mudhoney's Suck You Dry to Porch. (PJ and Mudhoney opened for Neil.) Mark Arm from Mudhoney is on co-lead vocals for Sonic Reducer. The Gorge is an amphitheatre with beautiful mountainous scenery and lakes behind the stage. The show started in the afternoon and ended after dark.
