Pearl Jam
June 3, 2006
Continental Airlines Arena
E. Rutherford, NJ

This is the FLAC (Lossless) version.

There was a technical problem at this gig that resulted in a
portion of the recording being taken from a secondary source.
Early in the 5th number (Animal), Jeff stopped playing and the
rest of the group soon followed.  There was some kind of power
outage.  Ed sat down and grabbed a smoke at the side of the stage.
Several minutes passed before things were corrected.  This was
arguably the best of the 5 shows I attended on this tour.

When I purchased this, Basecamp (http://bootlegs.pearljam.com)
issued a warning that there were some technical problems.

This show requires 3 CDs

I made my CDs with The first 5 tracks (from the secondary source)
on Disc#1 (As the artwork indicates). This includes everything up
until "Animal" is restarted and completed.  Disc#2 begins where
the primary sound source is used.  It's gets picked up shortly after
"Animal" begins for the second time, so there is some overlap of
material.
