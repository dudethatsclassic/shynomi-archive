I've been waiting to see Pearl Jam for years and they delivered in a big way. They played nearly three hours with two six-song encores.

I wasn't going to tape since they sell their shows, but decided to at least give it a try since they allow it. The show is complete, but it's a hat tape from Club Level 6 which is stage left near the turn in the arena. The seats were fine for viewing and the sound was good, but the recording sounds like what it is, a hat tape from a distance. 

There is occasionally a bit of noise between tracks, most noticeable during the encore breaks.  Unfortunately, there were also some loud asshats behind us that liked yelling for the sake of yelling. You'll hear them. Not much you can do about it. Instead of tracking all the Eddie stories, I left then at the end of the previous track.

It's listenable and should tide everyone over until the band makes the show available on Nov. 18. Just don't expect open taping from the floor quality. 

Thanks to PJ for a great show and allowing fans to record the shows. 

enjoy!
