We hit traffic at the Tobin Bridge at 5:25 and was stuck in it all the way to Fenway. The show had a start time of 6:30. By the time I parked my car and started walking it was 7:20 and we were about a mile walk away. While we were walking we didnt hear any music playing and when we got in they hadn't taken the stage yet. Got to our seats at 7:45 and at 7:50 I believe they took the stage. Thought we missed the first hour but somebody was watching out. At the second encore my R9 frozz and didnt seem like it was working. Nothing was moving and the time on it was not moving. I couldnt turn it off with out pulling out the batteries and when I did that I was sure it ereased the 2 hours of what I taped. I was so pissed off and was able to turn it back on and get the rest of the show. We bailed during Babe because to be honest I thought I lost everything. Turns out the rock gods where looking out. I thought this was gonna be so incomplete in many ways from missing the first hour, to erasing the first 2 hours. Its only missing YL and il take this as a win.
