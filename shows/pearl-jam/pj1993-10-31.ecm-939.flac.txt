Notes: One of the longest and best shows from the 1993 leg of the VS tour.  

An unknown generation of this recording has long circulated among PJ collectors, but with the wrong source information (Sony ECM-909 > Sony WM-D3). This was recorded on DAT, not cassette.  

The recording itself is decent, but suffers from some phasing and crowd noise.  I have done extensive editing to reduce some of the crowd noise, claps, and other annoyances. It’s still not perfect, but along with some EQ work, I think it makes for a more enjoyable listen.

Thanks as always go out to the original taper.
