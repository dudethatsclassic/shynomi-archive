This is one of the most famous and impressive Pearl Jam shows from the early era. The band played before one of the largest crowds in their short then history, that was altogether new experience for them. And they rocked one of the most agressive and passionate performances ever. As the band broke into the long "Porch" jam, Eddie made his most spectacular dive from the stage into the crowd. Must be seen!

This is a professional good quality broadcast video of almost entire set and a small after-show interview with Eddie.
