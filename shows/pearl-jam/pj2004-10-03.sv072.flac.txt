Show Notes (thanks to Five Horizons/TwoFeetThick):
Show begins with a great run of high energy songs. 'Light Years' is 
dedicated to Johnny Ramone and 'Sad' is dedicated to Linda. A new cover,
'Millworker' is by James Taylor, and starts the acoustic set with Ed 
playing solo. 'Masters Of War' includes the changed lyrics,"... for 
threatening my daughter, just born and just named." The 'Song X' tag is 
particularly appropriate given the times and the cause. Tim Robbins helps 
out with 'The New World' again. The second encore concludes on a high note 
with another new cover, 'Kick Out The Jams' by the radical, Michigan-based 
band, the MC5. Solid show and setlist.
