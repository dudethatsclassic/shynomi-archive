Recording Notes:
Sound has been remastered to great improvement. One or two stereo to mono shifts are the only flaw that remains.

Show Notes (thanks to Five Horizons):
'Habit' lyrics are changed from "speaking as a child of the 90s" to "speaking as someone who taken many pucks in the face." Lots of covers songs hinted at: three Who songs as 'Daughter' outros, an intense version of 'Blood,' with a teaser of David Bowie's 'Fame;' and some of Neil Young's 'Cinnamon Girl' improved into 'I Got Shit.'
