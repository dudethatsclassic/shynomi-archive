notes: This show is frequently referred to as "Mudfest" due to a downpour prior to 
the concert. One of the reasons that this show is so incredible is that PJ was jumping 
out of their collective skins to be able to cut loose for more than 90 minutes. Currently 
touring with Neil Young, they were confined to an early 45 minute set. They were offered 
this slot (and are the only non-Canadian band ever to play this large festival) and it 
became their night. Despite Ed's drinking, PJ is very high energy and intense.
