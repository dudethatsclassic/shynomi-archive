Show Notes:
This was a fun show.

The road trip had just started, and one of my long time Tesla-friends (Dana) was able to take my extra ticket off my hands. I didn't want her to pay, but she insisted on something, so we went to one of the 
restaurants that was featured on "Diners, Drive Ins & Dives", her treat.

The main thing I remember about it was there was a battered-fried green tomato "stacker" thing that was one of the most amazing appetizers I've ever had (the burger was fantastic too).  A meal that truly was fit for 
this show.

And the show itself?

This might have been one of the last PJ tours with 'epic' setlists (compared to the "24 and done" ones nowadays)....18-20 song main set, 7-9 song first encore, 5-6 song second encore was the formula this tour, and 
the band did not disappoint, playing for just over 3 hours, tour-debuts of more than a handful of songs....I'd totally forgotten this was sent to Dennis years ago, he did do up the Lexington one (prior to when 'show 
notes' were a thing on this end), but somehow this one slipped thru the cracks.

So crack a cold one and thank Dennis for his efforts.

A small box of value (analog masters from the 90's) will be headed his way shortly

-Fuck Hagar will eventually start taping again....someday ;)
