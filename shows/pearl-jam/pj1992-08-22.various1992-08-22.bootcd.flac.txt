Notes:

These are all fresh rips from the original discs, done just for these remasters.  Over 6hrs of music.  I think most of these are complete sets, except Pearl Jam and Red Hot Chili Peppers.  1992 was my first and favorite Lollapalooza year.

All shows audience source, all sounded so-so but needed a little help.  Track splits are all re-done.  I created a separate txt file and folder for each disc.  

Included are scans of the box set cover.  Vol 1 cover I scanned myself.  Vol 2 cover vanished long ago so I found images online.  
Also included a scan of my ticket from the Dallas 1992-09-06 show.  What a fantastic day... :-)

Silverdiscs > wav > Audacity > flac 8

enjoy!
-exene
