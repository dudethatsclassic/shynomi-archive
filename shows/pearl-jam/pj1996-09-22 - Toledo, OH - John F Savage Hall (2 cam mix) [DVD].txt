Notes and comments:
Taken from Five Horizons:

This was a very good performance and the band seemed pretty into it, especially evident on Leaving Here. The camera is quite shaky, however, and heads frequently get in the way, which is too bad because the camera was in a good position above the crowd with the best possible angle for getting all the band members in the shot.
