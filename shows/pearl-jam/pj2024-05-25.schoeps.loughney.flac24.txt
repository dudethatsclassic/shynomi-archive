Note:
Bottlerock 2024, Jam Cellars stage. Good set marred by some crowd noise, people were really pushing to go up, but then pushing to leave early. I suspect some alcohol was involved as well.  Last Kiss was added to the setlist at the last minute, and Eddie messes up the lyrics. Bradley Cooper joined to duet on Maybe It's Time and played tambourine on Rockin' in the Free World.
