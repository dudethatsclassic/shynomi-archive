Notes and comments:
Taken from Five Horizons:

Shot from left of center (facing stage). The camera is pretty shaky and has trouble staying fixed on the band. It follows Eddie mostly anyway so we almost never see Mike. Eddie is watching the crowd during Jeremy, and yells at them between lines. ("Be careful!... I'm watching you you fuckers!") Eddie after State of Love and Trust: "This band -- yeah we'll play for a cause every once in a while if it's a good one. Well, this is one that's gonna affect us all. Dee, the drummer from L7, has not had her period for almost two months -- she's a little bit worried about it. So, we dedicate the song Even Flow to her, to her menstrual cycle!" He then changes some of the words in the song, singing about pillows made of Tampax. Other than that there is nothing special about this performance, and the shaky camera work makes this only an average vid
