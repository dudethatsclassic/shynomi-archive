Lineage: Sony MZ R-700 plus Sony ECM-CS10 microphones >Wavelab>

Location: 15 meters away from stage, on the right side
The identifier is in CD1, track 7 at 01:33 (“…we never played in Düsseldorf..”)

This show was originally planned for the bigger LTU-Arena (50.000 capacity Football arena).

The ISS-Dome (capacity about 10.000 people, sold out that evening) is mainly used for ice hockey,
the sound was awful, the organisation and security desastrous, hundreds of people came in when Pearl Jam has started.
I had massive problems to bring in my recorder+mike, but it was ok.
Sound is getting better after half an hour or so.
