Wow what an amazing sounding recording.  I recommend this to everyone.  No EQ or other processing.

Whether Porch is from this show is debatable.  The published setlist lists it as the last song, but the recording sounds totally different than the rest of this show, mono, and tightly compressed like a broadcast. I've left it here for the sake of completion, but separate from the rest of the show.
