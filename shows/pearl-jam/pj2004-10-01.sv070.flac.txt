Show Notes (thanks to Five Horizons/TwoFeetThick):
After Ed finishes his preset, he says he's going to read a statement, opens 
up a piece of paper and talks about bringing a conservative senator for 
Pennsylvania on stage to give the conservatives fair time. An old man with 
his legs draped in a flag comes out in a wheelchair and he begins to play a 
song while the crowd boos. He takes hold of the microphone and seems to be 
electrocuted and he falls and the lights go down. But when the lights return, 
it was all an act by Tim Robbins and the Gob Roberts set begins. Poor 
acoustics at this venue and a stack blows to make it worse. A big highlight 
is 'Sad' (a Binaural outtake appearing on the Lost Dogs release), played at 
a live show for the first time tonight. The acoustic set begins with Ed 
saying, "Anybody who is undecided, if you think about it, being undecided is 
really dangerous, and I have a perfect example: people with mullets. That's 
indecision. "Do I want it short? Do I want it long? I just can't take a 
stand.'" Then he introduces 'Soon Forget': "This is for the rich guys, the 
really rich guys, who love it when you don't vote." Ed later teases that 
will play 'Bushleaguer,' but instead play a "love song" ('Nothingman'). 
Tim Robbins helps out with 'The New World' again. Mike totally misses his 
solo in 'Baba,' then realizes he needs to play (with Stone and Ed both 
looking his way) and botches it.
