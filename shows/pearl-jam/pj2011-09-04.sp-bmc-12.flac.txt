recording notes
First off, the recording is incomplete, my 240 volt battery recharger was not compatable with the US 120 volt system, something I did not realise till I put in the batteries before PJ took to the stage. I had used the 2nd set for Queens of the Stone Age, and The Strokes earlier in the day, if I had not recorded those sets I would have had enough juice to get the whole show, anyways the set that didn't recharge gave up the ghost during LBC, and by the time I put the other half used set in I'd missed the start of Even Flow, that got me through to the first encore and then died, so that is all I could capture. 
Also I was up on the lawn in the fan club section and due to the wind I decided to use the Omni mics over the Cardiods (I taped QOTSA with these and it's unlistenable) as they pick up the wind alot, the downside is the omnis pick up alot more surrounding noise, other than one total toolbag, the surrounding crowd were not too bad, but there is singing and clapping present in the recording.
