

This is rare, early Neil Young live.  The Wichita Falls tracks are solo acoustic (pre-Springfield). Very good sound qualities for the time. There are also two Buffalo Springfield outtakes.  As well, there are two 45 single demos with Neil’s Canadian group the Squires. 

Tracks 1-7; 10 and 11 = Live in Wichita Falls, Kansas 1-12-65
Tracks 8 and 9 = Buffalo Springfield studio outtakes
Tracks 12 and 13 = The Squires 45 single demos  ‘63

Enjoy!
